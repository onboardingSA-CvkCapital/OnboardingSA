Eskom is South Africa's state-owned electricity utility. It runs the coal-fired power stations in Mpumalanga and Limpopo, Koeberg nuclear station near Cape Town, and the transmission and distribution network that delivers power across the country.

Most head-office jobs are at Megawatt Park in Sunninghill, Johannesburg. Engineering, artisan and operating posts are at the power stations and regional offices. Eskom also runs graduate-in-training, apprenticeship and learnership programmes for young people.

How to apply

  • All Eskom jobs are applied for on the Eskom careers portal. Create a profile once, then apply for each post separately.
  • Salaries are usually advertised as "market related". The grade (for example T12 or M15) tells you the level of the post.
  • Upload your documents in the format the portal asks for. Incomplete profiles are filtered out automatically.
  • Many posts close quickly, often within one to two weeks, so apply early.

Official source: Eskom careers portal

Before you apply

  • Eskom does not recruit through WhatsApp groups or ask for payment. Only apply through the official portal.
  • Apprenticeship and graduate posts usually ask for maths and science results, so have your matric certificate ready.
  • OnboardingSA is free and never collects your application. Every job below links to the official advert.
