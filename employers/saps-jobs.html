The South African Police Service (SAPS) is one of the biggest employers in the country, with police stations, units and offices in every province. Its vacancies fall into two groups, and it helps to know which one you are looking at.

Police Act posts are for police officials: the Police Trainee intake, re-enlistments of former members, and specialised operational posts. Public Service Act posts are civilian jobs inside SAPS, such as admin clerks, finance, HR, IT, cleaners and drivers. These are employed under the same rules as other government departments.

How to apply

  • Police Trainee and some other posts are applied for online on the SAPS e-Recruitment portal, not by e-mail.
  • Other posts are advertised as PDF adverts on the SAPS careers pages. Each advert says which form to use and where to send it.
  • Police Trainee applicants go through screening that can include fitness, medical and psychometric assessments. A criminal record or pending case usually rules you out.
  • Read the age, citizenship and driver's licence requirements carefully. SAPS applies them strictly.

Official source: SAPS careers page

Before you apply

  • SAPS never asks for money to apply, to "secure a spot", or for training. Anyone asking is a scammer.
  • Keep your reference or application number. You will need it if you are called for assessment.
  • OnboardingSA is free and never collects your application. Every job below links to the official advert.
