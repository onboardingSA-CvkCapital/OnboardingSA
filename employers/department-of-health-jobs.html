Public hospitals and clinics are run by the provincial Departments of Health, so each province advertises its own posts. This page brings together the open posts from all provinces, grouped by province below.

You will find professional nurses, medical officers, pharmacists, radiographers and other health professionals, plus the admin, finance, porter, cleaning and maintenance staff that keep a hospital running.

How to apply

  • Use the current Z83 application form, filled in completely and signed. Applications on the old Z83 (before 2021) are rejected. Our Z83 guide walks through every section.
  • Attach a detailed CV. Most departments now ask for certified copies of your ID and qualifications only if you are shortlisted, but some still want them upfront, so do exactly what the advert says.
  • Quote the reference number of the post on your Z83. If you apply for two posts, send two separate applications.
  • Send it the way the advert says: e-mail, online portal, post or hand delivery. The address is different for every post.
  • Late applications are not accepted, and if you have not heard back within about three months, you can take it that you were not shortlisted.
  • Health professionals must show proof of registration with their council: SANC for nurses, HPCSA for doctors and most allied health professions, and SAPC for pharmacy.

Official source: DPSA Public Service Vacancy Circular

Before you apply

  • Health professional salaries follow the OSD (Occupation Specific Dispensation). The grade in the title, such as Grade 1 or Grade 2, depends on your years of experience after registration.
  • Apply to the hospital or district office named in the advert. It is often not the provincial head office.
  • OnboardingSA is free and never collects your application. Every job below links to the official advert.
