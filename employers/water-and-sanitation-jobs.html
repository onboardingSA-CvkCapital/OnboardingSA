Water and Sanitation jobs

Department of Water and Sanitation
Updated 28 Oct 2026

47 open posts · 5 provinces · 47 closing this week

The Department of Water and Sanitation (DWS) manages the country's water resources: dams, bulk water schemes, water quality and water-use licensing. It has a head office in Pretoria and provincial offices and construction units around the country.

Its vacancies include engineers, scientists, hydrologists, technicians, artisans and construction workers at dam sites, as well as admin and finance staff.

How to apply

  1. Use the current Z83 application form, filled in completely and signed. Applications on the old Z83 (before 2021) are rejected. Our Z83 guide walks through every section.
  2. Attach a detailed CV. Most departments now ask for certified copies of your ID and qualifications only if you are shortlisted, but some still want them upfront, so do exactly what the advert says.
  3. Quote the reference number of the post on your Z83. If you apply for two posts, send two separate applications.
  4. Send it the way the advert says: e-mail, online portal, post or hand delivery. The address is different for every post.
  5. Late applications are not accepted, and if you have not heard back within about three months, you can take it that you were not shortlisted.
  6. Engineering and science posts usually require professional registration (ECSA for engineers, SACNASP for natural scientists) or candidate status.

Official source: DPSA Public Service Vacancy Circular

Before you apply

  1. Construction posts at dam sites are often on contract for the length of a project. Check whether the post is permanent.
  2. OnboardingSA is free and never collects your application. Every job below links to the official advert.
