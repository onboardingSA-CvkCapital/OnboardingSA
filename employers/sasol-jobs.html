Sasol jobs

Sasol
Updated 28 Oct 2026

16 open posts · 3 provinces · 5 closing this week

Sasol is an energy and chemicals company. Its biggest South African operations are at Secunda in Mpumalanga and Sasolburg in the Free State, with mining operations around Secunda and offices in Johannesburg.

Typical Sasol vacancies are in engineering, operations, maintenance and artisan trades, mining, safety and health, and support functions. Sasol also offers learnerships and bursaries for young people.

How to apply

  1. Apply on the Sasol jobs site. You will need to register a profile before you can submit.
  2. Each post lists the minimum qualifications and years of experience. Meet them before applying, because screening is strict.
  3. Learnership adverts often limit applicants to people living near the operation (for example Secunda or Sasolburg). Check the area requirement.

Official source: Sasol jobs site

Before you apply

  1. Sasol never charges application or training fees. Report anyone who asks.
  2. Keep your CV up to date in your Sasol profile. It is what recruiters see first.
  3. OnboardingSA is free and never collects your application. Every job below links to the official advert.
