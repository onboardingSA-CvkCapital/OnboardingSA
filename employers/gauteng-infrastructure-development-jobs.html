Gauteng Infrastructure Development jobs

Gauteng Department of Infrastructure Development
Updated 28 Oct 2026
100 open posts1 provinceEngineeringSecurityManagement

The Gauteng Department of Infrastructure Development builds and maintains provincial government buildings such as schools, clinics, hospitals and offices, and manages the province's property portfolio.

Its vacancies lean towards the built environment (engineers, architects, quantity surveyors, project managers and artisans), plus property management, supply chain and admin posts.

How to apply

  • Use the current Z83 application form, filled in completely and signed. Applications on the old Z83 (before 2021) are rejected. Our Z83 guide walks through every section.
  • Attach a detailed CV. Most departments now ask for certified copies of your ID and qualifications only if you are shortlisted, but some still want them upfront, so do exactly what the advert says.
  • Quote the reference number of the post on your Z83. If you apply for two posts, send two separate applications.
  • Send it the way the advert says: e-mail, online portal, post or hand delivery. The address is different for every post.
  • Late applications are not accepted, and if you have not heard back within about three months, you can take it that you were not shortlisted.
  • Professional built-environment posts usually require registration with ECSA, SACAP or SACPCMP. Attach proof.

Official source: DPSA Public Service Vacancy Circular

Before you apply

  • This department often advertises many posts at once. Use the reference numbers to keep your applications apart.
  • OnboardingSA is free and never collects your application. Every job below links to the official advert.
