Job Advert Summary

To perform general repairs, maintenance and installations to pipe work, geysers and ablutions on property and plant relevant to trade, including the three satellite stations.
Minimum Requirements

 

  • Grade 12/Equivalent
  • Recognized Plumbing Trade Test Certificate
  • Completed a recognized Plumbing Apprenticeship
  • 5 years’ relevant experience in Plumbing
  • Valid driver's license
Primary Duties
  • Attend daily roll call and briefings in allocated conference area.
  • Receive job allocations from immediate supervisor.
  • To set out and perform work as per required standards and specifications.
  • To clean up machines and work area when job completed.
  • Required information recorded on job cards.
  • Fill in own and subordinates time sheets correctly work, and the manpower.
