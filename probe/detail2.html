Job Description:

COMPLIANCE COORDINATOR

Location: Dryden, Mpumalanga South Africa

Business Unit: BME Production and Logistics

Reports To: Plant Manager

Contract Type: Permanent

Submission Closing Date: 03 November 2026

Join BME and shape the future of mining.

BME, a division of Omnia Holdings, is a leading global provider of blasting and mining solutions, partnering with customers to improve safety, productivity and sustainability across the mining value chain. Combining advanced blasting technology, digital innovation, explosives and technical expertise, we help mining operations solve complex challenges, optimise performance and unlock value. From open-pit mines to underground operations, our solutions support responsible, efficient mining and enable the critical resources needed to power modern economies.

As part of Omnia Holdings Limited (JSE: OMN), you'll join a global, diversified business operating across the agriculture, mining and chemicals sectors. With operations in 23 countries, customers in more than 40 countries, over 70 distribution centres, and approximately 3,800 employees worldwide, Omnia develops innovative products, technologies and services that support food security, responsible mining and sustainable industrial development. Through science, innovation and collaboration, we help customers use resources more efficiently, solve complex operational challenges and create lasting value for a changing world.

At Omnia, you'll have the opportunity to work alongside industry experts, contribute to meaningful challenges, and grow your career in a purpose-led organisation committed to innovation, learning and sustainable impact.

Innovating to enhance life, together creating a greener future.

ROLE IMPACT

To provide an administrative and data collection service on environmental issues to the BME SHERQ management team, in collaboration with all BME sites.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Deliver production targets as per the schedule.
  • To monitor and track implementation of environmental legislation at all BME sites and verify closed-off actions
  • To track and monitor usage of diesel and other fuel sources
  • To conduct site audits on diesel inventory
  • To liaise with spillage response service providers
  • To track costs related to environmental incidents
  • To support with environmental investigations
  • To collate data for carbon footprint updates to group SHERQ
  • To coordinate all activities for the ISO 14001 audit including document control
  • To monitor and provide feedback on CSI projects at BME sites in collaboration with HR

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

Qualifications

  • Grade 12 or equivalent
  • SAMTRAC Certificate

Experience

  • 2 years demonstrated experience in providing on time reporting and value adding administrative and data collection support on risk, safety, health, quality and environment.
  • 1 year demonstrated experience in D365 administration / Isometrics

Key Competencies

  • Attention to detail
  • Numerical fluency/skills
  • Proven administration skills
  • Problem solving - identify problems and review information to find practical solutions
  • Critical Thinking
  • Committed to customer service backed with excellent communication skills (written, non-verbal and verbal)
  • Strong people management and interpersonal skills
  • Excellent interpersonal skills required, with the ability to liaise diplomatically and maintain solid business relationships with internal and external stakeholders
  • Ability to deal with pressure

DIVERSITY, EQUITY & INCLUSION

A diverse team drives stronger outcomes. At Omnia, we are committed to creating an inclusive workplace where every individual is valued, respected and empowered to contribute. We believe that diverse perspectives, experiences and backgrounds strengthen our ability to innovate, solve complex challenges and create sustainable value. We welcome applications from suitably qualified candidates who share our commitment to excellence, collaboration and making a positive impact.

THANK YOU FOR YOUR INTEREST

Thank you for considering a career with Omnia. We appreciate the time and effort invested in your application. Our Talent Acquisition team carefully reviews every application received. While only shortlisted candidates will be contacted, we encourage you to keep exploring future opportunities within the Omnia Group as we continue to grow our teams across agriculture, mining and chemicals. We wish you every success in your career journey and thank you for your interest in being part of our purpose: Innovating to enhance life, together creating a greener future.
