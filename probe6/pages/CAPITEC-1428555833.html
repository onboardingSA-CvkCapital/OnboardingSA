Software Development Manager

Apply now »

Date: 18 Oct 2026

Location: Stellenbosch, ZA

Company: Capitec Bank Ltd

Apply by: 03 September 2026

We're on the lookout for energetic, self-motivated individuals who share our passion for service in the banking industry. To be part of the journey, follow the steps below:

1. To see what life at Capitec is all about and complete a short assessment, please click here!

2.  Once you have completed the above finalize your application by clicking apply below.

Shape the future of a critical banking channel

We are looking for an experienced Software Development Manager: Cash Devices to lead the teams responsible for our cash-device technology platforms, terminal applications, integrations and operational delivery capability. This is a strategic technical leadership role for someone with strong ATM and transaction-processing expertise who can provide technology direction, manage key vendor and platform relationships, ensure the stability, availability and security of critical banking systems, and develop high-performing teams. The successful candidate will drive delivery excellence, operational resilience, continuous improvement and people development across a critical customer channel.

What you'll do

  • Lead multiple ATM technology delivery teams responsible for ATM applications and services.
  • Drive solution design, architecture governance, engineering standards and delivery quality.
  • Ensure system availability, performance, resilience and security across a critical banking channel.
  • Oversee software development, enhancements, maintenance and production support.
  • Lead major incident management, root cause analysis and recovery activities.
  • Build and develop high-performing technical delivery teams through coaching and mentoring.
  • Manage vendor relationships, technical roadmaps and delivery outcomes.
  • Partner with product, technology and business stakeholders to deliver strategic objectives.
  • Partner with the Product Head to translate the strategic product roadmap into executable delivery plans, release forecasts, milestones and team-level execution priorities across the cash-devices technology portfolio.
  • Lead agile planning practices with the team, translating prioritised business features into delivery-ready team epics and, through iterative refinement, clear user stories, while managing dependencies, capacity, sprint and release planning, and delivery tracking.
  • Drive engineering best practices including secure coding, code reviews, CI/CD and production readiness

What we're looking for:

  • 8+ years of direct ATM domain experience.
  • Proven experience within ATM delivery, support and operational environments.
  • Strong technical delivery leadership experience within a banking or financial services environment.
  • Proven ability to build high-performing technical delivery teams through coaching, mentoring, capability development, succession planning and career growth initiatives.
  • Experience leading technical teams in an agile delivery environment, including translating strategic roadmaps into delivery plans, decomposing prioritised features into team epics and user stories, forecasting delivery, managing dependencies and tracking execution.
  • Experience managing critical production systems and high-availability platforms.
  • Production resilience & Security (ie. Incident Management, Root cause analysis, problem management, disaster recovery, performance analysis and optimisation, production monitoring, ATM security Principles, Encryption concepts, PCI DSS and PCI Pin standards)

 

Technology Engineering & Integration

  • Proficiency in programming and scripting languages appropriate to the technology environment
  • API Integrations
  • Enterprise Integration Patterns
  • Architecture Governance
  • Secure Coding Practices
  • CI/CD Principles
  • Technical Design Reviews and Code Reviews

 

Critical technical expertise:

ATM Technologies

  • ATM transaction lifecycle
  • ATM application architecture
  • Device integrations
  • ATM deployment and estate management
  • Production support and device availability
  • Experience with established ATM manufacturer platforms; NCR SelfServ and Diebold Nixdorf are preferred, while experience with GRG, Hyosung or other enterprise ATM estates will also be considered.

 

Terminal Applications & Device Platforms:

  • NCR AANDC / NDC / NDCe
  • Diebold Agilis / VCP
  • Self-service banking platforms
  • ATM, cash recycler, cash deposit and assisted-service solutions
  • Other vendor terminal application platforms

Postilion (Essential):

Experience with:

  • Postilion ATM Switching
  • Terminal Drivers
  • ATM Application Integration
  • ISO8583 Messaging
  • Routing and Transaction Flows
  • Transaction Debugging
  • Postilion Configuration

 

Experience with Postilion ATM App and/or Postilion SDK Development will be highly advantageous.

 

Delivery & Planning Leadership

  • Translation of the Product Head’s strategic roadmap into executable delivery plans, milestones, release forecasts and team-level priorities
  • Practical application of agile methodologies, including Scrum, Kanban and scaled agile practices where appropriate
  • Release planning, forecasting and delivery tracking
  • Working from the Product Head’s prioritised backlog and partnering with the team to decompose business features into delivery-ready team epics and progressively refined user stories
  • Dependency, risk and issue management
  • Capacity and resource planning
  • Vendor and cross-functional delivery coordination
  • Stakeholder management and executive reporting

Advantageous experience:

  • NCR NDCe development and Builder experience
  • Multi-vendor ATM environments (NCR, Diebold, GRG, Hyosung)
  • Digital and omnichannel banking platforms
  • API and event-driven architecture
  • Kafka and event streaming
  • Cash recycler and deposit automation solutions
  • Azure, Kubernetes, DevSecOps and modern cloud engineering practices

Qualifications (Minimum)

  • Bachelor's Degree in Commerce or Information Technology - Computer Science

Qualifications (Ideal or Preferred)

  • Honours Degree in Commerce or Information Technology - Computer Science

What's in it for you?

  • Lead technology that supports one of the bank's most important customer channels.
  • Influence strategic technology decisions and long-term platform direction.
  • Work with complex, large-scale, business-critical systems.
  • Lead highly skilled technical delivery teams and strengthen capability across the cash-devices environment.
  • Drive modernisation initiatives across ATM, digital and engineering platforms.
  • Gain exposure to leading ATM technologies, switching platforms and vendor ecosystems.
  • Join a culture that values innovation, ownership, collaboration and continuous improvement.

Conditions of Employment

  • Clear criminal and credit record

Capitec is committed to diversity, applications to this position will strictly be considered in support of our employment equity goals.
