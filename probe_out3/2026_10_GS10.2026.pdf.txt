KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, TRAINING AND COMPETENCIES REQUIRED:
The incumbent will report to the Chief Executive Officer and will be responsible for managing and
monitoring the institution’s financial management and SCM functions to ensure sound financial
governance, effective budget control, accurate financial reporting and compliance with applicable
legislation, and departmental policies. The incumbent will support the Chief Executive Officer and
hospital management in promoting the effective, efficient, economical and transparent utilisation of
public resources to sustain quality patient care.

The ideal candidate must demonstrate the following knowledge, skills and competencies:

KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS:

     •   Coordinate and manage the implementation of sound financial management systems,
         procedures and internal controls to ensure compliance with applicable legislation, policies
         and financial management prescripts.
     •   Coordinate the preparation, motivation, consolidation and implementation of the institution’s
         MTEF budget in consultation with hospital management and relevant stakeholders.
     •   Monitor budget utilisation and expenditure against approved allocations, identify variances
         and recommend corrective measures to promote budget discipline and prevent
         unauthorised, irregular, fruitless and wasteful expenditure.
     •   Monitor institutional cash flow and expenditure commitments to support the timely
         settlement of obligations and prevent the accumulation of overdue payments.
     •   Prepare, analyse and consolidate accurate and timeous financial reports for submission to
         the Chief Executive Officer, hospital management and the relevant departmental structures.
