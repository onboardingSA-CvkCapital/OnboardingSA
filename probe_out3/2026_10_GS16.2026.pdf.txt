KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS:

   • Head the Department of Anaesthetics and Critical Care at Grey’s Hospital and manage its
     operational and strategic matters, including coordination of relevant services within Area 2.
   • Ensure the provision of safe, cost-effective and high-quality anaesthetic and critical care
     services, including appropriate clinical cover for elective and emergency procedures.
   • Oversee preoperative assessment, intraoperative anaesthetic care, recovery and postoperative
     support, in collaboration with surgical and other relevant clinical departments.
   • Provide operational oversight of critical care services within the department’s approved scope,
     including admission and discharge processes, bed utilisation, patient prioritisation and referral
     pathways.
   • Formulate and execute annual operational plans and medium- to long-term departmental
     strategies aligned with the Department of Health’s priorities.
   • Develop and implement strategies to meet APP and other performance targets, including
     theatre utilisation, cancellation rates, ICU bed occupancy, patient flow, clinical outcomes and
     waiting times, as applicable.
   • Manage staff performance, EPMDS, recruitment and retention, labour relations, disciplinary
     processes, conflict management and grievance handling.
   • Ensure appropriate staffing levels, skills and competencies of departmental personnel, including
     sessional practitioners where applicable.
   • Promote teaching and training of registrars, medical officers, interns, nursing personnel and
     undergraduate students, including postgraduate supervision and academic programmes.
   • Maintain liaison with the relevant departments and training institutions at the University of
     KwaZulu-Natal, CMSA, HPCSA and other academic partners, as applicable.
   • Ensure clinical governance through regular morbidity and mortality meetings, clinical audits,
     utilisation reviews, incident reviews and quality improvement activities.
   • Maintain and review the departmental risk register and implement measures to prevent and
     manage clinical, operational and medico-legal incidents.
   • Design, implement and evaluate quality assurance and improvement programmes to reduce
     patient safety incidents and improve service efficiency.
