KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, TRAINING AND COMPETENCIES REQUIRED: - Job Purpose: To develop,
facilitate, monitor and evaluate evidence-based strategies to i) reduce maternal morbidity and
mortality, including HIV-related and ii) vertical transmission prevention (VTP) of HIV, syphilis and
Hepatits B; and improve the quality of maternal and perinatal health through improving service
delivery, ensuring transfer of appropriate knowledge, skills and developing community
intervention strategies. Manage resource allocation and monitor implementation of maternal
health care and VTP policies within the province; comprehensive data collection, analysis and
strategic planning in the field of maternal health and VTP. The ideal candidate must possess the
following: Technical knowledge on maternal health policies and VTP. Knowledge of the current
health and public health services legislation. Financial management skills. Project management
skills. Understanding of epidemiology and statistics. Leadership skills, including good
communication, decision making and clinical skills. Possess sound knowledge of human
resources. Management skills, including budgeting, planning, implementation, and quality
assurance.

KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS:-                 Develop, review and ensure implementation of relevant
policies and guidelines: Performance standard. Update existing provincial maternal health and
VTP related guidelines and policies. Develop new guidelines and policies for the Province in
response to identified issues in maternal health and VTP field. Provide guidance to districts
regarding best practices for maternal health and VTP coordinators. Identify maternal healthand
VTP advocacy needs according to priorities. Program Management & Implementation:
Performance standard. Develop and implement strategies to reduce maternal and perinatal
health-related morbidity and mortality, including HIV-related in each key programme area
(preconception/ antenatal care; labour and childbirth, post-natal care) as per Saving Mothers
recommendations and consolidated HIV guidelines. Report on key activities conducted aligned to
these strategies. Facilitate engagement on programme performance at women’s health
management and provincial quarterly meetings. Offer assistance and technical support to all
districts to improve Maternal Health and VTP Services: Performance standard. Support the
district in devising district plans to address district-specific health issues. Provide technical
assistance to district and facility management team. Conduct outreach support visits (on site or
virtual to all districts) with priority given to districts with poorest performance. Facilitate training in
all key areas of women’s health programme. Offer assistance and technical support to all districts
to improve Maternal Health Services. Stakeholder Collaboration - Liaise with national and
provincial Departments of Health, funders (like UNICEF), partners, and local stakeholders to
ensure coordinated effort and program integration. Improve service delivery through capacity
building: performance standard: Facilitate training of midwives and doctors where applicable on
the new developments and protocols, as well as to maintain and improve skills and knowledge
(e.g. ESMOE, EMOTIVE); engage districts to conduct skills audits for relevant trainings; Organize
and provide orientation and training for healthcare providers and facility staff on VTP guidelines,
screening, and preventative therapies. Monitor and evaluate maternal health services:
performance standard. Review and update maternal health and VTP data elements. Quarterly
reports prepared with remedial actions. Correct data errors. Analysis of relevant indicator
performance with the team. Monitor quarterly the implementation of Saving mothers
recommendations. Undertake other managerial and administration duties; performance
standard. Coordinate provincial maternal mortality confidential enquiry. Coordinate Provinical
perinatal morbidity and mortality committee. EPMDS completed. Compile DORA. Participate in
provincial and district interviews as panel member.
