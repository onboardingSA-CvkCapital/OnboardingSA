DIRECTION TO CANDIDATES

1.    The following documents must be submitted:

           a)   Application for employment Form (Z83) only which is obtainable at any Government Department or website
                www.kznhealth.gov.za. Z83 Form should be completed and signed. Only new Z83 Form, effective from 01
                January 2021, will be accepted.

           b)   Detailed Curriculum Vitae

           c)   Applicants do not need to send copies of highest educational qualifications, ID document, Driver’s licence, other
                relevant documents, only shortlisted candidates will be required to submit certified copies of all supporting
                documents and must not be older than six (6) months

           d)   It is the applicant’s responsibility to have foreign qualifications evaluated by SAQA

           e)   The refence number and position applied for must be clearly indicated in the column provided on the Z83 form,
                e.g., KCPL/03/2026

          NB: Failure to comply with the above requirement will result in disqualification.

2.    The appointments are subject to positive outcomes from the State Security Agency (SSA) to following checks
          a) Security clearance,
          b) Vetting, criminal clearance, credit records, citizenship),
          c) Verification of Educational Qualifications by SAQA,
          d) Verification of previous experience from Employers and verification from the Company Intellectual
             Property Commission (CIPC)

3.    Please note due to the anticipated high number of applications we envisage to receive; applicants will not be
      acknowledged. Correspondence will be limited to shortlisted candidates. If you are not contacted within two (2) months
      after closing date, please consider your application unsuccessful.

4.    Please note due to financial constraints no subsistence and travel (S&T) payment will be made to candidates attending
      interviews.
