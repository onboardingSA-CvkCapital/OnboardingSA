MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS FOR THE POST:
   • Senior Certificate / Grade 12 or equivalent
   • An appropriate tertiary qualification (MBCHB) plus
   • Registration certificate with HPCSA as a Medical Practitioner.
   • Current registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA)
     as a Medical Practitioner after completion of community Services 2025/2026

Persons in possession of a foreign qualification must attach an evaluation
certificate from the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA).

RECOMMENDATION:
   •   At least 6 months’ experience in Anaesthetics would be a recommendation.
   •   The Diploma in Anaesthesia (DA) from the College of Medicine of South Africa
       (CMSA) or progress toward primary exams (FCA PART 1) will be an added
       advantage.
   •   Other valid additional certificates such as BLS, ACLS, and ATLS will be an
       added advantage.

KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, TRAINING AND COMPETENCIES REQUIRED:
   •   Good knowledge of Anaesthetic procedures and latest protocols.
   •   Knowledge and appropriate use of anaesthetic equipment
   •   Experience and ability to deal with a wide variety of Anaesthetic cases
   •   Ability to work in a multi-disciplinary team setting
   •   Good interpersonal and supervisory skills
   •   Provide training and guidance to members of the multi-disciplinary team
   •   Ability to diagnose and manage common anaesthetic problems including
       emergencies
   •   Sound moral values based on integrity, trust and judgement.
   •   Sound knowledge of the relevant legislative mandates, policies and prescripts
       governing the medical profession and public health in South Africa.

KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS:
   •   Provide safe, high quality clinical care for patients during pre-operative
       assessments, intra-operative regional and general anaesthesia administration and
       post- operative care including pain management.
   •   Manage emergency resuscitations, urgent medical stabilisation and intensive care
       needs.
   •   Ability to identify the need for and refer appropriately
   •   Work with other clinical care units
   •   Ability to manage patients in a holistic manner
   •   Give medical judicial evidence
   •   Maintain medical records
   •   Provide training to the members of the multidisciplinary team in theatre
