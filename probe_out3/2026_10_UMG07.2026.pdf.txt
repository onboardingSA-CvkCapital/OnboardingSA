            (Z83) which is obtainable from any Government Department OR from the website -

            Vitae. Applicants are not required to submit copies of qualifications and other relevant
            documents on the application. Such documents will be requested from shortlisted
            candidates only. No faxed applications will be considered. Applications received after the
            closing date and those that do not comply with the requirements will not be considered.

           employment verification).
