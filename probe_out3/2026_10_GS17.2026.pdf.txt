    •    Proof of current registration with the HPCSA as a Medical Specialist in Critical Care or

    •    Possess sound knowledge of human resource management, budgeting, the implementation
         and evaluation of quality assurance programmes, and information management.
    •    Possess knowledge of current health and Public Service legislation, regulations and

    •    Have sound up-to-date knowledge of clinical procedures, protocols and international

    KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS

•   Successful incumbent would be required to:
•   Manage Grey’s Hospital’s adult multidisciplinary tertiary Intensive Care Unit (ICU) as a
    component of the overall Metropolitan Critical Care system within the Pietermaritzburg
    Department of Anaesthesia, Critical Care and Pain Management.
•   Report to the Head of the Clinical Department of Anaesthesia and Critical Care at Grey’s
    Hospital and work jointly to ensure that Critical Care patient services are maintained
    efficiently and to an acceptable standard.
•   Develop and refine Critical Care admission and discharge policies appropriate for Grey’s
    Hospital, in line with the overall Metropolitan Critical Care policies, to ensure the fair and
    ethical distribution of ICU resources.
•   Actively participate in and support the progressive development of training programmes
    across the Metropolitan Critical Care system. These programmes should facilitate
    postgraduate Critical Care training for Medical Officers, Registrars specialising in other
    clinical disciplines, and Specialists undertaking Certification in Critical Care, while
    maximising the contribution of Grey’s ICU.
•   Accept overall responsibility for the Critical Care management of critically ill patients
    admitted to the Critical Care Unit of Grey’s Hospital
•   Lead the clinical Critical Care service of a tertiary hospital ICU that functions as part of the
    Metropolitan Critical Care service by:
         o Personally providing Subspecialist-level consultant cover and clinical leadership for
             Grey’s Hospital Multidisciplinary ICU.
         o Supervising and directing the clinical work of Specialists undertaking Certification in
             Critical Care
         o Personally providing (on a rostered duty basis) after-hours Subspecialist consultant
             cover for the ICU at Grey’s Hospital as well as for other ICUs in the Metropolitan
             Critical Care system.
         o Assessing pre- and post-admission high-risk patients in the wards, and providing
             clinical guidance to clinicians referring patients to the ICU at Grey’s Hospital.
         o Liaising and working with the Critical Care services offered by the Department of
             Paediatrics at Grey’s Hospital on matters relevant to both adult and paediatric
             critical care.
         o Participating in outreach programmes for the development of Critical Care services
             within the catchment area of Grey’s Hospital
