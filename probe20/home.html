    Getting to know OUTsurance

    How much does OUTsurance cost per month?

    Real OUTsurance clients pay from R578 a month for car insurance, R115 for life cover and R65 for pet insurance. Your premium depends on things like your vehicle or property, where you live, your claims history and the cover you choose. 

    View the advertising campaign terms and conditions for full premium details.

    Can I switch to OUTsurance from another insurer?

    Yes. Get a quote, choose a start date, and only cancel your old policy once your OUTsurance cover is active, so you’re never uninsured.

    What is OUTsurance’s contact number, and how can I reach you?

    Call 08 600 60 000. You can also use the app, log in online, or use the contact page.

    What is an OUT ID?

    Your OUT ID is your single login for managing OUTsurance policies, claims and documents in the app and online.
