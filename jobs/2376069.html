About the role

Our client is seeking an experienced Data Analyst / Business Intelligence Developer to join their team on a 3-month contract in Johannesburg. This opportunity is ideal for a data professional with strong Business Intelligence expertise who enjoys developing scalable reporting solutions, optimising data environments, and transforming complex data into actionable business insights. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop and maintain Business Intelligence solutions and reporting platforms. • Develop dashboards, reports and analytical models to support business decision-making. • Design and optimise ETL processes and data pipelines. • Develop and maintain robust data models and data warehouse solutions. • Gather business requirements and translate them into technical solutions. • Ensure data integrity, consistency and quality across reporting environments. • Optimise BI platforms for performance, scalability and usability. • Deploy, maintain and support Business Intelligence solutions throughout their lifecycle. • Collaborate with IT and business stakeholders to deliver high-quality data solutions. Requirements • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics or a related field. • Proven experience working as a BI Developer or in a similar technical role. • Demonstrated experience delivering end-to-end Business Intelligence solutions. • Strong experience with Microsoft Power BI, Qlik Sense and Microsoft Fabric. • Solid SQL and Python development skills. • Experience with Spark, ETL development, data modelling and data pipelines. • Knowledge of Synapse Data Engineering and Synapse Data Warehouse. • Strong understanding of database management and data warehousing concepts. • Experience gathering business requirements and supporting solution deployment. • Microsoft Certified: Data Analyst Associate and other advanced certifications will be advantageous.
