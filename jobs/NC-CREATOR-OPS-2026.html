About the role

NinjaChat is a fast-growing consumer AI company used by people around the world. We are building a single platform through which users can access leading AI models, create images and videos, conduct research and complete everyday tasks. We are looking for a highly organised and execution-focused Creator Operations Manager to build and operate our creator marketing engine. This is not a traditional social media management position. You will manage the systems, people and daily processes required to run UGC and influencer campaigns at scale across TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. You will work directly with the founder and have significant ownership over how NinjaChat recruits creators, launches campaigns, evaluates content and rewards performance. What you will do • Find and recruit UGC creators and influencers across multiple platforms. • Conduct creator outreach through email, direct messages and creator platforms. • Evaluate creators based on content quality, audience demographics and past performance. • Onboard creators and clearly communicate campaign requirements. • Prepare and distribute briefs, hooks, examples and publishing instructions. • Manage content deadlines and follow up consistently with creators. • Review submitted videos for quality, accuracy and brand alignment. • Maintain accurate trackers for outreach, submissions, publishing and payments. • Monitor views, engagement, registrations, installs and other campaign results. • Calculate creator payments and performance bonuses. • Identify suspicious traffic, fraudulent views and underperforming campaigns. • Communicate feedback to creators and help improve future content. • Identify successful formats and turn them into repeatable campaigns. • Coordinate with the growth, product and design teams when campaigns require new assets. • Produce clear weekly reports covering output, costs, performance and next steps. • Improve internal systems so NinjaChat can manage hundreds of creator relationships efficiently. Success in this position will be measured by the number of quality creators recruited, content published on schedule, campaign efficiency and users acquired—not by presentations or vanity metrics.
