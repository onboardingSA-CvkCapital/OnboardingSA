About the role

Position Summary The Steel Sales & Business Development Manager will play a key role in expanding company's steel business throughout Southern Africa. Will be responsible for new business development, customer relationship management, market intelligence gathering, commercial negotiations, and sales execution across a diverse steel product portfolio. This role requires a commercially minded professional who can quickly identify opportunities, make informed decisions, and turn ideas into tangible business results. The successful candidate must be highly proactive, responsive, and comfortable operating in a fast-paced international trading environment while collaborating closely with company headquarters in Korea. Key Responsibilities Business Development & Sales Identify, develop, and secure new business opportunities across Southern Africa. Build and maintain long-term relationships with distributors, fabricators, manufacturers, service centres, contractors, and end-users. Develop strategic sales plans to achieve volume, revenue, and profitability targets. Conduct regular customer visits, business presentations, and commercial meetings. Expand company International's customer base and market presence across priority industries. Actively manage the sales pipeline and convert opportunities into sustainable business Steel Product Sales & Trading Manage and promote sales activities for a broad range of steel products, including: Hot Rolled Coil (HRC) Cold Rolled Coil (CRC) Steel Plate Wire Rod Beam Long Products Coated Steel Products Stainless Steel Products Automotive Steel Products Responsibilities include: Preparing quotations and commercial proposals. Conducting pricing negotiations. Managing contract execution. Monitoring sales forecasts and product demand. Driving profitable growth across product categories Customer Relationship & Account Management Maintain strong relationships with existing customers while continuously developing new accounts. Understand customer needs and provide tailored commercial solutions. Respond promptly to customer inquiries and business opportunities. Lead commercial discussions regarding pricing, contracts, payment terms, and supply agreements. Ensure a high level of customer satisfaction and retention. Resolve commercial and operational issues efficiently and professionally. Market Intelligence & Business Strategy Monitor market trends, customer demand, steel pricing movements, and competitor activities. Analyze market opportunities and identify new growth areas. Track developments relating to safeguard duties, anti-dumping regulations, tariffs, and import policies. Gather and communicate market intelligence to management and headquarters. Support strategic decision-making through detailed market analysis and forecasting. Identify commercial risks and recommend mitigation strategies Coordination with Headquarters (Korea) Collaborate closely with company headquarters in Korea on sales strategy, pricing, business planning, and market development initiatives. Coordinate with product managers and trading teams regarding customer requirements, forecasts, and commercial opportunities. Prepare and submit regular sales reports, market intelligence updates, and business forecasts. Act as a key communication bridge between customers, suppliers, logistics partners, and headquarters. Participate in regional and global business review meetings. Support the execution of strategic initiatives driven by headquarters Supply Chain & Logistics Coordination Coordinate shipment schedules and vessel bookings. Liaise with suppliers, freight forwarders, and logistics providers. Monitor delivery performance and ensure smooth execution of customer orders. Support demand planning and supply chain optimization. Ensure timely and efficient delivery of products to customers. Reporting & Administration Prepare monthly sales reports, forecasts, budgets, and business plans. Maintain accurate CRM and customer databases. Monitor performance against sales targets and KPIs. Provide timely and accurate business updates to management and headquarters. Ensure compliance with company policies and reporting requirement
