About the role

Pay: $10 per hour Job Description: Payroll Support Administrator Working hours: 24 per week Wednesday - Friday (UK time) The Role We are looking for a part time Payroll Advisor / Support Administrator to join our busy payroll department. This role is central to supporting contractors with pay-related queries while assisting with payroll administration and processing. Reporting to the Payroll Manager, you will support contractors via phone and email, manage payroll-related administration, and work closely with recruitment agencies. This role would suit someone with previous UK payroll experience and can deliver high-quality service in a fast-paced environment. Key Responsibilities • Act as the first point of contact for contractor pay queries via phone and email • Confidently explain UK payslips, including income, PAYE, NIC, pension, CSA and attachment of earnings • Resolve pay-related queries by liaising with contractors and agencies • Process and support contractor payroll accurately and in line with legislation • Good Knowledge of Statutory Payments • Check and input contractor mileage and expenses in line with HMRC rules • Support auto-enrolment pension administration and pension related queries • CSA, attachment of earnings, etc • Complete general payroll and administrative tasks as required Ideal Candidate • Previous payroll experience is essential (umbrella or contractor payroll highly desirable) • Confident explaining payslips and statutory deductions • Excellent telephone and written communication skills • Comfortable handling and resolving challenging queries professionally • Highly organised with strong attention to detail • Proactive, reliable, and a strong team player • Thrives in a fast-paced, evolving environment with a service-focused mindset Experience: • UK payroll experience: minimum 1–2 years preferred • Umbrella / contractor payroll experience highly advantageous
