About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring an Assistant Director: Property Acquisition and Lease Management, based in Kimberly Regional Office. This is a permanent post, paying R605,742 a year. How to apply: Email Applications for the position of Assistant Director: Property Acquisition and Lease Management must be submitted to: RecruitKIM26-25@dpw.gov.za Reference number to quote: 2026/390 Closing date: 27 November 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Facilitate the property acquisition process in line with the approved acquisition framework: Conduct client needs assessment and establishes availability of funds for client departments.
  • Make findings regarding offices and procured accommodation.
  • Inspect and select suitable accommodation according to the requirements of client departments.
  • Negotiate terms and condition of contracts and leases.
  • Provide inputs and Implement Supply Chain Management Procurement Plan.
  • Report regularly on procured properties.
  • Facilitate the lease administration process for private and leasehold portfolios: Clarify terms and conditions of leases and expedite a lease agreement to create lease obligations between the tenant and the landlord so as to minimise contractual disputes at any stage of the contract.
  • Conduct property inspections prior occupation by the tenant and prior termination of the lease to determine the condition of the property.
  • Facilitate the provision of effective maintenance to improve the condition and preserve the value of the leased property.
  • Conduct regular property ownership verifications.
  • Provide support with the termination and renewal of leased accommodation.
  • Attend and resolve all requests and queries received from clients.
  • Conduct regular monitoring of the Archeus and the database on the leased properties: Verify if there is a valid lease agreement in place for the premises.
  • Ensure reconciliation of Archeus with lease agreement including the rental amount and descriptions.
  • Verify any changes on the landlord or client details and update accordingly.
  • Verify the current rental paid against what stipulated on the lease agreement.
  • Confirm the physical occupation of the premises and approximate square meters occupied.
  • Followup on leases approaching expiry date.
  • Ensure regular updating of the database.
  • Supervise employees and administration processes to ensure an effective service delivery: Render general supervision of employees.
  • Allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by supervisees.
  • Advice and lead supervisees regarding all aspects of the work.
  • Manage performance and conduct discipline supervisees.
  • Ensure that all supervisees are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively.
  • Compile relevant reports as required.
  • Support audit processes through provision of accurate and complete information.
  • Ensure effective management of records.
