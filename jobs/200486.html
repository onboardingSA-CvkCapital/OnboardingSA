About the role

Purpose of the role: We are looking for a skilled and reliable Handyman to join our dynamic team. This role requires a technically astute individual with proven experience working within a PCI (high-security) environment. The successful candidate will be responsible for the day-to-day maintenance, repairs and upkeep of buildings, facilities, equipment and associated systems. The role requires strong practical knowledge across electrical, plumbing and/or mechanical systems, supported by general handyman skills. Minimum Educational and experience required • Minimum 2–3 years' relevant post-qualification or practical maintenance experience. • Strong practical knowledge of maintenance, repairs and fault-finding. • General handyman skills, including carpentry, painting, tiling and minor building repairs. • Valid driver's licence. • Red Seal or equivalent recognised trade qualification in Electrical, Plumbing or Mechanical (Advantageous) • Proven experience in property, residential estate, facilities or building maintenance. • Experience working in a PCI, security-sensitive or controlled-access environment will be highly advantageous. • Based in or around Umhlanga, or willing to relocate. • Good communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to interact professionally with residents and site personnel. • Good organisational and time-management skills. • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision and as part of a team. • Physically fit and able to perform manual work, including lifting, climbing and working in confined or restricted areas. • Knowledge and understanding of occupational health and safety requirements.Key Areas of Responsibility: • Carry out planned, preventative and reactive maintenance across electrical, plumbing and mechanical systems. • Perform general handyman repairs, including carpentry, painting, tiling, minor building works and fixture/fitting repairs. • Diagnose faults and troubleshoot maintenance and equipment-related issues. • Conduct routine inspections to identify maintenance requirements, potential faults and safety risks. • Attend to maintenance requests and work orders within agreed timeframes. • Respond to maintenance callouts and emergencies, including after-hours call-outs where required. • Install, repair, assemble and maintain fixtures, fittings, equipment and building components. • Support preventative maintenance programmes and ensure maintenance tasks are completed to the required standard. • Maintain basic and accurate records of work completed, faults identified, repairs undertaken and materials used. • Liaise directly and professionally with residents, site staff, supervisors, contractors and service providers. • Ensure all work is carried out safely and in accordance with applicable health and safety requirements, site procedures and organisational standards. • Maintain a clean, safe and organised work environment. • Work with minimal supervision while ensuring all tasks are completed efficiently, professionally and to a high standard. • Operate and maintain tools and equipment safely and responsibly.Key Competencies: • Technical Trade Competence • Multi-Skilled Maintenance • Problem-Solving & Fault-Finding • Quality Workmanship • Reliability & Accountability • Health & Safety Awareness • Customer & Stakeholder Service
