About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AIpowered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined, future-fit role. We are looking for passionate, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace datadriven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job Understanding that this unit is one of the critical proof points/ “moments of truth” of the SAICA brand promise and brand experience strategy, the Customer Experience Support Team plays a pivotal role in translating the brand experience strategy into lived member experiences. Thus, this role serves a critical proof point and conduit between SAICA’s customers and it’s brand promise. The SAICA brand promise and experience can be made or broken at this touchpoint. Therefore, by deploying customer-experience obsession and brand love, the Customer Experience Support Agent commits to delivering memorable, exceptional customer experience that is seamlessly aligned to the SAICA CeX strategy, all the time! This entails intentionally educating yourself and assimilating SAICA’s CeX and brand strategies – cascaded into CeX playbooks – and using these to guide every customer interaction. As a first line recipient of queries, responsible for first line resolution, this role is responsible to ensure that the SAICA Contact Centre delivers exceptional member experiences by aligning processes, technology, and people strategies with SAICA's overarching goals and brand experience strategy. By fostering operational excellence, building and maintaining strong core relationships with all business units to ensure that the customer experience is unrivalled, the Member Experience Support Agent actively contributes to advancing SAICA's reputation for high-quality member services and supports the strategic objectives of the CXO function. Member Experience Support Agents require an in-depth working knowledge of all business units, processes and business cycles to efficiently and effectively resolve queries. Overall query resolution and monitoring forms part of the responsibility of every team member within the Member Experience Team This role focuses on enhancing member experiences and operational efficiency within the Member Support function, ensuring the highest level of service in the fastest possible time, with the least amount of inconvenience to all customers, thereby contributing to SAICA's overall strategic objectives, and fostering a culture of customer centricity. System expertise, customer care, and forward thinking remain key competencies of this role.
