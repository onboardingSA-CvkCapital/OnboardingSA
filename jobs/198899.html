About the role

Faircape Life proudly operates six luxurious retirement and lifestyle villages across the Western Cape, including Noordhoek, Kirstenhof, Tokai, Somerset West, and Onrus. Our estates are celebrated for their meticulously maintained gardens and welcoming environments. We are seeking a dedicated Groundsman to join our team at Bridgewater Manor, Somerset West. You will be responsible for maintaining lush gardens, vibrant flowerbeds, healthy trees, and immaculate lawns, ensuring the estate remains beautiful, well-kept and welcoming for our residents. If you love working outdoors, take pride in creating beautiful landscapes, and enjoy contributing to a serene, high-end environment, this is the role for you! Why Join Us?
