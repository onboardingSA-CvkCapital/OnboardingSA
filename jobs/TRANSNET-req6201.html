About the role

Transnet (Transnet Rail Infrastructure Manager (TRIM)) is hiring a Trackmaster - Piketberg, Caledon & Beaufort West, based in Various Locations. The role reports to the Production Manager. Grade: I2X. Ensure safe and affordable railway operation by executing activities and directing resources related to track maintenance manual.
