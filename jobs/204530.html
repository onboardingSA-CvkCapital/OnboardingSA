About the role

A leading water treatment and environmental solutions company is looking for an experienced SHEQ Manager with strong experience in integrated health and safety, environmental and quality management systems to join their team in Muizenberg, Cape Town. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining and improving the organisation’s SHEQ systems, ensuring regulatory compliance, managing ISO standards and certification audits, and driving a strong culture of health, safety, environmental protection and quality excellence. Responsibilities: • Maintain, improve and enhance the Occupational Health & Safety Management System in line with ISO 45001. • Conduct hazard identification, risk assessments and risk mitigation across operational areas. • Develop and enforce health and safety policies, procedures and work instructions. • Manage incident investigations and root cause analysis for accidents, near-misses and hazardous situations. • Conduct regular inspections, audits and safety walkthroughs. • Develop and deliver health and safety training programmes. • Maintain health and safety documentation, records and reports. • Coordinate with external health and safety authorities and manage regulatory compliance. • Implement corrective and preventive actions to address safety deficiencies. • Promote a strong safety culture through awareness campaigns and employee engagement. • Manage the procurement, issuing and maintenance of PPE for head office and operational sites. • Establish and maintain the Environmental Management System in compliance with ISO 14001. • Identify and assess environmental aspects and impacts of organisational operations. • Set environmental objectives and targets aligned with organisational strategy. • Ensure compliance with relevant environmental legislation and regulations. • Manage waste disposal, emissions monitoring and resource conservation programmes. • Develop and implement environmental emergency preparedness and response procedures. • Monitor environmental performance metrics and KPIs. • Conduct environmental audits and management reviews. • Maintain the Quality Management System in line with ISO 9001. • Manage quality planning, quality assurance and quality control processes. • Oversee internal QMS audits and ensure compliance with applicable standards. • Manage customer feedback mechanisms and quality complaints. • Manage supplier quality and vendor assessment procedures. • Establish and monitor quality performance metrics and KPIs. • Drive continuous improvement initiatives across organisational processes. • Maintain integrated SHEQ policies and management system documentation. • Ensure alignment and consistency across the ISO 45001, ISO 14001 and ISO 9001 management systems. • Coordinate internal audits across SHEQ functions. • Prepare for and manage external certification audits. • Maintain certification status across the applicable ISO standards. • Report on integrated SHEQ performance to senior management. • Identify and assess SHEQ training needs across the organisation. • Maintain training records and competency assessments. • Conduct induction training for new employees and ensure management understands their SHEQ responsibilities. • Monitor regulatory changes and assess their impact on the organisation. • Maintain regulatory compliance calendars and requirements tracking. • Lead and develop SHEQ staff and promote SHEQ awareness throughout the organisation. • Engage with internal and external
