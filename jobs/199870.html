About the role

🚀 We're Hiring: Procurement Specialist: Hardware and Equipment 📍 Location: Gauteng 📊 Reporting Line: Manager: Procurement and Supplier Relationship Management 💼 Job Purpose Lesaka Merchant is looking for a Procurement Specialist: Hardware and Equipment to intelligently procure fintech hardware and equipment through specialised procurement, technology, and vendor management expertise. This role is responsible for ensuring that hardware and equipment procurement supports operational requirements, infrastructure networking, lifecycle management, and security standards while remaining cost-effective, competitive, scalable, and compliant. The successful candidate will play a key role in mitigating procurement risks and ensuring the reliable delivery of hardware solutions that support merchant operations and business growth. 🔍 Key Responsibility Areas Procurement Catalogue, Capacity Planning & Technician Delivery Schedules Enabled • Develop a deep understanding of the Merchant Operations Division's procurement catalogue and supplier categories. • Analyse capacity plans, technician deployment schedules, historical purchases, and business demand to create structured procurement plans. • Align procurement activities to budgets, cash flow requirements, and operational needs. • Promote BBBEE and enterprise development initiatives while effectively managing risk. • Support strategic sourcing plans that ensure continuity of supply and operational readiness.Strategic Sourcing, Negotiation & Cost Management • Manage strategic sourcing activities for hardware and equipment categories. • Define supplier evaluation criteria, considering quality, scalability, API integration, and performance requirements. • Analyse procurement data to identify opportunities for consolidation and cost optimisation. • Negotiate favourable commercial agreements and drive supplier performance improvements. • Develop short and long-term procurement forecasts with Product, Sales, and Field Services teams. • Support supplier onboarding and education on Lesaka procurement processes and governance requirements. • Drive supplier segmentation and strategic supplier management initiatives.Procurement & Cost Control Enabled • Analyse spending patterns to identify maverick spend, cost-saving opportunities, and volume discount options. • Negotiate favourable pricing structures and payment terms. • Digitise and optimise procurement workflows, approvals, and purchase order generation. • Implement automated approval processes aligned to procurement governance standards. • Ensure vendor contracts comply with GDPR, PCI-DSS, and data security requirements. • Maintain complete and audit-ready Procure-to-Pay records. • Ensure procurement activities support merchant SLAs and customer experience objectives. • Proactively monitor technology and supply market developments to support business continuity.
