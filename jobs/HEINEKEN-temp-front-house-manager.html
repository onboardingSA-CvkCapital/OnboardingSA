About the role

Heineken Beverages is hiring a Temp Front of House Manager, based in Paarl. To lead and manage the Front of House team to deliver a consistent, high-quality guest experience, ensuring smooth day-to-day operations, effective team coordination, and strong service standards. This role combines operational leadership with hands-on execution, acting as the link between guest experience, team performance, and operational efficiency.

Responsibilities

  • Guest Experience & Operations Management
  • Lead the delivery of exceptional guest experiences across all touchpoints
  • Ensure daily operations run smoothly, including:
  • Guest flow management
  • Reservations and bookings
  • Service standards and presentation
  • Resolve guest queries and issues promptly and professionally
  • Monitor and improve service delivery through feedback and observation
  • Team Leadership & Capability Building
  • Manage, schedule, and supervise FOH staff
  • Provide coaching, training, and ongoing performance feedback
  • Ensure team adherence to service standards, SOPs, and brand guidelines
  • Support recruitment and onboarding of new team members
  • Coordination Across Departments:
  • Act as key liaison between FOH, Kitchen, Banqueting, and Operations
  • Ensure clear communication of daily requirements, bookings, and events
  • Coordinate staffing and resource allocation based on demand
  • Administrative & Reporting Responsibilities
  • Maintain accurate records of:
  • Attendance and rosters
  • Daily reports and service logs
  • Monitor operational KPIs and flag any risks or issues
  • Support reporting on guest experience metrics and performance trends
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Work closely with:
  • Operations, Banqueting, Kitchen, Marketing
  • Engage directly with guests to ensure satisfaction
  • Key Success Measures
  • Guest satisfaction scores and feedback
  • Smooth daily operations and service delivery
  • Team engagement and performance
  • Adherence to service standards
  • Achievement of all AP targets

Requirements

  • Experience & Capability Requirements:
  • 5+ years’ experience in hospitality or guest experience management
  • Strong leadership and people management skills
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving ability
  • Ability to operate in a fast-paced, guest-facing environment
  • HEINEKEN Behavioural Alignment
  • Deliver: Consistent excellence in service delivery
  • Shape: Champions guest-first thinking
  • Connect: Builds strong, motivated teams
  • Develop: Grows team capability and self
