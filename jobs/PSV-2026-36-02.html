About the role

The Department of Agriculture is hiring a Land Administrative Officer in Small-Holder Farmers Production, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to 20 Steve Biko Street, Agriculture Place, Arcadia, Pretoria, 0002 or by email LAOrecruit91@nda.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/3/1/91/2026 Closing date: 23 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Target: Whites, Coloured Females and persons with disability.

Responsibilities

  • Facilitate the disposal of Agricultural State Land:
  • Identify the repossessed farm by Debt Management
  • Appoint the caretaker to look after the farm
  • Facilitate the Evaluation of farm to determine the productive market value
  • Advertise the farm through local media
  • Facilitate the appointment of the successful candidate
  • Prepare the lease agreement
  • Provide Infrastructure support:
  • Ensure the proper maintenance and caretaking of farms
  • Conduct inspection on farms to determine the condition
  • Conduct needs analysis to check the gaps and what is needed
  • Eradication of poisonous plants
  • Construction of gabions and fire breaks
  • Rehabilitation of dams
  • Render advisory services to intergovernmental departments and clients on land management matters: Attend to state land queries from inter-governmental departments and clients.
  • Attend to parliamentary queries through the ministerial office.
  • Provide farming advise to leases/clients.
  • Facilitate the rental collection and other departmental levies: Send monthly statements to leases.
  • Make follow up on none paying leases.
  • Liaise with finance with regard to verification of payments.
  • Facilitate the eviction of none complying leases.
  • Refer none paying leases to legal services.
  • Maintenance of database:
  • Update provincial and state land database
  • Update asset management database
  • Update the new developments in the farm
  • Update job creation and applicant database

Requirements

  • A National Diploma in Agriculture (NQF Level 6).
  • At least 2 years’ relevant experience in Land or Land Reform Administration.
  • Job Related Knowledge:
  • Computer literacy
  • Analytical skills
  • PFMA
  • Planning and organizing
  • Project Management
  • Job Related Skills:
  • Facilitation skills
  • Planning
  • Initiative
  • Interpersonal skills
  • Problem solving abilities
  • Interpretation of findings
  • Analytical
  • Report writing skills
  • Drivers’ license
  • Extended working hours
