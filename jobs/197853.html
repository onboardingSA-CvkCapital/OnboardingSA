About the role

Purpose of the Role: The Recruitment Screening & Support Coordinator provides structured screening, coordination and administrative support to the iStore recruitment team. The role supports two Recruiters by managing high-volume first-level candidate screening, assessments, candidate communication, interview coordination, recruitment administration and accurate system updates. The role is expected to apply sound judgement rather than only process administration: identifying candidates who meet the core requirements, recognising gaps or risks, escalating exceptions and ensuring suitable candidates move through the recruitment process efficiently and professionally. Key Responsibilities: • Screen CVs and applications against approved vacancy criteria, including experience, qualifications, location, availability and role-specific requirements. • Conduct initial telephonic, video or structured screening where required and capture clear screening notes. • Provide day-to-day recruitment administration support to two iStore Recruiters across their vacancy portfolios. • Use approved screening questions and criteria consistently across candidates and vacancies. • Identify suitable candidates for Recruiter review and clearly motivate why the candidate meets the core requirements. • Identify and flag concerns such as unexplained gaps, job-hopping, salary misalignment, location constraints, qualification gaps or other material risks. • Search and filter candidate databases or recruitment platforms when additional candidate sourcing support is required. • Support high-volume retail campaigns by processing applications at a pace without compromising screening quality. • Escalate borderline or unusual profiles rather than rejecting potentially suitable candidates without Recruiter input. • Assist Recruiters with ad hoc recruitment-related administration required to keep vacancies moving. • Maintain vacancy trackers, candidate lists, interview schedules and recruitment documentation. • Schedule interviews between candidates, Recruiters and Hiring Managers and manage changes when required. • Maintain warm, professional communication that reflects the iStore and Core Group employer brand. • Monitor candidates moving through screening, assessment, interview and pre-employment stages. • Support recruitment projects, campaigns and peak recruitment periods across iStore. • Develop knowledge of iStore roles, store structures and recruitment standards to improve support quality over time. • Identify recurring administration delays, candidate drop-off points or system issues and raise them with the recruitment team. Minimum Requirements: • Matric or equivalent qualification. • Prior experience in an administrative or recruitment support role will be an advantage. • Proficiency in MS Office Suite (Word, Excel, Outlook). Competencies and Skills: • Excellent organisational and time management skills. • Strong verbal and written communication abilities. • High level of attention to detail and accuracy. • Ability to work well under pressure and manage multiple priorities. • A customer-focused approach to delivering a positive candidate experience. What We Offer: • Exposure to a fast-paced, innovative environment. • Opportunities for growth within Core Group. • A collaborative and supportive team culture.
