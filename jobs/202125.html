About the role

Job purpose The Support Lead is responsible for leading the IT Support function and ensuring consistent delivery of efficient, customer-centric and ITIL-aligned support services. The Support Lead will lead and develop the support team, monitor service performance, manage escalations, identify opportunities for improvement and drive the adoption of self-service, automation and AI-enabled support capabilities. The role is accountable for people leadership, service performance, operational effectiveness, customer experience, escalation management and continual improvement. The role is not primarily a hands-on technical support position. While the Support Lead is expected to maintain sufficient technical knowledge to provide guidance and manage complex escalations, day-to-day technical resolution remains primarily the responsibility of the Support Team. Key responsibilities PERFORMANCE LEADERSHIP & TEAM PERFORMANCE • Lead and manage the IT Support team, creating a high-performing, customer-focused support culture. • Set clear expectations regarding service quality, productivity, customer experience and professional conduct. • Provide coaching, mentoring and ongoing development to team members. • Conduct regular performance reviews and manage performance in accordance with organisational processes. • Identify skills gaps and implement appropriate development and training plans. • Support recruitment, onboarding and development of new team members. • Manage team capacity, workload allocation and resource planning to meet business demand. • Manage schedules and rosters to ensure appropriate service coverage, including after-hours requirements. • Develop a pipeline of capable support resources and identify opportunities for career progression within the team. • Promote collaboration, accountability and knowledge sharing across the support function. SERVICE DELIVERY & ITSM • Lead the day-to-day delivery of IT Support services in accordance with agreed SLAs, policies and ITIL-aligned processes. • Ensure incidents and service requests are appropriately logged, prioritised, assigned, monitored and resolved by the Support Team. • Monitor ticket queues, backlogs, ageing tickets and workload distribution. • Ensure appropriate escalation of tickets that cannot be resolved within the Support Team. • Ensure effective use of Jira Service Management and approved support channels. • Monitor service performance and identify trends, risks and opportunities for improvement. • Ensure appropriate adherence to Incident Management, Request Fulfilment, Escalation Management, Knowledge Management and Continual Improvement practices. • Work with direct and internal IT Management team to identify and implement improvements to service delivery processes. INCIDENT, ESCALATION & MAJOR INCIDENT MANAGEMENT • Act as the operational escalation point for complex, high-priority and business-critical support issues. • Provide technical guidance to the Support Team when incidents require additional expertise. • Coordinate escalations with Infrastructure, Applications, Security, vendors and other relevant technology teams. • Support the coordination and communication of major incidents affecting business operations. • Ensure appropriate stakeholder communication during significant service disruptions. • Monitor escalated incidents through resolution and ensure ownership is maintained. • Participate in post-incident reviews and ensure agreed corrective actions are followed through. • Identify recurring incidents and trends and collaborate with relevant teams to support root-cause analysis and problem management. SERVICE PERFORMANCE & REPORTING • Own and monitor IT Support performance against agreed KPIs and SLAs. • Analyse service desk data to identify trends, performance gaps and opportunities for improvement. • Provide regular performance reporting to the direct manager and relevant stakeholders. • Use data to coach the team and improve individual and overall team performance. • Monitor key indicators including: o SLA achievement o First Contact Resolution o Mean Time to Resolve o Ticket backlog o Ticket ageing o Reopened tickets CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE & STAKEHOLDER MANAGEMENT • Promote consistent, professional and customer-centric support experience across all support channels. • Monitor customer feedback and identify recurring service issues. • Ensure customer complaints and escalations are managed professionally and appropriately. • Ensure support communications are clear, timely and appropriate to the audience. • Identify opportunities to improve the end-user experience and reduce customer effort. AUTOMATION, AI & SELF-SERVICE • Identify opportunities to reduce repetitive and avoidable support demand through automation, self-service and AI. • Drive adoption of approved AI-enabled support capabilities, including AI chatbots, WhatsApp AI and other digital support channels. • Monitor the effectiveness and quality of AI-assisted support services. • Identify gaps in AI responses, workflows and knowledge content and coordinate improvements with relevant stakeholders. • Ensure appropriate human oversight of AI-enabled support processes. • Promote self-service and knowledge-based support to empower users to resolve appropriate issues independently. • Own the effectiveness of the IT Support knowledge management capability. • Ensure knowledge articles, FAQs, troubleshooting guides and support documentation are accurate and current. • Ensure knowledge is readily accessible to both support teams and end users. • Encourage the Support Team to contribute to and maintain the knowledge base. TECHNICAL SUPPORT & OPERATIONAL OVERSIGHT • Maintain sufficient technical knowledge to provide guidance and support escalation management. • Provide technical direction to Support Team members when issues exceed their current capability. • Review complex technical issues and ensure appropriate escalation where required. • Oversee the effective delivery of desktop, endpoint, application and connectivity support by the Support Team. • Support the planning and execution of technology upgrades and changes affecting end users. SECURITY, RISK & COMPLIANCE • Ensure Support Team activities comply with relevant information security policies, standards and procedures. • Ensure security-related incidents are appropriately identified and escalated. • Collaborate with SecOps and other relevant teams on security-related support matters. • Ensure appropriate controls are considered when implementing automation, AI and self-service solutions. • Promote security awareness and good technology practices within the Support Team and user community. • Ensure appropriate handling and escalation of security and data-related risks. CONTINUAL SERVICE IMPROVEMENT • Identify opportunities to improve service quality, efficiency and customer experience. • Use service data, customer feedback and operational trends to identify improvement opportunities. • Lead or participate in initiatives to improve IT Support processes and services. • Identify opportunities to eliminate repetitive work through automation and self-service. • Identify recurring incidents and support problems and work with relevant technology teams to reduce recurrence. • Measure the impact of improvement initiatives and communicate outcomes to stakeholders. • Promote a culture of continual improvement within the Support Team Experience and Qualifications • Minimum 5 years’ experience in IT Support, Service Desk, Technical Support, Customer Service or IT Service Management. • Proven experience leading or supervising a technical support team. • Working knowledge of IT Service Management principles and practices • Experience managing service performance against SLAs and KPIs. • Relevant IT, Business, Management or related diploma/degree, or equivalent practical experience. Knowledge, Skills & Abilities Required • People leadership • Coaching and mentoring • Customer focus • Stakeholder
