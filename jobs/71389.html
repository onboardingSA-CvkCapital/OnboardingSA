About the role

The Sales Manager will be responsible for managing, developing and growing relationships with major South African retail groups, with a specific focus on strategic key account management and commercial performance The ideal candidate will have a proven track record of managing major retail accounts such as Shoprite, Checkers, Pick n Pay, Woolworths, SPAR and Massmart, Amazon and will have established relationships and a strong understanding of the South African retail buying environment The role will have significant responsibility for annual trading term negotiations, pricing, rebates, promotional agreements, ranging, category growth and account profitability. This is a commercially focused role requiring an experienced retail sales professional who is comfortable with managing Key Accounts Managers, targets, budgets and profitability for each key account.
