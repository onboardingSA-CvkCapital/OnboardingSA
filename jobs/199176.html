About the role

At Cell C, we are not just a telecommunications company; we are a people-centric and consumer-focused organization committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our customers. In line with our dedication to customer-centricity, we are seeking a seasoned professional as an Senior Manager: Segment Marketing to join a dynamic team of #Unstoppables. Purpose of the Job: At Cell C, we are not just a telecommunications company; we are a people-centric and consumer-focused organisation committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our customers. In line with our dedication to customer-centricity, we are seeking a seasoned professional to join our dynamic team as the Senior Manager: Segment Marketing. This strategic role is at the forefront of driving brand resonance and business growth, leveraging deep consumer understanding and data-driven insights to shape and execute segment-specific marketing strategies. The Senior Manager: Segment Marketing plays a pivotal role in orchestrating Cell C's marketing efforts, focusing on the diverse needs of our consumer and business segments. Reporting directly to the Executive Head of Segment Marketing, this position is instrumental in defining and implementing marketing plans tailored to various segments, with the overarching goal of enhancing consideration, expanding our active customer base, increasing revenue market share, Brand Health and elevating our Net Promoter Score (NPS).
