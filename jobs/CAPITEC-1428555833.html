About the role

Capitec is hiring a Software Development Manager, based in Stellenbosch. Once you have completed the above finalize your application by clicking apply below. We are looking for an experienced Software Development Manager: Cash Devices to lead the teams responsible for our cash-device technology platforms, terminal applications, integrations and operational delivery capability. This is a strategic technical leadership role for someone with strong ATM and transaction-processing expertise who can provide technology direction, manage key vendor and platform relationships, ensure the stability, availability and security of critical banking systems, and develop high-performing teams. The successful candidate will drive delivery excellence, operational resilience, continuous improvement and people development across a critical customer channel.

Responsibilities

  • Lead multiple ATM technology delivery teams responsible for ATM applications and services.
  • Drive solution design, architecture governance, engineering standards and delivery quality.
  • Ensure system availability, performance, resilience and security across a critical banking channel.
  • Oversee software development, enhancements, maintenance and production support.
  • Lead major incident management, root cause analysis and recovery activities.
  • Build and develop high-performing technical delivery teams through coaching and mentoring.
  • Manage vendor relationships, technical roadmaps and delivery outcomes.
  • Partner with product, technology and business stakeholders to deliver strategic objectives.
  • Partner with the Product Head to translate the strategic product roadmap into executable delivery plans, release forecasts, milestones and team-level execution priorities across the cash-devices technology portfolio.
  • Lead agile planning practices with the team, translating prioritised business features into delivery-ready team epics and, through iterative refinement, clear user stories, while managing dependencies, capacity, sprint and release planning, and delivery tracking.
  • Drive engineering best practices including secure coding, code reviews, CI/CD and production readiness
