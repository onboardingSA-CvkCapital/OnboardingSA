About the role

We are recruiting on behalf of a well-established, multidisciplinary engineering and construction group with over 25 years of operational history and a strong footprint across Africa. Our client delivers turnkey electrical, civil, mechanical, instrumentation and telecommunications engineering solutions, They are looking to appoint an experienced Electrical & Instrumentation (E&I) Construction Manager to lead site execution on current and upcoming projects, with specific emphasis on overhead line construction and MV/HV reticulation. The Role • Manage E&I construction projects from mobilisation through to handover — on time, on budget and to specification • Lead and coordinate site teams, subcontractors and multidisciplinary interfaces across greenfield and brownfield projects • Oversee construction, installation and repair of overhead lines and MV/HV reticulation networks (up to 132 kV) • Manage installation of MV switchgear, mini-substations, Ring Main Units, and MV joints/terminations • Drive HSE compliance and quality assurance across all site activities, in line with ISO 9001/45001 standards • Act as the primary technical and client-facing point of contact on site, resolving construction constraints, technical queries and schedule risks • Coordinate QA/QC inspections, testing, punch list close-out and project handoverWhat We're Looking For • Proven track record as a Construction Manager / Superintendent within E&I construction, ideally within an EPC/EPCM or turnkey contracting environment • Demonstrable, hands-on experience in overhead line installation and/or repair — this is a non-negotiable requirement, not a nice-to-have • Solid working knowledge of MV/HV reticulation, switchgear, substations and related electrical infrastructure • Strong contractor and subcontractor management experience, with the ability to manage multiple workfronts simultaneously • Relevant technical qualification (Electrical Engineering / Electrical Trade Test / equivalent), plus current HSE certifications (e.g. Working at Heights, Construction Regulations) • Willingness and ability to work on project sites across South Africa and the broader African continent • Valid driver's licence and passport; own transport advantageousWhy Apply Join a company with a genuine legacy in African infrastructure delivery, a strong safety and quality culture, and a pipeline of projects that will put your overhead lines and E&I construction expertise to real use — not just on paper.
