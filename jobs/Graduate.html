About the role

Graduate Pharmaceutical Sales Representative (JB6179) Western Cape (Various Territories) R20 000 basic per month + Benefits: Provident fund, cellphone/device allowance, travel/petrol allowance and performance incentives Permanent Launch your pharmaceutical sales career with full training, valuable field experience and excellent growth potential. You will engage directly with doctors, primarily GPs, while developing scientific product knowledge, professional relationships and commercial skills. Available Territories Western Cape Central: Cape Town CBD, Milnerton, Panorama, Cape Flats and Blouberg, with West Coast travel to Langebaan and Vredendal. Western Cape South: Southern Suburbs and Helderberg, with travel to Hermanus and Plettenberg Bay. Western Cape North: Northern Suburbs, Winelands and Boland, extending to Robertson, Worcester, Montagu and Ceres. Minimum Requirements: A completed BSc or related health-science degree focused on human biology, anatomy or physiology. No experience is required. Valid driver’s licence and own reliable vehicle. Must be based in the Western Cape and able to service the allocated territory. Comfortable with daily travel and longer country trips. Confident communicating and presenting to doctors, primarily general practitioners. Able to understand and clearly explain scientific and clinical product information. Strong communication, presentation and relationship-building skills. Professional, ambitious and interested in developing a career in pharmaceutical sales. Organised and able to manage appointments, travel and a structured territory call cycle. Duties and Responsibilities: Promote and detail pharmaceutical products directly to doctors, primarily general practitioners. Explain product indications, clinical benefits and appropriate usage clearly and accurately. Complete pharmaceutical product, medical, scientific and sales training. Develop and maintain a thorough understanding of the assigned product portfolio. Remain informed about relevant medical, clinical and scientific developments. Build credible and long-term professional relationships with doctors. Plan and conduct regular structured visits to medical practices within the allocated territory. Schedule appointments and maintain an effective doctor call cycle. Prepare for one-on-one product-detailing discussions and presentations. Identify opportunities to increase product awareness and support territory growth. Work towards agreed activity, sales and performance objectives. Monitor competitor activity, market developments and feedback from doctors. Provide accurate market and customer feedback to management. Complete required call reports and territory administration. Use a personal vehicle to travel throughout the assigned territory. Undertake longer day trips and country trips where required.
