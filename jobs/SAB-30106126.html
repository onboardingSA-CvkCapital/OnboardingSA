About the role

SAB is hiring a FINANCIAL CONTROLLER VOPS, based in Sandton. The Key Purpose of this role is of this role is to ensure timely and accurate reporting of month end financial results for SAB Maltings for the P&L, Sales, VIC, ZBB, Interest & Inventory in line with approved policies and procedures and with the various stakeholders not limited to NOCC ATR team, Zone & Plant PPMs, Zone Control, Procurement, To manage the budget planning process for P&L, BOMs, Sales, VIC, ZBB, Interest & Inventory, To submit timely and accurate LE’s on Barley Price variance, ZBB People Cost and Inventory, To ensure adherence and proper documentation for MICS and SOX continuous improvement review of processes in the entities and maximise effectiveness and efficiency of process areas. The position performs activities on the financial transactions.

Responsibilities

  • Month end responsibilities
  • Drive General Ledger Close processes with all the stakeholders within the specific deadlines. Engage with various stakeholders to resolve issues within the specific deadlines.
  • Review & post MEC journals, review variables, ensure the P&L (Sales, VIC, ZBB, Interest) is accurate & complete while meeting deadlines
  • Review of the Maltings P&L and validation that the results reported are accurate and there is no material misstatement
  • Complete FCCS month end pack, provide commentary to Financial control team
  • Review Flash presentation, provide commentary, analyze variables
  • Confirm Sales prices charged to loaded prices, calculate true mark-up per intercompany party (SAB & Mubex)
  • Balance sheet Recons: review and list action items going forward, ensure action items are closed out
  • Heads analysis, review vacancies per position to budget
  • Calculate Barley price variance analysis & LE
  • Calculate Inventory & People Cost LE
  • Identify opportunities to continuously improve results and reduce MEC process
  • Adhere to internal control protocols and provide information as required for internal and external audit. Prepare, maintain and archive documentation to satisfy audit and statutory requirements. Providing ongoing financial accounting technical & business support to the zone.
  • Assist the team to meet weekly and monthly targets and all relevant SLA’s and KPI’s
  • Budget setting responsibilities (Top down & Bottom up)
  • Adhere to all deadlines, guidelines, milestones and submission requirements to the Zone Finance & PPM teams on the below items
  • Review all budget calculations for BOMs and own the end-to-end process
  • Lead the ZBB budget process for Maltings
  • Review VIC cost, COS model, Production & Sales volumes, Utilities & M&P usages, various price increases, by-product sales
  • Calculate the cost of Barley purchased and the respective interest for the year
  • Consolidate & calculate all ZBB, VIC and non-ZBB components to calculate the malt cost and calculate budget Sales and Cost of sales prices per plant
  • Consolidate, calculate & review the P&L for Maltings
  • Prepare management review presentations for Maltings, respecting guidelines, templates and timelines
  • Drive review sessions with the plant PPMs on all budget inputs
  • Ensure product costing budget is loaded on SAP (BOMS, Indirect cost & Volumes)
  • Ensure final budget is properly uploaded in all relevant tools (HFM, SAP, Anaplan)
  • Key Attributes and Competencies
  • High sense of accuracy, attention to detail & adherence to deadlines
  • Consulting and facilitation of key customers and stakeholders
  • Influencing diverse stakeholders
  • Implementing and achieving business results through other people
  • Thinking conceptually and strategically, while maintaining a cost-conscious and practical approach to using time and resources
  • Driving change and demonstrating resilience
