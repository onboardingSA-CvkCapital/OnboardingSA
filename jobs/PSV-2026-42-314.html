About the role

The Office of the Premier (Eastern Cape) is hiring a Head of Department: Department of Health, based in Bhisho. This is a contract post, paying R2,446,749 to R2,756,241 a year. (Five-Year Contract) How to apply: Applications must be submitted online through the provincial e-Recruitment System accessible at: https://ecprov.gov.za (under Careers) and/or at https://erecruitment.ecotp.gov.za (to view jobs without logging-in, click: Jobs). The Provincial e-Recruitment System is available 24/7 and closes at 23: 59 on the Closing Date. To report any e-Recruitment System challenges send an email with your (i) ID Number, (ii) profile email address, (iii) details of your issue to: HODrecruitment@ecotp.gov.za. Do not send any applications to this email address; should you do so, your applications/ CV will be regarded as lost and will not be considered. Technical support is limited to working hours: (08:00- 16:30 Mon-Thursday and 08:00-16:00 on Friday). Direct all enquiries to the specified enquiries person or email Address. Reference number to quote: OTP- Closing date: 04 December 2026. Applications received after closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Persons with Disabilities are encouraged to apply

Responsibilities

  • Oversee District Health Services
  • Oversee the implementation of District Health Programmes and the District
  • Health System across the Province
  • Coordinate Primary Health Care, community-based health services and priority
  • Health programmes in accordance with the District Health Service Package and provincial health priorities
  • Manage Emergency Medical Services and Forensic Pathology Services across the Province
  • Monitor and evaluate district health service performance, access, quality, health outcomes and compliance with applicable health standards
  • Lead district health system reform, including National Health Insurance readiness and integration across the provincial health service platform.
  • Lead Hospital and Clinical Support Services
  • Manage specialized, regional, tertiary, central, mental health and tuberculosis hospital services across the province
  • Lead the provincial hospital service platform with service demands, referral arrangements and catchment populations
  • Oversee clinical management, clinical governance and professional practice across the provincial health service
  • Lead pharmaceutical, allied health and other clinical support services
  • Regulate quality of care, patient safety, clinical outcomes and compliance with applicable health standards
  • Advance continuous clinical professional development across the provincial health system
  • Co-Ordinate and Manage Financial and Corporate Management Services: Account for departmental financial management, including budgeting, expenditure management, revenue management, financial accounting and financial reporting in accordance with the Public Finance Management Act and Treasury prescripts.
  • Oversee supply chain management, asset management and internal financial control.
  • Manage human resource management and development, organizational development and workforce planning.
  • Control information and communication technology, information management, legal services, security management and facilities management.
  • Coordinate departmental strategy management, organizational performance management, intergovernmental relations and stakeholder engagement.
  • Lead the allocation and utilization of financial, human, information and physical resources against departmental priorities and service delivery requirements.
  • Optimise the Planning, Resourcing, Implementation, Monitoring and Maintenance of Infrastructure: Oversee provincial health infrastructure planning in accordance with service delivery requirements and approved infrastructure priorities.
  • Manage the implementation of health infrastructure programmes and capital projects across the province.
  • Lead infrastructure investment and resource allocation against health service requirements, infrastructure conditions approved plans.
  • Regulate the specification, acquisition, utilisation and lifecycle management of health technology.
  • Monitor infrastructure maintenance, engineering and technical support programmes across the provincial health estate.
  • Ensure the Management of Executive Support, Internal Audit, Risk and Integrity Services: Oversee the Internal Audit function and consider assurance on internal control, risk management and organisational performance.
  • Regulate enterprise risk management, anti-corruption, fraud prevention, ethics and integrity management.
  • Monitor departmental responses to audit findings, investigations, identified control weaknesses and governance risks.
  • Manage executive support to the Office of the Head of Department, including executive administration, special programmes and institutional coordination.
  • Coordinate international relations and strategic partnerships assigned to the Office of the Head of Department.
  • Advise the Executive Authority on material organisational risks, audit outcomes, integrity matters and institutional interventions requiring executive consideration.
  • Manage the Department in Accordance with the Mandate of the Senior Management Service: Manage the Department in accordance with its constitutional and legislative mandate, government priorities and the policy direction of the Executive Authority.
  • Oversee the implementation of the Department’s Strategic Plan, Annual Performance Plan, operational plans and organizational performance commitments.
  • Account for the administration and resources of the Department in accordance with the Public Finance Management Act, Public Service Act, Treasury Regulations and applicable Public Service prescripts.
  • Manage the performance and development of the Department’s executive management team and maintain accountability for organizational performance.
  • Monitor departmental performance and compliance against approved plans, executive decisions, legislative requirements and government priorities.
  • Advise the Member of the Executive Council, Premier and Executive Council on departmental policy, strategy, legislation, organizational performance, health sector reform and emerging matters.
  • Represent the Department in executive, legislative, intergovernmental, oversight and stakeholder structures.
  • Fulfil responsibilities assigned to the Head of Department and Accounting Officer in terms of legislation, Public Service prescripts and delegations from the Executive Authority.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 (Senior Certificate), and an appropriate qualification at National Qualifications Framework (NQF) Level 8, as recognised by the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA), in one of the following fields: Medicine and Surgery (i.e. Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery - MBChB), or Public Health Management, or Health Systems Sciences, or Health Systems Management.
  • At least 10 years’ experience at Senior Managerial Level.
  • Demonstrated experience in executive governance, strategic planning, financial stewardship, organisational leadership and oversight of complex health service delivery systems.
  • Successful completion of the Senior Management Service.
  • (SMS) Pre-Entry Programme (Nyukela), and Valid Code EB driver’s license.
