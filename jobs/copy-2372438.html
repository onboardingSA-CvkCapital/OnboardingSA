About the role

The Regional Portfolio Manager is responsible for providing financial, operational, and property management support across the Gauteng property portfolio. This role has a strong emphasis on financial management, performance reporting, budgeting, and portfolio analysis, while also supporting the effective management of property operations and strategic initiatives. The incumbent will act as a key link between the regional team, Asset Management, Finance, and Property Management functions, ensuring accurate reporting, sound financial controls, effective portfolio monitoring, and optimal property performance.
