About the role

Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the-farm foods for consumers. Apart from its various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture, with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now operate across the entire food supply chain, including processing, logistics, storage, handling and national and international market channels. This "house of brands", established with the support of our farmer shareholders, forms the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. More about VKB Milling VKB Milling (Pty) Ltd is one of four subsidiaries of VKB Agri Processors (Pty) Ltd, the Agri-processing division of the VKB Group. VKB Milling operates white maize mills in Mokopane (Limpopo) and Mashishing (Mpumalanga), a wheat mill in Frankfort (Free State), as well as a maize mill in Christiana and a wheat mill in Modderrivier (Northern Cape). About the Role We are looking for a highly organised, detail-oriented, and proactive Administrative Assistant to join our team at our mill. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a structured administrative environment, enjoys maintaining accurate records, and takes pride in ensuring that information, documentation, and processes are managed efficiently and securely. The successful candidate will play a critical role in supporting daily operations through accurate administration, reporting, customer service, record keeping, and coordination across multiple functions. Key Responsibilities Administration & Documentation • Create and issue picking instructions for customer loads. • Process customer load invoicing accurately and timeously after picking. • Prepare and distribute daily reports, including sales order load reports and fuel usage reports. • File and maintain invoices, Proof of Deliveries (PODs), and other operational documentation. • Scan and upload PODs and supporting documentation onto File Director and other document management systems. • Ensure all required administrative documentation is available, updated, and accessible at the front desk. • Assist with the scanning, indexing, and digitisation of archived documentation. • Maintain accurate records and ensure compliance with company document control procedures.Customer Service & Cash Handling • Manage daily cash sales transactions (Groothandel). • Provide professional customer assistance and front-desk support. • Perform daily cash reconciliation and cash-up procedures. • Handle cash responsibly and in accordance with company policies and controls.Inventory & Office Support • Manage stationery stock levels and coordinate ordering when required. • Ensure adequate office supplies are available to support daily operations.Maintenance Administration • Support On-Key Maintenance administration functions. • Assist with maintenance planning, scheduling, and job card administration. • Ensure maintenance records are updated and accurately maintained.Logistics & Pallet Control • Manage pallet control administration, including: • Verification of documentation. • Follow-up with customers and transporters. • Reconciliation and tracking of pallet movements. • Resolution of pallet-related discrepancies.Finance Support • Provide relief support to the Assistant Accountant when required. • Assist with finance-related administrative functions as directed. Minimum Requirements • Grade 12 / NQF Level 4 qualification. • Minimum 2-3 years' experience in an administrative role. • Strong administrative, organisational, and record-keeping skills. • High level of numerical literacy and attention to detail. • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, with strong Excel capabilities. • Experience working with ERP, accounting, or business management systems will be advantageous. • Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities effectively. • Strong communication and interpersonal skills. • Valid driver's licence and own reliable transport. • Clear criminal record. • Verifiable references from previous employers in a similar role. • Willingness to work overtime or after-hours when operational requirements necessitate it. • Willingness to travel occasionally if required. Competencies & Personal Attributes The ideal candidate will demonstrate: • Exceptional attention to detail and accuracy. • Strong organisational and planning skills. • A methodical and systematic approach to work. • The ability to multitask and manage competing priorities. • Strong problem-solving abilities. • Integrity and confidentiality when dealing with company information. • Reliability and a strong sense of accountability. • The ability to work effectively with minimal supervision. • A positive attitude and willingness to support the wider team. • Self-motivation, initiative, and a proactive work ethic. • Professionalism in dealing with customers, suppliers, and colleagues. • The ability to perform repetitive but critical administrative tasks with a consistently high level of accuracy.
