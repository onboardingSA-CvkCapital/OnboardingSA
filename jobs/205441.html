About the role

Faircape Life manages six established retirement and lifestyle villages across the Western Cape, offering secure residential living, healthcare services and well-maintained facilities designed around quality of life and service excellence. We are looking for an experienced and hands-on Estate Manager to oversee estate operations across Heritage Manor and Bridgewater Manor, two of our retirement villages based in Somerset West. This is a broad operational management role responsible for ensuring that both estates are safe, well maintained and efficiently managed. You will oversee maintenance and facilities, security, service providers, operational staff, health and safety, and day-to-day estate requirements. The role requires someone who is visible on the ground, comfortable managing multiple priorities across two sites, and able to build strong working relationships with residents, staff, contractors and management. If you have a strong facilities or estate management background and enjoy taking ownership of operations, people and service delivery, we would like to hear from you.
