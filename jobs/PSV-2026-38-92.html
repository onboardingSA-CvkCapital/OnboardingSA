About the role

The Department of Correctional Services is hiring a Senior Administration Clerk: Registration, based in Kroonstad Training College. This is a permanent post, paying R280,278 a year. How to apply: should be submitted online at https://careers.dcs.gov.za/. Emailed, post or hand delivered applications will not be considered. Reference number to quote: HO 2026/11/88 Closing date: 09 November 2026 @ 15H45. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Appointment under the Public Service Act.

Responsibilities

  • Manage archives and registry sections.
  • Transfer records to archives.
  • Withdraw records from archives and registry.
  • Dispose files.
  • Control uniform filling system.
  • Safe keeping of departmental files.
  • General filing.
  • Management of assets.

  • 1-2 years’ relevant experience.
  • Computer literate.
  • Valid driver’s licence.

  • Presentation skills
  • Conflict management
  • Report writing
  • Time management

  • Understanding of public service policy and legislative framework

  • Problem solving skills
