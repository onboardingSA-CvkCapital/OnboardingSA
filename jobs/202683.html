About the role

Faircape Health Institute, a division of the Faircape Group, provides comprehensive healthcare services across the Western Cape, with established expertise in sub-acute care, rehabilitation, long-term care, and healthy ageing. Building on this clinical foundation, we are developing a Longevity and Preventative Health Centre that brings together medicine, advanced diagnostics, exercise science, technology, and personalised health interventions within a multidisciplinary clinical environment. We are seeking an experienced and forward-thinking Biokineticist to become an integral part of this developing service. We are looking for a dynamic, engaging and evidence-driven practitioner who enjoys working with people, takes initiative, and is confident in a highly visible, patient-facing role. The ideal candidate will be curious about the intersection of exercise science, medicine, technology, and longevity, and will be adaptable, resourceful, and comfortable contributing ideas while helping to build and shape a new service. This is not a conventional Biokinetics practice. Working alongside our Clinical Lead – Longevity & Preventative Medicine and broader multidisciplinary team, the successful candidate will use advanced strength and functional assessment tools, objective performance data, VALD technology, AI-integrated training equipment, and other technologies to assess physical capacity and develop highly personalised interventions. Central to the role is a closed-loop approach: assess, identify, prescribe, set measurable targets, reassess, and adapt. The Biokineticist will take ownership of this process while also contributing to the development of the protocols, guidelines, and programmes that support the service.
