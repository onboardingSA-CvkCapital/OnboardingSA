About the role

Frogfoot is seeking a ME Network Manager to join our Engineering team. Purpose of the Role The ME Network Manager is responsible for leading Frogfoot's Metro Ethernet (ME) Network function, ensuring a highly available, scalable, and resilient carrier-grade network that supports the organisation's strategic objectives. This role combines technical leadership with people leadership, driving operational excellence, continuous improvement, network automation, capacity planning, and service reliability. Working closely with Executive Leadership and Engineering Management, you'll ensure the network evolves to meet business growth while maintaining exceptional customer service and operational performance.
