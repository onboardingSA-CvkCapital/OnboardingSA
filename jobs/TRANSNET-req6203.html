About the role

Transnet (Transnet Rail Infrastructure Manager (TRIM)) is hiring a Track Inspector, based in Bellville. The role reports to the Production Manager. Grade: H2X. Responsible for the implementation and management of the track inspection programs to ensure that the track infrastructure is maintained to safe and serviceable standards and adhere to the relevant rail safety regulations and engineering standards. Assist in the compilation of the short-term and medium -term track maintenance plans, and various resources that will be required for its implementation. Assess, develop and maintain the competencies and skills within the track maintenance teams/ employees according to the job requirements, rail safety standards and occupational safety & health standards. To plan, co-ordinate and supervise resources relating to various track maintenance tasks, including inspections and execution, within a specific area, to ensure the safety of the public, employees and the safe passage of trains to support the business objectives.

Responsibilities

  • Assist the Production Manager with the compilation of the short and medium Perway maintenance plans based on the outcome of the inspection conducted, audit results and operational requirements and indicate the resources required in the implementation of the approved plans including the day-to-day activities

  • 6.
