About the role

CCG Systems is an ICT sector and is well known to specialize in the implementation and support of ERP systems as well as Payroll & HR Systems to which our clients are made of mostly Municipalities throughout South Africa. Our company is looking for a potential candidate to join our Finance team in the capacity of a Group Accountant, to be based at our Head Office. The Group Accountant is responsible for ensuring the effective financial management, reporting, and compliance of the group of companies. This role supports the CFO in managing the group’s financial health and integrity, providing consolidated financial insights, and ensuring robust financial operations across subsidiaries. The ideal candidate will drive process standardization, strengthen internal controls, and support the execution of strategic financial initiatives.
