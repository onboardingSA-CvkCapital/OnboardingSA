or vision for the position of Deputy-Dean (Research, Internationalisation and Postgraduate Studies (Maximum 2 pages) Duties and responsibilities •    Provide formal teaching for undergraduate and post-graduate programmes in the Faculty of Education. •    Act as a supervisor for postgraduate students in the relevant field. •    Engage in the Work Integrated Learning Programme of the faculty (Teaching Practice). •    Ensure active involvement in research in a relevant field of Education and publish research articles in accredited national and international journals. •    Active involvement in curriculum development relating to the relevant field of Education. •    Apply for external funding grants for research and/or teaching and learning projects. •    Mentor and support junior colleagues in the Faculty of Education. •    Contribute to leadership and administrative tasks in the faculty. •    Drive innovation efforts aligned to research-aligned domains •    Drive multi-, inter-, and transdisciplinary research recognising that innovation, internationalisation are at the heart of teaching, learning, and research. •    Identify and develop current and future research flagships of the faculty. •    Participate and strive for excellence in all aspects of the core business of the faculty, but with specific reference to postgraduate studies, research and innovation. •    Take co-responsibility with the Dean to provide effective and appropriate academic direction and to develop and implement the strategic mission of the faculty within the broader University context. •    Take co-responsibility with the Dean to ensure the maintenance of all academic standards for postgraduate programmes in the faculty. •    Take co-responsibility with the Dean to provide effective and appropriate research direction and to develop and implement the strategic mission of the faculty, within the broader university context, with a focus on postgraduate studies and research. •    Take co-responsibility with the Dean in the management of postgraduate student-related matters. •    Support the Dean in obtaining networks, partnerships, and new growth and innovation opportunities. •    Take co-responsibility with the Dean for securing strategic collaboration and third-stream income relating to postgraduate bursaries and research. •    Take co-responsibility with the Dean to oversee the financial management and financial sustainability of research and postgraduate activities of the faculty. •    Provide leadership on the quality assurance of the postgraduate programme offerings on different sites and the need to shift from competition to collaboration, coherence, and regulation. •    Provide research-driven leadership in the faculty in relation to Research, Innovation, and Postgraduate Studies. •    Participate in teaching, research, and engaged scholarship. •    Chair the Research Committee of the faculty. •    Chair the Higher Degrees Committee of the Faculty •    Participate in committees both at the faculty and university level. •    Act as Dean and report to the Deputy Vice-Chancellors and Vice-Chancellor and Principal when the Dean is absent. Inherent Job Requirements ​​​​​​​​​​​​​​•    A Doctoral degree on NQF Level 10 in any relevant field of Education. •    Five years of experience at a management level (i.e., head of department/school/centre/unit). •    Proven experience with supervision of master’s and PhD students. •    Experience as an external examiner for postgraduate students. •    Proven evidence of publishing in peer-reviewed journals. •    Proven record of research grant applications, management, and experience in third-stream income generation. •    Superior knowledge of research methodology, postgraduate student support, and management of postgraduate programmes and processes •    Proven international recognition for specialist expertise and research leadership in a particular area of academic scholarship (e.g., regularly serves as a reviewer for international/ISI/IBSS journals). •    An NRF rating or potential for rating. •    Proven experience in co-leading or leading one or more collaborative research projects in the past 5-years. •    Eligible for appointment as an Associate or full Professor at the University of the Free State. Recommendations ​​​​​​​​​​​​​​•    Established leadership and team-building capacity. •    Demonstrated ability to negotiate, collaborate, communicate, and facilitate in complex environments. •    Analytical problem-solving skills with impeccable attention to detail. •    Ability to set an overall research and innovation strategy and drive process improvement. •    Established research leadership and research team-building capacity. Key Competencies Required: •    Results-oriented: -    The ability to cope with a frequently changing environment and to adapt to evolving situations. -    The ability to be reliable, responsible, dependable, and to fulfill obligations. •    Strategic thinking: -    The ability to be creative and open-minded when addressing work issues. -    The ability to carefully analyse information and use logic to address issues and problems at work. •    Business Acumen: -    The ability to adhere to rules and strictly follow work regulations. -    Proficient in using MS Office and other relevant software programmes and platforms. •    Leading: -    The ability to maintain high levels of personal motivation, energy, and enthusiasm. -    The ability to be cooperative with others, display a good-natured attitude, and encourage people to work together. •    Building coalitions -    The ability to be self-assured and at ease with people in all types of social situations. -    The ability to make decisions through consultation, collaboration, and working with close supervision.
