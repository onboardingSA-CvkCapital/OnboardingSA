About the role

The Department of Sport, Recreation, Arts and Culture (Eastern Cape) is hiring an Assistant Director: Training & Capacity, based in Head Office (Qonce). This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 – R584,370 a year. Reference number to quote: DSRAC 04/09/2026 Closing date: 23 October 2026. Applications received after closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Persons with disabilities are encouraged to apply.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and facilitate training programmes for employees.
  • Manage the development, review the training & development policies, frameworks and guidelines.
  • Monitor consolidation of the departmental training needs.
  • Verify alignment with departmental priorities and skills plans.
  • Develop and maintain annual training schedules.
  • Liaise and engage with training providers.
  • Oversee training sessions to ensure quality and adherence to agreed standards.
  • Align human resource development training programs with strategic objectives.
  • Maintain training records and reporting.
  • Facilitate and lead skills audits.
  • Develop a detailed skills audit plan and implementation approach aligned with departmental objectives.
  • Develop, standardize tools and methodologies as per Public Service-Skills Audit Methodology Framework.
  • Engage and consult stakeholders on various HRD matters that needs to be consulted.
  • Analyse and interpret skills audit results.
  • Develop skills audit reports and recommendations.
  • Monitor the implementation of recommendations.
  • Administer the implementation of the bursary scheme policy.
  • Draft bursary advert and submit it to Superior for further processing.
  • Coordinate bursary application processes and submit it to bursary committee for selection.
  • Communicate outcomes with recipients and educational institutions.
  • Monitor bursary contracts.
  • Coordinate payment and financial administration.
  • Monitor academic performance and compliance.
  • Maintain records and compile reports.
  • Implement, monitor and evaluate the Internship and Learnership Programme.
  • Coordinate the screening, recruitment and placement of interns.
  • Supervise the administration of internship contracts.
  • Facilitate the training of mentors for the learnership programme support.
  • Monitor alignment of Learnerships Programmes to SETA, Quality Council for Trades & Occupations and departmental requirements.
  • Monitor, evaluate and report on the progress of the Learnership programmes including coordination of learner-facilitator and learner- mentor contact sessions.
  • Liaise with relevant stakeholders regarding the administration and successful delivery of Learnership Programmes.
  • Management of administration support.
  • Facilitate HRM audit process and ensure timeous submission of information requested by auditors.
  • Give input on the development of policies and operational plans.
  • Compile monthly and quarterly reports.
  • Manage financial and human resources.
  • Maintain staff discipline.

Requirements

  • National Senior Certificate plus a National Diploma (NQF L6) or preferably a B Degree (NQF L7) in Human Resource Management/Human Resource Development/Training and Development/Public Administration/Public Management qualification as recognised by SAQA.
  • At least 3 years’ experience in Skills Development and Training coordination, of which 3 years should be at a supervisory level (SL 7/8).
  • Skills Development Facilitator certificate and Train the Trainer certificate will serve as an added advantage.
  • Knowledge and skills:
  • Good interpretation and knowledge of the National Skills Development Strategy
  • Public Finance Management Act
  • Skills Development Act
  • Skills Development Levies Act
  • Public Service Regulations
  • Knowledge of the compulsory training programmes in the Public Service and training on various programs
  • Knowledge of PERSAL system
  • Planning
  • Coordination
  • Organising
  • Analytical and people management skills
  • Sound verbal and written Communication skills
  • Presentation skills
  • High sense of responsibility and accountability
  • Interpersonal
  • Time management skills
  • Attention to details
  • Experience in managing long term training programmes (Learnership, Internship, Workplace Integrated Learning)
  • Experience in developing and submitting Workplace Skills Plan and various SETA’s reports
  • Project management skills and ability to coordinate
  • Manage and closeup training and development projects
  • Computer literacy in the Microsoft package i.e. Word
  • Excel
  • PowerPoint
