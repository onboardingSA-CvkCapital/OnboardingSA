About the role

Company description: The Clinix Health Group (CHG) assists communities to live longer, healthier lives through the provision of quality and affordable healthcare. The group provides integrated world-class healthcare systems both internally and externally to the broader ecosystem as a trusted local citizen. Job Title              : Chief of Staff Location              : Clinix Health Group Head Office, Hyde Park Reports to           : Chief Executive Officer (CEO) JOB SUMMARY The Chief of Staff serves as a strategic advisor and trusted partner to the CEO, driving the execution of enterprise-wide strategic initiatives, governance priorities, performance management, executive decision-making, stakeholder engagement, and business transformation initiatives. The role acts as a bridge between the CEO, Executive Committee, Board, hospital leadership teams, and key stakeholders to ensure strategic objectives are effectively translated into operational outcomes.
