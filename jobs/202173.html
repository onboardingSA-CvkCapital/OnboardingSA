About the role

As a Labour Data Coordinator, you will play a critical role in collecting, analysing, and managing labour data to support decision-making and strategic planning. This position focuses on data integrity and reporting, ensuring that accurate and timely information is available for organisational needs and compliance. Key Responsibilities - Develop and maintain data management systems to ensure efficient collection and storage of labour-related information. - Reduction of the labour cost per assigned region from 01 October 2026. - Ensure Portal costings shifts are accurate and up to date with AM. - Collaborate with Operations on monthly labour performance and advise a course of action to rectify concerns. - Monitor key labour, trends monthly on absenteeism, sick leave and AWOL etc, providing timely reports and alerts to management - Improve efficiency by liaising with RM & AM’s on the changes needed for accurate data. - Ensure data integrity, privacy, and compliance with contractual scopes. - Continuously identify opportunities to improve reporting processes and dashboard functionality. - Actively move labour to a state of accuracy and reliability. - Assist Labour Data Specialist to track savings because of the implementation of Biometric systems Requirements - An appropriate qualification in Data Management, Human Resources, or a related field. - Demonstrable experience in data analysis, with a strong proficiency in data management software and analytical tools. - Proven track record in handling sensitive information while maintaining confidentiality and data security standards. - Excellent attention to detail and a high degree of accuracy in data entry and reporting. - Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret complex data sets. - Effective communication skills, both written and verbal, to convey insights to diverse stakeholder groups. - Experience working within the South African labour market and familiarity with relevant legislation. - Certification in data analysis or related fields is an advantage. - Knowledge of human resources practices and principles would be beneficial. - Proficiency in advanced data visualisation tools or programming languages for data analysis.
