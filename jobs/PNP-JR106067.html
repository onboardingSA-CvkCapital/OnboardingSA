About the role

Pick n Pay is hiring a Manager Deli (Kosher), based in Norwood, Gauteng. The Kosher Deli Manager is responsible for the overall management of the Deli Department, ensuring exceptional product quality, food safety compliance, customer service excellence, and adherence to operational standards. This role requires strong hands-on experience in Kosher food preparation and a thorough understanding of Kosher requirements, practices, and customer expectations. The successful candidate will drive sales growth, maximize department profitability, and create an outstanding customer experience by maintaining high merchandising standards, product availability, and service excellence within the Hypermarket environment.

Responsibilities

  • Core Competencies
  • Adherence to standards, policies and food safety requirements
  • Understanding of Kosher food requirements and practices
  • Customer Orientation
  • Communication skills - clearly conveying messages and actively listening
  • Business mindedness
  • Team Player
  • Leadership and people management
  • Attention to detail
  • Strong food quality and hygiene awareness
  • Ability to understand and respond to the specific needs of the Kosher customer base
  • Merchandising Management
  • Conduct regular quality checks on merchandise.
  • Execute plano guides 100%.
  • Handle products in accordance with SOPs and applicable Kosher requirements.
  • Ensure the department is ready for trade by store opening time.
  • Ensure sufficient product availability to meet customer demand.
  • Maintain appropriate product handling, storage and preparation standards.
  • Ensure all products are displayed and handled in accordance with required food safety and Kosher standards.
  • Maintain high standards of cleanliness and housekeeping throughout the department.
  • Monitor promotional activities and ensure adherence to the promotional calendar.
  • Monitor sell-by dates and ensure correct stock rotation.
  • Minimise waste while maintaining product quality and availability.
  • Kosher Food Standards & Compliance
  • Ensure that all Kosher food preparation, handling, storage and merchandising requirements are consistently maintained.
  • Ensure the department follows all applicable Kosher procedures and controls.
  • Maintain a strong understanding of the requirements associated with Kosher food preparation and retail.
  • Ensure that staff are appropriately trained and understand the importance of maintaining Kosher standards.
  • Work closely with relevant stakeholders to address any Kosher-related operational requirements or non-conformances.
  • Demonstrate sensitivity to the expectations and requirements of the Kosher customer community.
  • Food Safety & Hygiene
  • Ensure that hygiene and housekeeping standards are maintained.
  • Ensure correct stock rotation.

  • Respond to customer requests and queries professionally.
  • Understand the needs and expectations of the Kosher customer base.

  • Communicate critical information to staff.

  • Attend monthly regional meetings and communicate relevant information to staff.
  • Administration & Systems
  • Ensure all equipment is properly maintained and in effective working order.
  • Monitor waste and ensure procedures are adhered to.

  • Ensure PIDs are counted correctly in accordance with the National P&L schedule.

  • Minimum 2-3 year supervisory/management experience

  • Practical knowledge and understanding of Kosher food preparation, handling, storage and operational requirements
  • Experience working within a Kosher food environment and understanding the importance of maintaining Kosher standards and procedures
  • Experience in food preparation and/or a culinary environment will be advantageous
  • Ability to work effectively with customers and colleagues within a diverse community
