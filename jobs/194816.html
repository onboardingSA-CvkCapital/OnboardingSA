About the role

About Core Group Core Group represents some of the world's most loved technology brands across Sub-Saharan Africa. Our mission is to empower individuals and businesses through innovative technology solutions and exceptional customer experiences. Our expertise spans retail, sales, marketing, supply chain, customer experience, finance, human resources, business development, and technology services. With operations across South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, and Nigeria, we're passionate about developing talented individuals who want to build successful careers in the technology industry. About the Programme Are you a recent graduate with a passion for sales, technology, and building meaningful customer relationships? We're looking for ambitious, commercially minded graduates to join our 12-month Sales Graduate Programme. This programme has been designed to develop the next generation of sales professionals and future commercial leaders within Core Group. Throughout the programme, you'll gain hands-on experience across various sales channels and business functions, working alongside experienced sales leaders and high-performing teams. You'll develop the practical skills, commercial understanding, and confidence needed to succeed in a fast-paced technology sales environment. This is an exciting opportunity to kick-start your career with one of Africa's leading technology companies. During the programme, you'll: • Gain practical experience within Core Group's commercial and sales environment. • Support the Sales team in achieving business objectives and delivering exceptional customer experiences. • Build relationships with internal stakeholders, retail partners, and customers. • Analyse sales performance, customer trends, and market insights to support business decisions. • Assist with sales planning, reporting, forecasting, and operational activities. • Participate in projects aimed at improving sales performance and operational efficiency. • Learn about product launches, promotional campaigns, and commercial strategies across multiple technology brands. • Develop an understanding of retail operations, merchandising, and customer engagement. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Marketing, Retail Operations, Finance, Supply Chain, and Customer Experience. • Present ideas, recommendations, and project updates to business leaders. • Participate in ongoing learning, coaching, mentoring, and development opportunities.What We're Looking For We're looking for graduates who are curious, driven, and eager to build a career in sales and commercial leadership. Minimum Requirements A completed Bachelor's Degree obtained within the last three years in one of the following fields: • Business Management • Marketing • Commerce (BCom) • Economics • Industrial Engineering • Electrical Engineering • Supply Chain • Information Systems • Finance • Or a related qualification. • A valid driver's licence is advantageous. • Willingness to travel where required. • Based in or willing to relocate to Gauteng.You'll Stand Out If You Have • A genuine passion for technology and consumer electronics. • Strong interpersonal and relationship-building skills. • Excellent communication and presentation abilities. • Commercial awareness and an interest in sales and business development. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. • Confidence working with data and Microsoft Excel. • A customer-first mindset. • Initiative, resilience, and a proactive attitude. • Leadership experience through university societies, sport, volunteer work, or entrepreneurial ventures. • The ability to work effectively in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.What You'll Gain As part of the Sales Graduate Programme, you'll receive: • Hands-on experience across Core Group's commercial and sales operations. • Exposure to some of the world's leading technology brands. • Structured learning and development opportunities. • Mentorship from experienced sales and business leaders. • Practical experience in sales strategy, account management, retail operations, and customer engagement. • Exposure to cross-functional business projects. • Opportunities to develop your commercial, leadership, and problem-solving skills. • A strong foundation for future career opportunities within Core Group. • If you're passionate about technology, enjoy building relationships, and are ready to launch your career in sales, we'd love to hear from you. As part of our recruitment process, successful candidates may be required to undergo background screening, including criminal, credit, qualification, and reference checks where applicable.
