About the role

Transnet (Transnet NPA) is hiring a Chief Marine Eng Officer (RE-Advertised), based in Saldanha. The role reports to the Marine Technical Manager. Grade: F. To manage the marine engineering functions of a harbour tug in such a manner as to ensure safe and efficient operation of the plant and auxilliary equipent, the implementation of sound preventative maintenance practises interms of the planned maintenance schedules, fault diagnosis and on board repairs of equipmentand machinery, attend to overhauls of machinery and equipment including planned craft lay ups and unpalnned breakdowns.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain equipment in accordance with preventative maintenance schedules to ensure optimum tug availibility
  • Manage on board fault diagnosis and repairs
  • Manage operation of plant and auxiliary equipment in accordance with sound marine engineering standards and practices
  • Conduct checks of plant and equipment including operational surveillance whilst craft is underway
  • Manage periodic lay / overhaul of plant and equipment in conjunction with Marine Technical Manager
  • Manage and allocate resources including tools, power tools, spares etc.·
  • Train subordinates, mentorship training of trainee chief engineers
  • Ensure a safe working environment and the implementation of safe working practices in terms of Company policies and legislative requirements
  • Ensure that all legislative, regulatory and Company requirements and procedures are complied with in respect of the marine engineering department on board the tug
  • Attend to unplanned breakdowns
  • Maintain accurate and complete records of all engine room machinery.
  • Maintain equipment on tug according to Transnet's Code 29 guideline.
  • Manage and control Quality Managemental policies and standards.
  • Inculcating TRANSNET values.
  • Project management.
  • Management Information: To provide statistical data, inspections and audits for sound decision-making, elimination of deviations from planned processes, respectively.
  • Plan, implement and oversee crews' safety
