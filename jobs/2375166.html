About the role

An exciting opportunity is available for an experienced Estimator to join our client’s head office team in Edenvale. This role is suited to a middle-management professional with a strong background in construction estimating and the ability to contribute strategically to the tendering and estimating function. Requirements: ·        National Diploma or BTech/BSc/BEng in Quantity Surveying, Construction Management, Building Science or a related built-environment field ·        Approximately 5–8 years of relevant experience in a construction estimating environment. ·        Strong experience working specifically within an estimating department. ·        Previous middle-management or supervisory experience will be advantageous. ·        A background in construction estimating is essential; this is not a Quantity Surveying-focused role. ·        Strong understanding of construction costs, tendering and pricing methodologies. ·        Excellent analytical, organisational and communication skills. ·        Ability to work effectively in a head office environment based in Edenvale. Responsibilities: ·        Manage and coordinate estimating activities from tender review through to submission. ·        Prepare accurate and competitive cost estimates for construction projects. ·        Analyse tender documentation, drawings, specifications and project requirements. ·        Review pricing, supplier and subcontractor quotations. ·        Coordinate with internal departments and project stakeholders to ensure accurate tender submissions. ·        Identify commercial risks, pricing discrepancies and potential cost-saving opportunities. ·        Support the development and improvement of estimating processes and systems. ·        Provide guidance and leadership within the estimating department. Should you meet the minimum requirements for this position, please email your updated CV attached to engineering.jobs@tumaini.co.za , alternatively contact the team on 021 551 2621 or visit our website, www.tumaini.co Correspondence will only be conducted with short listed candidates. Should you not hear from us within 3 days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
