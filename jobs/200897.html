About the role

At Spitz, we are a leading force in premium footwear and apparel, offering world-class brands such as Carvela, Gianni Chiarini, Lacoste, Nina Roche, and Tosoni. With the addition of Carvela Donna & Bello body sprays and Verve by Kurt Geiger, we continue to elevate South African style, offering both luxury fashion and iconic fragrances. Our culture of craftsmanship, innovation, and teamwork fosters an environment where individual contributions are valued, and excellence is celebrated. If you’re passionate about fashion, thrive in a dynamic environment, and want to make a lasting impact, Spitz offers the ideal opportunity to grow your career. A rewarding opportunity awaits a skilled Planning Executive at Spitz in Bryanston, Johannesburg. This role is designed for a commercially astute, analytically strong and strategically minded individual who can help evolve the Planning function into a more insight-led, commercially responsive and technology-enabled capability. The position extends beyond traditional merchandise planning and requires an individual who can interpret complex trading data, identify behavioural and commercial patterns, challenge assumptions and translate insight into commercially actionable decisions across the business. Operating at the intersection of Planning, Commercial Strategy, Retail Intelligence, Data Analytics and Operational Decision Support, this role will play a key part in improving forecasting capability, inventory productivity, reporting frameworks and overall commercial responsiveness within the business. The successful individual will combine intellectual curiosity, analytical capability and commercial thinking with the ability to simplify complexity and influence decision-making at a senior level.
