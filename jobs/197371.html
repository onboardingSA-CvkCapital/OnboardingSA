About the role

Purpose of Role: The purpose of this role is to ensure compliance with Section 13A of the Pension Funds Act by monitoring employer contribution payments, identifying and investigating non-compliance, and implementing appropriate corrective actions. The role is responsible for analysing contribution data, engaging with employers, participating employers, and relevant stakeholders to resolve compliance matters, maintaining accurate records, and ensuring statutory reporting requirements are met. The role also provides guidance on legislative requirements, supports risk mitigation initiatives, and contributes to the effective administration and governance of retirement funds through proactive stakeholder engagement and compliance monitoring.
