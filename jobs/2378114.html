About the role

Purpose of the Role The Warehouse Controller is responsible for ensuring the effective receiving, storage, movement, inventory control and dispatch of raw materials, packaging materials, semi-finished and finished goods. The role ensures inventory accuracy through effective SAP transactions, stock control, cycle counts and compliance with Product Quality, Health & Safety and FIFO standards while leading warehouse personnel to achieve operational excellence. Key Responsibilities Inventory Management • Ensure the accurate movement of all stock between warehouses and production using SAP. • Maintain accurate inventory records by processing all stock transactions daily. • Capture manual transactions into SAP promptly during system downtime. • Conduct daily cycle counts and monthly stock counts, including weekend counts where required. • Investigate and resolve inventory discrepancies. • Monitor stock ageing and highlight obsolete or slow-moving inventory. • Ensure inventory adjustments are properly authorised and accurately processed. • Verify inventory reconciliation following stock adjustments. • Monitor minimum stock levels and escalate potential stock shortages. • Ensure all stock is correctly identified and labelled. Receiving Operations • Supervise all receiving activities for raw materials, packaging, toys and semi-finished products. • Verify received quantities against delivery documentation. • Ensure stock is checked, receipted, packed away and stored in the correct warehouse locations. • Coordinate with Production to ensure uninterrupted material availability. • Maintain accurate receiving documentation and records. • Assist with receiving activities during peak periods where required. Warehouse Operations • Ensure FIFO principles are consistently applied. • Ensure stock is stored in accordance with Ferrero Quality standards. • Maintain warehouse housekeeping in accordance with GMP and 5S principles. • Ensure efficient warehouse space utilisation. • Support picking, replenishment and dispatch activities where required. • Assist with forklift operations when operationally necessary. SAP & Inventory Control • Execute all warehouse inventory transactions accurately in SAP. • Ensure inventory movements are processed in real time. • Maintain accurate stock balances between physical inventory and SAP. • Support inventory reconciliation and stock investigations. People Management • Supervise warehouse employees and forklift drivers. • Allocate daily workload and monitor performance. • Coach employees on warehouse procedures and inventory accuracy. • Ensure compliance with company policies and standard operating procedures. • Support employee development and training. Quality, Safety & Compliance • Ensure compliance with Ferrero Quality Standards. • Adhere to Health & Safety, HACCP, GMP and company policies. • Maintain a safe working environment. • Participate in internal and external audits. • Ensure all warehouse documentation is completed accurately and retained for audit purposes.
