About the role

Capitec is hiring an Actuarial Manager, based in Stellenbosch. In this role, you will lead the process used to set and manage actuarial assumptions across the Valuations and Modelling department. You will be responsible for ensuring assumptions are accurate, well-supported by data, and governed through appropriate controls and approval processes. This includes analysing experience data, developing and reviewing assumptions, assessing business impacts, and presenting recommendations to governance forums. You will also oversee the overall basis-setting framework, including the methodology, governance, and operating model. Working closely with teams across the business, you will coordinate basis-setting activities, provide final assumption recommendations, and drive continuous improvements to the way assumptions are developed and managed.
