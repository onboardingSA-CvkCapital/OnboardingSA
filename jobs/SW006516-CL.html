About the role

RETAIL SALES BROKER (PRECIOUS METALS)Somerset West: Our client, a well-established specialist retailer and broker within the precious metals industry, is seeking a dynamic, highly sales-driven and customer-focused Retail Sales Broker to join their team. The ideal candidate will have a strong commercial mindset, a natural ability to build and nurture client relationships, and the drive to actively pursue opportunities and convert them into sales. A proactive, energetic “go-getter” with a genuine passion for sales and a strong client-first approach will thrive in this role. PURPOSE OF THE ROLE:  Driving sales of rare coins, gold coins and medallions through exceptional client service, relationship management and proactive business development KEY RESPONSIBILITIES • Selling rare coins, gold coins and medallions and consistently achieving sales targets • Building and maintaining strong relationships with existing and prospective clients • Actively prospecting for new business through networking, referrals, research and database development • Identifying sales opportunities within existing client portfolios • Providing a consultative and relationship-based sales experience • Following up consistently with clients to develop long-term relationships and maximise sales opportunities • Maintaining strong product knowledge, including pricing, new products and relevant economic and market developments • Processing sales transactions accurately and in accordance with company procedures • Maintaining excellent client service and presentation standards • Maintaining accurate CRM records and completing required administration and compliance • Safeguarding stock and adhering to operational, security and compliance proceduresCRITERIA • Matric • Proven sales track record, preferably within a high-value, luxury, financial or specialised product environment • Strong selling ability with a demonstrated ability to achieve targets • Excellent relationship-building, networking and communication skills • Highly driven, resilient and results-oriented • Strong client focus with excellent follow-up and follow-through • Professional, well-groomed and confident demeanour • Strong ethical standards and attention to detail • Computer literate with good Microsoft Office skills • Willingness to continuously develop product and industry knowledge
