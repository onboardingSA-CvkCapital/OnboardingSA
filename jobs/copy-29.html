About the role

This opportunity is with a long-standing, well established concern.  It is a one-of-its-kind FMCG related type operation, acting as the go-between "suppliers" and the "buyers". It would be better suited to candidates coming from a "tangible products" industry than a service industry, and it is not a call centre environment. You need to be specifically interested in, and have at least 1 to 3 years in a customer focused role, having liaised and built relationships with customers, processed sales orders, invoiced and followed up on payments.  Ability to start relatively soon would be a strong advantageous. Working Hours- 7.00am to 3.00pm Monday to Friday, with flexibility to work additional hours during peak seasons when required. Overview / Purpose of the role -Motivated, organised and customer focused with the as previously mentioned sales support / admin background to support the daily operations of this sales division. The successful candidate will liaise with both the suppliers and the buyers, process customer orders, perform administrative duties, capture invoices, and assist with collections to ensure the smooth running of the department. This position is ideal for someone who enjoys working in a fast-paced environment, has excellent communication skills, and pays close attention to detail.   The manager uses a collaborative, coaching-focused leadership style. She values team input, actively invests in her staff's personal and professional growth, and maintains an inspiring, team-oriented environment. Key Responsibilities: Sales Administration- • Liaise daily with buyers and suppliers regarding product availability and customer requirements. • Place weekly standing orders as well as additional orders during peak trading periods. • Assist customers with product enquiries, pricing, and availability. • Ensure all customer orders are accurately processed and communicated to growers. • Monitor outstanding orders and follow up to ensure timely deliveries. Administration- • Capture supplier and customer invoices accurately and timeously. • Verify invoice details before processing. • Maintain accurate customer and supplier records. • File sales documentation electronically and manually. • Prepare reports as required by management. Accounts & Collections- • Follow up on outstanding customer payments. • Send invoice copies and account statements to customers. • Assist with account reconciliations. • Work closely with the finance department regarding payment queries. • Maintain professional relationships while ensuring timely collections. Customer Service- • Build and maintain strong working relationships with buyers and suppliers. • Handle customer queries professionally and efficiently. • Communicate any supply shortages or changes promptly. • Provide excellent customer service at all times.
