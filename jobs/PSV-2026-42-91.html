About the role

The National School of Government is hiring a Director: Communications and Marketing, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. Job Purpose: To manage and provide effective communication, marketing, media, public relations, public participation and events management in fulfilling the National School of Government mandate. How to apply: Apply Using This Link: APPLICATION FOR EMPLOYMENT D: COMMUNICATIONS & MARKETING, REF NO: NSG: 21/2026. - Fill out form Reference number to quote: NSG 21/2026 Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for the following Key Results Areas: Develop effective communication and marketing strategies and tactics to communicate and market the NSG brand and programmes.
  • Manage and ensure proficient media and external stakeholder engagement.
  • Create and disseminate messages that convey the NSG's values, mission, and strategic goals to internal and external audiences.
  • Build relationships with media outlets to secure positive coverage and promote the NSG’s activities and education, training and development programmes and offerings.
  • Manage social media platforms to interact with clients, share updates, and address inquiries, fostering engagement and building a community.
  • Produce engaging content such as articles, blog posts, videos, and social media posts to connect with the target audience and provide value.
  • Lead the development of a unique brand identity and positioning in the market for the NSG (creating logos, taglines, and brand messaging that resonate with the target audience).
  • Lead the design and execution of promotional campaigns including exhibitions to generate awareness and interest in the products and services of the NSG.
  • Manage website content, public liaison, international communication strategies, plans and programmes.
  • Participate in relevant internal NSG governance structures to be up to date with upcoming events.
  • Participate in the national public liaison forums such as Government Communication and Information System (GCIS) structures and also engage with GCIS to utilize government platforms effectively.
  • Manage the resources (people, finance, systems, assets) allocated within the directorate.
  • Manage a team of employees and service providers who are expected to accomplish assigned duties efficiently, effectively, and competently and to strive for improvement and excellence in all work performed.

Requirements

  • A minimum bachelor’s degree or equivalent (NQF level 7) in communications, marketing, public relations, journalism or a related field.
  • Registration with a relevant professional body will also be an added advantage.
  • The successful applicant will be subjected to security vetting at an appropriate clearance level for senior managers.
  • Experience: 5 years’ experience at a middle/senior managerial level in a communication or marketing environment.
  • Knowledge: Knowledge in developing communication strategies tailored to different target audiences, platforms, and goals.
  • Knowledge of branding principles, including creating a unique brand identity, developing a brand positioning strategy, and maintaining brand consistency.
  • Understanding different advertising and communication channels, strategies, and tactic.
  • Knowledge of online marketing channels such as social media, email marketing, search engine optimisation (SEO), pay-per-click advertising, and analytics tools.
  • Understanding media relations management, social media platforms, their algorithms, engagement strategies, and tools for scheduling and monitoring.
  • Basic design knowledge to create visually appealing and effective marketing materials.
  • Knowledge and understanding of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa and public sector legislation.
