About the role

The Government Communications and Information System is hiring a State Accountant: Budget Office in Financial Management, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: Applicants must email their applications to recruitment@gcis.gov.za or hand deliver applications to Tshedimosetso House, 1035 cnr Francis Baard & Festival streets, Hatfield, Pretoria. For attention: Ms P Kgopyane Reference number to quote: 3/1/5/1-26/43 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: This post will be filled with a suitably qualified candidate whose appointment will assist the department in achieving its Employment Equity targets in terms of the Department’s Employment Equity Plan.

Responsibilities

  • Responsible for costing the staff establishment and compensation of employees as well as goods and services in accordance with Sections annual performance plans.
  • Do costing of the establishment to ensure the correct allocation of budgeted funds.
  • Provide support to the Budget Office in coordinating, consolidating and compiling weekly/monthly reports in respect of the budget and expenditure information.
  • The functions include amongst others, analysing monthly actual expenditure and projections against actual and planned outputs, determine cash flow drawings against planned outputs, analysing and costing Sections budget during the Medium Term Expenditure Framework (MTEF) and Estimates of National Expenditure (ENE), budget functions on the Basic Accounting System (BAS), etc, Monthly Projections: If required assist Sections with monthly projections to ensure that expenditure items reflect the correct allocation and that projections are in line with operational activities Advise where virement should be applied.
  • MTEF: Assist all Sections to cost their budget requirements during preparations for the MTEF submission.
  • Training
  • Guide Budget Office staff and Business Units in costing mechanisms
  • Assist Budget Office staff and Business Units how to cost activities in the APP and Sections’ operational plans
  • Campaigns/Projects/Aid assistance/Capital: Verify the expenditure trend of prioritised campaigns/projects and capital purchases and submit reports monthly to management committees.
  • Liaise with Branches and take into consideration budget and procurement planning such as the departmental demand and procurement plan, aid assistance projects, departmental prioritised campaigns and budget for capital assets.
  • Ensure the effective general handling of enquiries and overall assistance to the Assistant-Director/Deputy Director: Management Accounting.
  • Ensure compliance to the PFMA, Treasury Regulations, Financial Policies, Strategic Plan objectives and APP.
  • Ensure that all internal and external due dates, including those prescribed by National Treasury in line with the PFMA, are adhered to e.g. internal requests, budgeting schedule dates.
  • The successful candidate will also be the BAS System Controller which includes administering the BAS transversal system which include security procedures and User IDs.
  • Investigate and solve BAS related problems.
  • Involve IT and National Treasury when required.
  • Attend to all BAS user queries.
  • Monitor all users’ activities on the system monthly to ensure security and proper utilisation of the system.
  • Identify all BAS training requirements and schedule users for formal training or conduct BAS informal training to ensure that users are fully orientated on the system.
  • Create or terminate BAS user id’s and allocate BAS functions as per request and in line with users’ job functions, Reset users’ passwords when revoked.
  • Ensure that the Department closes its accounting month/financial year General Ledgers accounts timeously (as per National Treasury set dates) and without any exceptions.
  • In your area of responsibility, report any deviations to the Assistant-Director/Deputy Director: Management Accounting/Director: Financial Management.
  • Please detail courses passed in the CV as per the academic transcript.

Requirements

  • A National Diploma (NQF Level 6) as recognised by SAQA in Accounting/Cost and Management Accounting/Finance/Business Studies or related equivalent qualification.
  • At least 2 years’ relevant experience in budgeting, financial management is required.
  • Proven experience working with the Basic Accounting System (BAS) and a sound understanding of the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA), National Treasury Regulations, and other relevant legislative prescripts.
  • Computer literacy is essential.
  • Applicants must demonstrate the ability to work independently, perform effectively under pressure, and function as part of a team.
  • The incumbent must have good analytical, financial, and report-writing skills, strong attention to detail, excellent planning and organisational abilities, sound communication and interpersonal skills, proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, and the ability to interpret and apply financial policies, legislation, and treasury regulations.
