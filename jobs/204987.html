About the role

Faircape Health Tokai Estate is a Sub Acute Care and Rehabilitation Centre which provides restorative care and rehabilitation for patients recovering from a variety of medical conditions. We cater to patients in need of short-term, long-term, dementia, respite, and palliative care within a secure and nurturing environment. Our world-class care aims to bridge the gap between hospital and home. Our Multidisciplinary Team (MDT) of leading healthcare professionals administers care in the most therapeutic environment possible and supports healing outcomes in all aspects. We are seeking a Post Basic Pharmacist Assistant to join our team on a Fixed Term Contract, based at Tokai Estate in the Southern Suburbs. The Post Basic Pharmacist Assistant will be responsible for medication control and managing patients' prescriptions for Faircape’s healthcare facilities, under the supervision of the Responsible Pharmacist. Role Summary The successful candidate will support the Faircape Health pharmacy team by accurately preparing, dispensing, and distributing medication, while managing ward and emergency stock and supporting ordering and billing processes. This is a hands-on clinical role requiring strong attention to detail, adherence to regulatory standards, and the ability to work independently within strict deadlines. Key Responsibilities Prescription Preparation & Dispensing • Interpreting and preparing prescriptions • Repackaging and distributing medication • Ordering medicine and scheduled substances as instructed by the Responsible Pharmacist • Preparing, labelling, and dispensing medication in compliance with pharmacist-reviewed prescriptions • Alongside the Stock Controller and Admin Manager, managing and monitoring ward stock and emergency trolley supplies • Replenishing stock in emergency trolleys and cupboards as needed • Conducting weekly and monthly stock takes, reconciling actual versus system stock, and reporting discrepancies to the Responsible Pharmacist • Processing medication and consumables billing for sub-acute patients via Clinic Manager • When required, submitting medical aid claims through Propharm Requirements Qualifications and Registrations • Post Basic Pharmacist Assistant qualification • Registered with the South African Pharmacy Council (SAPC)Experience • Previous hospital experience will be advantageous • Previous experience with blister packaging would be advantageous • Previous experience with Propharm and Clinic Manager will be advantageous Skills and Attributes • Excellent customer service and communication skills • Knowledge of pharmaceutical terminology and medications • Ability to accurately dispense medications under supervision • Excellent attention to detail and strong organisational skills • Ability to handle confidential information with discretion • Proficiency in pharmacy software and computer systems • Understanding of pharmaceutical regulations and procedures • Team player with the ability to work collaboratively • Strong problem-solving abilities • Willingness to learn and adapt to new processes Specific Requirements • Clear health record • Clear criminal record and clear credit record • Written references Working Hours Normal working hours are Monday to Friday from 09h00 to 18h00, with 4 hours on alternate weekends. This position is offered on a Fixed Term Contract. We offer a competitive salary, dependent on qualifications, experience, and market-related factors.
