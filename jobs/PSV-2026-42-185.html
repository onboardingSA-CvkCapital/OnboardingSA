About the role

The Department of Science, Technology and Innovation is hiring a Director: Innovation Partnerships and Sustainable Ecosystems, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Applications must be emailed to dipse@dsti.gov.za Reference number to quote: DSTI 2026/21 Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic leadership and guidance with respect to the implementation of the functional framework for enabling innovation to technology innovation support policy instruments.
  • Ensure strategic management, engagement and coordination of the innovation ecosystem.
  • Strategic business and market intelligence gathering, analysis and dissemination.
  • Provide strategic management, including people management and empowerment and financial management of the allocated budget.

Requirements

  • A Bachelor's Degree (NQF level 7 qualification) in economics, technology management or a related field, or an equivalent qualification.
  • At least 5 years' experience at middle or senior management level within government policy processes and implementation environment.
  • Knowledge and understanding of the national system of innovation and international innovation systems analysis.
  • Practice in innovation instrument design, corporate governance and management.
  • Knowledge of government policy and systems.
  • Knowledge and understanding of policy analysis and implementation.
  • Knowledge and understanding of Public Service Act, 1994, and Regulations, and Public Finance Management Act, 1999, and other related legislation.
  • Strategic capability and leadership skills.
  • Programme and project management skills.
  • Financial and change management skills.
  • People management and empowerment skills.
  • Problem-solving skills, policy development and interpretation skills.
  • Writing, presentation and verbal communication skills.
  • Managerial and negotiation skills.
  • Research, analytical and events management skills.
  • Ability to network for the benefit of the Department.
  • Ability to work under pressure, individually and in a team.
  • Ability to articulate ideas.
  • Diplomatic and a strategic thinker.
  • Innovative and creative.
