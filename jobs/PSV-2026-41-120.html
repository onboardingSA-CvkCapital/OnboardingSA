About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring a Senior Admin Officer: Acquisition Management (SCM), based in Cape Town Regional Office. This is a permanent post, paying R413,002 a year. (Re-advertisement of PSVC 16 OF 2026, Ref No: 2026/201C, people who previously applied are required to re-apply) How to apply: Email Applications for the position of Senior Admin Officer: Acquisition Management (SCM) must be submitted to: RecruitCPT26-72@dpw.gov.za Reference numbers to quote: 2026/392, 2026/201C, people who Closing date: 27 November 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide admin support in the research and needs analysis for the Department.
  • Provide guidance to End-Users/Project Managers on testing the markets.
  • Lead the process of alignment of budgets with department objectives and Annual Performance Plans.
  • Consolidation of Procurement Plans and report monthly to Head Office.
  • Assist in the consultation with relevant stakeholders to determine challenges, opportunities and risks.
  • Collect and collate information for the Department Annual Procurement Plan, collect information from the relevant units according to the prescribed template.
  • Consolidate the information into Procurement Plan.
  • Provide support in the process of drafting specifications, advice whether specifications should contain special conditions.
  • Assist in identifying and implementation of preference points system, track all movements of specification and terms of reference submission.
  • Provide an appropriate response to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Ensure the effective management of Demand Management, Acquisition Management and Contract Management processes.
  • Coordinate input in the Demand Management Plan.
  • Ensure that procurement is in line with the approved Demand Plan.
  • Assist in compilation of Terms of reference, Specifications and Bid Documents.
  • Lead the tender/bid briefing sessions.
  • Review tender documents before placing advert to relevant media platforms.
  • Serve as member of Bid Specification Committee and Bid Evaluation Committee.
  • Provide secretariat support to the Bid Adjudication committee.
  • Monitoring of validity period of closed tenders.
  • Co-ordination and execution of the bidding process.
  • Arrange and attend bid Evaluation committee meeting.
  • Management of the bid processes and ensure effective functioning of all Bid Committees.
  • Management and effective utilisation of Transversal Contracts and Participation contracts.
  • Manage the process of sourcing of quotations.
  • Drafting of appointment letters and inform bidders of the outcome of the bid after the finalization of the bidding process.
  • Ensure awarded bids are published and update Contract Register.
  • Ensure proper document control and documentations.
  • Implement and Oversee Risk and performance management strategies in the supply chain management unit.
  • Identify and mitigate risks within the unit.
  • Ensure that all administrative matters, correspondence and complex enquiries are attended to promptly.
  • Compiling of monthly SCM performance reports.
  • Compilation /verification of all monthly SCM reports.
  • Ensure implementation and compliance of the Instruction Notes as issued by National Treasury as well as Departmental SCM Policy.
  • Ensuring performance development and performance management of staff.

Requirements

  • At least three-year tertiary qualification (NQF Level 06)/National Diploma in Supply Chain Management/Purchasing Management/Public Administration/Procurement or related qualification.
  • Relevant working experience in Supply Chain Management within Acquisition Unit (Tenders).
  • Coaching and Mentoring.
  • Experience in Construction or Infrastructure Procurement will be an added advantage.
  • Valid Driver’s licence.
  • Knowledge of Supply Chain Management Framework, procurement related legislation and regulations, including the Public Finance Management Act, Code of Conduct for Supply Chain Management Practitioners, Treasury Regulations and Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act, CIDB Act, CIDB Regulations and CIDB Practice Notes and Guides.
  • Understanding government procurement systems and processes within the built environment is essential.
  • Good verbal and written communication skills, the ability to manage confidential information, advanced interpersonal and negotiation skills, problem solving skills, decision making skills and computer literacy are critical competency areas.
  • The ability to work and deliver under pressure and within tight time frames is essential.
  • Prepared to travel, willing to adapt work schedule in accordance with office requirements.
