About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring a Medical Officers, based in Dr Pixley Ka Seme Memorial Hospital. This is a permanent post, paying R1,001,349 to R1,041. How to apply: ALL APPLICATIONS SHOULD BE EMAILED TO: Pixley.recruitment@kznhealth.gov.za Or Hand delivered to 310 Bhejane Street (Hospital Gate Number 2) Kwamashu Reference number to quote: DPISMH 32/2026 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  •  Provision of critical care services as determined by the Head of Critical Care at Dr Pixley Ka Isaka Seme Memorial Hospital.  Clinical duties as per hospital / departmental requirements, in- and outside of the Department of Critical Care  Mandatory participation in Group 3 commuted overtime.  Participate in the delivery of Critical Care services for the PINK area (Pheonix, Inanda, Ntuzuma and KwaMashu) and other hospitals outside of this area that require critical care services.  Provide initial resuscitative and peri-operative care of patients while maintaining the scientific principles of critical care.  Maintain and continuously improve professional and ethical standards of yourself and the Dept of Critical Care through Continued Professional Development activities.  Ensure sound labour relations in compliance with the relevant legislation while maintaining the interest of the patients.  To provide quality care by participating in clinical audit programmes, quality assurance, quality improvement projects, patient safety incident reporting, morbidity and mortality reviews when requested.  To assist in outreach programmes in the PINK area.  To assist the HCU with administrative and management duties of critical care services  To provide teaching and training for nurses, junior staff, interns and medical students when required.  To develop an interest in research in critical care  To be able to network with other Critical Care Units

Requirements

  • GRADE 1: Appropriate qualification that allows registration with HPCSA as Medical Practitioner.
  • GRADE 2: Appropriate qualification that allows registration with HPCSA as Medical Practitioner with a minimum of five (05) years appropriate experience as Medical Officer after registration with the HPCSA as Medical Practitioner.
  • GRADE 3: Appropriate qualification that allows registration with the HPCSA as Medical Practitioner with a minimum experience of 10 years appropriate Experience as Medical Practitioner.
  •  Senior Certificate /Grade 12 or equivalent  An appropriate tertiary qualification (MBChB)  Registration certificate with HPCSA as an Independent Medical Practitioner  Current registration with HPCSA as an Independent Medical Practitioner (2026/2027)
  •  Resuscitation courses: BLS, ACLS, PALS or APLS, ATLS, ITLS, BASIC for Doctors.  Applications with ultrasound experience: Any valid ultrasound course.  Applicants with experience working in an intensive care unit.  Applicants who have completed any CMSA Diploma.  Applicants who have passed a CMSA Primary or Intermediate exam.  Applicants who have completed registrar training in a clinical discipline.  Applicants who can commence duty immediately.  Unendorsed valid code B drivers' license (Code 08).  Currently employed applicants: Certificate of Service from current employer endorsed by Human Resource Department will be required from shortlisted candidates.
  •  Knowledge and skills in managing critically ill patients.  Sound communication, planning, negotiation, organising and interpersonal skills.  Development of management and leadership skills.  Ability to work in a multi-disciplinary team setting.  Ability to work and maintain meaningful relationships in a diverse community.  Ability to develop resilience and coping mechanisms in a resource limited environment.  Ability to make a positive contribution in a busy hospital and community.
