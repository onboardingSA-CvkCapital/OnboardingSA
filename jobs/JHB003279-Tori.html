About the role

The Maintenance Assistant is responsible for supporting the maintenance team in ensuring that all hotel, lodge, or resort facilities, equipment, and infrastructure are maintained to the highest standards. This role assists with preventative maintenance, repairs, inspections, and general upkeep to provide a safe, functional, and welcoming environment for guests and staff while minimising operational downtime.Key ResponsibilitiesAssist with preventative maintenance programmes for buildings, guest rooms, public areas, and staff facilities.Carry out general repairs, including basic plumbing, electrical, carpentry, painting, and masonry work.Perform routine inspections of facilities and report maintenance issues promptly.Respond to maintenance requests from departments in a timely and professional manner.Assist with repairing and maintaining HVAC systems, lighting, water systems, and general equipment where applicable.Conduct daily checks of generators, pumps, boilers, water treatment systems, and other operational equipment.Ensure guest rooms and public areas are maintained to the highest presentation and safety standards.Maintain swimming pools, gardens, pathways, and outdoor facilities where required.Assist with the setup and maintenance of conference rooms, events, and special functions.Ensure tools, equipment, and maintenance workshops are clean, organised, and secure.Monitor stock levels of maintenance supplies and report shortages.Assist contractors and service providers during maintenance projects and scheduled servicing.Follow health, safety, environmental, and fire regulations at all times.Complete maintenance records, job cards, and inspection reports accurately.Support emergency maintenance repairs and be available for after-hours call-outs when required. Minimum Qualifications • Grade 12 (Matric) or equivalent. • Trade certificate or vocational qualification in Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, Mechanical, or Building Maintenance (advantageous). •
