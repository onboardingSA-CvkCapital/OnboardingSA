About the role

SAB is hiring an AI & Analytics Lead, based in Sandton. The key purpose of the AI & Analytics Lead is to lead cross-functional jetski teams that identify, shape, deliver and scale high-value AI, automation and analytics solutions across the Africa business. As the problem owner for assigned initiatives, the role is accountable for defining the business problem, aligning stakeholders, coordinating delivery and ensuring that solutions achieve measurable adoption and value. The position combines business consulting, analytics, solution design, delivery leadership and change adoption. It requires a strong ability to translate ambiguity into structured business problems, define practical analytical approaches, coordinate technical delivery and ensure that outputs are adopted by users and embedded into business routines. The role is expected to be hands-on where appropriate: building early analysis, prototypes, dashboards, prompts, workflows, process documentation or decision-support tools, while knowing when to involve data engineering, architecture, AI engineering or automation specialists for scalable delivery.

Responsibilities

  • Problem Framing and Opportunity Discovery
  • Partner with business leaders and SMEs to understand business challenges, operational pain points and decision-making needs.
  • Facilitate structured discovery sessions to translate broad or ambiguous requests into clear problem statements, hypotheses and success criteria.
  • Identify where AI, analytics, automation, process redesign or data products can create measurable value.
  • Assess opportunities using business impact, urgency, complexity, feasibility, data readiness and adoption potential.
  • Develop concise opportunity briefs, value cases and delivery recommendations for product leadership review.
  • Jetski Leadership and Delivery Coordination
  • Lead the assigned jetski as the day-to-day problem lead, ensuring business context is understood and delivery remains focused on outcomes.
  • Coordinate input from business SMEs, data engineering, AI / automation engineering, architecture support and product management.
  • Maintain clarity on scope, milestones, dependencies, assumptions, decisions, risks and issues.
  • Translate business needs into requirements, user stories, process maps, acceptance criteria and test scenarios.
  • Support rapid delivery cycles from discovery through proof of concept, pilot, deployment, adoption and handover.
  • Analytics, AI and Automation Solution Shaping
  • Develop or support early-stage analysis, prototypes, dashboards, prompt flows, workflow concepts and decision-support tools.
  • Work with technical team members to shape practical solution options that balance value, speed, simplicity, governance and scalability.
  • Validate outputs with SMEs and users to ensure analytical logic, business rules and recommendations are fit for purpose.
  • Contribute to the design of AI and automation solutions by documenting use cases, user journeys, prompts, controls, exception logic, reporting needs and adoption requirements.
  • Ensure that solutions are understandable, usable and embedded into business routines rather than remaining as isolated technical deliverables.
  • Stakeholder Engagement, Adoption and Change
  • Act as the bridge between business users, technical specialists and product leadership.
  • Communicate complex analytical or technical concepts in clear, practical business language.
  • Prepare stakeholder updates, storytelling packs, training material, user guides and adoption support where required.
  • Drive user testing, feedback loops, adoption tracking, and continuous improvement after deployment.
  • Build trust with business teams by demonstrating pace, pragmatism, business empathy, and delivery discipline.
  • Value Realisation and Continuous Improvement
  • Define the expected business outcome, leading indicators, adoption indicators and benefit logic for each assigned initiative.
  • Track realised or evidenced value in partnership with business owners and product leadership.
  • Complete post-implementation reviews to capture lessons learned, scale opportunities and reusable assets.
  • Identify opportunities to replicate successful solutions across markets, functions, or business processes.
  • Contribute to the development of repeatable methods, templates and ways of working for the Africa Analytics & AI capability.
  • Key Attributes and Competencies
  • Problem Solving - Structures ambiguous business problems into clear questions, hypotheses, options and next actions.
  • Prioritisation - Balances value, urgency, feasibility and effort when shaping jetski work.
  • Curiosity - Explores new AI, analytics and automation possibilities without losing business focus.
  • Agility - Moves quickly between topics, functions and delivery stages while maintaining quality.
  • Excellence - Agility - Moves quickly between topics, functions and delivery stages while maintaining quality.
  • Resilience - Maintains momentum through ambiguity, shifting priorities, feedback and blockers.
  • Boldness - Challenges unclear requests, proposes practical options and drives action.
  • Collaboration - Works effectively across business, product and technical teams.
  • Develop - Shares methods, templates and lessons learnt to raise capability across the team.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Science, Economics, Statistics, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Engineering, Computer Science, Behavioural Science, Visual Communication or a related field.
  • 5+ years in analytics, insights, business analysis, strategy, process improvement, digital transformation or related roles.
  • Demonstrated experience translating business problems into analytical approaches, solution requirements or decision-support tools.
  • Experience working directly with business stakeholders and cross-functional delivery teams.
  • Experience managing multiple priorities, ambiguous requests and tight turnaround work.
  • Strong evidence of structured problem-solving, stakeholder communication and practical implementation.
