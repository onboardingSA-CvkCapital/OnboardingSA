About the role

The Office of the Premier (Eastern Cape) is hiring a Head of Department: Department of Agriculture, based in Bhisho. This is a contract post, paying R2,446,749 to R2,756,241 a year. (Five-Year Contract) How to apply: Applications must be submitted online through the provincial e-Recruitment System accessible at: https://ecprov.gov.za (under Careers) and/or at https://erecruitment.ecotp.gov.za (to view jobs without logging-in, click: Jobs). The Provincial e-Recruitment System is available 24/7 and closes at 23: 59 on the Closing Date. To report any e-Recruitment System challenges send an email with your (i) ID Number, (ii) profile email address, (iii) details of your issue to: HODrecruitment@ecotp.gov.za. Do not send any applications to this email address; should you do so, your applications/ CV will be regarded as lost and will not be considered. Technical support is limited to working hours: (08:00- 16:30 Mon-Thursday and 08:00-16:00 on Friday). Direct all enquiries to the specified enquiries person or email Address. Reference number to quote: OTP-DOA01/11/2026 Closing date: 04 December 2026. Applications received after closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Persons with Disabilities are encouraged to apply

Responsibilities

  • Provide Strategic Leadership, Governance and Executive Management of the Department: Provide strategic policy advice to the MEC.
  • Align the departmental planning to the Provincial Development Plan (Vision 2030), Medium-Term Development Plan, National Development Plan and other applicable national and provincial priorities.
  • Oversee integrated intra-and interdepartmental planning, collaboration and delivery of agricultural programmes and services.
  • Lead creation of an enabling environment for effective service delivery through mobilisation and utilisation internal and external resources.
  • Promote inclusive and sustainable agricultural productivity, food security, agro-processing and commercialisation.
  • Oversee implementation of Executive Council resolutions.
  • Serve as Accounting Officer in terms of the PFMA.
  • Provide Strategic Oversight of Agricultural Development, Producer Support and District Service Delivery: Control the effective implementation of sustainable resources.
  • Lead the implementation of Agricultural producer support and development.
  • Oversee the effective coordination of district services and Agricultural implementation.
  • Provide Strategic Oversight of Agricultural Regulatory, Biosecurity and Technical Services: Lead the effective provision of veterinary services.
  • Direct the provision of agricultural economics and commodity support services.
  • Direct the coordination of research technology development and transfer services.
  • Lead the adoption of modern technologies and best agricultural practices.
  • Oversee the Provisioning of Sound Financial Management Services: Control the provision of management accounting services.
  • Control the provision of integrated financial accounting services.
  • Oversee effective internal controls - ensuring the development of internal control tools and systems.
  • Oversee the provision of supply chain management and related services Monitor the provision of Asset Management services.
  • Oversee efficient, economical and effective utilisation of resources.
  • Oversee compliance with PFMA and Treasury Regulations.
  • Oversee the Provisioning of Strategic Planning, Systems, Human Resource Development and Management: Oversee the effective implementation of strategy and systems services.
  • Oversee the coordination of organisational development and service delivery improvement.
  • Oversee the coordination of government information and communication technology and digital transformation.
  • Oversee the promotion of sound and relevant strategic communication and customer care management services.
  • Provide strategic guidance on Human Resource Administration, planning and systems.
  • Lead the provision of Human Resource Utilisation and development programmes.
  • Oversee the provision of security and work environment services.
  • Provide Strategic Leadership for Corporate Governance, Ethics, Compliance and Accountability: Oversee compliance with the Constitution, applicable legislation, government policies and governance frameworks, including PAIA, PAJA and other statutory prescripts.
  • Oversee the effective functioning of internal audit, internal control systems and governance assurance mechanisms.
  • Lead the implementation of enterprise risk management, ethics, anti-corruption, fraud prevention and integrity management.
  • Direct implementation of integrated legal services strategy within the department.
  • Oversee the implementation of the Minimum Information Security Standards (MISS) and overall departmental information security.
  • Oversee facilitation and the implementation of special programmes.
  • Executive Leadership Responsibilities.
  • Monitor International and Regional Integration Programmes and Commitments: Promote intergovernmental relations and integrated governance.
  • Provide strategic leadership for Provincial transversal programmes.
  • Provide strategic leadership on intergovernmental relations across all spheres of government.
  • Account to the Provincial Legislature and its Committees on departmental performance and governance.
  • Support the Executive Council through implementation of Cabinet decisions and provision of strategic advice to the Executive Authority.
  • Promote strategic stakeholder management, partnerships and public participation.
  • Promote effective media engagement and public accountability.
  • Provide strategic leadership during disasters, emergencies and agricultural biosecurity incidents.
  • Champion climate resilience and adaptation initiatives within the agricultural sector.
  • Promote international cooperation and strategic partnerships that advance the Department's mandate.
  • Organizational Performance.
  • Oversee the development, implementation and review of the Departmental Strategic Imperatives.
  • Oversee implementation and monitoring of policy priorities as articulated in the Departmental Policy Speech.
  • Promote sound stakeholder relationships and participation.
  • Coordinate overall management of the Department.

Requirements

  • Matric/Grade 12 (Senior Certificate), National Qualifications Framework (NQF) Level 8 qualification as recognised by South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA), in Agriculture, Agricultural Economics, Veterinary Science, Development Studies, Public Policy, Public Administration, Management or Administration.
  • Successful completion of the Nyukela Pre-Entry SMS Certificate.
  • A valid Driving Licence with at least 10 years of experience at senior managerial level (including at least 3 years at the executive management level) preferably within the rural development or agrarian environment.
  • Professional registration with a relevant institution as well as experience within the agriculture sector or rural development would be added advantages.
  • The suitable candidate will be visionary, stable, innovative, and excellence-oriented and a developmental administrator whose predisposition is to serve the poorest of the poor with distinction and empathy and to lead the department on a path of sustained continuous improvement.
