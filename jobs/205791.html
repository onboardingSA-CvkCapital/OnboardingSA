About the role

🌟 VACANCY: PROJECTS ENGINEER 🌟 Ligpos Employment Agency invites qualified candidates to apply for the following position at one of our key clients: Position Details • Job Title: Projects Engineer • Work Area: Head Office – Projects Department • Job Grade: D1-3 • Remuneration / Salary: Market-Related (Negotiable based on experience and qualifications) • Effective Date: October 2026 Overall Purpose of the Position • Work closely with project managers, administrators & document controllers/expeditors to ensure projects’ objectives are met. • Provide technical support to the Senior Projects Manager, approaching projects from a technical, engineering perspective. • Manage all the engineering and technical aspects of a project, from conception to completion. • Oversee the entire project lifecycle, from initial planning and design to implementation and final delivery. • Plan, execute, and complete projects on time, within budget, and with high quality. • Coordinate with various teams, managing schedules, ensuring adherence to quality standards, and providing technical guidance to team members. Organisation Relationships • Direct Superior: Senior Projects Manager • Direct Subordinates: Junior Projects Engineer/Manager; Project Administrator; Document Controller Incumbent Specification (Minimum Requirements) Minimum Entry-Level Qualifications: • Academic: Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering or a related field. • Other: • Three to five years of engineering experience. • Master’s degree in engineering or project management is an advantage. • Registered Pr. Eng. (ECSA) is an advantage. Essential Entry-Level Knowledge: • Technical Knowledge: A solid foundation in the technical aspects of engineering — knowledge of engineering principles, concepts, and practices. • Communication: Effective verbal and written communication; ability to communicate complex technical information to team members, stakeholders, and clients. • Analytical & Problem-Solving Skills: Strong ability to analyze and interpret data, identify trends, make informed decisions, and solve problems independently and as part of a team. • Time Management & Leadership: Excellent time management, task prioritization, and strong leadership skills to manage a team and provide clear guidance. • Software Proficiency: Proficient in software such as Inventor, Solid Edge, SolidWorks, project management software, MS Office Package, and MS Projects. • Quality & Safety Systems (SHERQ): Experience with workplace safety and quality management requirements (specifically ISO 9001:2015). Performance Deliverables • Project Planning: Develop detailed project plans, schedules, timelines, budgets, and resource allocations (personnel, equipment, materials). Align project plans with strategic objectives (MPT’s Project 500 Strategic Plan and SHERQ Policy). • Design and Engineering: Ensure projects meet technical specifications and quality standards. Review and approve engineering drawings and plans. Resolve technical issues and implement ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems. • Resource Management: Ensure resources are allocated and used efficiently to meet timelines and budgets. Oversee hiring, resource tracking, and procurement of equipment/materials. • Risk Management: Identify potential project risks and develop mitigation/contingency plans. Monitor compliance with applicable codes, QA/QC policies, performance standards, and specifications. • Communication: Coordinate with project managers to provide regular updates to stakeholders and clients. Facilitate discussions to keep all stakeholders aligned on project goals. • Documentation: Compile Quality Control Plans (QCPs) and maintain the project Databook. Write OEM equipment manuals and ensure technical datasheets and certificates are in place. • Client Management: Compile project schedules/reports, track items behind schedule, and communicate operational updates effectively to clients. • Team Management: Monitor fabrication and assembly in the workshop to ensure compliance with standards. Assign work, provide guidance, and ensure team adherence to project deadlines. • Safety, Health, Environmental, Risk & Quality (SHERQ): Adhere to SHERQ policies, protect personal and team safety, wear required protective gear, and report safety risks promptly. Active participation in the SHERQ Management System. • Budget Oversight: Oversee project budgets, track actual spending against allowances, direct resource allocation, and manage contract/budget changes. • Reporting and Documentation: Compile and present progress/solution reports to stakeholders. Maintain comprehensive project documentation, process maps, and diagrams. • General: Execute additional reasonable tasks within your expertise as instructed by management. Client-related tasks take priority.
