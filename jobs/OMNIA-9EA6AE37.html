About the role

Omnia is hiring a Cost Accountant in Omnia Agriculture (Nutriology®), SSB Finance, based in Sasolburg. The role reports to the Inventory and Costing Manager. The Cost Accountant is responsible for ensuring effective stock control, maintaining of pricing process and variance analysis, by applying the techniques of standard costing specific to the requirements of a manufacturing concern, thereby ensuring accuracy of information provided for informed decision making by stakeholders.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor and maintain effective inventory control processes to ensure accuracy, reliability and compliance across manufacturing operations.
  • Provide key stakeholders with accurate product cost of sales information based on approved monthly standard costs to support strategic and operational decision-making.
  • Analyse financial and operational information to identify abnormal cost variances and investigate root causes.
  • Maintain and update Bills of Materials (BOMs) to ensure product costing accuracy within financial and operational systems.
  • Manage monthly updates of cost prices and transport rates to ensure current and accurate costing information.
  • Provide inventory and pricing support to decentralised business units, ensuring effective resolution of queries and operational challenges.
  • Support the Management Accountant by assisting with finance-related business unit queries and management reporting requirements.
  • Execute month-end close activities, ensuring all costing, inventory and reconciliation processes are completed accurately and within required deadlines.
  • Support year-end financial close activities and audit requirements through the timely completion of reporting and reconciliations.
  • Perform detailed reconciliations to validate transactions, identify discrepancies and strengthen financial control processes.
  • Participate in monthly and bi-monthly stock counts, ensuring inventory accuracy and compliance with stock management procedures.
  • Attend bi-annual maintenance spares counts and verify inventory records through the review and sign-off of stock documentation.
  • Investigate incorrect capturing of raw material codes and collaborate with relevant plant managers to implement corrective actions.
  • Monitor Goods-In-Transit balances and proactively resolve variances to maintain inventory integrity.
  • Prepare stock reports, providing commentary and insights on abnormal costs, variances and inventory trends.
  • Identify and investigate usage variances and losses, validating findings with relevant operational and plant management teams.
  • Ensure manufacturing work orders are accurately closed and maintain reporting of outstanding work orders at month-end.
  • Maintain the accuracy of standard costs and manufacturing recovery rates to support reliable financial reporting.
  • Analyse Purchase Price Variances (PPVs), assess reasonability and provide recommendations for corrective action where required.
  • Support budgeting activities by preparing manufacturing expense budgets and accurately calculating labour and overhead recovery rates.
  • Prepare manufacturing expenses and manufacturing recoveries templates for budget uploads and reporting processes.
