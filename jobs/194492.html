About the role

MAIN PURPOSE OF POSITION: The Commercial Internal Account Manager is responsible for managing and developing reseller relationships, driving revenue growth across allocated commercial accounts, and delivering exceptional customer service. This role supports the external sales team through proactive account management, sales administration, opportunity management, and effective partner engagement to achieve business objectives. Commercial Internal Account Manager Job Duties and Responsibilities Customer Success & Account Management • Regular telephonic contact with all partners • Assists and backs up account managers on a certain task. • Call partners to discuss current Brand activities and create brand awareness. • Influences customers to buy products by following a prepared sales talk to give service and product information. • Complete spreadsheets by recording names, addresses, dates called, and comments. • Quotes should be sent to partners within a 2-hour SLA. • Maintains operations by following policies and procedures; and reporting needed changes. • Contributes to team effort by accomplishing related results as needed. • Sales Certifications are to be done and maintained on the Dell brand. • Completing all Spiv & Campaign requirements on the given deadlines • Weekly KPI feedback sessions that include feedback on customer engagements, pipeline, quotes generated, closed and lost deals, e-mails sent, etc. 200 calls per month. • Attend training sessions when requested • Driving revenue targets through allocated accounts and Dell product sets in commercial accounts. • Driving resellers to enable the expansion of the FD Hardware brand portfolio within existing and potential new accounts. • Deliver first-class customer service. • Regular follow-ups on all Dell quotes and opportunitiesSales Administration • Ensure client mailing list details are updated at all times. • Request and evaluate quotes for submission internally. • Follow up and validate quotes. • Follow up on operations responses. • Manage SLA with brand operations. • Ensure CRM is always up to date and maintain CRM hygiene.
