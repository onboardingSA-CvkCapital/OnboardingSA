About the role

Are you passionate about helping students achieve their educational and career goals? As a Student Advisor, you will play a vital role in guiding prospective students through their higher education journey. You will engage with enquiries, build meaningful relationships with prospective students, provide expert advice on our programmes, and assist applicants through every stage of the enrolment process. Your ability to understand students' aspirations, match them with the right qualification, and deliver an exceptional student experience will contribute directly to achieving enrolment targets. Beyond recruitment, you will represent NewBridge Graduate Institute at open days, school visits, career exhibitions and promotional events, while maintaining accurate records and delivering excellent customer service. You will work closely with academic and support teams to ensure every prospective student receives professional guidance and a seamless admissions experience. If you are passionate about education, thrive in a target-driven environment, enjoy building relationships, and want to make a meaningful difference in students' lives while being part of a dynamic and growing institution, we would love to hear from you.
