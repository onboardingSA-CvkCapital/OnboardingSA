About the role

FET English Home Language Teacher Grades 8 – 12 | Radley Private School | Randburg, Johannesburg Full-time | On-site | SACE Registered Job purpose Radley Private School is looking for a caring, experienced and enthusiastic FET-qualified English Home Language educator who is passionate about developing confident communicators, critical thinkers and independent young adults. At Radley, education extends beyond academic achievement. We combine strong academics with entrepreneurial thinking, creativity, problem-solving and real-world learning. Our smaller classes enable educators to understand their learners as individuals and provide the focused support and challenge each learner needs to progress. The successful candidate will be responsible for planning and delivering engaging English Home Language lessons across the FET Phase, with a strong focus on Grades 10–12, IEB assessment requirements and examination preparation. The educator will develop learners’ competence and confidence across literature, language, comprehension, writing, oral communication and critical analysis. The role includes lesson preparation, assessment, evaluation and reporting on learner academic progress, intervention where learning gaps are identified, and comprehensive academic record keeping in accordance with school, IEB, Department of Education and legislative requirements. We are looking for an educator who wants to make a meaningful impact in a deliberately small, progressive and entrepreneurial school environment.
