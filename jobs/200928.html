About the role

Introduction Grain Field Chickens, a subsidiary of VKB Agriculture (PTY) Ltd, is a dynamic role player in the integrated broiler industry with an abattoir in Reitz in the North-Eastern Free State. We offer you the opportunity to establish yourself within a professional, corporate environment in the countryside. Job Description To engage in operating a power-driven mobile hoist/ reach truck with a lifting capacity exceeding 6 000 kg used in the loading, unloading, moving or stacking of goods and/ or pallets, but does not include an internal motor vehicle in alignment with predefined work objectives (as per agreed standard operating procedures). Job Requirements • Grade 12 or NQF Level 4 Equivalent. • Valid Reach Truck Operator Licence/Certificate – R/T Code F5 AM (1600kg). • 2–3 years' reach truck operating experience in a warehouse, logistics, or manufacturing environment. • Good understanding of warehouse operations and inventory movement. • Exposure to loud noise, extreme temperatures, dust, moving machinery and production equipment. • Must be physically fit (frequent lifting, bending and standing). • Must be able and willing to work overtime including shifts and weekends when required.Duties and Responsibilities • Operate a reach truck safely and efficiently to move raw materials, finished goods, and stock between production, storage, and dispatch areas in accordance with company policies and health and safety regulations. • Load, unload, move, stack, and retrieve pallets from high-level racking systems and designated warehouse locations securely and safely. • Replenish picking locations and retail floor stock as required. • Ensure all loads are handled carefully, ae secure, balanced, and transported without damage. • Accurately receive, locate, and store incoming goods. • Assist team members in picking, staging, loading, unloading, rotation, and storage of product. • Assist with stock control/ rotation, cycle counts and inventory audits. • Assist to repack sales orders and loading orders. • Verify stock quantities and report discrepancies immediately. • Rotate stock according to FIFO (First In, First Out) principles where applicable. • Conduct daily pre-operation inspection of reach truck and completion of reach truck checklist before start of shift and report defects or maintenance requirements. • Maintain cleanliness and organization of warehouse aisles, storage locations, and work areas. • Assist truck driver/s by ensuring that vehicles are loaded and unloaded in a timely manner. • Adhere to all health and safety procedures and regulation and warehouse operating standards at all times. • Wear required PPE at all times. • Complete all relevant documentation accurately. • Report accidents, near misses, hazards, damaged stock, shrinkage, and unsafe conditions immediately as per company policy. • Perform any other duties as may be required by management.Skills and abilities required • Safe reach truck operating practices. • Warehouse and dispatch procedures. • Stock handling and inventory control. • Loading and unloading techniques. • Health and Safety Act requirements. • Basic inspection and preventative maintenance checks. • Ability to read delivery notes, picking slips, and stock documentation. • Good communication and teamwork skills. • Attention to detail and accuracy. • Time management and organizational skills. • High-pressure environment with
