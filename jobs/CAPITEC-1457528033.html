About the role

Capitec is hiring a Company Secretary, based in Stellenbosch. To provide technical expertise and advice on the Companies Act and other relevant laws, for respective business entities, establish mechanisms and processes to guide and ensure adherence to all statutory requirements and to support the Group Company Secretariat team with the execution of corporate secretarial functions across the respective entities as required.

Responsibilities

  • Delivery of Corporate Secretarial Services
  • Effective execution of the operation of board and board committee meetings
  • Effective execution of the operation of executive and executive sub-committee meetings
  • Maintain accurate company registers and records
  • Custodian of/responsible for effective organisational corporate governance policies and processes
  • Stakeholder management
  • Delivery of Corporate Secretarial Services
  • Secretarial Services
  • Support the Group Company Secretariat team in the discharge of the statutory duties imposed on a Company Secretary as set out in the Companies Act as well as further duties as contained in King IV, and other relevant laws (as amended).
  • Embed regulatory requirements (imposed through existing and new legislation) in the relevant entities’ board processes.
  • Advise all directors and members of management within entities on the Companies Act and best practice corporate governance (including adherence to regulatory governance requirements), adhering to centralised standards and agreed upon processes, and being proactive in addressing these matters in all instances.
  • Ensure compliance with the relevant entity-based Memorandum of Incorporation through executing measurements and controls.
  • Support appropriate board training, including regular technical updates as well as development plans in terms of fit and proper requirements.
  • Maintain an appropriate and up to date induction file for directors.
  • Execute and guide processes relating to the approval, documentation, and governance of policies across the group.
  • Compliance with regulatory environment
  • Establish and manage the implementation and maintenance of practices, processes and routines that will deliver regulatory compliance.
  • Ensure the execution of the practices, processes, and actions to meet requirements per entity, to standard and on time:
  • Prudential Authority
  • Prepare and submit, subject to the authorised Company Secretary approval, the statutory applications/reports/correspondence in terms of the relevant entities’ Act and related regulations.
  • Ensure the maintenance of the records of all correspondence with the Prudential Authority.
  • Ensure the maintenance of key administrative information.
  • Compile and submit reports to the Prudential Authority as required.
  • Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC)
  • Responsible for the timeous and accurate completion, submission and filing of statutory forms prescribed by the Companies Act and Regulations, including iXBRL reports, compliance reports and beneficial ownership information, as applicable.
  • Corporate governance
  • Remain up to date with, and conduct research into best practice, developments, and changes in corporate governance to:
  • Develop and execute a compliance framework for relevant entity, to be approved by the authorised Company Secretary
  • Execute the statutory implementation, review, and oversight of the King Code.
  • Monitor the maintenance and relevance of policies.
  • Monitor and ensure compliance with the memoranda of incorporation of companies falling in the purview of the relevant entities.
  • Monitor the alignment and reflection of the corporate governance framework and practices of the relevant entities to best corporate governance practices and the King Code register.
  • Perform an annual review of the board and board committee/sub-committee charters to ensure that they remain relevant and up to date.
  • Policy and procedure maintenance and management
  • Ensure that proper processes and procedures are in place to facilitate appropriate administration of policies and procedures and that these documents are approved and maintained in accordance with best corporate governance practices.
  • Execute the operation of board and board committee meetings
  • Work with the Group Company Secretariat team and set up board / board committee meetings in line with business calendars and priorities (e.g., Board and AGM meetings
  • audit, risk & capital management meetings well as management committees, e.g., Insurance Exco, Risk Committee, ALCO, PACC and relevant ad hoc meetings) and ensure appropriate and desired outcome.
  • Implement and maintain annual work plans for the Board and board committees.
  • Implement and maintain effective board meeting processes, including consultative agenda setting, compilation of relevant board meeting papers and delivery of accurate records of the various meetings for relevant entities and subsidiaries.
  • Prepare, coordinate, and facilitate the distribution of board/committee agendas and packs in a timely manner.
  • Prepare all relevant minutes, documents, etc. for all meetings attended by the authorised Company Secretary
  • Take minutes at subsidiary meetings, executive committee meetings and committees of the executives and ensure proper record keeping of same.
  • Prepare accurate board and committee resolutions and facilitate signature thereof.
  • Ensure proper record keeping of all signed minutes of meetings and resolutions in compliance with the Companies Act (as amended).
  • Action items after the meetings emanating from minutes and/or as per the Company Secretary requirements.
  • Dispatch critical business information timeously to all directors as may be required to enable them to adequately execute their fiduciary duties.
  • Ensure that a proper board induction process is followed by preparing high level induction session agendas and packs.
  • Support the external auditors by ensuring that they receive all the required documentation in respect of the relevant entities to perform their audit.
  • Action requisite fit and propriety reviews of key persons annually and upon appointment.
  • Execute the operation of executive and executive sub-committee meetings:
  • Implement and maintain effective executive meeting processes, including consultative agenda setting, compilation of relevant meeting papers and delivery of accurate records of the various meetings for relevant entity and subsidiaries.
  • Prepare, coordinate, and facilitate the distribution of committee agendas and packs in a timely manner.
  • Prepare all relevant minutes, documents, etc. for all meetings attended by the authorised Company Secretary.
  • Take minutes at executive committee meetings and committees of the executives and ensure proper record keeping of same.
  • Prepare accurate committee resolutions and facilitate signature thereof.
  • Ensure proper record keeping of all signed minutes of meetings and resolutions in compliance with the Companies Act.
  • Action items after the meetings emanating from minutes and/or as per the Company Secretary requirements.
  • Maintenance of accurate company registers and records
  • Execute and maintain all company registers in accordance with the Companies Act (as amended).
  • Execute and maintain the relevant entities’ delegation of authority up to date and ensure that it is adhered to.
  • File requisite forms with the CIPC to ensure compliance with the Companies Act (as amended) at all times.
  • Establish, execute and maintain an appropriate filing system to ensure that statutory records/information are recorded accurately, are easily accessible and maintained for retention of records requirements in compliance with the Companies Act (as amended).
  • Ensure that accurate statutory reports/applications are submitted timeously to the Prudential Authority and maintain a record of these reports/applications.
  • Stakeholder management
  • Engage with and manage the exchange of relevant information with the Prudential Authority, external auditors and board of directors.
  • Identify internal and external parties or stakeholders and define their criticality and appropriate engagement method.
