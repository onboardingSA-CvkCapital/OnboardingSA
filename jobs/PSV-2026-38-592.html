About the role

The Department of Health and Wellness (Western Cape) is hiring a Chief Artisan Grade A in Rural Health Services, based in George Regional Hospital. This is a permanent post, paying R499,470 a year. How to apply: Applications are submitted online via www.westerncape.gov.za/health-jobs (click “online applications”). Closing date: 09 November 2026, 17:00PM Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: No payment of any kind is required when applying for this post. Shortlisted candidates may be subjected to a practical and/or competency test.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and implement annual preventive maintenance programmes, prioritised work plans and maintenance risk mitigation measures for hospital buildings, plant and equipment.
  • Coordinate preventive, reactive and emergency repairs to electrical, mechanical and building services, safeguard continuity of clinical-critical systems.
  • Allocate, supervise and quality-assure workshop work orders, contractor maintenance, statutory inspections and completion records.
  • Manage maintenance budgets, commitments, procurement specifications, contractor performance, workshop stock, tools and asset controls in accordance with prescripts.
  • Supervise and develop workshop staff, manage work allocation, performance, attendance, leave, training, standby arrangements and authorised overtime.
  • Implement occupational health and safety requirements, identify, document and escalate infrastructure, fire protection and technical compliance risks.
  • Maintain accurate service and inspection records and submit reports on maintenance performance, expenditure, unresolved faults and corrective actions.
  • Sound project management, technical analysis, troubleshooting, contractor management and quality-assurance skills.

Requirements

  • Minimum educational qualification: Appropriate recognised Trade Test Certificate/trade qualification in Electrical or Mechanical Artisanship.
  • Experience: 10 years’ appropriate post-qualification experience as an Artisan/Artisan Foreman.
  • Inherent requirement of the job: Valid (Code B/EB) driver’s licence and willingness to travel when required.
  • Willingness to perform authorised overtime, standby duties, unplanned call-outs and emergency maintenance to maintain critical hospital services.
  • Competencies (knowledge/skills):
  • Appropriate supervisory/managerial experience in a multidisciplinary workshop (Electrical, HVAC and Mechanical), including experience in procurement of goods and services and contracted maintenance
  • Proven technical and supervisory ability in electrical, mechanical and general building maintenance, including hospital engineering systems
  • Knowledge of the Occupational Health and Safety Act, machinery regulations, statutory compliance and safe maintenance practices
  • Ability to plan preventive maintenance, prioritise breakdowns, assess infrastructure risks and maintain continuity of critical services
  • Knowledge of the Public Finance Management Act, applicable Treasury and supply chain management prescripts, procurement and delegated authority.
  • Ability to prepare technical specifications, evaluate work completed, manage workshop stock and safeguard state assets.
  • Financial management, budgeting, expenditure monitoring and basic cost-analysis skills.
  • Strong leadership, staff supervision, planning, decision-making and change-management skills.
  • Effective communication, customer service, stakeholder engagement and teamwork.
  • Good technical report-writing, record-keeping and problem-solving skills.
  • Computer literacy in MS Word, Excel, Outlook and relevant electronic maintenance/work-order systems.
