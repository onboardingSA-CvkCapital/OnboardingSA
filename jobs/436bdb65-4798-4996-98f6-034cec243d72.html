About the role

Silverton General Ledger Clerk (Motor Industry exp essential) Financial Division – Motor Industry Group Location: Silverton, Pretoria Position Type: Full-Time, Permanent (Following a successful 3-month probation period) Industry: Automotive / Motor Sector Salary: R25,000.00 – R32,000.00 Basic Salary per month (Negotiable based on experience) + Benefits About Our Client Representing a diverse portfolio of the world’s leading passenger, commercial, and emerging electric vehicle brands, our client's dealerships offer an all-inclusive motoring solution—encompassing new and pre-owned vehicle sales, finance and insurance (F&I) services, specialized workshops, aftermarket performance tuning, and genuine parts procurement. Backed by a strong balance sheet, entrenched standard operating procedures, and industry-leading systems, this group is built on a culture of integrity, a "can-do" attitude, and a continuous commitment to the training and development of its workforce. About the Role On behalf of our client, we are seeking a meticulous, accurate, and experienced General Ledger Clerk to join their finance team. If you have a strong background in accounting principles, a sharp eye for detail, and solid hands-on experience within the motor industry, this is an excellent opportunity to advance your career. You will play a pivotal role in maintaining core financial records, performing account reconciliations, ensuring data accuracy, and supporting month-end close procedures. What’s on Offer? - Basic Salary: R25,000.00 – R32,000.00 Basic Salary per month (Negotiable based on experience). - Medical Aid: 50% company contribution (capped at a maximum of R9,260.50). - Gap Cover: Compulsory at R199.00 per month for all employees joining the medical aid scheme. - Pension Fund: 8% company contribution (Only applicable to permanent employment following the successful completion of a 3-month probation period). - Leave Days: 15 days per annum (Subject to company policy: leave cannot be taken for more than 10 consecutive working days and may not be taken over month-end). Working Hours & Environment - Standard Hours: Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 17:00. - Environment: Professional, office-based corporate finance environment. - Standard Working Procedures (SWP): A standard working procedures manual must be obtained from HR for your position; it must be read, signed, and returned to HR within your first week of employment. Key Responsibilities - Transaction Posting: Accurately post daily financial transactions and routine journal entries into the accounting system. - Account Reconciliations: Perform complete monthly reconciliations for assigned general ledger accounts. - Error Investigation: Proactively investigate, troubleshoot, and resolve accounting errors or data mismatches. - Audit Support: Prepare and compile detailed supporting documentation for internal and external audits. - Period Closing: Assist the financial team with month-end and year-end closing tasks according to set timelines. Requirements & Qualifications - Experience: Proven, stable experience as a General Ledger Clerk within the Motor Sector is strictly essential and non-negotiable. - Bookkeeping Knowledge: Strong, practical grasp of basic bookkeeping principles and accounting workflows. - Systems Knowledge & Skills: Advanced proficiency with spreadsheets (MS Excel) and financial/accounting software. - Attributes: High attention to detail, exceptional data accuracy, strong analytical capabilities, and the ability to work under pressure to meet strict deadlines. - References: Must be able to supply contactable references from previous employers for verification. Strict Legal Requirements Please Note: Due to the financial and sensitive nature of this role, the following criteria are non-negotiable. Background screening will be conducted on all shortlisted candidates. - Must be a South African-born citizen with a valid South African ID. - Must have a clean criminal background (Candidates with a criminal record will not be considered). - Must have a clear ITC (financial/credit history) with no judgments or defaults. Required Documentation for Application Please ensure that you submit the following documentation with your application: - Detailed CV: Must include contactable references clearly reflecting your dealership financial experience. - ID Copy: Required as proof of South African-born citizenship. - Drivers’ License Copy: Required for the operation of company vehicles. - 3 Months Recent Payslips: Your latest three consecutive months of salary advice. - Supporting Documents (Optional): Copies of all relevant qualifications, achievements/awards, and reference letters.
