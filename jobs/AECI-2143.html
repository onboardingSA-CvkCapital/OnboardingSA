About the role

AECI is hiring a Production Superintendent, based in Sasolburg. To provide operational leadership for the production function of the CS2, Xanthates & other manufacturing facilities, ensuring that products are manufactured safely, reliably, efficiently, on specification and in accordance with customer and business requirements.The Production Superintendent is accountable for the day-to-day performance of production operations, including safety, production output, quality, cost, plant utilisation, operational discipline, people capability and continuous improvement.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the production process and ensure production targets are achieved safely, efficiently and on time.
  • Ensure raw materials, labour, equipment and utilities are available to execute the production plan.
  • Ensure equipment availability and reliability by working closely with the Maintenance and Engineering teams.
  • Provide effective leadership, coaching and development of production teams and frontline leaders.
  • Optimise plant capacity, throughput, utilisation and production efficiency while minimising waste and production losses.
  • Maintain a strong safety culture and ensure compliance with SHEQ, OHS, process safety and company requirements.
  • Ensure compliance with SOPs, operating procedures, permits and critical safety controls.
  • Monitor production processes and operating parameters and address deviations and abnormal conditions.
  • Ensure products consistently meet quality specifications and minimise off-specification production, rework and waste.
  • Coordinate production planning, campaigns, changeovers and product transitions to meet customer requirements.
  • Work closely with Quality, Laboratory, Process Engineering, Maintenance, Planning and Supply Chain to resolve operational issues.
  • Lead or participate in risk assessments, HAZOPs, incident investigations and process safety reviews.
  • Maintain strong operational discipline, accurate production records and effective shift handovers.
  • Monitor production KPIs covering safety, production, quality, reliability, cost and people performance.
  • Control production-related expenditure, including raw materials, utilities, overtime, waste and maintenance costs.
  • Support shutdowns, maintenance activities, equipment modifications and CAPEX projects.
  • Drive continuous improvement, operational excellence and world-class manufacturing practices.
  • Maintain good housekeeping and ensure a clean, safe and organised production environment.
  • Plan shift rosters and coordinate production activities across shifts and operating areas.
  • Ensure production activities support customer demand and contribute to reliable customer service.

Requirements

  • Qualifications and expertise required (Must)
  • Grade 12 with Maths and Science.
  • Diploma / BTech / Degree in Chemical Engineering/ Process Engineering
  • Postgraduate qualification in Business Administration or Operations Management. (advantageous)
  • Minimum 8 years’ experience in a chemical/manufacturing environment.
  • Minimum 3 years’ management or production leadership experience.
  • Computer literacy, including MS Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
  • SAP knowledge/experience.
  • Competencies (skills, knowledge and characteristics)
  • Leadership.
  • Operational and technical problem-solving.
  • Analytical and reasoning skills.
  • Teamwork and collaboration.
  • Emotional intelligence.
  • Decision-making.
  • Time management and planning.
  • Cost and performance management.
  • Safety and quality focus.
  • Continuous improvement mindset.
