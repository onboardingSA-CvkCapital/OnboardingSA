About the role

Introduction: Wembley College is a leading, English Medium, co-educational, independent school situated on a beautiful campus amongst the rolling hills of Greytown in the KwaZulu Natal Midlands. Description: Wembley College is a leading, English Medium, co-educational, independent school situated on a beautiful campus amongst the rolling hills of Greytown in the KwaZulu Natal Midlands. An hour from Pietermaritzburg and an hour from Ballito makes for an ideal ‘country school’ within easy striking distance of a Central Business District and the warm KwaZulu Natal coast. Founded in 1997, the school enters candidates for the Cambridge Examinations that are set externally and recognised world-wide. The following position has become available for a Director of Sport for our School, based in Greytown. The Director of Sport holds accountability for sport across all three schools of Wembley College. The successful incumbent will author, own and drive the Wembley College Sport Strategy. The incumbent carries direct coaching responsibility for hockey and is expected to model, in that programme RESPONSIBILITIES: • Sport strategy and whole-College direction • Team performance and the competition programme • Coaching capability and internal coach development • Head Coach: hockey • Leadership of the Heads of Sport and sport operations • Athlete welfare, safeguarding and compliance • Facilities, budget and resources • Stakeholder engagement, profile and recruitment Requirements: REQUIREMENTS: Qualification - Essential • A relevant tertiary qualification (NQF Level 7 or higher) in Sport Science, Sport Management, Human Movement Science, Physical Education or Education. • A recognised, current national federation coaching accreditation in hockey at a senior level — SA Hockey Association Level 2 as a minimum, with Level 3 / FIH pathway progression strongly preferred. • Current first aid certification (or willingness to obtain within three months of appointment). • Valid driver’s license, with a PDP or willingness to obtain one. • Clearance in terms of criminal record vetting, the National Register for Sex Offenders and the Child Protection Register. Qualification - Desirable • A postgraduate qualification in sport leadership, coaching science, high-performance sport or education management. • SACE registration. • A coach educator / course facilitator accreditation with a national federation. • Formal qualification or certification in strength and conditioning, or in sport performance analysis. • Accreditation in a second code relevant to the Wembley offering. Experience • A minimum of 5 years experience in school, club, academy or federation sport, of which at least three to five years must be in a senior sport leadership role with accountability for a programme, a budget and a team of coaches. • Proven, demonstrable strategy work: the candidate must be able to show a sport strategy they personally authored, the evidence base behind it, how it was implemented and what measurably changed as a result. • Proven coach development experience. • Substantial hockey coaching experience Technical Knowledge • Long-term participant / athlete development models and their practical application in a school setting, including the South African Sport for Life framework. • The South African coaching framework, federation coaching pathways and the accreditation landscape by code. • Sport budgeting, procurement, facility planning and event management.
