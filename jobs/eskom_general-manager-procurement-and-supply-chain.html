About the role

Position/Task Grade: E-Band Areas of specialisation: Reporting to the Group Executive Group Capital Division, the General Manager Procurement and Supply Chain will provide strategic leadership and direction across sourcing, procurement and supply chain management; drive organisational synergies; enhance operational excellence; ensure the security and continuity of supply; and optimise procurement costs across the organisation. Business Unit: Procurement and Supply Chain Management. Department: Group Capital Division, P&SCM.

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Establish, develop and direct the Procurement and Supply Chain Management vision, strategy, objectives and plans aligned with organisational goals and statutory requirements.
  • Direct strategic sourcing, procurement governance, service delivery management, transformation, centres of excellence and shared services.
  • Develop commercial strategies, policies, processes and systems that enable effective service delivery, continuity of supply and value optimisation.
  • Develop and manage strategic relationships with internal customers, external stakeholders and key suppliers, including high-level negotiations for critical goods and services.
  • Direct financial, operational and people resources for the function, ensuring sound governance, cost control, performance management, talent development and a safe, diverse working environment.
  • Lead Procurement and Supply Chain Management operations toward excellent customer service and report functional performance to EXCO and other governance structures.
  • Transform the function through the assessment and implementation of new technologies, concepts and leading practices that improve organisational effectiveness.
