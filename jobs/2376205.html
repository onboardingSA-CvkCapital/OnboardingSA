About the role

About Stiles Tiles Stiles Tiles is one of South Africa's leading suppliers of quality tiles and bathroomware. We are looking for a reliable, hardworking and hands-on General Worker / Handyman to join our Rustenburg branch. If you enjoy practical work, take pride in maintaining a clean and organised workplace, and are willing to assist wherever needed, we'd love to hear from you. About the Role As our Driver/ General Worker / Handyman, you'll play an important role in keeping our branch running smoothly. From general maintenance and repairs to assisting with warehouse duties and deliveries, you'll be part of a supportive team that values reliability, teamwork and a strong work ethic. Key Responsibilities - Perform general maintenance and minor repairs, including basic plumbing, carpentry, painting and electrical work. - Assist with loading, unloading and moving stock, including tiles, tools and building materials. - Carry out basic tiling and grouting or assist installation teams when required. - Keep the warehouse, yard and work areas clean, organised and safe. - Maintain company tools, equipment and vehicles. - Assist with deliveries, collections and general errands. - Identify and report maintenance issues or safety hazards. - Support the team with general operational and physical tasks as required. Minimum Requirements - Code 10 driver licence - Grade 10 or Grade 12 (advantageous but not essential). - Previous experience as a General Worker, Handyman, Maintenance Assistant or similar role. - Experience using hand and power tools. - Basic knowledge of plumbing, carpentry, painting or general maintenance. - Physically fit and able to perform manual labour, including lifting heavy materials. - Reliable, punctual and able to work independently. - Positive attitude with a willingness to learn. - Clear criminal record. - Code 10 driver license - Own reliable transport or residence within reasonable commuting distance of Rustenburg will be advantageous. Working Hours Monday - Thursday: 08:00 - 17:00 Friday: 08:00 - 16:30 Every second Saturday: 08:30 - 13:30 What We Offer - Permanent, full-time employment. - Market-related salary of R8,000 - R10,000 per month, depending on experience. - On-the-job training and skills development. - Stable employment with an established South African business. - A supportive and collaborative working environment. How to Apply Please submit your updated CV, two contactable references, your current area of residence, and your notice period or earliest available start date. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
