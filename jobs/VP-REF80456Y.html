About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Professional Nurse Occupational Health, based in Rustenburg, North West. The role is responsible for delivering high-quality occupational health nursing care to all patients, with the aim of promoting their physical, psychological, and social well-being.

Responsibilities

  • Perform daily planning of activities and tasks to ensure timeous completion of tasks ahead of deadlines.
  • Allocation of staff to various stations.
  • Conduct pre-employment, pre-placement, transfer, and exit medical examinations in accordance with established medical examination protocols.
  • Provide baseline health assessments to determine fitness for duty.
  • Perform periodic health screening to detect early signs of target organ damage due to specific health hazard exposure.
  • Identify affected employees and ensure early intervention and referral to appropriate healthcare professionals.
  • Assist with and report on injuries on duty and occupational diseases.
  • Follow organisational standards and procedures for the medical surveillance process.
  • Conduct exit medicals to detect and exclude possible occupational diseases related to occupational exposure.
  • Present medical recommendations to line management.
  • Ensure accurate documentation and signing of medical records.
  • Provide input into incident investigations from a health perspective.
  • Implement health promotion activities within the organisation.
  • Prepare and distribute medical reports in accordance with prescribed standards.
  • Safety & Health Compliance. Consistently apply Safety & Health principles in all team interactions and take personal responsibility for self safety and others to ensure zero harm and elimination of fatalities.
  • Act in line and support a purpose-led, high performing culture, applying the company values and principles of inclusion and diversity
  • Act as a role model and actively engage as a team member in all team processes
  • Set clear and consistent expectations. Embrace and encourage feedback and constantly strive for improvements by engaging with teams to gain feedback and suggestions.
  • Communications and collaboration with the OHC team
  • Resolve issues and escalate as required
  • Effective two-way communication with employees

Requirements

  • Technical knowledge
  • Computer Literacy
  • Proficient in Microsoft Excel, Outlook, and Word, with the ability to manage data, communicate effectively, and produce professional documentation.
  • Communication Skills
  • Strong verbal and written communication abilities, enabling clear interaction with clients, colleagues, and stakeholders.
  • Time Management
  • Skilled in prioritizing tasks and managing time efficiently to meet deadlines and maintain productivity.
  • Organizational Skills
  • Capable of maintaining structured workflows, managing documentation, and ensuring operational efficiency.
  • Grade 12
  • Diploma or Degree in Nursing
  • BTech Occupational Health Nursing
  • Registered with SA Nursing Council
  • Basic Life Support certificate (Advantageous)
  • Certificate in Audiometry, Spirometry, Vision (Advantageous)
  • Registered with South African Society for Occupational Health Nursing (Advantageous)
  • A valid Drivers License
  • Experience
  • 2 years’ experience in similar role.
  • Knowledge of relevant mining & labour legislation
  • Compliance
  • Safety, health and environment, legislative, statutory, regulatory and permitting requirements.
