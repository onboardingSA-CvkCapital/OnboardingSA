About the role

The Department of Health (Free State) is hiring an Assistant Manager Nursing: (Pna4): Health Promotion, based in Corporate Office Bloemfontein. This is a permanent post, paying R720,819 a year. How to apply: Free State Provincial Department of Health utilises an e-Recruitment System (applicants must submit their applications via the online link https://www.erecruitment.fs.gov.za Reference number to quote: H/A/46/2026 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Strategic Planning, Policy and Programme Coordination
  • Develop departmental Health Promotion strategy aligned to departmental priorities
  • Oversee and coordinate Provincial and district Health Promotion activities across relevant clinical and public health programs
  • Incorporate Health Promotion priorities into relevant programme and service-delivery plans
  • Monitor Implementation of approved strategies, policies, guidelines and plans
  • Develop a risk action plan, implementation plan of underperformance identified, Required programme reports submitted within prescribed reporting periods.
  • Health Education, Communication and Social & Behaviour Change
  • Implement Evidence-based health education and behaviour-change interventions
  • Develop and adapt Priority health messages according to identified health needs and programme priorities
  • Develop and ensure availability of Information, Education and Communication materials for campaigns and community interventions
  • Communication interventions address identified barriers to healthy behaviour and utilisation of health services
  • Facilitate and monitor Effectiveness and reach of health communication interventions
  • Community Mobilisation, Advocacy and Stakeholder Management
  • Plan and implement Community mobilisation activities in support of priority health programmes
  • Establish and maintain Partnerships with relevant government departments, municipalities, NGOs, community structures and other stakeholders
  • Facilitate Stakeholder participation during health campaigns and community interventions
  • Community health needs and concerns incorporated into Health Promotion planning where appropriate
  • Implement Advocacy initiatives to support healthy lifestyle, early health-seeking and utilisation of preventive services
  • Develop/establish and maintain constructive working relationships with relevant stakeholders (i.e. inter-professional, inter-sectoral and multidisciplinary teamwork): Collaborate with members of the health and social care teams and participate in decision-making pertaining to health care delivery.
  • Consult within the multi-disciplinary health teams, organizations and special interest groups when dealing with community health issues and needs.
  • Disseminate information on epidemics, nutritional disease, maternal and infant morbidity and mortality and other common diseases.
  • Participate in multidisciplinary quality assurance task teams.
  • Monitor and ensure proper utilization of financial and physical resources: Participate in the development of the business plan and promote or identify ways of containing health care costs without compromising standards.
  • Motivate for relevant material resources.
  • Manage and control assets.
  • Develop the business plan in line with the strategic plan.
  • Ensure proper implementation of the budget by monitoring, projecting and reporting expenditure.
  • Ensure that spending is maximized in line with strategic objectives.
  • Monitoring, Evaluation, Reporting and Quality Improvement.
  • Monitor Health Promotion activities against approved plans and agreed indicators.
  • Provincial and district performance information collected, reviewed and consolidated.
  • Compile and submit routine performance reports according to reporting requirements.
  • Identify Performance trends, programme gaps and areas of underperformance and develop quality improvement plans to support programs.
  • Review Health-promotion interventions to determine whether intended outputs are being achieved.
  • Use Programme performance information to inform future planning and resource prioritisation.

Requirements

  • Basic R425 qualification (i.e. diploma/degree in nursing), 4 year's Bachelor degree or equivalent qualification that allows registration with the SANC as a Registered Nurse and Midwife.
  • At least 8 years appropriate/recognisable experience in nursing after registration as Professional Nurse with the SANC in General Nursing.
  • At least 3 years of the period referred to above must be appropriate/recognisable experience at management level.
  • Post graduation in health care management/Nursing Administration.
  • Current registration with South African Nursing Council (SANC) as a Registered Nursing.
  • Post graduate qualification in Public Health will be an added advantage.
