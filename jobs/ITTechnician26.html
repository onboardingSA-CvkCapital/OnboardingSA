About the role

The purpose of this position is to provide first line support and assistance to internal clients through the ticketing system as the first level triage. To provide service excellence in daily tasks when assisting clients with support requests, to provide correct information and advice as well as to train on basic troubleshooting to prevent the occurrence of repetitive problems. Supporting the Systems Administrator with all tasks allocated. Performing a wide range of administrative and support activities for the Systems Administrator and provide meaningful input in order to ensure that all IT Systems and all their associated components maintain a 99.9% uptime and operate at their highest efficiency levels. Keeping abreast with new developments and technology in the market. Main job tasks and responsibilities ·        End-user support function (Helpdesk and level 1 support capability) ·        Assisting the Systems Administrator with core systems support. ·        Supporting and maintaining peripheral hardware (i.e. printers/scanners, clocking systems, cell phones, access control, network switches, etc.) ·        Monitor the IT Tickets folder and escalate to the Systems Administrator when necessary. Report on the tickets weekly. ·        Monitoring of backups and reporting on failures. ·        Monitoring server health and reporting on failures. ·        Assisting with the monthly maintenance cycle. ·        Assist in maintaining the existing customized spreadsheets. ·        Provide assistance with the maintenance of e-mail rules, rights, and backup/archiving as and when required. ·        Factory restore / Format devices for redeployment, and/or perform software updates. ·        Preparation and deployment of devices and peripheral hardware. ·        Assisting with the procurement of IT and peripheral equipment when required. ·        Deploying new software, security, and software updates to devices. ·        Creation & deployment of e-mail and signature banners. ·        Ad-hoc image editing when required. ·        All other reasonable duties that might be assigned from time to time.
