About the role

Are you passionate about property investments, deal-making, and identifying opportunities that drive business growth? We are looking for a highly motivated Property Growth & Acquisitions Analyst to join our dynamic Growth & Acquisitions team. This exciting role offers the opportunity to be directly involved in expanding and strengthening a growing property portfolio through acquisitions, developments, and strategic disposals. You will play a key role in sourcing investment opportunities, conducting financial and market analysis, assessing development potential, and supporting high-value property transactions from inception to completion. Working closely with industry professionals, agents, developers, and senior leadership, you will contribute to decisions that shape the future growth of the business. This role is ideal for a commercially minded property professional who enjoys analysing deals, uncovering opportunities, building relationships, and seeing projects come to life. What You'll Be Doing • Identifying and evaluating new property acquisition opportunities. • Analysing investment opportunities and preparing financial feasibilities. • Conducting market research and assessing rental, valuation, and market trends. • Managing due diligence investigations on acquisitions and developments. • Presenting investment opportunities to the Deal Committee. • Building and maintaining relationships with agents, brokers, developers, and industry stakeholders. • Identifying development opportunities within the existing portfolio. • Assisting with the planning, financial assessment, and execution of development projects. • Supporting the marketing, leasing, and disposal of properties within the portfolio. • Contributing to the growth strategy and long-term success of the business. Why Join Us? • Be part of a high-growth property business. • Gain exposure to acquisitions, developments, investments, and property sales. • Work on strategic projects that directly influence portfolio growth. • Collaborate with experienced property and finance professionals. • Build your career in a role that offers variety, challenge, and significant commercial exposure.
