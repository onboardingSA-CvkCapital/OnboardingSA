About the role

We are looking for a motivated, results-driven External Sales Consultant to join our growing team. The successful candidate will be responsible for generating new business, developing customer relationships and driving sales growth within the garage door, construction and home improvement market. This role is suited to an energetic sales professional who enjoys working with clients face-to-face, identifying opportunities and closing sales. Key Responsibilities • Generate new business through prospecting, cold calling and networking. • Manage and develop an allocated sales territory. • Conduct customer site visits and take basic measurements where required. • Prepare quotations and follow up on sales opportunities. • Build and maintain relationships with builders, developers, contractors, installers, property managers and homeowners. • Manage sales administration accurately and timeously. • Plan and manage daily appointments and customer visits. • Deliver excellent customer service and encourage repeat business. • Represent Krazi Door professionally and uphold the company's brand and values.
