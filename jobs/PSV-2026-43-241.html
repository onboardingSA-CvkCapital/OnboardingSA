About the role

The Department of Health (Gauteng) is hiring an Assistant Director: Facility Management Unit, based in Gauteng College of Nursing (Central Office Krugersdorp). This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 to R573,897 a year. How to apply: Applications should be submitted strictly online at the following website https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za. No hand-delivered or emailed applications will be accepted. Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: In compliance with DPSA Circular 19 of 2022 certifying documents/qualifications will only be requested from shortlisted candidates on or before the interview process (Applicants are not required to submit copies of qualification and other relevant documents on the application but must submit the new fully completed Z83 obtainable from www.dpsa.gov.za and detailed Curriculum Vitae). Please make sure that the correct reference number is reflected on the Z83 application form. Employment history must reflect the complete calendar date (e.g. 01 April 2017) on the C.V. State all your competencies, training, and knowledge in your CV. Employment references must be Supervisors. It is the applicant’s responsibility to have foreign qualifications evaluated by the South African Qualification Authority (SAQA). All shortlisted candidates for SMS posts will be subjected to a technical exercise and interview. Following the technical exercise and interview, a maximum of three (3) SMS candidates will undergo psychometric assessments to assess cognitive capabilities, behavioural preferences, emotional intelligence, and integrity. All shortlisted candidates, including SMS, shall undertake two pre-entry assessments. One must be a practical exercise, and the other must be an Integrity (Ethical Conduct) Assessment. Selection panels shall score both technical exercises as an additional criterion in the interview process the successful candidate will be subjected to Pre employment Medical Surveillance conducted by the Occupational Health Nurse Practitioner (OHNP). Incomplete applications or applications received after the closing date will not be considered. The Provincial Government of Gauteng is committed to the achievement and maintenance of diversity in employment, especially of race, gender, and disability. It is a legislative requirement that all newly appointed staff members are subjected to Personnel Suitability Checks (PSC)- Verification, upon appointment within the Department. This verification process entails reference checks that should be an immediate current supervisor, identify verification, qualifications verification, and criminal record checks, social media, financial affordability). Correspondence will only be limited to shortlisted candidates. If you have not been contacted within 3 months of the closing date of this advertisement, please accept that your application was unsuccessful. The Department reserves the right not to make an appointment for the advertised post.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure overall management of Facility Management Unit, Parking, Accommodation, Garden services, Cleaning, Security, Waste management and DID - Artisans (Electrical, Mechanical, Building, Plumbing, Carpentry).
  • Supervise and manage resources in Facility Management Unit services departments within the proper implementation of the National Core Standard requirements of the FMU.
  • Ensure compliance and maintaining the quality standard required by the institution.
  • Monitoring and evaluation of DID staff performance onsite.
  • Communicating and liaising with the relevant inspectors and staff from DID AND Central Office Infrastructure Management.
  • Attend FMU and DID meetings and compile weekly, monthly and quarterly reports.
  • Participate in inter and intradepartmental committees that deal with issues of FMU.
  • Responsible for performance appraisal and development of staff under his/her supervision.
  • Ensure achievement of strategic objectives of the component by ensuring suitable infrastructure in terms of identified need of budget and monitoring construction.
  • Conduct building audits and identify areas that need attention.
  • Control FMU budget and monitor expenditure.
  • Ensure proper utilization of the infrastructure and Day to Day maintenance budget by monitoring and reporting expenditure.
  • Ensuring the implementation of FMU practices, guidelines and policies.
  • Responsible for implementation of Occupational Health and Safety Act.
  • Participate in the development of strategic and operational plans for Facility Management sections.
  • Attend meetings for projects and strategic planning.
  • Attend to human resource matters, do record keeping and statistics, compile and submit reports.
  • Plan and implement training programs for FMU staff.
  • Perform any duties as delegated by management.

Requirements

  • A matric/grade 12 certificate or the equivalent.
  • An appropriate Degree or National diploma (NQF Level 6) in Electrical/Mechanical/Building as recognized by SAQA with 5 years or more experience in the field of specialization of which 2 years should be at supervisory level in Electrical/Mechanical or Building in a public or private sector.
  • Qualification in Project management will be an advantage.
  • Competencies
  • Knowledge of PFMA
  • OHS Act
  • Project management
  • Inspection
  • Presentation and analytical skills
  • Knowledge of managing Facility Management services Knowledge of National Building Regulations e.g. Good financial management skills and conflict management.
  • Must be computer literate (MS EXCEL and PowerPoint).
  • Excellent time management and organizational skills.
  • Good communication skills (verbal and non-verbal, report writing skills, negotiation, teambuilding, problem solving, conflict resolution) and ability to work under pressure.
  • A valid driver’s license.
