About the role

Introduction: Mascor is a Company consisting of franchised Toyota dealerships in Greytown and Dalton. We offer professional and high-quality service and customer service standards. Description: Mascor is a Company consisting of franchised Toyota dealerships in Greytown and Dalton. We offer professional and high-quality service and customer service standards. Mascor Toyota Greytown is currently seeking a Sales Executive, to actively promote and sell new and pre-owned vehicles, ensuring excellent customer service, achieving sales targets, and upholding the Toyota brand’s reputation for quality and trust. RESPONSIBILITIES: 1. Sales & Customer Service • Proactively engage with prospective customers via showroom floor, online leads, telephonic inquiries, and referrals. • Conduct professional vehicle presentations, test drives, and product demonstrations. • Identify customer needs and recommend suitable vehicles and value-added products (warranties, service plans, finance options). • Negotiate trade-in values and finalise sales deals in line with dealership policies. • Maintain regular follow-ups with potential and existing customers to secure repeat business. 2. Administration & Compliance • Complete all sales documentation accurately (offer to purchase, finance applications, trade-in appraisals). • Ensure compliance with F&I. POPIA, and other applicable automotive retail regulations. • Work closely with the F&I Manager to secure finance approvals and insurance arrangements. • Maintain updated vehicle stock lists and pricing on dealership and third-party platforms. 3. Vehicle Preparation & Merchandising • Ensure all vehicles on display are clean, well-presented, and correctly priced. • Assist with vehicle photos and descriptions for online marketing. • Monitor the condition of used stock and report any issues to management. 4. Targets & Performance • Consistently meet or exceed monthly sales and gross profit targets. • Contribute to dealership CSI (Customer Satisfaction Index) targets through professional service. • Track personal performance and proactively seek improvement opportunities. Requirements: REQURIEMENTS: 1. Qualifications & Experience • Matric Certificate (Grade 12). • Minimum 2 years previous experience as a sales executive in a dealership environment (used vehicles preferred). • Valid South African driver’s license. 2. Skills & Competencies • Strong sales and negotiation skills. • Excellent verbal and written communication skills. • Product knowledge of Toyota vehicles and competitors' models. • Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines. • Computer literacy (MS Office, Dealer Management Systems, online lead platforms). • High energy level and results-driven mindset.
