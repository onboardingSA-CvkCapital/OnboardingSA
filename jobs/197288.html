About the role

JOB OVERVIEW: The overall objective of this position is to achieve zero downtime for any mechanical machine or equipment at Boma National Park. Ensure that an effective and systematic maintenance and repair workplan is in place for the fleet, which consists of: ● 6 land cruisers (4.2D) ● 2 John Deere tractors ● 1 Leyland ASHOK truck ● 1 JCB 3X Tlb ● Generators (between 6 and 50 kva) ● King Max Welders ● motorbikes ● Stihl grasscutters and chainsaws MAIN ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES: ● Actively perform all technical repair and maintenance functions of equipment used by the Park Operations (light and medium vehicles, generators, welders, motorbikes and grass cutters) in accordance with the manufacturer's best practices. ● Plan and uphold mechanical and maintenance schedules by preparing daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual maintenance schedules; ● Carry out regular inspection of motor vehicles and generators to proactively identify required repairs, spares, tools, equipment, and materials. ● Conduct inspections and checks and prepare vehicle dispatch notes before sending vehicles off-site for missions and conduct return checks with drivers ● Verify and document all work performed; attach service reports and parts used to each job record. ● Order, receive and manage spare parts and consumables; ensure correct part numbers and maintain stock levels. ● Prepare and maintain detailed inventory records for assigned vehicles/parts, noting condition and serial/asset numbers. ● Perform safety checks and road tests to confirm repair quality and vehicle roadworthiness after each service before a vehicle is given to a driver. ● Provide technical advice and recommendations to the Field Operations Manager on vehicle condition, repair priorities and replacement needs. ● Follow safety protocols and keep the work environment safe, clean and secure; ● Ensure tools are well kept, always cleaned and securely stored with active inventory ● Through a provided worksheet/logbook, prepare and submit to the Field Operations Manager a monthly report documenting maintenance activities, repairs and overall conditions of vehicles to ensure operational efficiency, safety and cost control; ● Schedule appointments with mechanics for major repairs carried out through subcontracting, and ensure follow‑up before, during, and after the service ● Maintain an up-to-date database on all vehicles, motorcycles, and other equipment, including registration certificates, insurance, usage and repair tracking sheets, and regularly inform the manager of any identified malfunction or administrative requirement. ● Maintain an up-to-date database on the documentation of all drivers of the different operational vehicles (including driving licences, ocular medical certificates, quarterly capacity‑building training on driving, and others) in accordance with the SOPs ● Assess the cost and time required for the repair or maintenance of vehicles. ● Supervise the stock of spare parts and lubricants ● Undertaking any additional duties as directed by the FOM with regard to maintenance HOW TO APPLY: To apply, please email your CV and cover letter to ssrecruitment@africanparks.org  or hand delivery to African Parks office Thonping, Synergy Suites – Plot No 849, Block No 3-K South by Friday, 21 August2026. Please mention the position name in the subject line of the email. In case you do not hear from us within two weeks after the closing date, this means that your application was not successful. We thank you for your interest in working for African Parks.
