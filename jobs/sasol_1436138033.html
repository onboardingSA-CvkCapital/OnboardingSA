About the role

Sasol is a global integrated chemicals and energy company with a 75-year heritage. Through our talented people, we use our expertise and selected technologies to safely and sustainably source, manufacture and market chemical and energy products globally. When you join Team Sasol, you are joining a company that puts people at the center of everything we do. Sasol invests in its employees along every stage of the career path and offers development opportunities to help you cultivate your career in a culture that embraces diversity and inclusion. Lead the HR project portfolio delivery capability, ensuring approved HR transformation, optimisation, HRIS, digital HR, data, analytics and organisation effectiveness initiatives are translated into integrated delivery plans, governed through clear project disciplines, and executed with measurable value realisation. The role provides specialist project, programme and portfolio leadership across cross-functional HR initiatives. It integrates business demand, delivery planning, dependency management, governance, stakeholder alignment, change adoption, risk management and benefit tracking across HR, IM, Finance, Legal, Procurement, vendors and relevant HR Centres of Excellence. The role enables delivery by coordinating plans, managing dependencies, maintaining governance discipline, escalating decisions and ensuring approved initiatives are delivered with clear adoption and value outcomes. The role does not own HR functional policy, HR process design authority, HRIS product ownership, People Analytics content ownership, OD specialist methodology, Talent / Reward decisions, or Digital HR innovation governance. These accountabilities remain with the relevant functional owners. Maintain an integrated portfolio view of approved HR projects, including scope, outcomes, milestones, dependencies, risks, owners, resources and value measures. Translate approved business priorities into practical delivery roadmaps and project plans. Coordinate project sequencing across OD, HRIS, Digital HR, People Analytics, HR governance and related improvement initiatives. Ensure initiatives are prioritised against strategic value, delivery readiness, risk, capacity and enterprise impact. Provide the Head Group OD, HRIS & People Analytics with an accurate view of portfolio health, key decisions, risks and value delivery. Lead planning, mobilisation, execution tracking, risk management and closure of approved HR projects. Set up project routines, workstream structures, delivery plans, issue logs, dependency maps and decision forums. Coordinate delivery across HR, IM, Finance, Legal, Procurement, vendors and business stakeholders. Ensure project teams operate with clear roles, decision rights, delivery expectations and escalation routes. Track delivery against agreed scope, timeline, budget, quality and adoption outcomes. Ensure project handover into business-as-usual ownership is clear and sustainable. Establish and maintain fit-for-purpose project governance standards, templates, stage gates and reporting routines. Support project sponsors and workstream leads with structured delivery methods and governance expectations. Prepare governance packs, steering committee updates, portfolio dashboards and executive reports. Ensure project risks, issues, dependencies and decision requirements are visible and actively managed. Ensure delivery approaches comply with Sasol governance, architecture, data privacy, cybersecurity, procurement, legal and relevant people-data requirements. Escalate material risks, delays, trade-offs and unresolved decision points in a timely and evidence-based manner. Support the Head Group OD, HRIS & People Analytics, and other HR leaders, in assessing new project demand and clarifying business outcomes. Facilitate intake discussions with HR leaders, CoEs, HRBPs, IM, Finance and relevant business stakeholders. Test whether proposed initiatives have clear ownership, value case, delivery capacity, funding route and governance pathway. Identify duplication, conflicting priorities, resource constraints and delivery overload across the portfolio. Recommend sequencing, phasing or consolidation of initiatives where required. Maintain visibility of internal and external delivery capacity, including vendor and implementation partner dependencies. Support the development of project value cases, benefit hypotheses and success measures. Partner with Finance, project sponsors and functional owners to define and validate benefit measures. Track value delivery across the project lifecycle, including adoption, efficiency, cost avoidance, risk reduction, productivity, cycle-time improvement and employee / manager experience outcomes. Ensure project reporting distinguishes between activity completed, adoption achieved and value realised. Identify underperforming initiatives early and recommend corrective actions, scope decisions or closure where value is no longer clear. Work with functional owners, change leads and communication partners to ensure change and adoption plans are embedded into project delivery. Coordinate stakeholder mapping, engagement routines, change readiness checks and adoption tracking across major initiatives. Ensure employee, manager and HR-user impacts are understood before implementation. Support alignment between business requirements, process implications, technology solutions and adoption needs. Coordinate project communications and executive updates in partnership with relevant communication owners. Track post-implementation adoption issues, feedback and improvement actions. Coordinate vendor and implementation partner activity against agreed project plans, deliverables and governance expectations. Partner with Procurement and Legal on vendor scope, commercial inputs, contracting dependencies and delivery performance. Partner with IM / Digital / Data teams on technical delivery dependencies, integration points, access, architecture, cybersecurity and data controls. Partner with HRIS, Digital HR, People Analytics, OD and other functional owners to ensure specialist input is available at the right point in delivery. Ensure vendor outputs are translated into sustainable internal ownership, not external dependency. Standardise project documentation, dashboards, delivery routines, governance packs and lessons-learned practices. Identify recurring delivery pain points and implement practical improvements. Build stronger project discipline among HR project sponsors, workstream leads and functional SMEs. Encourage reuse of proven methods, templates, adoption plans and value-tracking approaches. Support a culture of fewer, clearer, better-governed projects with stronger business outcomes.

Requirements

  • Relevant Bachelor's degree (preferably in Human Resources, Business, Information Systems, Programme / Project Management, Industrial / Organisational Psychology, Business Administration or a related field)
  • Advantageous / preferred:
  • Postgraduate qualification in HR, Business, Digital Transformation, Project / Programme Management, Change Management, Information Systems or related field.
  • Relevant project / programme / portfolio management certification, such as PMP, PRINCE2, MSP, Agile, Scrum, PMO / portfolio management or benefits realisation.
  • Change management certification would be advantageous.
  • Exposure to HR technology, data governance, analytics, AI governance, process optimisation or digital adoption methods would be advantageous.
  • 12 years relevant experience in project, programme or portfolio management, preferably in HR, HR transformation, HRIS, digital HR, shared services, data / analytics, organisation effectiveness or enterprise transformation environments.
