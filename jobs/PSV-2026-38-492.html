Responsibilities

  • Manage demand planning, procurement of goods and services, quotations and bids in line with the Hospital Annual Operational Plan and approved procurement plan.
  • Ensure efficient, economical and accountable management of SCM personnel, budgets and other allocated resources.
  • Engage end users and stakeholders to identify procurement needs, service-delivery gaps and SCM compliance risks.
  • Provide management with sound SCM advice, recommendation and corrective actions on procurement and contract matters.
  • Communicate and implement applicable SCM circulars, policies amendments, Treasury instructions and practice notes.
  • Ensure transparent, fair, competitive and cost-effective procurement and compliance with applicable legislation and delegations.
  • Provide technical guidance to hospital management on acquisition, logistics, contract administration and supplier performance.
  • Implement and monitor SCM procedure, internal controls, requisitions, purchase orders, good receipt and record keeping in preparation for Audits.
  • Compile and analyse monthly SCM performance, procurement, contract, accrual and compliance reports, address exceptions promptly.
  • Participate in multidisciplinary management structures to align SCM activities with clinical and operational priorities.
  • Manage SCM operations and staff performance, including procurement turnaround times, LOGIS processes and asset controls.
  • Ensure sound strategies to avoid Unauthorized, Irregular, Fruitless and Wasteful Expenditure and Reports thereof.
  • Identify staff skills gaps, coordinate SCM training and promote continuous development and ethical conduct.
  • Monitor compliance with the PFMA, Treasury Regulations, departmental SCM framework and supply chain prescripts, prevent and report irregular expenditure.

Requirements

  • Grade 12/Matric or equivalent, An appropriate Degree/National Diploma in Supply Chain Management or equivalent plus at least 3 years’ appropriate supervisory experience in Supply Chain Management.
  • Unendorsed Valid driver’s license.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Training and Competencies [Ne1.1].
  • Knowledge of Public Finance Management Act, Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act, Health SCM Delegations.
  • Good communication, leadership, interpersonal and decision making qualities.
  • Sound management, negotiation, inter-personal, decision making and solving problems.
  • Knowledge of Treasury Regulations and Practice Notes.
  • Good verbal and written communication skills.
  • Knowledge of current Health Legislation and policies at Public Institutions.
  • Ability to handle sensitive financial information in strictest confidence.
  • Working knowledge of the Basic Accounting Systems (BAS)/LOGIS and understand Promotion of Access of Information Act.
  • Knowledge on Security Management Act.
  • Computer literacy in MS Word and Excel.
