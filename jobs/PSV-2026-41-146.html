About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Scientist Manager Grade A in Water Resource Management Dir: Specialist Unit: Water Resource Policy, Strategy and Evaluation Sd: Disaster Management, based in Pretoria Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R1,143,468 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 271126/04 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will be responsible for, but not limited to, the following: Provide strategic leadership and direction for the implementation of water-related disaster management programmes, policies, frameworks, and plans.
  • Lead the identification, assessment, monitoring, and management of water-related disaster risk, which include conducting water-related disaster risk assessments, flood and drought risk profiles reviewed and updated annually, early warning advisories and risk reports issued to stakeholders and identified high-risk areas with updated mitigation plans.
  • Coordinate disaster preparedness, response, and recovery interventions to reduce the impact of water-related disasters.
  • Lead the development or review of contingency and emergency preparedness plans.
  • Coordinate the reporting on the disaster management activities between all relevant stakeholders, consolidate and submitted to the relevant structure including the National Disaster Management Centre.
  • Lead research on water-related disasters, climate variability, and climate change impacts.
  • Promote the use of GIS, modelling tools, predictive analytics, and other decision-support systems.
  • Identify and implement best practices and innovative approaches in disaster risk reduction and management.
  • Oversee the development of scientific reports, technical studies, and knowledge products.
  • Facilitate collaboration and ensure compliance with disaster management legislation and sector requirements.
  • Coordinate stakeholder forums, task teams, and committee meetings convened or attended and awareness and capacity building.
  • Monitor and evaluate the implementation and effectiveness of disaster management programmes and interventions.
  • Manage financial, human, and other resources to ensure effective programme delivery.
  • Provide scientific advice to the Department's senior management and relevant stakeholders on water-related disaster matters.
  • Establish and maintain networks of disaster management and water experts.

Requirements

  • Master's Degree (MSc) in Water Resource Management, Hydrology, Disaster Risk Science, Environmental Science, Geography, Climate Science or related Natural Science qualification.
  • PhD will serve as an added advantage.
  • 6 years post-qualification BSc Natural Scientific experience in the relevant Scientific field.
  • Compulsory registration with SACNASP as a Professional Natural Scientist.
  • The disclosure of a valid unexpired driver’s license.
  • Extensive knowledge of the Disaster Management Act, National Disaster Management Framework, National Water Act, National Water Resource Strategy, Climate Change legislation and related policies.
  • Good liaison, organizational skills and excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • A sound knowledge of programme and project management, application of scientific methodologies and models, and technical report writing.
  • Knowledge and experience of using electronic communication platforms and internet.
  • Knowledge of risk assessment tools and disaster management systems.
  • Understanding of hydrological, meteorological and climate information systems.
  • Knowledge of GIS, data analytics and decision-support systems.
  • Strategic leadership and people management skills.
  • Financial management skills.
  • Ability to work under pressure and travel extensively.
