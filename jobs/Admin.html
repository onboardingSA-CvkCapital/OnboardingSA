About the role

Portland Holdings is looking for a detail-driven administrator. Our Support Department keeps the office operation running, and it runs on people who back each other up. Depending on your skills, experience and competencies, this role could take shape as an Office Administrator, Front of House Administrator, or Support Administrator with a focus on HR, Safety and ISO management. We're looking for candidates who are multi-skilled, tech-savvy and comfortable assisting and supporting operational staff in the most effective ways — happy to help wherever the day requires it. The role may entail general administration across the group, or, for the right candidate, a more specialised focus on safety, quality and HR administration. Duties are broad and will be shaped by your strengths and the team's needs, and may include: • Front-of-house support • General management of company assets, stock and consumables • Data capturing, reporting and correspondence • Documentation creation, filing and record-keeping • General administrative support to management and the wider team For candidates suited to the Support Administrator (HR, Safety & ISO) track, you'll be the go-to person for NCR tracking, safety files, PPE and Safety & HR admin support across the group — equal parts paperwork guardian and problem solver, with a hand in office admin too. This may include: • Monitoring and maintaining ISO 9001, 45001 and 14001 records and documentation across all divisions • Managing the NCR process end-to-end — logging, investigations, close-outs and monthly reporting • Compiling and maintaining safety files for PHC sites, keeping appointments, risk assessments and permits current • Managing the PPE storeroom — stock control, issuing, and the annual stocktake • Supporting HR administration — onboarding/offboarding documentation, training records and performance review scheduling • Stepping in as second port for the switchboard Because our support team covers for one another, you can expect variety on a day-to-day basis, whichever track suits you best. Why join us You'll gain exposure across several admin areas of the business — the kind of breadth that's hard to find in a single position. We invest in people who show initiative and want to grow. Join a trusted industry leader.
