About the role

PURPOSE OF ROLE The Senior QA/QC Technician is responsible for assisting to ensure that all Engineering, Procurement and Construction activities carried out by Lesedi Nuclear Services and its subcontractors — for nuclear projects — conform to client requirements, applicable codes and standards, statutory regulations and internal SHEQ/IMS management systems, including nuclear-specific quality assurance requirements such as NQA-1 and IAEA 50-C/S-Q. The role ensures the effective implementation, maintenance and continual improvement of Quality Assurance and Quality Control systems across nuclear projects, providing senior technical oversight of QA/QC inspections, audits and documentation, and supporting safe, compliant and high-quality project delivery in liaison with other Lesedi departments and in compliance with the Lesedi IMS. KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS Management System (QMS) Implementation • Support compliance with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 19443, NQA-1 and other applicable accreditations within assigned projects and divisional activities. • Review and provide input into QA/QC procedures, work instructions, checklists and templates developed through the Central Quality Function Implement standardised QA/QC processes and project process control procedures across assigned sites andSupport continual improvement initiatives within the QMS framework and align procedures and policies to industry norms and standards. Quality Control Inspections & Surveillances • Perform QA/QC inspections during construction and fabrication activities. • Conduct receipt inspections of materials and verify compliance with QCPs, SIDs, ISs, TPs and WPs. • Witness welding qualification tests and issue Stop Work Orders where required. • Compile punch lists and perform Certificate of Conformance (COC) inspections. Audits, Compliance & Non-Conformance Management • Support internal audits and participate in divisional self-assessments as required by the Central Quality Function, ensuring records are maintained. Support supplier assessments, surveillance activities and technical evaluations as requested by Central Quality.Identify, report and document NCRs; log, track and help close out audit findings, NCRs and corrective/preventive actions; monitor trends and propose corrective and preventive actions. • Prepare audit documentation and evidence packs and support client audits and inspections in a professional manner. Training, Awareness & Stakeholder Support • Deliver project-specific QA/QC awareness and induction training based on approved corporate training material.Provide guidance to project teams on quality standards, codes and regulatory requirements. • Promote good work practices, conformance to requirements and a good health and safety culture on site. • Support project management teams with quality planning and execution. • Contribute to monthly QA communications, toolbox talks and project quality awareness initiatives. Project & Supplier Quality Support • Help prepare and review Project Quality Plans (PQPs) for nuclear projects. • Provide input to supplier performance evaluations, supplier quality assessments and supplier monitoring activities.Identify opportunities for improvement (OFIs) and best practices, and produce ideas and plans for efficiencies. • Provide continuous quality management and support to all projects to enhance compliance to procedures and processes. QUALIFICATION • Technical qualification in Engineering or Quality (e.g. Diploma in Quality Management Systems). • Welding Inspector certification advantageous. • Auditor qualification advantageous. • Working knowledge of ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 (mandatory). EXPERIENCE • Minimum 10 years' related QA & QC experience in an EPC environment. • Proven experience in QA/QC inspection and auditing, including supplier and internal audits. • Experience working with engineering codes, standards and welding processes. • Able to review designs and read Isometric and P&ID drawings. • Nuclear project exposure and training in nuclear power plant operation. • Experience in Root Cause Analysis (RCA) methodologies advantageous. COMPETENCIES Knowledge • Construction, installation and maintenance principles. • Quality management systems and auditing principles (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 19443, NQA-1, IAEA 50-C-Q). • Regulatory, statutory and nuclear safety governance requirements. • Welding processes, fabrication standards and inspection techniques. • Codes and standards relevant to engineering and nuclear environments. • Understanding of quality, environmental and safety management systems.Behaviour • High attention to detail and a methodical working style. • High degree of integrity, loyalty and ethical conduct. • Self-motivated with the ability to initiate activities independently. • Strong teamwork and collaboration mindset; able to generate team spirit and cooperation. • Professional conduct with clients, suppliers and colleagues. • Proactive, reliable and results-driven; takes pride in performance. • Ability to anticipate risks, opportunities and future quality requirements. • Portrays a good Health & Safety culture; diligent and conscientious.Skills • Quality inspection and auditing skills. • Technical interpretation of Isometric and P&ID drawings, procedures and standards. • Strong written and verbal communication skills. • Computer literacy (MS Word, Excel, etc.).
