About the role

Role Overview A growing distribution and ecommerce business is looking for a highly organised, computer-confident and detail-oriented Junior Catalogue Coordinator to assist with the creation, maintenance and optimisation of product listings across various ecommerce platforms. This is a hands-on administrative and ecommerce role suited to someone who enjoys working on a computer, is comfortable working with spreadsheets and large amounts of product information, and takes pride in accuracy and consistency. The Catalogue Coordinator will be responsible for ensuring that products are correctly represented online through accurate titles, descriptions, specifications, imagery, pricing and supporting product data. The role will also make extensive use of AI tools to assist with creating and enhancing product imagery, writing product descriptions and improving the overall quality of online catalogues. Key Responsibilities Product Listing Creation • Create new product listings across multiple online platforms and marketplaces. • Capture product information accurately, including: • Product titles • Descriptions • Features and benefits • Specifications • Dimensions and weights • Materials • Colours and variants • Barcodes • SKUs • Pricing • Product categories • Brand information • Ensure listings comply with the requirements of each platform. • Complete bulk listing templates and spreadsheet uploads. • Submit new products for approval and monitor their progress until successfully live. Catalogue Maintenance • Review and update existing product listings. • Correct inaccurate, incomplete or outdated information. • Identify duplicate listings and catalogue inconsistencies. • Update pricing, images, specifications and descriptions where required. • Ensure product variations and options are structured correctly. • Maintain consistency across different online sales platforms. • Monitor rejected or suppressed listings and work to resolve the underlying issues. • Regularly check that live listings display correctly to customers. Product Images & AI • Use AI tools to create, improve and adapt product imagery. • Resize and prepare images according to different marketplace requirements. • Create suitable lifestyle and supporting product images using approved AI tools. • Remove or replace image backgrounds where necessary. • Ensure images remain professional and accurately represent the product. • Maintain organised folders of product and brand imagery. • Liaise with internal teams and suppliers where additional images are required. Product Descriptions & Content • Use AI tools to assist with writing professional and engaging product descriptions. • Edit AI-generated content to ensure accuracy and consistency. • Create product highlights, features and benefits. • Improve titles and descriptions for ecommerce usability and search visibility. • Ensure content follows brand guidelines and marketplace requirements. • Proofread all content before publication. Spreadsheet & Data Management A significant portion of the role involves working with spreadsheets and product data. Responsibilities include: • Maintaining product master spreadsheets. • Completing marketplace upload templates. • Sorting and filtering large datasets. • Identifying missing or incorrect information. • Using formulas and basic spreadsheet functions. • Comparing data across multiple files. • Maintaining accurate SKU, barcode and product information. • Updating catalogue trackers. • Keeping clear records of products submitted, approved, rejected and outstanding. Strong spreadsheet accuracy is essential. Online Marketplace Administration The role will work across a variety of ecommerce platforms, which may include: • Takealot • Amazon • Makro • Leroy Merlin • Shopify • Wholesale ecommerce platforms • Other current and future online marketplaces Previous experience working with online marketplaces is advantageous but not essential, provided the candidate is computer literate and demonstrates a strong ability to learn new systems. Catalogue Quality Control • Regularly audit product listings for accuracy. • Compare live listings against source product information. • Check images, descriptions, pricing and specifications. • Identify catalogue errors before they impact customers. • Maintain consistency in product naming and formatting. • Escalate discrepancies where further information is required.
