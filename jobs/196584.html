About the role

Introduction Grain Field Chickens, a subsidiary of VKB Agriculture (PTY) Ltd, is a dynamic role player in the integrated broiler industry with an abattoir in Reitz in the North-Eastern Free State. We offer you the opportunity to establish yourself within a professional, corporate environment in the countryside. Job Description The incumbent will be responsible for providing expertise and support and perform employee relations activities which will include - but not limited to - Industrial Relations, workforce planning, AWOL and absenteeism management in order to increase plant quality and turnaround time, in alignment with predefined work objectives (as per agreed standard operating procedures). Job Requirements • Grade 12 or NQF Level 4 Equivalent. • Diploma or Degree in Human Resources Management, or Social Sciences majoring in Industrial Relations. • Strong demonstratable IR background of at least 5 years gained within the FMCG / Manufacturing/ Agriculture environment (particularly in Poultry), essential. • At least 3 years’ experience in a similar role will be an advantage. • Exposure to work within a highly unionized environment. • A proven track record in ER capacity building and union/ management relationship maintenance is a requirement. • Proven knowledge of HR information systems is essential. • Solid, proven and applied understanding of the LRA and BCEA. • Ability to understand and speak the vernacular used in the Eastern Free State (Southern Sotho) will be a definite advantage. • Must be able to maintain confidentiality at all times. • A willingness to enter the plant (cold and wet environment) on a regular basis, to address a large number of employees on the floor. • A driver’s license and own transport will be an advantage. • A willingness to work overtime, incl.
