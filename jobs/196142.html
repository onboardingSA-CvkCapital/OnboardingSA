About the role

• Site/Location - Airport Industrial, Cape Town • Reporting To: Divisional Food Services Manager ROLE OVERVIEW: The Logistics Planning Manager will be responsible for coordinating the movement of raw materials and finished products between fishing vessels, processing facilities, cold stores and customers to ensure products are delivered efficiently and on time. The role focuses on daily logistics planning, communication between departments and the effective utilisation of storage and transport resources. The incumbent will work closely with operations, production, sales and transport providers to support efficient product flow while maintaining product quality and customer service.
