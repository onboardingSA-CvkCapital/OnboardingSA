About the role

Job Title: ServiceNow Data and CMDB Specialist Office Location:  Johannesburg Department: IT Service Management Applications Salary: Market- Related Working Hours and Working Arrangements Working hours: 35 hours per week, 09h30–17h30. Additional hours may be required. The hybrid working approach allows for up to 40% of working time from home and 60% in the office. Role Overview The ServiceNow Data and CMDB Specialist is responsible for the governance, quality and ongoing development of data held within the ServiceNow platform, with particular responsibility for the Configuration Management Database (CMDB), service data, and reporting and insight delivered through or sourced from ServiceNow. The role ensures that Configuration Items (CIs), their relationships and supporting service data are accurate, complete and kept up to date. This provides a reliable source of information for service management processes, operational decision-making, risk management, automation and the introduction of new services and technologies. The role also oversees the quality of data supplied to ServiceNow by other systems. Working with source-system owners and integration teams, the role holder establishes clear ownership, agreed data standards and appropriate controls. The role does not own the source systems but is responsible for identifying and coordinating the resolution of issues that affect the quality of data used within ServiceNow.
