About the role

Motus Aftermarket Parts is searching for a National Head of Sales to join the Alert Engine Parts division. The purpose of the position is to provide strategic leadership and direction to the entire sales organisation, driving sustainable revenue growth, profitability, customer acquisition, market-share expansion and sales excellence across the national retail business. The role is accountable for developing and executing the national sales strategy, leading high-performing sales teams, strengthening customer and strategic partner relationships, identifying new market opportunities, and ensuring effective sales, pricing, budgeting, forecasting and performance-management practices. The role is based in Johannesburg and Pretoria. Role Profile Summary The Senior Manager: Head of Sales is a strategic and commercially accountable leadership position responsible for the overall performance and direction of the national sales organisation. The incumbent must combine strong strategic thinking with hands-on commercial leadership, customer engagement and people management.
