About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring 2 Senior Telecom Operator posts in Facilities Management Services, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R280,278 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivered during office hours to: 600 Lilian Ngoyi Street (formerly known as Van der Walt Street), Berea Park, Pretoria, 0002 or by email to Post246@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/246 Closing date: 16 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured and Indian Males, and Coloured and Indian Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Render telephone services.
  • Keep and maintain incoming and outgoing calls register of the component.
  • Screen and forward calls.
  • Receive telephone messages.
  • Record telephone messages.
  • Develop and maintain a database of contact numbers for all officials.
  • Provide information to clients and address all general enquiries.
  • Render customer services.
  • Ensure that consumers are referred promptly and correctly.
  • Ensure that customers are always attended to.
  • Supply basic information to customers regarding the department’s services when required.
  • Manage switchboard service.
  • Operate switchboard by always answering incoming and outgoing calls.
  • Keep database of other important contact numbers on an on-going basis.
  • Report faults on the telephone system and report to the supervisor and service provider when needed.
  • Identify and inspect default and defective equipment.
  • Utilise the Telephone Management System to monitor telephone costs on an ongoing basis.
  • Ensure that switchboard and all telephone lines are always in working condition.
  • Administer telephone accounts.
  • Complete telephone register and submit to supervisor.
  • Reconcile private telephone with register and submit to supervisor.
  • Implement barring and other facilities.
  • Update internal telephone directory when required.
  • Distribute internal telephone directory to internal users when required.
  • Print telephone cost report for each extension monthly.
  • Allocate and monitor pin codes.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements:
  • A Grade 12 Certificate
  • At least 1-year relevant working experience within a switchboard environment Job related knowledge: Knowledge of Microsoft packages
  • Job related skills:
  • Telephone etiquette
  • Computer literacy
  • Customer care
  • Good communication (verbal and written)
  • Interpersonal relations
  • Ability to operate a switchboard
  • Ability to communicate well with people at different levels and from different backgrounds
  • Ability to learn the Department's service delivery components
  • Ability to take initiatives
  • Ability to work independently
  • Ability to organise work
  • Ability to prioritise work
