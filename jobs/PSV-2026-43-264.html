About the role

The Department of Transport (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring a Human Resource Clerk (Production): Recruitment, Selection & Placement Section, based in Inkosi Mhlabazima Maphumulo House, Pietermaritzburg. This is a permanent post, paying R237,453 a year. Human Resource Administration Directorate How to apply: Applicants are encouraged to apply for posts through the KZN online e- Recruitment system at www.kznonline.gov.za/kznjobs. Applicants may also visit any one of our Designated Online Application Centres (DOACs) where our friendly staff will assist you with applying for jobs on the KZN online e- Recruitment system or receiving your hardcopy application. You can find the list of Designated Online Application Centres (DOACS) at www.kznonline.gov.za/kznjobs. Please note that applicants should only use one of the following methods when applying for a post: either through the online e-Recruitment system or submit a hardcopy application as directed. Forward your application, quoting the relevant reference number to: Recruitment & Selection Section, Human Resource Administration Directorate, Private Bag X9043, Pietermaritzburg, 3200. Applications may alternatively be hand-delivered to the Office of the Department of Transport, Inkosi Mhlabunzima Maphumulo House, 172 Burger Street, Pietermaritzburg. Reference number to quote: DOT 246/2026 Closing date: 11 December 2026 (at 16h00). Applications received after the closing date and time will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide general human resource administrative support.
  • Provide clerical support for recruitment and selection processes.
  • Provide information, guidance, assistance and training on Recruitment and Selection related matters to all customers.
  • Compilation of statistics in respect of the advertising and filling of vacant posts.

Requirements

  • A Senior Certificate/National Certificate (Vocational) NQF Level 4, plus.
  • A valid drivers’ licence (minimum code B).
  • Knowledge, Skills and Competencies Required
  • Knowledge of human resource administration, recruitment and selection processes
  • Working knowledge of the applicable public-service legislative and regulatory framework
  • Knowledge of records management, document storage and retrieval procedures
  • Understanding of registry practices, confidentiality requirements and document control
  • Knowledge of PERSAL and other relevant human resource information systems
  • Knowledge of Departmental HR policies and procedures
  • Knowledge of compliance with POPIA and confidentiality requirements
  • Knowledge of Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA)
  • Computer literacy, including Microsoft Word, Excel, Outlook
  • Planning, organising and time-management skills
  • Written and verbal communication skills
  • Customer service and telephone etiquette skills
  • Datacapturing and record-keeping skills
  • Interpersonal skills
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively as part of a team
  • The ideal candidate should be honest, reliable, innovative/creative, have the ability to work under pressure, individually and as part of a team, and the ability to maintain confidentiality.
  • He/she must have good interpersonal relations, be trustworthy, loyal, have a friendly disposition and be efficient and responsible.
