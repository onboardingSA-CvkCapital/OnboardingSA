About the role

Junior Assistant Front of House (FOH) Manager Position Overview • Position Title: Junior Assistant Front of House Manager • Department: Restaurants (Bistro Sixteen82 / Tryn) • Division: Hospitality • Reporting To: Senior FOH Manager • Second Level Superior: Restaurant Manager • Direct Reports: Waitrons (17), Bartenders (4), Runners (8), Hosts (3) • Salary: R15 000 – R20 000 CTC per month Role Summary The Junior Assistant Front of House Manager assists in overseeing, planning, organizing, training, and leading daily operational activities to achieve restaurant objectives in sales, cost management, staff retention, guest satisfaction, food quality, cleanliness, and sanitation. This role supports senior management in driving high service standards and fostering an energized, connected, and productive team environment. Key Result Areas & Responsibilities 1. FOH Supervision & Operations (40% Allocation) • Ensure all food and beverage products are presented and served in strict accordance with fine-dining standards. • Assist in creating shift rosters matched to business volume to optimize labor costs and shift coverage. • Support staff training and continuous professional and managerial development. • Inspect front-of-house equipment regularly to ensure proper cleanliness and operational maintenance. • Monitor daily breakages and waste, reporting discrepancies to senior management. • Assist in enforcing company personnel policies, administering fair and prompt corrective actions when required. 2. Guest Satisfaction & Service (30% Allocation) • Maintain an inviting atmosphere and ensure guests receive prompt, friendly, and responsive service at all times. • Build positive, lasting relationships with regular clients, guests, and estate residents. • Act as a primary point of contact for resolving guest feedback and customer complaints efficiently. 3. General Operations, Administration & Culture (13% Allocation) • General Duties (10%): Prepare required operational documentation, reports, and shift schedules on time. Assist on the service floor during peak periods, utilize POS systems, and ensure clear communication with kitchen and management teams. • Core Culture & Principles (3%): Apply leadership principles: consider the business as a whole, take accountability, embody professional excellence, invite feedback, and learn from operational experiences. 4. Stock, Reservations & Cash Management (7%+ Allocation) • Stock Control (4%): Receive inventory in line with delivery policies, conduct daily stock counts, and record variances. • Cash Up & Handover (3%): Handle cash and digital payments using established reconciliation procedures, assist with daily cash-ups, and ensure detailed shift handovers. • Reservations: Coordinate with hosts to capture bookings accurately in DinePlan and optimize table seating capacity. Ensure continuous staff menu and product knowledge training. 5. Health, Safety & Cleaning (8% Allocation) • Health & Safety (5%): Ensure full compliance with food safety, labor laws, and workplace health and safety regulations. • Hygiene & Maintenance (3%): Execute and inspect cleaning rosters to ensure front-of-house areas are scrupulously clean daily.
