About the role

FirstRand is hiring a Branch Delivery Operations Manager, based in Vredenburg. To execute and implement operational policies, procedures, risk and compliance processes to mitigate risk and improve operational efficiencies by providing support and solutions to ensure optimal operational efficiency to deliver an excellent customer experience Hello Future Branch Delivery Operations Manager As part of our FNB Points of Presence (POP) team, the Branch Delivery Operations Manager plays a critical role in executing and implementing operational policies, procedures, risk, and compliance processes within the branch. This role is accountable for improving operational efficiencies, managing risk, and enabling optimal service delivery to ensure an excellent and consistent customer experience. The Branch Delivery Operations Manager provides operational leadership, practical solutions, and people management to ensure day-to-day branch operations align to FNB’s operational strategy, governance standards, and customer expectations. Now’s the time to imagine your potential in a leadership role where operational excellence and service delivery come together.

Responsibilities

  • Execute and implement operational policies, procedures, and compliance processes to mitigate risk and monitor and continuously review operational performance to deliver sustainable results
  • Compile and present operational and performance reports to guide informed business decisions
  • Analyse data and trends to identify opportunities for efficiency and improved service outcomes
  • Plan and deliver value added process improvements, initiatives, and services aligned to operational strategy
  • Support the development of tactical plans and translate these into effective operational execution
  • Ensure branch operations consistently meet or exceed customer service expectations
  • Manage and lead employees by creating an environment that supports growth, accountability, and performance excellence
  • Drive a strong culture of compliance, ensuring adherence to legislative, audit, and governance requirements
  • Partner with stakeholders to resolve operational challenges and implement practical solutions

Requirements

  • Qualification & Experience Requirement
  • Minimum Qualification: Grade 12/ NQF Level 4
  • Preferred Qualification: NQF Level 5 Certificate in Banking, Business Management, or related fields
  • 4 to 5 years of experience in branch operations, or operational management within banking or financial services
  • You will be an ideal candidate if you possess the following
  • Strong understanding of operational risk, compliance, audit, and governance requirements
  • Demonstrated ability to identify inefficiencies and implement process improvements
  • Experience in managing people, performance, and team development
  • Ability to translate strategy into operational plans and measurable outcomes
  • You will have access to
  • A leadership opportunity within a dynamic Points of Presence environment
  • Exposure to operational strategy execution and continuous improvement initiatives
  • Ongoing learning and development aligned to leadership and operational excellence
  • The opportunity to influence service quality, risk outcomes, and operational performance
  • You can be a match if you are
  • Operationally focused with a strong risk and compliance mindset
  • Results driven with a passion for service excellence and efficiency
  • Analytical and comfortable working with data, reports, and insights
  • A people centric leader who builds capable, engaged teams
  • Proactive, solution oriented, and innovative in your approach
  • Organised, disciplined, and able to manage complexity in a fast-paced environment
  • Committed to ethical conduct and high governance standards
