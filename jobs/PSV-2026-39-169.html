About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring an Artisan Foreman Grade A in Infrastructure Management: Southern Operations CD: Water Resource Infrastructure Operations and Maintenance Dir: Operations Southern SD: Civil Maintenance, based in Breede/Gouritz (Western Cape - Worcester). This is a permanent post, paying R397,329 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 131126/23 Closing date: 13 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Preference will be given to candidates from the geographical area.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise and construct civil technical services and structures according to specifications and within limits of production capability.
  • Produce objects with material and equipment according to job specification and recognized standards.
  • Supervise the maintenance of civil technical services.
  • Repair civil structures and facilities according to standards and specifications.
  • Service civil structures and facilities according to schedule.
  • Perform administrative and related functions.
  • Update register on maintained and repaired faults.
  • Compile and submit reports as required.
  • Provide inputs to the civil maintenance plan.
  • Ensure adherence to safety standards, requirements, and regulations.
  • Plan resources available and schedule maintenance work.
  • Repairs of faults.
  • Identify training needs for your team, Leave management and personnel performance management.
  • Supervise and mentor staff.
  • Ensure compliance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, 1993 and Building Regulations.
  • Responsible for construction works and general maintenance on the canals, weirs, dams, and all departmental infrastructures.
  • The incumbent must be willing to work away from the office and home and will be required to perform duties at all schemes throughout the Western Cape.

Requirements

  • An appropriate Civil Trade Test Certificate in one of the following civil-related trades: carpentry, bricklaying, plumbing, or general construction.
  • 5 years’ post-trade-test experience as a qualified Artisan.
  • The A valid unexpired driver’s licence (minimum Code B, a Code C1 or higher is required for the travel requirements of the post).
  • Knowledge of legal compliance.
  • Knowledge of technical analysis.
  • Knowledge of the PFMA and the OHSA.
  • Strong leadership and management ability.
  • Ability to communicate effectively at operational level.
  • Must be computer literate.
  • Knowledge of technical report writing.
  • Decision making and problem-solving ability.
  • Ability to work independently and in a team.
  • Analytical and creativity skills.
  • Communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Planning, organising, and execution.
  • Willingness to travel throughout South Africa when required for operational purposes.
