About the role

Applications are invited from suitably qualified candidates for the position of the Child & Youth Care Worker, to be based at our Magaliesburg Campus. The successful candidate will report to the Senior Child & Youth Care Worker. PURPOSE OF THE JOB: To implement care plans, supporting developmental programmes and working collaboratively with multidisciplinary teams to meet the individual needs of each youth. Responsibilities and Duties Teach youth social, self-help academic and independent living skills as per treatment plans ·        Participate in ITP review meetings. ·        Implement the approved ITP by engaging in life-space teaching, using appropriate teaching interactions. ·        Complete youth cards as applicable to their level in the system. Foster & develop each youth by providing their emotional well-being ·        Counsel youth through personal problems using rational problem solving intervention. ·        Refer youth to other member of the MDT when required and recommended by immediate supervisor. ·        Work with parents and MDT to address the youth’s self-image, confidence and sense of belonging. Provide safe physical care of youth ·        Be available to respond to and monitor youth at all times when on duty and on campus. ·        Practise monthly fire drills as per Health & Safety Committee schedule. ·        Identify and report any potential, internal & external risks to youth safety. General • Monitoring the behaviour and activities of youth & serve as a positive role model. • Conduct and participate in basic life skills teaching & provide the youth assistance with their homework/schoolwork. • Assists in the developing and implementation of individual treatment plans and IDP’s. • Prepare for admissions and discharges of youth & complete required program documentation. • Complete reports as required & assists with administering medications etc. • Conducts security / safety checks & transport youth as necessary. • Participates in meetings and in-service training as required. • Shift work as per roster, including some weekends and public holidays.
