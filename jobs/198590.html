About the role

Main Purpose: Responsible for the leadership, management and profitable execution of all service-related activities within the company, including workshop refurbishment, field service operations, and fixed-price maintenance and repair projects. The role ensures the safe, efficient and cost-effective utilisation of resources while delivering high-quality services that meet customer expectations, contractual obligations, client specifications and Pressure Equipment Regulations. The Services Manager serves as the single point of accountability for service delivery performance, operational efficiency, quality, safety, customer satisfaction and profitability across all service functions. Key Performance Areas include: Service Operations Management • Lead and manage all workshop, field service and fixed-price project activities. • Ensure services are delivered safely, efficiently and in accordance with customer requirements and company standards. • Develop and implement operational plans to achieve business objectives. • Establish and maintain service delivery processes, procedures and performance measures. • Drive operational excellence and continuous improvement initiatives. Workshop Management • Oversee the refurbishment, repair, testing and inspection of valves and associated equipment. • Ensure effective scheduling and prioritisation of workshop workloads. • Monitor productivity, quality and turnaround times. • Ensure workshop equipment, tooling and test facilities are adequately maintained and utilised. • Drive adherence to quality standards and approved repair procedures. Field Service Management • Manage the planning and execution of site-based service activities. • Coordinate labour, equipment and subcontractor resources for maintenance outages and customer projects including mobilisation and timely demobilisation. • Ensure compliance with site-specific health, safety and quality requirements. • Review field service reports and performance outcomes. • Maintain high levels of customer satisfaction during site execution activities. Fixed-Price Project Management • Oversee the execution of fixed-price refurbishment, maintenance and repair contracts aligned with NEC contract requirements. • Compile project plans, monitor project schedules, budgets and deliverables. • Manage project risks and identify opportunities to improve margins. • Ensure compliance with contractual requirements. • Oversee variation orders, scope changes and claims where applicable. • Ensure projects are completed on time, within budget and to the required quality standards. Financial and Commercial Performance • Manage service-related budgets and expenditure. • Monitor job costing, profitability and gross margins. • Implement cost-control measures to maximise profitability. • Review labour utilisation and productivity metrics. • Support the development of accurate quotations and execution plans for service opportunities. • Provide operational input into tenders and commercial proposals. Resource Planning and People Management • Lead, motivate and develop service personnel. • Manage performance, training and skills development. • Plan staffing requirements across workshop and field services. • Ensure optimal utilisation of permanent employees and contracted labour resources. • Foster teamwork and collaboration across service functions. Health, Safety, Environment and Quality (HSEQ) • Promote and uphold a strong safety culture throughout the organisation. • Ensure compliance with all applicable legislation, customer requirements and company policies. • Lead incident investigations and implement corrective actions. • Champion quality assurance and continuous improvement initiatives. • Ensure compliance with quality management systems and procedures. Customer Relationship Management • Build and maintain strong working relationships with customers. • Act as the primary operational interface for service delivery matters. • Attend customer meetings, outage planning meetings and contract reviews. • Resolve customer concerns and service-related issues promptly. • Identify opportunities to expand service offerings and improve customer satisfaction. Strategic Contribution • Support the Executive Director in achieving the company's strategic objectives. • Contribute to business planning, growth initiatives and operational improvement programmes. • Identify new technologies, processes and service offerings that enhance competitiveness. • Assist in developing a high-performance, customer-focused service organisation. • Any other job-related duties as required from time to time. Competencies: Leadership • Strong people leadership and team development skills. • Ability to motivate and manage multi-disciplinary teams.Commercial Acumen • Understanding of project costing, profitability and contract management. • Ability to make sound business decisions that improve financial performance.Operational Excellence • Strong planning, scheduling and organisational skills. • Focus on productivity, efficiency and continuous improvement.Customer Focus • Strong relationship-building and communication skills. • Commitment to delivering exceptional service and customer value.Problem Solving • Analytical and solution-oriented thinker. • Ability to make effective decisions under pressure. Preferred Requirements: • Minimum - Technical qualification in Engineering, Maintenance Engineering or related discipline; or a qualified artisan with extensive valve industry experience. • Preferred - National Diploma or Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Project Management qualification, Management or Leadership training. • Advantageous - B.Eng. degree or equivalent. • 10 Years' experience in industrial maintenance, valve refurbishment, engineering services or a related field. • 5 Years' experience in a management or supervisory role. • Experience managing workshop operations and field service personnel. • Experience executing fixed-price maintenance, refurbishment or outage projects. • Strong understanding of industrial valves, maintenance practices and repair methodologies. • Proven track record in managing operational performance and profitability. • Strong technical skills in Mechanical Engineering. • Computer literate (MS Office).
