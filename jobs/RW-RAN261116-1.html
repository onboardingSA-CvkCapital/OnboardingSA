About the role

Rand Water is hiring a Regional Operator Temp x3 (12 Months) in Bulk Water Services - Operations, based at Rietvlei, Glenvista. Rand Water is currently looking for the Regional Operator that is going to be responsible for the safe, efficient operation and optimisation of wastewater treatment works to ensure that effluent and sludge comply with legislation, Water Use Licence and Green Drop requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Operate and optimise wastewater treatment processes to achieve efficient plant performance and compliant effluent quality.
  • Conduct effluent sampling and analysis in accordance with approved procedures.
  • Operate sludge-handling facilities and ensure sludge management complies with applicable guidelines.
  • Perform routine plant inspections, identify defective equipment and report breakdowns.
  • Implement standard operating procedures and maintain accurate operational records.
  • Collect plant-performance data and prepare weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual and incident reports.
  • Monitor analytical results, identify process deviations and implement corrective actions.
  • Prepare and maintain Green Drop audit files and update the Integrated Regulatory Information System (IRIS).
  • Provide on-the-job training and skills transfer to municipal plant personnel.
  • Liaise with maintenance teams, Water Services Authorities, the Department of Water and Sanitation, and other relevant stakeholders.
  • Enforce occupational health, safety and environmental requirements, and report identified hazards and incidents.

Requirements

  • Qualification: BSc or BTech in Chemical Engineering, Water Care Technology, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Professional registration: Eligible for registration with the Department of Water and Sanitation as a Class V Process Controller.
  • Experience: At least five years’ relevant experience in the operation, maintenance and management of municipal wastewater treatment processes, including activated sludge plants.
  • Knowledge
  • Sound understanding of wastewater treatment processes, laboratory analysis and basic chemistry or scientific principles.
  • Knowledge of National Water Act, Green Drop Certification, the Occupational Health and Safety Act, the National Water Act and the National Environmental Management Act
  • Basic knowledge and application of ISO 14001 and ISO 9001 requirements.
  • Skills
  • Strong written and verbal communication, administration, analytical thinking, fault-finding and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to use computer systems for record-keeping, data management and reporting.
  • Administrative skills.
  • Problem solving skills.
  • Attitude
  • Willingness and ability to work in wastewater treatment environments involving exposure to hazardous chemicals, untreated sewage and sludge while complying with prescribed safety procedures.
  • Demonstrates personal accountability, integrity, safety consciousness and a proactive approach, with the ability to remain composed under pressure, collaborate effectively with stakeholders and consistently follow operational and regulatory requirements.
