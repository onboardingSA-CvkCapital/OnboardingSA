About the role

The Government Pensions Administration Agency is hiring a Deputy Director: Policy and Research, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. Strategic Support Services Permanent The purpose of this role is to lead and coordinate the development, review and implementation of the GPAA’s policy and research frameworks. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.gpaa.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: DD/P&R/2026/11- Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) No late applications will be considered Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: One permanent position of Deputy Director: Policy and Research is currently available at the Government Pensions Administration Agency (GPAA): Strategic Support Services Unit

Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for a wide variety of tasks Develop, review and implement policy framework systems.
  • Lead and manage the development, review and implementation of the Policy on Policymaking to regulate and standardise the formulation of policies within GPAA.
  • Develop and manage standard operating procedures for the drafting and review of policies as guided by the GPAA Policy on Policy making.
  • Coordinate the development of new policies in accordance with the approved policymaking procedures and applicable legislative and regulatory requirements.
  • Develop and maintain the annual policy review programme and monitor compliance with review timeframes.
  • Coordinate policy reviews with business units and provide technical and advisory support in accordance with operational needs.
  • Identify policy gaps, overlaps and inconsistencies and facilitate corrective action to streamline and/or rationalise as deemed appropriate.
  • Coordinate the consultation of new and reviewed policies with relevant business units and governance structures such as the Policy Task Team and Policy Steering Committee and fulfil the Secretariat function for governance structures.
  • Develop and maintain an accurate and up-to-date central policy repository on the relevant GPAA platforms.
  • Provide advice on policy reform within the pensions administration environment.
  • Develop a research agenda and protocols to establish a credible knowledge base.
  • Continuously scan the pensions industry and government administration landscape to identify emerging trends likely to impact the GPAA.
  • Develop and maintain a Research Agenda aligned to organisational strategic priorities, pension administration gaps/challenges and government research frameworks.
  • Establish and maintain protocols for managing internal and external research, leveraging appropriate designs and methodologies to guide research projects.
  • Develop and communicate evidence-based research reports and recommendations with relevant stakeholders to improve pension administration.
  • Ensure research findings inform strategic decision making, planning, policy formulation, and reform within the GPAA.
  • Maintain the GPAA research repository and facilitate the dissemination and utilisation of research outputs.
  • Lead the establishment of adequate research capabilities within the GPAA.
  • Develop and implement relevant frameworks for monitoring and reporting on policy and research activities.
  • Develop and maintain dashboards and management reports reflecting the status of policy developments, reviews and approvals.
  • Develop and maintain mechanisms to ensure accurate, credible and timely reporting on research projects.
  • Monitor compliance with approved policy and research protocols and escalate delays, risks and bottlenecks.
  • Provide regular management information to the Director
  • Strategic Management and relevant governance structures
  • Address and coordinate the resolution of internal and external audit findings relating to the policy and research management functions
  • Maintain appropriate records, evidence and audit trails to support governance and assurance processes
  • Facilitate and coordinate stakeholder awareness and knowledge management
  • Ensure policies are accessible and communicated to all staff through appropriate platforms
  • Coordinate engagement with business units, governance structures and relevant internal and external stakeholders on policy and research matters.
  • Facilitate internal awareness and training sessions on policies, the Policy on Policymaking and broader GPAA Research Agenda.
  • Facilitate knowledge-sharing sessions and the dissemination of research findings.
  • Strengthen institutional knowledge management through effective maintenance of policy and research repositories.

Requirements

  • A three-year Degree/National Diploma in in Public Policy, Political Sciences, Public Administration/Management, Law, Business Management, Development Studies or an equivalent qualification with at least 5-6 years’ experience in Public Policy and Research of which 3 years is in an Assistant Director/Middle management role.
  • Knowledge of Policy formulation, Research methodologies and Designs, Computer literacy, Project management, Corporate Governance, Ethics, Risk Management, Public service Act, Monitoring and evaluation principles.
  • High level communication skills.
  • Data collection and analysis.
  • Qualitative and quantitative analysis.
  • Policy analysis.
  • Problem solving capability.
  • Critical and conceptual thinking.
  • Ability to analyse and interpret research findings.
  • Proficiency in professional report writing.
  • Quality assurance experience.
  • Strategic decision-making.
  • Analytical and innovative.
  • Results-orientated.
  • Ethical and accountable.
  • Resilience and reliability under pressure.
  • Adaptable team player with strong emotional intelligence and assertive communication skills.
