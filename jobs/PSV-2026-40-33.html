Responsibilities

  • Planning for Students Access
  • Plan and manage enrolments in line with the approved APP targets
  • Ensure a smooth process of applications and admissions of students
  • Engage in quality checks for the submission of enrolment data
  • Ensure that the targeted number of NSFAS allocations are fully utilised
  • Submit enrolment data
  • Management Teaching and Learning
  • Plan and implement teaching and learning plan as per the national directives
  • Plan and implement students support services framework in line with the national guidelines
  • Ensure internal systems and controls to support students towards an improved curriculum delivery
  • Ensure students and lecturers attend classes as planned
  • Compile and submit teaching and learning data
  • Ensure effectiveness and efficiency of Campus Academic Committee
  • Ensure that staff and students are placed for Work Integrated Learning (WIL) and WBE
  • Engage with Campus Representative Council
  • Management Of Occupational-Directed Programmes
  • Implement artisan training as part of the PQM
  • Equip workshops with up-to-date, industry relevant equipment and technology
  • Obtain accreditation for all artisan related programmes
  • Register workshops as trade test centres
  • Management Of Centre
  • Manage all the operational activities at the Centre
  • Conduct performance management reviews, identify service gaps and implement personal and/or capacity development plans of all staff members
  • Manage and monitor contract for security service providers at Campus level
  • Effectively manage and maintain all physical resources in the Campus and implement Occupational Health and safety policies
  • Implement systems for proper assets & stock management in the Campus
  • Ensure conduct of regular stock and asset inspections and verifications
  • Approve all asset transfers and exchange amongst Campuses according to prescribed policy and procedures
  • Develop and implement Risk Management Plan to mitigate identified risks
  • Initiate, maintain and manage financial sustainability at the Centre
  • Develop a draft Centre budget in line with central office guidelines
  • Monitor actual financial performance of the Centre against budget
  • Implement and manage the approved budget and oversee the daily petty cash usage at the Centre
  • Endorse all Campus procurement submissions in compliance with the SCM policy
  • Ensure full compliance to national policies, standard operating procedures, guidelines and management plans by ensuring compliance of all assessment activities across the Centre and also delivery points within the Campus.
  • Ensure effectiveness and efficiency of Centre Assessments Committee.
  • Ensure implementation of ICT Policies.
  • Audit number of Centre Infrastructure and facilities.
  • Report on % utilization of infrastructure and facilities.
  • Comply with DHET and College policies and procedures.

Requirements

  • A Grade 12/Matric/Level 4 Certificate.
  • An appropriate National Diploma/bachelor’s degree in engineering or equivalent qualification.
  • An Artisan Trade qualification and experience as an artisan facilitator, assessor and moderator is a prerequisite.
  • A qualification in Business Management including project management an added advantage.
  • 7 years’ work experience in the skills development sector, with at least 3 years’ experience at management level.
  • Demonstration of an understanding of and experience in the implementation of all learning programmes, particularly to trades and artisan development in a SETA environment is required.
  • Knowledge and understanding of SETAs, NSDS and legislation governing the implementation of artisan development programmes.
  • Knowledge of the Skills Development Act, 1998.
  • Knowledge of Skills Development Levies Act (SDLA), Knowledge of the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA) Act, 1995.
  • Knowledge of Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHS).
  • Computer literacy.
  • A valid driver’s licence.
  • An understanding of transformation issues and capacity building processes in the TVET sector.
  • Knowledge of CET Act.
  • Leadership and management skills, project management skills and communication inclusive of presentation skills.
  • Human Resource Development inclusive of Supervisory Management.
  • Policy development inclusive of relevant Legislation.
  • Interpersonal skills.
  • Good problem solving and analytical skills.
  • Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.
  • Ability to work independently as well as in a team.
  • Planning, organising, leading and control skills.
  • Research, report writing and presentation and innovation skills.
