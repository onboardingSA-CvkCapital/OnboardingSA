About the role

Eskom Distribution is looking for Technical Officials, positions/task is T06. The Department is Operations & Maintenance, and the Business Unit is Limlanga Cluster. The location Mpumalanga, Highveld Sector, Kammelnek & Mokobolle CNCs. Area of specialisation is to ensure the continuity of supply to customers by building, maintaining and repairing infrastructure and plant according to dead and limited live work practices, guidelines and service agreements on the electrical network up to and including 33 kilovolts (kV).
