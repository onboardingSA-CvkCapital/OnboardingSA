About the role

Financial Accountant VKB Agri Processors | Reitz, Free State About VKB Agri Processors VKB Agri Processors forms part of the VKB Group, one of South Africa's leading agricultural businesses. Our operations span the food and agricultural value chain, including processing, logistics, manufacturing, and market distribution. Rooted in agriculture and driven by innovation, we offer talented professionals the opportunity to build meaningful careers while contributing to an industry that impacts communities across the country. Build a Career that Feeds the Future At VKB Agri Processors, finance is more than numbers. It's about supporting a business that plays a vital role in South Africa's agricultural value chain, from farm to consumer. We are looking for a detail-oriented and commercially minded Financial Accountant to join our finance team. This role offers the opportunity to work across multiple entities, drive financial excellence, contribute to strategic decision-making, and play a key role in a dynamic and growing agribusiness environment. If you enjoy combining technical accounting expertise with business insight and continuous improvement, we'd like to hear from you. What You'll Do As Financial Accountant, you will be responsible for ensuring accurate financial reporting, driving compliance, supporting financial planning, and contributing to business performance. Key Areas of Responsibility Financial Reporting & Analysis • Prepare monthly management accounts and financial reports. • Compile annual financial statements in accordance with IFRS. • Analyse financial performance and provide meaningful insights to management. • Ensure balance sheet reconciliations are accurate and completed on time.Budgeting & Forecasting • Support the annual budgeting process. • Assist with forecasting and financial planning activities. • Analyse variances and identify opportunities for improvement.Tax & Regulatory Compliance • Prepare and submit VAT, provisional tax, and income tax returns. • Manage SARS queries and compliance requirements. • Ensure adherence to relevant tax and financial legislation.Audit & Risk Management • Prepare supporting schedules and documentation for internal and external audits. • Respond to audit requests professionally and timeously. • Ensure compliance with company policies, IFRS, and internal controls.Systems & Continuous Improvement • Support and optimise financial processes and systems. • Identify efficiency improvements and strengthen existing controls. • Contribute to finance and business improvement projects.Team Collaboration • Support and guide junior finance team members. • Build strong relationships with internal stakeholders. • Promote a culture of accountability, teamwork, and continuous improvement.What We're Looking For Minimum Requirements Qualifications • BCom Accounting or equivalent qualification. • Completed articles, SAICA, SAIPA, CIMA, or similar professional affiliation will be advantageous.Experience • 3-5 years' experience in a financial accounting environment. • Proven experience in financial reporting, budgeting, taxation, and audits. • Experience working on ERP systems, preferably Microsoft Dynamics NAV.Technical Knowledge • IFRS • South African tax legislation • Management accounting and reporting • Budgeting and forecasting • Treasury and cash flow management •
