Responsibilities

  • Financial Transactions and Accounting Records.
  • General Ledger Management and Reconciliations.
  • Financial Reporting and Period-End Procedures.
  • Payments, Receipts and Cashbook Accounting.
  • Financial Compliance, Internal Controls and Audit Support.
  • Financial Records Management and Operational Support.
  • Application for SETA discretion grants, implementation of projects, and any other related activities.
  • Assist area offices to compile budget forecast.
  • Assist with compiling the MTEF budget.
  • Analyse, interpret and implement the treasury guidelines.
  • Assist with the development of templates for the collection of budget information from line functionaries.
  • Assess where shifting of funds/virements is required and possible by reviewing expenditure against budget and make recommendations.
  • Assist to manage budget planning for raw setting water price, assist to ensure adequate utilisation of budget and Monitor compliance with financial prescripts.
  • Process budget shifts in accordance with applicable financial procedures and confirms budget availability before transactions are processed.
  • Coordinate and consolidate variance explanations from relevant Managers and ensure timely submission of supporting information.
  • Monitor and report on programme expenditure, identifying variances and expenditure trends.

Requirements

  • Grade 12/Matric/NCV Level 4 Certificate.
  • An undergraduate National Diploma/Degree NQF level 6 or 7 in Financial Management/Accounting/Cost Management Accounting as recognized by SAQA and any relevant qualification. at least 2 to 3 years’ relevant work experience in CET environment of which 2 years’ experience must be in financial accounting, general ledger administration, financial transaction processing, reconciliations or financial reporting, accompanied by the letter from the employer.
  • Knowledge
  • Financial accounting principles and practices
  • General ledger management and account reconciliations
  • Financial transaction processing and cashbook accounting
  • Preparation of financial reports and supporting schedules
  • Applicable financial management legislation
  • Regulations and institutional policies
  • Internal controls
  • Audit requirements and financial records management Computerised accounting and financial management systems

  • Audit requirements and financial records management
