About the role

A. Key Responsibilities The African Parks Incubation Programme (AP-IP) Funding and Reporting Manager (FRM) is a dual function, supporting the Incubation Programme's funding and reporting needs, as well as implementing project-specific needs, including admin, events and travel organisation, and Programme data management. The FRM is accountable for the effective and compliant delivery of IP funding and reporting needs, and for effectively packaging the IP's and partner’s identified funding needs. The FRM will ensure that all grant proposals, reports, and data align with the Incubation Programme plan, including support for funding (Fundraising strategies) and reporting needs of Incubation Partners. Additionally, he/she will contribute to the organisation's special events and exchange visits between AP and Incubation Partners, and to APIP data management. The position supports the APIP Manager and other team members in implementing the APIP strategy and annual work plans. This multifaceted position serves as a critical bridge between the AP-IP's strategy and operational reality and funders' objectives, preferences, and compliance requirements. The role requires strong communication skills, including excellent written and oral English (French is a plus), coordination and project management skills, a strong grasp of donor regulations and interests, and solid budgeting and monitoring & evaluation skills. Capturing the impacts of the AP-IP and partners' work is critical to the role.
