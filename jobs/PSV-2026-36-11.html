About the role

The Department of Employment and Labour is hiring a Medical Officer Grade 1, based in Provincial Office Limpopo. This is a permanent post, paying R1,041,402 – R1,121,241 a year. How to apply: Chief Director: Provincial Operations: Private Bag X 9368, Polokwane, 0700 Or hand deliver at 42a Schoeman Street, Polokwane or Email address: CFJobs-LP@labour.gov.za Reference number to quote: HR4/4/6/06 Closing date: 23 October 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Priority will be given to African Male, African Female, Coloured Male, Indian Male, Indian Female, White Male, White Female

Responsibilities

  • Manage medical advisory services for all COID services.
  • Manage and monitor healthcare.
  • Manage medical claims processing.
  • Manage and provide support to medical service providers.
  • Manage all resources in the sub-directorate.
