About the role

Omnia is hiring a Technician (Instrumentation) in Manufacturing, Operations and Supply Chain (MOS), Electrical & Instrumentation, based in Sasolburg, Free State. The role reports to the Engineer (Instrumentation). As a Technician (Instrumentation), you will play a critical role in improving equipment reliability, operational efficiency and maintenance performance across Omnia's production operations. This position is focused on specialist fault-finding, advanced problem-solving, equipment reliability improvement and maintenance optimisation within a world-class manufacturing environment. Working closely with engineering, reliability and maintenance teams, you will analyse equipment performance, identify recurring failures, support root-cause investigations and drive initiatives that improve plant reliability and operational performance. Your expertise will directly contribute to reducing downtime, optimising maintenance strategies and ensuring safe, reliable and cost-effective operations.

Responsibilities

  • Reliability & Equipment Performance
  • Analyse Mean Time To Repair (MTTR) and Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) data to identify equipment reliability trends and improvement opportunities.
  • Identify problematic equipment and determine preventative maintenance and asset management focus areas.
  • Review work order execution, schedule compliance and resource utilisation to improve maintenance effectiveness.
  • Review critical spares, preventative maintenance and asset management requirements to ensure equipment reliability.
  • Support and participate in root-cause analysis investigations and advanced problem-solving initiatives.
  • Execute reliability improvement projects that enhance plant performance and asset availability.
  • Maintenance Excellence
  • Provide specialist fault-finding and troubleshooting support on instrumentation and plant equipment.
  • Apply structured maintenance and reliability methodologies to improve equipment performance.
  • Work closely with the Reliability Engineer and maintenance teams to address recurring equipment failures.
  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives aligned to asset care and world-class manufacturing principles.
  • Safety, Compliance & Continuous Improvement
  • Ensure compliance with applicable Occupational Health and Safety legislation and statutory requirements.
  • Promote and drive the implementation of 5S principles within the maintenance environment.
  • Support initiatives that improve workplace safety, housekeeping and operational standards.
  • Contribute to a culture of continuous improvement and operational excellence.
  • Cost Management & Administration
  • Approve purchase orders within designated authority limits.
  • Obtain quotations from external service providers and suppliers.
  • Support effective budget control and cost management initiatives within the maintenance environment.
  • Maintain accurate records and provide technical information to support maintenance planning and decision-making.

Requirements

  • Qualifications
  • N6 or B.Tech in Engineering.
  • Relevant Trade Test (Red Seal).
  • Diploma in a relevant Engineering discipline.
  • CRMP (Certified Reliability Maintenance Professional) qualification.
  • Experience
  • 5-10 years' experience in a maintenance environment as a Technician.
  • Proven asset care and equipment reliability experience.
  • Experience in maintenance planning, root-cause analysis and reliability improvement initiatives.
  • Experience within a manufacturing, industrial, chemical or processing environment will be advantageous.
  • Key Competencies
  • Strong instrumentation fault-finding and diagnostic capability.
  • Advanced problem-solving and root-cause analysis skills.
  • Knowledge of maintenance systems, asset management and reliability methodologies.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office and computerised maintenance management systems (CMMS).
  • Strong planning, organising and decision-making capability.
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively across functions and teams.
  • High levels of integrity, accountability and reliability.
  • Strong customer service orientation and stakeholder engagement skills.
  • Adaptability, initiative and a results-driven mindset.
