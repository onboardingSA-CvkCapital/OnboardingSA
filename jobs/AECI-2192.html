About the role

AECI is hiring a Production Manager, based in Umbogintwini. Lead the team in successful completion of the production plan in accordance with goals. Achieve Safety, Environmental, Quality and OEE targets.

Responsibilities

  • Site KPA
  • Responsible and accountable for the effective leadership of production at the site
  • Identify “Best Practices", newest technologies and benchmark the plant against these.
  • Implement best practice for plant through put, quality improvement and energy reduction.
  • Develop and prepare “concepts” into tangible projects (for which funding can be secured)
  • Optimize existing processes to maximize production and reduce costs
  • Ensure that production costs are maintained within operational budgets.
  • Investigate plant bottlenecks, identify root causes, propose and implement solutions
  • Research and propose trials for new processes and equipment.
  • Responsible for all managerial functions including maintaining discipline and enabling the development of all production employees.
  • Manage and direct activities with a focus on creating a safe working environment through a zero-tolerance approach to unsafe practices and adherence to manufacturing best practices.
  • Responsible for excellent housekeeping and SHEQ standards.
  • Driver of daily Goal alignment meetings and engagement with production staff.
  • Participate in the production budgeting process on an annual basis and monitor and control the costs thereof.
  • Driving initiatives to ensure full compliance with safety, health and environmental objectives in line with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001
  • Ensure the effective commissioning of section or equipment to a specific state:
  • Ensure appropriate documentation is available for batch start of any new process.
  • MOC for any new process done is conducted and assessed appropriately.
  • Ensure Effective communication of process condition
  • Inform and Resolve process condition upsets.
  • Communicate in an accurate, concise, clear, and prompt manner to all departments.
  • Take correct action to solve any deviations to process conditions and NCR.
  • Log incidents on smart sheet on any deviation from process procedures and standards for investigations.
  • Ensure effective administration
  • Ensure that the hand over logbook is completed accurately.
  • Sign off work plans, logs, miscellaneous and records accurately.
  • Administration and Execution of Permit to work
  • Efficient control over Utility and raw material usage, to prevent wastage.
  • Sheq
  • Ensure that all SHEQ standards are maintained and updated.
  • Ensure JPlant Foremans and SHE Reps carry out their functions.
  • Ensure that Jplant Foremans and Operators, rectify and report safety hazards/near misses promptly.
  • Process safety management element owner.
  • Lead incident investigations.
  • Staff Interaction
  • Carry out PJOs on Team Leaders, identify areas that require attention.
  • Ensure that effective management is carried out on the production line.
  • Ensure Effective communication within the production team.
  • Draft and ensure compliance to Training matrix of all production employees.
  • Factory Development
  • Aid in determining short, medium and long-term factory development strategies.
  • Conduct feasibility studies for all new factory development ideas.
