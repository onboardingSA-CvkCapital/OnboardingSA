About the role

The Department of Community Safety (Gauteng) is hiring a Senior Admin Officer: Business Analyst in Information Communication Technology, based in Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applicants must apply online at: www.gautengonline.gov.za / http://professionaljobcentre.gpg.gov.za. Reference number to quote: REFS/057017 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • To analyse the various requirements of the organization and to bridge the gap between Business and Information and Communications Technology (ICT).
  • Planning strategically - The analysis of the organisation's strategic business needs.
  • Operating/Business Model Analysis - The definition and analysis of the organization's policies and market business approaches.
  • Identify new business opportunities.
  • Conduct feasibility studies. analyse users’ needs to be able to conceptualize, maintain and document programs and applications.
  • Requirements elicitation and documentation.
  • Develop project proposal.
  • Develop business case.
  • IT/Technical business analysis - the interpretation of business rules and requirements for technical and business systems.
  • Process definition and design.
  • Process identification and process modelling.
  • The business process modelling and design.
  • Solution assessment and validation through verification of the correctness of a proposed solution, support the implementation of a solution and assess possible shortcomings in the implementation. continuous process improvement.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 plus a 3-year National Diploma (NQF level 6)/Degree (NQF level 7) in ICT (Computer science or Informatics), Business Analysis certificate will serve as a strong advantage.
  • At least 2-3 years' experience in similar position.
  • All valid relevant driving licenses (A and EC).
  • No criminal record or any pending cases against you.
  • Knowledge and Skills:
  • System development Cycle
  • Business analysis techniques
  • Knowledge and understanding of different IT processes
  • Relevant Public Service Regulations
  • Understanding of expectations of customers
  • A good understanding of the organization's business and how IT contributes to it
  • Very strong communication skills:
  • Oral, written, presentation, Facilitation
  • Requirements elicitation and documentation
  • Technical understanding with ability to translate into business concepts
  • Feasibility studies
  • Ability to work under fire, handle stressful situations in a calm manner
  • Relationship management and conflict resolution skills
  • The ability to bring together and work with a team of people with varied backgrounds technical experts and process owners
  • A good understanding of the organization's IT infrastructure, services provided and customers/users Business process Reengineering
  • Statistical, numerical and analytical principles and processes
  • Innovative thinking with service quality, and its improvement, within limits of costs and business Testing against Requirements
  • Object and Structured Analysis
  • Project management
  • System specification
  • Business performance Management
  • Customer relationship Management
  • Technical credibility
