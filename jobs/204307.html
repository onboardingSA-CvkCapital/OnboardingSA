About the role

A company that operates as a local touchpoint for a major global freight forwarding and logistics network, helping local businesses manage the complex movement of goods across national and international borders, is seeking a PMO Project Manager end-to-end leads complex, cross-functional technology and business transformation initiatives within scope, schedule, budget, and quality constraints. Responsibilities: • Lead end-to-end delivery of large, complex initiatives using Agile, Waterfall, or Hybrid methodologies. • Define project scope, deliverables, detailed plans, and resource allocations. • Oversee demand management, timeline tracking, resource constraints, and overall budget execution. • Direct testing activities, including test case formulation, validation, and sign-off. • Facilitate requirements elicitation workshops and translate business needs into detailed specifications. • Author Statements of Work (SOW), business cases, and project charters. • Maintain real-time visibility across project portfolios using PMO software, dashboards, and RAID logs. • Enforce PMO governance, stage-gate controls, policy compliance, and audit-ready documentation. • Proactively identify, log, and mitigate risks and dependencies. • Drive quality assurance standards, continuous improvement, and post-project benefits realization. • Serve as the primary point of contact across internal teams, executive leadership, and external partners. • Plan and execute communication strategies, lead project meetings, and facilitate Steering Committees. • Lead change management, business readiness, training transition, and operational handovers. • Coordinate across regional teams and international time zones as required. • Manage third-party vendors, implementation partners, and commercial commitments. • Direct project closure processes, operational handovers to support teams, and post-implementation surveys. Minimum Requirements: • Experience: 6+ years of IT project and program management experience delivering large-scale, complex initiatives within cost, schedule, and scope constraints. • Methodologies: Proven expertise in Agile, Waterfall, and Hybrid delivery frameworks. • Governance & Delivery: Strong command of PMO governance, stage-gate reviews, risk mitigation (RAID), and continuous improvement processes. • Technical & Data Proficiency: Skilled in project management tools (e.g., Wrike, Planisware, Jira), executive dashboard reporting, and advanced data analysis. • Stakeholder & Vendor Leadership: Exceptional negotiation, conflict resolution, and communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences up to executive leadership. Demonstrated success managing third-party vendors. • Independence: Proven ability to work autonomously, lead cross-functional teams, and solve complex operational challenges. • Preferred Experience & Education • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Business Management, Engineering, or a related field. • Certifications: PMP, PgMP, PMI-ACP, Certified ScrumMaster (CSM), or equivalent project management certifications. Benefits: Competitive salary based on experience (salary can potentially be more based on experience/skills) IF you meet the above requirements and want to make a career-changing move, apply today by emailing your CV to itcareers@hireresolve.za.com
