About the role

Job Title: Apprentice - Level Year 1 Location: Bloemfontein Company: Motus Bloemfontein We are currently seeking a motivated individual to join our team as an Apprentice - Level Year 1 at various dealership. As an Apprentice, you will have the opportunity to learn from experienced technicians and gain valuable hands-on experience in the automotive industry. Apprenticeship programme; assisting with the repair of vehicles while working under the supervision of a qualified Technician; fulfilling the MerSETA requirements of the trade, mastering level tests and qualifying. Responsibilities: - Assist senior technicians with various tasks such as diagnosing and repairing vehicles - Learn to use diagnostic equipment and tools - Follow safety protocols and procedures - Maintain a clean and organized work environment -  Attend scheduled technical/ level training courses. -  To assist with the repair of vehicles in order to obtain practical experience under the supervision/instruction of a Technician. -  Prepare thoroughly for level tests in order to qualify within the record time -  Perform any other reasonable duties which may be required from time to time. Requirements: - Matric certificate with mathematics and Physical science - Accredited college N3 certificate -  Valid, unendorsed driver’s license -  Must have a clear criminal record. - Interest in the automotive industry - Basic knowledge of mechanical systems - Strong work ethic and willingness to learn - Good communication skills - Physically fit for heavy work; able to physically complete the duties associated with the role. - Enjoy working with your hands. - Able to work in noisy environments. - Have good eyesight and hand-eye coordination. - Possess very strong problem solving/ trouble shooting - Ability to work in a fast-paced environment If you are passionate about cars and looking to kickstart your career in the automotive industry, we encourage you to apply for the Apprentice - Level Year 1 position at Motus Ford & Mazda in Ford Kroonstad. Join us in delivering exceptional service and driving success for our customers.
