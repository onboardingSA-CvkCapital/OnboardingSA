About the role

Shadow Global is an impact-driven organisation passionate about creating careers and tackling youth unemployment in South Africa. With extensive experience in the BPO and customer experience sector, we work with employer partners to identify, prepare and connect talented young South Africans, many of whom have historically been excluded from access to meaningful employment with sustainable career opportunities. As our Cape Town operations continue to grow, we are looking for an experienced, driven and highly organised Recruitment Consultant to join our team in Mitchells Plain. This is a high-volume recruitment environment, but success is not measured by numbers alone. We are looking for someone who can combine pace with judgement, building strong candidate pipelines while ensuring that the people we select have the potential, behaviours and readiness to succeed in the opportunities ahead of them. Key Responsibilities: • Proactively source and attract candidates through multiple channels • Build and maintain strong candidate pipelines aligned to employer demand forecasts, skills requirements, and geographic and cohort-based needs • Represent Shadow Global's values, brand, and purpose consistently in all candidate interactions • Screen and shortlist candidates against role-specific criteria and readiness benchmarks • Coordinate and conduct telephonic, virtual, and in-person interviews ensuring fair, unbiased, and structured selection processes • Support employer partner interviews and selection days where required • Coordinate and manage all recruitment-related checks • Ensure all candidate documentation is complete, accurate, and compliant with funder requirements, POPIA, and Shadow Global governance and audit standards • Maintain accurate recruitment trackers, databases, and reporting tools • Support pre-onboarding and onboarding processes in collaboration with Training & Operations, employer partners, Compliance, and Finance teams • Ensure candidates are fully prepared for Shadow Global's training programme, employer expectations, and professional standards • Uphold a positive, values-driven candidate experience throughout the journey • Continuously review and improve recruitment processes, tools, and templates • Stay informed of labour market trends, youth employment dynamics, and best practices in inclusive recruitment • Actively participate in team reflections, planning sessions, and capability-building initiatives
