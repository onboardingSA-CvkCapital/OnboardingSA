About the role

Laboratory TechnicianMontague Gardens, Cape TownR10 000 to R15 000 per month, depending on experience and qualificationsPermanentA well-established company based in Montague Gardens is seeking a reliable, accurate and detail-oriented Laboratory Technician to assist with laboratory testing, sample preparation, analysis and quality-control procedures.The successful candidate must be comfortable working with chemicals, laboratory equipment and technical documentation while maintaining strict safety, quality and housekeeping standards. Key Responsibilities • Receive, label, prepare and test samples according to prescribed procedures. • Perform titrations and other routine chemical or laboratory analyses. • Accurately record, calculate and report test results. • Assist with quality-control testing of raw materials and finished products. • Operate, clean and maintain laboratory equipment. • Calibrate laboratory instruments and report equipment faults. • Prepare chemical solutions, reagents and testing materials. • Maintain accurate laboratory records, registers and documentation. • Identify and report irregularities, deviations or non-conforming results. • Ensure samples and chemicals are stored and disposed of correctly. • Maintain a clean, organised and safe laboratory environment. • Follow company procedures and health, safety, quality and environmental standards. • Assist with stock control of chemicals, consumables and laboratory supplies. • Support continuous improvement within the laboratory and production environment. Minimum Requirements • Grade 12 / Matric with Mathematics and Physical Science. • A relevant qualification in Chemistry, Analytical Chemistry, Chemical Technology, Laboratory Technology or a related field would be advantageous. • Previous laboratory, quality-control or chemical-testing experience would be advantageous. • Knowledge of sample preparation, titrations and basic analytical techniques. • Ability to work accurately with measurements, calculations and test results. • Basic computer literacy, including Microsoft Excel and Word. • Good written and verbal communication skills. • Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy. • Good problem-solving and organisational skills. • Ability to follow technical procedures and safety instructions. • Able to work independently and as part of a team. • Willingness to work in a laboratory or manufacturing environment. • Must be reliable, responsible and willing to learn. • Must be able to travel reliably to Montague Gardens.
