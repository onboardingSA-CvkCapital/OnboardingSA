About the role

The Department of Tourism is hiring a Deputy Director: Change Management and Service Delivery Improvement Mechanism, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Email application: Recruitment6226@tourism.gov.za Reference number to quote: DT62/2026 Closing date: 20 November 2026 by 16:30 (Late applications will not be considered) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Requirements: Coloured Male and White Male candidates as well as Youth and People with Disabilities are encouraged to apply.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will be responsible for designing and implement Change Management initiatives, Developing, review and monitor the implementation of the departmental change management framework, Developing and implement change management strategies in response to change initiative projects in the department, Conducting culture surveys and audits to assess the Departmental change requirements, Determining change requirements aligned to changes in systems and processes (including IT processes and systems), Collaborating with and influence stakeholders on change requirements, as the custodian of change in the Department, Developing interventions to address change requirements, Networking, influence, and liaise with operational managers and staff to ensure change buyin and implementation, Identifying and develop relevant communique and supporting change management material, Identifying change agents within the Department and ensure their effective onboarding, Facilitating the development of training to capacitate the Departmental change agents, Continuously measure and evaluate results and progress, Coordinating the development and implementation of Departmental service delivery improvement planning, Coordinating the planning, development , implementation , monitoring, reporting and evaluation of continuous service delivery improvement within the department, Designing and co-ordinate implementation of client orientation strategy and policy, Ensuring that the department has developed a functioning, well-managed complaints and compliments management system that informs critical service delivery and citizen satisfaction areas of improvement as outlined in the Complaints and Compliments Management Framework, (CCMF), 2013, Establishing and maintain correspondence with clients and stakeholders and respond queries, Designing, implement, and maintain suggestion system to improve customer service, Conduct customer service improvement surveys (internal and external), Participating in the National service delivery and innovation fora, Developing and review service standards delivery charter in the department, Coordinating the development, review and implementation of the minimum standards for the level and quality of services the department will provide, Developing and review the departmental service Delivery Charter, Monitoring departmental performance against standards and produce annual reports, Implementing Operational Management Framework (OMF) in collaboration with the Organizational Development unit, Coordinating and monitor the implementation of the Batho Pele Programmes, Managing the customer service improvement programmes, Developing, review and monitor the implementation of Batho Pele Plan, Managing the Batho Pele Programmes in the department, Ensuring continuous reporting on Batho Pele compliance, Representing the Department at all functions of Batho Pele meetings and forums, Facilitating the departmental Know Your Rights campaigns and the Service Excellence Awards in the department, Establishing Departmental Batho Pele Forum and co-ordinate its programmes, Ensuring improved client satisfaction, customer relations and customer care plans.
