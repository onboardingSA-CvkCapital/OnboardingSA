About the role

BACKGROUND: African Parks is a non-profit conservation organisation that takes on responsibility for the long-term management of protected areas in partnership with governments and local communities. African Parks manages 24 protected areas in 13 countries covering over 20 million hectares. In South Sudan, African Parks supports the management of the Great Nile Migration Landscape (GNML), centred around Badingilo National Park, Boma National Park, and the Jonglei landscape. Covering more than 120,000 km², the landscape supports the world’s largest remaining terrestrial mammal migration and is of major ecological importance regionally and globally JOB OVERVIEW: The Community Development Manager leads African Parks’ community programme for Badingilo National Park and its surrounding communities and is accountable for the delivery of the Community Development Plan (CDP). As the Park’s lead for community and external stakeholder engagement, the role builds and maintains strong relationships with local communities, traditional authorities, county and payam authorities, government partners and relevant organisations, while building a strong constituency for conservation through the 3E+ Strategy—Engagement, Education and Economy—and the development of community-based natural resource management (CBNRM) approaches. The role also contributes to the development and implementation of participatory land-use plans by supporting community engagement, consultation and local-level validation. institutions and relevant partners. • Maintain a regular field presence and build trusted relationships across the Lafon, western boundary and Mangalla North-Gemeiza community zones. • Develop and implement engagement plans that reflect the governance arrangements, needs, risks and conservation priorities of each zone. • Establish and support representative community engagement mechanisms, with meaningful participation by women, youth, displaced people and other groups that may be excluded. • Maintain an up-to-date stakeholder map, identify emerging concerns early and advise the Park Manager and relevant departments on appropriate responses. • Represent the Park in community and local-government forums as delegated, and ensure that commitments are authorised, recorded and followed through. • Community Development Plan and 3E+ Delivery • Translate the CDP into annual workplans, budgets, implementation schedules and measurable performance targets. • Lead delivery of the 3E+ strategy across engagement, education and economic opportunity, calibrated to the priorities and conditions of each community zone. • Develop and manage education, livelihood, enterprise and community-capacity initiatives included in approved park plans and budgets. • Use clear selection criteria and transparent communication so that communities understand the Park's role, available resources and limits. • Develop partnerships with government, civil-society organisations and technical specialists where they add practical value to programme delivery. • Participatory Land Use
