About the role

Are you an organised, detail-driven Supply Chain Administrator / Stock Controller who thrives in a fast-paced manufacturing environment?A well-established manufacturing operation is looking for a hands-on individual to support both the Production and Supply Chain functions, ensuring that stock, procurement, documentation and reporting are accurately controlled from receipt through to final allocation.The RoleThis is a varied position combining stock control, supply chain administration, procurement support, inventory management and reconciliations. You will play a key role in maintaining accurate stock records across multiple locations while ensuring that all supporting documentation and system information is up to date. Key Responsibilities • Manage and monitor inventory across multiple warehouses and locations. • Conduct monthly stock counts and regular cycle counts. • Investigate and reconcile stock variances and discrepancies. • Verify physical stock against purchase orders, invoices, delivery notes and system records. • Monitor stock levels and identify shortages, excess or obsolete inventory. • Ensure accurate processing of all stock movements within inventory and accounting systems. • Manage imported stock and ensure all import documentation is complete and accurately captured. • Handle daily POD scanning, verification and reconciliation of discrepancies. • Request and evaluate supplier quotations and assist with sourcing. • Process and track consumables, pallets and other procurement requirements. • Track production consumable usage and support sustainability reporting. • Assist with waste sales invoicing, tracking and control. • Prepare payment packs and maintain accurate supporting documentation. • • Assist with customer and supplier credits and delivery-related variances. • Prepare stock count reports and recommend corrective action where required. • Support the Production and Supply Chain Managers with reporting and general administration. What We're Looking For • Matric is essential. • Minimum 3 years' experience in Stock Control, Inventory Management, Warehouse Management or a similar role. • Manufacturing experience is essential. • Experience managing high-volume inventory and multiple stock locations. • Exposure to imported stock and import documentation. • Strong MS Office / MS Suite skills. • Excellent numerical, reconciliation and analytical abilities. • Exceptional attention to detail and accuracy. • Strong organisational and planning skills. • Ability to work under pressure in a high-volume environment. • High levels of integrity, accountability and ownership. Advantageous Experience within corrugated board, packaging, paper, FMCG or other high-volume production environments will be highly advantageous. If you're looking for a role where your stock control and supply chain expertise can make a real operational impact, this could be the opportunity for you. Apply now and take the next step in your supply chain career.
