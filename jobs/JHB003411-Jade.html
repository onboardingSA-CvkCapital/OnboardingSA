About the role

The Sales Executive / Sales Representative is responsible for generating new business, maintaining strong client relationships, and increasing revenue through the sale of accommodation, events, conferencing, dining, and other hospitality services. The successful candidate will proactively identify new market opportunities, conduct sales visits, negotiate contracts, and represent the property professionally within the hospitality industry.Key ResponsibilitiesIdentify and secure new business opportunities across the corporate, leisure, travel trade, and MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Events) markets.Build and maintain long-term relationships with travel agents, tour operators, corporate clients, event planners, and key stakeholders.Conduct regular sales calls, site inspections, client presentations, and networking events.Develop and implement sales strategies to achieve revenue and occupancy targets.Promote accommodation, conferencing, restaurant, wedding, and event facilities.Prepare and present proposals, quotations, and contracts to prospective clients.Negotiate rates and service agreements in line with company policies.Follow up on enquiries and convert leads into confirmed business.Maintain an accurate sales pipeline and update the CRM system regularly.Achieve monthly and annual sales targets.Monitor market trends, competitor activities, and customer needs.Attend trade shows, exhibitions, and industry networking events.Work closely with Reservations, Front Office, Food & Beverage, and Operations to ensure seamless service delivery.Prepare weekly and monthly sales activity and performance reports.Maintain excellent product knowledge of the property and surrounding attractions.Ensure high levels of customer satisfaction and encourage repeat business.
