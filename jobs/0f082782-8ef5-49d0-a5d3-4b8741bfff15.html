About the role

Atteridgeville Assistant Branch Manager Are you an energetic, customer-focused, and results-driven sales professional with extensive experience in the automotive parts industry? A leading retail network specializing in quality automotive replacement parts is looking for a dynamic Assistant Branch Manager to join their busy retail branch in Atteridgeville on a full-time, permanent basis. If you have a strong technical understanding of vehicle parts, excel at over-the-counter sales and floor leadership, and want to build a long-term management career with an industry leader, this is your opportunity. What’s on Offer? - Salary: R20,000.00 – R25,000.00 CTC PM (negotiable depending on experience and proven capabilities) - Position Type: Full-Time, Permanent (after 3-month probation period) - Benefits: Standard company-aligned benefits and statutory contributions (Provident Fund compulsory contribution of 7.5% gross salary with an 8% employer contribution, annual December bonus, and 18 days annual leave) - Hours: Standard Hours: Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 17:00, and Saturdays from 08:00 to 13:00. - Environment: This is strictly a branch-based environment. - Flexibility: Overtime and operational flexibility will be required during peak trade periods, monthly stocktakes, and retail promotional campaigns (flexibility required during peak retail trade, promotional deadlines, and periodic stocktakes) - Growth: Excellent mentorship, career progression, and stability within an established, national automotive retail network Key Responsibilities - Sales Leadership & Target Delivery: Actively drive counter and trade sales to meet and exceed monthly branch revenue and gross profit targets. - Customer Service & Parts Lookup: Provide walk-in, workshop, and trade customers with accurate vehicle parts identification, prompt quotations, invoicing, and expert technical advice. - Team Supervision & Support: Supervise, mentor, and support counter sales staff, warehouse teams, and drivers to maintain smooth daily branch workflows. - Inventory & Stock Management: Assist with stock control, regular perpetual stock counts, discrepancy investigations, receiving audits, and formal stocktakes. - Cash Management & POS Controls: Oversee point-of-sale cash-ups, card reconciliations, petty cash handling, and enforce strict branch security and loss-prevention measures. - Dispute Resolution & Customer Care: Swiftly and professionally resolve customer queries, return-for-credit requests, and warranty claims according to company policy. - Branch Administration: Support the Branch Manager with daily shift scheduling, opening and closing procedures, merchandising standards, and trade reporting. Are You the Perfect Fit? Minimum Requirements: - Education: Grade 12 / Matric qualification. - Experience: Minimum 3 to 5 years of hands-on sales experience within the automotive parts aftermarket, spares wholesale, or automotive retail environment. - Technical Knowledge: Strong technical knowledge of automotive replacement parts, engine components, and electronic cataloguing lookup systems. - Software Proficiency: Practical computer literacy, including point-of-sale (POS) systems, MS Excel, and Outlook. - Licensing: Valid South African Driver’s License. - Core Attributes: Strong leadership and motivational skills, high attention to detail, commercial acumen, clear communication, and a target-driven mindset. Beneficial Skills & Experience: - Prior supervisory, 2IC, or keyholder experience in a high-volume automotive parts branch. - Relevant tertiary certificate or diploma in retail management, sales, or business administration. - Established trade relationships with local mechanical workshops and fleets in the Pretoria West and Atteridgeville areas. Strict Legal Requirements & Documentation (Non-Negotiable): Background screening and verification checks will be conducted. Candidates must provide: - Valid South African ID (Proof of South African citizenship). - Valid Driver’s License. - Clear criminal record. - Clear ITC credit check (no judgments, defaults, or blacklistings). How to Apply Ready to take the next step in your automotive management career? Ensure you have the following documentation ready: - Detailed CV (clearly outlining your automotive parts experience and sales accomplishments). - ID Copy & Driver's License. - Matric Certificate & Supporting Qualifications. - 3 Months Recent Payslips.
