About the role

The Department of International Relations and Cooperation is hiring a Director: ICT Infrastructure in Information and Communication Technology, based in Head Office (Pretoria). This is a contract post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Please e-mail your application to Diricti26@dirco.gov.za Reference number to quote: DIRCO1127/03 Closing date: 21 December 2026 - Applications received after the closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic direction on the alignment of Information Technology (IT) services to business needs: Manage Human Resources and provide strategies direction regarding Infrastructure Management.
  • Manage financial matters regarding the procurement of ICT equipment.
  • Regulate and control tactical and strategic demands of the Department.
  • Review business service plans and infrastructure services on a regular basis.
  • Manage project management approach in planning and executing ICT projects: Participate in the ICT steering committee.
  • Management of Human Resource Administration of the personnel within the Directorate.
  • Manage the PMDS of the staff in the Directorate.
  • Manage the maintenance and optimisation of IT infrastructure, including networks, servers and storage systems: Oversee maintenance of the network, server and telephony environment at Head Office and Missions.
  • Monitor the telecommunications services.
  • Provide server management and monitoring.
  • Manage business and service level agreements.
  • Draft service level agreements.
  • Monitor compliance to the IT prescripts of the DPSA.
  • Formulate the Directorate Annual Performance Plan.
  • Manage the budget of the Directorate in line with the PFMA and other prescripts of the government and governance.
  • Manage Information Technology service delivery to ensure efficient and timely resolution of technical issues.
  • Attend and chair the infrastructure management and stakeholder meetings.
  • Monitor the performance of service providers against service level agreement targets convened.

Requirements

  • An NQF level 7 qualification in Information Communication and Technology or equivalent qualification.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at Middle Management/Senior Managerial level within the ICT environment.
  • Experience in the networking, server and telephony environment will be an added advantage.
  • A pre-entry certificate (Nyukela certificate) obtained from the National School of Government (NSG) submitted prior to appointment.
  • Knowledge
  • Knowledge of legislative framework governing the Public Service e.g. Electronic communications and Transactions (ECT) Act

  • Knowledge of Information Management best practices such as Information System Audit and Control Association (ISACA)
  • Knowledge of ICT Governance Frameworks such as ITIL
  • CoBIT and Corporate Governance of ICT Policy Framework (CGICTPF)
  • Knowledge of Enterprise Architecture Frameworks such as Government Wide Enterprise Architecture (GWEA) and the Open Group Architecture Forum (TOGAF)
  • Knowledge of the Voice over IP Infrastructure
  • Server environment
  • Business Continuity and Applications support
  • Knowledge of ICT Infrastructure management tools
  • Knowledge and understanding of SA Foreign Policy
  • Knowledge and understanding of National policies and priorities
