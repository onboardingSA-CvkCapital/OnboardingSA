About the role

Capitec is hiring a Technical Operations Support Specialist, based in Stellenbosch. As we continue to evolve in the connectivity space and queries become more complex, we need someone who is smart, prepared to investigate and able to come up with solutions. You'll work one level above first-line support, taking on the technical incidents and operational issues that need deeper analysis to resolve. Your work keeps Capitec Connect running reliably - and your insights help us build something better. Investigate technical incidents relating to the device-order journey - for example, orders stuck at a particular system state or incorrectly cancelled - and determine whether the cause is technical or operational Investigate Client Value Management (CVM) and rewards-related issues - such as clients not receiving data rewards they are entitled to - and determine why the failure occurred Investigate voucher-related queries where clients have purchased a voucher but it has not reflected correctly, and resolve or escalate these appropriately Take ownership of incidents that first-line support (Connect Care) cannot resolve, working one level above the T1 function to find answers Engage third-party vendors to establish why transactions or technical processes have failed, including investigating timing or communication issues between our systems and vendor systems Identify potential common recurring problems and suggest practical solutions Assess the scale and priority of identified problems - distinguishing issues that need systemic resolution from those that can be handled pragmatically in the short term
