About the role

Purpose of the role: Cape Foods is looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Finance Graduate to join our Finance team. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent Finance or Accounting graduate to gain practical, hands-on experience across financial accounting, reconciliations, reporting, inventory, budgeting and operational finance within an FMCG/manufacturing environment. Key performance areas include (including but not limited to): • Assist with bookkeeping, reconciliations and month-end accounting activities. • Assist with preparation of month-end audit files and supporting schedules. • Capture AP invoices on Traild • Customer and Supplier account maintenance on Syspro ERP • Assist with foreign invoicing and dispatch documentation. • Perform retailer, rebate, CHEP, pallet, crate and warehouse reconciliations. • Assist with maintaining the Fixed Asset Register and physical asset verification. • Assist with stock counts and inventory reconciliations. • Prepare daily sales and stock reports and weekly sales reports. • Assist with OEE reporting and time studies for overhead allocations. • Assist with cash-flow reporting, budgets and forecasts. • Investigate financial and operational variances. • Support the Finance team with reporting, analysis and process improvements. • Process and reconcile related party transactions. • General and other duties may be assigned by senior management. Required Qualifications and Experience: • Relevant degree or diploma in Accounting, Finance, Financial Management or a related field. • Recent graduate or final-year student completing their qualification. • Microsoft Excel • Previous work experience is advantageous but not essential. • Exposure to Syspro, Traild, or another ERP/accounting system will be advantageous. • FMCG or manufacturing exposure will be advantageous. Personal Profile, Skills and Attitude: • Strong attention to detail and accuracy. • Good numerical and analytical skills. • Excellent organisational and time management skills. • Good communication and organisational skills. • Ability to work independently and as part of a team. • Willingness to learn and develop within a Finance environment. • Ability to work under pressure and meet deadline. • Proactive and self-motivated. This role provides the opportunity to gain broad practical experience across: Financial Accounting | Bookkeeping | Reconciliations | Inventory | Fixed Assets | Management Reporting | Budgeting & Forecasting | Cash Flow | Operational Finance The successful candidate will work closely with experienced Finance and operational teams, developing practical skills and a strong foundation for a career in Finance.
