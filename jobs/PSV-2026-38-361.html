About the role

The Department of Infrastructure Development (Gauteng) is hiring a Senior Administrative Officer: Movable Assets in Goods And Services SCM, based in Head Office (Johannesburg). This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: To apply for the below position, please apply online at https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za Reference number to quote: REFS/048672 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide inputs into the development of the Movable Assets Register in accordance with Treasury guidelines.
  • Compile a list of all users and update the system.
  • Facilitate bar coding of new acquisitions.
  • Prepare monthly reconciliations between BAS and BAUD.
  • Update acquisitions on the system (BAUD).
  • Capture physical movement of assets and updating movements on the system.
  • Conduct annual asset verifications.
  • Facilitate issuing, signing and displaying of the updated inventory control sheet.
  • Verify receipt for correctness and printing of the updated inventory control sheet.
  • Facilitate Quarterly spot checks of assets.
  • Conduct bar coding of assets.
  • Render maintenance of assets.
  • Compile reconciliations between the asset register and ledger accounts.
  • Prepare monthly Section 40 Reports for Treasury.
  • Develop and maintain filling systems in the unit.
  • Record, organise, store, capture and retrieve correspondences and data.
  • Draft submissions, memos and letters.
  • Provide logistical support service for meetings.
  • Sign performance agreements for subordinates based on approved Job Descriptions, Strategic and Annual Performance Plans of the Department.
  • Supervise subordinates and maintain discipline.
  • Supervise training and development of subordinates according to agreed training interventions.
  • Develop and update the service delivery and work plan for subordinates.

Requirements

  • An undergraduate qualification at NQF Level 6 or 7 in Supply Chain Management/Logistics/Cost and Management Accounting/Financial Management/Financial Accounting.
  • At least 2 years of experience in management of Assets.
  • A valid driver’s license.
  • Competencies:
  • Knowledge of the Public Service Regulatory Framework
  • Knowledge of the department Strategy
  • Knowledge of the SCM policy and procedures
  • In depth knowledge of the Public Finance Management
  • Treasury Regulations’ Financial Delegations and Risk Management
  • Skills:
  • Communication
  • Computer literacy
  • Analytical
  • Presentation
  • Report writing
  • Attributes:
  • Team player
  • Able to work independently
  • Professional
  • Quality-driven
  • Ability to work under pressure
