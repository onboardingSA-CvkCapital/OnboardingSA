About the role

Responsibilities

  • Strengthen and deliver Neonatal clinical services in the department of Obstetrics &Gynaecology at Newcastle Regional Hospital.
  • Assist with the O&G services coordination between levels of care.
  • Assist in quality improvement activities including clinical audit, data management, monitoring and evaluation and regular reporting on key indicators related to O&G burden of disease.
  • Actively participate in the academic under-end post-graduate O&G training program (including clinic teaching).
  • Perform and supervise O&G operational research activities in the Speciality Department.
  • Perform overtime as required in the Speciality Department.
  • Participate in the academic and teaching programme and meetings in the O&G department.
  • Participate in the multidisciplinary team discussions in the unit.
  • Responsibility for care of patients in the out-patient and in-patient setting.
  • Provide outreach to PCH’s, CHC’s and District Hospitals.
  • ENQURIES: Dr. JJN Duze - Senior Manager: Medical Services Tel No: (034) 328 0000.

Requirements

  • Senior certificate/Grade 12.
  • Appropriate qualification that allows registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as a Medical Practitioner.
  • Appropriate tertiary qualification in the Health Science (MBChB) an appropriate qualification that allows registration with the HPCSA as a Medical Specialist in Obstetrics & Gynaecology.
  • Registration with the HPCSA as a Medical Specialist in O&G.
  • Current HPCSA registration 2026/2027.
  • Grade 1: No experience required.
  • Registrars who have completed their training may also apply on condition that their appointment will be subjected to them submitting documentary evidence of registration with the HPCSA.
  • Grade 2: In addition to the requirements for a Grade 1 specialist post, at least 05 years relevant experience after registration with the HPCSA as a Medical Specialist in O&G is required for appointment as a grade 2 specialist.
  • Grade 3: In addition to the requirements for a Grade 1 specialist post, at least 10 years relevant experience after registration with the HPCSA as a Medical Specialist in O&G is required for appointment as a grade 3 specialist.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Training And Competencies Required:
  • - Sound knowledge and experience in O&G. knowledge of current health and public service legislation, regulations and policies including medical ethics, epidemiology and statistics
  • Good communication, leadership, decision-making and clinical skills
  • Ability to teach junior doctors and participate in continuing professional development
  • Sound knowledge of clinical procedures and protocols within the discipline. assessment and management of patients
