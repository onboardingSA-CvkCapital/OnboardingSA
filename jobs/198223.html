About the role

Core Group is a leading premium consumer electronics distributor in South Africa, representing some of the world’s most recognised and admired technology brands, including Apple, Nintendo, DJI and other global innovators. We bring exceptional products and services to market that empower, enable, inspire and enhance the lives of consumers and businesses across Africa. Our success is driven by agile supply chain management, strategic brand development, effective channel management, enterprise sales and impactful media and marketing capabilities. We work closely with our global brand partners to build sustainable market presence, create demand and deliver world-class experiences across retail, e-commerce and enterprise channels. As a market leader in the consumer electronics industry, Core Group combines global brand expertise with deep local market knowledge, ensuring our partners and customers experience the highest standards of brand, marketing and commercial execution. About the Role The Marketing Manager – Premium Audio will lead the strategic planning, execution and performance management of marketing initiatives for a globally recognised premium audio brand. This is a high-impact role responsible for building brand equity, driving consumer demand, increasing product visibility and delivering commercial growth across retail, e-commerce, digital and broader channel environments. The Senior Marketing Manager will translate global brand strategy into locally relevant marketing programmes, ensuring that campaigns reflect the distinctive positioning, premium experience and innovation associated with the brand. The role will work closely with the brand principal, retail partners, sales teams, creative partners, media agencies and internal stakeholders to deliver integrated campaigns that create awareness, consideration, conversion and long-term customer loyalty. The ideal candidate is commercially minded, digitally fluent and highly analytical, with a passion for premium brands, consumer technology, audio and exceptional customer experiences. Key Responsibilities Brand & Marketing Strategy • Develop and execute the annual marketing strategy and brand plan in alignment with global brand direction and Core Group's commercial objectives. • Act as the brand custodian and champion, ensuring all communications, campaigns, retail activations, digital content and partner-facing materials adhere to brand positioning and global guidelines. • Translate global brand priorities into locally relevant campaigns that resonate with South African and African consumers. • Identify opportunities to strengthen brand awareness, premium positioning, product consideration and market share. • Monitor category trends, competitor activity, consumer behaviour and market developments to identify opportunities for growth. Retail, Channel & E-commerce Marketing • Develop and execute retail and channel marketing initiatives that elevate product visibility and reinforce the premium brand experience. • Partner with retailers to develop impactful campaigns, activations, displays and customer engagement initiatives. • Support e-commerce growth through digital merchandising, product visibility, promotional campaigns, content and conversion-focused initiatives. • Ensure retail and e-commerce marketing activity supports sell-through and commercial targets. • Collaborate with sales teams to align marketing activity with account strategies, product priorities and revenue objectives. Reporting & Performance Management The Senior Marketing Manager will be responsible for providing accurate, insightful and commercially relevant reporting, including: • Monthly advertising effectiveness reports. • Post-campaign performance and analysis reports. • Digital campaign performance reporting. • CRM journey reporting and optimisation. • Media and channel performance analysis. • Budget utilisation and marketing investment reporting. • Consumer, category and competitor insights. • Recommendations for campaign optimisation and future marketing activity.
