About the role

Marketing Coordinator – FMCG / Retail Western Cape – Northern Suburbs Salary: R30 000 CTC pm slightly negotiable A well-established company within the FMCG and retail sector is seeking to appoint an experienced and hands-on Marketing Coordinator to support and drive marketing initiatives across its retail store network in the Western Cape. This role is suited to a commercially minded marketing professional who enjoys both the strategic and operational side of marketing. The successful candidate will be involved in planning promotional campaigns, coordinating media and advertising activity, working closely with suppliers and service providers, monitoring competitor activity, and ensuring that marketing initiatives are effectively implemented at store level. The ideal candidate will have solid FMCG marketing experience, a strong understanding of retail promotions, pricing and margins, and the ability to manage multiple campaigns and deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
