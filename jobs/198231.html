About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies has an opportunity available for a Hub Advisor for their Pongola Junction (New Store). The Hub Advisor is the front-of-house concierge and ecosystem guide for Dis-Chem's Store of the Future. Positioned at "The Hub", this role delivers human-led, digitally enabled customer service, supporting customers across the Dis - Chem ecosystem, Better Rewards, Clinic, Cover, Dispensary, and the Dis-Chem App. The role is foundational to Dis- Chem's ecosystem strategy, ensuring customers are welcomed, guided and seamlessly connected to the right services at the right time. Drawing on a 7-star hospitality mindset, the Hub Advisor removes friction from health and retail journeys, builds trust, and elevates Dis-Chem from a traditional pharmacy retailer to an integrated health partner. Acting as a front door to Better Rewards and the Store of the Future, the Hub Advisor ensures that digital innovation feels intuitive, supportive, and human, helping customers confidently navigate their health journeys while reinforcing loyalty, adoption, and long-term engagement.
