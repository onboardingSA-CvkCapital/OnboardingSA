About the role

Capitec is hiring a Manager: Employee Relations, based in Stellenbosch. To shape and drive Capitec’s Employee Relations strategy by creating a legally compliant, fair, and values aligned workplace culture that protects the organisation from labour, operational and reputational risk. To interpret the Employment Relations (ER) strategy and business plan and develop and implement tactical and operational plans and practices, for a designated portfolio, resolving workplace conflicts, ensuring regulatory compliance and a positive workplace culture, enabling employee engagement and delivering the ER objectives and goals. To be responsible for the day-to-day management of the designated department portfolio, embed appropriate processes, practices, standards and delivery.
