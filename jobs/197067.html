About the role

Sales Executive Join Lesaka and Transform the Future of Hospitality Technology Are you a driven sales professional who thrives on winning new business, building relationships, and helping businesses grow? Do you enjoy being out in the field, meeting customers, and selling innovative technology solutions that make a real difference? At Lesaka, we're transforming the way South African businesses operate through innovative payment, software, capital, and value-added solutions. We're looking for ambitious, commercially minded Sales Executives who are passionate about sales and excited to help restaurants, cafés, hotels, guesthouses, lodges, and hospitality businesses embrace smarter technology. If you're motivated by uncapped opportunities, achieving ambitious targets, and building your own business territory, we'd love to hear from you. Why Join Lesaka? As part of our Growth Organisation, you'll be joining one of the most exciting commercial teams in the business.
