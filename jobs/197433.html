About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies require the services of a Foundation Support Assistant based at their head office in Midrand. The Foundation Assistant will be responsible for providing administrative support in the Dis-Chem Foundation and ensuring Corporate Social Investment objectives are met. Minimum Requirements: Essential: • Gr12/ Matric • Minimum 1-2 years’ experience in an administrative roleAdvantageous: • Certificate or National Diploma in Business Administration, Marketing, or related field • Similar experience in a non-profit environment Duties and Responsibilities: • Assist with defining and executing a plan focused on expanding and enhancing foundation and corporate relationships for assigned prospects. • Support the Foundation support lead with conducting due diligence for any organisations that the foundation collaborates with. • Support current and new CSI projects to ensure sustainability of all initiatives. • Coordinate corporate responsibility activities and investigations for the applicable initiatives and entities. • Draft correspondence, letter, and email as and when requested for approval and distribution. • Maintain campaign records, track pledges, send acknowledgements of donations received as assigned • Collaborate with DC teams to package and deliver required stock to external organisations timeously. • Deliver on and carry out action plans for Foundation work with major corporations and organisation. • Provide management support including facilitating meetings, working with group leads to plan, coordinate and document foundation activities and deliverables. • Create weekly reports on progress in relationship-building and secured commitments to share with the Foundation management. • Coordinate internal and external meetings and events including material preparation, conference room bookings etc. • Ensure the meetings are planned and coordinated according to target audience requirements. • Evaluate the event’s success and submit report based on recommendations. • Investigate and provide responses to corporate responsibility requests related to assigned designated entities. • Ensure a meaningful relationship with the stakeholders and external customers is always upheld. • Address stakeholder issues or concerns and ensure they are dealt with promptly and accurately. • Communicate information in various mediums, in a consistent and professional manner. • Assist in the handling of social and digital platforms for the Foundation. • Adhere to Dis-Chem Policies and Standard Operating Procedures. • Adhere to Health and Safety rules and regulations. Competencies Essential: • Strong ability in problem solving • English – Read, write and speak well. • Good interpersonal skills • Diligently attends to details and pursues quality in accomplishing tasks. • Good time-management and strong organizing skills. • Microsoft – Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook. Special conditions of employment: • South African citizen • Be able and willing to work under pressure and strict deadlines.
