About the role

AECI is hiring a Technical Manager, based in Sasolburg.

Responsibilities

  • To provide strategic and technical leadership across the manufacturing facility, ensuring effective coordination between Operations, Quality, Laboratory, Engineering, R&D, Commercial and customers. The role oversees product and process quality, technical governance, laboratory performance, problem-solving, audits and the transfer of new products and technologies into manufacturing, while leading technical teams to achieve operational, quality and business objectives.
  • Required functional outputs
  • Provide overall technical leadership across Laboratory, Quality, QC, Process Engineering and technical operations.
  • Establish and maintain technical standards, systems, governance and KPIs.
  • Manage and continually improve the Quality Management System and ensure ISO and regulatory compliance.
  • Lead internal audits, corrective actions, non-conformance management and quality improvement initiatives.
  • Ensure effective document control and maintenance of technical and quality documentation.
  • Coordinate technology transfer, product development, plant trials and scale-up from R&D into Operations.
  • Lead complex technical investigations involving product failures, process instability, quality deviations and manufacturing problems.
  • Lead investigations into significant customer complaints and ensure effective corrective and preventive actions.
  • Provide technical leadership to Laboratory Technical Superintendents, QC Managers and other technical teams.
  • Ensure products consistently meet specifications and support process capability, optimisation and variability reduction.
  • Provide technical support to Operations and Process Engineering, including process improvement, Management of Change and CAPEX projects.
  • Identify and manage technical, quality and process risks affecting safety, compliance, customers and business continuity.
  • Drive continuous improvement, innovation, automation and the use of data and statistical analysis.
  • Develop technical capability through training, coaching, mentoring, knowledge transfer and succession planning.
  • Provide technical input into SHEQ investigations, risk assessments and regulatory/customer audits.
  • Manage technical budgets, resources, external testing and specialist technical services.
  • Monitor technical and quality performance through appropriate KPIs and ensure measurable improvement.

Requirements

  • BSc, BTech, BEng or equivalent in Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Analytical Chemistry, Process Engineering, Materials Science or a relevant technical discipline.
  • Minimum 10 to 15 years' experience in technical, manufacturing, process engineering, quality or R&D environments.
  • Minimum 5 years' experience in a technical leadership or management role.
  • Proven experience in chemical/process manufacturing.
  • Experience managing technical, laboratory and quality teams
  • Experience with Quality Management Systems, ISO standards and internal auditing.
  • Experience in product development, technology transfer and scale-up.
  • Experience in technical investigations, customer complaints and manufacturing troubleshooting.
  • Experience with process optimisation, CAPEX/project implementation and Management of Change.
  • Postgraduate qualification, MBA/PDBA, Lean/Six Sigma, Project Management or ISO Lead Auditor qualification would be advantageous.
  • Strong knowledge of chemical/process manufacturing, quality systems, laboratory operations, process optimisation, statistical analysis, root-cause analysis and technical risk management.
  • Competencies
  • Strong technical leadership and strategic thinking.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Systems thinking and sound decision-making.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Strong quality and customer focus.
  • Ability to lead, coach and develop technical teams.
  • Strong knowledge transfer and knowledge-management capabilities.
  • Commercial awareness and business acumen.
  • Change leadership and continuous improvement mindset.
  • Ability to manage complex technical issues and make evidence-based decisions.
  • Strong accountability, attention to detail and commitment to technical excellence
