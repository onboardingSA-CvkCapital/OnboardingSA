About the role

SAB is hiring a TRAINING CONTROLLER, based in Prospecton Brewery. The Key Purpose of this role is to provide a specialized and effective training and development service to Prospecton Brewery department(s), focusing on the acquisition of competence and Performance to create and sustain a progressive learning environment by providing an effective service to the value chain focused on department performance and output.
