Description: Starke Ayres is the foremost African specialist and global supplier of premium vegetable, flower and lawn seed varieties – both for commercial and home garden planting. The following position has become available for two Research Technicians, based in Kempton Park. One of the positions will be focused on the tunnels. RESPONSIBILITIES: • Monitor and report plant health, stress indicators, and abnormalities under field, net-house, and glasshouse conditions • Organise weekly field walks with the farm manager, supervisor, and breeder (when available) • Execute planting schedules in coordination with the supervisor and field team • Oversee seed processing including harvesting, drying, cleaning, treating, labelling, cataloguing, weighing, and cold-room organisation • Collect and record field, tunnel, and nursery trial data accurately • Execute and supervise hand pollinations (selfing and crossing) • Identify and report off-type plants • Complete phytosanitary applications and support government officials during inspections • Organise and execute leaf sampling for DNA analysis • Maintain accurate seed receiving and shipment records • Report maintenance needs for tunnels, irrigation systems, equipment, and related infrastructure to the farm manager • Report housekeeping and hygiene issues in tunnels, fields, and workrooms to the farm manager and team supervisor • Coordinate labour requirements with the breeder and supervisor • Assist the supervisor in overseeing daily operations in the workroom, tunnels, and fields • Promote productivity, efficiency, and safe work practices • Report issues related to attendance and timekeeping to the farm manager and supervisor • Conduct daily scouting for pests and diseases in fields, tunnels, and net-houses • Monitor chemical spray efficacy and report any issues • Programming of fertigation system according to fertilizer recommendation based on commercial practices • Consulting with fertilizer/ nutrition expert on a monthly basis to prevent and eliminate potential problems before they arise. • Monitoring of EC, pH and run-off figures on a daily basis with appropriate record keeping and reporting. • Be confident in changing fertigation programmes after consultation with breeder and fertilizer expert.
