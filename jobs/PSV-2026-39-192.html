About the role

The Department of Education (Eastern Cape) is hiring a Deputy Director-General: District Coordination & Institutional Operations Management, based in Zwelitsha. This is a permanent post, paying R1,885,710 to R2,124,237 a year. How to apply: Applications must be submitted via the provincial e-recruitment system accessible at: https://ecprov.gov.za (under Careers) and/or at https://erecruitment.ecotp.gov.za. The Provincial e-Recruitment System is available 24/7 and closes at 23: 59 on the Closing Date. To report any e- Recruitment System challenges send an email with your (i) ID Number, (ii) profile email address, (iii) details of your issue to: erecruitment@ecdoe.gov.za. Do not send any applications to this email address; should you do so, your applications/ CV will be regarded as lost and will not be considered. Technical support is limited to working hours: (08:00 16:30 Mon-Thursday and 08:00- 16:00 on Friday). Direct all enquiries to the specified enquiries person or email Address. Reference number to quote: DOE01/10/2026 Closing date: 13 November 2026. Applications received after closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Oversee implementation of policy framework for provisioning of quality teaching and learning in public, Special schools and ECD Centres of Eastern Cape Province.
  • Ensure the coordination of consistent implementation of standard /uniform mode of operation for teaching & learning in public schools of Eastern Cape.
  • Ensure implementation intervention and tools supporting quality curriculum delivery in schools.
  • Ensure implementation of school management and governance legislation, policies and procedures in public and special schools in Eastern Cape.
  • Ensure coordination intergovernmental relation processes to ensure all education district offices are positioned for optimal service delivery.
  • Coordinate the provision of technical support for education district functionality.
  • Oversee provisioning and guidance on implementation of policy framework for Physical Resource Management in Eastern Cape Schooling system in line with the Division of Revenue Act.
  • Ensure coordination of physical resource planning services.
  • Ensure the provisioning of infrastructure delivery services.
  • Provide strategic guidance in development and monitoring of Disaster Recovery Plans impacting Eastern Cape Public and Special Schools.
  • Ensure accountability, monitoring and reporting on Infrastructure Grant Framework in Eastern Cape public, Special schools and ECD Centres.
  • Oversee implementation of policy framework for school resourcing and administration.
  • Ensure planning and budgeting for school resourcing needs including Learner Teacher Supporting Materials, school furniture etc. Ensure the coordination on the implementation of policy and regulations for the administration of home schooling, public ordinary, independent, special schools and Early Childhood Development Institutions.
  • Ensure coordination and management of policy and regulation for School Management and School Governing Bodies (SGB’s).
  • Ensure functionality and viability of public ordinary, special schools and ECD Institutions.
  • Oversee provisioning of leadership and guidance in supporting systems for delivery of quality education system in Eastern Province.
  • Ensure provision of school mapping services, revitalisation of schools and rural education.
  • Ensure management of facilitation of planning for school extension and new school implementation.
  • Ensure provisioning Quality Promotion Standard education research and data analytics.
  • Ensure implementation of Education Management Information system in all public & Special Schools.
  • Ensure management & evaluation interventions, learner attainment improvement strategy (LAIS) and vacation schools.
  • Manage the allocated resources of the Branch in line with legislative and departmental policy directives and comply with corporate governance and planning imperatives.
  • Maintain high standards by ensuring that the team/section produces excellent work in terms of quality/quantity and timeliness.
  • Resolve problems of motivation and control with minimum guidance from manager.
  • Delegate functions to staff based on individual potential provide the necessary guidance and support and afford staff adequate training and development opportunities.
  • Ensure timeously development of job description and implementation of Work Plans and Personal Development Plans (PDP’s) for all subordinates.
  • Manage daily employee performance and ensure timely Performance Assessments of all subordinates.
  • Ensure management, maintenance and safekeeping of assets.

Requirements

  • An appropriately recognised bachelor’s degree (NQF Level 8) in Public Administration, Public Management, Financial Management, Supply Chain Management, Resource Management, Education Management, Infrastructure Management, Governance, Business Administration or a related field.
  • A postgraduate qualification at NQF Level 8 or higher in any of the aforementioned fields will serve as a strong recommendation.
  • Successful completion of the Nyukela Public Service Senior Management Service (SMS) Pre-Entry Programme and possession of a valid driver's license (except for persons with disabilities).
  • At least 8 years of experience at a Senior Managerial Level within the public sector, of which substantial experience must have been gained in school resourcing, institutional support services, physical resource management, infrastructure planning, financial management, risk management, educational operations, budget management and service delivery oversight.
  • Competencies
  • The successful candidate must demonstrate extensive knowledge of public sector governance
  • Education policy
  • Institutional operations and resource management
  • The incumbent must possess strong strategic leadership capabilities with a proven ability to provide executive oversight of large-scale resource allocation, school resourcing and institutional support systems.
  • A sound understanding of the Public Finance Management Act, Treasury Regulations, Division of Revenue Act, risk management frameworks and corporate governance principles is essential.
  • The candidate must demonstrate advanced capabilities in financial management, strategic planning, policy development, programme oversight, monitoring and evaluation, stakeholder management and organisational performance management.
  • Strong analytical, decision-making, communication, negotiation and change leadership skills are required, together with the ability to drive accountability, compliance and service delivery improvement within a complex public sector environment.
