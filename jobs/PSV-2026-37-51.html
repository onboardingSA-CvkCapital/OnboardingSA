About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring an Assistant Director: Budget in Financial And Supply Chain Management Services, based in KwaZulu Natal (Pietermaritzburg). This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 1st Floor, 270 Jabu Ndlovu Street (formerly known as Loop Street), Pietermaritzburg, 3200 or by email to Post281@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/281 Closing date: 30 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: Indian and White Males, and African, Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Facilitate and coordinate budgeting programmes and projects.
  • Facilitate and compile the budget integrations (Medium Term Expenditure Framework (MTEF), Adjusted Estimates of National Expenditure (AENE) and Estimates of National Expenditure (ENE).
  • Facilitate and submit monthly projections.
  • Facilitate and submit drawings.
  • Monitor budget as per Standard Chart of Accounts (SCOA) allocation.
  • Oversee journals progress.
  • Monitor commitments.
  • Verify the allocations as per captured budget.
  • Ensure completeness and accuracy of financial information.
  • Coordinate budget performance and reporting.
  • Provide monthly expenditure reports.
  • Analyse and explain variances.
  • Report unauthorised expenditure and report irregular and wasteful expenditure.
  • Provide advice to management in relation to budget performance.
  • Maintain the provincial budget.
  • Analyse and report on expenditure trends of the provincial budget.
  • Ensure provincial budget is captured in line with the Demand Management Plan and Procurement Plan.
  • Ensure that quarterly budget realignment is done.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements:
  • A Grade 12 Certificate and National Diploma in Cost and Management Accounting/Auditing/Public Finance/Financial Management/B.COM Accounting or related equivalent qualifications
  • At least 3 years' supervisory experience in the budget environment
  • A valid driver’s licence
  • Job related knowledge:
  • Knowledge of Public Service Policies and Regulations
  • Treasury Regulations and Public Finance Management Act (PFMA)
  • Basic Accounting System (BAS)
  • Ability to monitor Budgets
  • Ability to liaise at different levels of management
  • Job related skills:
  • Computer literacy (Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Project)
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Planning and organising
  • Time management and Liaison
  • Work under pressure and meet deadlines and use a computer for prolonged periods
