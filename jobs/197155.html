About the role

The Faircape Group is a dynamic and growing organisation with a diverse portfolio across healthcare, retirement living, telecommunications, infrastructure, and property. Operating at scale, the Group manages multiple high-volume, operational entities, creating a complex and fast-paced financial environment. We are looking to appoint an Accountant to join our team. This role plays a key part in supporting multiple operational business units, providing strong financial analysis, reporting, and insight to drive performance. Working closely with cross-functional teams, you will be responsible for compiling management accounts, interpreting financial data, and contributing to informed decision-making across the business. This position is well suited to someone who thrives in a high-volume, operational environment and is comfortable working across multiple entities with varying financial requirements. Duties include but are not limited to the following: • Responsible for generating monthly management accounts for multiple entities, including balance sheets, income statements, and accompanying schedules within agreed timelines • Analyze budget vs actual variances and communicate findings • Conduct reconciliations and generate reports for various company accounts pertaining to cash flow • Provide management and guidance to Cashbook, Payments & Creditors Controllers,  and Billing Administrators • Prepare audit packs and collaborate with auditors on annual financial statements • Handle submission of statutory returns for VAT • Review payments for accuracy of allocation and VAT treatment • Perform regular review of cashbooks, invoicing, and debtors ageing analysis, assisting in credit control management • Authorise payments on the banking portal • Investigate expense variances and incorporate findings into income statement schedules as notes • Address and follow up on account queries promptly • Manage ad hoc projects, queries, and analyses as needed • Identify, recommend and communicate process improvements to enhance internal controls, operational efficiency and financial reporting across multiple operational entities. Qualifications: • BCom in Accounting or Financial Accounting • Completed or working towards a professional accounting designation (e.g. SAIPA or CIMA) (advantageous) Experience: • Proven experience as an Accountant • Advanced Excel experience • Proven experience in a commercial, corporate, or operational environment • Experience as an Accountant in an operational, high-volume environment • Solid knowledge of financial processes, systems, and relevant regulations • Experience with Google sheets (advantageous) • Must have experience managing the full accounting cycle (source to reporting), including preparing accounts to balance sheet and income statement level. • Must have been responsible for ensuring that accounts are accurate, complete, and provide meaningful financial insightSkills and Attributes • Strong commercial mindset – understands what drives sales and profitability across multiple operational entities • high-demand and high-volume environment • Strong analytical skills with attention to detail and ability to interpret financial data across multiple entities • Ability to manage and prioritise multiple tasks and deadlines across different business units • Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work independently • Driven, goal-orientated and motivated to succeed, learn and grow Specific
