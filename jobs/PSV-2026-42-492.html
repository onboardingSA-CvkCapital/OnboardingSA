About the role

The Department of Health and Wellness (Western Cape) is hiring a Chief Industrial Technician in Emergency Clinical and Services Support, based in Observatory Forensic Pathology Institute. This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applications are submitted online via www.westerncape.gov.za/health-jobs (click “online applications”). Closing date: 04 December 2026, 17:00PM Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: No payment of any kind is required when applying for this post. Shortlisted candidates may be subjected to a practical assessment. Candidates will be subjected to security clearance prior to appointment.

Responsibilities

  • Effective related administrative duties in a structured manner.
  • Effective control of scheduled services to OFPI.
  • Provide and maintain technical back-up/support to OFPI.
  • Effective management of Building Management System (BMS) which includes quality electrical, mechanical and any other building services to the OFPI.

Requirements

  • Minimum educational qualification: National Diploma in Mechanical or Mechatronics Engineering (T-stream) (or equivalent qualification).
  • Experience
  • Appropriate experience in refrigeration, air conditioning and heavy current electrical systems
  • Appropriate experience in repair and maintenance of electrical and mechanical equipment
  • Appropriate experience in automation and automated guided systems
  • Appropriate experience in managing, planning and organising maintenance schedules on equipment
  • Appropriate experience in building maintenance and management systems
  • Appropriate service level agreement contract management experience
  • Inherent requirement of the job
  • Valid (Code B/EB) driver's license
  • Ability to work standby duties and handle after-hour callouts
  • Competencies (knowledge/skills)
  • Knowledge of building regulations in line with relevant legislation
  • Knowledge of electrical regulations and prescripts
  • Understanding Health Infrastructure and equipment needs
  • Knowledge of legal, financial and procurement prescripts
  • Knowledge of what constitutes acceptable performance
  • Knowledge of procurement procedures and tender regulations
  • Knowledge of departmental norms and standards implementations and specifications compatible with international standards
  • In depth knowledge of mechanical, electrical and building trades
  • Technical expertise in health and facility maintenance
  • Ability to communicate clearly and discreetly in person and in writing
  • Above average computer and software skills with smart tech such as automated guided systems comprising of maintenance and management systems.
  • Financial planning, budgeting and financial monitoring skills.
  • Ability to deliver specifications timeously.
  • Ability to prepare comprehensive specifications for repairs and renovations.
  • Ability to compile technical specifications for equipment and adhere to all legal requirements, protocols and procedures.
  • Ability to provide clarity surrounding projects and work-related issues attainted/maintained.
  • Ability to fault-find and repair equipment.
  • Ability to apply electrical, mechanical and building trades.
  • Compliance with OHS Act of 1993 and it's relevant regulations, NOSA, Safety Health and Environment (SHE) standards, Manufacturers' standards and benchmarking against best practice.
