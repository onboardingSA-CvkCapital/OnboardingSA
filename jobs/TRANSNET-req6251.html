About the role

Transnet (Transnet Engineering) is hiring a Foreman, based in Witbank. The role reports to the Superintendent. Grade: H1X. To ensure production of roadworthy,reliable,&quality wagon on time to the client

Responsibilities

  • The foreman manages the repair to wagons effectively to support the operating department to meet the daily required trains schedule to ensure a reliable and predictable service.
  • Responsible for the planning and execution of the work allocated to his depot/area.
  • Investigate the reasons for damage caused to wagons by operating and client, recommend and implement corrective actions.
  • Responsible for planning of manpower requirements.
  • Interact with external clients to ensure that their requirements are met and manage the damages caused to rolling stock.
  • Responsible for the depot information system that will allow senior manager to evaluate the performance of the depot as well as the quality of work prepared.
  • Provide information on the following
  • Number of wagons repaired and released back to service.
  • Number of wagons labelled off VS.
  • Number of wagons CDR
  • Allocate workload to depot personnel as well as to establish and manage competency needs and ensure that the wagons are repaired according to the required quality standards.
  • Empower to request the acquisition of goods and service to an amount not exceeding the limit to be determined by management
  • Ensure that wagons are maintained to the requirements of the maintenance plan and to meet the client requirements, this must be done with minimal service interruption.
  • Draw the correct material from the warehouse to fit on the specific wagon/coach utilising the correct second-hand material when new material is not available.
  • Maintain breakdown equipment re-railing of rolling stock and knowledge of section duties.
  • Knowledge of the internal and external needs and preferences of clients as well as uses for the products and services needed by them.
  • Monitoring of the ITCMS system to ensure safe and smooth running of trains and prevent incidents and delays.
  • Create wagon notification to ensure that the workshop can work on a wagon.
  • Trace wagons that are due for service and card them to the workshop for repair.
  • Do WI in case of incident and supply the incident number to the people involved.
  • Skill at making effective verbal and written communications imparting information to others.
  • Ensure that the quality done on the wagon will meet the next maintenance cycle.
  • Maintain breakdown equipment re-railing of rolling stock and knowledge of section duties.
  • Compilation of documentation.
  • Ensure wagons are tested on completion and handed back to service.
  • Manage, control and order spares and material.
  • Enforce the use of PPE.
  • Housekeeping 5S.
  • Plan and execute shunting operations.
  • Conduct PJO.
  • Ensure correct utilisation of material and distribute accordingly.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 (NQF 4)
  • Min 5 years’ relevant experience as an Examiner & Repairer
  • Be qualified as an Examiner and Repairer.
  • Have a valid code 8/10/14 drivers’ licence.
  • Professional Driving Permit (PDP) will be an added advantage.
  • OR
  • Min 5 years’ experience working in the Wagons Maintenance Environment as an Examiner and repairer (relevant to the position applied for)
  • Competencies
  • Knowledge
  • Knowledge of all wagons, the commodities.
  • AAR-single car test
  • Oxy-Acetylene
  • NOSA/Act 85
  • Pro-active
  • Stress handling
  • Good customer relationships
  • Disciplinary codes
  • Grievance procedure
  • Attitudes
  • Perseverance
  • Quality awareness
  • Safety orientation
  • Shift work
  • Reliable and accurate
