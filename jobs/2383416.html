About the role

Nexeva Health Solutions is a South African healthcare company committed to delivering innovative, patient‑centred solutions across the continuum of care. As we continue to scale our oncology, renal and advanced healthcare technology distribution & service business, we are seeking a Technical Sales Administrator to provide technical and commercial administrative support to the Nexeva Sales Team – preparing quotations, tenders, technical proposals and equipment configurations. Key responsibilities: • Prepare accurate quotations for medical equipment, radiation therapy equipment, parts, services and associated products based on requirements provided by the Sales Team, including detailed cost estimates using approved pricing and commercial information. • Compile technical and commercial information for quotations and proposals, ensuring they accurately reflect the required equipment, components, accessories, services, quantities and pricing — and update them as requirements, configurations or pricing change. • Review quotations for completeness, accuracy and consistency before submitting them to the Sales Team, identifying missing or unclear information and obtaining clarification before finalising. • Prepare equipment configurations based on Sales Team requirements, capturing equipment, options, accessories, components and services accurately, and maintaining configuration documents under appropriate version control. • Escalate non-standard or technically complex requirements to the appropriate Sales or Technical Services specialist; technical solution design and approval remain their responsibility. • Assist the Sales Team with the administrative preparation of tenders and formal proposals — compiling pricing, technical and commercial documentation, and maintaining a tender compliance checklist and library of regulatory documents. • Coordinate with internal stakeholders and approved suppliers to obtain the information required for tender submissions, monitor deadlines, and escalate missing information or submission risks in good time. • Obtain confirmed stock availability or lead-time information from the Logistics & Stock Coordinator where required for a quotation and follow up on outstanding information needed to meet deadlines. • Maintain accurate, version-controlled records of quotations, configurations, tenders and proposals, ensuring approved pricing and technical information is current and appropriately authorised. • Following customer acceptance, compile the approved quotation, configuration and supporting documentation for handover to the Customer & Logistics Coordinator, highlighting any discrepancies identified. • Accurately capture quotation, configuration, tender and proposal information in Nexeva's relevant systems, and provide reporting on quotation and proposal activity when required by management.
