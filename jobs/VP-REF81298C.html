About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Learning Advisor Ops: Mining, based in Thabazimbi, Limpopo. The Learning Advisor plays a vital role in facilitating the learning and development activities within the operation. This role is dedicated to supporting the Section Manager Learning Delivery in ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, while strategically managing training programs to enhance employee skills, safety, and operational effectiveness. Safety: Performance and Delivery:

Responsibilities

  • Create a safety culture through productive and authorized operational systems/processes that allow all employees (including contracting partners) to provide their outputs in a risk-controlled environment. Oversee consistent application of Safety & Health principles in all discipline interactions and take personal responsibility for safety of self and others to ensure zero harm and elimination of fatalities.
  • Coordination and delivery of safety training programs in alignment with regulatory standards and organisational safety protocols.
  • Collaborate with the Section Manager Learning Delivery, peers, safety officers, and operational teams to ensure that the training materials are up-to-date and accessible to all employees.
  • Support the implementation of safety awareness campaigns and initiatives to promote a culture of safety excellence across the organization.
  • Coordinate the logistics and administration of training programs, including scheduling, venue arrangement, and participant enrollment.
  • Assist in the development and maintenance of training materials, presentations, and digital learning resources to support various learning initiatives.
  • Collaborate with technical experts to facilitate training sessions and workshops, ensuring that learning objectives are met, and outcomes are evaluated.
  • Provide administrative support for performance management processes, including training needs assessments, performance appraisals, and competency assessments.
  • Serve as a point of contact for employees regarding training inquiries, registration, and logistical support, fostering positive relationships and enhancing the employee experience.
  • Assist in the coordination of talent development initiatives, including mentoring programs, cross-training opportunities, and leadership development initiatives.
  • Collaborate with P&O and operational leaders to identify training needs and prioritize learning interventions based on business goals and workforce requirements.
  • Support the Section Manager Learning Delivery in promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives within the training and development programs.
  • Ensure compliance with training-related legal and regulatory requirements, maintaining accurate records and documentation to support audit readiness.
  • Assist in the development and implementation of compliance training programs, including anti-discrimination, harassment prevention, and ethics training.
  • Collaborate with regulatory agencies and industry partners to stay informed about changes in legislation and industry standards relevant to training and development.
  • Support the Section Manager Learning Delivery in conducting training-related audits, evaluations, and reporting to track compliance metrics and identify areas for improvement.
  • Stay informed about changes in legislation, regulations, and industry standards relevant to learning and development activities, providing updates to Learning Delivery Managers, Advisors and Supervisors as needed.
  • Ensure that the learning procedures and process flow are quality controlled, current, and adhered to at the operations.
  • People & Teams
  • Implement a purpose-driven, high-performing culture that is aligned with the company values, is inclusive and promotes diversity, supports engagement and fosters continuous improvement to work processes.
  • Compliance
  • Ensure compliance with internal and external regulatory, legislative and permitting requirements.

Requirements

  • Qualifications
  • Grade 12
  • NQF 6 OD ETDP Diploma
  • Related Degree would be desirable
  • Shift Supervisor Certificate
  • Mine Overseer Certificate
  • Assessor and Moderator Certificates
  • Risk Management Certification and ISO 9001 of 2015 Certification would be desirable.
  • Experience
  • Minimum of 7 years’ experience in Mining, including 5 years' as Shift Supervisor and 2 years as Mine Overseer.
  • Technical Skills
  • Basic understanding of South African labor laws, regulations, and industry standards relevant to training and development within the mining industry.
  • Familiarity with learning management systems (LMS or SIMS), digital learning tools, and administrative processes for managing training programs and resources.
  • Project Management experience will be advantageous.
  • Knowledge of auditing and quality assurance will be advantageous.
  • Knowledge of instructional design principles, adult learning theories, and training delivery methods to support the development and delivery of effective training programs.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and other software applications used for data management, documentation, and communication.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills to coordinate multiple training activities and deadlines effectively.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills to interact with employees, trainers, vendors, and other stakeholders in a professional and courteous manner.
  • Attention to detail and accuracy in data entry, record-keeping, and documentation to ensure compliance with training-related requirements.
  • Adaptability and flexibility to work in a dynamic and fast-paced environment, responding to changing priorities and urgent requests as needed.
