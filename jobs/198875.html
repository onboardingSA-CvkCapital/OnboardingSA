About the role

The Senior Graphic Designer's primary function is the end-to-end development of high-quality visual communication that supports Fidelity Services Group's brand, marketing, sales and internal communication objectives. The role is responsible for maintaining the creative quality and consistency of all design output, from initial concept development through to final artwork and production. The incumbent will translate technical fire-safety products, services and business information into clear, professional and visually engaging communication for both business-to-business and consumer audiences. Beyond day-to-day design production, the role has an evolving mandate to assist the Marketing Manager in establishing and formalising an internal marketing division. The incumbent will help develop the creative standards, systems, templates and workflows required to improve the division's agility and reduce its reliance on external agencies. Key Responsibilities Graphic Design & Creative Production • Conceptualise and produce high-quality creative work across corporate, marketing, sales, recruitment and internal communication requirements. • Design material for print and digital platforms, including social media content, digital advertising, brochures, product sheets, presentations, proposals, billboards, signage, exhibition material, website graphics and internal communication. • Translate technical fire-safety information into clear, accessible and visually engaging communication. • Develop campaign concepts, creative treatments, mood boards and visual mock-ups that respond effectively to marketing briefs. • Prepare accurate, production-ready artwork for print, digital publishing and external suppliers. • Adapt approved creative work into the correct sizes, specifications and formats for different media platforms. • Maintain organised working files and archives of completed artwork, templates, brand assets and campaign material. • Quality-control all final artwork for layout, spelling, technical accuracy and production readiness. Brand Development & Consistency • Maintain a consistent visual identity and brand tone across all Fidelity Services Group marketing and communication material. • Ensure that all creative output aligns with Fidelity Services Group brand guidelines. • Collaborate with the Marketing Manager on concept development for campaigns, launches, events and internal initiatives. • Work with photographers, videographers, agencies, printers and other creative suppliers to ensure that the approved creative direction is applied correctly. • Review and quality-control creative output produced by junior team members and external suppliers where applicable. • Identify opportunities to strengthen and modernise Fidelity Services Group's visual identity without compromising its corporate credibility.
