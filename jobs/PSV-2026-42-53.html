About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Technology Architect in ICT Planning and Governance, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 600 Lilian Ngoyi Street (formerly known as Van Der Walt Street) Berea Park, Pretoria, 0001 or by email to Post385@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/385 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: Coloured and Indian Males and African, Coloured, Indian and White Females and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Develop frameworks and/or hi-level designs technology architectures.
  • Develop technology architecture framework and principles incorporating security-by-design, risk management, and compliance requirements.
  • Oversee and provide input into the system development life cycle and ensure alignment to technology architecture framework and secure development practices, including applicable information security controls.
  • Provide product standards and roadmaps.
  • Provide input into technology architecture roadmaps to transition information architecture to target state aligned to data strategy while embedding data protection, confidentiality, integrity, and availability requirements.
  • Advise on and provide input into information architecture use cases to be implemented within area of scope with consideration of security, privacy, access control, and regulatory compliance implications.
  • Develop frameworks/policies/processes/procedures that integrate information security governance, control requirements, and secure technology standards.
  • Ensure adherence to the established group technology architectures.
  • Monitor and evaluate the implementation of the technology architecture framework including adherence to defined security controls and risk mitigation measures.
  • Ensure targets and solutions contribute towards achieving and integrated technology architecture that is secure, resilient, and aligned with enterprise security requirements.
  • Build-out data assets aligned to information architecture principles as part of roadmap while applying appropriate data security, classification, retention, and access management controls.
  • Review current deployed technologies and provide appropriate recommendations and roadmaps for enhancements.
  • Review current deployed technologies and identify interconnected problems, determine impact, patterns, trends and identify best fit alternatives to design and implement best practice solutions with due consideration of security vulnerabilities, control gaps, and risk exposure.
  • Work closely with a cross-functional team to assess technology architectural requirements for new and enhanced business operations including security, continuity, compliance, and risk requirements.
  • Establish and manage a continuous technology awareness program.
  • Drive technology architecture awareness (and related risks) for area of responsibility in consultation with relevant stakeholders including awareness of security risks, cyber hygiene, and secure technology adoption.
  • Provide required training to relevant stakeholders on technology architecture framework, policies, standards and principles within area of scope including applicable security requirements, secure usage practices, and compliance obligations.
  • Liaise and communicate the architectural design to the necessary audiences including relevant security considerations, control responsibilities, and risk implications.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements
  • A Grade 12 certificate and a National Diploma in Computer Science/Information Technology (NQF Level 6) or related equivalent IT qualification
  • At least 3 years' experience at junior management in information technology/ICT architectural/design capacity
  • Job related knowledge
  • Strong technical background particularly with respect TOGAF, ZACHMAN etc. Technology Architecture Methodologies
  • All the components of Enterprise Architecture including Business, Information (Data and Applications) and Technology
  • Understand and apply the principles of project management
  • Familiarity with basic graphical modelling approaches, tools and model repositories, and the ability to apply modelling techniques
  • Job related skills
  • Computer literacy
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Time management
  • Research ability
  • Organisational and analytical thinking
  • Interpersonal relations
  • Problem solving
