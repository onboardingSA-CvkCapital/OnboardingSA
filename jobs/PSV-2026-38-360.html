About the role

The Department of Infrastructure Development (Gauteng) is hiring a Senior Administrative Officer: Logistics & Disposals (Inventory) in Goods and Services SCM, based in Head Office (Johannesburg). This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: To apply for the below position, please apply online at https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za Reference number to quote: REFS/048671 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the receipt, verification and processing of requisitions for goods and consumables from internal clients.
  • Liaise with end users, suppliers, and procurement officials regarding delivery schedules and outstanding orders.
  • Coordinate the receipt of goods and verify quantities and specifications against purchase orders and delivery notes.
  • Capture received items on inventory and logistics management systems.
  • Prepare and maintain Goods Received Notes, issues vouchers, and distribution records.
  • Arrange and monitor the distribution of stock and consumables to user sections.
  • Verify that all supporting documentation is complete, accurate and filed.
  • Follow up on discrepancies, shortages, damaged goods and back orders.
  • Maintain a register of requests, receipts and issued items.
  • Coordinate the compilation of routine reports on stock movement and distribution activities.
  • Facilitate administrative control over warehouse operations and storage facilities.
  • Facilitate the storage of goods in an orderly, secure and accessible manner.
  • Monitor stock levels and identify items requiring replenishment.
  • Update bin cards, stock registers and electronic inventory records.
  • Facilitate compliance with occupational health and safety requirements within storage areas.
  • Coordinate the labelling and classification of stock items.
  • Monitor the safekeeping of high-value and sensitive inventory items Report on warehouse-related risks, losses and irregularities to management.
  • Facilitate compilation of monthly inventory status reports.
  • Coordinate and participate in scheduled and ad-hoc stocktaking exercises.
  • Reconcile physical stock counts with inventory records and investigate variances.
  • Prepare stock adjustment documentation for approval.
  • Maintain accurate inventory accounts and audit trails.
  • Compile reports on obsolete, redundant and slow-moving stock.
  • Facilitate compliance with asset and inventory management policies.
  • Provide administrative support during internal and external audits.
  • Facilitate maintenance of filing systems, databases and records related to logistics and inventory management.
  • Draft correspondence, memoranda and submissions.
  • Coordinate the preparation of monthly, quarterly and annual operational reports.
  • Facilitate responses to internal and external enquiries regarding logistics and inventory matters.
  • Coordinate meetings, prepare agendas and record minutes.
  • Sign performance agreements for subordinates based on approved Job Descriptions, Strategic and Annual Performance Plans of the Department.
  • Supervise subordinates and maintain discipline.
  • Supervise training and development of subordinates according to agreed training interventions.
  • Develop and update the service delivery and work plan for subordinates.

Requirements

  • An undergraduate qualification at NQF Level 6 or 7 in Supply Chain Management/Logistics/Cost and Management Accounting/Financial Management/Financial Accounting.
  • At least 2 years of experience in management of Assets.
  • A valid driver’s license.
  • Competencies: Knowledge of the Public Service Regulatory Framework.
  • Knowledge of the department Strategy, Knowledge of the SCM policy and procedures, In depth knowledge of the Public Finance Management Act, Treasury Regulations’ Financial Delegations and Risk Management.
  • Skills:
  • Communication
  • Computer Literacy
  • Analytic Presentation Report
  • Attributes:
  • Team player
  • Able to work independently
  • Professional
  • Quality-driven
  • Ability to work under pressure
