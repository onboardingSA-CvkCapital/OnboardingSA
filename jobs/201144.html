About the role

Our client is looking for an experienced and highly responsive After-Sales Coordinator to oversee and coordinate repairs, maintenance and customer support operations within a fast-paced renewable energy environment. The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating technician teams, managing daily operations, monitoring performance, handling customer communication and ensuring efficient after-sales service delivery. Key Responsibilities Team Leadership & Management • Lead, mentor and manage after-sales technician teams. • Coordinate team capacity according to operational requirements. • Manage timekeeping, attendance, discipline and accountability. • Maintain team structures and promote a culture of ownership and performance.Daily Operations • Coordinate daily technician readiness and job allocation. • Manage the flow of repairs and maintenance bookings. • Allocate technicians based on location, skills and job requirements. • Optimise technician routes to reduce unnecessary travel. • Monitor capacity and proactively manage potential backlogs.Fleet, Stock & Tools • Maintain effective organisation and housekeeping standards across technician vehicles. • Implement tool-control procedures and ensure regular checks. • Monitor stock levels and coordinate replenishment. • Conduct and oversee regular stock checks.Performance & Reporting • Set, monitor and report on technician KPIs. • Prepare productivity and fleet reports. • Maintain after-sales performance scorecards. • Monitor wasted travel and identify opportunities for improvement. • Apply Lean or Six Sigma principles where applicable.Customer Service • Manage customer communication via calls, emails and scheduling confirmations. • Handle and escalate customer complaints appropriately. • Follow up with customers regarding service concerns and negative feedback. • Ensure a consistently professional and customer-focused experience.Warranty & Technical Coordination • Manage the end-to-end warranty process. • Coordinate repairs, returns and replacement stock. • Liaise with OEMs, suppliers and internal technical teams. • Assist with resolving technical and service-related queries.Administration & Compliance • Manage attendance and clocking records. • Follow up on absenteeism and prepare attendance reports. • Ensure health and safety requirements are maintained. • Monitor and control departmental costs. • Maintain accurate operational records and documentation.Requirements • Diploma or Degree in Engineering, Business Management, Operations or a related field. • 3+ years' experience in technical support, customer success, service coordination or operational leadership. • Strong understanding of solar systems and the renewable energy sector. • Experience managing or coordinating technical/service teams. • Strong customer service and conflict-resolution skills. • Advanced proficiency in ticketing systems such as Asana, Zendesk or similar. • Experience with fleet management/telemetry platforms advantageous. • Valid Code 8 driver's licence.
