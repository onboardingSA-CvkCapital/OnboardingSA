About the role

The Department of Tourism is hiring an Assistant Director: Human Resource Administration, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Email application: Recruitment6626@tourism.gov.za Reference number to quote: DT66/2026 Closing date: 20 November 2026 by 16:30 (Late applications will not be considered) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Requirements: Coloured Male and White Male candidates as well as Youth and People with Disabilities are encouraged to apply.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will be responsible for supporting effective recruitment, selection and appointment processes through policy development and the implementation of such policy, Reduction of vacancy rate in line with the target in the strategic plan, Compiling and advertising of posts, Provisioning of advice to line managers on recruitment matters in line with the relevant legislative framework and internal policies, Making written follow-ups with line managers on delayed recruitment activities to ensure that Posts are filled within time frames, Ensuring proper recording of applications and submission of applications to line managers within agreed upon standards, Writing quality recruitment submissions in respect of transfers and secondments etc, Arranging competency assessments, Conducting personnel suitability checks and verification of qualifications, Attending to any other recruitment related activities in the end to end process, Supporting the administration and application of conditions of service and benefits, Quality control of leave administration, Quality control of completed pension forms destined to GEPF/GPAA, Providing advice to employees on pension related matters, Providing advice to employees regarding medical aid administration (GEMS), Processing of resettlement of employees on transfers, appointments and retirement, Processing of long service recognition and arrange payments and hand out letters and certificates, Managing housing allowance and state guarantee scheme applications, Facilitating the process of resignations, retirements, deaths and dismissals, Giving advice , provide relevant forms and recommend/approve leave gratuity payouts, Co-ordinating the PILIR process between the department and the Health Risk Manager, including writing submissions, Attending to any other service conditions and benefits related activities, Providing administrative support and supervisory duties, Logistical arrangements for meetings, Obtaining monthly PERSAL and Establishment reports in order to ensure alignment between recruitment processes and funded vacant posts, Taking minutes during meetings in which secretariat duties are required (e.g. interviews), and prepare where applicable action lists, Following-up on outstanding actions lists, Input during the review of HR systems, policies and processes, Auditing files, Maintaining various databases and HR related information on EDMS, Overseeing the performance of subordinates and manage their inputs, discipline and wellbeing, Structuring MMS/SMS packages and approve PERSAL transactions, Administer Injury of Duty cases, Administering probations, Presenting and conducting information sharing session on topical issues related to the job, Conducting various written reports and or submission, Participating in specialised projects, Supervising, manage and do quality control of the work of others, Implementing and approve various PERSAL transactions.
