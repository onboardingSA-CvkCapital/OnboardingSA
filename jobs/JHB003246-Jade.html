About the role

The Safari Manager is responsible for overseeing all safari operations, ensuring exceptional guest experiences while maintaining the highest standards of safety, conservation, and operational efficiency. This role manages the guiding team, safari vehicle fleet, bush walking activities, anti-poaching awareness initiatives, and the maintenance of all safari equipment.
