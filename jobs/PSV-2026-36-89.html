About the role

The Department of Education (Eastern Cape) is hiring a Chief Education Therapist – Speech and Language Therapist (Lspid), based in JOE Gqabi District Office. This is a permanent post, paying R598,260 – R664,410 a year. Responsibilities

  • Transversal Outreach:
  • Provide itinerant therapeutic support to designated Special Care Centres and Special Schools/Resource Centres for learners with LSPID/Mainstream schools and High level of support Units catering for learners with severe to profound needs
  • Support the rollout of the Learning Programme for LSPID through screening, specialized assessment, and tailored communication intervention
  • Function as a key member of the Districtbased Support Team (DBST), collaborating with Psychologists, Occupational Therapists, Physiotherapists, and Special Needs Teachers.
  • Train and mentor caregivers, parents, and school staff on the implementation of communication strategies and the Learning Programme.
  • Promote the rights of learners with disabilities to quality, public-funded education in line with Education White Paper 6 and the SIAS Policy.
  • Maintain accurate learner records, monitor progress, and compile comprehensive reports for the ECDOE and National Department of Basic Education (DBE).

Requirements

  • An appropriate recognized qualification that allows for registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as a Speech and Language Therapist.
  • Current, valid registration with the HPCSA in Independent Practice.
  • At least 3 years appropriate experience post-registration with the HPCSA as a Speech and Language Therapist.
  • Driver’s License:
  • A valid driver’s license is mandatory as the role requires extensive travel within the District
  • Advanced proficiency in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) for grant monitoring and data reporting
  • Knowledge and skills:
  • In-depth knowledge of Inclusive Education policies (SIAS, White Paper 6)
  • Expertise in Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) and its application for non-verbal learners with profound disabilities
  • Ability to take initiative, work under pressure, and apply innovative problem-solving in under-resourced care centre environments
  • Knowledge of safe positioning and modified textures is vital for their safety in care centres
  • Ability to understanding how sensory processing (e.g., tactile defensiveness or auditory hypersensitivity) impacts a learner's ability to communicate in a noisy care centre.
  • Excellent communication skills.
