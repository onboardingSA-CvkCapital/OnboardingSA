Responsibilities

  • Manage, plan and coordinate district skills development programmes, including: Identify skills development needs within the district, Develop and implement skills development plans aligned with provincial and national priorities, Coordinate skills development programmes within the district in collaboration with TVET colleges, training providers, municipalities, industry stakeholders, and community organizations, Coordinate site visits to ensure effective teaching and learning.
  • Management of stakeholder engagement and communication within the district relating to skills development, including: Facilitate and participate in regular stakeholder meetings and forums to promote collaboration, NGOs, cooperatives, women's groups, youth organizations, SETA, local business, municipalities etc.), Act as a liaison between provincial offices and local stakeholders, Advocate for local needs in provincial-level planning and funding decisions, Marketing and promote skills development programmes within the district.
  • Foster partnerships with the district stakeholders for internships, apprenticeships, and job placements, including: Liaise with the leaner placement officer at provincial office, Assist with the identification of workplaces within the district, Compile name list of available workplaces in the district, Support workplaces to be SETA compliant Ensure inclusion of marginalized groups (e.g. youth, women, persons with disabilities).
  • Participate in community outreach programmes within the district, including: Identify existing community outreach initiatives, Ensure access to marketing materials for the community outreach initiatives, Collect CV of learners and submit to provincial office for consolidation, Monitor the impact of participation of community outreach programmes within the district.
  • Implement effective monitoring and evaluation reporting systems within the district, including: Monitor the implementation of skills development programmes across the district, Collect data and prepare reports on training outcomes, learners progress, and employment opportunities, Evaluate skills development programmes effectiveness and recommend improvements, Maintain accurate records of training programmes, learner's performance, and outcomes/results, Compile progress reports to the provincial office.

Requirements

  • Appropriate qualification at NQF Level 7 in Public Administration/Human Resources Development as recognised by the South African Qualification Authority (SAQA) or equivalent.
  • Relevant experience 8 -10 years in administrative experience.
  • Knowledge of relevant Provincial and National legislation, Public Administration, Treasury Regulations, Ability to do research and benchmarking regarding skills developing activities and knowledge in Public Service policies especially regarding Training and Development.
