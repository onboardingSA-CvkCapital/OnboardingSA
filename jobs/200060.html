About the role

Job Purpose: The Chief Marketing & Growth (Business Development) Officer is the executive accountable for translating the firm’s strategy into a focused market and growth agenda. The role leads the integrated brand, marketing, communications, client experience and business development functions in support of the firm’s strategic growth objectives. The role builds market authority in agreed priority industries and services; identifies and secures new business opportunities, strengthens client and stakeholder relationships, enhancing the firm's market presence, and positioning the firm as a leading professional services firm. Working closely with Executive Leadership, Directors, Service Lines, Regions, and key stakeholders, the role ensures that all marketing and business development activities are strategically aligned, effectively delivered, measurable, and contribute to revenue growth and market expansion Job Description Strategic Marketing and Brand Management • Serve as a member of the Executive Committee and act
