About the role

Standard Bank is hiring a Senior Finance Manager, Corporate & Investment Banking, based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. We are seeking an experienced Senior Finance Manager to join our Corporate & Investment Banking division in Johannesburg, South Africa. In this pivotal , Contract role, you will oversee complex financial operations, drive strategic financial initiatives, and provide expert guidance on corporate and investment banking transactions. The successful candidate will demonstrate exceptional analytical capability, decisive leadership, and a meticulous approach to financial management within a fast-paced banking environment. Manage and oversee financial operations across corporate and investment banking portfolios, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and optimal performance. Conduct comprehensive financial analysis and develop sophisticated financial models to support transaction structuring, valuation, and investment decisions

Responsibilities

  • Lead the evaluation and execution of mergers, acquisitions, and capital markets transactions, providing strategic financial counsel to clients and internal stakeholders
  • Monitor and analyse market trends, economic indicators, and competitive landscape to inform strategic financial planning and risk assessment
  • Ensure strict adherence to regulatory requirements, including FSCA guidelines and JSE regulations, whilst maintaining robust internal controls
  • Prepare detailed financial reports and presentations for senior management and external stakeholders, communicating complex financial information with clarity
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including legal, compliance, and operations to facilitate seamless transaction execution
  • Mentor and supervise junior finance team members, fostering a culture of excellence and continuous professional development
  • Identify and implement process improvements to enhance operational efficiency and strengthen financial governance
  • Maintain and develop relationships with key clients, providing proactive financial advisory services and identifying new business opportunities

Requirements

  • Matric, Diploma ,Degree
  • Minimum 7 years of professional experience in corporate finance, investment banking, or related financial management roles
  • Proven expertise in financial modelling, valuation techniques, and transaction structuring
  • Strong knowledge of corporate finance principles, capital markets, and M&A processes
  • Advanced proficiency in financial analysis software and tools (Bloomberg Terminal, financial modelling platforms)
  • Comprehensive understanding of South African financial regulations and compliance frameworks
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with meticulous attention to detail
  • Demonstrated ability to manage multiple complex transactions simultaneously
  • Strong people skills and proven capability to lead and develop finance teams
  • Professional communication skills with ability to present financial information to diverse audiences
  • Desirable Qualifications
  • Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation or Chartered Accountant (SA) qualification
  • Experience with enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems and data analytics platforms Background in risk management and financial compliance within banking sector
  • Track record of successfully leading high-value transactions or financial projects
  • Knowledge of emerging financial technologies and digital transformation in banking
