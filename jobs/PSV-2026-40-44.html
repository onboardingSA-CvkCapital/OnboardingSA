Responsibilities

  • Demand and Acquisition Management: The manager must ensure that the procurement of all goods and services aligns with the College’s Annual Procurement Plan.
  • Key Tasks: Overseeing the specifications process, managing the quotation system for lower-threshold purchases, and ensuring all acquisitions are competitive and cost-effective.
  • Performance Indicator: 100% execution of the approved procurement plan within budget.
  • Logistics and Inventory Management: This area focuses on the physical movement and storage of goods across various campuses.
  • Key Tasks: Managing the central stores, overseeing the distribution of textbooks and consumables to satellite campuses, and maintaining optimal stock levels to prevent over-purchasing or stock-outs.
  • Performance Indicator: Accuracy of inventory records and reduction in stock loss/wastage.
  • Bid Committee Secretariat and Administration: The SCM Manager acts as the custodian of the formal bidding process.
  • Key Tasks: Facilitating the Bid Specification, Evaluation, and Adjudication Committees.
  • Ensuring that all bids are advertised, opened, and awarded in strict accordance with the PPPFA (Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act).
  • Performance Indicator: Zero successful appeals or legal challenges against awarded tenders due to procedural non-compliance.
  • Contract and Supplier Performance Management: Once a service provider is appointed, the SCM Manager must ensure value for money is realised.
  • Key Tasks: Monitoring the performance of service providers against Service Level Agreements (SLAs), managing contract renewals/terminations, and maintaining an updated and compliant database on the Central Supplier Database (CSD).
  • Performance Indicator: Timely delivery of services and documented quarterly supplier performance reviews.
  • Asset and Disposal Management: Responsible for the lifecycle of the College’s movable assets, from acquisition to retirement.
  • Key Tasks: Maintaining a GRAP-compliant asset register, ensuring all assets are barcoded and tracked across campuses, and managing the disposal of redundant or obsolete assets through auctions or transfers.
  • Performance Indicator: Successful reconciliation between the asset register and the general ledger during annual audits.
  • Compliance, Risk Management, and Audit Support: Ensuring the SCM unit always remains “audit-ready”.
  • Key Tasks
  • Developing and implementing internal SCM controls to prevent irregular, fruitless, and wasteful expenditure
  • Preparing all necessary documentation and "audit trails" for the Auditor-General (AG) or internal auditors
  • Performance Indicator: Achievement of a "clean audit" outcome for the Supply Chain Management unit.

  • Digital literate and competent in Word/Excel/PowerPoint/Outlook/Teams.

  • Asset Management: Knowledge of GRAP standards regarding asset registers and disposal.
  • Analytical Thinking: Ability to interpret complex financial data and procurement trends.
  • Negotiation: Proven ability to manage Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and vendor relations.
  • Communication: High-level verbal and written reporting skills for council and internal Section and Unit presentations.
  • Computer Literacy: Proficiency in MS Office (Advanced Excel) and relevant ERP systems (e.g., COLTECH).
  • Leadership
  • Strong team management and conflict resolution capabilities
  • Extensive experience in bid committee administration (specification, evaluation, and adjudication)
  • Proven track record in inventory control management
  • Experience in internal audit coordination, specifically addressing SCM findings
  • Experience in the development and implementation of SCM policies and standard operating procedures (SOPs)
  • A Postgraduate qualification (Honours or BCom Hons) in Supply Chain or Finance
