About the role

Junior Software Engineer Location: Cape Town, hybrid · Type: Permanent, full-time · Reports to: Senior Software Engineer About Lesaka Lesaka Technologies is a Southern African fintech building the connected ecosystem for financial inclusion — transactional banking, lending, insurance, payments and cash management for merchants, consumers and enterprise clients across the region. We're publicly listed (Nasdaq: LSAK / JSE: LSK) and growing fast. EasyPay powers airtime, data, electricity, lotto and 600+ bill payments through the largest retail footprint in South Africa. The role We need a junior engineer to help build and maintain the systems that process millions of bill payments a day. You'll work close to production code from week one; fixing bugs, shipping small features and learning how a high-volume payments platform actually runs. This isn't a training-wheels role: you'll ship real code into a live system, with support from senior engineers around you. What you'll own - Build and ship small-to-medium features across the EasyPay payments stack - Fix bugs reported from production and QA, from root cause to deployed fix - Write unit and integration tests for the code you write - Take part in code reviews- both giving and receiving feedback - Investigate and help resolve production issues alongside senior engineers - Keep documentation for the systems you touch up to date What you bring - 0-2 years' professional software development experience, or a strong portfolio from personal/academic projects - Working knowledge of at least one modern language (e.g. C#, Java, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript) - Understanding of relational databases and SQL - Familiarity with Git and collaborative development workflows - Ability to debug unfamiliar code and ask good questions when stuck - A CS degree, diploma, or equivalent bootcamp/self-taught background Nice to have - Exposure to REST APIs or microservices - Experience with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS) - Interest in payments, fintech, or high-volume transactional systems Our culture — what we expect from you Lesaka is a fast-paced fintech. The people who thrive here have a few things in common: - Ownership: You don't wait to be asked. If something is broken or unclear, you call for the ball and fix it. - Fast adaptation: Priorities shift, markets move, regulations change. You don't flinch — you re-plan and move. -Execution mindset: Ideas are cheap. Shipping is the job. You walk slow, run fast: think it through, then move with urgency. - Customer first: The merchant, consumer or enterprise client is why we exist. Your decisions trace back to them. If you'd rather optimise a process to perfection over six months than ship something useful in six weeks, this won't be the right fit.
