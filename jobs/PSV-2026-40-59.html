About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring an ICT Technical Manager in Deeds ICT, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Office of the Chief Registrar of Deeds at 600 Lillian Ngoyi Street, Berea Park, Pretoria, 0002. Candidates are also encouraged to apply by using this link http://deedsportal.deeds.gov.za (Please separate Application Form for each post) before the closing date as no late applications will be considered Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/342 Closing date: 20 November 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian and White Males, and African, Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Manage ICT service operations and operational command.
  • Lead daily operations for production applications, databases, infrastructure, networks, hosting, end-user support and service-desk interfaces within the post’s mandate.
  • Establish an integrated operational rhythm covering service health checks, shift or duty handovers where applicable, event monitoring, daily exceptions, weekly service reviews and monthly management reporting.
  • Coordinate response to major incidents and high-impact outages, ensure timely business communication, executive escalation, technical recovery and documented post-incident review.
  • Ensure incidents, service requests and operational exceptions are logged, prioritised, assigned, resolved and closed against approved service targets and escalation paths.
  • Maintain clear operational ownership, support models, contact matrices and runbooks for all critical services.
  • Assure service availability, performance, capacity and technical resilience.
  • Monitor end-to-end availability and performance of critical services and identify trends, constraints, single points of failure and emerging risks before they affect service delivery.
  • Manage capacity and demand planning for compute, storage, databases, middleware, connectivity and other operational platforms, aligned with business growth and digital transformation roadmaps.
  • Coordinate preventative maintenance, patching, upgrades, health checks and lifecycle interventions through approved maintenance windows and change controls.
  • Maintain accurate baseline information on supported environments, dependencies, configurations, warranties, licences and technical obsolescence.
  • Drive remedial plans for legacy, unsupported or unstable technology and escalate investment or procurement decisions with evidence-based motivation.
  • Manage incident, problem, request and continual service improvement practices.
  • Implement fit-for-purpose IT service management practices for incident, request, problem, knowledge and servicelevel management, aligned with approved departmental policy and standards.
  • Lead root-cause analysis for recurring and severe incidents, assign corrective and preventative actions, track implementation, and verify sustained control effectiveness.
  • Maintain a prioritised continual-improvement register based on service data, audit findings, user feedback, risk assessments and technology trends.
  • Develop service dashboards showing availability, responsiveness, backlog, recurring incidents, recovery performance, user-impact trends and improvement actions.
  • Promote proactive support, knowledge reuse, automation, standardisation and measurable reduction of avoidable incidents.
  • Control change, release, deployment and configuration across production environments.
  • Coordinate the end-to-end operational release and deployment process, including readiness assessment, scheduling, approvals, testing evidence, implementation, validation, back-out planning and early-life support.
  • Ensure production changes are authorised, segregated from development activities, traceable and supported by complete technical, security, business and operational documentation.
  • Coordinate build, test, staging and production environment teams and ensure release acceptance criteria and business signoff are obtained before deployment.
  • Maintain configuration and release records sufficient to support auditability, troubleshooting, service restoration and asset accountability.
  • Review change outcomes, failed changes and emergency changes and ensure lessons are converted into strengthened controls.
  • Oversee service continuity, backup, recovery, security operations and operational risk.
  • Coordinate operational implementation of ICT security requirements, including access control, vulnerability remediation, security patching, privileged access oversight, log monitoring and incident escalation, in collaboration with designated security functions.
  • Ensure backup schedules, restore testing, disaster recovery plans, recovery procedures and continuity dependencies are current, tested, evidenced and aligned with approved recovery objectives.
  • Maintain and monitor an ICT operational risk register, control action plans and treatment measures for services within the post’s scope.
  • Ensure operational data, systems and infrastructure are protected for confidentiality, integrity, availability, authenticity and accountability.
  • Escalate material risks, control failures and non-compliance promptly and track corrective and preventative actions to verified closure.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements: A Grade 12 Certificate and a National Diploma (NQF Level 6) in Computer Science, Information Systems, Information Technology, Software Engineering or a relevant equivalent qualification.
  • A recognised ICT operations and technical service management professional certification such as ITIL®, COBIT®, PMP®, PRINCE2®, Microsoft Azure, AWS, Oracle, Cisco, VMware, Red Hat, CISSP®, CCSP®, ISO 27001, ISO 22301, or equivalent.
  • At least 3 years junior management in, multi-site or high-availability IT operations environment.
  • Jobrelated knowledge
  • Infrastructure or platform support
  • Service management
  • Incident and problem management
  • Change and release control
  • Supporting government, regulated, land-registry, financial, records-management or other mission-critical services
  • ICT operations and service management principles, including incident, request, problem, change, release, configuration, availability, capacity, continuity, service-level and knowledge management.
  • ICT governance, internal control, risk management, audit evidence and records management.
  • Enterprise infrastructure concepts covering networks, servers, virtualisation, storage, databases, middleware, cloud/hybrid services, monitoring and automation.
  • Systems development and operational acceptance across the system lifecycle, including testing, deployment, rollback and earlylife support.
  • Cybersecurity operations, identity and access control, vulnerability and patch management, logging, backup, recovery and incident escalation.
  • Supplier, contract, licence, asset and financial management within the publicsector control environment.
  • Applicable public-service legislation, policies and governance requirements, including the PFMA, public procurement prescripts, information protection, records management, and occupational health and safety requirements.
  • The Deeds Registration business environment, eDRS, core Deeds systems and national registry service dependencies, or the ability to acquire this knowledge rapidly.
  • Job-related skills
  • Business continuity/disaster recovery
  • Operational risk and management reporting
  • Operational leadership, prioritisation and command during service disruption
  • Analytical thinking, systems thinking, root-cause analysis and risk-based decision-making
  • Service performance measurement, dashboarding, trend analysis and evidence-based reporting
  • Stakeholder engagement, facilitation, negotiation, conflict resolution and executive communication
  • Project, programme, change, release and vendor coordination
  • Policy, standard, SOP, runbook and technical-report writing
  • People management, coaching, performance management and multidisciplinary team leadership
  • Financial, contract and procurement oversight within delegated authority
  • High levels of integrity, judgement, accountability and attention to detail
  • Willingness to travel and to participate in planned architecture reviews or production-readiness activities when required
  • Valid driver's licence as travel forms an inherent requirement of the post
