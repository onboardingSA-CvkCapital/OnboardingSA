About the role

At Masthead (Pty) Ltd, our passion is keeping businesses in business. As a national supplier of practice management and business development services, and compliance monitoring of FAIS, FICA, POPI, and NCR, Masthead assists Independent Financial Advisors, corporate FSPs and other business sectors throughout South Africa so they can stay in business, improve productivity and prosper. We know this can only be achieved through the calibre of people we employ – people who are client focused, set themselves high standards of delivery and have a willingness to go the extra mile. POSITION SUMMARY We are looking for a highly organised and detail-oriented Operations Coordinator to support the daily operations of our RiskMaster. This role is critical in ensuring efficient handling of support requests, maintaining system performance standards, and acting as a bridge between end-users, IT teams, and risk management stakeholders. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Helpdesk Support & Query Management • Act as the primary point of contact for all RiskMaster-related queries • Log, track, and resolve helpdesk tickets within agreed SLAs • Monitor response timelines and proactively escalate complex issues to relevant technical teams • Provide timely feedback and maintain clear communication with users • Identify recurring issues and recommend improvements Organisational Structure Maintenance • Action and implement structural and user changes • Perform bulk updates to user assignments on active audit reports when role holders exit or change positions • Conduct monthly system access reviews to ensure accuracy of user roles, permissions, and reporting lines • Liaise with business units to ensure organisational changes are accurately and timeously reflected in the system • Maintain the integrity and accuracy of organisational and user data within RiskMaster. OneDrive & Document Management • Manage RiskMaster documentation within OneDrive • Ensure version control, accessibility, and compliance with governance standards • Maintain user guides, SOPs, and training materials General Operations & Professional Conduct • Support day-to-day helpdesk and operational activities • Adhere to company policies and compliance requirements • Maintain professionalism in all stakeholder interactions • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives Internal Compliance Operations Function • File and update internal records and registers. • Checklist procedure to identify any gaps in records. • Assist with the maintenance of data by updating and capturing as required. • Generate reports at required intervals. • Assist with the preparation of consolidated reports. • Provide administrative support in the management of data banks and reporting systems. • General office duties such as filing, email and assisting with other functions on an ad-hoc basis. • Assist the Compliance team with telephonic queries, email correspondence and tracking. Front Office Support • Provide administrative and coordination support to business users • Assist with onboarding and training of new RiskMaster users • Facilitate communication between business and technical teams • Ensure a positive and responsive user experience • Support day-to-day RiskMaster helpdesk, compliance and operational activities. • Work collaboratively with business and technical stakeholders to resolve issues and improve service delivery
