About the role

Rand Water is hiring a Manager Partnerships & Collaboration in Rand Water Institute, based at Rietvlei, Pretoria. To provide strategic leadership and direction to the partnerships, collaboration, and international water cooperation Division within the Rand Water Institute. This includes the initiation, conclusion, and management of strategic Research, Development, and Innovation (RDI) and water partnerships with local and global institutions.
