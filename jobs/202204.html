About the role

Motus Aftermarket Parts is seeking an experienced Accountant to join the team at the Meadowview Head Office. The successful candidate will be responsible for the financial and administrative management of NAPA operations, including the implementation, monitoring, and maintenance of effective financial controls designed to safeguard company assets and ensure accurate financial reporting. The role will provide financial support and guidance to the Financial Manager and area teams, hile ensuring that financial policies, controls, and procedures are consistently applied in accordance with company standards. Qualifications and Experience • BCom or BCom Honours degree. • Minimum 5 years’ progressively responsible accounting experience within a medium to large organisation. • Strong accuracy, attention to detail, and problem-solving skills. • Strong administrative and analytical capabilities. • Excellent organisational skills. • Ability to work under pressure and meet strict deadlines. • High level of integrity, honesty, and trustworthiness.Knowledge, Skills and Abilities • Proven experience in supervising, managing, and supporting staff. • Ability to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team. • Strong financial acumen and accounting knowledge. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills. • Proficiency in MS Office, databases, and accounting software. • Advanced Excel skills are essential. • Strong administrative, analytical, and managerial capabilities.Key Performance Indicators • Financial Operations • Review bank reconciliations, ensuring all reconciling items
