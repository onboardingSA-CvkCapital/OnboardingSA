About the role

PURPOSE OF ROLE We are seeking a skilled and driven Mechanical Engineer/Technologist to provide technical expertise in the delivery of compliant, cost-effective, and constructible engineering designs across the full project lifecycle — from tender through to handover. The successful candidate will ensure professional engineering outputs are delivered on time, within budget, and aligned with client requirements, applicable codes and standards, and internal engineering management systems. The role also provides technical support across projects, contributing to proposal development, procurement, commissioning, and handover activities.
