Responsibilities

  • To provide an efficient and effective administrative autopsy service.
  • Preparation of relevant documentation required during autopsy process.
  • Identification of body to doctor.
  • Taking verbatim notes as directed by the doctor.
  • Fingerprinting of deceased for identification of deceased.
  • Photographing (including downloading of images into relevant database) for identification and record keeping.
  • Collection of exhibits and samples/specimens under direct supervision of medical practitioners.
  • Documentation and appropriate safe keeping and handover to SAPS of all exhibits and specimens to maintain chain of evidence.
  • Maintain chain of evidence register.
  • Assist families with identification process.
  • To provide effective transportation service in the collection and offloading of bodies from crime scene to mortuary.
  • Collection and transportation of corpse between death scene and medico legal mortuary including loading and offloading of corpse.
  • Ensure completion of all relevant documentation necessary in collection and transportation process.
  • Admission of bodies into medico legal mortuary in accordance with prescripts.
  • To maintain a technical autopsy service.
  • Perform reconstruction of a corpse under direction/instruction/supervision of the medical offer in charge of case/s.
  • Movement of weighing corpse.
  • Ensure washing, shrouding and labelling of bodies post examination and ensuring area of work is maintained in a clean and hygienic condition.
  • Handle and safeguard properly of deceased and disposal of property to next of kin in the prescribed manner.
  • To perform administrative duties including registry, fleet, assets and stock control activities.
  • Typing of all postmortem and any other subsequent documentation generated because of the postmortem examination.
  • Communication and making necessary arrangements with relevant role players in preparation for postmortem examination as per medical officer’s instructions.
  • Maintain an effective and efficient filing system for all postmortem related documentation and ensure safe custody thereof.
  • Develop and maintain a database of all information pertaining to the autopsy service and timely provision of monthly summary statistics to relevant stakeholders.
  • Stock control including early warning arrangements.
  • Reception and switchboard duties.
  • Liaising with next of kin, undertakers, municipalities, hospitals, and SAPS to arrange burials, cremations and pauper burials of unclaimed bodies.
  • Liaising with court, negotiating court attendance by medical officers and other medico mortuary staff.

Requirements

  • Grade 12.
  • A valid driver’s license minimum code 08.
  • Recommendations Computer Literacy (MS Word, Excel, Power Point).
  • Experience in mortuary services at least 06 months.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Behavioural Attributes and Competencies Required Knowledge of mortuary administrative processes and policies.
  • Knowledge of Medico-Legal protocols and prescripts.
  • Knowledge of criminal justice system.
  • Knowledge of waste management policy.
  • Knowledge of infection control protocols and OHS.
  • Knowledge of cleaning materials and use of cleaning equipment.
