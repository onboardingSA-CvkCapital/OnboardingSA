About the role

PURPOSE OF THE ROLE The project manager is responsible for ensuring seamless and successful delivery of construction/corporate identity installation projects by ensuring all allocated projects are completed within the stipulated quality, cost and time parameters. This role requires a self-starter, motivated and accountable individual with the knowledge and experience of managing multiple site projects simultaneously. The role reports to the Head of Facilities and operates with multiple Business Units and cross-functional teams. An individual who is self-led, highly motivated and can engage professionally with multiple stakeholders at different organization levels. align teams and achieve goals effectively • Set project goals, milestones and objectives that support and prioritise Business objectives • Align key project deliverables to ensure efficient delivery and achievement of targets and objectives • QUALITY AND RISK MANAGEMENT • Develop frameworks to identify, manage and mitigate project risks and quality metrics • Proactively identify, analyse, review align with appropriate quality managements systems and satisfy stakeholder requirements • Create early warning detection systems within the project to minimise deviations and correct them to align to project parameters • CONTRACTOR SUPPLIER AND VENDOR MANAGEMENT • Align vendor management with project objectives to create and maximise value and output for successful project delivery • Build collaborative supplier partnerships for mutual benefit • Optimize resource selection for optimal performance and compliance achievement • Monitor supplier performance through predetermined vendor
