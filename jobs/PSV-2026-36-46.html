About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Chief Land Claims Commissioner in Restitution, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a contract post, paying R1,885,710 a year. How to apply: Nominations or applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 600 Lilian Ngoyi Street (formerly known as Van Der Walt Street) Berea Park, Pretoria, 0001 or by email to Post274@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/274 Closing date: 23 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: Coloured and Indian Males, and African, Coloured and Indian Females, and Persons with disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Provides strategic leadership and guidance for the provision of Restitution services in the following areas: Provide strategic leadership and direction as well as implement the functions of the Commission are described in the Restitution of Land Rights Act No. 22 of 1994.
  • Provide access to rights in land, including land ownership and sustainable development.
  • Serve as the Head of the Commission on Restitution of Land Rights and the Branch Restitution.
  • Advise the Executive Authority on policy development and implementation.
  • Interface with broader processes around land reform at the national level.
  • Interface with the Director-General:
  • Land Reform and Rural Development in his/her capacity as the accounting officer of the Commission
  • Ensure that Restitution projects are of high quality and conform to policy objectives of poverty eradication and sustainable development
  • Ensure effective governance, accountability and compliance with relevant legislation
  • Drive organisational performance, transformation and service delivery
  • Coordinate strategic planning and compilation of APP
  • Provide financial management services to the Commission
  • Provide legal support to Commission in terms of restitution issues
  • Provide risk and compliance services to the Commission
  • Manage Commission finances, human resource and assets
  • Coordinate service delivery
  • Provides strategic management and direction to the Branch:
  • Develops Restitution strategic plan in agreement with the Director-General: Department of land Reform and Rural Development
  • Develops and reports on annual Restitution operational and work plans linked to the Restitution Act
  • Prepares financial reports/statements on a monthly, quarterly and annual basis
  • Estimates progress review and budget adjustments on an annual basis
  • Prepares technical procedures and standards according to policy
  • Sets realistic goals and objectives for the Commission and the Branch to be attained
  • Coordinates the development and maintenance of Branch system and controls
  • Ensures protection of the Branch from risk and fraud at all times
  • Develops a well-defined communication strategy for the Branch
  • Provides strategic direction towards an effective and efficient commission: Oversee implementation towards establishing schedule 3 (A) Entity.
  • Approves business Case and legislation.
  • Redesign the settlement models in sector categories.
  • Foster stakeholder relations and partnerships across government, business and civil society.
  • Lead and Manage the Business unit in accordance with good Governance: Ensure participation at Quality Control Committees (QCC) and signing approval of claims.
  • Manage Restitution Legal Matters including Litigation and Compliance matters.
  • Manage IT Systems.
  • Manage expenditure risk.
  • Ensure that revised institutional form is implemented.
  • Develop a detailed business process linked to the standard operating procedures and time frames to ensure effective information and records management.
  • Ensure that the Statutory commission meetings are held in compliance with the Restitution.
  • Decide on request for access to information in terms of PAIA: Undertake functions as the information officer of the Commission.
  • Manage the identification and locate records.
  • Ensure that the third parties are notified where required.
  • Take decisions on whether to grant/deny access.
  • Manage the calculated charges applicable to fees.
  • Provide access to records if the decision is to grant access.

  • At least 8 years of experience at senior managerial level in Land Reform/Restitution Environment
  • Demonstrated leadership in a large complex organisation
