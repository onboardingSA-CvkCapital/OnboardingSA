About the role

The Department of Infrastructure Development (Gauteng) is hiring a Deputy Director: Strategic Planning and Service Delivery in Strategic Planning and Service Delivery, based in Head Office Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: To apply for the below position, please apply online at https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za. Only online applications will be considered and for general enquiries please contact Human Resource on /. Reference number to quote: REFS/057431 Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the development and review of the Departmental 5-year Strategic Plan and Annual Performance Plan aligned to the environment sector priorities, Government’s Medium-Term Development Plan and Government’s Planning Frameworks.
  • Manage and coordinate the departmental planning cycle in accordance with applicable planning frameworks, prescripts, timelines and management directives.
  • Manage and facilitate the consolidation and quality assurance of programme inputs to ensure alignment with departmental priorities, provincial priorities, legislative mandates and national frameworks.
  • Provide technical and advisory guidance to programme managers on the formulation and refinement of strategic priorities, outputs, indicators and targets.
  • Manage and facilitate strategic planning sessions, Lekgotlas, APP reviews and related management engagements.
  • Manage and coordinate the identification of strategic planning gaps, inconsistencies and risks and advise management on appropriate interventions.
  • Monitor the quality and relevance of strategic planning outputs and recommend improvements to strengthen departmental planning.
  • Manage the development, review and implementationreadiness of Annual Operational Plans (AOPs) across departmental programmes Coordinate the translation of Strategic Plan and APP commitments into programme-level operational outputs, activities, milestones and deliverables.
  • Facilitate the assessment of AOP indicators and targets to ensure that they are SMART.
  • Manage and coordinate the development of Annual Operational Plans (AOP).
  • Manage and facilitate the development of AOP emanating from strategic outcome and performance outputs in line with the Annual Performance Plan.
  • Facilitate alignment between AOP commitments, budget allocations, procurement planning and human resource planning inputs.
  • Facilitate the identification and assessment of implementation, risks, resource constraints and dependencies that may affect planned outputs.
  • Monitor the integration and consistency of operational plans across programmes and recommend corrective measures where required.
  • Coordinate the departmental performance reporting cycle and ensure timely consolidation and submission of quarterly and annual performance information.
  • Manage and coordinate the quality, consistency and integrity of performance information received from departmental programmes.
  • Manage and coordinate analysis of organizational performance trends, deviations, under-performance and emerging risks and provide management with strategic advice.
  • Manage the preparation and presentation of consolidated performance reports and management information.
  • Coordinate the identification of corrective actions and performance improvement measures arising from performance analysis.
  • Manage and coordinate the development and review of the Department’s Service Delivery Improvement Programme and Plan, Service Delivery Charter and Service Delivery Model.
  • Provide strategic support to Branches on the development of Service Delivery Improvement Plan, Service Delivery Charter and Service Delivery Mode.
  • Produce Service Delivery quarterly and annual citizen’s reports as per set departmental, Department of Public Service and Administration and Office of the Premier policies, frameworks and guidelines.
  • Manage the development and maintenance of required statistical data on programme and service delivery monitoring targets.
  • Coordinate the assessment of customer satisfaction and ensure that findings are incorporated into service delivery improvement initiatives.
  • Coordinate the identification and assessment of service delivery challenges, trends and systemic areas requiring improvement.
  • Manage and coordinate research, environmental scanning and benchmarking activities relating to developments that may impact on departmental service delivery and strategic priorities.
  • Manage and coordinate the preparation and analysis of environmental scans, SWOT assessments, scenario-planning and strategic research reports.
  • Facilitate the identification of opportunities for service delivery innovation, process improvement and improved organizational performance.
  • Coordinate the provision of strategic research and analytical inputs to management planning, performance reviews and decision-making processes.
  • Manage performance agreements for subordinates based on approved Job Descriptions, Strategic and Annual Performance Plans of the Department.
  • Manage subordinates and maintain discipline.
  • Manage training and development of subordinates according to agreed training interventions.
  • Develop and update the service delivery and work plan for subordinates.
  • Manage funds in the unit in compliance with Public Finance Management Act and the Treasury Regulations.
  • Monitor under spending or overspending by the unit in terms of the departmental budget allocations.

Requirements

  • An undergraduate qualification at NQF level 7 as recognised by SAQA in Public Management/Public Administration/Business Management/Social Science.
  • At least 5 years’ experience in the Strategic Planning and Service Delivery environment, of which 3 years must be at ASD level.
  • A valid driver’s license.
