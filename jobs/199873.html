About the role

🚀 We're Hiring: Procurement Specialist: Stock, Services and Infrastructure 📍 Location: Gauteng 📊 Reporting Line: Manager: Procurement and Supplier Relationship Management 💼 Job Purpose Lesaka Merchant is looking for a Procurement Specialist: Stock, Services and Infrastructure to intelligently procure fintech stock, services, and infrastructure through the application of specialised procurement, supplier management, and commercial expertise. This role is responsible for ensuring that stock, services, and infrastructure are procured in a manner that mitigates risk, supports operational continuity, enables technology and infrastructure requirements, and ensures compliance with all applicable specifications, standards, and lifecycle management requirements. The successful candidate will balance cost effectiveness, supplier performance, regulatory compliance, operational resilience, and innovation to support Lesaka's growth and customer experience
