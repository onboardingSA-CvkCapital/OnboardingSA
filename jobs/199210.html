About the role

Location: Cape Town – Southern Suburbs, Kenilworth Employment Type: Full-time, based at the Kenilworth offices (Subject to applicable Company policies and operational requirements.) Reporting: Sales Operations Manager – Dax Data Employment Arrangement: The successful candidate will be employed directly by Dax Data and will form part of the Dax Data team.
