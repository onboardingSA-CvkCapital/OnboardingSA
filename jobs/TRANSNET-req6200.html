About the role

Transnet (Transnet Corporate Centre) is hiring a Snr Manager Operating Model & Transformation, based in Johannesburg. The role reports to the GM: People Managemnt & Org Effectiveness. Grade: C. The role translates corporate strategy into an integrated operating model covering structure, governance, accountabilities, capabilities, processes, people, technology and performance management. It also serves as a strategic partner to executive leadership, ensuring that transformation initiatives move beyond structural redesign to deliver sustainable improvements in organisational effectiveness, operational performance, customer outcomes and value creation. The role will lead and integrate transformation initiatives across target operating models, organisational structures, governance, business processes, roles and accountabilities, workforce capabilities and ways of working, ensuring that the organisation transitions effectively from its current state to the defined future state.

Responsibilities

  • ENTERPRISE OPERATING MODEL STRATEGY
  • Lead the development and implementation of the enterprise Target Operating Model aligned with organisational strategy and long-term business objectives.
  • Translate strategy into clear operating model design principles, organisational capabilities and enterprise design requirements.
  • Ensure alignment between organisational structure, business processes, governance, decision rights, technology, workforce and performance measures.
  • Assess the current operating model and identify structural, capability, process and governance constraints that inhibit organisational performance.
  • Establish an enterprise roadmap for transitioning from the current state to the agreed future-state operating model.
  • ORGANISATION DESIGN
  • Provide strategic leadership for enterprise-wide organisation design and restructuring initiatives.
  • Define organisation design principles, methodologies and governance standards.
  • Lead the design of organisational structures, layers, spans of control, accountabilities and decision rights.
  • Ensure organisation design solutions support operational efficiency, appropriate managerial accountability and effective deployment of organisational capability.
  • Oversee role design, role clarification and organisational interfaces across functions and business areas.
  • ENTERPRISE TRANSFORMATION
  • Lead complex, multi-year transformation programmes involving significant changes to operating models, organisational structures, processes and ways of working.
  • Translate transformation strategy into an integrated portfolio of programmes, initiatives and implementation priorities.
  • Establish transformation governance, programme dependencies, milestones, decision points and performance measures.
  • Identify systemic barriers to transformation and work with executive stakeholders to resolve them.
  • Ensure benefits identified in transformation business cases are tracked and realised.
  • EXECUTIVE ADVISORY AND STAKEHOLDER LEADERSHIP
  • Act as a trusted strategic adviser to the Executive Committee and senior leadership on operating model and transformation matters.
  • Facilitate executive-level discussions and decisions on organisational design, accountabilities, capabilities and transformation priorities.
  • Build alignment across business units and functions where transformation requires changes to mandates, resources or decision rights.
  • Present complex organisational issues, options and recommendations in a clear, commercially grounded manner.
  • Constructively challenge existing organisational practices where they no longer support strategic objectives.
  • TRANSFORMATION GOVERNANCE
  • Establish governance arrangements that provide effective executive oversight of the transformation portfolio.
  • Define clear accountability for transformation outcomes across executives, business leaders and programme owners.
  • Establish mechanisms for managing transformation risks, interdependencies and escalations.
  • Provide transparent reporting on implementation progress, benefits, risks and organisational impacts.
  • Ensure key operating model decisions are appropriately documented and translated into implementation requirements.
  • WORKFORCE AND CAPABILITY TRANSFORMATION
  • Determine the organisational capabilities required to support the future operating model.
  • Partner with Human Capital leadership on workforce planning, capability building, talent implications and organisational transition.
  • Assess the workforce implications of operating model decisions, including role changes, capability requirements and resource deployment.
  • Ensure organisational capability interventions are aligned with future-state requirements.
  • CHANGE AND ADOPTION
  • Provide strategic direction for organisational change and adoption across the transformation portfolio.
  • Ensure operating model changes are translated into clearly understood changes in behaviours, responsibilities, processes and ways of working.
  • Work with leaders to build ownership of transformation outcomes.
  • Monitor organisational readiness, adoption risks and barriers to sustainable.
  • PERFORMANCE AND BENEFITS REALISATION
  • Establish measurable outcomes and success indicators for operating model and transformation initiatives.
  • Develop mechanisms to monitor whether implemented changes are delivering intended organisational and operational benefits.
  • Identify opportunities for continuous operating model optimisation.
  • Provide executive leadership with evidence-based recommendations where transformation initiatives require corrective intervention.

Requirements

  • A postgraduate qualification at NQF Level 8 in Business Management, Organisational Development, Human Resources, Industrial/Organisational Psychology, Strategy, Engineering, Commerce or a related field.
  • Master's degree or MBA will be advantageous
  • 12 years' relevant experience, with substantial experience in transformation, strategy, operating model or organisation design.
  • At least 5 years at senior leadership/executive level leading large-scale organisational transformation.
  • Proven experience designing and implementing Target Operating Models in large and complex organisations.
  • Demonstrated experience engaging executives and senior governance structures.
  • Experience translating strategy into organisational structures, capabilities, governance arrangements and implementation programmes.
  • Requirement of trust and honesty in the handling of Finances as per the National Credit Act Amendment 19.
