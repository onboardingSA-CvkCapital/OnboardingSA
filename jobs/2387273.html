About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AI-powered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined, future-fit role. We are looking for enthusiastic, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace data-driven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job The Regional Committee Assistant provides administrative, governance, and coordination support to the Regional Lead and regional committees, ensuring the effective execution of regional governance processes and stakeholder engagement initiatives. This role works closely with the Company Secretariat in the (GRC) department to uphold governance and compliance standards, while also collaborating with the Experiential Marketing team in the Customer Experience function to enhance regional stakeholder engagement activities. The incumbent will leverage technology, AI, and digital tools to optimise administrative efficiency, improve stakeholder interactions, and support data-driven decision-making. Key/main outputs Output 1 Committee Administration and Governance Support • Provide secretarial and administrative support to regional committees, subcommittees, and working groups • Coordinate meeting logistics, including scheduling, agenda preparation, document collation, and minute-taking • Ensure compliance with governance requirements, record-keeping, and committee reporting obligations • Support the Regional Lead in preparing committee reports and presentations Output 2: Stakeholder and Member Engagement Support • Collaborate with the Experiential Marketing team to plan and execute regional stakeholder events,workshops, and networking sessions • Manage invitations, RSVPs, and logistical arrangements for regional events • Assist in tracking stakeholder feedback and engagement insights using AI and CRM tools • Facilitate communication between regional committees and SAICA’s broader stakeholder network Output 3 Process Optimisation and Technology Integration • Leverage AI and workflow automation tools to improve committee administration and reporting efficiency • Maintain and update digital governance platforms, member databases, and committee portals • Identify opportunities to streamline administrative processes using technology and best practices Output 4: Compliance and Risk Management • Ensure alignment with SAICA’s governance frameworks and regulatory requirements. • Support the Company Secretariat in implementing risk mitigation strategies for regional governance activities. • Assist in maintaining audit-ready records and governance documentation Output 5 General Administrative and Team Support • Provide general administrative support to the Regional Lead and the regional team. • Assist with special projects, ad hoc research, and report preparation as needed. • Coordinate travel, expense claims, and procurement for regional committee-related activities. Output 6: Embrace and drive organisational transformation • Actively contribute to cost containment efforts by identifying opportunities for operational efficiencies,optimising resources, and ensuring value-driven decision-making in daily activities. • Support SAICA’s transformation into an insights-driven organisation by leveraging data and analyticsto enhance decision-making, improve processes, and drive continuous improvement in your area ofwork • Effectively utilise digital tools and technology to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and improve service delivery. Stay informed about new and emerging technologies relevant to your role • Adopt and embrace AI-driven tools and automation to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and effectiveness in job-related tasks, while continuously seeking opportunities to improve processes through innovative technology solutions • Identify and support SAICA’s management of risks related to sustainability, including environmental risks, regulatory changes, and social risks, and integrating them into business continuity and riskmanagement plans
