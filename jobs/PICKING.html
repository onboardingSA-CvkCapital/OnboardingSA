About the role

Ensure that all orders are correctly picked and ready for dispatch on a daily basis and to ensure all staff complete all allocated tasks on a daily basis.  This function includes ensuring that all procedures are followed during picking and checking, and to ensure that picking and checking is done correctly in accordance with standards.
