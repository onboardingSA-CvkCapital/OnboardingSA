About the role

The National Treasury is offering an internship in Assistant Director: Southern African Development Community in Economic Policy and International Cooperation, based in Pretoria. The stipend is R605,742 a year. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.treasury.gov.za/eRecruitment Reference number to quote: S128/2026 Closing date: 07 December 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Policy Analysis
  • Assist with the formulation of National Treasury’s position on regional economic and financial integration agenda in SADC
  • Provide inputs into cabinet memoranda and coordinate the Inter-Ministerial Committee process
  • Monitor and develop an in-depth profile of identified strategic SADC countries
  • Assist with the development of policy positions within SADC member countries on finance and development
  • Regional Integration
  • Assess the extent of existing institutional arrangements and assistance to attain regional integration
  • Provide input to policy on co-operation with regional and global multilateral institutions on the development of SADC
  • Coordinate the payment of all funds due to AFRITAC South
  • Stakeholder Engagement
  • Update views on SADC emanating from business and other non-state organisations and organise knowledge-sharing platforms
  • Co-ordinate the National Treasury’s task team on SADC that involves Ministries of Finance
  • Coordinate capacity building within SADC countries
  • Coordinate dialogue between the South African Ministry of Finance and SADC Ministries of Finance
  • SADC Cooperation
  • Assist in negotiations within SADC and bilaterally with SADC countries
  • Coordinate and update in-house publications on SADC projects

Requirements

  • A Grade 12 is required coupled with a bachelor’s degree (equivalent to NQF level 7) in Economics and Econometrics, and Political Science or Development Studies.
  • A minimum 3 years’ experience within a development institution or policy or financial environment.
  • Knowledge of policy analysis and formulation of drafting notes and cabinet memoranda.
  • Proven knowledge and experience of benchmarking and research with recognised international institutions.
  • Knowledge and experience in economic policy, and research diplomatic level will serve as an added advantage.
