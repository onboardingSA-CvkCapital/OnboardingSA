About the role

LiquidCapital is a world leader in its field of specialisation offering a variety of financial products for motor vehicles. LiquidCapital aims to creatively solve problems and add value to people by ensuring Mobility for Good. LiquidCapital forms part of the Motus Group, a leading JSE listed Company in the Automotive Industry where performance is driven by three keystones, 1. Leaders, 2. Innovation & 3. Customer Champions. LiquidCapital is seeking to employ a qualified and experienced Junior Accountant. The successful candidate will join a dynamic and highly skilled Finance Team. The successful candidate will report directly to the Finance Manager. JOB DESCRIPTION: The successful candidate will be responsible for supporting the finance function through the accurate processing of financial transactions, preparation of reconciliations, month-end reporting activities, invoicing, and regulatory compliance. The role assists in maintaining the integrity of financial records, ensuring deadlines are met, and providing financial support across multiple business entities SPECIFIC ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES: •
