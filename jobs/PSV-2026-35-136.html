About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring an Architect (Landscape) Grade A (Reis), based in Head Office (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R791,604 a year. How to apply: for the position of Architect (Landscape) must be emailed to: Recruitment26- 78@dpw.gov.za Reference number to quote: 2026/353 Closing date: 16 October 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide landscape architectural planning and design services:
  • Assess landscape architectural requirements for new and existing state-owned properties and infrastructure projects
  • Conduct site investigations and analyse existing site conditions, including landscape, plants, land use and environmental characteristics
  • Prepare site development plans, pre-design high-level development proposals, landscape concepts, master plans, layouts and detailed landscape designs.
  • Develop appropriate hard and soft landscape solutions.
  • Prepare planting designs and recommend suitable plant species, materials and landscape elements.
  • Provide landscape architectural inputs into feasibility studies, project briefs and infrastructure planning processes.
  • Integrate landscape requirements with architectural, engineering and other built-environment designs.
  • Incorporate universal accessibility, sustainability, water efficiency and climate-responsive principles into landscape designs.
  • Ensure landscape projects comply with applicable legislation, policies, environmental requirements, norms and standards.
  • Promote environmentally sustainable landscape planning and design.
  • Develop and manage landscape architectural documentation:
  • Prepare and/or review landscape drawings, specifications, schedules, bills of quantities/input to bills of quantities and related technical documentation
  • Develop detailed documentation for hard landscaping, soft landscaping, planting, irrigation and external landscape elements
  • Prepare technical specifications for landscape works and materials
  • Coordinate landscape documentation with architectural, civil, structural and other professional disciplines
  • Review landscape documentation prepared by consultants for technical accuracy, completeness and compliance
  • Provide landscape architectural inputs into tender and procurement documentation
  • Facilitate relevant approvals and maintain appropriate technical and project records
  • Ensure approved design amendments are incorporated into project documentation
  • Keep abreast of developments, technologies and best practices within the landscape architectural profession
  • Develop landscape architectural related processes, methods and practices in line with applicable prescripts
  • Provide landscape architectural services during project implementation: Monitor landscape construction and implementation to ensure compliance with approved designs, specifications and standards.
  • Conduct site inspections and participate in project, technical and site meetings.
  • Monitor the quality of hard and soft landscaping works, planting, irrigation systems and related installations.
  • Identify deviations from approved landscape designs and recommend corrective measures.
  • Provide professional advice and technical clarification on landscape-related matters during construction.
  • Review proposed substitutions, design changes and variations from a landscape architectural perspective.
  • Participate in practical completion, works completion and final completion inspections.
  • Identify landscape defects and monitor their rectification.
  • Monitor establishment and maintenance requirements for newly completed landscapes where applicable.
  • Provide inputs into project close-out documentation and as-built information.
  • Provide professional landscape architectural advice and stakeholder support: Provide professional and technical advice on landscape architectural matters to management, client departments and project teams.
  • Advise stakeholders on landscape planning, design, rehabilitation and maintenance requirements.
  • Participate in multidisciplinary planning, design review and project meetings.
  • Liaise with relevant authorities on environmental, landscape and related statutory requirements.
  • Provide technical inputs into departmental policies, norms, standards and guidelines relating to landscaping and external environments.
  • Prepare technical reports, recommendations, presentations and professional opinions on landscape architectural matters.
  • Participate in the assessment of existing landscaped environments and recommend improvement or rehabilitation interventions.

Requirements

  • At least bachelor’s degree in landscape architecture or equivalent landscape architectural qualification.
  • A minimum 3 years relevant experience in Landscape Architectural Services.
  • Registration with SACLAP as a Professional Architect (Landscape).
  • Drivers’ license.
  • Prepared to travel.
  • Willing to adapt to working schedule in accordance with office requirements.
  • Provide advice and guidance on landscape architectural related matters.
  • Manage the drafting of plans and designs to suit every client department.
  • Engage and interact regarding the effective management of the budget.
  • Liaise and interact with regards to payment of service providers.
  • Liaise regarding procurement of goods and services.
  • Provide advice and guidance on landscape architectural and contractual matters.
  • Provide inputs and advice regarding professional matters and the state.
  • Liaise regarding registration of plans.
