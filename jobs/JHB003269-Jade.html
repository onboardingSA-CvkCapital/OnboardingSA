About the role

The HR Administrator is responsible for providing comprehensive administrative support to the Human Resources department within a hospitality environment. The role ensures the efficient administration of HR processes across the employee lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, employee records, payroll support, training coordination, and compliance with South African labour legislation. The HR Administrator plays a vital role in fostering a positive employee experience while supporting operational departments in achieving exceptional guest service standards.Key ResponsibilitiesMaintain accurate employee records, personnel files, and HR databases.Assist with the recruitment process by advertising vacancies, screening CVs, scheduling interviews, conducting reference checks, and preparing employment documentation.Coordinate the onboarding and induction of new employees.Prepare employment contracts, offer letters, and all HR-related correspondence.Administer leave, attendance, timekeeping, and staff rosters where applicable.Capture and update employee information on HR and payroll systems.Support payroll by ensuring all employee information, overtime, leave, and deductions are submitted accurately and on time.Coordinate employee training, skills development, and statutory compliance training.Maintain training records and assist with Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) and Annual Training Report (ATR) submissions.Assist with Employment Equity (EE) reporting and B-BBEE administration where required.Ensure compliance with the Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA), Labour Relations Act (LRA), Employment Equity Act (EEA), and company policies.Prepare documentation for disciplinary hearings, grievances, performance management, and incapacity processes.Monitor probation periods and performance review schedules.Assist with employee wellness initiatives and staff engagement activities.Respond professionally and confidentially to employee HR-related queries.Produce HR reports...
