About the role

Frogfoot is seeking a Core Engineer to join the Engineering team. Purpose of the Role As a Core Engineer at Frogfoot, you'll play a key role in designing, operating, maintaining, and optimising our national carrier-grade core network. You'll ensure the stability, performance, and availability of our IP and optical transmission infrastructure while supporting the delivery of reliable, high-quality connectivity to our customers. Working closely with internal teams, vendors, and service providers, you'll contribute to network growth, continuous improvement initiatives, and technical projects that enable Frogfoot to deliver world-class fibre connectivity and carrier solutions. What You’ll Be Responsible For Network Operations & Engineering • Design, implement and maintain carrier-grade IP and optical transmission networks. • Configure, commission and support core network infrastructure. • Perform network capacity planning and optimisation to ensure network scalability. • Maintain network availability and proactively identify potential risks.Service Delivery • Provision, commission and maintain services on the transmission, IP Core and IPT networks. • Support customer service activation and complex technical implementations. • Evaluate technical solution requirements for bespoke customer requirements.Fault Management • Investigate, troubleshoot and resolve complex network faults, in the event of escalations to the Core team. • Assist with providing technical inputs for root cause analysis following major incidents. • Escalate and coordinate technical issues with vendors where required. • Participate in major incident response and restoration activities.Network Optimisation • Continuously improve network performance and resilience. • Recommend infrastructure upgrades and design improvements. • Maintain best practices for managing vulnerability and risk associated with network device firmware, system versions, and remote access to network devices through approved access authorization solutions. • Implement network enhancements through approved change management processes.Technical Documentation • Produce and maintain accurate network documentation. • Update network diagrams, topology maps and operational procedures. • Document technical solutions and standard operating procedures.Project Implementation • Lead network expansion, standardization and optimization projects. • Participate in technology evaluations and proof of concepts. • Support vendor integration and equipment commissioning.Collaboration • Work closely with NOC, Field Operations, Service Delivery and Product teams. • Provide technical expertise
