About the role

Job Title: Customer Security Services Engineer Location: Johannesburg (Waterfall City) – Hybrid Salary: Competitive Job Type: Full-time About Us Are you ready to be part of something exciting? Vox Telecom is a trailblazing ISP in the Telecommunication industry, dedicated to delivering cutting-edge Fibre, telephony and IT solutions that make a difference. We thrive on innovation, customer success, and a dynamic work culture where every team member is empowered to excel. The Role – Your Next Big Opportunity! Join the Customer Security Services team and take ownership of the security posture of some of South Africa's most recognisable enterprise brands. You'll design, deploy and manage the full Sophos managed security stack, XGS firewalls and SD-WAN, Endpoint and XDR, MDR service delivery, Email Security and Device Encryption, across the entire customer lifecycle, from pre-sales design through to ongoing managed support. This is hands-on engineering with real customer impact, in a practice that's growing fast. What You'll Be Doing: • Designing and deploying Sophos XGS firewall and SD-WAN solutions, including VPN, IPS, web filtering, application control and TLS inspection • Configuring SD-WAN profiles, SLA policies and traffic steering across DIA, MPLS and LTE transports • Deploying and managing Sophos Endpoint protection, threat protection policies and device control across customer estates • Performing XDR alert triage, threat hunting and investigation, and supporting MDR service delivery • Deploying and managing Sophos Email Security, including Mailflow, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and DLP • Managing Central Device Encryption policies, recovery key escrow and compliance reporting • Supporting customer migrations from incumbent security platforms, including phased cutover planning • Responding to customer security incidents, identification, containment and mitigation • Producing security posture and compliance reporting aligned to POPIA, the Cybercrimes Act and ISO 27001 • Maintaining the RightFax hosted service platform • Producing build documentation, network diagrams and customer handover packs • Providing remote enablement and training to customer IT teams Who You Are: • Minimum 2 years' hands-on experience in network or information security, including firewall administration • Strong grounding in network protocols, routing and switching • Experience configuring enterprise firewalls (Sophos, Fortinet, Palo Alto, Cisco or equivalent) • Working knowledge of EDR/XDR alert triage, email security and Microsoft 365/Entra ID • Methodical troubleshooter with the discipline to verify findings before reporting them • Able to explain technical concepts clearly to technical and non-technical audiences • Comfortable managing concurrent deployments and shifting priorities • Available for after-hours change windows and standby rotation • Valid driver's license and own transport Certifications The following are required within 6 months of appointment, fully funded by Vox: CompTIA Network+, CompTIA Security+, AZ-900, and Sophos Certified Engineer for Firewall, Endpoint and Email. Sophos Architect certification forms the expected development path. SC-200, CySA+ or ISO 27001 qualifications are advantageous. Why Join Us? • Competitive salary • Fully funded certification path with Sophos Certifications • Career growth with clear progression • Hybrid working environment • Enterprise-scale exposure across a national customer base • Comprehensive benefits, paid leave, bonuses and study opportunities Ready to Elevate Your Career?
