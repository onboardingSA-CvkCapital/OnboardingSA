About the role

FirstRand is hiring a Legal Head, based in Randburg. Hello Future Legal Head The Head of Legal is responsible for leading and directing the legal function supporting HSL. The role serves as a strategic business partner to Executive Management and senior leadership, providing commercially focused legal advice, legal risk oversight, governance leadership, and regulatory guidance to enable the achievement of business objectives. The incumbent is accountable for developing and implementing the legal strategy aligned to business priorities, ensuring effective management of legal risk, sound legal governance, and compliance with applicable legislative and regulatory requirements. The role provides legal leadership across Home Loan products being Pension Backed Loans, Home Loans, Structured Loans, Single Facility and Securities Based Lending. The Head of Legal acts as a trusted advisor to Executive Committees, governance forums and senior stakeholders, influencing strategic decision-making, supporting transformation initiatives, enabling sustainable business growth and promoting positive customer outcomes. The role is responsible for leading a high-performing legal team, fostering a culture of accountability and continuous improvement, and maintaining constructive relationships with regulators, industry bodies and external legal service providers.
