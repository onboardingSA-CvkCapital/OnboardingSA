About the role

To prepare accurate journal entries and trial balances and participate in preparing financial statements in order to ensure accurate reporting as per reporting standards

Responsibilities

  • Budget process
  • Assisting budget owners to compile a budget aligned to the budget guidelines and targets
  • Completing forecasting timeously and accurately at each cycle.
  • Completing the recovery model inputs during budgeting cycles
  • Upload and balance budgets in SAP
  • Assist with and submit PVPY
  • Ensure integrity and completeness of budget information used for management reporting and decision-making.
  • Assist with Group budget presentations
  • SAP, Financial Data & Reporting
  • Maintain appropriate cost/profit centre structures and financial reporting structures relevant to the business.
  • Prepare SAP journals including accruals, provisions, account hygiene and ensure they are valid accurate and complete.
  • Monthly reporting to budget owners providing actuals vs forecast/budget and variance explanations, reports to be sent to budget owners.
  • Support your stakeholders on ad hoc management accounting requests
  • Variance analysis
  • Support reconciliation between financial systems and management reporting.
  • Identify opportunities to improve financial reporting processes and systems.
  • Supporting the YE and HYE process by raising the necessary accruals and provisions.
  • Prepare half-year end and year end recons including the required disclosures.
  • Attend to audit queries in area of responsibility.
  • Financial Controls, SOX & Audit
  • Ensure effective execution and oversight of relevant financial controls.
  • Maintain appropriate supporting documentation and evidence.
  • Support internal and external audit requirements.
  • Monitor compliance with SOX and internal control requirements.
  • General

Requirements

  • 3-4 year Relevant University Degree
  • 3+ relevant years
