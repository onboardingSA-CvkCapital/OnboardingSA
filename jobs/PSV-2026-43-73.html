Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for a wide variety of tasks which include but are not limited to the following: Coordinate and Deliver Stakeholder outreach, education and training: Plan, coordinate and deliver approved client outreach programmes, roadshows and retirement education campaigns.
  • Conduct workshops and training sessions for employer departments on pension administration processes and documentation requirements.
  • Present approved pension and benefit information to members beneficiaries and employers.
  • Evaluate outreach and education initiatives and recommend service delivery improvements.
  • Promote new and existing GPAA services to current and prospective users through outreach and information sessions.
  • Establish and maintain collaborative stakeholders’ networks.
  • Collect, pre-verify and process member documentation.
  • Collect pension related documentation from employers, members and beneficiaries.
  • Verify documentation for completeness, accuracy, compliance and quality standards.
  • Capture verified documents and case information accurately.
  • Barcode, link, index and store documents on approved systems.
  • Provide feedback on incomplete or noncompliant documentation.
  • Escalate material discrepancies and document processing risks.
  • Manage, Investigate and resolve Client enquires
  • Receive, register, track and manage client enquiries through approved CRM systems
  • Investigate and resolve pension related enquiries, claims, medical and Injuryon-duty matters
  • Liaise with employer departments and stakeholder to obtain outstanding information
  • Maintain accurate member and case information on approved systems
  • Communicate case progress and outcomes to clients and stakeholders
  • Escalate unresolved matters with complete supporting assessments against GPAA rules, products and submission standards
  • Identify, record and report members data, contribution and process compliance exceptions
  • Monitor implementation of corrective actions and closure of findings
  • Maintain accurate operational records and performance statistics
  • Compile and submit complete weekly and monthly operational reports

Requirements

  • A relevant 3-year degree/national diploma in Public Administration, Public Management, Communications, Business Management or equivalent (at least 360 credits) 4-5 years’ relevant experience in the Client Relations Management environment or Employee Befits or Pension Environment.

  • Driving skills.
