About the role

Our client is seeking a Property Manager’s Assistant to join their team based in Kuils River, Cape Town. The ideal candidate will have a Matric (Grade 12) qualification and previous experience in property administration or a similar support role. Strong organisational and administrative skills are essential, together with the ability to assist with the day-to-day management of Sectional Title schemes and Homeowners’ Associations. The successful candidate will support the Property Manager with client and stakeholder communication, meeting coordination, maintenance requests, documentation, and general property administration. Proficiency in MS Office is required, while experience with property management systems will be advantageous.
