About the role

Summary of The Role The Senior Accountant will take full ownership of the debtors, creditors, banking, treasury, foreign exchange, intercompany loan transactions, intercompany balances, confirmations, cash flow and reconciliation functions. The role is responsible for ensuring that customer collections, supplier payments, bank reconciliations, cash forecasting, foreign exchange administration, intercompany confirmations, clearing accounts and control accounts are managed accurately, timeously and within a strong control environment. This is a hands-on leadership role requiring a structured, assertive and accountable finance professional who can manage people, deadlines, risk, documentation and stakeholder relationships in a high-volume environment. The incumbent must be proactive, output-driven and disciplined, with strong respect for controls, governance, audit readiness and continuous improvement. Job Purpose The Senior Accountant is accountable for the end-to-end management of the debtors, creditors, treasury, foreign exchange, banking, cash, clearing, intercompany loan transactions, intercompany balances, confirmations and reconciliation functions within the business. The role is responsible for safeguarding cash flow, managing working capital, maintaining strong financial controls, managing financial and operational risk, ensuring governance compliance, and delivering accurate and timely financial information to support decision-making. The incumbent will act as a gatekeeper of controls, risk and governance within the finance function, ensuring that processes are efficient, compliant, well documented, audit-ready and continuously improved. The role requires a structured, assertive, disciplined and output-driven leader who takes accountability for the full function and operates effectively under pressure. Key Responsibilities Debtors Management • Take full ownership of the debtors function, including the management, collection and control of outstanding debtor balances. • Drive collection strategies and ensure overdue accounts are proactively followed up and escalated where required. • Review debtor ageing reports, identify risk areas and implement action plans to reduce overdue balances and bad debt exposure. Calculation of Expected Credit Losses. • Ensure debtor accounts are accurately maintained, reconciled regularly and supported by appropriate documentation. • Monitor debtor trends and emerging risks, and provide meaningful commentary to management. • Build and maintain effective relationships with customers, operational teams and internal stakeholders. Creditors Management • Manage the full creditors function, ensuring suppliers are paid accurately, timeously and in accordance with approved processes. • Oversee creditor onboarding, including documentation control, compliance checks and adherence to governance requirements. • Review creditor reconciliations, ageing analysis and payment proposals to ensure accuracy, completeness and control. • Manage supplier queries and relationships professionally and efficiently. • Ensure all supporting documentation is complete, accurate, approved and retained in line with company requirements. • Manage working capital carefully through disciplined payment planning, purchase order management and supplier balance control. Treasury, Banking, Cash Flow and Foreign Exchange • Manage daily treasury, banking and cash management activities. • Prepare and maintain cash flow forecasts, including short-term and medium-term funding requirements. • Monitor liquidity requirements and optimise cash utilisation across the business. • Manage bank administration, banking documentation, mandates, facilities and related banking processes. • Oversee bank reconciliations and ensure all reconciling items are investigated and cleared timeously. • Manage foreign exchange activities, including foreign currency payments, exposure monitoring, forward exchange contracts where applicable, and liaison with banking partners. • Monitor foreign exchange gains and losses and provide analysis and commentary on the impact of exchange rate movements, forward points and related costs. • Ensure compliance with exchange control requirements, treasury policies and internal approval processes. Clearing Accounts, Cash Accounts and Balance Sheet Reconciliations • Oversee reconciliations for debtors, creditors, bank accounts, cash accounts, clearing accounts, suspense accounts and related general ledger control accounts. • Ensure reconciliations are prepared, reviewed and resolved within required deadlines. • Drive the timely investigation and clearance of reconciling items, exceptions and unmatched transactions. • Maintain the integrity of balance sheet accounts and ensure financial information is accurate, complete and supportable. Intercompany Loans, Balances and Confirmations • Manage intercompany loan transactions, balances and reconciliations, ensuring they are accurately recorded, supported and reviewed. • Coordinate intercompany confirmations for loans, debtors and creditors, ensuring balances are agreed, differences are investigated and supporting documentation is retained. • Monitor intercompany debtor and creditor balances and ensure ageing, recoverability, settlement and classification risks are appropriately managed. • Ensure confirmations, reconciliations and supporting schedules are available for internal and external audit requirements. • Liaise with related parties, group finance and internal stakeholders to resolve intercompany differences and improve alignment of balances. Financial Controls, Risk and Governance • Act as the gatekeeper of financial controls, governance and compliance across the relevant finance functions. • Design, implement, maintain and enhance internal controls to support accuracy, accountability and risk mitigation. • Ensure adherence to company policies, approval frameworks, documentation standards and financial procedures. • Identify control weaknesses, operational risks and process gaps, and implement corrective actions. • Promote a culture of discipline, control awareness, accountability and ethical conduct within the team. • Ensure work is performed consistently, proactively and within required timelines. Financial Reporting, Expense Analysis and Cost Control • Assist with monthly financial reporting and management reporting requirements. • Prepare expense analyses, variance explanations and commentary for management review. • Support the preparation and review of management accounts, balance sheet substantiation and financial reporting packs. • Monitor costs carefully and drive cost awareness across relevant departments. • Manage budgets, variance controls and departmental cost reporting. • Prepare departmental budgets and assist with forecasting and planning processes. Audit Management and Compliance • Manage internal and external audit requirements relating to debtors, creditors, bank, cash, treasury, VAT, foreign exchange, intercompany loans, debtor and creditor confirmations, reconciliations and control accounts. • Coordinate and provide accurate audit information, schedules and supporting documentation within required deadlines. • Ensure audit findings are addressed timeously through practical and sustainable corrective actions. • Maintain audit-ready documentation and support a strong control environment. • Assist with VAT administration, banking compliance and related regulatory information requests. Stakeholder and Relationship Management • Manage relationships with banks, suppliers, customers, auditors, senior managers, group finance and internal business stakeholders. • Collaborate effectively with peers and operational teams to resolve issues and improve processes. • Manage upwards by providing clear, timely and accurate updates, risks, escalations and recommendations. • Delegate appropriately while maintaining oversight, ownership and accountability for outcomes.
