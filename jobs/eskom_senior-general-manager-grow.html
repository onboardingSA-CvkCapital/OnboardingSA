About the role

Position/Task Grade: FAA Area of specialization: Reporting to the Group Executive Distribution the Senior General Manager will be responsible for providing integrated strategic leadership across the Eskom Distribution (EDx) Grow portfolio by directing, aligning and optimising the EDx Edge and EDx Serve lines of business so that customer and revenue retention, market and revenue share growth, partnerships, innovation and profitability are advanced in conjunction with equitable customer service, electrification, affordability and developmental obligations. This will protect and grow Eskom Distribution’s revenue base, safeguard service continuity and deliver a coherent, financially sustainable and customer-centric value proposition within an evolving and increasingly competitive electricity market. Department: Distribution Grow. Location: Sunninghill -Megawatt Park.
