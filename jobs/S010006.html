In some areas, the fuel station also has a convenience store, take-away or mini market store offering customers a one-stop solution . We offer professional and high-quality service and customer service standards RESPONSIBILITIES: Shift and Store Operations • Open or close the store in accordance with approved procedures and security requirements. • Conduct shift handovers and communicate shortages, incidents, maintenance issues and outstanding tasks. • Allocate daily duties, confirm employee coverage and monitor execution throughout the shift. • Ensure shelves, service areas, tills, storerooms and entrances are clean, organised and ready for trade. • Monitor refrigeration, lighting, point-of-sale equipment and other store facilities; report faults promptly. • Ensure company promotions, pricing and displays are implemented accurately. Customer Service and Sales • Set and maintain friendly, efficient and professional customer service standards. • Assist customers and resolve queries, complaints, refunds and returns within delegated authority. • Support employees during busy periods to maintain prompt service and reduce queues. • Ensure employees understand current products, promotions and service expectations. • Identify opportunities to improve sales, availability and the customer experience. Employee Supervision • Brief employees at the start of the shift and assign responsibilities according to operational priorities. • Monitor attendance, punctuality, uniform, conduct, productivity and adherence to procedures. • Provide on-the-job coaching and assist with the induction and training of new employees. • Address routine performance or conduct concerns promptly and escalate formal matters to management. • Promote teamwork, fair treatment, clear communication and a customer-focused work environment. • Confirm that employees take authorised breaks without compromising store coverage. Cash Handling and Administration • Issue and verify till floats and ensure cashiers follow approved cash and card procedures. • Monitor till activity, voids, refunds, discounts, paid-outs and safe drops. • Complete or oversee shift cash-ups and investigate or report discrepancies immediately. • Ensure cash, keys, documents and sensitive information are secured at all times. • Complete shift reports, incident records, checklists and other required administration accurately. Stock, Merchandising and Loss Prevention • Monitor stock availability and ensure shelves are replenished, faced up and correctly priced. • Receive deliveries by checking quantities, quality, documentation and product condition. • Apply stock rotation principles and monitor expiry dates, damaged goods and temperature-sensitive products. • Assist with cycle counts, stocktakes, variance investigations and stock adjustments authorised by management. • Enforce access, key-control and security procedures to minimise theft, wastage and shrinkage. • Immediately report suspicious activity, significant variances or breaches of procedure. Health, Safety, Hygiene and Compliance • Enforce occupational health and safety, hygiene, food-handling and housekeeping requirements. • Conduct routine checks and ensure aisles, exits, storage areas and workstations remain safe and unobstructed. • Respond to emergencies, accidents, spillages, security incidents and equipment failures in line with procedure. • Ensure incidents and injuries are reported, recorded and escalated without delay. • Maintain compliance with company policies, legal requirements and applicable OK Minimarket operating standards. Planning and Working Conditions • Plan shift activities according to trading patterns, staffing levels, deliveries and operational priorities. • Work rotating shifts, weekends and public holidays as required by the store roster. • Remain on feet for extended periods and perform routine handling of stock within safe working practices. • Meet daily deadlines relating to store readiness, cash reconciliation, stock control and shift reporting. Requirements: REQUIREMENTS: Qualification • Grade 12 (Matric) or equivalent qualification. Experience • At least two years’ retail, supermarket or convenience-store experience, including cashiering and stock-control exposure. • Previous experience leading or supervising employees is preferred. • Working knowledge of point-of-sale systems, cash-up procedures, merchandising, stock rotation and basic retail administration. • Ability and willingness to work rotating shifts, weekends and public holidays. Key Competencies • Leadership and team coordination • Customer-service orientation • Integrity, reliability and accountability • Attention to detail and numerical accuracy • Planning, organising and prioritising • Clear communication and conflict resolution • Problem-solving and sound judgement • Ability to remain composed and productive under pressure • Basic computer literacy and sound numerical ability.
