About the role

The National Treasury is offering an internship in Director: Internal Operations Executive Support (Ate) in Office of The Director-General, based in Pretoria. The stipend is R1,317. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.treasury.gov.za/eRecruitment Reference number to quote: S109/2026 Closing date: 19 October 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Strategic Support: Coordinate the effectiveness of meetings pertaining to the EXCO and assist with logistical arrangements.
  • Provide advice pertaining to concerns addressed in submissions relating to Programme 1 departmental matters and fast-tracking explanatory submissions for approval.
  • Monitor progress and confirm completion by divisions of required activities as directed by the DG in respect of Programme 1 matters.
  • Coordinate and assist on outstanding Programme 1 related strategic deliverables, and progress on divisional strategic objectives for implementation.
  • Manage the stakeholders’ engagement pertaining to concerns and queries for resolution and provide feedback on matters relating to Programme 1.
  • Coordinate the management information system in respect of Programme 1 matters in compliance with the governance of financial management needs of the office.
  • Assist with the coordination of projects for execution.
  • Administrative and Office Support:
  • Draft memoranda, submissions and correspondence for approval on matters relating to Programme 1
  • Assist with the scoping, co-ordination and administration of projects, from start to finish as required
  • Perform secretariat responsibilities for forums for senior management meetings and follow-up on outstanding plans
  • Coordinate the preparation of documents and perform proofreading and quality control on documents for correctness
  • Exercise oversight on the document management and electronic filing system for effectiveness in respect of Programme 1 matters
  • Coordinate the logistics around corporate functions, conferences, management meetings etc
  • Stakeholder Engagement:
  • Engage stakeholders on all correspondence relating to Programme 1 matters and disposed thereof appropriately and timeously
  • Liaise with all spheres of government, public entities and private sector organisations in relation to Programme 1 matters
  • Financial Management:
  • Comply with relevant legislation frameworks including the PFMA and Public Service Act
  • Manage the budget
  • Identify and recommend the movement of funds between items in consultation with the relevant stakeholders
  • Policy Analysis and Research:
  • Initiate research and stay abreast of policies, procedures and due processes in compliance with legislation
  • Analyse policies and prescripts and other documents applicable to the smooth operation of the Department and apply accordingly

Requirements

  • A Grade 12 is required coupled with a minimum Bachelor’s degree (equivalent to NQF level 7) in Public Administration or Management or Project Management.
  • A minimum 5 years of experience at a middle/senior managerial level obtained in management and facilitation of programmes.
  • Knowledge of the Public Service Policy Framework.
  • In-depth knowledge and experience of the operational environment of an executive office.
  • Knowledge and experience of the policy analysis and interpretation.
  • Successful completion of the Nyukela Public Service Senior Management Leadership Programme as endorsed by the National School of Government available as an online course on https://www.thensg.gov.za/training-course/sms-pre-entry-programme/, prior to finalisation of an appointment.
