IT Support Engineer (JB6138) Location: Springs, Johannesburg Salary: R20,000 per month Benefits: None Employment Type: Permanent If you are the kind of IT professional who gets a genuine kick out of solving problems, thrives in fast-moving environments, and wants to actually build skills rather than wait in a queue to escalate a ticket, this one is worth your attention. A growing Managed IT Services Provider based in Springs is looking for a Junior to Intermediate IT Support Engineer to join a tight-knit technical team that punches well above its weight. This is not a siloed helpdesk seat where you read from a script and pass the hard stuff upward. The company operates across a wide range of client environments, with a strong Microsoft 365 and cloud focus, and the engineers here are expected to figure things out, own the problem, and deliver. The upside is real exposure, genuine variety, and a clear path toward senior-level engineering over the next 12 to 24 months for the right person.
