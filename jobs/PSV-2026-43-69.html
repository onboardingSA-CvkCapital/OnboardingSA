About the role

The Government Pensions Administration Agency is hiring a Deputy Director: Business Risk, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. Enterprise-Wide Risk Permanent The purpose of this role is to promote and enhance the development and implementation of GPAA’s risk management plan and oversee the day -to- day operations of the directorate How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.gpaa.gov.za/ Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) No late applications will be considered Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: One permanent position of Deputy Director: Business Risk is currently available at the Government Pensions Administration Agency (GPAA): Enterprise-Wide Risk Unit

Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for a wide variety of tasks which include but are not limited to the following: Provide risk management services to the GPAA.
  • Provide risk training to the GPAA staff.
  • Monitoring compliance regarding risk related matters.
  • Co-facilitate risk awareness programmes with stakeholders.
  • Implement risk awareness programme for the GPAA.
  • Manage centralized risk management software.
  • Provide advice regarding risk related matters and emerging risks.
  • Develop and implement risk action plans for business units to manage risks effectively.
  • Liaise with external and internal auditors.
  • Develop risk mitigation strategies to manage risk exposure.
  • Provide support for the Risk Champions.
  • Manage the implementation of risk methodologies, policies, processes, and framework.
  • Develop risk policies, terms of references, framework, and standard operating procedure.
  • Review risk strategies in line with GPAA outcomes.
  • Develop, maintain, manage, and continuously improve risk management processes, policies, standard operating procedures, and guidelines.
  • Ensure that risk is identified and assessed.
  • Oversee the implementation of the risk management plan.
  • Monitor the implementation of the risk awareness plan.
  • Monitor and support the effectiveness of the risk management committee and a risk champion forum.
  • Identify gaps in policies and procedures and establish mechanisms to alleviate them.
  • Comply with legislation and adjust strategies, plans and procedures accordingly.
  • Monitor compliance with risk control measures.
  • Identify, procure, implement, and maintain an appropriate risk management system to improve effectiveness and efficiency of risk management.
  • Oversee the training in the use of risk management tools and techniques.
  • Implement risk awareness programmes for the GPAA.
  • Manage the central risk programme.
  • Monitor the completion of the risk register.
  • Maintain the risk appetite framework levels to serve as alerts to management.
  • Monitor compliance with risk management committee terms of reference.
  • Manage, implement, and maintain enterprise risk scorecard report.
  • Provide risk assurance on business process.
  • Provide guidelines for business units and ensure that each business strategy incorporates risk management principles.
  • Provide risk assurance on business processes.
  • Proactively monitor and manage identified risks to minimize risk exposure.
  • Ensure the undertaking of risk assessments to determine the GPAA’s risk exposure.
  • Internal and external stakeholder reporting.
  • Provide feedback reports to relevant stakeholders and business units.
  • Compile risk action plan monitoring reports.
  • Draft monthly, quarterly, and annual reports to management.
  • Generate ad-hoc reports and submit to management.
  • Report on risk action plans monthly.
  • Report on key risk indicators and/or performance indicators as required.
  • Provide input to the strategic management of the section.
  • Compile comprehensive operational plans, quarterly and annual reports.
  • Keep abreast of changes in relevant guidelines and other legislation, to make recommendations about where risk management governance documents need to be amended.
  • Develop, enhance, and implement policies, processes and procedures that are relevant to the section and enhance service delivery.
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to implement new systems and processes, enabling integration into other areas.
  • Manage all resources of the unit.
  • Set, agree, and monitor performance of direct reports, ensuring alignment with operational plan targets.
  • Allocate work according to the directorates plan.
  • Identify development and succession planning requirements.
  • Ensure employment equity compliance.
  • Monitor that output is achieved against business requirements.
  • Facilitate staff productivity and efficiency, minimizing absenteeism and turnover.
  • Motivate staff through various mechanisms.
  • Facilitate departmental communication through appropriate structures and systems.
  • Manage the budget of the unit and monitor expenditure patterns as per the prescripts.

Requirements

  • A three-year degree/national diploma in Risk Management, Business Management and related qualifications with at least 5-6 years’ experience in Business Risk/Internal Audit or relevant environment of which 3 years is in an Assistant Director/Middle management role.
  • Computer literacy that would include a good working knowledge of Microsoft Office products.
  • Knowledge of Risk management frameworks (COSO, ISO3100 and ISO22301), Knowledge of IT Management and Governance Frameworks (COBIT, PRINCE2), Knowledge of King Code on Corporate Governance, Knowledge of Risk Management Software, Knowledge of Public Finance Management Act.
  • Public Service Regulations and Other Government prescripts, Knowledge of Treasury Regulations, Knowledge of Risk Management and Corporate Governance.
  • Analytical skills, Business ethics, Communications skills, Presentation skills, Planning and organizational skills, Project management skills, Problem solving skills, Report writing skills, Assertive, Proactive, analytical mindset, Meticulous and detail oriented, Integrity and honesty, Ability to work under pressure, Ethical behaviour, Independent and self-motivated, Resilience.
