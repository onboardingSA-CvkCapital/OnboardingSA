About the role

FEM is looking to appoint a Junior QA Tester at our Head Office. The successful candidate will support the delivery of reliable, high-quality software by executing defined quality assurance activities within a DevOps and Agile delivery environment. The role focuses on manual testing, defect management, and developing automation testing skills under supervision, while contributing to continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) processes. The successful candidate will work closely with developers, business analysts and senior QA team members to ensure software quality standards are maintained throughout the development lifecycle. The individual will be responsible for executing test cases, identifying and reporting defects, supporting quality assurance activities, and continuously developing their technical and business knowledge within a collaborative software development environment. The following skills and experience will be required: Quality Assurance & Testing • Manual test planning and execution • Test case creation and maintenance • Regression testing • Defect identification, logging and tracking • Test data preparation and management • Functional and integration testing • User story and acceptance criteria validation • Quality assurance best practicesTechnical & Delivery Support • Basic understanding of Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) • Basic understanding of Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC) • Exposure to Agile and Scrum methodologies • Basic API testing knowledge • Basic SQL and data validation skills • Experience working in development and UAT environments • Understanding of CI/CD concepts and DevOps principles • Exposure to software testing tools and methodologiesOther Skills • Strong attention to detail • Problem-solving ability • Willingness to learn and develop new skills • Clear written and verbal communication • Ability to follow defined processes and procedures • Good organisational and time management skills • Ability to work effectively within a team environment • Confidentiality and data sensitivity awarenessThe minimum requirements of the position are a Matric qualification together with ISTQB Foundation Certification (or currently working towards certification), software testing short courses, relevant bootcamps, internships or equivalent practical exposure. A Diploma or Degree in IT, Computer Science, Information Systems or a related field will be advantageous. Candidates should have between 0 and 2 years' experience in software testing. Exposure to academic testing projects, internships, graduate programmes, Agile delivery environments, test case creation and execution, as well as basic SQL knowledge, will be advantageous. This position is based in Rosebank, Johannesburg. In exchange for the candidate's successful services, FEM offers a competitive total cost to company remuneration with good benefits. Candidates are invited to submit a detailed CV by close of business on 25 September 2026. Applications may not be considered if the minimum criteria of the position are not met. People with disabilities are welcome to apply. FEM promotes the principles of Employment Equity, and the appointment of this candidate is at FEM's sole discretion.
