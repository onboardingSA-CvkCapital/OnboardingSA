About the role

Our client, a well-established international engineering integration and manufacturing company, has an opportunity for a highly experienced Senior Electrical Engineer with at least 10 years + operational and design experience (inclusive of relevant design program exposure) to join their team in Pretoria The position is twofold, being managerial and technical related. The primary purpose of this role is to lead, plan, and execute professional electrical designs. This involves managing the full design lifecycle to deliver client-specific solutions while ensuring full compliance with relevant electrical codes and legal standards. Requirements: • B.Eng in Electrical Engineering Degree or equivalent NQF (preference). Alternative Diploma or B.Tech/M.Tech. • Pr. Eng/Tech in Electrical Engineering. • Minimum 10 years + operational and design experience (inclusive of relevant design program exposure) • Professional registration with ECSA as Pr.Eng or Pr.Tech Eng. will be highly advantageous • Proven experience within mining, industrial, or heavy engineering environments. • Experience managing multidisciplinary engineering projects. • Strong exposure to industrial plant design and electrical infrastructure projects • Sound knowledge of SANS standards and international electrical engineering standards. • Thorough understanding of the Mine Health and Safety Act (MHSA) and Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA) • Proficiency in electrical engineering design software. • Knowledge of PLC and SCADA systems • Strong Technical product knowledge •
