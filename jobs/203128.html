About the role

Role Purpose: The Portfolio Manager – Consumer Asset Finance is a commercially driven new-business role responsible for originating, developing and converting new asset finance opportunities. The primary focus is to actively build and grow a sustainable portfolio of suppliers, referral partners and clients through disciplined prospecting, relationship development and deal origination. The successful candidate will be a natural hunter with a strong entrepreneurial mindset, capable of independently identifying opportunities, opening new markets and converting relationships into sustainable finance volumes. The role requires persistence, commercial creativity and a strong bias toward action, while operating within Merchant West's credit, risk and governance frameworks. Main Accountabilities: • New Business Development & Origination • Proactively identify, prospect and secure new Consumer Asset Finance opportunities. • Develop a consistent pipeline of new suppliers, referral partners and end-clients. • Take ownership of new-business generation from initial prospecting through to successful payout. • Actively identify untapped markets, asset classes and commercial opportunities. • Maintain a high level of external market activity through supplier visits, client meetings, cold prospecting and networking. • Consistently deliver against agreed origination, application, payout and portfolio-growth targets. • Supplier & Channel Development • Identify, engage and onboard new suppliers and strategic referral partners. • Build a diversified supplier network capable of generating recurring Consumer Asset Finance opportunities. • Develop suppliers into sustainable, productive relationships rather than once-off sources of business. • Position Merchant West as the preferred finance partner within targeted Consumer asset classes. • Regularly engage suppliers to identify pipeline opportunities and increase finance penetration. • Take full commercial ownership of supplier relationships and associated deal flow. • Client & Relationship Management • Build trusted, long-term relationships with clients, suppliers and strategic partners. • Understand client requirements and proactively structure appropriate asset finance solutions. • Identify cross-sell, repeat-business and referral opportunities within the existing portfolio. • Maintain ownership of relationships throughout the finance lifecycle. • Deal Origination & Execution • Take end-to-end ownership of originated opportunities from initial engagement through application, credit assessment and payout. • Understand client requirements and structure commercially appropriate finance proposals. • Work closely with Credit and internal stakeholders to package high-quality applications. • Proactively manage outstanding requirements and obstacles to drive transactions toward conclusion. • Reporting and Portfolio Administration • Prepare and maintain accurate portfolio reports and documentation. • Ensure all client records and portfolio data are up to date and compliant. • Provide regular updates on portfolio performance to management. • Credit Governance and Compliance • Ensure adherence to credit policies, procedures, and governance frameworks. • Maintain proper documentation and audit trails for portfolio activities. • Support audit and compliance processes related to portfolio management. Qualifications: • Relevant Bachelor’s degree is required, preferably in Commerce, Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Management or a related discipline Experience: • Minimum 5 years’ proven experience in business development, sales, relationship management or deal origination within financial services • Demonstrable track record of originating and converting new business. • Asset finance experience is strongly preferred. • Proven experience in developing supplier, dealer, intermediary or referral networks. • Evidence of consistently achieving or exceeding sales and revenue targets. • Existing relationships within relevant Consumer, supplier or asset finance markets will be advantageous. • Experience managing a portfolio independently with limited supervision. Skills: • New business development and prospecting • Consultative and solution-based selling • Supplier and channel development • Deal origination and structuring • Pipeline and sales funnel management • Client relationship and account management • Financial and credit assessment capability • Commercial presentation and stakeholder engagement • Financial statement interpretation and basic financial analysis Competencies: • Commercial judgement and financial acumen • Hunter mentality – naturally driven to identify, pursue and convert new opportunities • Analytical thinking and problem solving • Decision making and sound risk judgement • Stakeholder engagement and collaboration • Negotiation and influencing capability • Accountability and ownership • Results orientation and performance focus • Integrity and governance discipline
