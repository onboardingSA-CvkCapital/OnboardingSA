You will be supporting them with all related administrative functions for effective and efficient service delivery. WHAT WILL YOU DO? Provide General Secretarial Support to the Assigned Group Executive and Heads • Arranging meetings and managing the diaries of the assigned Group Executive and HODs • Compiling of monthly reports for circulation per agreed timelines • Arranging and coordinating departmental meetings as and when necessary • Typing of reports, letters, presentations, circulars and general correspondence • Handling and managing departmental queries Travel Arrangements • Responsible for the end-to-end travel arrangements for the assigned Group Executive, Heads and the division • Processing all financial/budget requests for sign-off • Minute taking • Drafting agendas for the periodic meetings as required • Circulation of accurate and quality minutes for the meetings within the agreed timelines General Administrative Duties • Follow up on deadlines to subordinates by means of electronic task requests • Diarize committee circulars and deadlines • Assist with budget control • Manage the departmental documents on SharePoint through editing, version control and publishing • Upload month end reports on SharePoint • Booking meeting rooms as requested • Stationery orders • Responsible for Invoices and Opex management • Assist with basic administration duties and project coordination support (logistics for projects) • Assist with event management WHAT WILL YOU GET IN RETURN?
