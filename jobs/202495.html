About the role

PURPOSE OF ROLE We are seeking an experienced Senior Electrical Engineer/Technologist to lead and manage the Electrical Engineering function within nuclear design projects. The successful candidate will deliver compliant, safe, and technically sound electrical designs, support tendering and project execution, and provide specialist expertise across multidisciplinary teams — all while ensuring strict adherence to nuclear regulatory, quality, and safety standards.
