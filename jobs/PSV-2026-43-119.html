About the role

Independent Police Investigative Directorate is hiring a Director: Strategic Management, based in National Office Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Independent Police Investigative Directorate, National Office Private Bag X941, Pretoria, 0002 or hand deliver to Benstra Building, 473 Stanza Bopape & Church Street, Arcadia, Pretoria, 0001 or Recruitment12@ipid.gov.za (Please indicate the post name and reference number on the subject line) when applying through e-mail For attention: Ms RE Sikhwari Tel No: (012) 399 0214 Reference number to quote: Q9/2026/68 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Oversee the development and implementation of departmental strategic, annual performance and operational plans, provide strategic vision and direction for the Department.
  • Provide direction to the Department in realising its strategic outcomes.
  • Develop detailed strategic and operational performance plans to execute strategic priorities.
  • Assist in defining performance measures in order to evaluate the success of departmental strategies.
  • Coordinate strategic planning sessions for Strategic Plan (SP) and Annual Performance Plan (APP).
  • Coordinate the development of the Department's Strategic Plan and Annual Performance Plan.
  • Coordinate the development of the Departmental Annual Operational Plan.
  • Provide strategic guidance in the development of programme performance information reports: Coordinate the development of the work plan for compilation of quarterly performance report and annual performance report.
  • Co-ordinate the development of the quarterly and departmental annual reports.
  • Facilitate the institutional quarterly performance review sessions.
  • Coordinate the responses/queries relating to predetermined objectives.
  • Coordinate the departmental reporting tools and guidelines.
  • Coordinate the reporting of workshops in the Department.
  • Coordinate the development of the Mid-Term and End-Term Assessment Reports.
  • Facilitate the compilation and tabling of the Department’s Annual Report in Parliament: Manage provision of the Tabling of strategic plans, annual performance plans and annual reports in Parliament timeously.
  • Present performance reports and plans timeously to EXCO meeting, Audit and Risk Committee and Portfolio Committee on Police or any other structure deemed necessary by the Executive Director.
  • Brief the Minister of Police on quarterly and annual performance reports and performance plans.
  • Ensure timeous submission of reports and plans to DPME, National Treasury and AGSA.
  • Monitor and evaluate the strategic objectives of the Department: Manage the planning and monitoring of Provincial Police Secretariats planning sessions.
  • Monitor implementation of Provincial Budget Programme structures.
  • Provide support to provincial strategic planning work sessions.
  • Provide strategic support to the Secretariat Programmes and Provinces with regard to customised indicators.
  • Facilitate and coordinate the development of the Department’s SDIP and monitor the implementation thereof: Develop and review the Department’s Service Delivery Improvement Plan (SDIP) in line with applicable legislative framework.
  • Monitor the implementation of the SDIP.
  • Provide technical support to programme managers and IPID employees on SDIP requirements and implementation.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of the service delivery improvement initiatives.
  • Recommend improvements to enhance service delivery outcomes.
  • Management of Resources
  • Effective and efficient management of Human Resource
  • Effective and efficient management of Financial Resource
  • Effective and efficient management of Equipment/Assets
