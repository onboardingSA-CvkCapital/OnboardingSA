About the role

Support the sustainable maximisation of Sasol’s profitability by enabling effective planning and optimisation, including development and execution of HUB production budgets and monthly plans, maintaining accurate LP models and mass/volume balances, evaluating plan performance and operational constraints, identifying production optimisation opportunities, and ensuring the P&O toolset remains relevant while supporting and training its user community.

Responsibilities

  • Support sustainable maximisation of Sasol’s profitability through enabling planning via an optimised toolset.
  • Support planning and budget process with any process constraints and philosophy changes
  • Align to the Planning & Optimisation (P&O) Functional strategy by supporting development of the 2-year budget and monthly plans for the HUB.
  • Execution of the annual production budget
  • Ensure that the site mass and volume balances are adequately reflected in the P&O toolset (via the Linear Programing (LP) models).
  • Evaluate the HUB plan accuracy through back-casting of models (specifically on LP performance)
  • Develop and maintain optimisation toolset (particularly LP models) to ensure relevancy and accuracy
  • Ensure that the P&O toolset user community is adequately trained and supported.
  • Adherence to all governance processes within LP modelling.
  • Business Results
  • This job is concerned with the optimisation of the HUB's operation. Integration occurs across functions and product value chains.
  • The incumbent will work in a matrix organization with functional responsibility of the LP model for planning production for operations and the requirement to lead, influence and direct production units.
  • The environment is relatively uncertain, and the person will need tolerance for ambiguity.
  • AI use cases to optimize workload for LP activities and super users of LP model.
  • Leadership and Strategic Direction
  • Understanding the complexity of value chains and interdependencies of HUBs and SBUs.
  • Assessing the reliability and accuracy of sources of data to be used in unit uncertainty calculations and balances.
  • Challenging/Updating sources of data inputs and profitability constraints.
  • Accuracy of the production and inventory planning tools.
  • Backup peers to ensure continuity.
  • Automation of LP model related activities for super users of the model.
  • Production Budget/Plan (Rotation)
  • Co-ordinate yearly/monthly production budget/plan through detailed validations and value-adding scenarios.
  • Governance and improvement of budget/plan process.
  • Present results to Senior Management.

Requirements

  • University Degree in Engineering
  • 6 years relevant experience. Prior experience with linear programming, scheduling and blending tools, as well as simulation tools is preferable.
  • The incumbent should understand the value chains within Secunda Operations and be able to interpret the results of planning, LP and blending tools. The incumbent should have a sound understanding of process unit operations including the constraints. The incumbent also needs to have skills and competencies to interpret planning and blending issues within Secunda and a strong level of troubleshooting skills. Basic process engineering skills and experience in complex mass balances and troubleshooting are therefore a must.
  • The position requires working in teams with technical, operations, and planning resources with a twofold focus of: -
  • creating models that appropriately, accurately represent the capabilities, flexibilities and constraints of the assets and
  • to identify profit improvement opportunities within the asset for future development.
  • A high degree of computer literacy skills and interaction with others are needed to fulfil this position.
