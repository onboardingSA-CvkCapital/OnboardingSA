About the role

Join Entyce Beverages Coffee & Creamer in Isando, where we produce some of South Africa’s favourite brands, including Frisco, Koffiehuis, Hug in a Mug, House of Coffees and Ellis Brown. From production and engineering to quality, planning, maintenance and supply chain, you’ll work alongside specialist teams in a fast-paced manufacturing environment where what you do matters. Build your skills, take ownership and grow your career behind iconic brands with Entyce. Why Join National Brands Limited (NBL)? • Make an Impact: Contribute to producing South Africa’s favourite coffee and creamer brands, influencing daily life. • Innovative Growth: Be part of a forward-thinking environment that encourages product and process improvement. • Career Development: Access a range of roles and growth opportunities at one of NBL’s 5 world-class facilities. • Collaborative Culture: Work in a supportive, team-driven environment where your contributions matter. Shape the Future with Us! A rewarding opportunity awaits a skilled Welder at NBL Coffee and Creamer in Isando, Kempton Park. Your role will be to ensure that all Finished Goods leaving the factory are of the best quality and are food safe. Reporting Structure: • Reports to: Maintenance Manager - Utilities Key Areas of Impact: • Fabrication of components and structures to support plant maintenance and improvement initiatives • Welding of stainless steel and aluminium components in accordance with required standards • Conducting general welding repairs on plant equipment and infrastructure • Assisting with preventative maintenance and breakdown repairs • Ensuring all work is completed in line with safety, quality, and food safety standards • Maintaining tools and equipment in good working condition • Supporting continuous improvement and cost-saving initiatives within the engineering function What It Takes to Succeed: Experience that set you up for success: • 5 or more years’ stainless steel welding and fabrication experience (preferably in a food manufacturing environment) Qualifications & Certifications that will contribute to your success: • Grade 12 / Matric • Relevant Welding Trade Test is essential • Good knowledge of fabrication and welding techniques (Tig and Arc) • Ability to read and interpret basic technical drawings Additional Requirements that will enhance your impact for success: • Willingness to work overtime when required • Good understanding of health and safety practices • Ability to work at heights and in confined spaces when required Take the Next Step with us Join a leader in FMCG with a proud history and exciting future. If you're passionate about quality, innovation, and teamwork, Entyce Beverages offers a rewarding career. Apply now and grow great brands, with us!
