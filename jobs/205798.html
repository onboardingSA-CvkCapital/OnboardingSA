About the role

National Asphalt is a leading supplier of asphalt in Southern Africa, renowned for its commitment to quality, innovation, operational excellence, and sustainability. We manufacture a comprehensive range of asphalt products and operate both commercial and mobile asphalt plants across the country. We are seeking a dynamic, results-driven Plant Manager to join our team at Cliffdale. The successful candidate must be willing to relocate if required and possess the leadership capability to drive operational performance in a fast-paced manufacturing environment Purpose of the Role The Plant Manager is responsible for ensuring the efficient, safe, and profitable operation of the asphalt plant. The successful incumbent will lead all plant activities to achieve production targets while maintaining the highest standards of cost control, employee safety, employee relations, product quality, and customer service.
