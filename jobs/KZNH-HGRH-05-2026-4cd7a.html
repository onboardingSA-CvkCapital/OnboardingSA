About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring an Operational Manager Nursing (Primary Health Care), based in HARRY GWALA REGIONAL HOSPITAL. This is a permanent post, paying R656,964 to R748,683. How to apply: ALL APPLICATIONS SHOULD BE FORWARDED TO Reference number to quote: HGRH 05.2026 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure implementation of operational imperatives set by the district as per Health policies and programmes.
  • Ensure provision of quality health care services.
  • Provide contextual planning, monitoring and evaluation.
  • Coordinate service area to ensure a primary health approach within the whole area.
  • Facilitate and advocate for the required resources for service delivery including staff development.
  • Ensure cost effective and appropriate use of resources. 15
  • Ensure appropriate implementation of Batho Pele and Patient’s Rights.
  • Implement Complaints, Compliments and Suggestions in the facility and thereof reduce them by 10%.
  • Ensure that the clinic comply with OHSC and ICRM and sustain thereof.
  • Ensure development, implementation and evaluation of EPMDS in line with departmental prescripts
  • Manage and support education, in-service training, and practice development initiatives in the facilities, professional growth, ethical standards and participation in training and research.
  • Oversee disciplinary and grievance matters including monitoring and managing absenteeism.
  • Implement consequence management for non-compliance.
  • Implement Operation Phuthuma Strategy to improve health outcomes and quality of care
  • Ensure implementation of TUTT strategy in the facility to achieve long and healthy lives.
  • Ensure quality data management at facility and sub-district level.
  • Maintain inter-sectoral collaboration with other government structures through Operation Sukuma Sakhe Activities.
  • Provide supervision to facility to ensure that it provide comprehensive quality Primary Health Care and School Health services for the catchment population.
  • Participate in Primary Health Care Sub -District Meetings.
  • Ensure proper Implementation of DMOC to decongest the facility
  • May be required to work extended hours and weekends.

Requirements

  • Senior Certificate/National Senior Certificate/Matric
  • Degree /Diploma in General Nursing and Midwifery (obtainable from University/ College)
  • Registration certificate with SANC as a General Nurse and Midwife
  • Post Basic Qualification in Primary Health Care registered with SANC
  • Current SANC registration (2026 Receipt)
  • A minimum of NINE YEARS’ appropriate/recognisable nursing experience after registration as Professional Nurse with the SANC in General Nursing
  • FIVE YEARS of that period must be recognisable experience after obtaining a one year Post Basic Qualification in Primary Health Care.
  • Certificate of Service endorsed by HR Department must be attached
  • Computer literacy with a proficiency in MS Office Software applications
  • Code B Driver’s license.
  • Financial Management. Leadership, Organizational, decision making and problem solving.
  • Knowledge of Public Service policies and other Health Related prescripts. Sound knowledge of Code of conduct. Good interpersonal skills. Human Resources Management and Labour Relations Act.
  • Knowledge of Professionalism and Ethics in the work place.
