About the role

Regional Operations Centre (ROC) Role Overview The Regional Operations Centre (ROC) Agent is responsible for monitoring and optimizing telecommunications network performance and overseeing contractor activities within a designated region. This role plays a critical part in troubleshooting network issues, managing contractor performance, maintaining support channel communication, and driving Net Promoter Score (NPS) improvements Key Responsibilities 1. Contractor & Maintenance Management • Oversee contractor activities concerning network maintenance, installation support, order activations, and repairs. • Ensure contractors strictly adhere to safety guidelines, company policies, and agreed Service Level Agreements (SLAs). • Monitor contractor performance, maintain accurate training/performance records, and provide feedback and coaching for continuous improvement. • Compile and generate daily, weekly, and monthly contractor performance reports. • Track the impact of ongoing maintenance activities and facilitate prompt service restoration. • Document operational best practices to support continuous workflow improvement. 2. Support Email & Escalation Management • Monitor and manage the primary support email queue, ensuring prompt responses to queries and escalations. • Categorize, prioritize, and log inbound issues based on business impact and urgency. • Coordinate cross-functionally with internal engineering teams, Area/Regional Network Owners, vendors, and external partners to resolve network issues. • Maintain complete documentation of email communications, case histories, and follow-up actions. 3. Network Performance Monitoring & Optimization • Analyze network performance metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to identify and address anomalies proactively. • Work alongside Maintenance teams to implement optimizations that enhance overall network efficiency and service reliability. • Evaluate ticket and case data trends to optimize operational processes and elevate the end-user experience. 4. Customer Experience & Net Promoter Score (NPS) • Analyze customer ticket trends and feedback to pinpoint root causes affecting service quality. • Partner with customer service teams to address network-related complaints and eliminate recurring issues. • Drive Net Promoter Score (NPS) growth by ensuring high service reliability, swift issue resolution, and clear stakeholder communication. Requirements & Qualifications Education • Essential: Grade 12 / Matric diploma (or equivalent qualification). Experience • 1–3 years of experience in network operations, technical support, or a related telecommunications role. • Proven experience using network monitoring tools, fault management systems, and ticketing applications. • Hands-on experience with CMS/CRM platforms (e.g., Salesforce). • Experience with contractor management is highly advantageous. Technical Knowledge • Basic understanding of Fiber Optic Networks (FTTH/FTTB). • Foundational knowledge of IP networking, routing, and switching. • Strong practical ability to diagnose and troubleshoot connectivity issues. Competencies & Personal Attributes • Problem Solving: Strong analytical abilities to diagnose trends and troubleshoot technical issues quickly. • Communication: Excellent written and verbal communication skills; comfortable dealing with internal and external stakeholders. • Organization: Strong organizational and planning skills with the ability to manage competing priorities under pressure. • Mindset: Highly self-motivated, proactive, accountable, and solution-oriented. • Collaboration: Proven team player comfortable working across multiple functional departments.
