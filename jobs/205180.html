About the role

Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the-farm foods for consumers. Apart from its various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture, with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now operate across the entire food supply chain, including processing, logistics, storage, handling and national and international market channels. This "house of brands", established with the support of our farmer shareholders, forms the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. More About VKB Finance The VKB Finance function serves as a strategic business partner across the entire VKB Group. Our Finance team does more than report numbers. We enable informed decision-making, strengthen governance, support business growth, and ensure that the systems and controls underpinning our operations remain robust and future-fit. As the VKB Group continues to evolve and expand, our finance professionals play a critical role in ensuring that financial information, processes, systems, and controls support operational excellence across a diverse group of businesses. Why This Role Matters As a Financial Controller, you will be at the intersection of Finance, Technology, and Business Operations. 2 Financial Controller positions available. This role is responsible for ensuring that the VKB Group's financial systems, controls, and ERP environments support accurate reporting, operational efficiency, and sound decision-making. You will work closely with Financial Managers, Operational Managers, and project teams to improve processes, implement system enhancements, strengthen controls, and ensure consistency across the Group. This is an exciting opportunity for a finance professional who enjoys solving complex business challenges, improving systems, analysing processes, and helping to shape the future of financial operations in one of South Africa's leading agricultural businesses.
