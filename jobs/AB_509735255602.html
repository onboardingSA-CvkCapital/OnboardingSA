About the role

At our company, we believe that our future depends on developing the next generation of talent. As our Talent & Learning Specialist, you will play an important role in identifying, attracting and developing young talent for the future of our organisation. Talent Acquisition & Early Careers • Build and maintain relationships with universities, TVET colleges, agricultural colleges, schools and other educational institutions to identify emerging talent aligned with Dutoit Agri's current and future workforce needs. • Develop and maintain young talent and graduate pipelines for critical, scarce and future skills within the organisation. • Identify promising students, graduates and young professionals through campus engagement, career fairs, graduate programmes, industry events and other talent initiatives. • Coordinate and support student placements, internships, work-integrated learning (WIL), learnerships, graduate programmes and apprenticeships in collaboration with relevant stakeholders. • Establish and maintain relationships with career offices, academic departments, lecturers and other institutional stakeholders to strengthen Dutoit's access to emerging talent. • Support the development and implementation of initiatives that position Dutoit Agri as an employer of choice for young and emerging talent. • Maintain an accurate database of prospective students, graduates and emerging talent for current and future opportunities. • Work with managers to understand current and future critical skills requirements and align talent-pipeline initiatives accordingly. • Support the selection and placement of suitable candidates into approved opportunities within the organisation. • Monitor the progress, placement outcomes and development of students, interns, graduates and other participants entering Dutoit through early-career programmes. • Prepare reports on talent pipelines, institutional partnerships, student placements, graduate programmes and early-career initiatives. • Support recruitment activities where required, including candidate sourcing, screening, interview coordination, reference checks and pre-employment processes. Learning & Development • Coordinate annual and ongoing Training Needs Analyses with managers and employees. • Assist with individual development plans and role-specific development plans. • Coordinate internal and external training programmes from planning through to completion. • Manage training schedules, logistics, attendance and supporting documentation. • Liaise with training providers, SETA structures and relevant institutions. • Support employee induction and onboarding programmes. • Coordinate leadership, supervisory, technical and operational development initiatives. • Support values, culture and employee development programmes. • Coordinate learnerships, internships, graduate programmes, bursaries and apprenticeships where applicable. • Monitor learner and employee progress and programme completion. • Evaluate training effectiveness through feedback, assessments, attendance and manager input. • Maintain accurate and up-to-date training records and reports. Talent Management & Employee Development • Support succession planning and internal talent identification. • Assist with the development of talent pipelines and career development initiatives. • Support managers in identifying employee development needs and practical development solutions. • Follow up on agreed employee development actions. • Contribute to employee retention and engagement initiatives. • Identify opportunities for internal mobility and career progression. • Support the development of future leaders and critical skills within the organisation. Skills Development, Employment Equity & Compliance • Support the implementation of the organisation's Skills Development initiatives. • Assist with Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) and Annual Training Report (ATR) processes. • Maintain accurate Skills Development records and supporting documentation. • Support AgriSETA-related learning programmes and requirements. • Monitor training information and provide accurate reports for HR and management. • Coordinates Employment Equity planning and monitoring processes. • Assist with the compilation and maintenance of Employment Equity plans and reports. • Monitor progress against agreed Employment Equity targets. • Support HR compliance with applicable labour and employment legislation. • Ensure recruitment, employee and training information is handled confidentially and in accordance with POPIA. • Maintain accurate records to support internal and statutory reporting COMMUNITY & EMPLOYEE SUPPORT As part of Dutoit's broader people strategy, the role will also support initiatives that contribute to the wellbeing and development of employees and their families, including: • Dutoit Agri study loan scheme administration. • Support for employee crèches and after-care programmes. • Community and employee support initiatives. • Educational and development programmes. • Initiatives aimed at improving employee wellbeing and family support structures. REPORTING & ANALYTICS You will be expected to turn HR information into meaningful insights. Responsibilities include: • Preparing monthly and quarterly recruitment reports. • Preparing learning and development reports. • Monitoring recruitment and training trends. • Maintaining accurate HR and training data. • Tracking recruitment and learning KPIs. • Identifying gaps and recommending practical corrective actions. • Using Excel, HRIS and digital/AI tools to improve HR processes and reporting. DIGITAL & TECHNICAL SKILLS You should be comfortable working with: • Microsoft Office, particularly Excel • HR Information Systems • Recruitment platforms • HR reporting and analytics • Digital learning platforms • AI-enabled HR and productivity tools • Recruitment and talent database The ability to use technology and AI tools to improve HR processes, reporting and employee experiences will be highly advantageous. LEGISLATIVE KNOWLEDGE A practical working knowledge of the following will be required: • Labour Relations Act • Basic Conditions of Employment Act • Employment Equity Act • Skills Development Act • Skills Development Levies Act • Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) Knowledge of B-BBEE Skills Development requirements will be advantageous
