About the role

Are you commercially minded, analytical, and passionate about using data to drive business growth? Lesaka is looking for a Financial Analyst to join our FP&A team and support strategic decision-making across our growing international portfolio. As a leading South African fintech, Lesaka is expanding its footprint across Africa and seeks a finance professional who can deliver meaningful insights, support commercial decisions, and drive financial performance. Reporting to the Head of FP&A – Community, with a dotted line to the Executive Head of International, you will support forecasting, financial modelling, business cases, performance reporting, and expansion initiatives across multiple African markets. You will also partner with stakeholders across Community and Corporate portfolios, including ADP, Card, and Hospitality. This is an exciting opportunity to gain exposure to executive-level stakeholders while helping shape Lesaka's growth journey across Africa Key Responsibilities • Support the monthly forecasting process, variance analysis, and management reporting for the international portfolio. • Partner with business leaders on commercial decisions, business cases, and investment analysis for new and existing regions. • Build and maintain financial models for strategic planning, forecasting, scenario analysis, and business cases. • Assist in establishing financial reporting, budgeting, and forecasting frameworks for new regions across Africa. • Prepare board and ExCo reporting packs, including financial commentary, KPI analysis, and risk reporting. • Provide financial analysis and reporting support across ADP, Card, Hospitality, and other business portfolios. • Develop management dashboards and ad hoc reports to support business performance. • Monitor costs against budget and identify cost-saving opportunities. • Support month-end financial reporting and ensure data integrity across FP&A and reporting processes. • Act as a trusted finance business partner to operational and commercial stakeholders. • Manage margin costing, supplier contract controls, margin analysis, and related reporting activities. Minimum Requirements • Bachelor's Degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, Statistics, or a related field. • CA(SA) qualification will be advantageous. • Minimum of 3 years' experience in FP&A, financial analysis, commercial finance, business analytics, strategy, or consulting. • Strong financial modelling, forecasting, and analytical skills. • Advanced Microsoft Excel proficiency. • Experience using financial systems and reporting tools. • Experience designing reports and dashboards in Power BI or a similar platform. • Strong written communication and financial commentary skills for board and investor reporting. • Exceptional attention to detail and problem-solving capability. What Will Make You Successful? You are a highly analytical individual who enjoys turning data into actionable insights. You are comfortable engaging with senior stakeholders, can communicate complex financial information clearly, and thrive in a fast-paced environment where no two days are the same. You bring a collaborative mindset, excellent organisational skills, and the ability to manage multiple priorities while maintaining a high standard of work. Exposure to AI tools and modern ways of working will be advantageous. Why Join Lesaka? • Be part of one of South Africa's leading fintech businesses. • Work directly with senior executives and business leaders. • Gain exposure to international operations and expansion across Africa. • Contribute to high-impact strategic and commercial decisions. • Grow your career in a fast-moving, innovative, and collaborative environment. If you are passionate about financial analysis, strategic decision-making, and helping drive business growth across Africa, we would like to hear from you.
