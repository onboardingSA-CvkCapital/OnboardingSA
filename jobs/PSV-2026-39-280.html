About the role

The Department of Economic Development (Gauteng) is hiring a Chief Director: Economic Empowerment and Transformation in Chief Directorate: Economic Empowerment and Transformation, based in Umnotho House Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R1,554,696 a year. How to apply: Apply online to the http:jobs.gauteng.gpg.gov.za website only. Reference number to quote: REFS/057142 Closing date: 20 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the development and implementation of strategic plans for economic empowerment and transformation.
  • Oversee the formulation of policies that support inclusive economic growth and monitor their effectiveness.
  • Evaluate and identify barriers to economic empowerment faced by historically disadvantaged groups.
  • Support the implementation of policies and initiatives to promote entrepreneurship and economic inclusion among groups.
  • Guide the development of partnerships with private enterprises and NGOs to create opportunities for skills development and capacity-building.
  • Assess the impact of empowerment programmes on improving socio-economic conditions.
  • Advocate for policies and regulations that support the economic upliftment of marginalised communities.
  • Collaborate with educational institutions to create pathways for education and training for underrepresented groups.
  • Engage with government officials, community leaders and stakeholders to ensure programmes are culturally sensitive and inclusive.
  • Develop a strategic roadmap for targeted programmes aimed at industry development and economic transformation.
  • Allocate resources and funding to support the implementation of the programs across key sectors.
  • Collaborate with industry leaders to identify growth opportunities and address bottlenecks hindering transformation.
  • Monitor program implementation to ensure alignment with transformation objectives and desired outcomes.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of industry-focused initiatives in driving economic transformation.
  • Facilitate access to markets and investment opportunities for emerging businesses from historically marginalised backgrounds.
  • Conduct regular assessments to measure the impact of the programs on industry growth and transformation.
  • Guide the implementation of programmes to promote industrial development, transformation of industries and to enhance competitiveness.
  • Manage the development and implementation of mechanisms to monitor equity ownership by previously disadvantaged groups as well as compliance and enforcement of BBBEE codes.
  • Assess the implementation of BBBEE and local production and content compliance.
  • Provide advisory services regarding various transformation charters.
  • Lead the development of strategies to bridge the gap between job seekers and employment opportunities, especially for marginalised groups.
  • Establish partnerships with public and private entities to create job placement programs.
  • Manage and guide the designing of initiatives to support entrepreneurship as a means of job creation and economic participation.
  • Monitor labour market trends and analyse data to identify areas with high potential for job creation.
  • Collaborate with educational institutions to align curricula with market demands and promote skills development for employability.
  • Oversee mentorship and coaching programs to prepare individuals for employment or entrepreneurship.
  • Advocate for inclusive hiring practices within businesses and organisations.
  • Build and maintain partnerships with public and private sector entities.
  • Facilitate dialogue and cooperation among stakeholders to advance inclusive economic growth, transformation and empowerment.
  • Provide expert advice to government and relevant stakeholders on inclusive economic growth, transformation and empowerment.
  • Represent the department in forums and committees related to inclusive economic growth, transformation and empowerment.
  • Perform strategic and operational planning.
  • Oversee the administrative support functions.
  • Perform people management functions.
  • Perform financial management functions.
  • Perform asset management functions.

Requirements

  • NQF level 7 qualification in Economics/Development Studies/Public Policy/Public Administration/Business Administration.
  • A postgraduate qualification will be an added advantage Nyukela Certificate for entry into SMS (Senior Management Service) from the National School of Government submitted prior to appointment.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at senior management level in Economic Empowerment and Transformation environment.
  • Code 8/10.
