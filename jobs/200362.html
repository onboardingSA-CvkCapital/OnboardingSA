About the role

An industry leader in the cosmetic manufacturing industry which is based in the Gauteng Province is recruiting an experienced and skilled Chief Financial Officer. The CFO provides strategic financial leadership, safeguards the organisation's assets and ensures the integrity of financial reporting and internal controls. Experience The successful incumbent must possess at least seven (7) years’ experience within a financial constituency and pertaining to an executive managerial level. Formal qualifications • A degree in Finance/Management Accounting • A relevant post graduate degree in Finance/Management Accounting • Master of Business Administration (MBA) or similar post-graduate qualification will be advantageous • Registered with the South African Institute for Chartered Accountants (SAICA)Responsibilities • Ensure development and monitoring of financial policies and numerous financial tasks and projects • Formulate and regularly review management procedures and policies • Establish an internal audit, risk management function and monitor assessments to ensure Responsible for developing and monitoring a system of internal controls to adequately safeguard financial resources. • Manage the preparation of the annual budget. • Develop and implement the
