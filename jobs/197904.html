About the role

58Fleet is a world leader in its field of specialisation offering a variety of financial products for motor vehicles. 58Fleet aims to creatively solve problems and add value to people by ensuring Mobility for Good. 58Fleet forms part of the Motus Group, a leading JSE listed Company in the Automotive Industry where performance is driven by three keystones, 1. Leaders, 2. Innovation & 3. Customer Champions. 58Fleet is seeking to employ a qualified and experienced Senior Finance Manager. The successful candidate will join a dynamic and highly skilled Finance Team. The successful candidate will report directly to the Managing Director. JOB DESCRIPTION: The purpose of this role is to lead business & its development through collaboration with the Executive Team, Board of Directors, & finance team. To direct, add value & enable business decisions through strong leadership capabilities, exceptional strategic thinking, & attention to detail. The role requires an understanding of South African financial & compliance regulations. The role will report to the Managing Director of the division and have 6 direct reports. DUTIES/ KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: Main activities will include: • Overall responsibility & oversight of the division’s financial department & results. • Responsibility for all financial matters, including management & financial reporting; budget development, planning & analysis, billing, debtors, creditors & fund accounting. • The management of operational matters, including directing of staff & monthly payroll. • Leading financial and processes while ensuring cost containment & financial discipline, effective management of internal & external audit. • Engage with operations and systems teams to develop, test and implement processes to improve operational efficiencies. Strategic Financial Leadership: • Lead the development & implementation of the division's financial strategy in alignment with business objectives. • Advise the executive team & board on financial planning, forecasting, & risk management. • Champion projects that have broad implications across the organisation by aligning & influencing all key stakeholders. • Provide key insights to support business growth, acquisitions, & other strategic initiatives. • Participation in board, executive & insurance fund committees. Financial Management & Reporting: • Oversee accounting standards selected & implemented by the organisation. • Oversee the preparation of accurate & timely financial statements & other financial reporting in compliance with IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards) & relevant South African regulations. • Ensure the integrity of the division's financial reporting, including monthly, quarterly, & annual financial statements & other financial reports. • Identify & implement best practice management; provide strategic insight on the content of reports. • Select & drive the development of appropriate financial & operational indicators. Analysis, forecasting & budgeting: • Lead budgeting & forecasting processes & ensure financial plans are aligned with business objectives. • Set standards for business analysis to support strategic direction & comparison; develop & implement finance solutions to complex & high-value commercial decisions including risk & business delivery. Financial Process, Control, Compliance & Risk Management: • Analysis of fleet performance, funding allocation, vehicle allocation and interact with banks accordingly. • Ensure compliance with local tax laws, financial regulations, & governance standards in South Africa. • Manage internal controls & implement best practices to mitigate financial risks. • Identify & manage financial risks & ensure the development of appropriate risk management frameworks. • Oversee the auditing process & liaise with external auditors, tax department, & other regulatory bodies. • Set demanding & realistic targets for process improvement & ensure changes to existing processes are carried out in a systematic & logical way. Team Leadership & Development: • Lead, mentor, & develop the finance team, fostering a high-performance culture. • Ensure proper staff training, performance evaluations, & succession planning within the finance department. EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: • CA (SA) or ACMA CGMA; preferably worked across industries including financial services and/or fleet • Experience in managing teams in a dynamic environment. • Experience with governance, policies, & financial controls. • Strong operational finance & people management experience • Experience with business & financial systems. • Good current-day knowledge of IFRS. • Experience in a leadership role in a similar environment. • Experience in the motor or financial services industry will be advantageous. KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, & ATTRIBUTES REQUIRED: • Critical thinking & inquisitive nature. • An entrepreneurial spirit. • Strong analytical ability to interpret financial information, attention to detail. • Ability to multi-task, cope under pressure, & succeed in a high-performance culture. • Strong focus on ensuring internal controls are in place to mitigate risk & ensure the safeguarding of business assets. • Strong ability to adapt to change. • Strong verbal & written communication. • Strong MS Office skills • Clear criminal and credit records. • Own and live up to the Company values. • Attend training initiatives to improve work quality and enhance own skills
