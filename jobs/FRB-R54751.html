About the role

FirstRand is hiring an External Sales and Service Advisor Lead OBR, based in Estcourt. To drive sales performance by applying established processes to achieve individual targets within the assigned area. Build and sustain strong customer relationships through effective engagement and exceptional service delivery. Contribute to team success by consistently meeting objectives and supporting a superior customer experience.
