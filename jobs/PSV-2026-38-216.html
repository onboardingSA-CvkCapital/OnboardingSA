About the role

The Office of the Chief Justice is hiring 2 Junior Developer posts, based in National Office Midrand. This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 – R486,501 a year. How to apply: Applications can be sent via email to 2026/154/OCJ@judiciary.org.za Reference number to quote: 2026/154/OCJ Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: The Organisation will give preference to candidates in line with the Employment Equity goals.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop technical specifications for single or multiple software, design, develop and customize software solutions in line with the software development life cycle management methodology, conduct code reviews, maintain IT software solutions once they are in production environment and back up files from back end to local directories for recovery.
  • Develop test plan, test cases and communication plan, participate in the User Acceptance Testing process, prepare deployment guide for approval, report development progress, results and key metrics.
  • Deliver quality solutions in line with the business case, evaluate code to ensure it meets industry standards, apply software development principles in all developed applications, provide quality assurance report to all the relevant stakeholders and initiate Change Management procedure to deploy the developed application to production.
  • Create, maintain and review developer documentations, disseminate knowledge of existing functionality and new functional requirements.
  • Edit, write, design application content and store all Development code in the appropriate library.
