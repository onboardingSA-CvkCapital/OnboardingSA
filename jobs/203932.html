Draughtsperson, C5 Seriti, Middelburg Mine Services, Mpumalanga Internal & External Ref no: MMS091/26GS Closing date: 05 October 2026 The role: Create and maintain statutory mine plans and operational mine plans to ensure safe execution of mining and processing as determined by the Mine and Health Safety Act. Be an administering aid to the survey section for general office administration. Responsibilities will include but not limited to: • Responsible to create and maintain all statutory mine plans stipulated by the Mine Health and Safety Act, other acts applicable to mining, Code of Practices and SOPs approved by the mine • Plans to maintain, plot and distribute includes: • Annually submit statutory plans to DMR • Monthly updates of Traffic Management Plans • Monthly updates of Hazard Plan • Monthly updates of Rehabilitation Plans for mine closure • Quarterly updates of Ventilation and Rescue Plan for underground operations • As required by customers - Water Management Plans • Quarterly update of Statutory Plans stipulated by MHSA • Daily creation of ad hoc plans requested by production, engineering or processing • Monthly General Section Plans of production areas required for safe mining as applicable for the specific operation • Area of Responsibility plans required for legal appointment letters • Placing of orders for resourcing of survey equipment and filing of signed off receipts of survey notes • Responsible to assist Survey Manager & Section Surveyor in identifying and notifying operations and engineering regarding mining restrictions imposed by the MHSA for protection of features that needs protection Requirements: • Chamber of Mines Certificate in Draughting • Diploma (CAD) plus demonstrated competency in operating a GIS • Minimum of 3 years’ experience as Draughtsperson / Junior Surveyor • Member of the GIS fraternity & membership of IMSSA • In depth knowledge of design standards • Knowledge of prescriptions of statutory plans in MHSA • Experience and knowledge to convert sketches in working drawings • Experience in CAD / MicroStation software • Computer literacy in MS Office Suite • Valid driver’s license • In possession of or able to obtain a valid certificate of fitness Advantageous: • Transferring CAD data from survey records onto required mine and statutory plans Please note: • Please use Google Chrome as default browser when applying to enable the attachment of documents • A CV with certified copies (certification within a 3-month period) of relevant qualifications, together with ID document & valid driver’s license must be accompanied with your application • Appointments will be made in line with Employment Equity targets • All applicants are thanked for their interest • Applications which have not been responded to within 30 days of closing date should be regarded as unsuccessful • Communication will be with shortlisted candidates only
