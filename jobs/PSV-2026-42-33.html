About the role

The Department of Higher Education and Training is hiring 2 Senior Provisioning Officer: Supply Chain, Asset and Fleet Management posts, based in Gauteng Community Education and Training College (Ormonde). This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applications can be hand delivered to Gauteng CET College, 100 Northern Parkway, Ormonde 209, Crown Wood Office Park Block D or submit to the following email quoting the reference number to: SPO01@GP.CETC.edu.za Reference number to quote: GPCETC01/10/2026 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 12h00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Applications must be submitted on Z83 form obtainable from any Public Service Department or on the internet at www.gov.za/Documents and must be accompanied by a recently updated comprehensive CV (With Three Contactable References). A fully completed and signed Z83 form and a detailed Curriculum Vitae will be considered (PDF document to a maximum of 10 megabytes). The College will not be responsible for server delays. Only shortlisted candidates will be required to submit certified documents/copies of qualifications and other relevant documents to support the application on or before the day of the interviews. It is the applicant’s responsibility to have foreign qualifications evaluated by the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA). Candidates whose appointments promote representativity in terms of race, gender, and disability will receive preference. The candidate(s) will be required to sign an annual performance agreement, disclose his/her financial interests, and be subjected to security clearance. If you have not been contacted within three (3) months of the closing date of this advertisement, please accept that your application was unsuccessful. Suitable candidates will be subjected to personnel suitability checks (criminal record check, citizenship verification, qualification/study verification, and previous employment verification). Applications received after the closing date will not be considered. Gauteng CET College is committed to providing equal opportunities and practising affirmative action. Responsibilities

  • Coordinate procurement planning, specifications and purchase requisitions.
  • Facilitate quotations, bid administration, purchase orders and supplier management.
  • Monitor contract implementation, compliance, renewals and expiry dates.
  • Coordinate asset acquisition, recording, verification, transfers and disposal.
  • Maintain and reconcile the asset register with financial records.
  • Coordinate vehicle allocation, maintenance, licensing, insurance and utilisation.
  • Monitor fuel consumption, vehicle expenditure, logbooks and fleet compliance.
  • Monitor adherence to SCM, asset and fleet management policies and procedures.
  • Prepare SCM, asset and fleet reports, audit schedules and supporting documents.
  • Supervise personnel, allocate duties and monitor performance.
  • Liaise with suppliers, service providers and internal stakeholders.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 (Matric) or equivalent qualification.
  • National Diploma (NQF Level 6) or Bachelor's Degree (NQF Level 7) in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Financial Management, Public Management or a relevant qualification.
  • At least 2 to 3 years' relevant experience in supply chain, asset or fleet management.
  • Supervisory experience will be an added advantage.
  • Knowledge of the PFMA, Treasury Regulations, PPPFA and applicable SCM prescripts.
  • Knowledge of demand, acquisition and contract management processes.
  • Understanding of asset management, asset registers, verification and disposal procedures.
  • Knowledge of fleet administration, vehicle maintenance, licensing and utilisation monitoring.
  • Knowledge of procurement and financial management systems.
  • Computer literacy, particularly MS Excel, Word and Outlook.
  • Valid driver's license.
  • Procurement and contract administration skills.
  • Asset and fleet management skills.
  • Supervisory and team coordination skills.
  • Planning, organising and analytical skills.
  • Financial administration and reporting skills.
  • Problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Attention to detail, integrity and accountability.
