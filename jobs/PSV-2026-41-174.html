About the role

The Department of E-Government (Gauteng) is hiring a Deputy Director: Transversal Support in ICT Operations, based in Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: To apply for the below positions, please submit your application strictly online at http://professionaljobcentre.gpg.gov.za. No hand-delivery or emailed applications will be accepted. Reference number to quote: REFS/057157 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the design and implementation of Microsoft Host Integration Server (HIS).
  • Lead the architecture and configuration of HIS within e-Government (e- Gov) to support transversal systems, ensuring seamless integration and support.
  • Manage the evaluation and improvement of HIS Configuration.
  • Manage and make certain that there is system compatibility and stability.
  • Manage and anticipated downtimes.
  • Execute daily diagnostic protocols to assess and address technical anomalies before escalation.
  • Manage and update software regularly to optimize system performance, including implementing changes when directed by National Treasury.
  • Manage parallel setup to minimize disruption for government clients during a full system rebuild when there is a system transition and configuration.
  • Advice key stakeholders of critical system anomalies and mitigate protocols to ensure operational continuity.
  • Conduct post-migration reviews to confirm system stability and user satisfaction.
  • Maintain uninterrupted system integration to optimise network performance and reliability for transversal systems.
  • Administer technical support to Gauteng Provincial Government (GPG) Departments.
  • Manage the maintenance and monitoring of users’ access to Host Integration Server (HIS) connections and configuration.
  • Adhere to National Treasury directives, through compliance with National Treasury Standards.
  • Manage audit readiness and data integrity for transversal systems like Basic Accounting System (BAS) and Personnel and Salary Administration System (PERSAL).
  • Oversee HIS Migration of upgrades and updates.
  • Collaborate with State information technology information agency (SITA) to plan and manage the migration process, to make certain there is minimal disruption to critical transversal systems.
  • Ensure continuous conformity to established technical and operational standards to mitigate compliance, reflecting the latest best practices and security requirements as per guidance from National Treasury and SITA mainframe administrators.
  • General supervision of employees.
  • Allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by supervisees.
  • Oversee the workflow processes and deadlines of staff.
  • Manage performance, conduct and discipline of supervisees.
  • Ensure that all supervisees are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively.

Requirements

  • Qualification in Information Technology/Computer Science at NQF level 7 as recognized by SAQA.
  • 3 years first level/middle management (ASD level) experience in Host Integration Server (HIS) administration environment.
