About the role

We are looking for an experienced, strategically minded Head of Service Operations & Transformation to take ownership of our end-to-end service operation across Africa and lead the transformation of how we deliver repairs, after-sales support and customer service. This is a senior Head of Department opportunity for an individual who combines Industrial Engineering expertise, operational leadership, commercial acumen and transformation capability with the ability to manage complex, multi-site operations across multiple African markets. You will be responsible for building a scalable, efficient and commercially sustainable service operating model while ensuring that our operations consistently deliver an exceptional customer experience and meet Apple Authorised Service Provider standards. As the Head of Service Operations & Transformation, you will provide strategic and operational leadership across the iStore Africa service network. You will own the complete service journey – from customer collection and diagnostics through repair, parts management, logistics and cross-border movement to final delivery and customer recovery. A key focus of the role will be to assess the existing operating model, identify inefficiencies and opportunities, redesign processes and implement scalable solutions that improve productivity, turnaround times, quality, cost, customer experience and commercial performance. This is an opportunity to shape and scale a critical function across Africa while working with world-class technology brands and diverse teams and markets.
