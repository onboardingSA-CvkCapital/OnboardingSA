About the role

The Reservationist is responsible for managing all guest reservations for the game lodge, ensuring a seamless booking experience from the initial enquiry through to guest arrival. The successful candidate will provide exceptional customer service, maintain accurate reservation records, coordinate guest itineraries, and work closely with operational departments to ensure every guest enjoys a memorable safari experience.
