About the role

Pick n Pay is hiring a Product Owner, based in Kenilworth, Cape Town. We're looking for a Product Owner to take ownership of one of the product areas within the Customer App domain. Reporting into the Customer Applications Product Lead, you will own the vision, roadmap and backlog for your area, and work closely with engineering, design, data and business teams to deliver features that create measurable value for our customers and the business. You will be the voice of the customer and the business within your delivery team, translating strategy into well-defined, prioritised work, making clear trade-off decisions, and ensuring that what we ship performs in production and delivers against the agreed KPIs. The specific product area for each appointment is set out in the Role Focus section below. Grade 12/Matric Degree or diploma in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Marketing or a related field (beneficial but not required) Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) or equivalent certification preferred Working knowledge of tools such as Jira, Confluence and product analytics platforms is advantageous

Responsibilities

  • Define, communicate and maintain a clear product vision and roadmap for your product area, aligned to the Customer App strategy, business goals and OKRs.
  • Own and prioritise the product backlog, balancing new features, continuous improvement, technical debt and business-critical changes.
  • Translate business and customer needs into clear epics, user stories and acceptance criteria, working with Systems Analysts and Architects on solution design.
  • Drive Agile delivery by working closely with engineering teams.
  • Champion data-driven decision-making, using customer insight, analytics, experimentation and market and competitor research to guide direction.
  • Define, track and report on product KPIs, monitoring the performance of key customer journeys and taking corrective action where they underperform.
  • Work with business stakeholders in Loyalty, Marketing, Commercial, Finance and Customer Services to launch features, drive adoption and support go-to-market activities.
  • Manage relationships with external partners and service providers that form part of your product area, including integration, performance and commercial considerations.
  • Ensure your product area complies with relevant regulations, data privacy (POPIA), security and payment standards, and Pick n Pay policies.
  • Coordinate releases with the wider app team, communicating release content and key changes clearly to stakeholders.
  • Work with engineering and support teams on the stability, scalability and operability of your products, including incident follow-up and root-cause actions.

Requirements

  • 3 to 5+ years' experience as a Product Owner or Product Manager, ideally on a consumer mobile app or eCommerce platform.
  • Experience in loyalty, digital marketing, CRM or Value Added Services / digital payments (aligned to the role focus) is highly advantageous.
  • A proven track record of developing and executing product roadmaps and delivering measurable customer and business outcomes.
  • Strong experience in writing user stories and acceptance criteria and managing a backlog within an Agile (Scrum or Kanban) environment.
  • A strong background in measuring product performance, defining success metrics and using data to make decisions.
  • Experience working with third-party partners, platforms and APIs.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to balance customer, commercial and technical considerations.
  • Outstanding communication, negotiation and influencing abilities, with confidence engaging senior stakeholders.
  • A customer-first mindset with a strong appreciation for user experience and accessibility.
  • Experience in retail, grocery or omnichannel environments will be advantageous.
