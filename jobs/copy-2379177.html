About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AIpowered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined,future-fit role. We are looking for passionate, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace datadriven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job To plan, execute, and deliver robust, meaningful and engaging experiences that align to the annual experiential plan and are underpinned by SAICA’s Customer Experience (CeX) and strategic objectives, contributing to enhanced member experience and brand loyalty. This role involves managing all logistical and operational aspects of assigned projects, collaborating with internal and external stakeholders, and ensuring seamless execution, while providing guidance and support to Experiential Marketing Assistants and departmental interns.
