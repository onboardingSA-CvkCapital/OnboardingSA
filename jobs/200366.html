About the role

At Cell C, we are not just a telecommunications company; we are a people-centric and consumer-focused organization committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our customers. In line with our dedication to customer-centricity, we are seeking a seasoned professional as a Lead Specialist: Solutions Delivery & Testing to join a dynamic team of #Unstoppables. Purpose of Role: To manage and execute specific business requirements through both Business as Usual (BAU) and release processes, by engaging with various business departments to align requirements, propose solutions and facilitate testing and User Acceptance Testing (UAT), ensuring the successful integration and delivery of projects.
