About the role

SAB is hiring a QUALITY MANAGER, based in Polokwane Brewery. The key purpose is manage, lead and support the Quality Assurance team and to support internal customers (Plant Management S&D, and Manufacturing and Innovation/marketing) so that product quality and integrity are maintained across the value stream and business is conducted in an environmentally friendly and safe manner.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain safe, healthy and risk-free working environment
  • Implement safety, health/environmental procedures and legislation
  • Maintain safety and housekeeping standards
  • Manage Human Resources
  • Develop employees
  • Ensure a positive employee relations climate
  • Manage and apply personnel practices
  • Quality control governance for the brewery
  • Standards are in place and well understood
  • Training is done against the specified methods
  • Verification of the execution of the procedures to ensure that they comply with the standards
  • Ensure that equipment is calibrated and is functioning as per original standard
  • Highlight of any deviations to the Brewing manager, Packaging manager and Plant manager for action and stopping of the process when necessary
  • Manage quality control environment
  • Conform to value chain requirements
  • Manage QC administration
  • Optimise the availability of measuring equipment
  • Maintain and control consumable stock levels
  • Optimise quality control management system
  • Optimise team performance
  • Facilitate effective team dynamics
  • Maintain performance management system
  • Communicate effectively in the workplace
  • Lead Quality strategy as per VPO
  • Improve quality compliance and management on site and reduce reputational risks for the site
  • Achieve VPO targets and Quality KPI and PI targets for the site
  • Key Attributes and Competencies
  • Assertive and analytical
  • Proactive
  • Proven leadership ability
  • Good understanding of the principles and practices of Manufacturing Excellence

Requirements

  • Knowledge of Core Laboratory practices
  • Communication / interpersonal skills to communicate at all levels
  • Conceptual thinker. Analytical. High level of numeracy
  • Problem solving
  • Conducts audits, interprets results and disseminates information to ensure operational efficiently
  • Sets and manages department costs within budget constrains
  • Ensures safety standards are adhered to
  • Develops , monitors and controls the QA department
  • Develops Quality procedures and systems for approval and subsequent implementation
  • Develops and manages the Supplier Partnership Programme
  • Provides leadership in the area of the application of quality principles and techniques across the value chain
  • Minimum requirements
  • Bachelor of Science in Chemistry, Biochemistry and Microbiology
  • 2- 3 Years in Laboratory, Brewing or Packaging operation
  • Understanding of process control within a brewing and packaging process environment
