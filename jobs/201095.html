Key Responsibilities Assist customers in identifying and selecting the appropriate automotive parts and accessories based on their needs. - Maintain an in-depth knowledge of the product lines, including features, specifications, and applications. - Process sales transactions accurately and efficiently, ensuring a positive customer experience. - Manage inventory levels, including ordering, receiving, and stocking products, while ensuring that the display is organized and attractive. - Provide excellent after-sales support, addressing customer inquiries and resolving issues in a timely manner. - Collaborate with team members to enhance overall sales performance and share insights on customer preferences and market trends. - Prepare quotations and proposals for customers as needed, ensuring clarity and competitiveness. - Conduct regular follow-ups with customers to build loyalty and encourage repeat business. Job Requirements Demonstrable experience in automotive parts sales or a related field, such as retail sales or customer service. - Strong knowledge of automotive parts, systems, and repair processes. - Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to engage effectively with diverse customers. - Proven track record of achieving sales targets and contributing to team success. - Proficient in using point-of-sale (POS) systems and basic computer applications for processing orders and inventory management. - Ability to work in a fast-paced environment while maintaining a high level of accuracy and attention to detail. Preferred Qualifications - Certification or formal training in automotive technology or parts management is an advantage. - Experience with inventory management software or systems. - Bilingual proficiency in English and Afrikaans or another local language is preferred. - Knowledge of current trends in the automotive industry, including emerging technologies and eco-friendly options.
