About the role

Capitec is hiring a Department Head: Client Value Management, based in Johannesburg. Lead and champion the strategic design, optimisation, and governance of client value and experience frameworks within Capitec Bank's Emerging Markets division. Drive the seamless translation of business objectives into actionable plans that foster innovation, enhance customer satisfaction, and improve competitive positioning. This role requires inspiring collaborative, cross-functional teams to deliver solutions that align with organisational strategies, ensuring operational excellence, regulatory compliance, and continuous team development.
