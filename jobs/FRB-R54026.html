About the role

FirstRand is hiring a Business Development Manager, based in Johannesburg. Ashburton Investments is a new generation investment manager that offers investors access to more sources of return and more ways to manage risk, within the South African retail framework. We pride ourselves by offering solutions that support business strategies in the reallocation of investment capital, importantly in the face of increasing market and regulatory pressures, that are in the interest of the clients who chose to partner with us. getting those that are not saving to save, getting those who are saving to save more effectively, and helping to fund Africa Ashburton Investments has built a successful brand in both the retail and institutional market for both listed and unlisted capabilities. The business has seen exponential growth in assets under management since inception in 2013 to over R156bn. The business wishes to further capitalize on the strong positioning in the market by continuing to attract assets from retail clients into various asset classes; Equities, Multi Asset, Fixed Income and offshore to credit, LDI and other. For this purpose, Ashburton Investments now seeks to deepen their sales effort by appointing a retail Investment specialist. The individual will form part of the Institutional sales function for Ashburton Investments focusing on Investment Consultants, Multi Managers, Retirement Funds and Medical Schemes.
