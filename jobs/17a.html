About the role

This is not for a call centre. This role, known by the company as "Internal Customer Representative" is a mult-skilling one that  supports the sales & purchasing departments.  Your proven experience should be customer / processing of orders focused with elements of procurement / import / export.  Whilst English is the main language used, fluent French would be advantageous but not essential. Orientation towards an industrial / manufacturing environment & previous experience in the compressor industry would be a plus. Based in ALRODE, an industrial area in Alberton, Johannesburg South & transport must not be an issue. A strong work ethic, performance driven with stable work history are valued attributes. This closely-knit successful SA team value their people and have an extremely low turnover, and are part of a large global. Remuneration - Approximately R25-R28k gross basic + Excellent benefits i.e., Co. contributes 50% of medical aid up to Discovery Cassic Priority regardless of dependents; Co. contributes up to 10.5% of basic n terms of retirement fund; guaranteed 13th + discretionary additional bonus based on individual & Co.'s performance up to 12% of  annual salary. Overview - Role requires an individual who communicates effectively with customers, suppliers & colleagues, addresses their concerns and ensures a seamless exerience with their products and services. Strong problem-solving skills & a customer-centric approach is necessary. You would be performing clerical / administrative support duties for the sales & purchasing departments. Prepare & distribute various sales reports when required. Maintain customer & supplier files, assist sales team & handle inquiries about products or services. Project a professional company image through all interactions. Key Responsibilities: Customer Interation -  Respond to customer inquiries via phone, email and online channels regarding required services and parts. Check & assist with reviewing internal costings leading to preparing customer quotations. Supplier Interaction - Obtain RFQ's from suppliers based on customer requirements. Respond to vendor inquiries via phone, email and online channels. Prepare Vendor Purchase Orders, monitor supplier deliveries. Order Management -  Process customer orders, ensuring accuracy in product specifications, delivery dates, Incoterms and payment details. (Capture orders and goods received notes on relevant systems, eg: SAP/Service Next. Procure spares, co-ordinate deliveries, Raise, send & save documentation. Import & Export Control - Prepare & collate required documentation and deal with freight forwarders to obtain Import Licenses, enure that correct Inco-terms are being applied. Issue Resolution - Handle customer complaints or issues, escalating to the appropriate department when necessary, while maintaining a professional and positive demeanor. Sales Support - Assist with upselling parts, service agreements, and additional products based on customer needs. Documentation - Maintain accurate records of customer interactions, transactions, inquiries, and complaints in relevant systems. Collaboration - Work closely with the sales, technical support, and stores teams to ensure timely and accurate delivery of products and services as well as controlling/maintaining stock levels in relation to open sales orders and customer contracts. Liase with the workshop  regarding processing of Job cards/picking lists and production orders. Follow-up - Proactively follow up on customer satisfaction, order status and any post-purchase support needs.
