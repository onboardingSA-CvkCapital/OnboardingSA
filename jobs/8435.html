About the role

Introduction Komatsu is seeking an experienced Millwright Artisan to join their dynamic team at Mogalakwena. The preferred candidate should have at least 3 years of post-trade experience on heavy earthmoving equipment within the mining industry and a relevant Millwright Trade certificate. Job Specification • Remains up to date with health and safety procedures. • Participates in the planning of health and safety events and campaigns, taking SHE into account in the planning of work output. • Conducts hazard identification and risk assessment. • Implement the Komatsu Way 5S sorting, organizing, cleaning, ordering, and training. • Adheres to SHE policies and procedures. • Complies with and instructs colleagues and subordinate staff in the wearing of protective gear and clothing. • Participates in workplace safety events. • Stay informed about emergency first aid kits/fire extinguishers and evacuation procedures. • Operates any vehicles (trucks, cars, forklifts) within safety standards and procedures, even when not supervised. • Checks that the safety signs and signals are in place. • Implements safety awareness in all activities. • Reports hazardous situations, incidents, or accidents. • Provided feedback on the effectiveness of safety events. • Reports equipment that is not in good working order. • Reports on vehicle usage and incidents. • Plans for health checks and inspection of equipment. • Informs the leadership of the work steps and technical issues. • Conducts visual machine/equipment inspection – health condition, breakdowns. • Conduct general inspection in terms of appearance, damage, or missing components. • Inspect for leakages, check machine operations, check rust, check for wrong or missing parts, download machine data, identify cracks visually, take samples (oil, coolants, etc.) • Test and adjust faulty components/parts/systems. • Monitors the functionality of the component/equipment and progress made. • Reports inspection results, as well as any abnormalities or damage. • Reports problems and issues to the manager. • Complete daily activity and service reports. • Provide feedback regarding continuous improvement/suggestions to the manager. • Obtain necessary consumables items/parts/tools from the warehouse via approved procedures. • Apply judgment in the replacement or re-use of parts. • Gain technical advice or approval to replace a part/component. • Execute mechanical/electrical repairs. Clean equipment/parts/component. • Perform basic welding repairs. • Plans for service of equipment regarding date, workshops, nominations of service, stock, and other resources. • Assess work/materials/items/parts required to resolve problems. • Plans and prepares for anticipated service calls in conjunction with leadership. • Plan maintenance for equipment in conjunction with leadership. • Execute services on machines. • Obtain technical advice on complex repairs. • Checks returns unused consumables stock and parts into stock. • Reports service results as well as any abnormalities or damage. • Obtains relevant manuals and resources and makes this available for execution of work. • Checks that tools/equipment required for work are available and in good working order. • Remains abreast of changes/decisions implemented about key issues/problems/improvement. • Orders parts and consumables as per plan. • Executes overhaul of equipment according to the procedure. • Inspect equipment for compliance and quality. • Reports on overhaul job statistics, problems, issues, and product feedback. • Complete the component changeout form, commissioning report and customer feedback, and warranty certificates. • Plans troubleshooting process of equipment. • Conduct fault finding. • Collects facts about breakdowns. • Measure and compare readings – gauges, multimeters, heat guns, etc. to specifications. • Identify abnormalities using five senses. • Obtain technical advice on complex troubleshooting. • Check specification values against actual values and recommends repair solutions.
