About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies has an opportunity available for a Print Production Manager for their Head Office.The Print Production Manager is responsible for the end-to-end production, finishing, and distribution of marketing materials across the Dis-Chem ecosystem, including retail, pharmacy, clinics, insurance, promotions, and activations/events. The role leads a multi-functional production team, ensuring creative is accurately adapted, produced, finished, and delivered to the correct locations for the correct channels. The role works in close coordination with the Creative Studio Traffic Manager to ensure seamless workflow from creative development into production, aligning timelines, priorities, and resource allocation. The role also drives operational excellence across print production, including substrate selection, cost management, wastage reduction, and process optimisation.
