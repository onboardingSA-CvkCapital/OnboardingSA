About the role

The Department of Infrastructure Development (Gauteng) is hiring a Chief Director: Human Resource Management in Human Resources Management, based in Head Office (Johannesburg). This is a permanent post, paying R1,554,696 a year. Reference number to quote: REFS/057327 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Oversee the recruitment, selection, appointment, transfer, promotion, acting appointment and onboarding of employees.
  • Oversee the administration of conditions of service, including leave, housing allowance, medical aid, pension benefits, overtime, service terminations and related employee benefits.
  • Oversee accurate and complete personnel records and service histories.
  • Oversee human resource information, payroll services and PERSAL system control.
  • Oversee the monitoring of human resources administration data and reports to support workforce planning and management decision-making.
  • Oversee the improvement of human resources administration processes and systems for enhanced efficiency and service delivery.
  • Oversee collective bargaining and engagement with recognised labour unions and bargaining structures.
  • Oversee grievances, disciplinary cases, misconduct investigations, disputes, incapacity and poor performance matters.
  • Oversee the coordination of conciliation, mediation and arbitration processes and represent the Department where required.
  • Oversee the monitoring of labour relations trends and implementation of measures to reduce disputes and legal risks.
  • Oversee the promotion of sound employee relations and effective conflict resolution processes across the Department.
  • Advise Executive Management on labour relations matters.
  • Oversee the implementation and monitoring of the Performance Management and Development System (PMDS).
  • Oversee the development and implementation of the Workplace Skills Plan and Annual Training report.
  • Oversee training and development programmes for employees at all levels.
  • Oversee career development, mentoring, coaching and succession planning initiatives.
  • Oversee skills development programmes, bursaries, internships and learnerships.
  • Oversee the monitoring of compliance with Skills Development legislation and SETA requirements.
  • Oversee the evaluation of the impact of training and development interventions on organisational performance.
  • Oversee human resources planning, staffing forecasts, vacancy analysis and workforce reporting.
  • Oversee organisational design, structural reviews and job evaluation processes.
  • Oversee the provision of Employee Health and Wellness Programmes, including counselling, health promotion, trauma support and work-life balance initiatives.
  • Oversee organisational culture and change management interventions.
  • Oversee the coordination of human resources management responses to audit findings and any legislative queries.
  • Oversee the monitoring and reporting on the implementation of human resources planning, organisational design and wellness programmes.
  • Oversee the development of policies, strategies, and plans related to recruitment, appointments, service benefits, conditions of service, leave administration, termination processes, personnel records, labour relations, learning and development, skills development, career management and human resources planning, employee wellness, organisational design and change management.
  • Oversee the alignment of the unit’s strategic priorities and work plans with those of the Department.
  • Oversee the preparation of annual operation plans.
  • Oversee the implementation of risk and mitigation plans.
  • Oversee the preparation and presentation of management reports for human resources management.
  • Oversee written contributions to departmental quarterly progress reports, Annual Report, SCOPA, Budget speeches, Annual Performance Plan, etc. Oversee compliance with the Public Service Act, Public Service regulations, Basic Conditions of Employment Act and other applicable prescripts.
  • Manage performance agreements for subordinates based on approved Job Descriptions, Strategic and Annual Performance Plans of the Department.
  • Manage subordinates and maintain discipline.
  • Manage training and development of subordinates according to agreed training interventions.
  • Develop and update the service delivery and work plan for subordinates.
  • Manage funds in the unit in compliance with Public Finance Management Act and the Treasury Regulations.
  • Monitor under spending or overspending by the unit in terms of the departmental budget allocations.

Requirements

  • An undergraduate qualification at NQF Level 7 in Human Resources Management/Human Resources Development/Labour Relations/Industrial Psychology/Public Administration/Public Management/Management Services/Organisational Development/Operations Management.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at senior managerial level in the human resources management environment.
  • A valid driver’s license.
  • A Pre-Entry SMS Certificate (Nyukela) submitted prior to appointment.
