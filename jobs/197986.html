About the role

IT Intern – 12 Month Internship Programme Location: Sommerset West Duration: 12 Months Stipend: R5,500 per month About the Opportunity We are looking for a motivated and enthusiastic IT Intern to join our team. This opportunity is ideal for a candidate who has completed a Degree in IT or a related field and is looking to gain practical workplace experience in IT support. Minimum Requirements • Completed a Degree in Information Technology (IT) or a related field. • Computer literate with good knowledge of Microsoft Office. • Basic understanding of IT support and troubleshooting. • Good communication and interpersonal skills. • Strong problem-solving skills. • Eager to learn and develop practical IT skills. • Professional, reliable, and able to work well in a team.What You'll Do • Assist with responding to and resolving IT helpdesk tickets. • Provide basic technical support to employees. • Assist with setting up computers, laptops, and other IT devices. • Assist with installing and configuring software and applications. • Perform basic troubleshooting of hardware and software issues. • Assist with user account and access-related requests. • Support the IT team with basic networking and systems support. • Assist with maintaining IT equipment and records. • Escalate complex technical issues to the relevant IT team members. • Assist with general IT administration and other duties as required.Skills and Attributes • Good troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. • Good attention to detail. • Strong communication skills. • Willingness to learn and take direction. • Ability to work independently and as part of a team. • Professional and customer-focused approach. • Exposure to networking or systems support will be an advantage.If you have a Degree in IT or a related field and are looking for an opportunity to gain practical workplace experience, we encourage you to apply.
