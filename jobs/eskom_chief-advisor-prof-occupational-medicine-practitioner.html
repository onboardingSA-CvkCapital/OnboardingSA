About the role

Eskom Distribution Division is looking for a Chief Advisor Prof Occupational Medicine Practitioner at task grade P18. The department is Human Capital: Health and Wellness. The location is Gauteng: Sunninghill Megawatt Park. Area of specialization is to provide strategic direction regarding occupational health and wellness matters, leadership in the implementation of occupational health and wellness services in the region and subject matter advice to business partners.
