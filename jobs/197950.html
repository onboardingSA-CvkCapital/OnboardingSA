About the role

Lead. Inspire. Deliver excellence in agri-processing. About the VKB Group The VKB Group is a proudly South African agricultural company, providing support to farmers and producing fresh, farm-to-consumer foods. Beyond our consumer brands, VKB is represented regionally by VKB, GWK, and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in farming, and over the years we’ve grown into a full-service agribusiness, operating across the food supply chain — from farm inputs to processing, logistics, and market distribution both nationally and internationally. This “house of brands,” built with the support of our farmer-shareholders, is the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. About VKB Milling (Pty) Ltd VKB Milling is a key subsidiary of VKB Agri Processors (Pty) Ltd, the agri-processing division of the VKB Group. With operations including white maize mills in Mokopane, Louis Trichardt, Mashishing, and a wheat mill and bakery in Frankfort, VKB Milling delivers world-class products and services to the South African market. We pride ourselves on continuous improvement, operational excellence, and adding meaningful value to the agricultural sector. Our employees are given opportunities to develop their skills in a challenging, rewarding, and collaborative work environment. The Role We are seeking a Dispatch Shift Supervisor to lead our Modderrivier plant’s Dispatch team. You will be responsible for supervising daily dispatch operations, ensuring compliance with quality standards, and coordinating closely with the Maintenance Manager to keep equipment running efficiently. Key Responsibilities • Supervise and manage dispatch staff to achieve daily targets and objectives. • Implement and oversee quality control checks to ensure compliance with environmental, statutory, health, and safety regulations. • Monitor packing weights, investigate discrepancies, and correct deviations. • Conduct daily, weekly, and monthly stocktaking in line with VKB procedures. • Manage claims, returned stock, and provide feedback to internal and external stakeholders. • Control stock movement and process flows for accurate accounting practices. • Complete all required administrative duties, including daily, weekly, and monthly reports. • Implement cost-saving measures across all operations. • Conduct regular team meetings to engage and motivate staff. • Build and maintain long-term relationships with internal departments, external clients, and other stakeholders. • Ensure compliance with FSSC and other relevant auditing processes. • Confirm statutory compliance in line with VKB and international standards. • Oversee daily housekeeping and maintain a safe, organized work environment. • Contribute to production and budget forecasting for the plant. • Perform scheduled basic maintenance on all plant equipment. Requirements • Grade 12 / NQF Level 4 minimum. • Computer literate. • 2–3 years’ experience in a similar supervisory role within Logistics or food production. • Clear criminal record. • Traceable references in a similar capacity. • Willingness to work overtime, standby, and night shifts. • Valid drivers' license, EC (Code 14) will be an advantageSkills & Competencies • Strong communication skills (written and verbal). • Personal resilience and adaptability. • Negotiation, planning, and organizational skills. • Effective conflict management and team leadership abilities. • Strong time management and ability to prioritize tasks. • Customer-focused and process-oriented. • Ability to work collaboratively across multiple departments and stakeholders. • Technical skills to do basic maintenanceWhy VKB?
