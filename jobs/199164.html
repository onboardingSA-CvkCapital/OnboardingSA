About the role

At Cell C, we are not just a telecommunications company; we are a people-centric and consumer-focused organization committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our customers. In line with our dedication to customer-centricity, we are seeking a seasoned professional as a Senior Manager: Service Design to join a dynamic team of #Unstoppables. Purpose of the role: Cell C is a leading telecommunications company in South Africa, committed to providing innovative solutions and exceptional service to our consumers (B2C) and business customers (B2B). Cell C is positioned as a Spirited Disruptor and values integrity, transparency, honesty, and simplicity. The Senior Manager: Service Design is accountable for defining, integrating and governing the service design strategy, standards and enterprise-wide customer journey frameworks across digital and assisted channels. The role translates customer insight, operational data, commercial priorities and technology capability into scalable service solutions that improve customer satisfaction, enable omnichannel execution and support business performance. In addition, the role is the line manager for the UX/UI design team and is accountable for the combined service design and UX/UI discipline. It sets design direction, governs design quality across all customer-facing touchpoints, and builds a single coherent design capability spanning end-to-end service journeys and the interfaces through which customers experience them. The role provides senior functional leadership across customer journeys including onboarding, sales, support, retention, billing and digital self-service. It oversees the portfolio of service design and UX/UI initiatives, drives cross-functional alignment, manages design governance and mitigates customer, operational and delivery risks across the service ecosystem. The role is transformational in nature and is expected to build service design and UX/UI capability, embed customer-centric ways of working and position design as a sustainable organisational competence within Cell C.
