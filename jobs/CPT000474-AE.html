About the role

Our client, based in Paarl, is seeking a motivated and analytical Junior Analyst Trader to join their growing team. This is an excellent opportunity for a candidate looking to build a career in financial markets, foreign exchange (FX), and trading. Key Responsibilities Conduct market research and analyse financial and economic trends. Assist with the execution and monitoring of foreign exchange transactions. Prepare market reports and trading documentation. Support trade settlements and reconciliations. Liaise with banks, clients and internal stakeholders. Ensure compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements. Maintain accurate records and reporting. Minimum Requirements Relevant tertiary qualification in Finance, Economics, Investments, Accounting or a related field. RE5 qualification (FAIS compliant) is essential. Strong analytical and numerical skills. Advanced Microsoft Excel and MS Office proficiency. Excellent communication and organisational skills. High attention to detail with the ability to work accurately under pressure. Knowledge of financial markets, foreign exchange and banking processes will be advantageous. Professional, reliable and eager to learn. The Successful Candidate Will Be Analytical and detail-oriented. Proactive and self-motivated. Able to manage multiple priorities. A strong team player with excellent interpersonal skills. Committed to maintaining confidentiality and professionalism. If you are passionate about financial markets and looking to develop your career within a dynamic trading environment, we would like to hear from you.
