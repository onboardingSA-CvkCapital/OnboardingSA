About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring an Assistant Director: Employee Health and Wellness in Corporate Services, based in Northern Cape (Kimberley). This is a permanent post, paying R487,167 a year. This is a re-advertisement, applicants who applied previously are encouraged to re-apply. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: Magistrate Court Building, 6th floor, Knight Street, Corner of Stead Street, Kimberley, 8300 or by email to Post397@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/397 Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: Coloured, Indian and White Males, and Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Implement and co-ordinate wellness services management services.
  • Provide psychosocial support services to promote employee resilience.
  • Render and promote utilisation of counselling and support services in accordance with the Service Level Agreement and referral procedure.
  • Render specialised intervention in cases of crises/trauma.
  • Promote a positive organisational culture to enhance service delivery through work-life balance and life-skills programmes.
  • Measure the impact of programmes that reduce psychosocial and physical demands of the workplace and trigger stress.
  • Promote healthy life-style programmes through nutrition, education and awareness.
  • Promote physical wellness and departmental sports and recreation activities.
  • Implement and co-ordinate health and productivity management services.
  • Coordinate/facilitate health and wellness awareness and promotional programmes.
  • Coordinate periodic health screening of employees for prevention and early detection of communicable and non-communicable illnesses.
  • Assess health risk manager's reports and advise management on Incapacity, Disability and III Health Retirement.
  • Develop and implement comprehensive toolkit for mental health promotion in the workplace.
  • Implement and promote strategies to address stigma and discrimination against mental diseases.
  • Implement Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) and Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS), Tuberculosis (TB) and Sexually Transmitted Infection (STI) management programmes.
  • Co-ordinate/facilitate HIV and AIDS, TB and STIs management programmes.
  • Coordinate HIV and AIDS peer educators training and support.
  • Implement and promote strategies to enhance access and breakdown barriers for effective HIV and AIDS, TB and STIs management programmes.
  • Distribute condoms and monitoring usage in all offices.
  • Implement a comprehensive and effective employee health and wellness administration in the province.
  • Provide management with timely feedback and progress reports on referred cases while maintaining confidentiality.
  • Support and advice management on handling employee health and wellness challenges.
  • Participate in provincial and national employee health and wellness structures.
  • Ensure statutory reporting requirements are met.
