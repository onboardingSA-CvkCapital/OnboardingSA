About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Deputy Director: Compliance Monitoring and Enforcement in Pongola-Umzimkhulu Catchment Management Agency Division: Compliance Monitoring and Enforcement, based in Durban. This is a permanent post, paying R1,101,468 a year. How to apply: Interested applicants must submit their applications via the online link https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za Reference number to quote: PUCMA 37 Closing date: 16 October 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Implement and enforce the National Water Act.
  • Ensure Implementation of compliance monitoring and enforcement policy, strategy, the standard operating procedure.
  • Develop plans for inspections and audits.
  • Ensure that compliance inspections/audits are conducted for various sector-related activities against legislation, policies, regulations and licenses.
  • Analyse and verify inspection reports.
  • Provide advice on further actions needed to be taken.
  • Provide information/support to the education and awareness component to promote compliance.
  • Ensure that all dam safety inspections are conducted, and compliance with national norms and standards.
  • Facilitate and manage investigations of non-compliance with water use license conditions and other unlawful water uses and prepare reports.
  • Implement enforcement action in the form of formal or informal warnings, administrative enforcement such as statutory notices, compliance notices and directives, criminal and court applications to enforce notices.
  • Provide advice and input on criminal, administrative and civil litigation.
  • Initiate prosecution against non-compliance through administrative enforcement.
  • Prepare necessary information, reports and evidence for court applications.
  • Assist in the development of policies and regulations.
  • Ensure liaison, cooperation and coordination with other enforcement agencies and government institutions involved in compliance and enforcement.
  • Ensure management of assets and human resources.

Requirements

  • At least 4-year degree qualification in the field of Natural Science, Environmental Law, or Environmental Science or relevant NQF level 7 qualification.
  • At least (8) 8 years’ experience in Compliance Monitoring and Enforcement, of which (5) 5 years should be at middle management level in Water Resource Management or related field.
  • Knowledge of the National Water Act, 1998, the Water Services Act, 1997, related policies, strategies and guidelines and other relevant Acts.
  • Technical knowledge and competence in project and programme management, policy and strategy development, communication/interpersonal skills, Presentation Skills, Negotiation Skills, Development Skills and General Management.
  • Willingness to work irregular hours.
  • Knowledge of human Resources policies and Public Finance Management Act.
  • A valid driver’s license.
