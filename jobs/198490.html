About the role

ROLE PURPOSE The Manager: Pricing, Solution Design & Commercial Excellence is responsible for developing commercially accurate, competitive and operationally executable Facilities Management pricing and solution models in support of tenders, renewals, strategic pursuits and client-retention opportunities. The role owns the end-to-end pricing model for complex FM opportunities, including total cost of ownership, gross profit, margin and commercial-risk validation. Working closely with the Bid Management team, Portfolio Executives, operations, procurement and finance, the incumbent translates RFP requirements, hard and soft service scopes, technical inputs and contract obligations into clear assumptions, validated cost inputs, benchmarked price outputs and commercially sound bid submissions that support profitable growth. KEY PERFORMANCE AREA End-to-End Pricing and Commercial Modelling Develop, maintain and continuously improve bottom-up pricing models, total cost of ownership models, commercial assumptions and financial submissions across the following service categories and client environments: • IFM Contracts • Technical Maintenance • Contracts • Soft Services & Workplace Services • Security & Landscaping • Public Sector and SOE bids • Corporate Real Estate Portfolios • Build complex bottom-up labour, productivity, roster, shift, equipment, consumables, subcontractor, mobilisation, technology, lifecycle and transition cost models. • Model total cost of ownership, gross profit, margin, investment/return, escalations, scenarios and sensitivity outcomes for each bid or renewal. • Take ownership and accountability for pricing accuracy, input-cost validation, output-price validation, benchmarking, market analysis and bid-deadline adherence. • Challenge assumptions and validate cost inputs from operations, procurement, technical specialists and service providers before final pricing is submitted. • Ensure final pricing is aligned to approved commercial parameters, margin
