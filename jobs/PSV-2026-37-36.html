Responsibilities

  • Implement and monitor Asset management policies and procedures in line with GRAP standards and relevant legislation.
  • Coordination of physical verification of assets.
  • Manage the Fixed Asset register (FAR) and ensure reconciliation to General ledger (GL).
  • Develop, implement and monitor acquisition, maintenance, and disposal plans for assets, and effectively implement the Asset Management Strategy within the College.
  • Manage asset write offs and disposal and ensure accurate Asset disclosure on Annual Financial Statements AFS as stipulated on GRAP.
  • Ensure the implementation and monitoring of Supply Chain policy.
  • Introduce a system for provisioning, procurement, stock control and reporting.
  • Identify preference point system and appropriate goals per commodity in terms of preferential procurement policy objectives.
  • Conduct awareness on approved SCM policies.

Requirements

  • Matric or NCV Level 4 and with a recognised three-year (3) degree/diploma in Finance Management, Asset management, Accounting or Auditing or related qualification, 3-5 years’ experience in a Financial Management environment and implementation of relevant legislation, prescripts, policies and procedures.
  • Knowledge of BAS/LOGIS & COLTECH system.
  • Knowledge of assets disposal procedure, Knowledge and understating of legislative framework governing the Public Services, Knowledge of Assets Management, Knowledge of Supply Chain Policies, Knowledge of Department of Higher Education mandate.
