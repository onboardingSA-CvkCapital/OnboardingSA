About the role

Role Overview The Service Manager – MSP is responsible for overseeing the operational health, service delivery, and customer experience across managed cloud environments within a Managed Services Provider (MSP) environment. The role sits at the intersection of technical delivery, people leadership, and commercial accountability, ensuring customers receive proactive, reliable, and value-driven cloud operations services. The successful candidate will lead engineering delivery teams, manage client relationships, and ensure operational excellence across cloud infrastructure, service management, and delivery governance. Role breakdown 50% Technical / 50% People Management (Customer & Engineering Delivery Team) Core Purpose: To ensure uptime, performance, and value delivery across our clients’ cloud environments through a structured, proactive, and people-led approach — all while delivering a unified, seamless experience as One Synthesis Team Key Responsibilities • Oversee day-to-day operations of customer cloud environments and managed services platforms. • Ensure proactive monitoring, incident management, and adherence to agreed service level agreements (SLAs). • Drive operational activities including patching, backups, disaster recovery, and platform reliability initiatives. • Monitor cloud cost performance, budget utilisation, and optimisation opportunities across client environments. • Continuously improve cloud security posture through operational enhancements and governance practices. • Act as the primary point of contact for customer relationships and operational service engagements. • Bridge technical conversations between engineering teams and customers to ensure clarity and alignment. • Guide prioritisation, risk management, and delivery assurance activities across operational teams. • Lead and manage engineering delivery teams with accountability, structure, and performance focus. • Coordinate cross-functional teams to ensure delivery timelines and operational dependencies are effectively managed. • Drive customer satisfaction and ensure a seamless end-to-end service experience. • Track and manage multiple client budgets to avoid operational leakage and ensure commercial sustainability. • Monitor engineer utilisation and alignment to contracted customer scope and service agreements. • Forecast delivery capacity, identify operational risks early, and implement proactive mitigation plans. • Collaborate with leadership teams to support sustainable growth and operational excellence initiatives. Essential Skills & Experience • Strong experience in Managed Services Provider (MSP) operations or cloud service delivery environments. • Hands-on understanding of AWS cloud infrastructure and cloud-native operations. • Strong understanding of infrastructure operations, cloud systems, and operational service management. • Experience managing incident response, operational support, and SLA-driven service environments. • Strong customer relationship management and stakeholder engagement skills. • Experience leading engineering delivery teams and coordinating cross-functional operational activities. • Strong commercial awareness including budget tracking, utilisation management, and operational forecasting. • Ability to communicate effectively between technical teams and business stakeholders. • Strong analytical, organisational, and problem-solving abilities. • Ability to manage competing priorities within fast-paced operational environments. Desirable Skills & Experience • Experience with cloud security, disaster recovery, and operational governance practices. • Exposure to DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), or cloud automation environments. • Experience managing enterprise customer environments and multi-client operations. • Exposure to ITIL or operational service management frameworks. • Experience working with cloud monitoring, observability, and operational tooling. Qualifications • Relevant degree or diploma in Information Technology, Cloud Computing, Engineering, or a related field. • AWS certification is essential. • Additional cloud or service management certifications would be advantageous. Behavioural Competencies • Strong leadership and people management capabilities. • Excellent customer-centric mindset and relationship-building skills. • Strong operational discipline and accountability. • Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement abilities. • Ability to remain calm and effective under operational pressure. • Proactive problem-solving and continuous improvement mindset. • Collaborative approach with strong team leadership capability. What We Offer • Competitive salary and benefits package. • Opportunity to lead enterprise cloud operations and managed services delivery. • Exposure to modern cloud technologies and operational environments. • Collaborative and high-performing service delivery culture. • Professional growth and leadership development opportunities. • Opportunity to contribute to meaningful customer success and operational excellence initiatives.
