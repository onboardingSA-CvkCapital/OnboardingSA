About the role

The primary responsibility of the Academic Student Counsellor is to support the academic success and well-being of students at Belgium Campus iTversity. The role involves providing counselling services, interventions, and support programs to students with academic or psychological challenges. The aim is to prevent academic failure, promote mental well-being, and ensure that students have the necessary tools to succeed in their studies, this includes assistance to students with special needs. 1.      Job overview The aim of this profile is to ensure that the role of the student counsellor here at Belgium campus ITversity is well integrated into the organizations’ strategic vision and mission. Furthermore, that as key participants in the available support services for the students studying at Belgium campus, the candidate accounts for the wellbeing of both the students’ academic progress. Ensuring that they prevent, promote, intervene and refer when appropriate to do so, as a means to adequately assist the student during the fulfilment of their academic studies. Belgium Campus is committed to offering students with disabilities, or specific needs access to all courses provided by the institution, to ensure the following: •  Assist students to fully participate, as allowed by your condition and circumstances •  Assist students to study effectively and on equal terms with other students As an institution of higher education, Belgium Campus ITversity prides itself with being the first South African institution to have a special program for deaf and disabled students. To date, we cater for students with varying disabilities, partially sighted, hard of hearing and profoundly deaf students. 2.    Duties & Responsibilities The aim of the duties and responsibilities detailed below are intended to provide a general overview of what entails the core duties that are directly linked to the professional aspect of the role. Furthermore, the general duties that highlight the additional measures that the candidate is required to fulfil. Student support refers to tutoring, mentoring, guidance and counselling to maximise academic performance and attending to students’ academic, and psychological needs – that may impinge on the students’ academic development. The education faculty, academic lecturers and the student support department are partners in the above-mentioned endeavours. a.  Core duties & Responsibilities – These include student advocacy; student welfare – this includes both social and academic wellbeing; liaison & interaction; special projects and general administration. b. General Duties & Responsibilities –Attending training for career development; be responsible for ones’ own continuing self-development; ensure good communication systems. Core duties & Responsibilities Area Outcomes Student counselling - Act as an advisor and a consultant to students on issues related to their academic development. - Provide assistance to at-risk students with academic challenges by offering guidance on tools such as study methods, time management skills and note taking skills. - Screening and identification of academic challenges through individual student counselling - Providing preventative, developmental counselling services and academic interventions for students who are at-risk. - Develop and manage intervention programmes with associated intervention programmes and processes made available by the institution. Special needs students - Assist students with equity and welfare issues that may be affecting their ability to study. - Liaise with and refer students to appropriate departments or staff for assistance. Customer interaction - Contacting the responsible person who for the students’ fees if the student is reluctant to get assistance for this at-risk academic behaviour. - Referring the students if need be, to a local educational psychologist. - Organize and facilitate student support workshops for students at all campuses. - Organize on-campus activities including one-on-one with students and group-based workshops e.g. career seminars Reporting - Provide regular reports to both management and the student support department on the following: - Quarterly sponsored student reports - The number of students that the counsellor(s) assisted and mechanisms that were utilized to assist students
