About the role

SAB is hiring a Commercial Execution Inspector, based in Bryanston. The key purpose of this role is to protect the company’s commercial investments by independently auditing and validating in-trade execution, customer discounting, promotional activities, pricing compliance and route-to-market controls. The role identifies revenue leakage, non-compliance, execution gaps and control weaknesses, and drives corrective action to ensure customer investments are applied as approved and deliver the intended commercial outcomes.

Responsibilities

  • Commercial Investment Assurance
  • Conduct risk-based audits of customer discounts, rebates, promotional funding, trade spend and commercial investment programmes.
  • Validate that customer investments are supported by approved agreements, evidence of execution and accurate claims.
  • Identify and quantify revenue leakage, over-discounting, duplicate claims, unsupported payments and unauthorised commercial spend.
  • Assess whether trade investments are implemented as intended and escalate material control exceptions.
  • In-Trade Execution Audits
  • Conduct physical audits across outlets, wholesalers, distributors and key accounts to verify agreed execution standards.
  • Assess compliance with Outlet Health, merchandising, visibility, pricing and promotional requirements.
  • Confirm that point-of-sale materials, Coolers and other commercial assets are deployed, used and maintained in line with approved agreements.
  • Validate outlet existence, customer classification and route-to-market execution where relevant.
  • Pricing and Discount Compliance
  • Audit customer pricing, discount structures, deal mechanics and promotional offers against approved mandates and governance requirements.
  • Identify deviations from approved price lists, discount thresholds and commercial approval processes.
  • Investigate suspected discount abuse, off-invoice leakage, irregular claims or other commercial control breaches.
  • Provide fact-based findings to support recovery, remediation and consequence management processes.
  • Risk Identification and Investigation
  • Use sales, volume, pricing, discount and customer profitability data to identify high-risk customers, channels, territories and transactions.
  • Conduct targeted reviews in response to anomalies, whistle-blower allegations, management concerns or recurring control failures.
  • Support Internal Audit, Compliance, Commercial Finance and Legal teams with investigations where required.
  • Maintain complete, accurate and defensible audit evidence for all findings.
  • Reporting and Corrective Action
  • Prepare clear audit reports setting out the issue, evidence, root cause, financial exposure, accountable owner and recommended action.
  • Report material findings promptly to the relevant business and control owners.
  • Maintain a tracker of findings, recovery actions and remediation commitments, and follow through to verified closure.
  • Provide trend analysis and recurring risk themes to inform commercial governance and process improvements.
  • Governance and Capability Building
  • Promote adherence to commercial policies, delegated authorities and trade investment controls.
  • Partner with Sales, Trade Marketing, Revenue Management, Commercial Finance, Internal Audit and Compliance while maintaining appropriate independence.
  • Provide practical guidance and training to commercial teams on recurring control gaps and required standards.
  • Recommend improvements to systems, processes and preventive controls based on audit findings.

Requirements

  • Key Competencies and Attributes
  • Strong understanding of FMCG sales operations, customer investment, pricing, discounting and route-to-market processes.
  • Knowledge of audit methodology, risk assessment, internal controls and evidence management.
  • Ability to analyse sales, volume, margin, pricing and discount data and translate findings into commercial risk insights.
  • Advanced Excel capability
  • experience with Power BI or other analytics tools is advantageous.
  • Strong report writing, issue articulation and presentation skills.
  • High integrity, independence and sound judgement.
  • Commercial acumen and an owner’s mindset.
  • Analytical, curious and investigative approach.
  • Courage to challenge non-compliant practices constructively.
  • Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills.
  • Attention to detail, disciplined follow-through and resilience in the field.
  • Ability to communicate clearly with operational teams and senior leaders.
  • Willingness and ability to travel frequently into trade across assigned markets and territories.
  • Ability to work independently, manage a risk-based audit schedule and meet reporting deadlines.
  • Commitment to confidentiality, objective evidence and consistent application of company standards.
  • Valid driver’s licence where required for field travel.
  • Ability to use company-provided mobile and IT equipment for evidence capture and reporting.
  • Minimum Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Auditing, Business, Commercial Management or a related discipline.
  • Professional audit, forensic, risk or compliance qualification would be advantageous.
  • Five or more years of relevant experience in FMCG sales, commercial finance, revenue management, trade marketing, internal audit, investigations or compliance.
  • Practical experience reviewing trade spend, discounts, promotional claims, customer investments or route-to-market execution.
  • Experience working in field-based environments and engaging customers, distributors and commercial teams.
