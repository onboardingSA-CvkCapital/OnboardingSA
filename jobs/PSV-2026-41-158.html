About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring an Assistant Director Management Accounting in Finance WTE CD: Financial Management Services Dir: Management Accounting, based in Pretoria Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 271126/16 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Assist area offices to compile budget forecast and tariff settings template.
  • Compile the MTEF budget.
  • Analyse, interpret and implement the treasury guidelines for the estimates of national expenditure (ENE).
  • Develop templates for the collection of budget information from line functionaries.
  • Provide variance explanations for the MTEF and ENE.
  • Assess where shifting of funds/virements is required and possible by reviewing expenditure against budget and make recommendations.
  • Assist to manage budget planning for raw setting water price, assist to ensure adequate utilisation of budget and Monitor compliance with financial prescripts.
  • Compile and maintain National Treasury budget databases, including MTEF, ENE and AENE information, ensuring accuracy, completeness and compliance with reporting requirements.
  • Process budget shifts in accordance with applicable financial procedures and confirms budget availability before transactions are processed.
  • Coordinate and consolidate variance explanations from relevant Programme Managers and ensure timely submission of supporting information.
  • Monitor and report on programme expenditure, identifying variances and expenditure trends.

Requirements

  • A relevant tertiary qualification in Financial Management at NQF level 07.
  • 3 years relevant supervisory experience (Senior State Accountant).
  • Knowledge and understanding of Human resource Management, Legislation, policies, practices and procedures.
  • Public Finance Management Act (PFMA), Treasury Regulations and guidelines.
  • Public Service Anti-Corruption Strategy and anti-corruption and fraud prevention measures.
  • Knowledge of equal opportunities and affirmative action guidelines and laws.
  • Knowledge of administrative and clerical procedures and systems.
  • Departmental policies and procedures.
  • Governmental financial systems.
  • Principles and practice of financial accounting.
  • Framework for managing performance information.
  • Business strategy transaction and alignment.
  • Problem solving and Analysis.
  • People and diversity management.
  • Client orientation and customer focus.
  • Good Communication skills.
  • Accountability and ethical conduct.
