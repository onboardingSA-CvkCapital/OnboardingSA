About the role

At Cell C, we are not just a telecommunications company; we are a people-centric and consumer-focused organization committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our customers. In line with our dedication to customer-centricity, we are seeking a seasoned professional as a Executive Enterprise Products Services & Partnerships to join a dynamic team of #Unstoppables. Purpose of Role: To build, scale, and commercially deliver Cell C’s Enterprise product portfolio from the ground up, targeting SME, Corporate, and Government segments. This is a greenfield mandate requiring the establishment of a fully operational Enterprise product capability — including enterprise product development, partner ecosystem ownership, and DSP/content monetisation. Accountable for delivery against a R130M Enterprise revenue ambition from a near-zero base. Purpose of the Role In addition, the role is accountable for the end-to-end ownership and monetisation of the Enterprise partner ecosystem, including Digital Services and Content (DSP) portfolios. This includes defining partner strategy, structuring commercial models, and driving revenue realisation across partner-delivered and content-based products and services. The role owns a complex, interdependent portfolio of 20+ concurrent product streams across 10+ partners, spanning connectivity, unified communications, cloud, cybersecurity, IoT, digital services, VAS and content monetisation. This includes managing multiple commercial models across infrastructure-led, partner-led, and content-led revenue streams, requiring orchestration across technical, commercial, regulatory, and operational domains to ensure successful commercialisation and sustainable revenue growth. As part of the Executive Leadership Team (ELT), the role is responsible for leadership, performance management, and creating an environment that enables execution of the Growth Division’s strategic objectives.
