About the role

We are looking for an energetic, reliable and customer-focused Retail Sales Representative to join our Pretoria team. The successful candidate will assist customers, drive sales and take responsibility for the smooth day-to-day operation of the store. This position is ideal for someone who enjoys working with people, takes pride in keeping a store organised and can confidently handle sales, stock and administrative duties. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Store Opening and Closing • Open and close the store according to company procedures. • Ensure the store is clean, organised and ready for customers before opening. • Switch on and prepare point-of-sale systems, card machines, lighting and other equipment. • Conduct opening and closing security checks. • Ensure doors, windows, gates and security systems are properly secured. • Activate and deactivate the alarm system when required. • Complete daily opening and closing checklists. • Ensure all cash, stock and equipment are safely secured at the end of the day. • Report security concerns, suspicious activity or maintenance problems immediately. Customer Service and Sales • Welcome and assist walk-in customers in a friendly and professional manner. • Identify customers’ needs and recommend suitable products. • Provide accurate information about products, pricing, availability and promotions. • Demonstrate products and explain their features and benefits. • Prepare quotations and assist customers with purchasing decisions. • Process customer orders accurately and efficiently. • Follow up on quotations, enquiries, orders and outstanding customer requests. • Handle telephone, email, WhatsApp and social media enquiries where required. • Build positive relationships with new and existing customers. • Promote additional products through upselling and cross-selling. • Work towards individual and store sales targets. • Inform customers about promotions, new products and special offers. • Resolve customer complaints calmly and professionally. • Process returns, refunds and exchanges according to company policy. • Maintain a high standard of customer service at all times. Cashier and Point-of-Sale Duties • Process cash, card, EFT and other approved payments. • Operate the point-of-sale system accurately. • Issue receipts, invoices and credit notes where applicable. • Ensure prices and discounts are correctly captured. • Maintain accurate records of all transactions. • Count and balance the cash register at the beginning and end of each shift. • Complete daily cash-ups and investigate discrepancies. • Prepare cash and supporting documents for banking. • Safeguard cash, card machines and store financial information. • Follow all company procedures relating to cash handling and fraud prevention. Stock Ordering and Receiving • Monitor stock levels and identify items that need to be reordered. • Prepare and submit stock orders to approved suppliers. • Follow up with suppliers regarding outstanding orders and delivery dates. • Receive stock deliveries and check goods against purchase orders and supplier invoices. • Confirm that quantities, product descriptions and prices are correct. • Inspect delivered stock for damage, defects or missing items. • Report delivery discrepancies and damaged stock immediately. • Capture received stock accurately on the stock or point-of-sale system. • Label, price and display new stock. • Ensure stock is stored safely and neatly. • Maintain organised stockroom and storage areas. • Keep supplier documentation, delivery notes and invoices properly filed. Stock Control and Stocktaking • Conduct daily, weekly, monthly and annual stock counts. • Perform regular cycle counts on high-value and fast-moving products. • Compare physical stock with system records. • Investigate stock shortages, overages and other discrepancies. • Report stock losses, theft, breakages and damaged items. • Maintain accurate stock movement records. • Monitor slow-moving, obsolete and expired stock. • Assist with stock transfers between branches or departments. • Ensure returns to suppliers are properly documented and processed. • Help prevent stock shrinkage through careful monitoring and loss-control procedures. • Maintain accurate records of demo units, reserved stock and customer orders. Merchandising and Store Presentation • Keep shelves, displays, counters and customer areas clean and organised. • Replenish stock on the sales floor throughout the day. • Ensure products are correctly priced and clearly labelled. • Arrange merchandise in an attractive and customer-friendly manner. • Set up promotional displays and in-store marketing material. • Remove damaged, expired or unsuitable products from display. • Ensure displays comply with company branding and merchandising standards. • Keep the entrance, sales floor, counter and stockroom presentable. • Assist with general cleaning and housekeeping duties. • Report damaged fixtures, equipment or signage. Administration and Reporting • Complete daily sales, stock and cash reports. • Maintain accurate customer, order and quotation records. • Capture customer information correctly and protect confidential data. • File invoices, receipts, delivery notes and stock documents. • Keep records of returns, exchanges, damaged goods and supplier claims. • Assist with weekly and monthly sales reporting. • Monitor progress against sales targets. • Communicate important store information during shift handovers. • Report customer feedback, recurring complaints and product requests to management. • Assist with promotional activities, campaigns and store events. • Perform other reasonable retail and administrative duties when required. Store Safety and Loss Prevention • Follow all health, safety and security procedures. • Remain alert to possible theft, fraud and suspicious behaviour. • Ensure high-value products are securely displayed and stored. • Report accidents, hazards and security incidents. • Keep emergency exits and walkways clear. • Follow company procedures for handling keys, passwords and alarm codes. • Protect company property, customer information and confidential records. • Support a safe and professional environment for customers and colleagues. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS • Grade 12/Matric. • Previous retail sales, customer service or store assistant experience is preferred. • Experience with point-of-sale systems and cash handling will be advantageous. • Basic knowledge of stock ordering, stock receiving and stocktaking. • Good verbal and written communication skills. • Confident, friendly and professional when dealing with customers. • Basic computer skills, including email and Microsoft Office. • Strong numerical and administrative skills. • Ability to work accurately with cash, invoices and stock records. • Ability to work independently and as part of a team. • Willingness to work retail hours, including Saturdays, weekends and public holidays when required. • Reliable transport to and from the workplace. • Must be punctual, trustworthy and responsible. THE IDEAL CANDIDATE We are looking for someone who is: • Sales-driven and customer-focused. • Honest, dependable and well organised. • Energetic and willing to take initiative. • Presentable and professional. • Detail-oriented and accurate. • Able to multitask in a busy retail environment. • Comfortable taking responsibility for store opening and closing. • Able to remain calm when handling complaints or difficult situations. • Committed to achieving sales targets and maintaining excellent store standards.
