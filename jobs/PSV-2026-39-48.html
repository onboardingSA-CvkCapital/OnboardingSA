About the role

The Government Printing Works is hiring an Artisan (Production): Equipment Maintenance Mechanical, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R253,341 a year. How to apply: Link: GPW 26/025 Reference number to quote: GPW 26/025 Closing date: 17 November 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure and carry out efficient maintenance and repair of all equipment at GPW sites: Adhere to scheduled maintenance to previously established manufacturing sequences and lead times, as instructed by GPW.
  • Keep to the schedule to track and build an accurate history of equipment maintenance, through completion of maintenance work orders.
  • Escalate and expedite maintenance operations that delay production or pose a risk to health, safety and environment.
  • Assist to perform root cause analyses on equipment breakdowns and report thereon.
  • Ensure the following-up and communicating of the schedule to all resources: maintenance, production, outside services, stores, and engineering.
  • Assisting machine agents or OEM should be required.
  • Inspect all mechanical equipment as per agreed timelines and manufactures instructions.
  • Report any signs of shortcomings and damage immediately to Supervisor.
  • Keep up to date with new developments in the functional area.
  • Perform and monitor quality assurance ensuring that all maintenance and repair work is compliant with the standards set by GPW and other relevant authorities: Review factors that could affect the quality of maintenance/repair work and put corrective measures in place if required.
  • Monitor progress to track performance against the plan and to identify early warning signs.
  • Provide inputs to prepare maintenance and repair reports.
  • Work order management
  • A) Get work orders from the supervisor every morning and attend to required jobs as soon as possible
  • Engage requestor, artisan, or supervisor responsible for the machine
  • Fill in the work order completely after attending to the fault
  • Report back to the supervisor with a work order that is completed
  • Administration functions
  • Submit written and verbal reports to supervisor as per agreed timelines
  • Management of one’s leave
  • Time and attendance management
  • Attend standing and ad-hoc meetings as and when required
  • Attend training, workshops, courses, and any intervention proposed by supervisor, management or GPW
  • Ensure Safety, health, environment, risk, and quality (SHERQ) in the Division: Always Adhere to health and safety prescripts during all phases of maintenance on site.
  • Proper use of PPE.
  • Do regular risk assessments to eliminate injuries or incidents to operators, artisans, and anyone around the machine.
  • Full adherence to any safety, health and environmental policy implemented within GPW and its sites.

Requirements

  • Grade 10 or equivalent qualification plus a completed apprenticeship.
  • Trade Test Certificate in Printing Industry.
  • Grade 12 will be an added advantage.
  • No experience required.
  • Experience in printing industry will be an added advantage.
  • Must be able to obtain a positive security clearance.
  • Good knowledge of applicable processes and equipment.
  • Understanding of Public Service Act, Public Service Regulations, Labour Relations Act, Safety, Health, Environment and Quality (SHREQ) management requirements Act, Skills Development Act.
  • Understanding all relevant safety requirements for staff in a manufacturing environment.
  • A valid driver's license is required.
