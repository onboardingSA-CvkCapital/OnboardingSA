About the role

Job Title: Security Fleet Controller Fidelity Security Services is seeking highly skilled Security Fleet Controllers to join our team.  This critical role combines high-level security command with line-haul logistics oversight from our central fleet station. Main purpose will be to oversee the secure dispatch and real-time tracking of bulk cargo vehicles, utilizing advanced security technology and integrated systems to protect assets, mitigate risks, and maximize operational throughput. Minimum Requirements Qualifications • Grade 12 / Matric Certificate. • Valid PSIRA Grade A/B Registration (mandatory). • Control Room Certification will be advantageous. • Security Risk Management qualifications will be advantageous. Experience • Minimum 2 to 4 years' experience in: • Security Control Rooms. • Fleet Tracking and Monitoring. • Line-haul Logistics Operations. • Bulk Cargo Dispatch Environments. • Security Incident & Response Management. Technical Requirements Proven operational experience with: • CCTV and Surveillance Systems. • GPS Vehicle Tracking Systems. • Microsoft Office. Additional Requirements • Strong communication and report writing skills. • High attention to detail and situational awareness. • Ability to perform under pressure in a fast-paced environment. • Willingness to work rotating 12-hour shifts, including weekends and public holidays. • Ability to work independently and as part of a team. Key Responsibilities Control Room Operations • Maintain continuous monitoring of fleet, security, and operational systems. • Conduct routine system functionality checks across CiiMS, IRIS, Crystal, HikCentral, and Avigilon. • Perform virtual patrols of facilities, perimeter fences, access points, and high-risk zones using CCTV and PTZ camera systems. • Ensure all control room systems remain operational and functioning effectively. Fleet Tracking & Monitoring • Monitor fleet movements and line-haul vehicles in real time. • Ensure route adherence and investigate route deviations. • Identify unauthorized stops, delays, or security concerns. • Respond immediately to geofence breaches, panic signals, and route anomalies. • Maintain visibility of vehicle movements from dispatch to destination. Surveillance & Security Monitoring • Monitor live CCTV feeds, alarms, and perimeter security systems. • Review CCTV footage and conduct investigations when required. • Monitor high-risk areas and identify suspicious activity or security breaches. • Detect, manage, and escalate incidents in accordance with company procedures. • Maintain accurate incident records within designated systems. Cargo Dispatch & Security • Oversee the secure release and dispatch of cargo vehicles. • Verify cargo seal integrity and dispatch documentation. • Ensure that vehicle, cargo, and security compliance checks are completed before departure. • Support asset protection initiatives and cargo security controls throughout the transport cycle. Access Control & Verification • Verify driver, contractor, and visitor credentials using approved biometric and security systems. • Manage access control procedures relating to fleet dispatch operations. • Ensure compliance with site and client security requirements. Reporting & Compliance • Complete daily operational, security, and fleet reports. • Maintain accurate incident records and tracking logs. • Manage shift handovers and communicate outstanding issues effectively. • Ensure compliance with all company SOPs, security policies, and client requirements. • Support operational audits and investigations where required. Key Competencies The successful candidate should demonstrate: • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities. • Excellent observation and attention to detail. • Sound decision making under pressure. • Effective communication and coordination skills. • Strong security and risk awareness. • Professionalism, integrity, and accountability. • Ability to multitask across multiple systems and information sources. • High levels of concentration and situational awareness. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Performance will be measured against: • Fleet monitoring effectiveness. • Route deviation and security incident detection. • Cargo security compliance. • Incident response and escalation times. • Accuracy and quality of reporting. • CCTV and surveillance monitoring standards. • System compliance and operational discipline. • Quality of shift handovers. • Attendance, reliability, and adherence to operational procedures.
