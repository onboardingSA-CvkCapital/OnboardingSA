About the role

Introduction: The Strategy Support Manager will play a pivotal role in shaping and executing the strategic initiatives that propel our business forward. Description: THE JOB AT A GLANCE The Strategy Support Manager will play a pivotal role in shaping and executing the strategic initiatives that propel our business forward. This individual will report to the Head: Strategy and work closely with senior leadership to develop and implement strategies that align with long-term business goals. What Will You Do? 1. Strategic Planning Support • Support the Head: Strategy in developing RMA’s strategic plan/framework and annual operating plans • Assist in formalizing and documenting the strategic planning process • Gather and analyse relevant data to inform strategic decision-making • Prepare strategic planning materials and facilitate strategic planning sessions • Ensure alignment between Group Strategy, business strategy, and functional business plans • Help communicate the strategy clearly to all internal and external stakeholders 2. Market Analysis and Intelligence • Conduct in-depth market analysis and competitive landscape assessments • Monitor industry trends and identify market opportunities and potential risks • Prepare competitive intelligence reports for senior management • Research and analyse market data to support strategic recommendations • Identify strategic opportunities for business growth and expansion • Develop insights on competitors’ strengths, weaknesses, and strategic moves 3. Strategic Performance Management • Support the development and implementation of KPIs to track strategic objectives • Assist in creating and maintaining dashboards for monitoring strategic performance • Analyse performance data and prepare regular reports for leadership • Identify performance gaps and recommend corrective actions • Support the cascade of strategic goals to divisional and individual KPAs • Track and monitor group, divisional, and individual KPI performance 4. Strategic Project Management • Coordinate and manage the execution of strategic initiatives and projects • Develop project plans, timelines, and resource requirements • Monitor project progress and ensure timely completion • Identify and mitigate project risks • Facilitate cross-functional collaboration to support project execution • Prepare project status reports for senior leadership 5. Financial and Strategic Analysis • Collaborate with the Data and Analytics team on analytical models • Perform financial analysis to support strategic decision-making • Evaluate business cases for new strategic initiatives • Assist in scenario planning and financial modelling • Support the development of capital plans aligned with organizational strategy • Analyze ROI of strategic initiatives and recommend improvements 6. Stakeholder Management and Communication • Support the Head: Group Strategy in stakeholder engagement • Prepare presentations, reports, and strategic communications • Facilitate strategic discussions with cross-functional teams • Assist in building strategic partnerships and alliances • Create compelling materials for executive meetings and board presentations • Ensure strategic initiatives are clearly communicated across the organization Requirements: What Will you Bring to the Table? • NQF Level 9: Master’s degree • Minimum 5 years’ experience in management consulting and financial services • Excellent understanding of business operations and procedures • Strong data/information sourcing and analysing to drive strategic insight • Experience leading project execution teams What Will You Get in Return? We offer great opportunities for personal and professional development in a stable company that's 130 years strong. The role comes with a competitive salary package and various benefits. Flexible work arrangements (combination of remote and in the office). Furthermore, you will be a part of a dedicated group of colleagues who value teamwork and collaboration.
