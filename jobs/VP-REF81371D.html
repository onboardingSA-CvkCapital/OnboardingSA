About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Reliability Engineer, based in Mokopane, Limpopo. This role is at a Band 6 level reporting to the Senior Engineer Reliability.

Responsibilities

  • Analyse equipment performance, maintenance, condition-monitoring and operational data to identify reliability risks, recurring failures, adverse trends and improvement opportunities.
  • Conduct and facilitate root cause analyses and equipment-failure investigations within the assigned area, using appropriate causal-analysis methods, and develop technically sound corrective and preventive actions.
  • Implement approved defect-elimination and reliability-improvement initiatives and monitor results to confirm that the intended performance improvement is achieved and sustained.
  • Apply asset-criticality, failure-mode and reliability-centred maintenance principles when reviewing equipment risks, maintenance tactics and asset performance.
  • Provide technical input into Failure Modes and Effects Analysis and Failure Modes, Effects and Criticality Analysis, equipment maintenance strategies, task lists and preventive and predictive maintenance requirements.
  • Review equipment maintenance history, failure patterns and work-management information and recommend improvements to maintenance tactics, intervals, standards and data quality.
  • Support the implementation and continuous improvement of condition-monitoring practices, including the closure of identified gaps and the technical interpretation of condition-monitoring results.
  • Perform equipment inspections, technical audits and in-field verification activities to assess equipment condition, maintenance quality and compliance with approved engineering standards and work requirements.
  • Support asset-integrity activities by analysing inspection findings, identifying emerging risks and recommending appropriate corrective actions.
  • Provide reliability-engineering input into maintenance, shutdown and capitalised-maintenance planning where equipment condition, failure risk or lifecycle performance must be considered.
  • Analyse the reliability implications of proposed equipment, process or technical changes and provide input into the applicable technical change and risk-management processes.
  • Monitor agreed reliability indicators and improvement actions, identify deviations and escalate material risks or recurring performance concerns to the Senior Engineer Reliability.
  • Provide technically accurate input into operational asset-management plans, equipment-improvement plans, forecasts, maturity assessments and performance reviews.
  • Maintain reliability-engineering records and technical information in accordance with applicable document-control, master-data and asset-management system requirements.
  • Engage with engineering, maintenance, production, planning, condition-monitoring and supply-chain stakeholders to support the effective implementation of reliability solutions.
  • Evaluate relevant equipment and reliability technologies and make recommendations for consideration through the operation’s approved technical and investment processes.

Requirements

  • BTech, Advanced Diploma or equivalent NQF 7 qualification in Mechanical, Electrical, Mechatronics, Automotive or a related Engineering Technology discipline.
  • Alternatively, a relevant NQF 6 National Diploma supported by substantial experience in the maintenance and reliability of mechanised mining equipment
  • Advantageous
  • Postgraduate qualification in Reliability Engineering, Maintenance Engineering, Asset Management or a related field.
  • ECSA professional registration appropriate to the qualification and discipline.
  • Relevant Reliability or Asset Management certification, such as CMRP or equivalent.
  • Experience, technical knowledge and skills
  • Minimum five years' relevant experience in maintenance, reliability engineering or asset management of mechanised mining equipment, including demonstrated competence in reliability improvement, root cause analysis, defect elimination and maintenance strategy optimisation.
  • Candidates with an NQF 6 National Diploma must have a minimum of eight years' relevant experience, including substantial practical experience in the maintenance and reliability of mechanised mining equipment.
  • Reliability engineering and asset-management principles.
  • Reliability-centred maintenance (RCM) and maintenance optimisation.
  • Root cause analysis (RCA), failure analysis and defect elimination.
  • Equipment criticality assessment, failure modes and risk-based maintenance.
  • Preventive, predictive and condition-based maintenance principles.
  • Condition monitoring, asset integrity and equipment-performance management.
  • Maintenance planning, scheduling and work-management principles.
  • Reliability performance indicators and data-analysis principles.
  • Engineering master data and enterprise asset-management systems.
  • Technical risk assessment and engineering change-management principles.
