About the role

PROJECT MANAGER Purpose of the Role The Facilities Project Manager is responsible for the end-to-end planning, coordination and delivery of Facilities Management projects and services across assigned sites or key accounts. The role ensures that facilities projects and operational services are delivered safely, efficiently, on time, within budget and in accordance with contractual requirements, service level agreements (SLAs), statutory legislation and client expectations. The role combines project management, facilities operations, technical services, contract management, financial control, stakeholder engagement and people leadership to drive operational excellence, client satisfaction, continuous improvement and sustainable business growth.
