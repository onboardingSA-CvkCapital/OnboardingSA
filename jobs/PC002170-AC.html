About the role

Our client, a well-established company based in Epping, Cape Town, is seeking an experienced and hands-on Warehouse Manager to join their team. The successful candidate will have strong supervisory or management experience, excellent organisational skills, and a solid understanding of warehouse, operations, production, or logistics environments. Applicants must have Grade 12/Matric, be fully bilingual in Afrikaans and English, be computer literate, and have their own reliable transport. Experience with an ERP system and a relevant qualification in Operations, Production, or Logistics will be highly advantageous. unnecessary reprinting. • Liaise with the Dispatch department regarding any foreseeable delays or operational issues. • Restrict access to the Whole Goods and Finished Goods warehouses to authorized personnel only, including controlling access by sales representatives, customers, and members of the public. • Ensure that all warehouse entrances are locked when not in use and that stock remains secure at all times. • Manage the use of warehouse forklifts, ensuring that they are operated correctly and serviced and maintained according to schedule. • Manage pallets on a daily basis and ensure with current systems, procedures, and good warehousing practices, including stock recovery, samples, stacking, and related processes. • Effectively manage temporary, casual, and container staff on a
