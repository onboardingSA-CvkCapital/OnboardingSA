About the role

The Presidency is hiring a Chief Network Controller in Information Technology, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: The Presidency, Private Bag X1000, Pretoria, 0001 or Hand deliver at Government Avenue, Union Buildings, Pretoria or by email: applications@presidency.gov.za For attention: Ms M Malele Reference number to quote: CNC/PRES/2026 Closing date: 20 November 2026 (16:30 walk ins and 00:00 on email) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: NB: Email applications must on the subject line state only the Reference number: CNC/PRES/2026 (with no spaces).

Responsibilities

  • Configuration of Windows Server operating system (Microsoft) and ensuring maximum performance data/information backups and restores.
  • Maintenance and support of WAN/LAN infrastructure.
  • Maintenance and support of Microsoft Exchange Servers.
  • Maintenance and support of specialised hardware and software technologies (e.g. VMware, Dell VXRail storage).
  • Maintenance and support of Cisco infrastructure (e.g. Routers, Core switches, Access Switches and Call Managers).
  • Maintenance and support of the Transversal system (BAS, PERSAL, Logis).
  • Draft and update network infrastructure standard operating procedures and network configuration documentation.
  • Provide 3rdline support to the Presidency staff, including the principals.
  • Also, support the principals with video conference facilities during virtual meetings.
  • Assist in the implementation and testing of network security measures and the Disaster Recovery plan.
  • Implement computer and server network policies and other projects.
  • Compile and maintain network configuration and disaster recovery documentation.
  • Ensure updates of anti-virus software and security patches for servers.
  • Keep up to date with server, and network technology.
  • Ensure maximum uptime of network equipment through accurate and early response.
  • Be prepared to travel and work after hours, including Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays when required.
  • Must be prepared to be on standby.

Requirements

  • A Senior Certificate plus an appropriate National Diploma/Advanced Certificate in Information Technology or an equivalent qualification on NQF level 6.
  • At least 3 to 4 years’ applicable ICT experience.
  • Core competencies
  • Knowledge of LAN/WAN infrastructure, Windows Server, Microsoft Exchange, transversal systems, backups, and restoration
  • Advanced troubleshooting and fault-finding skills, communication, both oral and written
  • Client orientation and customer focus
  • Honesty and integrity
  • Service delivery innovation
  • Knowledge Management
  • Problem-solving and analysis
  • Project management
  • Public Service Regulatory Framework
  • Policy formulation process with government
  • Monitoring and evaluation methods, tools, and techniques
