About the role

About the company TechMarkit was established to provide customers with affordable, high-quality technology for both personal and professional use. We offer exceptional value across a wide range of new and refurbished devices, including laptops, desktops, tablets, smartphones, Kindles, notebooks and accessories, all backed by trusted service and reliable warranties. About The Role The Store Manager is responsible for the overall performance and profitability of the store by leading the team, driving sales, delivering an exceptional customer experience and ensuring operational excellence. This role oversees all aspects of the business, including sales performance, people management, stock control, administration, merchandising, compliance and customer service, while ensuring company policies and procedures are consistently maintained. Key Responsibilities • Sales & Commercial Performance • Drive the achievement of daily, weekly and monthly sales targets. • Monitor store performance and implement initiatives to maximise revenue and profitability. • Analyse sales reports and key performance indicators to identify opportunities for improvement. • Promote upselling and cross-selling opportunities across all product categories. • Ensure sales targets are communicated and understood by the team.Leadership & People Management • Recruit, coach and develop a high-performing team. • Conduct regular one-on-one coaching sessions and performance reviews. • Set, monitor and manage individual and team KPIs and Key Performance Areas (KPAs). • Motivate the team to consistently achieve sales and customer service objectives. • Manage employee performance, attendance and disciplinary matters in partnership with HR. • Foster a positive, collaborative and customer-focused culture.Staff Scheduling & Workforce Planning • Prepare and manage staff rosters to ensure optimal floor coverage. • Manage leave, attendance and timekeeping. • Allocate resources effectively to meet trading requirements while managing labour costs.Stock Control & Inventory Management • Ensure accurate receiving, grading, labelling and storage of all stock. • Maintain stock accuracy through regular cycle counts and stock takes. • Ensure effective replenishment and merchandising of all products. • Identify, report and manage faulty, damaged or missing stock. • Minimise stock losses through strong inventory controls.Store Operations & Administration • Ensure all sales, returns, banking, cash-ups and manual invoices are processed accurately and according to company procedures. • Maintain accurate administration and ensure all
