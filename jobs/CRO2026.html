About the role

Job Summary •Provide support, manage the client relationship and address any client issue that may arise. Assist with support to the members registered on our plans. Key Tasks Build Relationships with the Employers •Responsible for daily servicing of employers. •Attend PCM phones and WhatsApp phones. •Check monthly employee schedules for accuracy. •Assist with any issue an employer may have around the membership of their employees on the cover. •Provide monthly interaction with the clients. •Build ongoing relationships with key stakeholders at clients. •Perform any other task entrusted by Management. Provide Customer Care Support to Members/Companies •Provide customer care support to members/companies. •Assist with all forms of enquiries from customers and management (telephonic and email). •Manage inbound calls collaboratively with team members across all lines assigned to Group Schemes. •Assist with the Please Call Me numbers. •Ensure that the necessary departmental reporting is adhered to. •Provide support and assistance to the Sales Executives and Take on Team. •Assist any Group Schemes member with any query or claim that may be received by the department, follow up and provide feedback to the relevant party. •Ensure that treating customers fairly is adhered to at all costs. Conduct General Administrative Duties •Responsible for an assigned group of companies dealing with interalia: oEnsuring that new companies are captured correctly on CRM. oMaintenance of the assigned companies’ information. oCompliance with FICA regulations. oCapturing of new member applications and ongoing applications. oEnsuring all the necessary member information is received from the assigned company. oChecking that policies are captured correctly and correct any errors. oConfirming new memberships by sending a membership certificate to the Company together with the expected premium. oRequesting membership packs from fulfilment and ensure all information is accurately reflected. oResponsible for delivering the Welcome packs to the company. oNotifying Finance of any new members, member additions or cancellations for the companies. oMonthly checking of the Bordereau to ensure it matches the CRM system. oMonthly checking of the assigned companies Bordereau to ensure that the amounts are correct. oEnsuring that membership movements are correctly received by the various Providers (PPN, Mediscor, PAMC, DRC, Specsavers). oResponsible for all necessary communication is sent to the assigned company contact. oRegular contact with assigned company contacts doing general PR calls. •Ensure that your assigned companies are fully compliant and that the required monthly premiums are paid timeously. •Conduct general administrative duties.• Perform any other task entrusted by Management.
