About the role

An established manufacturing business is seeking a qualified and experienced Maintenance Millwright to join its Production team. This is a hands-on role focused on improving plant reliability, reducing production downtime and ensuring manufacturing equipment is maintained to a high operational standard.The position is available for immediate placement, although the start date can be negotiated for the right candidate. Key Responsibilities • Attend to mechanical and electrical breakdowns. • Diagnose recurring machine faults and identify root causes. • Maintain production equipment, including moulding, assembly, printing, conveying and ancillary machinery. • Maintain motors, gearboxes, bearings, belts, chains and shafts. • Fault-find electrical panels, sensors, contactors, relays and motor circuits. • Maintain hydraulic and pneumatic systems. • Develop and implement preventative maintenance schedules. • Conduct planned servicing and equipment inspections. • Maintain accurate machine and equipment registers. • Record breakdowns, repairs, downtime and parts used. • Identify critical spare parts and recommend appropriate stock levels. • Liaise with machine suppliers and specialist contractors. • Assist with machinery installation, relocation and commissioning. • Maintain high housekeeping and contamination-control standards within the production environment. Requirements • Recognised Millwright Trade Test Certificate – essential. • Matric or equivalent qualification. • Minimum 3 years' post-trade Millwright experience within a production or manufacturing environment. • Strong electrical and mechanical fault-finding skills. • Experience with three-phase motors and industrial electrical systems. • Good working knowledge of hydraulics and pneumatics. • Ability to read electrical and mechanical drawings. • Able to work independently and make sound decisions during breakdowns. • Basic computer literacy and ability to maintain accurate maintenance records. • Willing and able to work overtime and provide after-hours breakdown support when required. Working Hours & Remuneration Monday to Friday: 07:00 – 16:00 Candidates must be willing to work overtime, weekends and occasional night shifts in the event of breakdowns or operational requirements. Salary: Market related – candidates should advise their wage/salary expectation. Payment: Fortnightly.
