About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Chief Network Controller in Corporate Services, based in Limpopo (Polokwane). This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 61 Biccard street, Polokwane, 0700 or by email to Post267@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/267 Closing date: 23 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian and White Males, and Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Provide IT user support to clients (office).
  • Resolve IT support calls, queries or issues as they arise.
  • Log IT support calls on Information Technology Service Management (ITSM).
  • Render IT security.
  • Ensure a secure environment by installation and uploading antivirus software.
  • Ensure that users log on to the workstation using passwords.
  • Install and update Anti-virus software to all workstations.
  • Identify IT requirements.
  • Advise clients on IT equipment procurement.
  • Inspect all the switches and network points.
  • Ensure that all ICT related work is done according to agreed standard and quality.
  • Verify the warranty of ICT equipment before allocation.
  • Maintain IT applications.
  • Ensure that all applications are up and running daily.
  • Uninstall unwanted applications on the workstation.
  • Provide server management services.
  • Monitor access to server rooms.
  • Maintain inventory of all server room related equipment.
  • Monitor Local Area Network (LAN) performance and report any anomalies.
  • Maintain networks and hardware.
  • Install and troubleshoot hardware and software.
  • Assist with planning, design, implementation, and maintenance of LAN infrastructure.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements:
  • A Grade 12 Certificate and National Diploma in Computer Sciences/Information Technology or related equivalent qualifications (NQF Level 6)
  • At least 2 years relevant experience in Information Technology (IT) Support
  • Experience with hardware and software
  • Experience in network support
  • Experience in IT technical support
  • Appropriate network management experience
  • A valid driver’s licence
  • Job related knowledge:
  • Technical aspects of Information and Communications Technology (ICT) goods and services
  • Information Technology Acts and Policies
  • Government ICT systems
  • Understanding management of information and the formal reporting system
  • Internal systems change control
  • Project management principles and tools
  • Job related skills:
  • Planning and Organising
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Advanced computer literacy
  • Project management
  • Interpersonal relations
  • The ability to work efficiently and effectively at all times
