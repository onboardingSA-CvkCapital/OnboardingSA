About the role

A leading fashion accessories company is looking for an experienced Internal Sales, Key Account & Export Manager with strong experience in key account management, internal sales operations, order management, national retail accounts and export business to join their team in Sandton. This hands-on commercial management role will be responsible for managing and growing key customer relationships, leading the Internal Sales function, overseeing end-to-end order processing and driving sales growth across national retail and cross-border markets. The successful candidate will act as a key commercial link between customers and internal departments, ensuring accurate execution, strong customer service and effective operational control.
