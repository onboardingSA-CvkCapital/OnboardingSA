About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies’ in Stikland, Cape Town has an opportunity available for a DC Dispatch Trainee Manager to join the team. The main purpose of this role will be to achieve operational excellence within the Dis-Chem Distribution centre incorporating functions in necessary areas, ensuring that both legal and customer requirements are met. Minimum Requirements… Essential: • Grade 12 / Matric • Degree/Diploma in Logistics or Supply Chain Management • Minimum 1-2 Years relevant experience in Distribution, Logistics or Supply Chain Environment Advantageous: • Relevant experience in the field of Logistics/Supply Chain Management • Experience in the Retail/Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Experience with SAP, EWM or other ERP systemsJob Specification… Planning: • Optimal planning and forecasting of department resources to meet the daily strategic objective defined by the quantity of orders in the system. • Ensure that customer orders and jabs are pulled daily and that Wave item clearing is maintained with no orders in excess of 2 days. • Ensure that the system is monitored and cleaned up by using the management tools available on SAP EWM. • Ensure that all stock is received and packed away and accurately maintained on the system. • Assist with implementation and optimization of new projects related to improving the efficiency of operations. • Ensure that all equipment is maintained and in safe operating conditions. Planning and Deliveries: • Ensure optimal route and space planning to meet the day-to-day strategic objectives. • Understand and execute the planning process involved when stock leaves the distribution centre. • Ensure routing of vehicles to ensure optimum utilisation and vehicle turnaround times. • Ensure all orders that are picked and processed timeously and accurately and are delivered as per operational requirement. • Arrange returns and collection of the upliftment, which must be tracked throughout the return process. • Ensure drivers are debriefed, paperwork is filled and queries resolved. • Ensure admin and daily checking of all delivery log sheets takes place, to facilitate queries and credit note request. Human Resource Management: • Report personnel problems by analysing data, investigating issues, and identifying solutions with Management and take corrective action. • Ensure all supervisors in department manage their staff efficiently. • Ensure training of new staff in that they have the necessary tools and equipment required to perform their duties efficiently. • Set employee goals and objectives in line with Management views. • Monitor staff performance and report to Management. • Work with Management to manage employees that do not comply with Dis-Chem Policies and procedures according to the disciplinary code. • Develop an environment that creates, maintains or improves the ability to carry out work related obligations. • Responsible for the succession planning of subordinates. Administration: • Efficient and accurate administration, filling and reporting for transactional excellence. • Management and adherence to GWP, SHE program and OHS act. • Document and revise department standard operating procedures and system instructions where required. • Ensure department’s consumable needs are met. Customer Service: • Effective and efficient resolution and communication of internal and external customer day-to-day queries. • Build and maintain good relationships with all 3rd party stakeholders and communicate effectively to resolve any supply chain issues. • Ensure that all deliveries are delivered in full, on time and to the correct customer. Security and Operating Costs: • Prevent and act on security or risk issues and ensure that risks are dealt with proactively. • Ability to analyse and interpret any shrinkage indicators and work with Management to act accordingly. • Be security conscious all the time and avoid committing theft or fraud. General: • Housekeeping must be in accordance to Dis-Chem standards. Make sure that work areas under your control are always clean and tidy. • Adhere to Dis-Chem Policies and Standard Operating Procedures. • Adhere to Health and Safety rules and regulations. • Adhere to Dis-Chem Uniform and personal appearance policy. • Adhere to Dis-Chem Disciplinary and Counselling Policy and Procedures. Competencies Essential: • Ability to work under pressure • Deadline driven • Demonstrate an understanding of the necessary leadership principles, values, principles of behaviour and leads by example. • Demonstrate personal initiative. • Take accountability for own actions. • Demonstrate an understanding of and a commitment to corporate governance principles • Demonstrate a service mind-set and ensures everything within reason is done to achieve service excellence. • Have the ability to build effective and productive relationships at all levels. • Have the ability to influence service providers and build effective relationships within a team environment. • Have the ability to acknowledge conflict, give positive / difficult feedback and make decisions while maintaining individual dignity and respect.Special conditions of employment: • South African Citizen • MIE, clear criminal and credit • Driver’s license and/or own reliable transportRemuneration and benefits: • Market related salary • Medical aid • Provident fund • Staff account ONLY SUCCESSFUL APPLICANTS WILL BE CONTACTED.
