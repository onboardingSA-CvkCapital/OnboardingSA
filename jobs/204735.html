About the role

Regional Property Portfolio Manager Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the-farm foods for consumers. Apart from its various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture, with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now operate across the entire food supply chain, including processing, logistics, storage, handling and national and international market channels. This "house of brands", established with the support of our farmer shareholders, forms the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. More About VKB Group Properties The VKB Group owns and manages a diverse portfolio of agricultural, industrial, commercial, retail, storage, and operational properties across the Free State. These properties are critical to supporting the Group's operations and long-term growth strategy. From grain handling facilities and storage infrastructure to offices, workshops, operational sites, and commercial properties, our portfolio supports every step of the agricultural value chain. Managing these assets requires balancing operational needs, infrastructure investment, compliance, asset preservation, and long-term value creation. The Property Division plays a vital role in ensuring that our facilities remain safe, efficient, financially sustainable, and capable of supporting the future growth of the VKB Group. Regional Property Portfolio Manager Why This Role Matters? The VKB Group's property portfolio is far more than a collection of buildings; it is a strategic business asset that enables the movement, processing, storage, and distribution of agricultural products across the Free State. As Regional Property Portfolio Manager, you will serve as the custodian of a significant property portfolio that supports the Group's agricultural and commercial operations. You will be responsible for protecting, maintaining, improving, and optimising these assets while guiding investments that support future growth. You will influence property strategy, lead infrastructure upgrades, manage capital projects, oversee facilities management, and help ensure that our properties continue to deliver long-term value to the business. This is a rare opportunity to combine property portfolio management, facilities leadership, infrastructure development, and strategic asset management within one of South Africa's leading agricultural organisations. Why Join VKB? Joining the VKB Group means becoming part of a business that plays a meaningful role in supporting agriculture, food production, and rural economic development. At VKB, you will have the opportunity to: • Manage a diverse and strategically important property portfolio across the Free State. • Influence long-term property and infrastructure investment decisions. • Lead capital improvement and development projects that create lasting business value. • Collaborate with operational, engineering, financial, and executive stakeholders. • Drive asset optimisation initiatives that directly impact business performance. • Work in an environment that values accountability, innovation, integrity, and continuous improvement. • Grow your career within one of Southern Africa's leading agribusiness groups.What Success Looks Like?
