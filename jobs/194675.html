About the role

MAIN PURPOSE OF POSITION: The DevOps Engineer is responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining OSS platform architecture, automating deployment and operational processes, integrating systems, and continuously optimizing customer environments. The role bridges development and operations by ensuring reliable software delivery, system performance, and scalability while providing technical support, customer collaboration, and ongoing system enhancements. DevOps Engineer Duties And Responsibilities System architecture planning and implementation of OSS components: • Design, development, and implementation of OSS platform components to achieve the client’s objectives. • Ensure system coherency and integration between various platforms. • Facilitate and manage system integration with the customer’s development team and/or external contractors. • Assist the client with pre-launch (go-live) system testing to get sign-off on acceptance based on the Statement of Work. • Conduct training for clients on all system components relevant to the specific customer’s environment. • A good relationship needs to be built and maintained with both FirstDMT vendors and the client on all levels of these organizations. Continuous assessment of the customer's environment • Continued input into how to optimize the systems for best performance within the customer’s own environment. • Expand the system capabilities to facilitate continued growth and increased value proposition to existing customers. • Obtain in-depth knowledge of the inner workings of customers’ systems to understand the environment to be able to make informed decisions and suggestions. OSS development: • Compile user specification documents (SOW and/or Change request forms) that accurately capture the requirement. • Work closely with in-house and external developers to review requirements and functions developed. • Develop and improve existing functions. • Document and track code written using provided tools. • Deploy the functions into a production environment using the provided tools (e.g Git/Ansible) • Ensuring that functions operate as required in test and production environments. REQUIREMENTS: Internal training associated with competencies: • During the first week of your employment, you'll be presented with a meticulously crafted learning map de-signed to seamlessly guide you through your on-the-job training experience. Office Requirements: • Hybrid home/office environment. • Flexi hours are allowed based for after-hours requirements. • Smart-casual dress code. • Excellent international business language skills (English), both written and verbal. • Reliable transport to and from the office.
