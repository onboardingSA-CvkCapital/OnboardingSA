About the role

Transnet (Transnet Pipelines) is hiring a Specialist: Employee Relations, based in Durban Central. The role reports to the Senior Specialist: Employee Relations. Grade: F. Preference will be given to persons with disabilities. Employee Relations (ER) Specialist will be responsible to render an ER function, provide advice and support to Transnet Pipelines (TPL) management, employees and the business in order to promote harmonious working environment by handling and reporting on investigation, suspension, grievance, PFMA violations, dispute and disciplinary processes and applying policies and procedures in compliance to Transnet policies and applicable legislation, and also ensure that collective bargaining and engagement processes are in place and effective.

Responsibilities

  • Implement, communicate and ensure compliance with collective agreements concluded between Transnet and recognised unions, including management of stakeholders both internal and external
  • Provide policy interpretation, reviews, advice and capacitate line management and employees on People Management policies and assist with policy formulation
  • Coordinate ER internal controls such as monthly update of ER SAP, compile People Management, industrial action, PFMA and ER weekly/monthly reports
  • Attend on behalf of Transnet Pipelines various ER forums such as Business Continuity Management (BCM) meetings, Employment Equity (EE) Forum and Skills Development (SD) Committee meetings, as well as industrial action meetings
  • Arrange and assist in taking (including drafting) minutes and recordings of National Business Committee (NBC) and Local Business Committee (LBC), EE and SD meetings
  • Represent Transnet Pipelines at Transnet Bargaining Council and CCMA mediation and arbitration processes, including disciplinary and grievance meetings as well as any other forum as determined by Senior Specialist: ER and Head People Management
  • Coordinate and facilitate processes such as suspensions, disciplinary, grievances and other dispute resolution processes to resolve conflicts, including assisting in taking minutes and recording of the said meetings
  • Investigate ER and tip-offs reported cases and submit recommendations to management
  • Provide assistance, support and advises to management, People Management community and employees on ER matters.
