About LicencePath We help experienced tradies and workers — carpenters, electricians, bricklayers and more — get their trade certificate and licence so they can take the next step in their career. This is a regulated industry and a legal requirement for every tradie to have. Plenty of good tradesmen have the skills and the years on the tools but not the piece of paper the government requires to work for themselves, charge properly, or run their own jobs. We close that gap — through short-course gap-training and recognition of the experience they already have — and get them licensed much faster than the traditional way. We're talking years to weeks. For the tradesman, a licence changes everything: their own contracts, uncapped income, the freedom to run their own show. For you, it's a sales role you can actually stand behind. You're not talking anyone into anything they don't need — you're helping someone unlock the career they've already earned. That's rare in sales, and it makes the job easier and the money better. We have our own blue ocean, focusing on specific licences that are very difficult for anyone else to offer. There's barely any competition. So it's not hard to show people the value in this. The role We're hiring experienced high-ticket closers for inbound calls only. That makes the sale close to a no-brainer, and your job is to push it over the line.
