About the role

Purpose of the Role: The Technical Contracts Coordinator is responsible for supporting Tosas' strategic customer accounts and key projects by coordinating technical information, laboratory results, investigations, reporting, and customer engagement. The role acts as a central link between customers, Technical, Laboratory, Operations, and Commercial teams, ensuring effective communication, proactive issue management, accurate reporting, and continuous improvement in product performance and customer service. follow-up. • Identify trends, risks, and opportunities for continuous improvement. • Maintain contract documentation, action registers, and performance dashboards. • Assist in root cause Strong attention to detail and problem-solving ability. • Proficient in Microsoft Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and data analysis tools. • Experience in a laboratory, manufacturing, technical services,
