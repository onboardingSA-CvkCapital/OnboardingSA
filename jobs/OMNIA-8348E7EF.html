About the role

Omnia is hiring a Technical Officer (Emulsion) in BME Production and Logistics, based in ﻿Losberg. The role reports to the Production Manager. To provide advanced technical support to the emulsion production operations across the Losberg region, ensuring optimal plant performance, product consistency, and technical compliance.

Responsibilities

  • Technical Problem Solving & Support:
  • Provide expert-level technical support to Innopak, Innovex, and Emulsifier plants.
  • Lead root cause analysis for critical process deviations and equipment-related issues.
  • Support shift teams and plant managers in resolving complex operational problems.
  • Process Optimisation & Standardisation:
  • Identify and implement opportunities for efficiency improvement, waste reduction, and throughput optimisation.
  • Standardise process parameters and operating procedures across all emulsion sites.
  • Monitor, analyse, and report on process performance indicators.
  • Formulation Integrity & Governance:
  • Ensure formulation control processes are adhered to and documented.
  • Coordinate implementation of new formulations and modifications across sites.
  • Collaborate with R&D and product management on product performance feedback and improvements.
  • Project Execution Support:
  • Provide technical input during project scoping, design, commissioning, and handover phases.
  • Ensure technical alignment with safety and environmental standards (e.g., PSCE, MOC).
  • Validate and oversee trials related to process modifications and new equipment.
  • Compliance & Process Safety:
  • Contribute to the identification, validation, and maintenance of Process Safety Critical Equipment (PSCE).
  • Support the execution of HAZOPs, baseline risk assessments, and safety audits.
  • Ensure compliance with internal standards, industry codes, and regulatory requirements.
  • Training & Capability Development:
  • Mentor junior engineers, process controllers, and team leaders in technical competencies.
  • Facilitate technical workshops, onboarding sessions, and process troubleshooting clinics.
  • Support succession planning by documenting and transferring critical technical knowledge.
  • International Technical Support:
  • Support international emulsion plants through remote assistance and site visits.
  • Free up Plant Managers for international assistance.
  • Documentation & Reporting:
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date records of technical interventions, formulation changes, and audits.
  • Prepare detailed technical reports and presentations for senior management and cross-functional teams.

Requirements

  • Qualifications:
  • ﻿B.Eng. in Chemical engineering or equivalent
  • Project Management
  • Six-sigma certification.
  • Process Safety qualification﻿.
  • Experience:
  • 3 years demonstrated experience in emulsion manufacturing, explosives or chemical production environment
  • Proven experience with project implementation, formulation governance, and plant support
  • 3 year's working experience in a chemical plant environment (Advantageous)
  • Key Competencies:
  • Deep understanding of emulsion manufacturing processes
  • Process safety, PSCE, and formulation integrity
  • Statistical process control and root cause analysis
  • Ability to influence without authority
  • Stakeholder engagement and cross-functional collaboration
  • Coaching and capability development
  • High analytical ability
  • Practical, structured approach to operational challenges
  • Fast response to plant failures and technical issues
  • Strong written and verbal communication
  • Able to communicate complex technical matters to non-technical stakeholders
  • Excellent reporting and presentation skills
