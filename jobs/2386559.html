About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AI-powered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined, future-fit role. We are looking for passionate, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace data-driven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession Purpose of the job The Regional Lead serves as the strategic leader for their designated region, ensuring alignment with SAICA’s strategic imperatives while addressing the unique needs and opportunities of the region. Acting as a senior representative of SAICA in the region, the role involves translating National Council directives into region-specific execution plans, driving member engagement, delivering tailored services, leading, and developing regional teams, and safeguarding SAICA’s brand reputation and member experience. The role works closely with key SAICA units: Standards, Capabilities and Endorsements, Governance and Customer Experience to provide the regional perspective, understand latest developments, and drive awareness and adoption of these within the regions.
