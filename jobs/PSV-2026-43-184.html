About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a State Accountant in Vaal-Orange Catchment Management Agency Div: Asset Management, based in Bloemfontein. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: VOCMA 69 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: The Vaal-Orange Catchment Management Agency is a Public Water Resource Management Agency, established in line with the provisions of the National Water Act, 1998 (Act no 36 of 1998) (NWA) and National Water Policy for South Africa. It is an entity of the National Department of Water and Sanitation. Employees appointed in the CMA have similar employment terms and conditions as those of employees appointed in terms of the Public Service Act, though not employed in terms of the Public Service Act.

Responsibilities

  • Validate accurate records of movements of inventory, goods and services procured Conduct physical verification of Asset Manage the movement of assets Manage lease contracts Identify redundant, non-serviceable and obsolete equipment for disposal Keep records of asset to be disposed Manage the inventory levels to ensure that the department has the necessary materials and supplies to meet operational needs Manage reconciliation of asset register Develop asset management plans, including acquisition, operational, maintenance and disposal plans Prepare monthly and quarterly reports on asset management.
  • Provide information for the preparation of the annual financial statements.
  • Gather and submit information requested by auditors within the required time Attend to audit-related inquiries.

Requirements

  • A National Diploma (NQF level 6) in Financial Management/Management Accounting/Financial Accounting.
  • 1 year relevant experience.
  • Knowledge and understanding of financial legislation, policies, practices and procedures.
  • Knowledge of SAP system.
  • The disclosure of a valid unexpired driver’s license.
  • Knowledge and practical experience on SAP system.
  • Computer literacy such as MS Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.
  • Sound understanding and knowledge of the asset management process.
  • People and diversity management.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Accountability and ethical conduct.
  • Problem solving and analysis.
  • Knowledge of administrative clerical procedures and systems.
