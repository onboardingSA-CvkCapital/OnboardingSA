About the role

Senior Financial Manager – CFO Succession Durbanville, Cape Town Salary: R70 000 CTC pm negotiable, dependent on experience An established company operating within the fast-paced agricultural export sector is seeking an experienced, commercially minded Senior Financial Manager to join its leadership team.  This is a CFO succession role rather than a traditional reporting-focused finance position. The successful candidate will initially assume responsibility for the company’s financial operations and lead a small finance and administration team, and over time progressively take over the full CFO portfolio. The role requires an operationally involved finance professional who understands how financial decisions affect logistics, margins, customer risk, supplier costs and daily cash flow. The successful candidate must be decisive, highly accurate and comfortable working in a demanding environment where issues need to be addressed promptly. Minimum Requirements • Qualified CA(SA). • 4+ years’ post-articles financial management experience. • Experience within agriculture, exports, logistics, shipping, FMCG, international trade or a related environment. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and Afrikaans. • Valid driver’s licence and own reliable transport. • Advanced Excel and financial systems proficiency. • Experience in an operational, high-volume or deadline-driven commercial environment. • Proven responsibility for cash-flow management, management accounts, audit and financial controls. • Experience supervising or managing a finance or administration team. • Strong commercial understanding, with the ability to evaluate financial and operational risk. Key Responsibilities Financial Management and Control • Take ownership of the company’s day-to-day financial function. • Manage daily cash flow across the company’s banking platforms. • Prepare and present monthly management accounts and financial information for board meetings. • Oversee debtors, creditors, reconciliations, provisions, VAT and payroll processes. • Review customer accounts, credit limits, payment arrangements and financial exposure. • Manage annual budgets, forecasts and financial performance reporting. • Coordinate the year-end audit and ensure the audited annual financial statements are completed within strict deadlines. • Maintain accurate financial records, controls and statutory compliance. Commercial and Operational Finance • Review service-provider and shipping-related invoices against agreed rates and standard costings. • Investigate pricing, costing and margin variances that could affect profitability. • Oversee weekly turnover, gross profit and margin reporting. • Work closely with the commercial, logistics, operations and sales teams. • Assist with customer, supplier and shipping-related claims and recoveries. • Provide financial input into operational and commercial decisions. • Build relationships with customers and suppliers, including travelling to meet key stakeholders where required. Treasury and Risk • Manage forex requirements, foreign currency accounts and forward exchange contract reporting. • Assist with banking negotiations and South African Reserve Bank applications and approvals. • Monitor liquidity, customer exposure and credit risk. • Make commercially sound recommendations regarding customer credit limits and exceptions. • Ensure incoming funds, payments, transfers and drawdowns are accurately reflected in cash-flow reporting. This position will suit someone who: • Takes full ownership of their responsibilities without requiring close supervision. • Works with urgency while maintaining exceptional accuracy. • Thinks beyond the accounting function and understands the wider business.
