About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Scientist Manager Grade A in Water Resource Management Dir: Climate Change Analysis, based in Pretoria Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R1,143,468 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 131126/02 Closing date: 13 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: This is an affirmative action position. Preference will be given to candidates from previously disadvantaged groups, in accordance with the Department’s Employment Equity Plan and applicable legislation.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will be responsible for, but not limited to, the following: Provide scientific leadership and strategic direction in climate change analysis within the Department.
  • Coordinate and guide the analysis of historical and projected climate trends and their implications for South Africa's water resources.
  • Identify emerging climate risks and scientific developments relevant to the Department's mandate.
  • Coordinate climate vulnerability assessments at national, provincial, catchment, and Water Management Area (WMA) levels.
  • Assess the implications of climate change for water availability, water quality, droughts, floods, groundwater, water infrastructure, and water services.
  • Provide scientific evidence to support climate-resilient water resources planning and infrastructure development.
  • Lead the generation, analysis and interpretation of climate information and climate intelligence relevant to the water and sanitation sector.
  • Ensure alignment of the Department's climate change response with relevant national and international policy and legislative requirements.
  • Provide scientific and technical inputs into the development, review and implementation of climate change policies, strategies, frameworks and programmes within the water and sanitation sector.
  • Coordinate the identification of climate-resilient interventions for different Water Management Areas.
  • Promote innovative approaches to building climate-resilient water and sanitation systems.
  • Develop and manage strategic scientific research programmes addressing climate change and water.
  • Identify opportunities for collaborative research, innovation, knowledge generation and technology development.
  • Provide scientific advice to the Department's senior management and relevant stakeholders on climate change and water-related matters.
  • Establish and maintain networks of climate change and water experts.

Requirements

  • A Master of Science (MSc) degree (NQF Level 9) in Climate Science/Environmental Science/Hydrology/Water Resources Management/Atmospheric Science/Environmental Management/Engineering or relevant Scientific discipline.
  • A PhD (NQF Level 10) in Climate Science/Environmental Science, Atmospheric Science, Hydrology, Water Resources Management, Atmospheric Science, Engineering or another relevant scientific discipline will be an added advantage 10 years' appropriate experience in the relevant scientific field, climate science and/or the application of climate information to water resources management.
  • Compulsory registration with SACNASP as a professional scientist.
  • The disclosure of a valid unexpired driver’s licence.
  • Knowledge on and a clear understanding of integrated water resource management and/or water related sciences will be an added advantage.
  • Knowledge of international and national climate change policies, water resources, and the water sector as a whole.
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills (verbal and written).
  • Good liaison and organizational skills.
  • Skills in financial management, decision making, strategic capability and leadership, analytical, creativity, office administration, programme and project management, scientific methodologies and models, technical report writing.
  • Knowledge and experience of using electronic communication platforms and internet.
  • Applicants must be able to travel nationally and internationally as required to represent the Department and the sector.
