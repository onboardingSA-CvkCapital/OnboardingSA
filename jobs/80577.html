About the role

Ensure a customer centric approach in the efficient and effective, hands-on, daily delivery of training and development goals aligned with organisational design and South African legislative requirements. Design and implement training initiatives aligning with global standards to meet the South African requirements and work cross functionally, on all levels, to upskill the workforce and develop its people. To drive and manage the sourcing and acquisition of top talent, meeting organizational workforce needs (including the Employment Equity Plan requirements), whilst ensuring a fair, efficient and compliant hiring process. Ensure the efficient and effective design, implementation and delivery on all legislated, developmental and technical training requirements. Manage a very operational role with no reportees. Develop and implement effective training programs, including needs analysis, lesson planning, and content creation. Design and implement a training plan aligned with the organisational design requirements and relevant South African legislative requirements. In particular the EE plan of the business. Deliver classroom-based and virtual training across branches and dealerships nationally. Ensure good corporate governance regarding management and use of all external training service providers. Ensure professional and effective service to customers and all cross functional activities and ability to communicate with global and local stakeholders to fully meet the required training outputs. Excellent verbal and written communication skills for presentations, training materials, and stakeholder engagement. Ensure effective administration and reporting on all matters relating to acquisition and training. A good understanding of the Education Act, Skills development Act, Employment Equity Act and relevant regulations. Obtain and maintain SD certification and co-ordinate all SETA grant submissions timeously to obtain benefit of required grants and skills development benefits. Coordinate career development and career aspirations of Company into individual skills development actions plans and pathways, also in terms of Company Performance and Management Processes
