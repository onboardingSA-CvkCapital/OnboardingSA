About the role

AWS Cloud Engineer About the Role We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced AWS Cloud Engineer to join our growing technology team. This role is ideal for a cloud professional who is passionate about designing, implementing, securing, and optimising enterprise-scale cloud environments. As an AWS Cloud Engineer, you will play a key role in architecting and managing modern cloud solutions, supporting cloud migrations, driving automation through Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and ensuring environments remain secure, scalable, resilient, and cost-effective. You will also provide technical leadership, mentor team members, and contribute to cloud best practices across the organisation. Key Responsibilities Cloud Architecture & Engineering • Design, implement, and manage complex AWS cloud environments, including multi-account, multi-region, hybrid cloud, and multi-cloud architectures. • Lead cloud transformation and migration initiatives from on-premises and other cloud platforms to AWS. • Develop and maintain Infrastructure as Code (IaC) solutions using Terraform and/or AWS CloudFormation. • Ensure cloud environments align with AWS Well-Architected Framework principles, including Security, Reliability, Operational Excellence, Performance Efficiency, Cost Optimisation, and Sustainability. • Design and implement high availability, disaster recovery, and business continuity solutions.AWS Platform Management • Deploy, configure, and manage AWS services including: • IAM, Identity Center (SSO), and access governance • AWS Organizations, Organizational Units (OUs), and Service Control Policies (SCPs) • EC2, Auto Scaling Groups, and Load Balancers • VPCs, Transit Gateways, VPC Peering, Endpoints, VPNs, and Direct Connect • Security Groups and Network ACLs • Route 53 and DNS services • S3, lifecycle policies, access controls, and storage optimisation • RDS and DynamoDB • KMS and encryption key management across AWS accounts • CloudWatch, CloudTrail, Config, and operational monitoring • Control Tower and multi-account governance • Lambda and serverless solutions • Step Functions and workflow orchestration services • EKS, ECR, container platforms, and Kubernetes workloadsDevOps & Automation • Design and implement CI/CD pipelines using AWS native services and tooling such as GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, and CodePipeline. • Automate infrastructure deployment, configuration management, and operational processes. • Develop and maintain scripting solutions using Bash, PowerShell, Python, or similar languages. • Promote version control best practices, branching strategies, and DevOps operating models across teams.Security & Compliance • Drive cloud security best practices, governance standards, and compliance initiatives. • Implement identity, access management, encryption, monitoring, and auditing controls. • Support compliance frameworks such as GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001, and similar security standards. • Conduct security assessments and recommend remediation strategies.Monitoring, Support & Troubleshooting • Lead investigation and resolution of critical cloud incidents. • Perform advanced troubleshooting across infrastructure, networking, security, and application layers. • Diagnose connectivity issues across AWS, hybrid, and on-premises environments using tools such as VPC Flow Logs, CloudWatch, Route 53, and network tracing methodologies. • Support DNS troubleshooting, including integration with enterprise DNS solutions such as CoreDNS and Infoblox. • Establish monitoring, alerting, logging, and observability best practices.Leadership & Collaboration • Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior and intermediate engineers. • Contribute to architecture reviews, technical standards, and cloud governance initiatives. • Facilitate knowledge-sharing sessions, technical workshops, and cloud training programs. • Work closely with engineering, operations, security, and business stakeholders to deliver successful cloud solutions. • Contribute to Agile delivery practices, including Scrum and Kanban methodologies.Key Competencies • Strong cloud architecture and solution design capabilities. • Deep understanding of AWS services, cloud-native design patterns, and enterprise architecture principles. • Exceptional troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. • Strong communication and stakeholder management abilities. • Ability to lead technical discussions and influence cloud strategy. • Commitment to automation, operational excellence, and continuous improvement. • Strong mentoring and team development skills. • Ability to balance security, performance, scalability, and cost optimisation requirements.Required Skills & Experience • 5+ years' experience in AWS cloud engineering, cloud infrastructure, or cloud architecture roles. • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience). • AWS Certified Solutions Architect Professional, AWS Specialty and Amazon EKS • CI/CD pipeline implementation • Infrastructure automation and scripting • Networking and security architecture • Disaster recovery and business continuity planning • Experience with software development practices and the software development lifecycle (SDLC). • Strong understanding of Git and version control methodologies. • Knowledge of Active Directory, identity federation, and Single Sign-On (SSO) implementations. • Experience managing cloud costs through Reserved Instances, Savings Plans, and optimisation strategies. • Familiarity with Agile delivery methodologies.Nice to
