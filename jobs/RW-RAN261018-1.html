About the role

Rand Water is hiring a Business Analyst x2 in Strategy, based at Rietvlei, Glenvista. To provide a professional business analyst role, conduct market intelligence and industry trends, analyze insights and determine the impact on the Rand Water Business. Support the business with the compilation of business plans for new investments, programs, and projects as well as compelling business cases.

Responsibilities

  • Contribute to designing the Water Pricing Models as input to Tariff Structure
  • Conduct intelligence at the Sector level
  • Shareholder Compact compilation and Drafting
  • Conduct Trend Analysis on the Water Sector, both at a macro and micro level
  • Conduct Feasibility Studies for new investments
  • Contribute to the compilation of Business Cases
  • Contribute to the compilation of Business Plans
  • Contribute to the design of the Water Pricing Model as inputs to the tariff structure.
  • Business Plans and design Water Pricing Model as inputs to tariff structure.
  • Determine the shareholder value map with respect to business cost drivers
  • Support Cost Containment initiatives
  • Analysis of the business results, annual reports, and determine opportunity areas for business improvement
  • Prepare Governance Reports (Board Packs, EXCO Reports, and Business Reports)
