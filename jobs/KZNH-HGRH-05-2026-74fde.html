About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring an Operational Manager Nursing (Speciality Unit), based in HARRY GWALA REGIONAL HOSPITAL. This is a permanent post, paying R720,819 to R821,454. How to apply: ALL APPLICATIONS SHOULD BE FORWARDED TO Reference number to quote: HGRH 05.2026 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Co-ordination of optimal, holistic specialized nursing care provided within the set standards and professional / legal framework
  • Supervise provision of an effective efficient quality patient care
  • Foster team spirit and commitment among all categories of staff
  • Manage staff performance (EPMDS)
  • Reduce patient complaints, manage and mitigate Patient Safety Incidents in the unit
  • Manage disciplinary and labour issues including monitoring and managing absenteeism
  • Manage effectively and efficiently the utilization and supervision of all resources in the unit.
  • Coordinate provision of continuous professional development (CPD) in-service training and development of staff.
  • Participate in the analysis, formulation and implementation of nursing guidelines, standard operating procedures and practices
  • Establish and maintain constructive working relationship with Nursing and other Stakeholders
  • Maintain and sustain high standards of Infection Prevention and Control in Critical Care unit.
  • Ensure proper management of patients on mechanical ventilation.
  • Monitor and sustain OHSC Norms and Standards, Ideal Hospital Realization & Maintenance Framework audits and develop QIP for gaps identified.
  • Maintain accurate patient related data management system and record keeping. 4
  • Develop and implement quality assurance progress, Standard Operating Procedures, quality improvement projects & operational plans
  • Maintain professional growth/ ethical standards and self-development
  • Relief Assistant Manager Nursing in her /his absentia to act in her/his position with authority and accountability

Requirements

  • Senior certificate/National Senior Certificate/Matric
  • Diploma/Degree in General Nursing Science and Midwifery or equivalent qualification that allows registration with SANC as a Professional Nurse
  • Current registration with the South African Nursing Council as a Registered Nurse and Midwife/ Accoucheur
  • Post Basic qualification -One year Diploma in Critical Care Nursing Science
  • Current South African Nursing Council receipt - license to practice (2026)
  • A minimum of NINE YEARS appropriate experience in nursing after registration as a Professional with SANC in General Nursing
  • Atleast FIVE YEARS of the period referred above must be appropriate recognizable experience in the Critical Care speciality after obtaining the abovementioned Post Basic qualification
  • Certificate of Service endorsed by HR Department must be attached
  • Knowledge of nursing statutes and other relevant legislative frameworks
  • Knowledge of nursing care processes and procedures 3
  • Operational management, co-ordination, networking liaison skills
  • Knowledge of Critical Care and High Care Services
  • Knowledge of disaster management.
  • Knowledge of Health Care Service Delivery and financial management skills
  • Knowledge of disciplinary processes
  • The ability to function well with a team
  • Communication, counselling, problem solving and time management skills
  • Understanding of Human Resource needs and developments
  • Planning, organizing, decision making & report writing skills
  • Interpersonal skills in dealing with conflict management
  • Knowledge and implementation of Batho Pele principles
  • Supervisory and analytical thinking skills
