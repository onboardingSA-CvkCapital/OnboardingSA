About the role

Description: Hire Resolve's Client is currently looking for an experienced Legal Manager to join their mining company based in North-West. You will be responsible for providing strategic legal leadership, ensuring regulatory compliance, protecting mineral and corporate interests, and managing legal, contractual, litigation and stakeholder risks within a mining environment. Responsibilities: • Provide functional leadership and direction for the legal function. • Manage and oversee mining rights, prospecting rights and permits in accordance with the MPRDA. • Manage DMRE submissions, including Section 11, Section 102, renewals and Mining Work Programme requirements. • Ensure compliance with the MPRDA, MHSA, NEMA, NWA, Mining Charter and B-BBEE Act. • Manage Environmental Authorisations, Water Use Licences, Waste Licences and Air Emissions Licences. • Monitor and advise on changes in mining legislation, Mining Charter requirements, carbon tax and royalty legislation. • Draft, review and negotiate commercial agreements, including mining contractor, off-take, sale, transport, logistics, equipment hire, diesel supply, power, JV and shareholder agreements. • Draft and manage Service Level Agreements. • Manage procurement contracts and ensure appropriate governance and anti-corruption compliance. • Manage legal aspects of Social and Labour Plan requirements, including community development, local employment and procurement. • Handle land claims, surface rights disputes, resettlement matters and access-related issues. • Advise on royalty and community trust structures and community shareholding compliance. • Manage litigation, arbitration, labour disputes and regulatory appeals. • Instruct and manage external attorneys, counsel and advocates, including legal strategy and cost control. • Manage Section 54 and Section 93 MHSA appeals, DMRE directives and NEMA Section 28 directives. • Manage legal risks relating to investigative reporting, media and community unrest. • Act as Company Secretary and ensure compliance with the Companies Act. • Maintain statutory records, board resolutions, minutes and CIPC filings. • Manage compliance relating to POPIA, FICA and PAIA. • Provide legal advice to the Board, EXCO and relevant stakeholders. • Advise on employment law, contractor versus employee risks and union negotiations. • Manage legal processes relating to disciplinary matters, retrenchments and industrial action. • Manage stakeholder and community-related legal risks within the mining operation. Requirements: • LLB Degree • Admitted Attorney of the High Court of South Africa • Membership of the Law Society of South Africa / Legal Practice Council • Minimum 10 years' experience within the mining industry (Chrome/PGM REQUIRED) • Experience with MPRDA applications and DMRE engagement. • Experience drafting and negotiating mineral or chrome ore off-take agreements. • Experience with SLP and Mining Charter compliance. • Expert knowledge of the MPRDA, MHSA, NEMA, NWA and Mining Charter • Valid Driver's License Benefits: • Salary: Negotiable • Paid Time Off (PTO) (If Applicable) Contact Hire Resolve for your next career-changing move • Our client is offering a highly competitive salary for this role based on experience. • Apply for this role today, contact Janoke Booysen or Menina Campher at Hire Resolve or on LinkedIn • You can also visit the Hire Resolve website: hireresolve.us or email us your CV:  mining@hireresolve.za.comWe will contact you telephonically in 5 days should you be suitable for this vacancy.
