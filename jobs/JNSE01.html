About the role

Job Purpose To provide junior-level network and systems support to Afrocentric IP (Pty) Ltd and its clients, assisting with the maintenance, monitoring and troubleshooting of network and server infrastructure. The Junior Network & Systems Engineer works under the guidance of senior engineers to develop technical competence while ensuring reliable day-to-day support of IT infrastructure.  Note: This job description applies to two (2) identical Junior Network & Systems Engineer positions. Key Responsibilities 1. Network & Systems Support: •        Provide first- and second-line support for network and server infrastructure issues. •     Assist in the configuration of VLANs, Port-Channels, SVI's and Firewall policies. •     Perform routine checks and basic troubleshooting on network and server environments under guidance. •     Perform discovery and Assessment during new customer onboarding. •     Install Veeam Backup and Replication server and prepare the software with all required configurations to allow replications and backups. •     Perform data restores, failover and disaster recovery tests. •     Understanding of FortiGate firewall traffic filtering, port forwarding and alerts. •     Basic Linux command-line understanding. •     Escalate complex issues to senior engineers in a timely manner. •     Monitoring, Managing and troubleshoot Microsoft Hyper-V cluster. •     Monitoring. Managing and troubleshooting VMWare cluster. •     Configure Virtual Switches and Virtual Ports as required. 2. Monitoring & Maintenance by: •         Monitor network and systems performance dashboards, flagging anomalies to senior engineers. •     Assist with scheduled maintenance activities, including patching and backups. •     Support capacity and performance monitoring of network and server infrastructure. •     Monitor Veeam backups and replications and troubleshoot when there are failures. •     Monitor Veeam server storage capacity. 3. Documentation & Reporting by: •       Maintain accurate records of support tickets, configurations and maintenance activities. •     Update network and systems documentation as changes are implemented. •     Prepare basic reports on support activity and recurring issues. •     Prepare Weekly and Monthly backup and replication reports. •     Produce a DR Test report. 4.   Skills Development & Teamwork by: •         Actively participate in on-the-job training and mentoring provided by senior engineers. •     Pursue relevant technical certifications to build skills and competence. •     Collaborate effectively with team members to ensure consistent service delivery.
