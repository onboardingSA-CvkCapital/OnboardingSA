About the role

At the Faircape Group, we’re more than a collection of companies; we’re a dynamic ecosystem driving progress across sectors that matter. From renewable energy, telecommunications, and infrastructure, to retirement living and property, our impact is broad, meaningful, and purpose-driven. As our marketing function continues to grow, we’re expanding the team and introducing a new Brand Manager role. The Brand Manager will take ownership of Faircape Life Faircape Life is focused on premium retirement living, creating environments that are secure, well-managed, and designed for quality of life.
