About the role

Motus Aftermarket Parts is seeking an organised, detail-oriented, and deadline-driven Administrator: Safety & Sustainability to support the Risk Office at Meadowview with the administration, consolidation, and reporting of safety, sustainability, and carbon-related information across the organisation. The successful candidate will be responsible for collecting, validating, consolidating, and analysing safety and sustainability data from branches, ensuring the accuracy, completeness, and integrity of reporting. The role requires close collaboration with branches and stakeholders to support reporting requirements, maintain accurate records, and contribute to audits, assessments, and continuous improvement initiatives.
