About the role

At Faircape Health Institute and Faircape Life, we are committed to delivering high-quality healthcare services within our luxury retirement villages and healthcare environments. Our focus is on promoting quality of life, continuity of care, and clinical excellence through well-supported multidisciplinary teams and holistic resident care. We are seeking a compassionate, clinically confident, and service-driven Medical Officer – Geriatric & Primary Care Services to join our team, working across Heritage Manor and Bridgewater Manor in Somerset West. The role will provide medical care to long-term healthcare patients as well as residents living independently within our retirement villages. This position is ideal for a doctor who enjoys building long-term relationships with patients, residents and their families, providing continuity of care and managing a broad range of healthcare needs associated with ageing. Rather than a traditional high-volume practice environment, this role provides the opportunity to get to know your patients and residents, follow their healthcare journey over time, and provide proactive medical support as their needs change. You will be exposed to a broad range of healthcare needs, including geriatric medicine, chronic disease management, preventative healthcare, primary care and multidisciplinary care planning. The successful candidate will work closely with nursing staff, allied healthcare professionals, care teams and village management across Heritage Manor and Bridgewater Manor to ensure our long-term patients and village residents receive proactive, holistic and high-quality medical care.
