About the role

🚀 We’re Hiring: Senior Operations Manager | Lesaka International Are you an operational leader with a passion for process optimisation, project execution, digital transformation, and driving operational excellence across multiple markets? Lesaka International is looking for a Senior Operations Manager to join our Operations team. This role is ideal for a highly organised and influential professional who can drive cross-functional coordination, operational governance, process improvement, and strategic execution across multiple countries while supporting scalable growth within a fast-paced fintech environment. 💼 Job Purpose To drive operational coordination, governance, and execution across Lesaka International's operations, providing scalable operational support to in-country markets. The successful candidate will act as a central integration point across Operations, Corporate POS, Risk & Compliance, Customer Care, and Group Shared Services, ensuring alignment, visibility, governance, and continuous improvement. The role supports operational excellence through process optimisation, project delivery, AI and automation enablement, operational reporting, and ownership of the International Operations Playbook. 🔍
