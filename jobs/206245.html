About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies has an opportunity available for a Communication & Storytelling Lead for their Head Office. The Communication & Storytelling Lead is responsible for defining and embedding communication frameworks that ensure clear, consistent, and on-brand messaging across internal and external channels. The role leads employee and external communication, aligning messaging to business priorities, strengthening engagement, and protecting Dis-Chem’s reputation within an integrated healthcare retail environment.
