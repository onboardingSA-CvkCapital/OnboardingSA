About the role

The Department of Health (Mpumalanga) is hiring a Provisioning Administrative Officer: Logistics Management, based in Provincial Office, Mbombela (Nelspruit). This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: Departmental Online Application System: www.mpuhealth.gov.za Reference number to quote: MPDOH/OCT/26/1645 Closing date: 23 October 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise and render demand and acquisition services.
  • Supervise and undertake logistical services.
  • Supervise and control the work of sub-ordinate (including training) in the supply chain management unit.
  • Deal with more advanced and complicated matters pertaining to provisioning administration.
  • Manage all aspects of orders such as quotations, purchase and processing of requisitions, authorization of procurement advice and guidance to all clients, chief user clerks and responsibility managers.
  • Ensure compliance with departmental and Treasury Regulations.
  • Evaluate and test compliance of all purchasing transactions.
  • Manage all open orders.
  • Deal with Human resources matters such as leave and perform development system of quarterly evaluation reports.
  • Adhere to regulations and legislation pertaining supply chain management unit.
  • Facilitate and administer requisitions for good and services.
  • Implement internal procurement policies, regulations systems and procedures.
  • Maintain procurement and provisioning systems and processes.
  • Administer payment processes.
  • Knowledge of BAS and LOGIS financial systems.
  • Ability to work under pressure.
  • Supervision of the subordinates.
  • Attend and respond to audit queries.

Requirements

  • Senior Certificate/Grade 12 or equivalent qualification plus 3 years relevant experience in Supply Chain Management or Diploma/Degree (NQF Level 6/7) in Finance/Logistics/Procurement/Supply Chain Management/Financial/Accounting Management as recognised by SAQA.
  • Knowledge and experience of provisioning administration, main focus in the following areas: LOGIS, Preferential Procurement Policy Framework (PPPFA), Public Finance Management Act (PFMA) and Treasury Regulations and general knowledge of Basic accounting system (BAS).
  • Computer literacy.
  • Sound interpersonal and communication skills (written and verbal).
  • Good management and supervisory skills.
  • Ability to work independently and tight deadlines.
  • Valid driver’s licence will be an added advantage.
