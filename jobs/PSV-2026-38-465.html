Responsibilities

  • Assessing and inspecting of vehicles: Inspect accident - damaged vehicles and assess panel, chassis and mechanical damage to scope repair work, Conduct pre-repair and post repair inspections to confirm all work meets OEM specifications and SABS standards, Verify repair estimates vs actual damage to prevent over quoting and fraud by panel beaters/mechanics.
  • Control compliance and quality: Check panel beating quality for example panel alignment, filter thickness, paint match, overspray and panel gaps, Check Mechanical quality: Engine, brakes, suspension, steering, exhaust, electrical systems post repair, Ensure road worthiness compliance with NRTA and vehicle licensing requirements before signing off.
  • Control cost and documentation: Compile inspection reports with photos, checklists and recommendations with SLA, Approve or reject invoices based on scope of work completed vs quoted, Maintain a vehicle repair register and track turnaround times, repeat faults and warranty claims.
  • Liaise with workshop staff: Liaise with panel beaters, mechanics and service providers to rectify defective workmanship, Investigate breakdowns, repeated failures or customer complaints and submit findings, Advise on vehicle condition for condemnation, auction or continued use decisions.

Requirements

  • Minimum Requirement
  • NQF Level 4 Motor industry certificate and Trade Test as Motor mechanic, Panel Beater or Motor Body repair
  • Driver’s License
  • No criminal record. knowledge of Planning and organizing, Knowledge and understanding of Treasury and SCM framework, Relevant legislation and Public Service Regulations and communication practices and good knowledge of the public service management framework.
  • Relevant experience: 1-2 years hand on panel beating/mechanical and inspection.
