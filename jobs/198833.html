About the role

Reporting to: Creditors Manager Location: Westlake *Please note that the location of this role will be moving to Montague Park in May 2027. Cape Herb & Spice has grown from entrepreneurial roots into a global FMCG player, employing over 500 people and operating across 4 sites in Cape Town. Centered around innovation and quality, Cape Herb and Spice gives any candidate a great opportunity to gain extensive international experience and build an exciting career. Purpose: The Creditors Controller is responsible for the accurate and timely processing, reconciliation, and payment of supplier accounts, ensuring strong supplier relationships, compliance with company policies, and effective cash flow management. Key Responsibilities: Accounts Payable Management • Process supplier invoices accurately and timeously • Match invoices to purchase orders and delivery notes • Ensure correct VAT treatment in line with SARS requirements • Capture invoices onto the accounting system • Prepare creditor reconciliations monthly and resolve discrepanciesPayments & Cash Flow • Prepare weekly and monthly payment runs • Ensure payments are made according to agreed supplier terms • Obtain required approvals before payments are released • Manage ad-hoc and urgent payments where requiredSupplier & Stakeholder Management • Maintain accurate supplier master data • Respond to supplier queries professionally and timeously • Build and maintain strong relationships with suppliers • Resolve pricing, quantity, and invoice disputesCompliance & Controls • Ensure adherence to company financial policies and procedures • Maintain proper documentation and audit trails • Assist with internal and external audits relating to creditors • Support month-end and year-end closing processes Reporting & Administration • Prepare creditors age analysis and reports • Highlight overdue accounts and potential risks • Assist with forecasting creditor payments • Perform general finance administration as required Role Capabilities: • Accuracy of creditor reconciliations • On-time supplier payments • Reduction in outstanding and disputed invoices • Compliance with internal controls and deadlines • Strong numerical and reconciliation skills • High attention to detail and accuracy • Good understanding of VAT and accounting principles • Proficient in accounting systems (e.g. Pastel, Sage, SAP, Syspro) • Strong Excel skills • Excellent communication and organisational skills • Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines Education and Required Knowledge: • Diploma or Certificate in Finance, Accounting, or Bookkeeping • Accounting-related qualification advantageous Personal Characteristics: • Trustworthy
