About the role

ROLE PURPOSE Broll Property Group is recruiting a full-time Account Manager within its Integrated Facilities Management (IFM) portfolio. This is a full-time account management role in which the successful candidate may be required to manage multiple client accounts. The Account Manager will lead client engagement and IFM service delivery across allocated client site(s), driving operational excellence, compliance, cost control, and client satisfaction through proactive, data-led facilities management. KEY RESPONSBILITIES 1) Client & Stakeholder Management • Act as the primary contact for client stakeholders at site and area levels. • Build and maintain trust-based relationships across operational and commercial levels. • Lead client operational engagement reviews, Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) and tactical discussions. • Ensure alignment between client expectations and contracted service delivery. 2) Operational Management • Lead day-to-day IFM operations and continuous improvement across allocated site(s). • Ensure delivery of contracted services including planned preventative maintenance (PPM), reactive maintenance, inspections and soft services. • Drive compliance with service level agreements (SLAs), key performance indicators (KPIs) and contractual obligations. • Implement best practices and standard operating procedures to ensure consistent service delivery. • Leverage head office support to strengthen processes, quality and operational performance. • Identify and deliver continuous improvement initiatives across all operational areas. 3) Financial Management • Manage site and regional budgets including baseline, reactive and non-controllable spend. • Identify cost-saving opportunities and drive efficiencies while protecting compliance and service quality. • Analyse spending trends, implement corrective actions and ensure disciplined financial control. • Deliver accurate forecasting and reporting for governance and decision-making. • Lead or support benchmarking initiatives as required. 4) Team Leadership & Development • Provide leadership and support to Site Leads and operational teams to meet performance requirements. • Build a high-performance culture with clear accountability and ownership. • Ensure appropriate resourcing and workforce planning aligned to site demand and scope. • Address performance gaps and support structured improvement plans and coaching. 5) Compliance & Risk Management • Ensure compliance with statutory, safety and environmental requirements. • Plan and oversee audits and inspections, and drive closure of corrective actions. • Manage contractor compliance, including safety documentation, permits and site controls. • Identify and mitigate operational and contractual risks proactively. 6) Systems, Reporting & Digital Enablement • Drive effective use of CMMS and digital tools for asset and maintenance management. • Lead or support digital implementation projects within the client site environment. • Ensure accurate data capture to enable reliable reporting and decisions. • Prepare and present weekly, monthly and quarterly performance reports. • Use analytics to identify trends, root causes and improvement opportunities. 7) Continuous Improvement & Innovation • Convert reactive work into planned maintenance where feasible and beneficial. • Promote innovation in service delivery (e.g., IoT, predictive maintenance, automation) where appropriate. • Improve service quality and operational efficiency through structured improvement initiatives. • Support digital transformation initiatives that strengthen IFM outcomes. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS(KPIs) • SLA and KPI compliance across allocated site(s). • Client satisfaction, engagement quality and retention. • Budget adherence, forecasting accuracy and cost optimisation. • Reduction in reactive maintenance spend over time. • Audit results and compliance performance. • Team performance, engagement and stability. QUALITIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE • Qualification in Facilities Management and/or Engineering, and/or related field OR 10 years proven IFM experience on industrial and/or manufacturing and/or large commercial sites. • Proven experience in the Integrated Facilities Management sphere as a site lead or 2nd in charge (min 10 years’ experience) • Strong Management, Operational, Financial and Commercial acumen. (min 10 years’ experience) • Strong CRM, Operations and administrative knowledge of an IFM environment. (min 10 years’ experience) • Experience with CMMS and reporting tools is an advantage CORE COMPETENCIES • Strong leadership and people management skills. • Strategic thinking combined with hands-on execution. • Excellent communication and stakeholder management. • Structured problem-solving and sound decision-making. • High attention to detail and accountability. • Project management capability and continuous improvement mindset.
