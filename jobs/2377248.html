About the role

Our client is seeking an experienced Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Manager to lead the maintenance and operational performance of a large-scale renewable energy facility based in De Aar, Northern Cape. This leadership role offers the opportunity to oversee utility-scale solar PV and battery energy storage assets while ensuring world-class operational performance, safety and compliance. Key Responsibilities • Lead all operations and maintenance activities to maximise plant performance and reliability. • Manage site personnel, supervisors, contractors and subcontractors. • Ensure compliance with contractual obligations, technical standards and regulatory requirements. • Drive Health, Safety and Environmental (HSE) excellence across all site activities. • Monitor plant performance, investigate faults and implement corrective actions to minimise downtime. • Manage preventative and corrective maintenance programmes. • Oversee contractor performance and ensure quality service delivery. • Support budgeting, reporting, forecasting and operational planning. • Coordinate plant upgrades, optimisation initiatives and post-EPC close-out activities. • Maintain effective relationships with stakeholders, clients and internal teams. • Ensure compliance with project agreements, permits, licences and governance requirements. Minimum Requirements • BSc Engineering, BTech or equivalent in Electrical Engineering. • Qualified Electrician with GMR 2.1 certification. • Minimum of 10 years' experience in electrical and/or mechanical maintenance. • Proven experience within utility-scale renewable energy projects, preferably wind or solar plants exceeding 50MW. • Strong leadership, contractor management and conflict resolution skills. • Knowledge of SCADA systems, HV switching and power plant operations. • Thorough understanding of the Occupational Health and Safety Act. • Strong administrative, reporting and planning skills. • Willingness to travel and work overtime or shifts when operationally required.
