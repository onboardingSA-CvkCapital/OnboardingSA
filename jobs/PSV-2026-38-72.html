About the role

The Department of Correctional Services is hiring a Senior Provisioning Administration Officer: Provisioning Administration System Training in Logistics, based in National Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: should be submitted online at https://careers.dcs.gov.za/. Emailed, post or hand delivered applications will not be considered. Reference number to quote: HO 2026/11/69 Closing date: 09 November 2026 @ 15H45. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Appointment under the Public Service Act.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor LOGIS system.
  • Ensure that all prescribed files are opened, completed and signed per financial year.
  • Set up and maintain store specific codes for the store to function as expected.
  • Undertake LOGIS system Logistical inspection.
  • Allocate functions to user.
  • Assist in performing inspections at stores.
  • Monitor the implementation of Logistics policy.
  • Ensure that LOGIS users follow all procedures set by the LOGIS policies.
  • Ensure compliance with relevant policies by facilitating adherence to processes in Logistical services.
  • Monitor LOGIS Balance Score Card.
  • Check the scores per store and identify areas for improvement as per the standard set by National Treasury.
  • Assist users in clearing the transactions on the system.
  • Management of performance information, human resources, finance and assets.

Requirements

  • Recognized Degree or National Diploma in Logistics/Supply Chain Management/Public Administration.
  • 3-5 years relevant experience.
  • Knowledge of LOGIS System.
  • Computer literate.
  • Valid driver’s licence.
  • Competencies And Attributes:
  • Knowledge of PFMA
  • Knowledge of Treasury Regulations, Operational Plan, Strategic plan, Public Administration Service Act, White Paper
  • Understanding of policies, manuals and circulars
  • Knowledge and understanding of LOGIS system. communication, planning and organising, interpersonal, analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Liaison (internal and external)
  • Honesty, integrity and commitment
  • Creativity, logical and innovative
