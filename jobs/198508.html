About the role

1. Programme Background ForAfrika is a leading African humanitarian and development organisation operating in eight countries, with a vision of an Africa that thrives. Under a three-year grant from the donor, ForAfrika South Africa will assume full operational responsibility for existing solar-powered rural water systems across Mpumalanga, Limpopo and KwaZulu-Natal. The programme's central premise is that sustainable rural water service delivery can be elevated into a Solar Water Enterprise Hub — a community-owned platform combining a managed rural water utility, a formalised Water Management Committee (WMC), and a nucleus for local economic activity including kiosk-based last-mile distribution, productive use of water, and community savings structures. ForAfrika's role is to maintain, operate, and transfer these systems to community ownership. 2. Purpose of the Position The Community Mobilisation Officer is the programme's frontline community-facing role within their assigned cluster. The position drives the social, governance, and economic mobilisation work that makes the technical infrastructure sustainable: establishing and strengthening Water Management Committees, activating community savings groups (VSLA-equivalent structures) under ForAfrika's social enterprise division, mobilising Hub kiosk operators, facilitating community engagement and behaviour change, and building the community ownership pathway. The KwaZulu-Natal position is designated the highest-intensity governance support deployment in the South Africa portfolio, given the lowest committee coverage in the portfolio; the Mpumalanga position manages the largest site concentration; and the Limpopo position supports the portfolio's most advanced cluster toward accelerated community self-management. 3.
