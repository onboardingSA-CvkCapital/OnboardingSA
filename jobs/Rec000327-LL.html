About the role

We are recruiting on behalf of a proudly South African engineering company specialising in innovative mineral processing solutions, with a strong footprint in the mining and manufacturing sectors. The business is expanding its Klerksdorp operation and is looking for a Red Seal qualified Millwright to keep a busy steel manufacturing facility running safely and reliably. Duties and Responsibilities • Perform preventive, predictive and breakdown maintenance across the facility • Maintain and repair mechanical systems, including gearboxes, drives, conveyors and rotating equipment • Inspect, maintain and troubleshoot overhead cranes, hoists and fixed lifting systems, and provide maintenance support to the mobile equipment fleet such as forklifts and similar material handling equipment • Diagnose and repair industrial hydraulic and pneumatic systems • Conduct electrical fault finding on motors, control circuits, limit switches, sensors, VSDs and basic PLC I/O • Read and interpret mechanical, electrical, hydraulic and pneumatic drawings and schematics • Identify root causes of failures and implement permanent corrective actions to improve equipment reliability • Assist with the installation, commissioning and modification of new and upgraded plant equipment • Ensure strict compliance with health, safety and lock out procedures • Complete job cards, inspections, maintenance reports and statutory records • Work closely with production teams to minimise downtime and improve operational efficiencyExperience and Qualifications • Red Seal qualified Millwright (trade tested) • Grade 12 or equivalent • 3 to 5 years' experience maintaining steel manufacturing or heavy industrial plant equipment • Solid mechanical, hydraulic and electrical maintenance and fault finding experience • Proven experience with industrial hydraulic systems and a strong understanding of hydraulic theory and components • Experience in preventative and corrective maintenance • Strong troubleshooting and problem solving ability • Able to work independently and as part of a team • Physically capable of lifting heavy components and working in confined spaces • Background in manufacturing, mining, construction or heavy equipment environments
