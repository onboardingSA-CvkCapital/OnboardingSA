About the role

Omnia is hiring an Assistant (Quality Controller) in BME Production and Logistics, based in Losberg. The role reports to the Production Manager. The purpose of the position is to assist the Process Controller in their duties and is also responsible for the safe and efficient determination of product conformance through sampling and measurement of product characteristics.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct sampling and measurement of cartridges
  • To constantly conduct sampling and measurement of cartridges
  • Record results during the manufacturing process and identify defects or non-conformance of products as early as possible in the process to minimize waste and ensure quality products to customers
  • Standby duties as a Process Controller
  • Assist the Process Controller during start-up and shutdown and do minor maintenance of the Rota-clip machine to ensure manufacturing efficiency of 85% of design capacity.
  • Process efficiency
  • Safe and timeous preparation of gassing solution during the manufacturing of cartridge explosives thus ensuring production of less than 0.5% waste.
  • Optimize productivity and quality
  • Proactively plan for resources that may be needed during the shift to minimize downtime
  • Adherence to sound SHERQ principles
  • Wear correct PPE
  • Adhere to good housekeeping principles
  • Constantly monitor the production process to ensure quality standards are met.

Requirements

  • Qualifications
  • Grade 12 or equivalent (Maths and Science)
  • Suitable Technical qualification (Advantageous)
  • Experience
  • 1 year's demonstrated experience in a manufacturing environment or plant operation.
  • Key Competencies
  • Proven written and verbal communication skills
  • Knowledge of the production processes
  • Ability to meet daily target
  • Proven computer literacy in Excel
  • Proven written and verbal communication skills
