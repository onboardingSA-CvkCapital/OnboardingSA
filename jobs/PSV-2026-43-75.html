About the role

The Government Pensions Administration Agency is hiring an Administrator: Risk, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R280,278 a year. Enterprise-Wide Risk Permanent The purpose of this role is to provide effective administrative and operational support to the Risk Management function through the coordination of risk management processes, maintenance of risk information systems, monitoring of risk action plans, and facilitation of risk management activities and reporting. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.gpaa.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: ADMIN/RISK/2026/11-1P Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) No late applications will be considered Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: One permanent position for Administrator: Risk is currently available in the Enterprise-Wide Risk Unit at the Government Pensions Administration Agency - Head Office

Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for a wide variety of tasks which include but are not limited to the following: Risk Governance Administration.
  • Governance structure committee packs compiled and distributed.
  • Agendas prepared.
  • Minutes and attendance registers maintained and resolutions/action items tracked.
  • Risk engagement meetings scheduled and coordinated.
  • Risk assessment workshops facilitated.
  • Serve as first point of contact for risk champions and business units.
  • Risk Information Administration.
  • Risks, controls risk action plans captured on BarnOwl.
  • Risk status updates maintained.
  • Risk reports generated.
  • Risk database maintained.
  • Support evidence collection for audits and reviews.
  • Maintain records.
  • Risk Analysis and Monitoring process.
  • Risk registers updated.
  • Risk action plans monitored.
  • Progress reports compiled.
  • Draft risk reports prepared.
  • Corrective actions tracked on BarnOwl.

Requirements

  • Grade 12/Senior certificate, with at least 1 to 2 years' experience in risk management, governance, compliance, internal control or related administrative environment.
  • Knowledge of Public Finance Management Act (PFMA).
  • Knowledge of Risk Management Framework.
  • Knowledge of Barn Owl system.
  • Knowledge of Public Service Act and Regulations.
  • Knowledge of Risk Assessment Methodologies.
  • POPIA.
  • Knowledge of Corporate Governance Principles.
  • Time Management Skills.
  • Interpersonal Relations Skills.
  • Report Writing Skills.
  • Communication Skills.
  • Facilitation Skills.
  • Planning and Administration Skills.
  • Ability to work effectively under pressure.
  • Result Oriented.
  • Initiative.
  • Attention to detail.
  • Integrity and Confidentiality.
  • Client Service Orientation.
  • Accountability.
  • Reliability.
