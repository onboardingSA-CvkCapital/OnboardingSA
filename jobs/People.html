About the role

People Officer - Distribution (JB6187) Location: Northriding, Johannesburg Salary: R40,000 - R50,000 per month (CTC) Employment Type: Initial 3-Month FTC, with possible conversion to permanent. If you are an HR professional who thrives on being in the field, working across diverse business environments, and making a real difference to how people and businesses operate, this role is worth a serious look. A direct-selling company with a well-established distributor network across South Africa and select African countries is looking for a People Officer who will take HR compliance from a concept to a lived reality at distributor level. This is not a desk job. The successful candidate will travel regularly to distributor sites, conduct structured HR compliance audits, identify gaps, and work hands-on with distributors to close them. The role exists at the intersection of HR expertise, relationship management, and legislative knowledge, and it requires someone who can engage credibly at all levels of a business while navigating the practical realities of multi-site, cross-border travel. What's in it for you CTC package of R40,000 to R50,000 per month, inclusive of a travel allowance and fleet card for field travel A role with genuine variety, no two distributor sites are the same Exposure to cross-border operations across southern and east Africa The opportunity to build and shape HR compliance frameworks from the ground up across a national and regional network A company franchisees, distributors, or branches Sound working knowledge of South African labour legislation including the BCEA, LRA, EEA, National Minimum Wage Act, Skills Development Act, OHSA, and COIDA
