About the role

General Foreman / Senior Foreman – Building Construction | Cape Town Leading Cape Town Building Contractor A well-established and highly regarded building contractor is seeking an experienced General Foreman / Senior Foreman to join their team in Cape Town. This is an excellent opportunity for a hands-on construction professional with strong leadership skills and a proven track record managing building projects from site establishment through to completion. Key Responsibilities • Take overall responsibility for the day-to-day management and coordination of construction activities on site. • Lead and manage Foremen, subcontractors, tradesmen and site teams. • Ensure projects are completed safely, on programme and to the required quality standards. • Coordinate subcontractors and ensure efficient sequencing of works. • Monitor productivity, materials, plant and resources. • Interpret and work from construction drawings, specifications and programmes. • Liaise closely with the Site Agent, Contracts Manager, Project Manager and professional team. • Identify and resolve construction challenges proactively. • Maintain high standards of workmanship, housekeeping and site discipline. • Ensure compliance with health and safety requirements. • Monitor progress against project programmes and report on potential delays. • Assist with quality control, snagging and project close-out.Requirements • 10+ years' experience within the building construction industry. • Proven experience as a General Foreman, Senior Foreman or equivalent senior site position. • Strong experience on commercial, residential, retail, industrial or similar building projects. • Solid understanding of construction methodologies, sequencing and site operations. • Ability to read and interpret architectural, structural and engineering drawings. • Strong subcontractor and workforce management skills. • Excellent leadership, communication and problem-solving abilities. • Ability to work under pressure and meet demanding project deadlines. • Relevant construction qualification will be advantageous.The Ideal Candidate We are looking for a strong site leader who is practical, organised and respected by the teams around them. You should be comfortable taking ownership of a busy construction site and have the confidence and experience to drive productivity, quality and programme.
