About the role

JUNIOR PAYROLL ASSISTANT -- 3-MONTH CONTRACT Department: Payroll / Retail Division Employment Type: Temporary -- 3-Month Contract Industry: Temporary Employment Services (TES) / Labour Broking Purpose: Peak-Season Support POSITION SUMMARY We are looking for a Junior Payroll Assistant to provide additional payroll administration support within our Retail Division during the peak season. The successful candidate will primarily be responsible for the accurate and timely capturing of employee timesheets onto Excel, as well as general payroll administration and data-capturing support. This is a high-volume, deadline-driven environment, and the successful candidate must be comfortable working with large amounts of information while maintaining a high level of accuracy. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Capture employee timesheets accurately onto Excel. Check timesheets for completeness and accuracy before capturing. Verify hours worked and identify discrepancies or missing information. Follow up with supervisors or relevant departments regarding incomplete or incorrect timesheets. Assist with the preparation and checking of payroll information. Capture and maintain employee payroll-related data. Assist with general payroll administration and filing. Maintain accurate records and ensure all information is captured within required deadlines. Assist the payroll team with additional administrative duties during the peak season. Handle confidential employee and payroll information appropriately. Work closely with the payroll and operations teams to resolve queries and ensure accurate payroll inputs. REQUIREMENTS Matric. Previous administration, data capturing or payroll administration experience. Strong Excel skills. Excellent data-capturing accuracy and attention to detail. Previous timesheet capturing experience will be advantageous. Experience in a high-volume environment will be advantageous. Ability to work to strict deadlines. Good numerical ability. Strong organisational and administrative skills. Ability to identify discrepancies and follow up to obtain the correct information. Good communication skills. Reliable and able to work effectively under pressure. ADVANTAGEOUS EXPERIENCE Experience in any of the following environments will be beneficial: Temporary Employment Services (TES) Labour broking Recruitment Payroll services Retail Warehousing Logistics High-volume staffing environments IDEAL CANDIDATE The ideal candidate will be administratively strong, highly accurate and comfortable working with Excel and large volumes of timesheet data. This person does not necessarily need to be an experienced payroll professional. The most important requirements are accuracy, Excel competency, numerical ability, attention to detail and the ability to work quickly under pressure during peak season. CONTRACT DETAILS Contract Duration: 3 Months Division: Retail Reason for Contract: Peak-Season Payroll Support
