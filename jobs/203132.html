About the role

Kern doel van die pos: Om volle verantwoordelikheid te neem vir die doeltreffende dag-tot-dagbestuur van OVK se Distribusiesentrum in Bloemfontein, met ‘n sterk fokus op dienslewering, voorraadbeheer, roete-uitvoering, mensebestuur, kostebeheer en verlies beperking. Minimum Vereistes: • Graad 12; ‘n toepaslike kwalifikasie in logistiek, voorsieningsketting, pakhuishouding of bestuur is ‘n sterk aanbeveling; • Minstens 5 jaar toepaslike ondervinding in ‘n verspreidingsentrum, pakhuis of logistieke omgewing; • Minstens 2 jaar ondervinding in ‘n toesighoudende of bestuursrol; • Bewese ondervinding in roetebeplanning, voorraadbeheer, personeel- en vloot bestuur; • Ondervinding in ‘n landbou-, kleinhandel- of FMCG-verspreidingsomgewing sal ‘n aanbeveling wees; • Kennis van aankoopprosesse, begrotingsbeheer, beroepsgesondheid en veiligheid, sowel as toepaslike vervoervereistes sal ‘n aanbeveling wees; • Goeie rekenaarvaardigheid en ervaring in AS400, voorraad- of pakhuisstelsels; • Geldige rybewys en bereidwilligheid om buite normale ure beskikbaar te wees wanneer bedrywighede dit vereis. Vaardighede: • Sterk organisasie-, kommunikasie- en probleemoplossingsvermoë; • Akkuraatheid, verantwoordbaarheid en die vermoë om onder operasionele druk te presteer; • Diensgerigte ingesteldheid en goeie samewerking met interne rolspelers. Verantwoordelikhede: • Bestuur die Distribusiesentrum se daaglikse bedrywighede en verseker veilige, doeltreffende en koste-effektiewe uitvoering; • Beplan en skeduleer afleweringsroetes; monitor nakoming van roetes, afsnytye en diensvlakke; • Skakel deurlopend met groepaankopers en ander interne rolspelers oor voorraadbehoeftes, beskikbaarheid en prioriteite; • Behartig aangewese aankope binne goedgekeurde beleid, begrotings, magtigings en voorraadparameters; • Pas toepaslike operasionele, voorraad-, finansiële en sekuriteitskontroles toe en rapporteer afwykings; • Bestuur personeel, drywers, prestasie, dissipline, opleiding, skofte en kapasiteitsbeplanning; • Bestuur die vloot, insluitend voertuigbeskikbaarheid, lisensies, dienste, instandhouding, brandstofverbruik en inspeksies; • Koördineer voorraadopnames en siklustellings; ondersoek en korrigeer voorraadverskille; • Verseker netheid, orde, veilige stapeling en goeie huishouding in die pakhuis en werf; • Bestuur terugsendings, eise en opvolgaksies tot finale afhandeling en beperk verliese, skade en verouderde voorraad. OVK is ‘n gelyke geleenthede werkgewer. Ons moedig persone uit aangewese groepe, asook persone met gestremdhede, aan om aansoek te doen. Core purpose of position: To take full responsibility for the efficient day-to-day management of OVK's Distribution Centre in Bloemfontein, with a strong focus on service delivery, inventory control, route execution, people management, cost control and loss limitation. Minimum Requirements: • Grade 12; an appropriate qualification in logistics, supply chain, warehouse housekeeping, or management is a strong recommendation; • At least 5 years of relevant experience in a distribution center, warehouse, or logistics environment; • At least 2 years of experience in a supervisory or managerial role; • Proven experience in route planning, inventory control, personnel and fleet management; • Experience in an agricultural, retail, or FMCG distribution environment will be a recommendation; • Knowledge of purchasing processes, budget control, occupational health and safety, as well as applicable transportation requirements will be a recommendation; • Good computer skills and experience in AS400, inventory or warehouse systems; • Valid driver's license and willingness to be available outside normal hours when operations require it. Skills: • Strong organisational, communication, and problem-solving abilities; • Accuracy, accountability, and the ability to perform under operational pressure; • Service-oriented mindset and good cooperation with internal role players. Responsibilities: • Manage the Distribution Center's day-to-day operations and ensure safe, efficient, and cost-effective execution; • Plan and schedule delivery routes; monitor compliance with routes, cut-off times, and service levels; • Continuously liaise with group buyers and other internal role players about inventory needs, availability and priorities; • Manage designated purchases within approved policies, budgets, authorizations, and inventory parameters; • Apply appropriate operational, inventory, financial, and security controls and report deviations; • Managing staff, drivers, performance, discipline, training, shifts, and capacity planning; • Manage the fleet, including vehicle availability, licenses, services, maintenance, fuel consumption, and inspections; • Coordinate inventory surveys and cycle counts; investigate and correct inventory differences; • Ensure cleanliness, order, safe stacking and good housekeeping in the warehouse and yard; • Manage returns, claims, and follow-up actions to final settlement and limit losses, damage, and obsolete inventory. OVK is an equal opportunities employer. We encourage individuals from designated groups, as well as individuals with disabilities, to apply.
