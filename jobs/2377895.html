About the role

Operations Manager (Wastewater Treatment) - Gauteng (Site-based) - Salary: R35 000 CTC + Medical Aid + Fuel Card About the Role An exciting opportunity has arisen for a results-driven Operations Manager to oversee the day-to-day performance, safety, and compliance of multiple wastewater treatment plants. This role ensures reliable plant performance, regulatory compliance, operator development, and operational excellence across all sites. You will lead a team of seven operators, providing technical guidance, performance management, and operational leadership. Key Responsibilities • Operational Oversight: Manage the daily operations of multiple plants to ensure optimal performance, uptime, and compliance with design parameters. Monitor plant KPIs, effluent quality, chemical usage, and process stability. • Team Leadership & Development: Lead, mentor, and supervise a team of seven operators. Conduct performance reviews, skills assessments, and training plans to foster a culture of accountability and safety. • Compliance & Quality Assurance: Ensure all plants operate in full compliance with national water quality regulations and environmental standards. Coordinate sampling, laboratory testing, and maintain accurate operational logs. • Technical Support & Troubleshooting: Provide hands-on technical guidance for process optimization, troubleshooting, and corrective actions. Collaborate with engineering teams on upgrades and support commissioning activities. • Financial & Budgetary Control: Prepare, manage, and monitor the monthly chemicals and consumables budget. Forecast monthly requirements, monitor actual usage vs. budget, and identify cost-saving opportunities through process optimization. • Health & Safety (HSE): Enforce strict adherence to HSE protocols across all sites, conducting routine safety audits and risk assessments. Monthly Measurable Deliverables (KPIs) • Plant Performance: Achieve plant uptime across all sites and maintain plant capacity on volumes. • Incident Management: Ensure zero unresolved critical alarms older than 48 hours. • Workforce Development: Log a minimum of 2 hours of training per operator per month and conduct monthly performance check-ins. • Safety Excellence: Maintain zero lost-time incidents (LTIs) per month. • Resource Management: Ensure zero occurrences of chemical and consumable stockouts and keep chemical usage variance against the monthly budget.
