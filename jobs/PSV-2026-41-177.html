About the role

The Department of E-Government (Gauteng) is hiring a Deputy Director: Performance Management and Development in Corporate Management - Human and Physical Resource Management, based in Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: To apply for the below positions, please submit your application strictly online at http://professionaljobcentre.gpg.gov.za. No hand-delivery or emailed applications will be accepted. Reference number to quote: REFS/ 035061 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and monitor the implementation of (Performance Management Development System) PMDS and skills development legislation, policies, strategies and plans: Develop and approve Human Resource Development Policies and Plans.
  • Develop a training needs assessment plan to identify training needs of employees.
  • Design a competency framework to analyse the skills and knowledge gap for each position in the organisation.
  • Conduct skills audit in all components and analyse identified skills gaps.
  • Create a strategic succession plan.
  • Monitor PMDS and training programs comply with the legislation and regulations.
  • Keep abreast with trends and technologies identifying skills gaps and onset projects and initiatives.
  • Manage the training and development process and programmes Manage implementation of compulsory and mandatory training programmes for all employees.
  • Conduct training needs analysis.
  • Manage the implementation of the training and development plan.
  • Design systems and survey for feedback from employees.
  • Provide career pathing initiatives and interventions.
  • Engage with stakeholders for innovative programmes designed for learning and development processes.
  • Conduct a return on investment (ROI) analysis.
  • Plan and coordinate the implementation of the recognition of prior learning (RPL) and recognition of improved qualification (RIQ) programmes in the Department.
  • Determine the budget and the numbers of qualifying employees for each year.
  • Manage the sourcing of successful provider to conduct RPL for the staff in the Department.
  • Manage the provisions of reports of programmes to the line managers, Skills Development Committee and relevant stakeholders.
  • Manage the implementation of learnership and internship programmes: Manage the identification of learnership and internship needs for the Department.
  • Identification of placement opportunities and Mentors.
  • Facilitate placement of graduates for development opportunities.
  • Manage and oversee the administration of Learnership and securing accredited training providers for implementation of the programme.
  • Oversee development and implementation of work and training plans.
  • Oversee the coordination of mentoring of learners/interns and implementation of structured training/programme with respective Branch /line mangers, accredited service providers, line Seta’s and relevant stakeholders.
  • Monitor the effective implementation of the Internship & Learnership programme and liaise with the appropriate Sector Educational and Training Authority (Seta) established in terms of the Skills Development Act of 2008 (Act 37 of 2008).
  • Monitor and evaluate the impact of Internship & Learnership programmes and the interventions thereof.
  • Report the administration of the programmes to the line managers and relevant stakeholders.
  • Manage and monitor implementation of performance management systems: Manage performance management system for all employees within the Department.
  • Administer the contracting and assessment of employees’ performance is communicated within the organisation.
  • Manage the alignment of employees’ performance contracts with all departmental strategic goals.
  • Manage the monitoring, compliance and reporting of performance management.
  • Encourage managers to conduct performance reviews, and annual performance review.
  • Oversee the implementation of the Performance Management on Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) System, application, Product and Data Processing (SAP) Module.
  • Plan, manage and administer internal and external Bursaries
  • Develop and implement needs analysis to determine department’s skills shortage with respective Branch, relevant Committees
  • Advise committee on the awarding of new bursaries
  • Facilitate the advertising of bursary requisite as per departmental needs
  • Coordinate administration and implementation of bursaries
  • Liaise with Institutions of higher learning/Higher Education Training (HET) and other service providers on bursary status quo
  • Monitor, evaluate and advise on bursary progress
  • Ensure the facilitation and reconciliation of bursary payments, debt recovery
  • Effect the debt takeovers process and ensure that necessary documents are forwarded to the relevant office (Human Resource Planning, Finance unit).
  • Report to the Committee on the Bursary Programme on the progress and developments.
  • Manage resources
  • General supervision of employees
  • Allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by supervisees
  • Advise and lead supervisees regarding all aspects of the work
  • Manage leave, performance, conduct and discipline of supervisees
  • Enforce that all supervisees are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively
  • Manage utilization of physical resources
  • Review Job profiles and sign off
