About the role

Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the-farm foods for consumers. Apart from its various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture, with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now operate across the entire food supply chain, including processing, logistics, storage, handling and national and international market channels. This "house of brands", established with the support of our farmer shareholders, forms the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. Why this role matters? This role will be responsible for designing, developing and/or maintaining server-side applications, APIs, and databases which services front end applications. Solutions will primarily be based on the .NET ecosystem. You will work with business representatives, analysts, front-end developers, integration developers and QA engineers to deliver functionally accurate, secure, scalable, and high-performance solutions. What success looks like? Success in this role would be demonstrated by consistently delivering secure, scalable, and high-performing .NET back-end solutions that meet business requirements and integrate seamlessly with front-end applications. The successful candidate takes ownership of projects from design through deployment, collaborates effectively with technical and business teams, produces high-quality, well-tested code, resolves issues proactively, and contributes to continuous improvements that enhance system performance, reliability, and the overall user experience.
