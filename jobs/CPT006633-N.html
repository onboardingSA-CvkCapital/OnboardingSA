About the role

Our client, a well-established manufacturing and project delivery company, is seeking an experienced and driven Production Manager to join their team. The successful candidate will oversee end-to-end production processes, including planning, workshop operations, quality control, and project coordination, while working closely with senior management in a fast-paced environment focused on delivering high-quality, custom-built solutions on time and within budget. Responsibilities • Manage end-to-end production of projects from concept to completion within workshop and site environments. • Plan, schedule, and coordinate production workflows, resources, and labor to ensure on-time project delivery. • Oversee workshop operations including carpentry, steel fabrication, spray, finishing, assembly, and installation preparation. • Coordinate between design, technical drawings, procurement, production, and installation teams. Translate client briefs and technical drawings into accurate, buildable production outputs. • Oversee manufacturing of joinery, shopfitting elements, fixtures, and bespoke builds. Monitor production progress, manage deadlines, and implement corrective action where delays occur. • Ensure efficient use of labor, machinery, workshop capacity, and resources. • Implement and maintain quality control and assurance standards across all production stages. • Conduct inspections to ensure compliance with company standards and client specifications. • Reduce waste, inefficiencies, and rework through continuous improvement. • Manage packing, labelling, dispatch, transport coordination, and installation readiness. • Support on-site installation logistics and problem-solving when required. • Manage supplier relationships, material requirements, stock control, and lead times. • Support procurement and cost control initiatives to improve margins and efficiency. • Ensure compliance with health and safety standards in workshop and site environments. • Maintain a safe, organized, and efficient workshop facility and oversee equipment maintenance. • Optimize workshop layout, systems, and production processes for improved efficiency. • Supervise and manage workshop teams, including carpenters, fabricators, installers, and subcontractors. • Manage performance, attendance, discipline, and productivity of staff. • Mentor and develop production teams and drive a culture of accountability and teamwork. • Support implementation of production systems and digital/process improvements. • Prepare production reports, schedules, and progress updates for senior management. • Maintain accurate production records, job tracking, and documentation. • Ensure all projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to required quality standards.Requirements • Proven experience as a Production Manager in manufacturing • Strong knowledge of workshop operations, production planning, and manufacturing processes. • Proficient in Microsoft Office and production/project management systems. • Strong attention to detail and quality standards. • Ability to work under pressure and meet strict deadlines. • Valid driver’s license and willingness to travel to sites when required. • Relevant qualification in Production/Operations/Engineering/Construction
