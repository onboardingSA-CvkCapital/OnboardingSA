About the role

The Department of Employment and Labour is hiring a Senior Claims Assessor: Compensation Fund, based in Komani Labour Centre. This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Deputy Director: Labour Centre Operations, P.O. Box 323, Komani, 5320. Hand deliver at No.10 Robinson Road Komani, 5320. Email Jobs– ECQTN@labour.gov.za Reference number to quote: HR4/4/1/84 Closing date: 06 November 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: African Males, White Males, White Females. Coloured Males, Coloured Females, Indian Males, Indian Females and People with Disabilities are encouraged to apply.

Responsibilities

  • Adjudicate registered and fatal claims per delegation’s requirements.
  • Verify and refer complex claims to medical services for further adjudication.
  • Authorise compensation benefits as per delegations.
  • Handle complex inquiries and advocacy sessions.
  • Supervision of staff.

Requirements

  • Senior Certificate plus a 3-year recognized qualification (NQF6 SAQA recognized) in Public Management/Business Management/Operations Management/Management/Human Resources Management/Nursing/Law/LLB /B.
  • Com/Finance/Administration.
  • A valid driver license.
  • 2 years’ experience in Compensation or Medical Claims Processing Environment.
