About the role

MAIN PURPOSE OF THE ROLE: To oversee the technical services department in carrying out all building-related activities by effectively planning, managing and monitoring of all aspects of site-specific building maintenance and refurbishments to ensure that quality standards are maintained and continually improved. The position is responsible for ensuring the infrastructure remains in good condition and ensure that assets are safe, well-functioning and ensure compliance of facilities to OHSA & other statutory requirements. This role operates as the backbone of the business and serves as the focal point betweenclient and IAFM to ensure operational efficiency and client expectations are timeously met. Establish and maintain mutually beneficial relationships with client and service contractors.
