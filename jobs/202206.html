About the role

The Main Purpose of the job: The Support Technician is an integral part of the IT department, responsible for providing technical assistance to end-users and addressing issues related to computer hardware, software, and network connectivity. The primary responsibility of a Support Technician is to ensure the smooth functioning of the IT infrastructure and resolve any problems that arise. Education and Experience required: • Matric Certificate • Tertiary Qualification in Information Technology • In-depth understanding of diverse computer systems and networks Knowledge, Skills, and Competencies: • Excellent problem-solving skills • Administration and Coordination Skills • Organizational and time-management Skills • Good verbal communication Skills • You must be smart, polite and well presented at all times • Excellent Customer Service Skills • Strong Team player Key areas of responsibility: • Provide technical assistance to clients and users by installing, configuring, testing, and upgrading computer software and hardware. • Developing and maintaining local networks in ways that optimize performance. • Ensuring security and privacy of networks and computer systems. • Providing training and guidance to users on how to operate new software and computer equipment. • Organizing and scheduling upgrades and maintenance without deterring others from completing their work. • Performing troubleshooting to diagnose and resolve problems (repair or replace parts, debugging etc.) • Maintaining records/logs of repairs and fixes and maintenance schedule. • Identifying computer or network equipment shortages and placing orders. • Collaborate with tech specialists such as software developers and engineers to develop and implement IT • support strategies. • Involves reading and researching better ways to understand the issue at hand and support client. • Direct clients to more experienced personnel for effective resolution of an issue. • Prepare and present reports to update management on IT support operations. • Assist in the procurement process for IT hardware, software, and services. • Work with team members to complete duties\projects as needed. • Log and track warranty claims with approved suppliers and vendors • Respond and resolve all calls allocated with in the agreed SLA times. • Escalate unresolved issues to higher-level support teams and follow up to ensure timely resolution. • Maintain asset allocations\reallocations database. • Provide cover and support to the engineers during absent\busy periods. • Provide cover and support to the Helpdesk coordinators during absent\busy periods. • Attend educational programs, workshops, and seminars to stay abreast with developments in the IT industry. • Prepared to take ownership of customer problems through to completion. • Assist in areas where there may be a shortfall, within reason. • Assist with ad hoc tasks as and when required Empact Group is committed to Employment Equity in line
