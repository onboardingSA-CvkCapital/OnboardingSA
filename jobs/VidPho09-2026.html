About the role

Who we are: Where we fit In At UXi Private Education Group, we’re not just shaping minds—we’re redefining how education looks, feels, and inspires. Our institutions, like Belgium Campus iTversity, IMM Graduate School, and Open Window, are hubs of innovation and creativity. We’re hunting for a videographer and photographer who doesn’t just capture moments but crafts stories that spark curiosity and push boundaries. If you’re ready to blend cutting-edge tech with bold ideas, let’s create something extraordinary together. Who we are looking for We’re on the hunt for a creative visual storyteller and photographer who lives for crafting visuals that stop the scroll and demand action from the viewer. A creative multitasker who thrives in a fast-paced team and delivers jaw-dropping content on tight deadlines. Someone who isn’t afraid to experiment to make our content stand out on platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and beyond. We need someone who understands video trends for all ages and who are not afraid to utilise the latest emerging video platforms and trends. Your work will shape how thousands see and engage with Africa’s top education brands. What you need for the job Technical Skills: • Capture high-quality stills and video using professional camera gear, with a heavy emphasis on composition, lighting, and framing. • Operate professional cameras and audio equipment to capture high-quality video and stills. • Set up cameras, lighting, and microphones for interviews, ensuring crisp visuals and clear audio in diverse settings. • Produce and edit engaging content using Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Photoshop/Lightroom, with an eye for colour grading and composition. • Create dynamic motion graphics and titles using After Effects or similar tools to enhance storytelling. • Capture and edit photos with strong composition, lighting, and editing skills for marketing campaigns and social media. • Stay ahead of trends by creating platform-native content for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or emerging platforms, with an understanding of algorithms and audience engagement. • Meticulous file management and version control and regularly cleaning up your machine. • Following a consistent editing process, and folder structure in your project file to ensure projects can easily be handed over if needed. • Bonus: Experience with AI editing tools (e.g., Runway, Descript), AR/VR filters, or DaVinci Resolve for advanced colour grading. Soft Skills: • Unleash bold, original ideas that turn educational content into viral, memorable stories. • Stay up to date with social media trends, adapting quickly to new platforms, styles, and audience preferences. • Collaborate seamlessly with designers and copywriters while confidently owning solo projects without micromanagement. • Master time management to juggle multiple shoots, edits, and deadlines with precision and polish. • Solve problems creatively, adapting to challenges like tight deadlines, equipment issues, or last-minute changes. • Clear communication with your team and management around progress of a project.
