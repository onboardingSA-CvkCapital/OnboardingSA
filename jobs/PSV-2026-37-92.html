About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Scientist Manager Grade A in Water And Sanitation Services Management: EC Provincial Operations: Eastern Cape Sd: Geohydrological Services, based in Kugompo. This is a permanent post, paying R1,143,468 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 301026/05 Closing date: 30 October 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and provide strategic leadership for geohydrological services.
  • Plan, coordinate and oversee groundwater-resource monitoring, assessment and information management programmes.
  • Provide specialist geohydrological advice to management, municipalities, water-sector institutions and other stakeholders.
  • Oversee groundwater-quality and groundwater-level monitoring programmes.
  • Manage groundwater data, modelling and dissemination of scientific information.
  • Lead scientific research and promote the application of innovative groundwater management approaches.
  • Provide input on groundwater projects, budgets and procurement processes.
  • Manage staff performance and development.
  • Mentor candidate scientists and facilitate scientific skills transfer.
  • Represent the department in technical committees, stakeholder forums and intergovernmental structures.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable legislation, policies, professional standards and scientific codes of practice.
  • Develop and implement geohydrological methodologies, policies, strategies, procedures and technical standards.
  • Review and approve scientific reports, groundwater studies, models and technical recommendations.
  • Ensure the development and implementation of policies, systems, and procedures.
  • Review and recommend/approve scientific projects, facilitate the setting of scientific standards, specifications, and service levels according to organizational objectives to ensure optimum operational service.
  • Monitor scientific efficiencies according to organizational goals, and facilitate the compilation of innovation proposals to optimize limited resources in achieving increased levels of compliance within industry.

Requirements

  • MSc Degree or equivalent qualification in Geohydrology/Hydrogeology/Earth Science.
  • 6 years of post-qualification natural sciences experience required.
  • Compulsory registration with SACNASP as a Professional Natural Scientist.
  • The disclosure of a valid unexpired driver’s license.
  • Experience in groundwater exploration, monitoring, assessment and sustainable resource management.
  • Experience in water resource management.
  • Scientific methodologies and models.
  • Research and development.
  • Scientific presentation.
  • Analysis and management of groundwater data.
  • Business processes to ensure data flow.
  • Professional judgement.
  • Scientific mentoring.
  • Strategic capability and Leadership.
  • Financial Management.
  • Change Management.
  • Problem solving and analysis.
  • Knowledge management.
  • People Management and Empowerment.
  • Client Orientation and Customer Focus.
  • Excellent communication, report-writing and presentation.
  • Conflict management.
  • Accountability and ethical conduct.
  • Knowledge of the National Water Act and related water-sector legislation.
  • Experience in scientific research, project management, stakeholder engagement, technical report writing and the management of professional scientific personnel.
  • Knowledge of GIS and groundwater-modelling tools such as MODFLOW will be an added advantage.
