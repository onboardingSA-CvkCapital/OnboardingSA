About the role

Introduction: Lead, develop and execute the national sales strategy for the B&T Division to accelerate market-led growth, increase trade market share, improve customer visibility and deliver sustainable profitable revenue growth across all regions. Description: Strategic Sales Leadership • Develop and implement a national sales strategy aligned to divisional and company growth objectives. • Establish annual, quarterly and monthly sales plans and targets. • Drive sustainable revenue growth, market share expansion and margin improvement. • Develop trade-focused customer growth strategies to increase market penetration. • Identify market opportunities, customer trends and competitor activity to inform business decisions. • Lead the execution of customer-led growth initiatives across all regions. • Establish go-to-market strategies for key customer segments and target markets. Sales Transformation and Operating Model Development • Conduct a comprehensive assessment of the current sales operating model. • Evaluate the effectiveness, productivity and structure of internal and external sales functions. • Review customer segmentation, territory allocation, workload distribution and customer coverage models. • Benchmark sales structures and performance against industry best practice. • Develop recommendations regarding: • National and regional sales structures. • Sales resource requirements. • Reporting lines and span of control. • Territory design and customer allocation. • Sales role definitions and accountabilities. • Customer segmentation models. • Sales governance frameworks. • Lead the implementation of approved organisational and sales transformation initiatives. • Establish a scalable and sustainable sales operating model that supports future growth. Leadership and Team Development • Lead, coach and develop Regional Sales Managers to deliver consistent sales performance. • Build a high-performance sales culture focused on accountability, customer service and commercial results. • Drive employee engagement and capability development within the sales function. • Ensure performance management processes are effectively implemented. • Identify succession talent and develop future sales leaders. • Support recruitment, onboarding and development of sales personnel. Sales Operations and Performance Management • Establish and manage the national sales budget and sales targets. • Implement standardised sales processes, methodologies and operating standards. • Develop sales scorecards, KPIs and performance management frameworks. • Drive accuracy in sales forecasting and pipeline management. • Monitor regional sales performance and implement corrective action where required. • Ensure effective utilisation of CRM and sales management systems. • Drive continuous improvement in sales productivity and effectiveness. Customer and Market Development • Develop and strengthen relationships with major trade customers and key accounts. • Drive customer acquisition, retention and customer growth strategies. • Increase customer visibility and engagement across all regions. • Expand share of wallet within existing customer portfolios. • Identify opportunities for strategic partnerships and market development. • Provide customer and market insights to support business planning and decision-making. • Ensure customer expectations and service commitments are consistently achieved. Governance, Compliance and Commercial Excellence • Establish sales governance standards and operating disciplines across the business. • Ensure compliance with company policies, procedures and commercial frameworks. • Balance sales growth objectives with profitability and risk management requirements. • Collaborate with all internal stakeholders to optimise business performance. • Drive adoption of data-driven decision-making and sales analytics. • Ensure consistency in customer engagement and sales execution nationally. Reporting and Performance Analysis • Prepare and present monthly, quarterly and annual sales performance reports. • Provide meaningful sales analysis and recommendations to executive leadership. • Monitor key sales and customer metrics to identify trends and opportunities. • Measure the effectiveness of sales initiatives and transformation programmes. • Report on progress against strategic growth priorities and sales transformation objectives. Requirements: Minimum Qualifications • Degree in Business Management, Commerce, Sales, Marketing or related field. • Relevant postgraduate qualification advantageous. Experience • Minimum 8-10 years sales leadership experience within a multi-site commercial environment. • Minimum 5 years' experience in a senior sales management role. • Proven experience leading geographically dispersed sales teams. • Demonstrated success in developing and implementing national sales strategies. • Experience in sales transformation, organisational design or operating model implementation. • Experience within building materials, hardware, wholesale, retail or construction-related industries would be advantageous. • Proven track record of delivering sustainable revenue and market share growth.
