About the role

Junior Bookkeeper – Cape TownManufacturing / DistributionFull-time | On-site Our client, a leading company within the manufacturing and distribution industry, is seeking a detail-oriented Junior Bookkeeper to support the finance team with day-to-day accounting and transactional processes. This role is suited to a candidate who is structured, accurate, and able to manage multiple financial functions in a deadline-driven environment.
