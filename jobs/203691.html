About the role

Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company, providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the farm-foods for consumers. Apart from the various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now also operate in all areas of the food supply chain: processing, logistics, and national and global market channels. This “house of brands” we’ve established with the support of our farmer shareholders is the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. The Role We are looking for a highly organised and service-oriented Payroll Support Administrator to join our Human Capital team at Head Office in Reitz. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys helping people, solving administrative queries, and maintaining exceptional accuracy in a structured, deadline-driven environment. This position serves as the first point of contact for payroll-related employee queries and support requests across the Group. The successful candidate will provide administrative support to the Payroll Specialists, assist employees with payroll and statutory documentation, and ensure the accurate completion of a wide range of payroll support activities. The role requires an individual with exceptional attention to detail, strong administrative capabilities, and the ability to remain focused and productive in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. The successful candidate must be based in Reitz and work from the Group Head Office. Key Responsibilities Payroll Support & Employee Service • Act as the first point of contact for payroll-related employee queries. • Assist employees with payroll support requests and payroll tickets. • Provide employee service confirmations and employment-related documentation. • Reprint and distribute payroll documentation, including payslips, IRP5 certificates, and related records. • Assist with payroll-related reporting and administrative support.Statutory Administration & Compliance • Assist Payroll Specialists with UIF administration and related employee documentation. • Prepare and process UIF documentation for maternity, illness, unemployment, and other claims. • Submit monthly UIF declarations and maintain supporting records. • Assist with ITA88 and other SARS-related payroll documentation. • Support payroll compliance activities, audits, and Department of Labour enquiries. • Maintain accurate filing systems and payroll records in accordance with company and legislative requirements.Employee Self-Service (ESS) Administration • Create, maintain, and terminate Employee Self-Service (ESS) user profiles. • Reset ESS passwords and provide user support. • Assist employees with ESS setup and usage on mobile devices. • Support leave-related administration and ESS leave approval processes.Payroll Administration • Perform weekly Bank AVS (Account Verification Service) validations to ensure employee banking details correspond with active employees. • Support basic payroll processing activities on Sage 300 People. • Assist Payroll Specialists with routine payroll administration and system maintenance.Minimum Requirements • Grade 12 (Matric) • Additional short courses, certificates or payroll related training will be advantageous • Minimum 2 years' experience
