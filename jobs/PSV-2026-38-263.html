About the role

The Department of Community Safety (Gauteng) is hiring an Assistant Director: Road Safety in Traffic Management, based in Head Office Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Applicants must apply online at: www.gautengonline.gov.za / http://professionaljobcentre.gpg.gov.za. Reference number to quote: REFS/057035 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Assist to coordinate institutional arrangements for effective implementation of the GSS.
  • Assist with the alignment of Road Safety activities within and outside the Department.
  • Assist with the implementation of road safety strategies as per national department (NDoT) directive to schools and the general public.
  • Ensure Public Education through training of road users and communities with the focus on Community outreach programmes, road shows and promotional projects.
  • Ensure training of all Gauteng educators on road safety education.
  • Assist with coordination and management of Gauteng Pedestrian Management plan with all stakeholders.
  • Development and implementation of the operational plan for the regional office.
  • Effective performance management and capacity building of the regional staff.
  • Recommend Disciplinary hearing against any Officer under your jurisdiction who is reasonably suspected of having committed an act of Misconduct in the execution of their duties.
  • Manage grievances according to prescribed procedures.
  • Facilitate at least one team building activity per quarter.
  • Ensure that leave is utilized and managed according to the relevant policies and instructions.
  • Develop and implement in-service training programme and monitor formal training needs of the unit.
  • Monitor and evaluate operational performance of the region to ensure continuous alignment and development.
  • Ensure the management of Performance Management and Development System as per the approved PMDS policy.
  • Ensure proper record keeping of activities of the centre including minutes of every meeting convened.
  • Compile monthly reports and submit.
  • Attend operational meetings and any other meeting as required by strategic leadership.
  • Manage assets as per inventories of the centre.
  • Ensuring appropriate use of all facilities.
  • Ensure effective loss control measures are in place to address loss of equipment's.
  • Monitor vehicle costs for costs for the station/centre and identify and monitor members involved in accidents and ensure that motor vehicle fleet is managed properly and that vehicles are in good running order.
  • Coordinate stakeholder relations with other state departments, NGOs, Road Safety Officials and Law enforcement agencies (LEAS).
  • Identify relevant risks.
  • Develop plan to address risks.
  • Implement and monitor risk plan.
  • Monitor monthly expenditure and inspection reports from Province and implement corrective action in problem areas.
  • Regular budget and management discussions as part of management meetings.
  • Document overtime planning based on priorities.
  • Participate in budget planning as required by the province.
  • Respond to requests from head office regarding financial queries within provided time limits.
  • Monitor and manage the procurement needs of the centre.
  • Evaluate progress.
  • Coordinate Road Safety projects with regional team leaders.
  • Draft business plans for the project.
  • Draft an activity plan/detailed work plan.
  • Manage project budget and procurement process.
  • Ensure effective implementation of the respective project activities.
  • Compile monthly project progress report on status.
  • Engage with various role players on project implementation.
  • Encourage road user compliance to road rules.
  • Coordinate formal, informal and non-formal education and communication through projects and programmes targeting the categories of road users in order to change their behaviour and attitudes and increased knowledge and skills.
  • Identify and engage key stakeholders in road safety promotion initiatives.
  • Establish partnerships with relevant departments, local government etc. to foster mutual relations and pursue common interests.
  • Participate in the communication forum for Law Enforcement Agencies.

  • A Grade 12 certificate plus a relevant tertiary qualification (3-year Diploma/Degree or equivalent).
  • 3 years' experience at administrative/supervisory level in the field of Road Safety.
  • All valid relevant driving licenses (A and EC).
