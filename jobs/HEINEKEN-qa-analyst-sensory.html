About the role

Heineken Beverages is hiring a QA Analyst Sensory, based in Springs.

Responsibilities

  • Reduction in sensory cost of non-compliance
  • Reduction in product retention/release
  • Enable growth
  • Prioritized sensory panel expansion within budget
  • Sensory governance framework delivered and maintaining the LSS standards
  • Trend analysis on site sensory performance
  • Overall site sensory competence maintained at >90%
  • Drive superior product quality delivery within Springs Cidery
  • Lab excellence delivered
  • Training and assessment schedules developed and executed
  • Lab SLA targets achieved >90%
  • Top 3 improvement projects supported
  • Drive business efficiency and growth
  • Initiating internal process improvements and the implementation thereof.
  • Effective utilization of resources and systems
  • Instilling a quality culture in his/her area of responsibility and sphere of influence.
  • Building and maintaining effective and trusting relationships with internal customers and peers.
  • Enhancing customer experience by achieving and/or exceeding targeted customer service levels
  • Drive manufacturing reliability enabling capital & asset efficiency
  • Performing all relevant administrative and process duties.
  • Initiating and assisting in problem solving.
  • Improve manufacturing cost competitiveness
  • Innovation initiatives capitalized
  • Step change environmental sustainability
  • Utilities usage reduction initiatives supported
  • Growing the business through support of cross-functional improvement and new development projects.
  • Participating in TPM practice implementation.
  • Deliver growth, profitability and shareholder return objectives

Requirements

  • The successful candidate must have the following qualification, skills and experience:
  • At least a diploma in biological or food science / BSc degree or equivalent
  • At least 2 years relevant work experience in the fast-moving consumer goods environment preferably in the alcoholic beverage industry
  • Previous working experience with a Food Safety Management System
  • Advanced computer skills and excellent communication skills are also needed
  • Must be self-motivated and able to work without supervision
  • Ability to work under pressure
  • Methodical, accurate and conscientious worker
  • Working knowledge of safety procedures and housekeeping standards
  • Have reliable transport
