About the role

The Account Manager is responsible for managing assigned customer accounts, ensuring service delivery excellence, contract compliance, customer satisfaction, and profitable account growth. The role serves as the primary interface between the company and its customers while ensuring operational performance through effective management of Site Supervisors and branch support functions. The incumbent is accountable for customer retention, SLA compliance, service delivery oversight, account profitability, business development, and operational performance within their assigned portfolio. Customer Relationship Management • Serve as the primary contact for assigned customers. • Build and maintain strong customer relationships. • Conduct regular customer visits and review meetings. • Manage customer expectations and service requirements. • Resolve customer complaints and escalations. • Ensure high levels of customer satisfaction. Contract and SLA Management • Manage service contracts and customer agreements. • Ensure SLA compliance. • Monitor contractual obligations and service performance. • Conduct contract review meetings. • Identify contractual risks and implement corrective actions. Operational Management • Lead Site Supervisors and Branch Administrators. • Monitor maintenance performance, breakdown response, and machine availability. • Ensure operational resources are deployed effectively. • Review operational KPIs and service delivery performance. • Escalate operational risks to the Field Services Manager. Business Development • Identify growth opportunities within existing customers. • Promote Chromotion products and services. • Support quotations, tenders, and proposals. • Develop account growth plans. • Participate in industry and customer engagement activities. Financial Management • Monitor account profitability and performance. • Control operational expenditure. • Support budgeting and forecasting processes. • Monitor labour utilisation and service costs. • Assist with debtors management and invoice resolution. Customer Service Oversight • Monitor customer satisfaction. • Ensure service quality standards are achieved. • Review customer feedback and implement improvements. • Support customer audits and inspections. Compliance and SHEQ • Ensure compliance with contractual, legal, and company requirements. • Support safety initiatives and compliance programmes. • Participate in customer audits and reviews. • Ensure operational teams comply with SHEQ requirements. Reporting • Prepare monthly account performance reports. • Review operational and financial performance. • Monitor account KPIs. • Report risks, opportunities, and customer concerns.
