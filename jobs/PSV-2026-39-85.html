About the role

The Department of Home Affairs is hiring 7 Security Officer posts, based in Branch Counter Corruption and Security Services. This is a permanent post, paying R170,226 to R200,523 a year. How to apply: Applications compliant with the “Directions to Applicants” above, must be submitted online at https://erecruitment.dha.gov.za or Head Office, Hallmark Building: Physical address: 230 Johannes Ramokhoase Street, Pretoria, 0001 Reference number to quote: HRMC 71/26/3 Closing date: 13 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidates will be responsible for, amongst others, the following specific tasks: Perform access control functions.
  • Ensure safety in the building and the premises by undertaking patrols, to identify fire hazards, suspicious objects and packages.
  • Ensure that equipment’s and store do not leave or enter the premises unauthorized.
  • Administer the control room.
  • Monitoring of (CCTV) close circuit television.
  • Handling keys.
  • Reporting of faulty security equipment.
  • Administration of all control room activities
  • Key control, incident management, visitors and contractors and registers
  • Handling documents at point of entry according to prescript
  • Apply emergency procedure in event of emergency

Requirements

  • Grade 12 National Senior Certificate at NQF level 4 as recognised by SAQA.
  • Basic security officer’s course, Grade C.
  • Basic education and training.
  • PSIRA Grade C Certificate is required Knowledge and understanding of the Public Service Regulatory Framework.
  • Knowledge and understanding of the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA) and Treasury regulations.
  • Knowledge of the Minimum Information Security standards (MISS).
  • Knowledge of the National Intelligence Strategy Act.
  • Knowledge of the Protection of Information Act.
  • Understanding of investigative techniques and methodology.
  • Knowledge of Control of Access to Public Premises and vehicle Act.
  • Knowledge of departmental legislation as well as Human Resources legislation and prescripts.
  • Knowledge of the Criminal Procedure Act of 1977 as amended.
  • Knowledge of Protection of Personal Information Act no 4 of 2013 as amended.
  • Shift working is required.
