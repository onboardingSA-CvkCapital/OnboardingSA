About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies' requires the committed services of a customer-oriented Cosmetics Merchandiser for their Store in Strubensvalley. The purpose of the position is to ensure basic Dis-Chem merchandising standards are properly executed and maintained. Provide proper customer service in accordance with Dis-Chem policies and procedures. Essential: • Grade 12 / Matric • Minimum of 0 – 6 Months Retail experienceAdvantageous: •  Minimum of 1 year experience within retail Job Description: Stock Control: • Restock merchandise when needed to ensure maximum sales. • Delivered stock or stock pulled from the store room must be stored and packed in the correct space immediately. • Ensure the stock on the shelves has not reached sell by dates. • Ensure full ranges of products are on the shelves at all times. • Facilitate the rotation of stoke on a regular basis and adhere to Dis-Chem stock control procedures. • Keep the storeroom organised, neat and tidy. • Report low stock levels, items that are out of stock, damaged stock and expired stock to management. • Stock must be rotated on a continual, first in first out basis. • Assist with the counting of stock files and general stock taking when needed. • Ensure boxes are flattened after unpacking stock and taken to designated area. • Identify and report damaged and expired stock immediately to one of the store management team members. Merchandising: • Uphold the Dis-Chem merchandising policy and procedure standards. • Adhere to Dis-Chem operating standards, store layout and planograms. • Front shop shelves and products must be neatly presented and visible at all times. • Labels and pricing must be correct at all times. • Report all price discrepancies to management. • Ensure you are aware of new products and have up to date product knowledge. • Ensure restocking of merchandise and supplies as needed to ensure maximum sales. • Merchandising displays must be built, faced up, stocked and maintained according to Dis-Chem procedures. • Maintain the daily physical upkeep of the store and merchandise displays and be responsible for your designated stock in the store room as well as on the shop floor. • Assist with the loading and off-loading of stock, when needed. • Ensure items without barcodes are clearly marked. • Be available to assist with back shopping. • Assist with the training of new and current staff members. • Handle all merchandise carefully to prevent any losses. Customer Service: • Provide friendly, helpful and courteous assistance and advice to all customers. • All out of stock queries from customers must be followed up with the customer service out of stock list. • All customer stock queries must be dealt with and resolved. • The correct uniform and name badge must be worn at all times as per the Dis-Chem uniform policy.General: • Housekeeping must be in accordance to Dis-Chem standards. Make sure that your work station is always clean and tidy. • Adhere to Dis-Chem Policies and Standard Operating Procedures. • Adhere to Health and Safety rules and regulations. • Adhere to Dis-Chem Uniform and personal appearance policy.Competencies: Essential: • Knowledge - Merchandising standards and FMCG industry. Working with Planograms. Category Knowledge • Cognitive Skills - Be able to analyse a ZMOR reports for ordering and stock taking. • Communication Skills - English – Read, write, speak and understand. Writing Skills, Oral Communication, Listening and, Document Use. • Interpersonal Skills - Customer, management, staff members, Head Office. Trustworthy and honest. Client Service, Interpersonal Relations, Interpersonal Awareness, Thoroughness, Organization Sensitivity, Identification with Management, Cooperation and, Customer Orientation. • Self-Management Skills - Give constant feedback to store manager. Interaction with management and customers. Good time management. Self-discipline and good appearance. Creative Thinking, Ethics, Even Temperament, Initiative, Personal Credibility, Results Oriented, Accountability, Attention to detail, Workmanship, Result-Orientedness, Learning Ability, Need to Achieve, Integrity, Initiative, Insight, Energy, Focus on Quality, Conduct and, Target and Deadline Driven • Leadership Skills - Report bad and suspicious behaviour – staff and customers. Lead by example. • Technical skills - Computer Skills, Technical Equipment (Scanners, check pricing). Numerical skills. Mathematics (Standard), Merchandising and, Customer Service.Advantageous: • Communication Skills - 2nd language.Special conditions of employment: • Willing and able to work retail hours • South African citizen • Clear credit and criminal records Remuneration and benefits: • Market-related salary • Medical aid • Provident fund • Staff account ONLY SUCCESSFUL APPLICANTS WILL BE CONTACTED.
