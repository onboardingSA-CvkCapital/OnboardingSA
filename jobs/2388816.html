About the role

Our client requires a seasoned Site Manager with extensive residential construction experience to take responsibility for the effective coordination and delivery of site activities in Tshwane. This role requires a hands-on leader who can manage teams and contractors while maintaining strict technical, quality and programme standards. The Role • Direct and coordinate day-to-day residential construction activities. • Plan and control the delivery and allocation of site resources. • Ensure contractors follow the correct sequence of trades and project requirements. • Communicate daily targets, responsibilities and deliverables to site teams. • Ensure technical requirements are incorporated into project planning and executed at the appropriate stages. • Monitor workmanship and maintain high standards of quality throughout the project. • Ensure quality-control records and engineer sign-offs are completed correctly. • Monitor project progress and submit accurate reports to management. • Identify and communicate potential risks before they impact project delivery. • Handle employee management and disciplinary matters where required. • Engage with council and other relevant parties regarding site matters. • Manage month-end administration and ensure timesheets are accurately completed. • Coordinate unit handovers and ensure the required procedures are followed. • Use site measuring equipment effectively and accurately. • Review drawings and technical details and ensure they are correctly implemented. • Assist contractors and site personnel with technical and operational challenges. • Manage competing priorities while maintaining deadlines and quality standards. Minimum Requirements • Diploma or Degree in Construction. • Valid driver's licence. • At least 10 years' residential construction experience. • Strong site and project management experience. • Excellent staff and contractor management abilities. • Strong technical understanding of residential construction. • Excellent communication and stakeholder-management skills. • Strong organisational and planning capabilities. • Ability to solve problems quickly and effectively. • Comfortable working under pressure and managing multiple tasks. • Deadline-driven with a high sense of urgency.
