About the role

Livestock Reproductive Veterinarian Location: Bloemfontein, Free State, South Africa Position Type: Permanent, Full-Time Remuneration: Depending on experience Bonus: Annual discretionary bonus based on employee performance and the company's financial performance. Travel Requirement: Extensive travel within South Africa and international travel from time to time About Absolute Genetics Absolute Genetics is a leading provider of advanced livestock reproductive technologies, elite genetics, and breeding solutions across South Africa and Africa. Our services include IVF, Ovum Pick-Up (OPU), embryo technologies, artificial insemination, reproductive management, genomic solutions, and genetic consulting for dairy and beef producers. We are seeking a passionate and driven Livestock Reproductive Veterinarian to join our growing team and contribute to the advancement of livestock genetics and reproductive excellence throughout Africa. Key Responsibilities Perform Ovum Pick-Up (OPU) procedures. Conduct Artificial Insemination (AI) programmes. Perform reproductive ultrasound examinations and pregnancy diagnosis. Conduct bull breeding soundness evaluations and fertility testing. Perform semen collection and reproductive health assessments. Provide herd health and reproductive management services. Support IVF, embryo transfer, and advanced reproductive programmes. Deliver professional veterinary and technical support to clients. Maintain accurate programme records and reports. Assist in training, development, and implementation of new reproductive technologies. Represent Absolute Genetics at client visits, industry events, and training programmes. Travel extensively throughout South Africa and occasionally internationally. Advantageous Experience Experience in bovine reproduction and herd health. Artificial Insemination and pregnancy diagnosis experience. Semen collection and processing experience. Knowledge of dairy and beef production systems. Experience working with advanced reproductive technologies and genetics programmes. Experience working across diverse farming operations and environments. Practical experience in Ovum Pick-Up (OPU). Embryo flush and transfer experience. Personal Attributes Professional, ethical, and client-focused. Passionate about animal reproduction and genetics. Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills. Adaptable and able to perform under pressure. Detail-oriented with high standards of work quality. Self-motivated with a willingness to learn and grow. What We Offer Competitive remuneration package. Opportunity to work with industry-leading reproductive and genetic technologies. Exposure to elite livestock breeding programmes across Africa. Ongoing professional development and training opportunities. Dynamic, innovative, and growth-oriented working environment. Opportunity for local and international professional exposure.
