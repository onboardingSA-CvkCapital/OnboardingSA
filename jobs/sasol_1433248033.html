About the role

Sasol is a global integrated chemicals and energy company with a 75-year heritage. Through our talented people, we use our expertise and selected technologies to safely and sustainably source, manufacture and market chemical and energy products globally. When you join Team Sasol, you are joining a company that puts people at the center of everything we do. Sasol invests in its employees along every stage of the career path and offers development opportunities to help you cultivate your career in a culture that embraces diversity and inclusion. To operate process equipment and systems at Komatipoort compressor station within the Sasol environment to achieve production requirements in a safe manner.

Responsibilities

  • Operate processes at Komatipoort compressor station
  • Start up and shutdown processes according to standard operating procedures (SOP)
  • Operate plant and equipment under normal operating conditions as per SOP’s
  • Communicate and assist team members in controlling the plant process
  • Perform sampling for quality testing and fault finding purposes
  • Perform inspections on equipment (valves, pumps, vessels, etc.)
  • Examine equipment and complete safety checklists and procedures to maintain and improve plant stability
  • Identify and correct abnormal conditions in the plant
  • Commission and de-commission plant equipment and systems (New projects, plant modifications and pre/post maintenance work)
  • Adhere to SHERQ standards
  • Comply with legal requirements
  • Loading and off-loading of hazardous and non-hazardous products/ chemicals
  • Execute tasks safely and economically
  • Report and respond to incidents, hazardous conditions and/or emergencies
  • Identify and report deviations and all unsafe conditions to Foreman
  • Loading and prioritization of SAP notifications
  • Conduct fault finding and incident investigations
  • Adhere to permit to work procedure
  • Issue permits (if required) and perform safe making duties
  • Conduct basic risk assessments
  • Exchange information on process conditions at handover (completion of log sheets)
  • Perform overtime and be on shift cover when required
  • Complete training as per training matrix requirements
  • Maintains good housekeeping and adheres to waste disposal procedures.
  • Take lead during emergency exercise / response onsite
  • Assists with the review and development of SOP’s
  • Conducts on the job coaching of learners and team members
  • Work independently
  • Work 12-hour shifts
  • Give and receive feedback to other disciplines (partners on daily issues).
  • Gives input at toolbox talks to improve plant safety.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 with Maths, Science and English / N3 certificate (must)
  • NQF4 occupational certificate in Chemical Operations / CPO occupational qualification (must)
  • Valid Driver’s license – Min Code 08 (must).
  • 2+ years relevant experience in a similar work environment
  • PtW issuing competency
  • Safety Standby and Gas monitor experience
  • OSH Act experience
  • Computer literacy
