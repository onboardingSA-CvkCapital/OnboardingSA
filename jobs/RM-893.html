About the role

Introduction: As the Managing Executive: Relationship, your role is to originate and lead the enterprise client relationship strategy across RMA’s customer segments (Exclusive, Premium, Concierge/COID Assist and Essential), delivering superior commercial performance (growth, retention, profitability) and segment‑differentiated service. Description: THE JOB AT A GLANCE As the Managing Executive: Relationship, your role is to originate and lead the enterprise client relationship strategy across RMA’s customer segments (Exclusive, Premium, Concierge/COID Assist and Essential), delivering superior commercial performance (growth, retention, profitability) and segment‑differentiated service through integrated governance, deep partnership models and end‑to‑end value chain orchestration. Your role owns the segment P&L, sets a multi‑year portfolio growth roadmap, and ensures combined assurance across client governance, claims trends, service SLAs and regulatory compliance while building a high‑performing leadership bench (the four Heads) and driving cross-functional execution with Sales & Distribution, Operations, Actuarial, Finance, Technology and CX, improves performance metrics and ensures alignment with the organisation’s Partnership Framework. WHAT YOU WILL DO Strategy Origination & Portfolio Leadership • Originate the 3–5‑year Relationship Portfolio Strategy for all segments; define the value propositions, service models and governance forums per tier • Approve annual segment operating plans, targets and budgets; allocate resources across heads/segments based on value and risk. Commercial Performance & P&L Ownership • Own consolidated Revenue, Retention, Loss Ratio levers and Cost‑to‑Serve for the relationship portfolio • Approve deal architectures (pricing levers, benefit constructs, commercial terms) within delegated authority; escalate beyond threshold to CEO: C&P as required. Client Governance & Strategic Partnerships • Chair quarterly Integrated Client Governance for Exclusive and Premium; ensure governance maturity for Concierge/Essential • Lead strategic engagements with Exco/C‑Suite of Top 100/300 employers, Trustees, organised labour and industry bodies. Claims, Risk & Compliance (Combined Assurance) • With Claims/Medical Management and Risk/Compliance, set portfolio‑level risk appetite, claims intervention playbooks and re‑pricing triggers • Ensure regulatory adherence (COID and related statutes) and audit readiness across all segments. People & Culture Leadership • Lead and develop the four Heads; build succession and talent pipelines; set performance contracts and incentives tied to portfolio KPIs. Cross‑Functional Collaboration • Align Sales & Distribution (hunting) and Relationship (farming) motions to eliminate leakage and maximise lifetime value • Collaborate with Operations, Finance, Actuarial, IT, Strategy & CX to deliver end‑to‑end outcomes.
