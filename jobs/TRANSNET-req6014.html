About the role

Transnet (Transnet Port Terminals) is hiring a Work Integrated Technical Learner : Diesel Mechanic (Richards Bay & Cape Town), based in Various Locations. The role reports to the Specialist: Youth Development. Grade: WITL. The Artisan In Training Programme aims to provide recently qualified apprentices ( with a red seal) with practical work experience, allowing them to apply their academic and vocational or practical knowledge in a real-world environment. This Programme is designed to enhance participants' employability by offering hands-on training, mentorship, and exposure to industry-specific tasks. The Artisan In Training Programme is conducted over a period of 24 months.

  • Ages between 18 and 35

  • Competencies
  • Beginner: Basic understanding of tools and safety procedures. Intermediate: Ability to perform tasks independently with minimal supervision. Advanced: Expert-level skills, capable of supervising and training others.
