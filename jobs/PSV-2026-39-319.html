Responsibilities

  • Facilitate the process of application for possible admission to Umgeni thus liaising with all the relevant external stakeholders and Umgeni MDT.
  • Conduct psychosocial assessments of mental health care users and their families.
  • Arrange and attend screening meetings.
  • Provide counselling, social support, and psychoeducation to families living and caring for mental health care users with intellectual disabilities and other medical conditions.
  • Strengthen family relations after admission.
  • Provide pre-discharge planning.
  • Design and implement quality improvement programmes.
  • Develop and maintain good working relations with both external and internal stakeholders for better coordination of services and effective service delivery.
  • Produce and maintain accurate records of social work interventions, process and outcomes.
  • Keep up to date with new developments in the social work profession.
  • Attend to ward rounds and meetings with other departments and non-governmental institutions to take cognizance of the latest developments in the relevant fields.
  • Perform all the administrative functions required of the job, including writing comprehensive social work reports.
  • Conduct home visits.
  • Relieve in the absence of Social Worker.

Requirements

  • Senior Certificate/Grade 12 or equivalent.
  • Bachelor of Social Work.
  • Registration with the South African Council for Social Service Professions.
  • Valid Driver’s licence.
  • Computer certificate.
  • Experience: No experience required.
  • Knowledge And Skills
  • Knowledge and understanding of Mental Health Care Act No: 17 of 2002, Intellectual disability and physical disabilities
  • Knowledge and understanding of human behaviour and social system
  • Knowledge of government policies, Acts, legislation pertaining to social work practice
  • Theoretical knowledge, practical knowledge of all social work methods and social work principles
  • Decision-making, problem-solving skills, crisis intervention, planning and organizing skills
  • Ability to work under pressure with good insight, diplomacy and work with multidisciplinary team members
  • Good interpersonal relations and listening skills
  • Ability to write comprehensive social work reports
  • Ability to safeguard confidentiality
  • Verbal and written communication skills both in IsiZulu and English
