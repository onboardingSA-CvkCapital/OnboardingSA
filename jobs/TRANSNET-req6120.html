About the role

Transnet (Transnet NPA) is hiring a Chief Flight Instructor, based in Durban. The role reports to the Senior Manager - Aviation. Grade: AS5. Manage and co-ordinate all training for Marine Pilot Service. • To ensure compliance with the SACAA regulations, the Transnet SOC LTD Training and Procedures manual as well as the Rotorcraft Flight Manual at all times when undertaking his/her duties as flight crew and/or helicopter instructor at all times. • To ensure that he/she maintains a high standard and level of safety, to carry oneself in a disciplined and orderly manner as well as to maintain neat appearance at all times. • Application of the Civil Aviation regulations as well as the National Ports Act No. 12 of 2005. • Ensure uninterrupted operation of TNPA helicopter service to deliver high quality service and enhance port efficiency. • The Commander of the aircraft for both Day and Night operations depending on line position. • Ultimately responsible for the safety of the crew and passengers on board the aircraft as per SACAA CARs 91.02.7 and 91.02.8. • To manage day time and night time flight crew during day and night operations in accordance with the SACAA regulations and TNPA SOP’s. • Flight planning, preparation of aircraft to conduct day and night flights for the marine pilot service and Port related functions in accordance with TNPA requirements, standard operating procedures and policies. • Undertake experiential training to meet the standards set by national and international aviat

The CFI is responsible for any accident or incident on the operation under Part 127 (Commercial air transport and general aviation operations for Helicopters under the AOC (Air Operator Certificate) and under Part 141 (Aviation Training Organization.
