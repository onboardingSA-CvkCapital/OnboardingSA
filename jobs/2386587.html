About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AI-powered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined, future-fit role. We are looking for enthusiastic, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace data-driven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job The Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Assistant role exist to provide support with the HSE Legal Compliance and Risk Management. The role will further support in the administration of Occupational Health, Safety and Environment (OHS&E) governance and legal compliance management. The OHS Assistant will continuously monitor the implementation of Safe Work Procedures, maintains, and updates all relevant OHS&E department administrative duties. Key/main outputs Output 1: OHSE Legal Compliance Support • Contribute to ensuring that the organisation meets all health and safety legislation objectives and thatappropriate risk mitigation/control measures are in place. • Support in documenting, updating, and maintaining HSE programmes, SOPs in compliance with the relevant legislations. • Support in the HSE performance improvements and any other statutory requirements that need to beimplemented. • Ensure SHERQ documents and data control system are maintained. • Support in monitoring the good safety governance, pro-active and positive safety culture. • Storage and handling are clearly indicated. Ensure that the MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheets) are available. • Support in monitoring compliance to safety, health, and security, requirements in the workplace. • Support in conducting Planned Task Observations, identify risks and report to the Line Manager. • Compile SHE performance reports on occurrences and provide statistical information to Line Manager. Output 2: Emergency Evacuation Support • Provide administrative support during drills and emergency evacuations. • Ensure all people are counted for and report on the roll call to the Primary Emergency Response Team Leader. • Note the date, time, and any observations and submit an action plan. • Controls access to the emergency scene and keeps people away from unsafe areas. Output 3: Contractor Administration Support • Ensure continuous update of contractor lists and maintain safety files for internal risk management requirements. • Monitor Contractors performance on site in accordance with applicable Regulations. • Record and submit any non-conformances to Line Manger. • Support in monitoring the quality control requirements as per the inspection sheet. Output 4: HSE Inspections and hazard identification • Conduct monthly SHE internal inspections and identify safety related issues hazards (e.g., slip andfalls, exposed electrical wires) • Coordinate ERT monthly inspections and submissions of reports. • Identify and report unsafe acts/situations or conditions. • Constantly walk through the buildings floors and premises to ensure that safety protocols are followedby employees and log any maintenance and HSE action items. • Verify and inspect that all machinery, equipment’s are in a serviceable condition and safe for use. • Support in implementing prescribed risk controls and mitigation measures relative to identifiedhazards, risk, and / or incidents/accidents. • Support in regular review of safety protocols. • Coordinates all Safety data sheets for chemicals and any related materials/products. • Support in site inspection and mitigations for special projects (SAICA events, camps, and exams). • Document and submit inspection reports. • Distribution and record of PPE to employees. Output 5: Incident Administration Support • Support in the assessing and recording of work-related incident/accidents, near misses. • Support in implementation of risk control measures, corrective action plan for all work-relatedincidents/accidents and near misses. • Keep and maintain incident records and statistics. • Support in promoting and monitoring safe working practices at all SAICA offices. Output 6: Audit Administration Support • Participate to internal SHE audits. • Gather and submit relevant documentation required for the audit as per audit programme. • Follow-up and ensure action plans are implemented by the relevant business units Output 7: HSE Employee Awareness and Training Administration Support • Facilitate bookings for internal HSE related trainings and safety programs. • Maintain and update safety training matrix plan, records, and register. • Participate in emergency drills and exercises on how to improve responses to emergency situations. • Support in updating safety manuals and guidelines. • Support in script content writing for the Newsletter guide. • Support during employee and stakeholder (Tenants/Contractors) induction sessions Output 8: OH&S Administration • Provide the necessary administrative support to the Line Manager. • Maintain effective collaboration and communication with the ERT members and ensure reports are submitted within the SLA timeframe. • Coordinate and record action items on all monthly and quarterly HSE Performance meetings. • OH&S quarterly meetings - prepare OH&S Committee agenda, record minutes and action plan for approval by the Line Manager and Chair. • Maintain and update Legal Appointments register. • ERT Quarterly meetings - prepare OH&S Committee agenda, record minutes and action plan forapproval by the Line Manager and Chair. • Record and submit OHS&E unit’s monthly invoices and SAICA Makro Trade account invoices spreadsheets for divisional reporting by Line Manager. • Gather and order FMS stationery requirements. • Report client’s safety concerns, request to the Line Manager and tracking and monitoring progressagainst actions. • Monitor Clients on completed and outstanding assignments relating to OH&S matters and report toLine Manager. Output 9: Embrace and drive organisational transformation • Actively contribute to cost containment efforts by identifying opportunities for operational efficiencies,optimising resources, and ensuring value-driven decision-making in daily activities. • Support SAICA’s transformation into an insights-driven organisation by leveraging data and analytics to enhance decision-making, improve processes, and drive continuous improvement in your area ofwork • Effectively utilise digital tools and technology to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and improve service delivery. Stay informed about new and emerging technologies relevant to your role • Adopt and embrace AI-driven tools and automation to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and effectiveness in job-related tasks, while continuously seeking opportunities to improve processes through innovative technology solutions • Identify and support SAICA’s management of risks related to sustainability, including environmental risks, regulatory changes and social risks and integrating them into business continuity and risk management plans
