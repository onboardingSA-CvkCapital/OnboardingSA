About the role

The Department of Public Works, Roads and Transport (Mpumalanga) is hiring a Director Building Infrastructure Programme Management, based in Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,834 a year. How to apply: Applications must be submitted online through the link erecruitment.mpg.gov.za Reference number to quote: D/BIPM/HO/ Closing date: 30 October 2026 at 12h00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic and operational leadership in the planning, design, delivery and management of provincial building infrastructure programmes and projects.
  • Direct the development and implementation of infrastructure project pipelines, ensuring alignment between client department requirements, approved infrastructure plans and available budgets.
  • Oversee multidisciplinary planning and design processes, project readiness assessments, procurement planning, contract readiness and infrastructure implementation.
  • Ensure effective management of consultants, contractors and built-environment professionals, while monitoring project performance, quality, expenditure, risks, compliance and delivery targets.
  • Lead stakeholder engagement with client departments, Provincial Treasury, municipalities and other strategic partners to facilitate infrastructure delivery.
  • Ensure sound governance, financial management, risk management and compliance with applicable legislation, Treasury prescripts, IDMS, FIDPM and related regulatory frameworks.
  • Oversee project completion, commissioning, handover and close-out processes, and manage human, financial and other resources of the Programme Management Unit to achieve departmental infrastructure delivery objectives.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 plus an appropriate recognised Bachelor’s Degree (NQF Level 7) in Architecture/Construction Management/Quantity Surveying/Engineering/Project Management/Town Planning or a related Built Environment qualification.
  • A postgraduate qualification in the Built Environment, Infrastructure/Project Management or related field will be an added advantage.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at Middle/Senior Managerial level. within infrastructure planning, design, construction or programme/project management.
  • Appointment is subject to the signing of a performance agreement and financial disclosure.
  • Successful completion of the Nyukela Public Service Senior Management Leadership Programme as endorsed by the National School of Government available as an online course on https://www. thensg.gov.za/training-course/sms-pre-entry-programme/, prior to finalisation of an appointment.
  • Knowledge.
  • Skills and Competencies:
  • Extensive knowledge and practical understanding of public-sector infrastructure delivery
  • Including the PFMA
  • Treasury Regulations
  • Infrastructure Delivery Management System (IDMS)
  • Framework for Infrastructure Delivery and Procurement Management (FIDPM)
  • CIDB prescripts
  • Supply chain management requirements and applicable builtenvironment legislation and standards
  • Knowledge of infrastructure programme and project planning multidisciplinary design management, procurement documentation, construction contract management, cost management, risk management, project monitoring and infrastructure closeout.
  • Registration or eligibility for registration, with an appropriate statutory/professional council in the built environment will be an added advantage.
  • A valid driver’s licence.
