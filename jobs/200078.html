About the role

Faircape Management Services (FMS) is a recognised leader in sectional title and homeowners' association management, proudly managing more than 90 residential schemes across the Western Cape. Our reputation is built on professional service, strong governance, innovation, and an experienced support structure that enables our Portfolio Managers to focus on delivering exceptional service to trustees, owners and residents. We are looking for an experienced Portfolio Manager – Sectional Title & HOA to join our established team based in Claremont, Cape Town. This opportunity is ideal for a property professional who thrives on building trusted client relationships, managing complex portfolios, and ensuring legislative compliance while working within a highly supportive environment. The Opportunity As a Portfolio Manager, you will take ownership of a portfolio of sectional title and homeowners' association schemes, acting as the primary advisor to trustees while ensuring each scheme is professionally managed. Supported by dedicated Legal, Finance, Maintenance, Procurement, Insurance and HR teams, you'll be able to focus on strategic portfolio management rather than day-to-day administration.
