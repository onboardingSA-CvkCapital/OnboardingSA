About the role

Launch Your Career with CTP Cartons & Labels! Are you ready to build a successful career in Sales? Do you thrive on challenges, enjoy working with people, and have a positive attitude that inspires those around you? CTP Cartons & Labels has an exciting opportunity for a Sales Intern who is eager to learn, grow, and gain valuable hands-on experience in a fast-paced manufacturing environment. About the Internship This 18-month internship is ideal for a Business or Sales Management student who requires workplace experience to complete their qualification. You'll gain practical exposure to every aspect of the Sales function while working alongside experienced professionals who are committed to helping you develop your skills and confidence. If you're someone who enjoys solving problems, embraces challenges, and is determined to make a difference, this opportunity is for you! What We're Looking For We are looking for a motivated, energetic, and resilient individual who: • Has excellent communication and interpersonal skills. • Is highly organized and able to manage multiple priorities. • Pays close attention to detail and produces accurate work. • Is comfortable working with Microsoft Excel and Outlook. • Takes initiative and is always willing to learn. • Works well independently as well as within a team. • Maintains a positive attitude, even during busy and demanding periods. • Thrives in a fast-paced environment where priorities can change quickly. • Is resilient under pressure and remains calm when faced with challenges. • Has a "go-getter" mindset, takes ownership of their work, and looks for opportunities to contribute. • Is customer-focused and passionate about delivering excellent service. • Is eager to grow both personally and professionally. What You Will Learn During your internship you will gain valuable experience in: • Processing customer sales orders. • Building and maintaining strong client relationships. • Handling customer queries and resolving complaints professionally. • Maintaining and updating customer information. • Monitoring stock levels and participating in stock counts. • Working on a production management system. • Coordinating artwork, samples, and product designs. • Understanding work tickets, quality assurance processes, and the importance of meeting customer delivery deadlines. • Developing commercial awareness and understanding how Sales contributes to business success. Who Should Apply? If you are someone who embraces challenges, enjoys learning something new every day, and wants to build a successful career in Sales, we encourage you to apply. Bring your enthusiasm, determination, and positive attitude—we'll provide the experience and support to help you grow into a successful Sales professional.
