About the role

This Isn’t a “Just Another Sales Job” This is uncapped sales commission and an opportunity for you to be in control of you success. Let’s be straight with you. This role is not for everyone. It’s for someone who’s done the hard yards in sales, knows how to run a proper discovery call, isn’t afraid of cold outreach, and wants to earn well above market rate from the comfort of their own home. We’re a fast-growing New Zealand company that helps real estate agents sell more properties and build predictable listing pipelines. We need someone who can sell like a professional, build relationships with clients, and close deals on video calls. You’ll work night hours from South Africa - but you’ll work from home, with complete autonomy, and with uncapped earning potential that rewards performance without a ceiling. The Opportunity • Position: Business Development (Remote) • Company: Real Growth Solutions - Christchurch, New Zealand • Location: Work from home, anywhere in South Africa • Hours: 8:00 PM – 5:00 AM SAST (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM NZT) • Schedule: Monday to Friday (NZ business days) (Sunday to Thursday South African time) Contract • Full-time, permanent • Base Salary: R26,000 per month • Commission: Uncapped - we expect top performers earning R26,000+/month in bonuses within 12 months. There is no cap. • Total OTE after 12 months: R52,000+/month within 12 months or (R624,000+ per year) • Equipment: You provide your own laptop, headset, and reliable internet Important: The commission structure is designed so that a high performer should be doubling their base within 12 months. We don’t put a ceiling on what you can earn. The more you close, the more you make. Period. What You’ll Actually Do Every Day • This is a full-cycle sales role with a strong focus on prescriptive, consultative selling. You’re not taking orders - you’re diagnosing problems and prescribing solutions. • Cold outreach to New Zealand real estate agents via phone, email - you’ll be prospecting and booking your own meetings alongside warm leads from our appointment-setting team • Running discovery calls and sales presentations via Google Meet - you’ll present our products, handle objections, and close deals on video • Managing a pipeline of past, present, and future clients - following up relentlessly. • Conducting client support and onboarding calls with new customers to ensure they activate properly and stay long-term - reducing churn is part of your role • Booking follow-up meetings on every single call (BAMFAM: Book A Meeting From A Meeting) - no call ends without the next step locked in • Reporting weekly on pipeline, activities, show rates, and close rates - you’ll be measured on results, not effort alone Why This Role Is Different • Most remote sales jobs from SA pay you a flat rate and cap your upside. We don’t. • Uncapped commission - there is literally no limit on what you can earn. Top performers will out-earn most senior managers in South Africa. • Work from home permanently - no commute, no office politics. Just results. • Sell a product that genuinely works - our clients see real, measurable results (25–70 buyer inquiries per campaign, 5–10+ future listing opportunities identified per campaign). You’re not selling a bad product!. • Autonomy and ownership - we don’t micromanage. We set targets, give you the tools, and get out of your way. • Be part of a growing company at the ground floor - we’re scaling from 1M to 2.5M+ in annual revenue. You’ll have a direct impact on that growth, and your career will grow with the company. • Night shift premium lifestyle - your days are completely free. Gym, family, side projects - your mornings and afternoons belong to you.
