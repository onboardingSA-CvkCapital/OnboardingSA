About the role

The Department of E-Government (Gauteng) is hiring a Deputy Director: Employeee Relations and Wellness in Corporate Management - Human and Physical Resource Management, based in Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: To apply for the below positions, please submit your application strictly online at http://professionaljobcentre.gpg.gov.za. No hand-delivery or emailed applications will be accepted. Reference number to quote: REFS/ 035060 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the development of the departmental employee relations policies, guidelines and processes: Manage structured and compliance to legislative frameworks, policies, practices and processes and provide support where there are gaps.
  • Maintain effective policies and procedures so that labour disputes and grievances are minimized.
  • Interpret policies, procedures and negotiated agreements and advise management on their practical application and the management of deviations.
  • Make recommendations for changes to existing policies and procedures to ensure compliance with new or proposed regulations.
  • Contribute to the development of best practices and alignment with industry standards.
  • Drive the adoption of employee relations documentation and policies by management.
  • Direct employee relations activities, including conflict resolution, disciplinary actions, and grievance procedures.
  • Provide guidance and coaching to managers and employees on employee relations matters, ensuring fair and consistent application of policies.
  • Ensure that the department is up to date with employment laws and regulations and ensure compliance in all employee relations practices.
  • Manage Departmental disciplinary code and procedure: Provide procedural advice to both management and employees regarding the interpretation and application of Disciplinary code and procedures, Incapacity code and procedure (poor performance and ill health), guidance on policy, legislation and advising management on work adaptations and alternative work options.
  • Manage workshops and advocacy on labour relations matters (conflict, Sexual harassment, delegations, misconduct, code of good practice on dismissals, rules of evidence on disciplinary hearing.
  • Manage Collective Bargaining, Dispute resolution and Grievances: Manage investigation of reported cases.
  • Administer completion of cases within policy requirements.
  • Attending all Conciliations and Arbitrations as scheduled by General Public Service Sector Bargaining Council (GPSSBC).
  • Represent the Department in Co-ordinating Chamber of the PSCBC for Gauteng Province (CCPG (provincial), The General Public Service Sector Bargaining Council (GPSSBC) (national) and other forums.
  • Manage proper implementation of the Department disciplinary code and procedures.
  • Advise on proper procedures to be followed during dispute resolution and grievance management process.
  • Educate management and employees on policies, procedures, collective agreements and relevant legislation continuously.
  • Examine the legal analysis and risk evaluation of potential disputes and disputes that are declared, to determine the appropriate management strategy.
  • Manage investigation of misconduct cases and the implementation of arbitration awards and collective agreements: Coordinate investigation of misconduct cases reported at Head Office.
  • Facilitate disciplinary hearings and coordinate the appointment of chairpersons and initiators of disciplinary hearings.
  • Represent the employer in disciplinary hearings.
  • Coordinating the lodged appeals and facilitating the implementation of the awards thereof.
  • Liaise with the Legal Department with regards to the evaluation of disputes and disciplinary cases and determining to whom the dispute or pre-dismissal arbitration should be assigned, to present management’s case.
  • Coordinate the preparation e-Gov case, heads of argument and pleadings for noncomplex cases.
  • Oversee and advise legal counsel during statutory and non-statutory arbitration or pre-dismissal arbitration proceedings.
  • Assist with the analysis, evaluation and implementation of arbitration and pre-dismissal arbitration awards to determine possible grounds for rescission or review.
  • Liaise with service providers on training needs by the organization.
  • Manage the development and implementation of employee health and wellness strategies and programmes: Improve and implement comprehensive health and wellness programs that promote physical, mental, and emotional well-being.
  • Collaborate with crossfunctional teams to design and deliver wellness initiatives, such as fitness programs, stress management workshops, and mental health support services.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of wellness programs through data analysis, employee feedback, and participation rates, and make recommendations for improvement.
  • Foster partnership with external vendors and benefit providers to negotiate contracts and ensure the delivery of highquality wellness services.
  • Prepare and present reports for submission to relevant stakeholders.
  • Promote and facilitate the implementation of occupational health, safety and environmental management strategies and programmes: Manage the implementation of Safety Health, Environment, Risk, Quality (SHERQ).
  • Manage the compliance Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) in the building.
  • Manage the inspection of workplace.
  • Manage resources
  • General supervision of employees
  • Allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by supervisees
  • Advise and lead supervisees regarding all aspects of the work
  • Manage and monitor performance and appraisal of employees
  • Manage conduct and discipline of employees in the subdirectorate
  • Facilitate the training and development all employees in the subdirectorate
  • Respond prompt to audit queries
  • Review Job profiles and sign off
