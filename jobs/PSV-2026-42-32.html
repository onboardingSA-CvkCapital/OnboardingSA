About the role

The Department of Higher Education and Training is hiring an Assistant Director: Marketing and Communication in Technical and Vocational Education and Training Component: ORBIT TVET College. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Direct your application to: The HR Manager: ORBIT TVET College, Private Bag X 82096, Rustenburg, 0299 or hand deliver to Cnr Bosch & Fatima Bhayat Street, Rustenburg (Mon-Thu 07:30 to 15:30 and Fri 07:30 to 13:30) or email to recruiment@orbitcollege.co.za, quoting the post reference number on the subject. Reference number to quote: ORB22/2026 Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: The application must include a fully completed and signed new Z83 form obtainable from any Public Service Department or on the internet at www.gov.za, and a detailed curriculum vitae only. Certified copies of identity document, senior certificate and highest required qualification as well as a driver’s licence where necessary, will only be submitted by shortlisted candidates to HR on or before the day of the interview date, following communication from HR. Incomplete and unsigned application will not be considered. Applications received after the closing date will not be accepted. The filling of the post will be done in terms of Department approved Employment Equity Plan. ORBIT TVET College is committed to providing equal opportunities and practicing affirmative action employment. All shortlisted candidates, including SMS, shall undertake two pre-entry assessments. Selection panels shall score both technical exercises as an additional criterion in the interview process. The College reserves the right not to make an appointment. If you do not receive any response within eight weeks after closing date of this advertisement, kindly accept that your application was unsuccessful. Responsibilities

  • Develop and manage the college brand identity and brand strategy.
  • Manage all college signage and document branding (letterheads, certificates, banners, brochures, pamphlets and programme information).
  • Formulate and ensure implementation of the college branding strategy.
  • Monitor brand compliance across all college communications and platforms.
  • Manage all media and general community liaison and communications, including coordination of media queries and responses.
  • Track all positive and negative media stories about the college and forward to relevant managers.
  • Manage all college events including exhibitions, graduations, official functions and special marketing events.
  • Regularly communicate college campaigns on social media platforms and digital channels.
  • Maintain and regularly update the content, design and layout of the college website.
  • Develop and implement the college’s social media strategy across all platforms (facebook, Instagram, Linkedin, Twitter, etc).
  • Coordinate with ICT and other units for timely content updates.
  • Draft articles and serve as sub-editor for the monthly electronic newsletter, gather articles from colleagues and college activities, verify and submit for approval.
  • Provide photographic services at college events and market the college through branding at events.
  • Render management services to staff, manage staff development, performance agreements, manage units’ budget and supervision of the unit.
  • Provide communication administrative support for all college events and coordinate college events planning.

Requirements

  • A recognised 3 year National Diploma or Bachelor’s Degree (REQV) in Communication, Marketing, Public Relations or Journalism qualification.
  • Added advantage - a postgraduate qualification in the relevant field.
  • 3 to 5 years’ working experience in a communication or marketing environment.
  • Experience in a TVET or higher education environment will be advantageous.
  • Knowledge of policies and governance environment of TVET Colleges including annual reporting requirements of Higher Education Institutions.
  • Knowledge of digital marketing, social media and website management.
  • Knowledge and understanding of TVET administration and Higher Education sector policies and relevant legislation.
  • Computer literacy - proficiency (MS Office, design software, social media tools).
  • Planning and organising skills.
  • Communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Report writing, analytical and project management skills.
  • People management and team leadership skills.
  • A valid driver’s licence.
