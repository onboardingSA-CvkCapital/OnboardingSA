About the role

Transnet (Transnet NPA) is hiring a Safety & Quality Officer : Aviation, based in Richards Bay. The role reports to the Safety & Quality Manager. Grade: G. Implement, evaluate and maintain an effective Safety and Quality Management System within the aviation Business Unit.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure implementation of a Safety and Quality management system that complies with internal procedures and external regulatory requirements.
  • Planning and facilitation of TNPA Aviation Safety and Quality Meetings.
  • Ensure an effective incident management process is implemented and sustained.
  • Implement and continuously review an Incident/Accident Reporting and Investigation Programme.
  • Conduct internal audits/Ad Hoc audits in accordance with Safety and Quality Management System.
  • Identify and Coordinate Safety and Quality Training needs and advise management accordingly with respect AOC, AMO and ATO requirements for personnel.
  • Ensure that Safety and Quality Risk Assessments are planned, executed and reports compiled and communicated.
  • Develop, implement and review Safety and Quality procedures on the Safety and Quality Management platform in accordance with legislative, internal requirements and ensure continuous improvement.
  • Implement Regulatory requirements and maintenance thereof on extensions of TNPA aviation scope of approval when business needs arise.
  • Ensure legal and other requirements are complied with in terms of safety health and environment disciplines.
  • Ensure that incidents are reported to Safety and Quality Manager, Accountable Manager, Regulator, respective OEMs, and attend to their investigations or inquiries when required.
  • Conduct and review Safety Risk Assessments in the interest of aviation safety.
  • Coordinate Safety and Quality requirements in Projects in accordance with the needs of the aviation Business Unit and ensure alignment with broader organizational strategic objectives.
  • Document, Implement and review Safety and Quality Procedures as per the organisational Safety and Quality Management System.
  • Coordinate TNPA aviation Safety and Quality Meetings.
  • Provide safety data to management committee in Safety and Quality meetings.
  • Reporting to TNPA Aviation on matters regarding DIFR or LTIFR
  • Ensure regular contribution and participation in Sustainability Management (energy saving and reducing carbon footprint) within aviation operations - quality effort.
