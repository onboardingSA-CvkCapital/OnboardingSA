About the role

Purpose:                    Lead Brights Hardware’s full omni-channel marketing strategy across stores, franchises and eCommerce. Drive brand growth, customer acquisition, retention and revenue across a leading Western Cape hardware and Do It Yourself (DIY) retailer. You will own the marketing function and deliver measurable impact on sales, profit and brand equity. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: • Brand & Omni-Channel: Develop and execute integrated marketing plans across retail and digital. Manage campaigns, media and Return On Investments (ROI) reporting • eCommerce & Digital: Grow online sales, optimise the customer journey and manage digital adverts and web platform. • Product & Promotions: Turn technical product information into compelling campaigns. • Drive promotional performance • Customer & Public Relations (PR): Grow loyalty, retention and brand advocacy. Manage PR, agencies and key stakeholder relationships • Leadership & Commercials: Lead the marketing team, own the marketing budget and report to the Chief Executive Officer and Executive Board on performance • Manage social media marketing and content platforms (Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, TikTok Ads, YouTube) • Manage marketing automation, Customer Relationship Management and customer data platforms • Manage eCommerce platforms and product information management systems • Utilise Business Intelligence tools including Google Analytics 4, Power BI, Tableau and Looker Studio to track performance, report on ROI and inform omni-channel marketing decisions. Experience with retail ERP systems such as Kerridge K8, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics or Oracle will be highly valued.KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS: • Key Performance Indicators (Omni-Channel Focus) • Revenue and profit contribution from marketing-driven initiatives (in-store + online). • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), retention rate and lifetime value (LTV). • eCommerce sales growth, conversion rate, average order value, and digital ROI. • Brand awareness, equity and share-of-voice metrics. • Campaign Return on Investments (ROI) and effectiveness across paid, owned and earned media. • Loyalty programme enrolment, engagement and redemption rates. • Media coverage quality and volume (PR value). • Team performance, capability development, and retention. • Budget adherence and efficiency of Advertising and Promotions spend. • Net Promoter Score / customer satisfaction linked to marketing touchpoints.
