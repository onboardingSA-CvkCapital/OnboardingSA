About the role

Ready to kickstart your career in Customer Service? At Frogfoot, we believe that every customer interaction is an opportunity to create a positive experience. We're looking for a Customer Service Intern who is passionate about helping people, solving problems, and making a difference with every conversation. This is an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced customer service environment while developing valuable skills in customer communication, problem-solving, service delivery, and professional workplace practices. If you're ready to hop into the pond, learn new skills and make an impact, we'd love to meet you! About Frogfoot Frogfoot is a leading open-access fibre infrastructure provider in South Africa. We build and operate fibre networks that connect homes, businesses and communities, working with multiple Internet Service Providers to deliver reliable connectivity across the country. We're passionate about connecting people — not only through fibre, but by creating opportunities for talented individuals to learn, grow and build meaningful careers. Purpose of the Role The Customer Service Intern will support our Customer Service team by assisting with customer enquiries, service-related queries and administrative tasks while learning how to deliver exceptional customer experiences. You'll gain practical exposure to communicating with customers across different channels, understanding customer concerns, resolving queries and working with internal teams to find solutions. This internship is designed to help you develop your communication, customer service, problem-solving, system, and professional workplace skills while learning from experienced professionals. What You'll Be Doing Customer Support & Communication • Assist with responding to customer enquiries professionally and timeously. • Support customers across different communication channels, including telephone, email and other digital platforms. • Listen to customer concerns and gather the relevant information needed to assist them. • Provide accurate information about Frogfoot products, services and processes. • Ensure customers are directed to the correct person or department when required. • Communicate with empathy, professionalism and respect.Customer Query & Problem-Solving on outstanding customer queries and information. • Contribute to creating a positive and supportive team environment. • Take ownership of assigned tasks and ask for guidance when needed.Learning & Development • Learn about Frogfoot's products, services, and customer processes. • Develop an understanding of customer service standards and best practices. • Learn how customer service performance
