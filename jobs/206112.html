About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies' requires a Buyer for their DC in Gqeberha. You will ensure the efficient and accurate receiving and packing of stock in line with Dis-Chem’s SOPs and requirements, and customer needs within the store. Essential: • Grade 12 - Matric or Senior Certificate with Mathematics • Tertiary Qualification in Supply Chain or similar field. • Minimum of 2 years’ experience as a buyer Advantageous: • 3+ years related experience in Customer Service, Planning, and Business Analysis within Retail Industry. supplier deliveries and maintain required day of supply as set out with management. • Maintain daily stock cover in line with the group or buyer requirements. • Oversee inventory and suggest transfers stock turn, sell through and stock on hand are within required ranges. • Attend to supplier queries relating to stock, pricing, returns, transfers, and new vendor applications. • Accurately list, load
