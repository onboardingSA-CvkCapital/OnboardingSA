About the role

At Cell C, we are not just a telecommunications company; we are a people-centric and consumer-focused organization committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our customers. In line with our dedication to customer-centricity, we are seeking a seasoned professional as an EBU Manager: Indirect Sales to join a dynamic team of #Unstoppables. Purpose of the Job: Cell C is a challenger in the B2B tech industry, providing cutting-edge solutions that empower businesses to thrive in the digital age. We are committed to excellence, innovation, and growth, and we are seeking a dynamic and experienced EBU Manager – Indirect Sales focused on growing and scaling our Indirect channel to join our team. This role will focus on developing and expanding our channel partner relationships to drive revenue and market presence. The EBU Manager – Indirect Sales will be responsible for developing and executing a comprehensive sales strategy that leverages channel partners to achieve revenue targets. This role requires a strategic thinker with a deep understanding of the tech industry and a proven track record of success in B2B sales through channel partnerships.
