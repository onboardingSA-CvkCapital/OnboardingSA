About the role

Start Your Teaching Journey at Glenstantia Primary School Start Date: 13 January 2027 Closing Date for Applications: 20 September 2026 Bring your training into the classroom and grow through real teaching experience. At Glenstantia Primary School in Pretoria, this opportunity is aimed at qualified teachers who are ready to build practical experience in the Intersen Phase and strengthen their confidence in the classroom. We are looking for 2 Intern Teachers who are enthusiastic about teaching, comfortable working in English and Afrikaans, and willing to contribute both in the classroom and through the school’s extramural and co-curricular activities. This is an opportunity to put your qualification into practice, work with learners in the Intersen Phase and continue developing your skills as an educator. What We Are Looking For • A recognised REQV14 qualification – a four-year Education Diploma or Bachelor of Education in the Intersen Phase • SACE registration • Advanced proficiency in English • Proficiency in teaching Afrikaans as a First Additional Language • Strong computer literacy skills, including Microsoft Office • Willingness and experience in participating in extramural and co-curricular activities • Residing in Pretoria Applicants must have no criminal record and must provide a valid police clearance certificate. About the Opportunity These are Governing Body posts at Glenstantia Primary School. For a newly qualified or developing teacher, the role offers the opportunity to gain practical teaching experience in the Intersen Phase while contributing to both classroom learning and broader school activities. Important – Prepare Before You Apply Please ensure the following documents accompany your online application: • A detailed CV • A cover letter • Your valid SACE certificate • A valid police clearance certificate • Copies of all relevant qualifications How to Apply • Applications must be submitted through the online application portal. • Please note: Hand-delivered CVs will not be accepted.
