About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Senior Auxiliary Services Officer in Maintenance and Cadastral Spatial Information Services, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R280,278 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 524 Stanza Bopape and Steve Biko Street, Suncardia Building, 6th Floor, Arcadia, 0002 or by email to Post386@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/386 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: Indian and White Males, and African and Indian Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Provide support services to technical and professional staff.
  • Assist in retrieval and filing of documents.
  • Report any errors in or damage of documents, assist in the rectification process thereof.
  • Report any errors in digital records, assist in the rectification process thereof.
  • Provide basic administrative support to the section, e.g stores inventory, asset management, section notices.
  • Assist in printing, copying and scanning documents.
  • Provide support in the training process of junior and new staff members.
  • Supply maps and photographs to internal and external clients.
  • Supply topographical information such as maps and orthophotos to clients over the counter or by post.
  • Provide cadastral documents and related information in hard copies and digital format.
  • Provide hard copies of diagrams, General Plans, Compilation Plans, Survey Records and Sectional Plans.
  • Provide digital copies of diagrams, General Plans, Compilation Plans, Survey Records and Sectional Plans.
  • Complete a proforma invoice for the collection of revenue.
  • Check payment before releasing information.
  • Provide property description updates to clients.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements
  • A Grade 12 Certificate or related equivalent qualification
  • At least 1 year experience in the cadastral information system
  • Job related knowledge
  • A thorough knowledge of existing Cadastral Information System
  • Knowledge of the functioning of Surveyor General’s Office
  • Computer Literacy in a Cadastral Survey environment
  • Job related skills
  • Computer literacy
  • Archiving ability
  • Good interpersonal relations
  • Organisational and supervisory ability
