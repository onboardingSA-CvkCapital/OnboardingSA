AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTION MANAGER Bergendal–Maneberg is an established mixed farming operation in the beautiful Citrusdal region and is looking for a Agricultural Production Manager with strong technical agricultural knowledge to join their team. Responsibilities will include (but not be limited to): • Production Operations: Assist with the planning, implementation, monitoring and supervision of irrigation, agricultural processes, livestock and general production activities to ensure optimal productivity and quality. • Technical Management: Assist with the planning, implementation and monitoring of agricultural programmes, including crop health and crop protection. Ensure the optimal utilisation, maintenance and functioning of farm equipment and systems. • People Management: Monitor, supervise and motivate employees to ensure productivity and the effective execution of tasks. Identify development needs and support the development of employees’ potential. Essential qualifications, skills, and experience: • Tertiary qualification in Agriculture; • At least 1 year experience in a similar position, including in a supervisory or management capacity; • Strong technical knowledge of fruit crops and agricultural processes; • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills; • Good computer skills, with specific knowledge of Microsoft Office.; • Good organisational and problem-solving skills.
