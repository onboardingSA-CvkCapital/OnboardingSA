About the role

We are looking for an experienced and commercially driven Account Manager to manage and grow strategic retail partnerships within the consumer technology and lifestyle products environment. The successful candidate will be responsible for driving sales growth, account development, retail execution and partner relationships across a portfolio of leading retail and e-commerce customers. This role requires someone who understands the dynamics of large retail environments and can effectively manage relationships across e-commerce, sporting goods, specialist and mass retail channels. You will work closely with retail partners and internal product, sales, marketing, finance and field teams to develop business plans, maximise sales opportunities, optimise stock levels and ensure exceptional in-store and online execution. Key Responsibilities Account & Relationship Management • Manage and develop strong, long-term relationships with key retail and e-commerce partners. • Develop annual account plans and lead quarterly business reviews with assigned customers. • Establish clear commercial objectives and ensure agreed business plans are effectively executed. • Build relationships at both head-office and store level. • Conduct regular customer meetings and store visits to identify opportunities, resolve challenges and strengthen partnerships. • Analyse customer performance, identify risks and develop actionable plans to drive growth. • Deliver agreed quarterly and annual sell-in and sell-out targets. • Develop and execute strategies to increase revenue and market share across assigned accounts. • Secure forward orders and proactively manage customer demand to minimise stock-outs. • Identify and convert new commercial opportunities within existing retail partnerships. • Develop account-specific growth initiatives and track delivery against agreed objectives. • Work with partners to maximise promotional, marketing and seasonal trading opportunities. • Negotiate effectively to achieve mutually beneficial commercial outcomes.Stock Planning & Performance Management • Monitor weekly sales, sell-out and stock-on-hand performance. • Analyse stock cover, sales trends and inventory ageing to identify potential risks. • Forecast future stock requirements and work with partners to maintain appropriate stock levels. • Develop corrective action plans for underperforming or overstocked products. • Provide accurate and timely sales and performance reporting to internal stakeholders.Retail & E-commerce Execution • Ensure products are effectively represented across retail stores and digital channels. • Drive strong merchandising, product visibility and display execution. • Identify opportunities to improve product positioning and customer conversion. • Work with field teams and retail partners to execute national and account-specific initiatives. • Monitor execution against agreed retail standards and business plans. • Support promotional campaigns and ensure agreed activities are implemented on time.Account Administration & Financial Management • Manage partner statements, billings, expenses and marketing claims in accordance with agreed processes and budgets. • Ensure account queries are resolved promptly and effectively. • Maintain accurate account records and reporting. • Monitor customer activity and provide insights to support commercial decision-making. • Collaborate closely with Product, Sales, Marketing, Finance and Field teams. • Communicate customer insights, opportunities and risks to relevant internal stakeholders. • Coordinate cross-functional initiatives to ensure successful execution. • Represent the business professionally when engaging with senior retail stakeholders.
