About the role

Marketing Intern | Kickstart Your Career with Frogfoot Ready to kickstart your marketing career? Frogfoot is looking for a Marketing Intern to join our Marketing team and gain hands-on experience across campaigns, social media, content, events, activations and communications. If you're creative, curious, organised and eager to learn, this is your opportunity to gain real-world experience, develop your skills and make an impact in a fast-paced and innovative environment. Who We Are Frogfoot is a leading open-access fibre infrastructure provider in South Africa. We build and operate fibre networks that connect homes, businesses and communities, working with multiple Internet Service Providers to deliver reliable connectivity across the country. We're passionate about connecting people, and we're looking for the next generation of talent to help us grow. What You'll Be Doing As a Marketing Intern, you'll get involved in a variety of exciting marketing activities, including: • Supporting marketing campaigns and promotional activities. • Assisting with social media content, captions, scheduling and monitoring. • Supporting events, activations and community marketing initiatives. • Assisting with content creation, website updates and communications. • Supporting artwork briefing, proofing and brand/Corporate Identity checks. • Maintaining marketing trackers, calendars, databases and reports. • Assisting with marketing stock, promotional materials, printing and courier requirements. • Supporting supplier, agency and internal stakeholder coordination. • Preparing presentations, activation packs and marketing materials. • Contributing fresh ideas for campaigns, content and customer communication. • Taking ownership of assigned tasks and meeting agreed deadlines.Who We're Looking For We're looking for someone who is ready to learn, grow and get involved. You should: • Be a South African Citizen. • Be between 18 and 35 years of age. • Have completed Grade 12 / Matric. • Have completed a 3-year Degree or Diploma in Marketing, Digital Marketing, Communications, Public Relations, Media, Business Marketing or a closely related field; OR • Have completed a relevant Higher Certificate or Occupational Qualification at NQF Level 5. • Have a genuine interest in marketing, content, social media and communications. • Be eager to learn and develop your professional skills. • Be organised, reliable and able to meet deadlines. • Have good written and verbal communication skills. • Be comfortable working independently and as part of a team. • Be willing to take initiative and hop into the pond! 🐸No previous formal marketing work experience is required. Exposure through tertiary projects, volunteering, campus activities, part-time work or personal marketing/social media projects will be advantageous. Computer Skills We Value You don't need to know everything, we're looking for someone who is comfortable with technology and willing to learn. We value familiarity with: • Microsoft Word • Microsoft Excel – basic formulas, formatting and data capturing • Microsoft PowerPoint • Microsoft Outlook and email • Microsoft Teams • Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn • Canva or similar design tools • Online forms, shared documents, cloud folders and digital trackers • General computer literacy and the ability to learn new systems quicklyWhat You'll Gain This internship will give you the opportunity to gain practical experience in: • Marketing campaigns • Digital and social media • Content creation • Brand and Corporate Identity • Events and activations • Internal and external communications • Marketing administration and reporting • Supplier and stakeholder engagement • Working in a professional corporate environmentYou'll learn from experienced professionals, build your confidence and develop skills that will help you take the next step in your career. Internship Details Job Type: 12-Month Internship | Fixed Term Duration: September 2026 – August 2027 Location: Cape Town Salary: Market-related internship Ready to Hop Into Your Career? If you're ready to learn, grow, bring your ideas and make an impact, we'd love to welcome you into the Frogfoot pond.
