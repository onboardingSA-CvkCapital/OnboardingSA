About the role

The Department of International Relations and Cooperation is hiring a Director: Employee Health and Wellness in Corporate Management, based in Head Office (Pretoria). This is a contract post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Please e-mail your application to Direhw26@dirco.gov.za Reference number to quote: DIRCO1127/04 Closing date: 21 December 2026 - Applications received after the closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic direction on employee health and wellness at head office as well as in the missions by: Developing and reviewing employee wellness policies and programmes and monitoring implementation, Providing strategic leadership and direction to the Employee Health and Wellness team in realising the Organisation’s strategic objectives, Developing the Directorate’s Annual Performance Plan and managing the implementation thereof, Ensuring continuous professional development of EHW officials, Providing guidance and support to managers on management of employees with emotional, behavioural and other health related issues.
  • Develop and manage health and productivity programmes by, Developing the DIRCO’s HIV & AIDS, TB, STI operational plan as per (DPSA) requirements, Promoting Health and Wellness of DIRCO employees and the family members, Establishing and maintaining the on-site health facility, facilitating the development of the EHW health calendar and ensuring implementation of health promotion activities, promoting and ensuring effective management of DIRCO sports teams, developing and implementing support interventions for designated groups (women, people with disabilities and youth), conducting regular workshops and training on psychological issues.
  • Ensure provision of psychosocial support services
  • Ensuring that counselling, crisis management, and trauma debriefing are provided to DIRCO officials and their family members, ensuring effective communication and marketing strategies of EHW services, ensuring that EHW officials provide confidential, professional and timely management and psychosocial support services, ensuring effective support for transferred officials and their families
  • Manage overall performance of the Directorate and participate in Corporate Governance
  • Formulating and managing the implementation of the operational plan, contributing to corporate governance in the Department, Representing the Directorate in Departmental governance structures and processes to contribute to improved governance, participating in the development and monitoring of the Audit Action Plan, participating in the development of the Departmental Strategic Plan, managing the Directorate’s budget in line with the PFMA and other relevant prescripts of government, empowering and capacitating officials through their personal development plans, Ensuring that performance agreements are in line with the Directorate’s Business Plan.

Requirements

  • An NQF level 7 qualification in Behavioural Sciences or related qualification.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at Middle Management/Senior Managerial level within the Employee Health and Wellness environment.
  • Valid registration with the relevant professional body (Health Professions Council of South Africa - HPCSA, South African Council for Social Service Professions - SACSSP or Employee Assistance Programme Association of South Africa - EAPA-SA).
  • Knowledge
  • Knowledge of the Public Service prescripts governing employee wellbeing
  • Knowledge of all aspects of employee health and wellness
  • Knowledge and experience in designing and implementing HIV and AIDS workplace programmes
  • Knowledge of psychosocial issues in the workplace, trauma and crisis management
  • Competencies
  • Strategic Capability and Leadership
  • Financial Management
  • People Management and Empowerment
  • Client Orientation and Customer focus
  • Change Management
  • Knowledge Management
  • Service Delivery Innovation
  • Project Management
  • Counselling skills
  • Facilitation and presentation skills
  • Diplomacy
  • Networking skills
  • Communication skills
  • Negotiation skills
  • Problem-solving and Analysis
  • Language proficiency
  • Sense of urgency
  • Initiative
