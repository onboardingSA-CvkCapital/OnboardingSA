About the role

Learner Experience (LX) Coach - Remote - R8 000 - R16 000 per month Anchor young talent on their remote learning journey as a dedicated mentor and community builder. About Our Client The organization is dedicated to reducing social inequality by connecting talented youth globally to meaningful learning and career opportunities. Driven by a mission to turn capability into real-world livelihoods, it fosters a supportive environment built on trust, continuous learning, and shared success. Operating as a remote-first team, the culture prioritizes authentic connection, constructive accountability, and creating positive impact for every learner. The Role: Learner Experience (LX) Coach The LX Coach serves as the consistent human presence and primary relationship anchor for learners moving through their multi-phase educational pathways. Managing a portfolio of up to 50 learners, the Coach acts as the first line of support, actively building cohort community and maintaining meticulous data records across platforms like Salesforce, Retool, and HESK. This role ensures candidates remain engaged, informed, and accountable from onboarding to programme completion. Key Responsibilities • Maintain consistent, proactive contact with a portfolio of up to 50 learners across various programme phases. • Serve as the first point of contact for learner queries, academic or personal challenges, and support ticket triage via HESK. • Log, update, and manage complete learner records in Retool and Salesforce to ensure seamless data tracking and M&E compliance. • Write clear, warm, and highly professional communications across digital channels like email, Slack, Circle, and WhatsApp. • Identify at-risk learners early, routing and tracking appropriate internal escalations or external support interventions. • Build cohort identity, belonging, and community engagement through regular peer connection moments and celebrations. • Collaborate daily with peer coaches, project managers, and lab leads to share cohort health insights and support strategies.
