About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Senior Community Relations Manager, based in Mokopane, Limpopo. This role is at a Band 5 level reporting to the GM External Relations & Sustainability.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the Community Relations function, ensuring delivery within approved budgets, headcount and governance requirements.
  • Develop, implement and maintain the Stakeholder Engagement Plan, including stakeholder mapping, prioritisation, engagement objectives, annual plans and monitoring of outcomes.
  • Identify and proactively manage community and stakeholder risks, incorporating social and human rights impacts, grievances, incidents, operational requirements, CAPEX/SIB projects, environmental matters and emerging community concerns.
  • Ensure communities within the Area of Influence are appropriately informed, engaged and consulted on matters that affect them, the operation and their interests.
  • Establish and maintain effective community engagement forums and stakeholder platforms that enable structured dialogue, issue resolution and sustainable relationships.
  • Provide leadership and direction to Community Relations Officers and Coordinators, ensuring engagements are executed consistently with approved plans, standards and business priorities.
  • Strengthen community communication through appropriate channels, materials and technology, working with Communications and other relevant functions.
  • Monitor community sentiment and emerging risks through engagement records, media, social media, perception surveys, grievances and other intelligence, and initiate corrective or preventative action where required.
  • Ensure community grievances and incidents are promptly referred, investigated, managed and resolved through the appropriate governance processes.
  • Develop and implement strategies to prevent, de-escalate and resolve community conflict, protecting employees, communities, operational continuity and the company's social licence to operate.
  • Work closely with Security and operational teams to identify and mitigate community-related threats that could affect employee safety, mine access or continuity of operations.
  • Establish long-term community engagement objectives and, together with the M&E function, monitor performance, effectiveness and stakeholder outcomes.
  • Provide community relations input into sustainability delivery, cross-functional integration, assurance reviews, governance requirements and public disclosures.
  • Coordinate BU-level stakeholder and community communication, ensuring alignment with Valterra Platinum sustainability commitments, standards and priorities.
  • Represent Community Relations in relevant governance forums, including the OSMC, providing clear reporting on material risks, trends, interventions and outcomes.
  • Build and lead a purpose-driven, inclusive and high-performing Community Relations team, aligned with company values and business priorities.
  • Set clear accountabilities and performance expectations, develop team capability and promote collaboration, continuous improvement and disciplined execution.
  • Strengthen coordination between Community Relations and operational functions to ensure stakeholder management is treated as an integrated business responsibility rather than a standalone activity.
  • Coordinate internal assurance, governance information requests, third-party reviews and reporting requirements relating to Community Relations.
  • Maintain appropriate records and evidence to support assurance, regulatory compliance, sustainability reporting and Valterra Platinum public disclosures.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in sociology, economics, commerce, mining engineering, industrial engineering, community development, regional planning, negotiation and conflict resolution or related discipline
  • Post-graduate qualification in related field will be advantageous
  • Experience, Technical knowledge and skills
  • Minimum 10 years of experience dealing with extractive industry external/ community relations or similar in a developing country, preferably South Africa and preferably in an IFC or Equator Banks-financed site/project where the IFC Performance Standards were applied.
  • Minimum of 8 years working in a team lead position
  • Experience with implementing the IFC Performance Standards
  • Experience in working with government institutions and communities
  • New technologies and their impact on operational activities within scope of role.
  • Business improvement tools and techniques.
  • Key value drivers across value chain and interdependence of key levers.
  • Leadership tools for creating a culture of purpose-led high performance and change.
  • Proficient in the practical application of the Operating Model (workflows, configurations, routines)
  • Advanced skills in the interpretation of benchmarks, and development of targeted, effective plans to achieve measurable operational improvements.
  • Advanced skills in identifying structuring, allocating, and management of work packages across a portfolio of project activities to effectively delegate to team.
  • Proficient in applying commercial and business principles in decision-making across the value chain to optimize value.
  • Advanced proficient in business case development, quantifying value and addressing stakeholder requirements.
