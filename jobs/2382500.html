About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AIpowered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined,future-fit role. We are looking for passionate, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace datadriven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job Underpinned by SAICA’s Customer Experience (CeX) strategy, the purpose of this role is to proactively lead and empower a team of Experiential Marketers in the execution and optimisation of SAICA's experiential strategy, ensuring the delivery of unified, engaging and impactful experiences across diverse audience groupsand touchpoints. This role focuses on providing strategic guidance and leadership, fostering executional excellence and continuous team development enabling consistent and timeous delivery of high-quality,impactful experiences that enhance overall brand experience, stakeholder engagement and loyalty. The role is responsible for driving the development, orchestration, and pre-emptive monitoring of SAICA’s annual experiential plan/roadmap aligned to the SAICA annual IGTM (Integrated Go-To-Market) plan. This includes leading the conceptualisation of experiential projects and fostering integrated, collaborative project teams across business units, leveraging partnerships and continuously enhancing experiential execution through industry innovation and best practice benchmarking.
