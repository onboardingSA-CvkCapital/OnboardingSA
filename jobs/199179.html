About the role

Purpose of the Role: Partnering closely with the Junior Compliance Manager, the Compliance Administrator provides structured administrative support to Dis-Chem Life’s Compliance function by maintaining accurate records, coordinating compliance processes, supporting monitoring activities, and ensuring regulatory documentation is complete, current, and audit-ready. Operating in a regulated financial services environment, this role helps ensure that clients, internal teams, and relevant stakeholders operate within applicable regulatory frameworks, internal policies, and ethical standards. The role strengthens the compliance environment by keeping processes organised, evidence reliable, and stakeholders supported. Success in this role is defined by accurate compliance administration, efficient stakeholder support, timely monitoring support, reliable issue tracking, and disciplined follow-up on actions, deadlines, registers, and compliance evidence. Role Summary The Compliance Administrator is responsible for supporting the core compliance administration activities that underpin the effective operation of Dis-Chem Life’s Compliance function. At a high level, success in this role requires the ability to operationalise regulatory requirements through accurate record keeping, document control, FICA/KYC and financial crime administration, intermediary onboarding and monitoring, representative onboarding, compliance monitoring support, reporting, and reliable follow-through on actions and deadlines. The Compliance Administrator works closely with the Junior Compliance Manager and cross-functional business stakeholders to ensure that compliance records, registers, evidence packs, policies, procedures, and reporting inputs are complete, well organised, and maintained in line with internal and regulatory standards.
