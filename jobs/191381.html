About the role

🚀 We’re Hiring: CRM Support Manager Deliver the support experience that keeps Africa's most loved Merchant Operating Platform running smoothly every day. 🏢 Business Unit: Technology 👤 Reports to: Head of CRM Why This Role Matters A great CRM platform is only as good as the support behind it. Every sales opportunity, merchant onboarding, customer interaction and service request depends on a CRM platform that is reliable, responsive and continuously improving. As CRM Support Manager, you will lead the operational support of our Salesforce ecosystem, ensuring stable platform performance, effective incident resolution and an exceptional support experience for users across the Merchant Division. From service desk operations and SLA management to knowledge sharing and user adoption, you will ensure CRM remains a trusted business platform. About Lesaka Lesaka is a fintech company on a mission to digitise commerce in Africa. We are listed on NASDAQ (LSAK) and the JSE (LSK) and serve merchants, consumers and enterprises across the continent. From spaza shops and taxi ranks to restaurants and city traders, our products power the small exchanges and the big steps of African commerce. One platform. Many products. One ambition: to be the most loved Merchant Operating Platform in Africa. How We Operate Our strategy starts from a simple operating truth. Exceptional customer experiences come from exceptional staff experiences. We balance four pillars across everything we do: • Customer Experience • Staff Happiness • Cost Discipline • Risk Control Each one amplifies the others when properly aligned. We have organised around one merchant journey with clear execution and accountability. Our North Star Deliver exceptional experiences to our customers and our staff. We measure ourselves against CHAP (Customer Happiness), SHAP (Staff Happiness), Cost-to-Serve Efficiency and Regulatory Impact. Happy customers. Happy staff. Disciplined cost. Controlled risk. The Role You will lead CRM support operations across the Salesforce ecosystem, ensuring users receive responsive, high-quality support while maintaining platform stability and service excellence. Working closely with CRM Development, Product, Sales, Operations, Customer Care and Architecture teams, you will oversee incident management, user support, knowledge management and continuous improvement initiatives that maximise CRM performance and user adoption. The role contributes directly to Customer Experience through reliable CRM services, Cost Discipline through efficient support processes and automation, and Risk Control through disciplined incident management, governance and operational stability. You will lead a team of three CRM support specialists responsible for delivering exceptional support across the Merchant Division. Ensure timely incident response and issue resolution. • Monitor support performance and service quality. • Deliver consistent support coverage across all CRM users. 🚨 Incident & Problem Management • support and guidance to CRM users. • Deliver user training and onboarding programmes. • Develop user guides, FAQs and support documentation. • Promote CRM best practices and platform adoption. • Improve user confidence and productivity across the business. ⚙️ Support Process Optimisation • Continuously improve CRM support workflows. • Introduce automation to streamline repetitive support Management • Maintain and continuously improve the CRM knowledge base. • Document solutions, workarounds and best practices. • Promote knowledge sharing across the support team. • Ensure documentation remains accurate and current. • Reduce reliance on individual expertise through structured knowledge management. 📊 Reporting & Insights • Produce regular operational support reports and dashboards. •
