About the role

ROLE PURPOSE Directs the acquisition of goods, materials, and services an organization needs to operate efficiently while controlling costs and reducing supply chain risks. Leads long-term sourcing plans, negotiates high-value contracts, and manages supplier relationships to optimize costs and secure the supply chain. Ensure compliance with Nexio’s procurement and BBBEE procurement policies. ROLE REQUIREMENT • Develop and execute category strategies and multi-year purchasing plans aligned with overall business goals • Runs tenders (RFP/RFQ) and aligns acquisitions with business goals. • Manage the supplier onboarding process across direct and support costs. • Build relationships with reliable suppliers and partners. • Maintain an update and accurate supplier database to evaluate and tracking performance as well as for risk mitigation. • Compile annual supplier Health Check to ensure the sustainability of suppliers. • Lead complex commercial negotiations to secure favorable pricing and delivery terms • Legally protects the company and ensures adequate Service Level Agreements (SLAs). • Manages, trains, and sets performance metrics (KPIs) for purchasing staff and buyers. • Partner with finance, legal, operations and support teams to forecast future needs and align workflows. • Building strong partnerships with key suppliers. • Ensures adherence to SPM policies, conflict of interest policies and confidentiality of supplier information in procurement process. • Ensure that supplier relations are managed by means of a contract where Standard Terms and Conditions of the Purchase Order are insufficient to cover the deliverable • Ensure Preferential Procurement with respect to all Nexio’s suppliers by ensuring compliance with the DTI’s BBBEE Codes of Good Practice. • Conduct ad hoc governance and strategic procurement projects as directed by management • Monitor and drive optimal for procurement of employee travel and ensure that reservations distributed timely • Monitors and optimised travel costs and corporate connectivity costs and implements controls for fair usage. QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE • Matric • Bachelor’s Degree in Supply Chain Management, Finance, Law or equivalent. • Post-graduate commercial Degree would be advantageous • At least 5 – 8 years relevant experience LEADERSHIP COMPETENCY REQUIREMENTS • People management skills. • Clear communicator, both orally and in writing. • Ability to generate solutions, evaluate them, and choose the most appropriate option for the contract. • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision. • Ability to execute in a high-pressure environment. • Entrepreneurial and commercial thinking. • Conflict Management Skills. • Relationship Management. • Negotiation Skills. • Project Management. • Policy Development
