About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AI-powered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined, future-fit role.We are looking for passionate, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace data-driven decision-making,and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job The Manager: Financial Crime and Emerging Legislation is responsible for providing technical expertise, guidance, and support to SAICA members on matters related to regulatory reporting and governance as they impact members. This includes amongst others, financial crime, anti-money laundering (AML), combating the financing of terrorism (CFT), and other emerging regulatory frameworks. The role ensures SAICA members are equipped to navigate and comply with legislative and regulatory requirements while advocating for compliance, ease of doing business and best practices.Reporting to the Lead: Legislation and Governance, this role plays a critical part in enhancing SAICA’s influence in shaping policy, supporting the profession when it comes to legislative and governance developments including addressing emerging risks in financial crime and legislation.The Manager: Financial Crime and Emerging Legislation is also responsible for SAICA’ involvement with respect to policy and strategic direction of the profession, standard-setting and supplementary guidance, related laws and regulations. Some of the key activities under the leadership of the Manager: Financial Crime and Emerging Legislation, are: • Advocating in the public interest by effectively harnessing member structures and consulting members, applying proper analysis and research to the policy, standards, laws and regulations and engaging with relevant stakeholders with solutions and recommendations; • Responding to technical queries submitted by members on matters relating to legislation and governance. • Maintaining and building on the reputation and the relevance of the profession by demonstrating SAICA’s ability to analyse and interpret proposed or new policy, standards and legislation through the media, events,stakeholder collaborations and research papers while harnessing our local and international strategic alliances; • Managing and hosting, on a regular basis, the SAICA Compliance in Practice monthly series as well as other SAICA events where legislative and governance developments are presented to members. • Supporting members through communication of relevant and important developments, developing technical resources and undertaking technical support activities for members; • Supporting and enhancing a culture of ethical behavior within the profession and standards settings.
