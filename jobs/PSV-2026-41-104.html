About the role

The Office of the Chief Justice is hiring an Assistant Director: Human Resource Management. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 to R573,897 a year. How to apply: Applications can be sent via email to 2026/169/OCJ@judiciary.org.za Reference number to quote: 2026/169/OCJ Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: The Organisation will give preference to candidates in line with the Employment Equity goals.

Responsibilities

  • Identify funded vacant posts, ensure request to advertise are submitted to Head Office, ensure submission of panel memo for approval, monitor the compilation and implementation of the recruitment plan and submit to Head Office, conduct shortlisting and interviews, provide advice and guidance on recruitment and selection matters, ensure compliance in line with the implementation of appointments (promotions, transfers and movements) and monitor maintenance of staff establishment.
  • Capture office leave plan, quality assure all necessary documents and forms completed and approved/disapproved leave on PERSAL, ensure that the leave register has been updated, conduct leave audit, ensure submission and completion of PILLIR applications, monitor completion and submission long service, Housing allowance, Probation reports and EHW forms, monitor completion and submission of termination documents.
  • Ensure all officials sign and submit performance agreements, mid-year review and annual assessments, monitor the payment performance reward, quality assure data for outstanding PMDS documents and take remedial actions and compile a report, facilitate premoderation meetings and take minutes, facilitate appeals emanating from the moderation process within the province, compile and submit training needs, implement the training plan in the province.
  • Ensure compliance on labour relations reporting, investigate and implement the recommendations on grievances, provide support to investigations and disciplinary hearings in consultation with labour relations, implement the outcomes of disciplinary hearing, dispute and appeal cases, provide support on conciliations and arbitrations and implement the EHW programmes in the province.
  • Ensure general supervision of employees, allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by officials, manage leave of staff, advise and lead supervisees with regard to all aspects of the work, manage performance, conduct and discipline of employees, ensure that all employees are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively, develop, implement and monitor work systems and processes to ensure efficient and effective functioning, address enquiries and provide advice and guidance on asset allocation and control.

Requirements

  • National Diploma in Human Resource Management/Public Administration/Public Management, Personnel Management or relevant qualifications at NQF level 6 with 360 credits as recognised by SAQA.
  • At least 3 years’ experience in the relevant field of which 1 year should be at salary level 7/8 or supervisory level.
  • PERSAL Certificate in Leave & Personnel Administration.
  • A valid driver’s license will serve as an added advantage.
  • Knowledge and skills
  • Knowledge and understanding of the Legislation/Prescript and framework governing the Public Service i.e
  • Public Service Act
  • Public Service Regulation
  • Labour Relations Act
  • Employment Equity Act
  • Basic Conditions of Employment Act
  • Skills Development Act
  • Collective Agreements
  • Codes of Remuneration and the Public Finance Management Act
  • Knowledge of HRM related standards
  • Practices
  • Processes and procedure
  • Knowledge of PERSAL and Batho Pele principle
  • Skilled in computer literacy, communication, report writing, analytical thinking, problem solving, planning and organising, decision making, motivation, presentation and time management.
