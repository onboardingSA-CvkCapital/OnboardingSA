About the role

The Government Pensions Administration Agency is hiring an Assistant Director: Physical Security, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. (Re-advertisement: applicants who applied previously are encouraged to reapply) Security Management Permanent The purpose of this role is to coordinate, implement, monitor and evaluate physical security services, security compliance measures, risk mitigation initiatives and security systems to ensure the protection of GPAA personnel, assets, information and facilities in accordance with applicable legislation, standards and security prescripts. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.gpaa.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: ASD/PS/2026/11- Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) No late applications will be considered Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: One permanent position of an Assistant Director: Physical Security is currently available at Security Management of GPAA.

Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for a wide variety of tasks which include but are not limited to the following: Coordinate and Monitor Physical Security Operations.
  • Attend to physical security incidents and assess impact of incidents.
  • Assist in investigations into security breaches and maintain incident tracking database.
  • Implement physical security risk assessment processes such as threat and risk assessment appraisals and security audits.
  • Analyse risk factors for new and existing facilities, identify physical security vulnerabilities and formulate remediations plans.
  • Assist in the assessment of the impact of incidents and make recommendations.
  • Manage the schedules for security personnel and track their attendance.
  • Render required physical security services to related contractors/provinces and agencies.
  • Review and report accidents that happened within the vicinity, inspect all areas for safety and fire hazard, and file reports on safety precautions.
  • Liaise with law enforcement, security related and relevant external and internal and authorities.
  • Monitor all unauthorised vandalism and removal of property, review and report accidents.
  • Ensure all security related systems remain operational and taking appropriate action to quickly remedy any failures.
  • Implement physical security and vetting policies and procedures.
  • Ensure both physical and vetting policies are implemented.
  • Provide employees with training on both security policies and procedures.
  • Maintain compliance on security policies and procedures.
  • Assist with guidance to Facilities management to develop, implement and maintain security policies, procedures, and processes, in accordance with best practice standards, frameworks and regulations.
  • Keep abreast with new effective policies and procedures implemented in other departments.
  • Manage Physical Security Systems and Services.
  • Ensure availability of duty roasters for 24-hour security in the organisation.
  • Monitor that security surveillance equipment is properly installed.
  • Respond to equipment failures and emergencies during off hours.
  • Maintain security related systems, including the DVR system, alarm, and access control systems for all facilities.
  • Ensure physical security at all facilities and contacting emergency services when appropriate.
  • Provide physical security awareness and training programmes.
  • Assist with the physical security management reports on all projects and initiatives.
  • Provide oversight and direction to security staff, including the scheduling of work assignments.
  • Assists with lock and key requests and tracking.
  • Ensure integration of all security related aspects with regards to personnel, documents, physical security, communications, computers and surveillance activities.
  • Conduct security related tasks ensuring the safety of employees and clients.
  • Maintain the total security function within the GPAA.
  • Support in the development and implementation of an effective Business Plan and budget for Security to support the achievement of GPAA’s strategic objectives.
  • Assist management regarding the security implementations of executive decisions.
  • Ensure the screening of employees and the organisation.
  • Maintain records of security incidents.
  • Ensure the fire safety systems throughout GPAA offices.
  • Ensure Occupational Health and Safety procedures.
  • Assign daily work for security staff based on assessment of fitness for specific assignments.
  • Implementation the Minimum Information Security Standards (MISS), Minimum physical Security Standards and compliance with Control Access of Public Premises and Vehicle Act 53 of 1985.
  • Manage and develop staff.
  • Manage the performance of the unit which involves coaching, mentoring, and take corrective action where required, develop performance standards and evaluate team and individuals.
  • Monitor staff regarding human resources such as leave, recruitment and grievances.
  • Compile the work plans for the section including the consolidation of operational plans into the directorate’s overall work plan.
  • Allocate work according to the individual workload, expertise and development needs of the individual.
  • Develop maintenance team performance standards.

Requirements

  • A relevant three-year National Diploma/Degree in Security Management, Policing, Public Administration, Safety Management, Criminology, Forensic Investigation.
  • Coupled with 3-5 years’ experience within Security Management environment with at least 2 years supervisory/junior management experience.
  • A driver’s license.
  • Grade “A” Registration with Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PSIRA).
  • Knowledge of Access Control to Public Premises and Vehicle Act , Criminal Procedure Act , Fire Brigade Act , Occupational Health and Safety Act , Minimum Information Security Standards , Protection of Personal Information Act , Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority Act , Disaster Management Act , Public Service Act , Firearm Control Act , Minimum Physical Security Standards (MPSS) , Government Immovable Asset Management Act (GIAMA) , Security directives (NIA, SAPS, NPA) , Investigations awareness programme , Relevant and applicable security directives, legislations and regulations.
  • Analytical Skills, investigation skills, facilitation skills, presentation skills, compliance monitoring skills, computer literacy skills, conducting research, problem solving, risk assessment, organisation and coordination, communications, project management, conflict resolution, decision making, leadership and supervision.
  • Objectivity, professional judgement, ethical conduct, ability to build strong network relationship, work independently, persuasiveness, reliability, attention to detail.
  • Self-efficacy, integrity, confidentiality.
