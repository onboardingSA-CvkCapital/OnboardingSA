About the role

AECI is hiring a Concept Draughtsman, based in Modderfontein. To develop and manage high-quality engineering concept drawings that support research, product development, innovation and capital projects. The role ensures designs comply with engineering standards, legislative requirements and drawing office procedures while supporting operational excellence.

Responsibilities

  • Concept Design & Drafting
  • Develop detailed concept drawings, layouts, assemblies and engineering design proposals using approved CAD software.
  • Prepare concept drawings based on engineering requirements and customer specifications.
  • Produce accurate engineering drawings that comply with company drafting standards.
  • Develop multiple design iterations to support engineering decision-making and project requirements.
  • Customer Engagement
  • Engage with engineers, project teams and internal customers to understand design requirements.
  • Participate in design discussions and provide technical drafting input.
  • Clarify engineering concepts and incorporate customer feedback into design revisions.
  • Build effective working relationships with stakeholders to ensure successful project delivery.
  • Research & Development Support
  • Produce concept drawings that support research, product development and innovation initiatives.
  • Assist with feasibility studies and engineering design reviews.
  • Support prototype development and evaluation of new engineering solutions.
  • Contribute to equipment improvement and product enhancement projects.
  • Drawing Control & Document Management
  • Maintain effective control of concept drawings, revisions and approvals.
  • Ensure engineering documentation is accurate, complete and stored according to document control procedures.
  • Maintain drawing registers and revision histories.
  • Ensure all engineering documentation is readily available for project teams and operational requirements.
  • Engineering Standards & Compliance
  • Ensure all drawings comply with company engineering standards, drafting procedures and specifications.
  • Apply relevant engineering codes, standards and legislative requirements during the drafting process.
  • Ensure compliance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, Explosives Act and other applicable regulations.
  • Maintain high standards of drawing quality and technical accuracy.
  • Project Support
  • Provide technical drafting support for engineering projects, prototype development, equipment upgrades and capital projects.
  • Assist engineering teams with design modifications and technical documentation.
  • Support project planning by producing drawings within agreed timelines.
  • Participate in engineering design reviews and project meetings.
  • Continuous Improvement
  • Identify opportunities to improve drafting standards, drawing quality and engineering processes.
  • Recommend innovative design solutions that improve operational efficiency and reliability.
  • Support engineering change management and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Contribute to the standardisation of engineering drawings and drafting practices.
  • Reporting & Communication
  • Provide regular progress updates to the Drawing Office Manager on project status and priorities.
  • Report on outstanding work, resource requirements and project risks.
  • Communicate effectively with engineering teams and stakeholders throughout project execution.
  • Maintain accurate records of completed work and project documentation.
  • Governance & Compliance
  • Comply with company policies, procedures and engineering governance standards.
  • Adhere to quality management systems and document control requirements.
  • Support engineering audits and compliance reviews.
  • Ensure confidentiality and integrity of engineering documentation.
  • Safety, Health, Environment & Risk
  • Comply with all Safety, Health, Environmental and Risk Management requirements.
  • Follow safe working practices during plant visits, engineering inspections and site activities.
  • Identify and report hazards and unsafe conditions.
  • Promote a culture of safety and compliance across engineering activities.

Requirements

  • Qualifications
  • Matric.
  • Higher National Diploma in Draughting (Mechanical or related engineering discipline).
  • In-depth knowledge of at least one relevant CAD drawing package (Inventor preferred).
  • Microstation knowledge would be advantageous.
  • Sound knowledge of engineering principles and drafting standards.
  • Knowledge of the Occupational Health and Safety Act and Explosives Act.
  • Knowledge of maintenance practices within a manufacturing environment.
  • Sound understanding of contract management, asset care and hazard control.
  • Intermediate proficiency in MS Project.
  • Experience
  • Minimum of 3 to 5 years' relevant experience in an engineering drafting environment.
  • Experience supporting engineering projects, product development and concept design.
  • Experience working in a manufacturing or industrial environment will be advantageous.
  • Experience with engineering document control systems and revision management.
  • Competencies
  • Strong engineering design and mechanical drafting skills.
  • Advanced CAD modelling and drawing interpretation capabilities.
  • Knowledge of Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T)
  • Understanding of manufacturing and fabrication processes.
  • Strong engineering problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Excellent technical report writing and documentation skills.
  • Strong planning, organisation and time management abilities.
  • High attention to detail and quality orientation.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills.
  • Customer-focused with strong relationship-building abilities.
  • Innovative mindset with a commitment to continuous improvement.
  • Professional integrity, accountability and sound decision-making.
