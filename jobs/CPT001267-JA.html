COURIER / LOGISTICS SALES REPRESENTATIVE – WEST COASTLocation: West Coast, Western CapeSalary: R20 000 – R22 000 Basic + Commission up to R11 500 per month + Business Travel ReimbursementAn established international business within the courier and logistics sector is seeking an experienced Courier / Logistics Sales Representative to manage and grow an existing customer portfolio across the West Coast.This role is suited to a commercially driven sales professional who understands courier, freight, transport or logistics solutions and enjoys developing long-term customer relationships.You will take responsibility for an established portfolio of customers while actively identifying, developing and securing new business opportunities within your territory.The successful candidate will be confident in consultative selling, account management, negotiation and territory development, with a proven ability to achieve revenue and growth targets. an accurate business travel log • Own reliable vehicle required KEY RESPONSIBILITIES • Manage, retain and grow an established portfolio of customers • Identify and secure new business opportunities agreements while protecting margins • Achieve monthly sales, revenue and portfolio growth targets • Identify cross-selling and additional service opportunities within existing accounts • Prospect for
