About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring a Speech Therapist Grade 1, 2 or 3 in PAMS, based in Dr Pixley Ka Isaka Seme Memorial Hospital. This is a permanent post, paying R413,121 – R472,359 a year. How to apply: Directives To Candidates: Applications to be hand delivered to 310 Bhejane Street (Hospital Gate Number 2) KwaMashu or Emailed To: pixley.recruitment@kznhealth.gov.za or Couriered To: 310 Bhejane Street (Hospital Gate Number 2) KwaMashu, 4360. For attention: Deputy Director: HRM Reference number to quote: DPKISMH 22/2026 Closing date: 23 October 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: The following documents must be submitted, Application for employment form (Z83), which is obtainable at any Government Department or form websitewww.kznhealth.gov.za. Responsibilities

  • Provision of effective and comprehensive assessment and treatment for paediatrics and general speech therapy to services to all patients.
  • Sound knowledge of paediatric dysphagia assessments and management.
  • Comprehensive caregiver training, patient education and related concerns.
  • Perform outreach services as needed, conduct group work/therapy and or block therapy as identified.
  • Confidentiality and ethical multidisciplinary approach to treatment of patients.
  • Conduct home and school visits and other community visits as needed.
  • Administrative work related to patient care:
  • Reportwriting, statistics
  • Meeting, case conference, legal reports as needed e.g. disability grants, medico legal work and insurance
  • Quality improvement plan initiatives:
  • Clinical guidelines, teaching and training, audits (core standards, infection control, ideal hospital, etc.) Effective communication with all stakeholders (Patients
  • NGOs, caregivers etc.) Ensure professional development in line with patient care profile
  • Support the supervisor in all projects and transformative efforts

Requirements

  • Grade 1: No experience required after registration as Speech Therapist.
  • Grade 2: at least 10 years relevant experience after registration as Speech Therapist.
  • Grade 3: at least 20 years relevant experience after registration as Speech Therapist.
  • Other Benefits:13thCheque, Medical Aid (Optional), Housing Allowance: Employee must meet prescribed requirements.
  • Senior Certificate/Grade 12 plus a National Diploma/Degree in Speech Therapy plus Current registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as a Speech Therapist plus Certificate of service endorsed by HR department from previous and current employer will be required only for shortlisted candidates.
  • Successful candidate will have to spend at least 1 year in-service.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Training and Competence Required:
  • Speech therapy paediatric and general diagnostic, therapeutic procedures
  • Knowledge of departmental administrative tasks, Knowledge of ethical code and scope of practice
  • Ability to problem solving and to apply analytical process to patient care
  • Supervisory skills for junior staff and students
  • Unendorsed valid Code B driver’s licence (Code 08) and Proficient in IsiZulu
