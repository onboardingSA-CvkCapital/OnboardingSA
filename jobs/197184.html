About the role

Kern doel van die pos: Effektiewe hantering van alle administratiewe take met betrekking tot kontraktering deur die Graan afdeling. Minimum Vereistes: • Graad 12 met rekeningkunde; • 3 jaar administratiewe ondervinding verkieslik in ‘n rekeningkundige omgewing.Vaardighede: • Goeie kommunikasie- en taalvaardighede in Afrikaans en Engels; • Rekenaarvaardig (MS Office met spesifieke klem op Excel); • Goeie interpersoonlike en konflik hanterings- vaardighede; • Syfervaardigheid, noukeurigheid en akkuraatheid essensieel; • Moet onafhanklik en onder druk kan funksioneer; • Goeie organisasie en prioritiserings- vermoë.Verantwoordelikhede: • Nakoming van interne kontroles,prosedures en beleide; • Opstel, opvolging en kontrolering t.o.v. alle gesluite kontrakte; • Versekering van data akkuraatheid vir operasionele en finansiële verslagdoening; • Nakoming van streng sperdatums en werksvloei dissipline; • Neem eienaarskap van administratiewe prosesse en uitsette; • Samewerking met alle personeel en bestuur om operasionele voldoening te verseker; • Bewustheid van en optrede teen onreëlmatighede of moontlike bedrog. Main purpose of the position: The effective handling of all administrative tasks with regards to the Grain division contracts. Minimum Requirements: • Grade 12 with accounting; • 3 year’s experience in an administrative position preferably in an accounting environment.Skills: • Sound communication and language skills in Afrikaans and English; • Computer literate (MS Office with a strong emphasis on Excel); • Good interpersonal and conflict handling skills; • Good numerical ability as well as the ability to work meticulously and pay attention to detail; • Must be able to function independently and cope under pressure; • Good organisation and prioritising abilities.Responsibilities: • Adherence to internal controls, procedures and policies; • Preparation, follow-up and controling with regard to all entered contracts; • Ensuring data accuracy for operational and financial reporting; • Adherence to strict deadlines and workflow discipline; • Taking ownership of administrative processes and outputs; • Collaborating with all staff and management to ensure operational compliance; • Awareness of and action against irregularities or potential fraud.
