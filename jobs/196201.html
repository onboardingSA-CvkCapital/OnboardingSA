About the role

Overview The primary objective of this role is to promote campus community, vibrant social activities and sports at the campus level to encourage a balanced lifestyle amongst the student body. This position requires an energetic extroverted personality and uniquely blends hands-on event execution, proactive student relationship-building, and creative visual storytelling to maximize both physical participation and digital engagement across all campus platforms. Key Responsibilities • Photograph and film key campus milestones, including Open Days, scheduled student excursions, and daily campus life activities for both our Salt River and Tygervalley campuses. • Create and design all necessary digital and physical promotional materials, event posters, invitations, and stationery. • Script, film, and edit engaging "How-to" videos to help students navigate campus systems. • Partner directly with national marketing teams and external creative agencies to supply requested video assets and local footage. • Update physical campus posters on the walls, create fresh visual displays, and physically maintain noticeboards to ensure the campus remains professional and completely clutter-free. • Coordinate a busy campus calendar featuring both large-scale events (like Orientation Week) and smaller recurring activations (like Student Fun Fridays). • Coordinate closely with the Student Representative Committee (SRC), help market their initiatives, and co-organize student-led events. • Handle all preparation logistics, including buying necessary supplies, managing RSVPs, setting up venues, and coordinating on-site first aid and security rosters. • Physically assemble event spaces, which includes heavy lifting of tables, chairs, banners, and sound equipment. • Plan, promote, and run community outreaches, charity donation drives, and volunteer initiatives. • Monitor equipment conditions, manage inventory logs, report broken gear, and establish and enforce safety rules for gym users. • Interact with students daily on campus to foster positive, trusting, and supportive peer-to-peer relationships. • Manage official student communication groups and chats, ensuring announcements are broadcast multiple times and remain exceptionally clear, clean, and detailed. • Resolve ongoing student queries and conduct student surveys to gather actionable feedback on campus life. • Organize resume and CV creation workshops, offering constructive, practical feedback to help students prepare for the workforce. • Create networking opportunities between students and potential employers to assist with work placements. • Maintain flexibility to work selected weekends to assist with campus events like Registration Days and Open Days. Skills and Competencies • Event Planning and Coordination • Communication Skills • Leadership and Mentorship • Interpersonal Skills • Organizational Skills • Creativity and Innovation • Student-Centred Approach • Cultural Awareness and Sensitivity • Problem-Solving • Team Collaboration • Enthusiasm and Energy Requirements • Own car and valid driver’s license • Qualification (NQF level 6) in Marketing, PR, Sport, Events or related field • 2 years in a similar role • Event management experience • First Aid knowledge
