About the role

The Department of Health is hiring a Chief Director: Communication and Stakeholder Management in Corporate Services, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R1,554,696 a year. How to apply: The Director-General, National Department of Health, Private Bag X399, Pretoria. 0001. Hand delivered application may be submitted at Reception (Brown Application Box), Dr AB Xuma Building, 1112 Voortrekker Rd, Pretoria Townlands 351-JR or should be forwarded to recruitment@health.gov.za quoting the reference number on the subject e-mail For attention: Ms M Shitiba Reference number to quote: NDOH 89/2026 Closing date: 14 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide Strategic leadership and direction for communication services.
  • Develop communication priorities and plans aligned with the Department’s strategic objectives, programmes and service delivery priorities.
  • Manage public, media and corporate communication.
  • Manage media relations and coordinate responses to media enquiries on matters relating to the Department.
  • Manage media relations and public relations.
  • Provide strategic direction on media relations and public relations activities of the Department.
  • Manage internal communication and information dissemination.
  • Ensure effective compilation, editing and quality assurance material for internal communication platforms.
  • Provide communication support to the Ministry and Director-General.
  • Coordinate communication support for the Minister and Director-General during major events, campaigns, public engagements and media activities.
  • Manage stakeholder relations and national health communication forums.
  • Develop and maintain effective relationships with key stakeholders within and outside government.
  • Manage communication governance, risk, audit and resources.
  • Manage responses to internal and external audit findings and communication-related audit queries.
  • Manage the human, financial and other resources of the Chief Directorate in accordance with the delegated authority and applicable prescripts.

Requirements

  • A Grade 12 certificate (NQF 4) and a qualification at NQF 7 in Journalism/Communication/Marketing/Public Relations or Media Studies.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at the Senior Management level in corporate communication, stakeholder management, media liaison or related environment.
  • Knowledge of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996, Public Service Act, 1994, Public Finance Management Act, Public Service Regulations, 2016.
  • Knowledge of strategic communication management, communication strategy development and integrated communication planning.
  • Knowledge of media relations, public relations, corporate communication, and stakeholder communication.
  • Knowledge of internal and external communication platforms, digital communication, social media and public information dissemination.
  • Knowledge of communication legislation, policies, protocols and government prescripts governing public information, media relations and access to information.
  • Knowledge of communication campaigns, content development, editing, messaging and media monitoring.
  • Good communication (verbal and written), strategic capability and leadership, people management and empowerment, financial management, change management, programme and project management, knowledge management, service delivery innovation, problem solving and analysis, client orientation and customer focus skills.
  • A valid driver’s license.
  • SMS pre-entry Certificate is required for appointment finalisation.
