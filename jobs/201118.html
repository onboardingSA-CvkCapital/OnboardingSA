About the role

Lead the Properties, Facilities and Capital Projects that Support the Future of Agriculture At VKB, we don't just support agriculture, we help shape its future. As a leading agricultural business, we continuously invest in the facilities, infrastructure, and property assets that enable our operations to thrive. We are looking for an experienced Regional Property Manager to take ownership of our property portfolio across the Free State, ensuring our assets remain safe, efficient, financially sustainable, and fit for growth. This role offers a unique opportunity to influence major capital projects, optimise property investments, and play a strategic role in one of South Africa's most respected agricultural businesses. The Opportunity Reporting into the Property function, the Regional Property Manager will oversee construction projects, property development initiatives, facilities management, maintenance planning, and leased property management throughout the region. You will serve as the link between operational stakeholders, contractors, consultants, and service providers, ensuring projects are delivered safely, efficiently, on budget, and in line with business requirements. This position is ideal for a commercially minded property professional who enjoys balancing strategic planning with hands-on project execution.
