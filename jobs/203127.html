About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies has an opportunity available for a Clinic Nurse Practitioner at our Diamond Pavilion, KImberley Store in the Northern Cape. The successful candidate will be responsible for managing and operating a profitable Primary Health Care Clinic within the Dis-Chem store, while ensuring high-quality patient care and operating within the scope of practice of a registered nursing practitioner. Minimum Requirements Essential • Matric or equivalent Senior Certificate. • Diploma or Degree in General Nursing and Midwifery. • Registered with the South African Nursing Council (SANC). • Nursing Practitioner must obtain BHF registration. • Valid driver’s licence. • Minimum of 2–3 years’ experience practising as a Primary Health Care Clinic Sister. Duties and Responsibilities Clinic Operations & Compliance • Ensure the clinic is open and operational according to the hours stated in the operational agreement. • Ensure all electronic and verbal communication is followed up within a reasonable timeframe. • Ensure daily checklists are completed, including Clinic Consumables and Temperature Checklists. • Accurately and timeously process claims to medical aids/health insurance. • Ensure all required clinic services are available and report any service gaps to management. • Maintain accurate records of patient details on the electronic system. • Maintain accurate and confidential patient records. • Adhere to healthcare regulations, safety standards and infection control protocols. • Collaborate with pharmacists and other healthcare professionals to enhance patient care. • Ensure patient queues are effectively managed. Communication • Communicate all compliments and complaints to management. • Provide management with feedback regarding the average “feet per hour” count, including set targets. • Ensure all clinic services are available and report any service gaps to management. • Ensure effective communication with the Clinic Department in accordance with standard operating procedures. Stock Control • Perform a full inventory and stock count upon arrival at the clinic for newly appointed and internally transferred Nursing Practitioners. • Conduct monthly inventory of assets to maintain standards. • Conduct bi-weekly stock counts to maintain adequate stock levels. • Manage government stock in accordance with the government stock Standard Operating Procedure. • Ensure all stock in the clinic is checked for expiration before being used on patients. • Dispose of expired stock in accordance with Dis-Chem’s Standard Operating Procedure. • Adhere to vaccination compliance Standard Operating Procedures when packing the refrigerator. • Ensure adequate stock levels are maintained in the clinic. Customer Service • Manage relationships with key stakeholders, including: • Patients. • Staff. • Medical aids. • Suppliers and representatives. • Key role players in the community. • Other healthcare professionals. • Ensure you are present in the clinic when on duty, in accordance with contracted hours. • Build and maintain healthy working relationships with Store and Dispensary Management. • Be visible around the store and assist customers when not attending to patients. General Responsibilities • Adhere to the clinic’s uniform policy while on duty. • Adhere to patient schedules in accordance with standard operating procedures. • Adhere to Dis-Chem customer service policies and procedures. • Adhere to Dis-Chem policies and Standard Operating Procedures. • Adhere to Health and Safety rules and regulations. Competencies Knowledge • Community health nursing. • Primary health care and/or occupational health nursing. • Family planning. • Cytology. • IUD. • Pharmacology. • EPI / Cold Chain Management. • Sexually transmitted diseases. • Emergency care. • HIV/AIDS pre- and post-counselling. • Breastfeeding. • Pathology. • Wound care. • Good knowledge and understanding of standard operating procedures. Cognitive Skills • Ability to understand monthly clinic statistics and commission. Business Acumen • Ability to balance clinical expertise with the delivery of high-quality patient care. Communication Skills • Strong command of English – read, write, speak and understand. Interpersonal Skills • High emotional intelligence. • Entrepreneurial flair. • Trustworthy and honest. Quality Orientation • Commitment to high-quality patient care, safety and patient satisfaction. Self-Management Skills • Focuses time and effort on key tasks. • Groups related tasks to work more efficiently. • Easily transitions between tasks and picks up where left off when interrupted. Technical Skills • Computer skills, including Microsoft Office: • Outlook. • Excel. • Word. Special Conditions of Employment • Must be able to assist at other stores, in addition to the base store, when necessary and according to business needs. • Physically fit and healthy to manage a Primary Health Care Clinic within a Dis-Chem store. • South African citizen. • MIE, no criminal record and clear credit rating. • Valid driver’s licence and own reliable transport. Remuneration & Benefits • Market-related salary. • Medical aid. • Provident fund. • Staff account. Important Information ONLY SUCCESSFUL APPLICANTS WILL BE CONTACTED.
