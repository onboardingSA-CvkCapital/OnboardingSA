About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring 2 Medical Officers Grade 1, 2 or 3 posts in Anaesthetics, based in Dr Pixley Ka Isaka Seme Memorial Hospital. This is a permanent post, paying R1,041,402 – R1,121,241 a year. Responsibilities

  • Provide safe, high quality clinical care for patients during pre-operative assessments, intra-operative regional and general anaesthesia administration and post- operative care including pain management.
  • Manage emergency resuscitations, urgent medical stabilisation and intensive care needs.
  • Ability to identify the need for and refer appropriately.
  • Work with other clinical care units.
  • Ability to manage patients in a holistic manner.
  • Give medical judicial evidence.
  • Maintain medical records.
  • Provide training to the members of the multidisciplinary team in theatre.
  • Participate in continued professional development and teaching programmes.
  • Participate in departmental audits and all other quality improvement initiatives.
  • Knowledge of the clinical governance framework and the legislated norms and standards.
  • The successful applicant will be required to perform after hour duties.

Requirements

  • Appropriate qualification plus registration with HPCSA as Medical Practitioner.
  • No experience required after completion of Community Service.
  • Senior Certificate/Grade 12 or equivalent.
  • An appropriate tertiary qualification (MBCHB) plus Registration certificate with HPCSA as a Medical Practitioner.
  • Current registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as a Medical Practitioner after completion of community Services 2025/2026.
  • Persons in possession of a foreign qualification must attach an evaluation certificate from the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA).
  • The employment equity target for this post is an African Male.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Training and Competence Required:
  • Good knowledge of Anaesthetic procedures and latest protocols
  • Knowledge and appropriate use of anaesthetic equipment Experience and ability to deal with a wide variety of Anaesthetic cases
  • Ability to work in a multi-disciplinary team setting
  • Good interpersonal and supervisory skills
  • Provide training and guidance to members of the multi-disciplinary team
  • Ability to diagnose and manage common anaesthetic problems including emergencies
  • Sound moral values based on integrity, trust and judgement
  • Sound knowledge of the relevant legislative mandates, policies and prescripts governing the medical profession and public health in South Africa.
  • At least 6 months’ experience in Anaesthetics would be a recommendation.
  • The Diploma in Anaesthesia (DA) from the College of Medicine of South Africa (CMSA) or progress toward primary exams (FCA PART 1) will be an added advantage.
  • Other valid additional certificates such as BLS, ACLS, and ATLS will be an added advantage.
