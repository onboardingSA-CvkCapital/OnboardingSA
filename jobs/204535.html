About the role

Lead. Inspire. Grow. RubiBlue, one of Raizcorp's partners, is looking for an experienced Sales Manager who thrives on building high-performing sales teams, driving revenue growth, and winning new business. This is an opportunity for a commercially driven leader to take ownership of our sales function, develop and coach a talented team, and play a key role in expanding RubiBlue's footprint across South Africa and Africa. If you're passionate about people leadership, achieving ambitious targets, and building long-term client relationships, we'd love to hear from you. Why join RubiBlue? RubiBlue is a leading South African software development and support services company with over 20 years of experience delivering innovative technology solutions to businesses. Our success is built on our people. We foster a collaborative, energetic environment where innovation, accountability, wellbeing, and continuous learning are part of our everyday culture.
