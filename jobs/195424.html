About the role

We are an innovative, consumer facing fintech specialising in providing fast loans, affordable insurance products and value-added services to our rapidly growing customer base. We are obsessed with customer experience, innovation and building great tech in our pursuit of delivering on our vision to be our customers’ favourite digital financial services provider. Our team consists of passionate individuals that work in multi-disciplinary teams, where our strength in execution lies in our teamwork. Collectively we have driven significant customer growth and profitability over the past 15 years. We focus on the detail, being hands-on and executing against our plans. As part of our continued growth, we are seeking a Talent Acquisition Specialist to join our People Team. This role is central to building our talent pipeline and ensuring we attract, select, and retain the best people across all areas of the business from operations and customer experience to product and technology. If you’re looking for a place where you will have a positive impact on lives, learn more about and contribute to the Fintech industry, and be involved in multiple products, projects, and technologies, then look no further than FinChoice. The Role: Talent Acquisition Specialist The team: The People team forms an integral part of the FinChoice business, responsible for the people strategy. Our aim is to attract and retain top talent, that is nurtured in an inclusive, empathetic culture that rewards and recognizes high performance whilst promoting well-being to ensure sustainability for our people and the business. Location: Wynberg, Cape Town Role Purpose The Talent Acquisition Specialist is responsible for managing the full recruitment lifecycle from workforce planning and sourcing to selection, offer management, and onboarding. This role partners closely with leaders across all departments to design and deliver effective hiring strategies that support the company’s growth objectives and culture. The ideal candidate will have strong experience in talent acquisition within a fast-paced or high-growth environment, with exposure to tech, product, and digital roles.
