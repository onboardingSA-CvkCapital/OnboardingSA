About the role

Our client, a well-established logistics, clearing and forwarding company, is seeking a suitable candidate for a Customs Entry Clerk to join their team in Observatory, Cape Town. Duties • Check import/export controller registrations – prompt them to fix incorrect information or get information that is missing. • Ensure that bills of entry where documents and registration have been captured correctly are processed within the team’s turnaround time. • Maintain a list of files that have been sent for entry and reasons why the bill of entry has not been processed. • Remind controllers about what is required before their bill of entry can be processed. • Send out clearing instruction information to controllers and finance team. • Follow instruction of controller as read with permissions from finance team to make submissions. • Make sure that no submission goes in prior to it being checked and approved, and before the finance team gives permission. • Notify controllers when entries are ready for cash payment or when they are on query. • Do escalations as necessary. • Maintain a client parts/HS codes library. • Do VOC’s & Provisional payments as requested / Keep record/make sure of liquidations of provisional payments. • Prompt controllers as needed for information so that provisional payments can be liquidated. • Rebate permits: Maintain a rebate permit usage record. Ensure that limits are not exceeded. Communicate with clients to make sure that they are also maintaining a rebate permit usage record. • Import permits: Maintain a list, ensure that clients are aware of limits and cut off dates. • Take note of tariff updates / each time an update to a tariff comes through. Take a report from Shipshape and compare the report to tariff update. Notify team leader/notify clients (if required by team leader) of imminent tariff/rate of duty/regulatory changes. • Keep a record of the latest customs contact details including our CRMs details. • Keep a file of updated SARS/OGA regulations.
