About the role

Job Purpose To lead and manage the cross-functional Aftersales division, formulating and positioning the aftersales operating model and strategy in order to ensure the efficient, profitable and compliant operation thereof.  To provide direction and coordination within the different operational portfolios to achieve the agreed business objectives in line with manufacturer standards and targets as well as those of Motus. Key Performance Areas The Executive: Aftersales manages cross-functional teams across the following portfolios: • Aftersales Training – Technical & Non-TechnicalDelivery of the technical and non-technical training strategy. • Develop and maintain technical (workshop/diagnostic) and non-technical (service advisor, parts, customer-facing) training curricula aligned to OEM/manufacturer standards. • Manage the network training academy/programme, training budget and accreditation of dealer technicians and staff. • Track dealer training compliance and competency levels, and close identified skills gaps. • Aftersales Marketing & Customer LoyaltyOwn the aftersales marketing strategy and customer retention/loyalty programmes. • Develop and execute service marketing campaigns, promotions and loyalty programmes to drive workshop retention and repeat business. • Manage the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) programme and associated budget. • Monitor competitor activity and market trends to keep the aftersales value proposition relevant. • Dealer Aftersales & Commercial Business DevelopmentGrow the commercial performance of the dealer network's aftersales operations. • Develop and implement network aftersales standards and methods (facilities, systems, processes) and monitor dealer compliance. • Identify and drive business development opportunities that grow aftersales turnover, market share and dealer profitability. • Build and maintain strong commercial relationships with the dealer network on aftersales matters. • Oversee dealer use of Dealer Management System (DMS) and related IT tools for service booking, parts ordering and CRM to improve dealer effectiveness. • Manage Roadside Assistance, Service/Maintenance Plan and Extended Warranty product offerings. • Parts & Accessory Demand Planning and OrderingEnsure the right parts and accessories are available at the right time, at the right value. • Manage demand planning, forecasting and ordering of parts and accessories to meet dealer network service level requirements. • Maintain stock holding within budgeted/approved values, balancing availability against working capital targets. • Monitor fill rates, obsolescence and slow-moving stock, and drive corrective action. • Inbound & Outbound LogisticsEnsure supply chain performance meets agreed service levels. • Manage inbound logistics (import/supply from manufacturer or supplier) and outbound distribution to the dealer network within Service Level Agreement (SLA) parameters. • Monitor logistics costs, transit times and delivery performance, and drive continuous improvement with logistics partners. • Ensure business continuity and risk management across the supply chain. • Technical Support to the Dealer NetworkProvide escalation-level technical support for complex diagnosis and repair. • Provide (via the technical support team) expert assistance to dealers on difficult diagnostics, repairs and technical queries. • Manage the technical helpline/expertise function and liaise with the manufacturer on technical bulletins and campaigns. • Ensure timeous resolution of escalated technical cases to minimise vehicle downtime and customer inconvenience. • Where applicable, oversee body shop/accident repair technical standards and the use of genuine parts. • Warranty ManagementGovern warranty spend and compliance across the network. • Monitor, review and approve dealer warranty claims in line with manufacturer policy and delegated authority levels. • Manage the warranty expense budget and investigate abnormal trends, fraud risk or excessive claims. • Ensure warranty administration and reporting meets manufacturer and statutory requirements. • Financial Management of the DivisionEnsure the division operates within approved financial parameters. • Compile, manage and report against the aftersales division budget (training, marketing, warranty, customer care, parts and logistics). • Ensure all financial functions (management accounts, forecasts, cost control, variance analysis) are completed accurately and on time. • Identify and implement cost-saving and profitability-improvement initiatives across the division. • Warehouse ManagementEnsure the parts warehouse operates to the required standards. • Ensure warehouse operations (receiving, binning, picking and dispatch of parts) are executed within prescribed requirements. • Maintain inventory accuracy, security and housekeeping standards in line with company policy. • Drive warehouse efficiency, productivity and service-rate targets to the dealer network. • Customer Care ManagementEnsure customer queries and complaints are resolved effectively. • Oversee the customer care department's management of customer queries, complaints and escalations in conjunction with the dealer network. • Ensure complaint resolution turnaround times and quality meet company and manufacturer standards. • Use customer care insights to drive process and product/service improvements. • Strategic Planning & Board ReportingProvide strategic direction and feedback on the aftersales business to the company board. • Develop the short-, medium- and long-term aftersales strategy in support of overall business growth objectives. • Prepare and present strategic plans, performance feedback and business cases to the company board and executive committee. • Represent the aftersales division in engagements with the manufacturer/OEM corporate office. • Voice of Customer (VoC) ExperienceOwn the achievement of Voice of Customer experience targets. • Monitor customer satisfaction and experience metrics (e.g. CSI/VoC surveys, benchmark studies) across sales and aftersales touchpoints. • Drive action plans with the dealer network and internal departments to close gaps against VoC targets. • Report VoC performance trends and improvement initiatives to executive management and the manufacturer. • Health & Safety ManagementHold overarching health and safety accountability across all departments in the division. • Ensure all departments (warehouse, training facility, offices) comply with applicable health and safety legislation and company policy. • Promote a safety culture, ensure risk assessments, incident reporting and corrective actions are managed effectively.Organisational Structure and Direct Reports The Executive: Aftersales retains overall strategic accountability, financial ownership and board reporting for all portfolios, while day-to-day management of each functional area is delegated to a dedicated direct report. This keeps a single point of accountability for aftersales strategy while ensuring each specialist domain has focused, hands-on management.
