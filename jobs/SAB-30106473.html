About the role

SAB is hiring a Premium Beer PPM, based in Bryanston. Leads the integrated planning, portfolio and performance-management agenda for Premium Beer. The role connects strategy, brand plans, commercial priorities, investment and execution, giving leadership a clear view of performance, risks, trade-offs and decisions. Functional teams retain delivery ownership; this role integrates, challenges and tracks the plan across functions.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the development and integration of the Premium Beer long-term strategy, three-year plan and annual one-year plan.
  • Translate the growth ambition into clear strategic choices, portfolio priorities, initiatives, milestones and measures.
  • Coordinate brand, channel, customer, commercial and functional plans into one integrated Premium Beer plan.
  • Maintain the consolidated portfolio of growth initiatives, with clear business cases, owners, milestones and success measures.
  • Support leadership to prioritise initiatives based on growth potential, strategic importance, investment, risk and organisational capacity.
  • Identify overlaps, dependencies and resource conflicts, recommending where initiatives should be accelerated, corrected, deprioritised or stopped.
  • Lead monthly performance reviews across volume, share, brand power, net revenue, MACO, investment and execution measures.
  • Identify gaps against plan, diagnose causes and coordinate corrective actions with the accountable teams.
  • Provide forward-looking visibility of risks, opportunities, trade-offs and decisions required, not only historical reporting.
  • Establish fit-for-purpose governance for priority initiatives, with clear accountabilities, milestones and decision points.
  • Maintain an integrated leadership action and decision log
  • monitor critical-path delivery and escalate unresolved issues early.
  • Partner with Finance and budget owners to track actual and committed investment against approved plans.
  • Support investment trade-offs across brands, channels and activities, linking spend to strategy and expected outcomes.
  • Act as the central integration point across functions and facilitate decision-oriented leadership reviews.
  • Build strong BU South, Zone and Global relationships where relevant, and prepare concise senior-leadership updates.
  • Develop ad hoc tactical presentations and data analysis based on Growth Company Director briefings, translating the brief into clear insights, recommendations and decision-ready materials for senior leadership.

Requirements

  • Relevant degree in Business, Commerce, Marketing, Finance, Engineering or a related field
  • postgraduate qualification advantageous
  • Strong commercial and financial acumen across budgets, business cases, performance measures and investment trade-offs
  • Proven ability to lead cross-functional programmes and deliver through influence
  • Strong analytical capability, converting complex information into decisions and actions
  • Strong senior-leadership communication and presentation skills
  • Advanced PowerPoint and Excel capability
  • Power BI experience advantageous
  • Understanding of consumers, brands, channels and commercial execution
  • Practical, collaborative and ownership-oriented, able to manage competing priorities
