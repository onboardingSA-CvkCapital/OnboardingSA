About the role

SAB is hiring a Commercial Data Specialist, based in Bryanston. The key purpose of this role is to connect, harmonise and quality-control commercial data across multiple source systems, ensuring that trusted and business-ready information is available for analytics and decision-making. The role acts as the bridge between data owners, Commercial stakeholders, Master Data and Data Technology teams to resolve issues and continuously improve data usability.

Responsibilities

  • Data Quality, Harmonisation & Automation
  • Collaborate with commercial stakeholders on the data requirements, needs and pain points.
  • Collaborate with technical stakeholders ensuring governance standards are followed ensuring long term solution quality.
  • Connect and reconcile data from multiple commercial sources to create a consistent view of key measures and dimensions.
  • Perform data quality checks, root-cause analysis and issue tracking across completeness, accuracy, consistency and timeliness.
  • Harmonise definitions, mappings and business rules in partnership with source owners and Master Data.
  • Automate repeatable quality-control, reconciliation and maintenance activities using SQL and appropriate tools.
  • Data Enablement & Stakeholder Coordination
  • Act as the messenger and coordination point between Commercial teams, source owners, Master Data and Data Technology to resolve and advance data issues.
  • Maintain data documentation, definitions, controls, issue logs and change records.
  • Provide business requirements and quality criteria for technical teams and contractors delivering datasets, semantic models or Power BI assets.
  • Apply a basic understanding of data pipelines, Databricks, engineering and architecture to guide delivery without owning pipeline development.
  • Key Attributes and Competencies
  • Demonstrated ability to reconcile data, identify quality issues and translate findings into clear remediation actions.
  • Good commercial and business knowledge, with the ability to understand how source data supports decisions and processes.
  • Strong stakeholder coordination, documentation and problem-solving skills.
  • Working knowledge of data engineering concepts, architecture, ETL/ELT and data pipelines.
  • Experience guiding the development of semantic models, Power BI datasets or business-ready data products.
  • Experience with automation, data cataloguing, master data or data governance processes.
  • Exposure to FMCG commercial data, customer hierarchies, product masters or route-to-market datasets.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Data Engineering, Computer Science, Business Analytics or a related field.
  • Minimum 2 years’ relevant experience in commercial data, analytics engineering, data quality, business intelligence or a similar field.
  • Strong SQL skills and practical experience working with Databricks or comparable modern data platforms.
