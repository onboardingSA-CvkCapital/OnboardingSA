About the role

Area of specialization: To provide a comprehensive occupational health and primary health service according to Eskom`s health policies, procedures and other regularity requirements including the Occupational Health and Safety Act, No 85 of 1993 Position/Task Grade: P14 Department: Health and Wellness Business Unit: Human Resources Location: Bellville (Western Cape)

Responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Implement an occupational health surveillance programme in the business unit (BU)/division/health centre according to occupational risk exposure profile (OREP).
  • Prepare and coordinate cases for proposed medical boarding, incapacity, sick leave management, disability and assessments in consultation with the Eskom occupational health medicine practitioner(OHMP).
  • Monitor and refer acute and chronic cases to the relevant health and wellness function inside or outside Eskom.
  • Provide an advisory, consultative and specialist service to all stakeholders.
  • Capture and record data of all activities on Systems, Applications and Products (SAP) Environmental, Health and Safety (EHS).
