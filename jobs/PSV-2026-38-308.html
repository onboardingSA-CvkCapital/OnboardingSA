About the role

The Department of Co-Operative Governance and Traditional Affairs (Gauteng) is hiring a Deputy Director: Capacity Development in Capacity Development, based in Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Applications should be submitted strictly online at https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za/Public/Jobs.aspx For attention: Human Capital management Reference number to quote: REFS/057051 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor and ensure support in the implementation of the scarce and critical skills strategy: Provide guidance and advice with regards to the implementation of the scarce and critical skills strategy through municipal engagements.
  • Ensure the coordination and facilitation of meetings with municipalities with regards to the scarce and critical skills strategy.
  • Manage the relationship between Local Government (LG) SETA and the Municipalities.
  • Represent both COGTA and municipalities at LG SETA engagements.
  • Manage the relationship between key stakeholders and Municipalities.
  • Represent Gauteng CoGTA at DCOG (national COGTA) meetings, and report on capacity building initiatives in the province.
  • Manage the updating of monthly reports timeously.
  • Ensure the provision of quarterly reports timeously.
  • Quality assure and ensure the provision of quarterly reports timeously.
  • Provide technical and hands on support to the training and learning development units in municipalities: Monitor and ensure the compilation of the Workplace Skills Plan and Annual Report by all 11 Municipalities as well as the skills audit process.
  • Develop Skills Audit Template and process flow.
  • Provide guidance and support on implementation of Skills Audit process.
  • Ensure the functioning of Workplace Skills Committees in Municipalities.
  • Direct and facilitate capacity building programs through coordination, collaboration, leveraging and consolidation of various sector initiatives and strategic established stakeholders.
  • Share knowledge and best practice with regards to capacity building.
  • Manage and facilitate various engagements including stakeholder engagements, joint HR forums.
  • Facilitate and manage capacity building programs for municipalities (Soft Skills): Ensure the development of materials for selected soft skills training.
  • Manage and coordinate trainings at various municipalities.
  • Facilitate the identified trainings.
  • Manage the update monthly reports timeously.
  • Perform Deployment functions at identified municipalities:
  • Manage and provide support at identified municipalities in respect to Pillar 5: Building Institutional Capabilities namely
  • Monitor senior vacancies
  • Monitor critical vacancies and scarce vacancies
  • Report on training that is facilitated by COGTA
  • Follow-up and action the recommendations made by South African Board of People Practice (SABPP)
  • Lead the pillar in meetings with both the identified municipalities and the CoGTA team
  • Manage and update all progress reports
  • Escalate all challenges to both CoGTA and identified municipalities
  • Management of Sub Directorate:
  • Management of day-to-day operations
  • Plan and delegate work and monitor progress
  • Manage weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings
  • Manage the skills development plan of the Sub Directorate
  • Manage the PMDS of the Sub Directorate
  • Manage the audit finding and queries of the Sub Directorate
  • Facilitate the review and planning sessions and the submission of the strategic, operational plans, reports of the programme

Requirements

  • Matric plus NQF Level 7 in Human Resource Development/Human Resource Management/Industrial Psychology or relevant qualification as recognised by SAQA.
  • Coupled with 3-5 years of experience at a supervisory (ASD) level in Human Resource Development/Capacity Building environment.
  • A Skills Development Facilitator and Education Training and Development Practitioner Certificates will be an added advantage.
  • Competencies:
  • Strategic Thinking
  • Stakeholder management
  • Conflict Management
  • Communication
  • Project Management
  • MS office
  • Report Writing
  • Budget Management
  • Leadership
  • Capability and strategic thinking
  • Interpersonal skills
  • Supervisory Skills
  • Planning and Organizing
  • Risk Management and Problem Solving
  • A valid driver’s licence
