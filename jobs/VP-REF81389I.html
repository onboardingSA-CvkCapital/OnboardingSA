About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring an Operational Risk Advisor, based in Mokopane, Limpopo. To coordinate and support the effective implementation and execution of the operational risk management (ORM) process across mining and process operations, and to assess, consolidate and report on ORM performance, actions and emerging risk concerns. Oversee the effective application of the four ORM layers at the operation: Baseline risk management Provide practical, risk-based advisory support to integrate ORM into operational practices and programmes, including: The Operating Model This role is at a band Level 6 and reporting to the Section SHE Manager Sandsloot Project.

Responsibilities

  • Support the planning, coordination and implementation of ORM, with specific focus on baseline, issue-based, task-based and continuous risk management activities.
  • Oversee the effective application of the four ORM layers at the operation:Baseline risk management
  • Issue-based risk management
  • Task-based risk management
  • Continuous risk management
  • Coordinate ORM execution in line with the integrated risk management framework, processes, systems, standards and procedures.
  • Support the implementation, adoption and sustainable use of the ORM digital platform.
  • Promote a purpose-driven, inclusive and high-performing culture that supports engagement, learning and continuous improvement.
  • Ensure ORM activities comply with applicable internal requirements and external statutory, regulatory, legislative and permitting obligations.
  • Assist in developing and delivering the annual ORM program, aligned with operational targets and key result areas.
  • Conduct monthly assessments of operational risk performance and prepare clear status reports for Senior Project Managers, the Senior Leadership Team (SLT) and operational risk role-holders.
  • Support action planning, escalation and follow-up to address deviations, overdue actions and emerging material risk concerns.
  • Provide practical, risk-based advisory support to integrate ORM into operational practices and programmes, including:The Operating Model
  • Learning and incident investigation
  • Assurance activities
  • Critical control verification
  • Build effective relationships and influence stakeholders across operational, technical, contractor and leadership levels.
  • Apply recognized risk assessment techniques, including WRAC, Bow-Tie Analysis, HAZOP/HAZAN and other fit-for-purpose methodologies.

Requirements

  • Environment (SHE), or a related field.
  • A valid EB/Code 08 driver’s license.
  • Advantageous
  • Mining Engineering degree.
  • Training in ISO management systems, including ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 (or legacy OHSAS 18001).
  • Auditing and/or risk management qualifications.
  • ORM-specific training.
  • Health and Safety Management training.
  • Experience, Knowledge and skills
  • Minimum five years’ relevant experience in mining operations, with a strong understanding of mining processes and operational challenges.
  • Minimum three years of mechanized underground mining experience.
  • Demonstrated ability to translate ORM principles into practical applications in mining, processing and engineering contexts.
  • Experience in risk management within a technical, operational or assurance environment.
  • Working knowledge and application of Bow-Tie Analysis, HAZOP/HAZAN, WRAC and other industry-recognized risk assessment methodologies.
  • Experience using, SHE and IRM digital platforms and coordinating critical control verification and assurance activities.
  • Proven ability to influence, collaborate and coordinate across all organizational levels.
  • Sound understanding of the PGM value chain, operational risk trends and SHE performance drivers.
