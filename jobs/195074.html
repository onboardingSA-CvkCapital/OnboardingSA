About the role

🚀 Senior Product Manager (Payments | Card Acquiring) 📍 Linbro Park, Johannesburg We are looking for an experienced, commercially driven Senior Product Manager to lead the strategy, development, and optimisation of innovative payment solutions. This role is ideal for someone passionate about shaping the future of payments, with deep expertise in card acquiring. 🔍 About the Role As Senior Product Manager, you will take ownership of the payments product portfolio—driving strategic direction, innovation, and commercial success. You’ll work cross-functionally to deliver scalable, customer-centric solutions that enable growth and enhance user experience. ✅ Key Responsibilities • Define and execute the payments product roadmap and strategy • Identify market trends, customer needs, and new opportunities within the payments space • Lead end-to-end product lifecycle management (ideation to commercialisation) • Drive product innovation, launches, and ecosystem growth • Translate business needs into commercially viable payment solutions • Collaborate with internal teams (tech, operations, sales) to ensure successful delivery • Oversee product performance, testing, optimisation, and reporting • Lead and mentor product team members🎯 Key Requirements • Bachelor’s degree in Business, IT, or related field (postgraduate advantageous) • Minimum 8 years’ experience in product, innovation, or strategy within fintech, banking, or payments • Extensive and proven experience in card acquiring (ESSENTIAL), including: • Card-present (POS) and card-not-present (e-commerce) environments • Acquiring transaction processing and payment flows • Strong understanding of the payments ecosystem and industry trends • Demonstrated success delivering commercially impactful solutions • Experience with product lifecycle management, go-to-market strategy, and revenue optimisation • Familiarity with Agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban, JIRA)💡 What We’re Looking For • Strategic thinker with a strong customer focus • Strong commercial acumen and results-driven mindset • Exceptional stakeholder management and communication skills • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, high-performance environment • Strong analytical and problem-solving capability🌟 Why Join? • Competitive salary and benefits • Innovative, collaborative environment • Opportunity to shape leading-edge payment solutions • Career growth and professional development
