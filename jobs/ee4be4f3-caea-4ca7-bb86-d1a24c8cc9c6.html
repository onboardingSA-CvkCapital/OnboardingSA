About the role

Admin Clerk / Driver Co-ordinator (Midrand / Fourways Area) Are you an organized, detail-oriented individual with strong coordination skills? A well-established business is looking for a dependable Admin Clerk / Driver Co-ordinator to join their dynamic team for a full-time position. If you take pride in accurate administration, possess strong communication skills, and excel at managing delivery logistics in a fast-paced environment, this is your opportunity to ensure smooth daily operations. What’s on Offer? - Salary: R7,845.00 Gross PM - Status: Permanent following a successful 3-month probation period - Benefits (Post-Probation): MICFA Provident Fund (7.5% employee / 8% employer contribution) + Prorated December Annual Bonus - Leave: 18 days paid annual leave per annum - Hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 | Sat 08:00–13:00 (Overtime paid per MIBCO regulations) Key Responsibilities - Store Administration: Manage day-to-day administrative duties, maintain organized records, handle filing, and process store paperwork. - Delivery Co-ordination: Plan, schedule, and assign daily delivery routes to ensure timely customer dispatches. - Driver Tracking & Dispatch: Brief drivers daily, track delivery progress, and resolve on-the-road queries or delays. - Documentation & PODs: Verify delivery notes and invoices, ensuring all Proof of Deliveries (PODs) are collected, checked, and filed accurately. - Order Verification: Assist with cross-checking outgoing orders against delivery sheets to maintain complete dispatch accuracy. Are You the Perfect Fit? Minimum Requirements: - Essential Experience: Previous experience in store/office administration combined with driver coordination, dispatch, or logistics is strictly required. - Skills & Education: Matric certificate (Grade 12), computer literacy (MS Office), strong numerical accuracy, and good administrative capabilities. - Location: Must reside within or close to the Midrand / Fourways area. - Track Record: High reliability, punctuality, strong problem-solving skills, and a stable work history with contactable references.
