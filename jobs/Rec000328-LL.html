About the role

We are recruiting on behalf of a leading designer and manufacturer of mineral processing equipment with more than 65 years of supply into the global mining industry. Operating from a 22,000 m² manufacturing facility, the business builds world class processing plants for clients across Africa and internationally. We are looking for a hands on Production Foreman to lead the Material Preparation and Machine Shop operations. Reporting to the Production Manager, you will make sure production commitments are met safely, on time, within budget and to the highest quality standards.
