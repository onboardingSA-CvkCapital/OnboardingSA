About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring 6 Project Officer: Labour Tenants posts in Tenure Reform Implementation, based in Mpumalanga (Gert Sibande). This is a contract post, paying R338,106 a year. (12 Month Contract) Directorate: Tenure Reform Implementation How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 17 Van Rensburg Street, 7th Floor Bateleur Building, Mbombela, 1200 or by email to Post396@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/396 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian and White Males, and Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and issue notices of labour tenants’ applications to affected landowners.
  • Trace/locate applicants, property and conduct an in loco inspection.
  • Conduct research on the claim/application and compile land rights enquiry report.
  • Ensure that farm owners details are located and updated.
  • Engage Deeds and Surveyor General offices to get property details.
  • Engage National Geospatial Information (NGI) to get Aerial photographs of the property.
  • Prepare section (17)(2) notices for the acquisition of land and servitudes are served to the landowners and or person in charge.
  • Prepare gazette notice in terms of section 17(2)(c) of the Land Reform (Labour Tenants) Act, 3 of 1996.
  • Process labour tenants’ applications (receive and respond to responses from affected landowners and other stakeholders).
  • Prepare the project at Provincial Projects Approval Committee (PPAC) meeting.
  • Include the project at Demand Management Plan (DMP) and Procurement Plan (PP).
  • Ensure registration of project on Basic Accounting System (BAS).
  • Conducting land rights enquiries.
  • Compile Terms of Reference (TOR's) and memorandums for subdivision, valuation and conveyancing.
  • Compile submissions of projects for approval by approving structures.
  • Provide monthly progress report on each applications allocated.
  • Upload and update projects on Result Management Office (RMO) system.
  • Respond to enquiries from Walk in clients, landowners and other stakeholders.
  • Administer information for referral where there are no agreements.
  • Filling of documents in registry according to Records Management Plan of the Department.
  • Negotiate the settlement of labour tenant applicants.
  • Arrange meetings with the landowners or people in charge to discussion of the resolutions of claims through agreement in terms of section 18 of the Act.
  • Prepare offers for approval and send them to landowners.
  • Facilitate the settlement agreement.
  • Settle labour tenants’ applications.
  • Prepare the submission for land acquisition, donation, and certification.
  • Prepare submissions for concurrence approval by Treasury for disposal project.
  • Ensure compliance towards the Deed of donation to avoid any deviation from original settlement agreement for donation project.
  • Ensure that the applications signs waiver rights agreement for certification.
  • Ensure that all relevant documents are submitted to the Land Rights Officer (LRO) for referrals.
  • Ensure submission of all relevant documents to enable the transfer of the project.
  • Facilitate establishment and registration of legal entities if any.
  • Facilitate the implementation of post settlement support development if any.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements
  • A Grade 12 Certificate and a National Diploma in the fields of Law/Social Science/Development Studies (NQF Level 6) or related equivalent qualifications
  • At least 1 year experience in the relevant field
  • A valid driver’s licence
  • Job related knowledge
  • Knowledge of land reform policy, land law and other relevant acts and legislative prescript
  • Contract Management
  • Knowledge of the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA)
  • Understanding of all land reform programmes
  • Civil and commercial law and its application
  • Job related skills
  • Project management
  • Negotiation
  • Leadership
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Computer literacy
  • Ability to draft terms of reference for service providers
  • Ability to manage consultants
  • Willingness to travel, to spend extended periods in the field and work irregular hour
  • Willingness to relocate if require to do so as a result of operational requirements of the employer
