About the role

Capitec is hiring an Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Manager, based in Stellenbosch. Our Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer sets the financial crime strategy and this role leads the policy and advisory team that delivers it. As we grow across Personal Banking, Business Banking and insurance and serve more than 15 million clients on our banking app alone, the complexity of our financial crime obligations grows with us. The role requires technical expertise in anti-money laundering law and the ability to turn that legislation into clear, workable policy. You'll lead our AML Policy and Advisory team within the Compliance function and report directly to the Head: Compliance. You'll provide assurance to regulators and senior committees on a function whose work touches every part of our business. You'll maintain our AML governance framework, including the Group AML Committee and the Financial Crime Risk Forum, and build and manage our financial crime training framework.

Responsibilities

  • Keep our Risk Management and Compliance Programme (RMCP) current and accurate, ensuring compliance with South Africa’s money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing legislation.
  • Advise the business, Executive Committee and board committees on the design, content and application of the RMCP, including compliance implications of new products, channels, delivery models and third-party arrangements before launch.
  • Design and maintain AML policies, standards and procedures aligned with the Financial Intelligence Centre Act, Regulations and guidance, covering client due diligence, enhanced and ongoing due diligence, transaction monitoring, and suspicious and unusual activity reporting.
  • Act as the principal point of contact with regulators and industry bodies, responding to regulatory enquiries and coordinating inspections and audits.
  • Lead the policy and advisory team by setting clear team and individual goals, regularly reviewing performance and providing feedback.
  • Support the Head: Compliance with reporting to senior governance structures, including the Audit Committee and Risk and Capital Management Committee, and prepare reporting for external bodies.

Requirements

  • 12+ years’ legal/regulatory compliance experience, preferably in banking, financial services or a similarly regulated industry.
  • Experience developing and maintaining an AML compliance programme, including board approval and regulatory submission.
  • Proven ability to translate FICA, Regulations, Directives and Guidance Notes into implemented policies and procedures.
  • Experience with Prudential Authority/FIC inspections, remediation and regulatory correspondence.
  • Experience advising on pre-launch financial crime risks, including third-party payments and money/value transfers.
  • 7+ years’ experience leading specialist teams.
  • Expert knowledge of FICA, AML/CFT requirements, Prudential Authority expectations and FATF Recommendations.
  • Knowledge of targeted financial sanctions and proliferation financing requirements, including FICA sections 26A-26C and 28A, UN Security Council regimes and terrorist property reporting.Familiarity with goAML registration and reporting
  • Strong analytical, legal interpretation, influencing and stakeholder-management skills.
  • Relevant commerce, finance or law qualification
  • Honours or higher preferred.
  • CAMS or equivalent AML/financial crime qualification.

  • Clear criminal and credit record
