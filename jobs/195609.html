About the role

FMS Sales and Leasing, a proud part of the prestigious Faircape Group with nearly 40 years of experience in the Western Cape, is on the lookout for a driven and ambitious Property Sales and Rental Agent to take our portfolio to the next level! 👉 This role offers a competitive basic salary + commission – giving you both security and excellent earning potential right from the start. This is not just any property role – you’ll be stepping into a position where you can make an immediate impact, working with our established portfolio across the Atlantic Seaboard area. Instead of being limited to a set territory, you’ll focus on high-value buildings where we already have a strong presence, giving you the perfect platform to grow your client base, nurture existing leads, secure new mandates, and drive outstanding rental and sales results.
