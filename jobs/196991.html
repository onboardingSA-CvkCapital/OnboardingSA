About the role

Introduction This position will be based in the Northern Cape Region. Description The purpose of this position is to plan, direct and manage all operational activities within the Minopex area of responsibility to ensure adherence to contractual requirements, provide value-add to the Client and continuously improve the service delivery according to organisational objectives. Duties and Responsibilities • Ensure effective client relationship management and compliance within contractual limits are prioritized and maintained. • Interpret site objectives and needs across the business value chain to ensure adherence to organizational objectives • Ensure the implementation and adherence to contractual requirements • Providing leadership to ensure adherence to Health, Safety and Environmental standards that exceed industry best practice and any applicable legislative / statutory requirements and all Company systems, policies and procedures • Ensure the maintenance of Process Standards, Procedures and COP’s is complied with and improved where necessary. • Manage the implementation and adherence to ISO and other quality related systems • Ensure operations and maintenance management according to quality and quantity specifications, ensuring the implementation of the maintenance plan / program • Monitor maintenance efficiencies to ensure the attainment of organisational objectives • Provide technical direction and support to the operations team • Ensure that agreed targets are achieved in the areas of plant availability, throughput and recovery • Ensure that the plant remains fully optimized • Financial performance, ensuring that process operating, maintenance costs and capital expenditure are in line with the budget • Effective people management and training, ensuring that the team meets their performance objectives • Create a culture of cohesive, high-performance teamwork • Accurate reporting of costs, development and production
