About the role

The National School of Government is hiring a Deputy Director: Education Training & Development (Etd) Trainer Professionalisation, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Apply Using This Link: APPLICATION FOR EMPLOYMENT DD: EDUCATION, TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT TRAIN, REF NNSG: 23/2026. - Fill out form Reference number to quote: NSG 23/2026 Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for the following Key Results Areas: Manage the implementation of the Education, Training and Development (ETD) Practitioner policy and the ETD Practitioner Professionalisation Framework for the NSG.
  • Develop and implement norms and standards for ETD practitioner curriculum and professional development.
  • Ensure that appropriate strategies and learning areas for ETD Practitioner professionalisation are cutting-edge and relevant.
  • Serve in project teams for ETD Practitioner professionalisation interventions.
  • Review and make recommendations for the update of existing learning material to ensure relevance and being current.
  • Develop and improve an integrated ETD Practitioner Performance Management framework aligned to NSG objectives and quality standards.
  • Establish and maintain facilitator performance, competency, service, and measurement standards for internal and contracted practitioners.
  • Facilitate ETD Practitioner professional development interventions.
  • Monitor facilitator performance, covering facilitation effectiveness, professional conduct, curriculum delivery, and learner satisfaction.
  • Implement quality assurance mechanisms to standardise facilitation across programmes and delivery modes.
  • Analyse performance and stakeholder feedback to identify trends, development needs and risks.
  • Manage facilitator performance reporting, dashboards, and compliance with accreditation, professional body, and CPD requirements.
  • Provide coaching, mentoring, and advisory support to improve facilitation, assessment, and moderation practices.
  • Implement practitioner development pathways and capacity-building initiatives aligned to professionalisation requirements.
  • Conduct practitioner performance and capability assessments to identify development needs and gaps.
  • Implement development and remedial interventions to address practitioner performance and capability gaps.
  • Analyse quality findings and monitor corrective actions to improve practitioner performance.
  • Maintain approved facilitation methodologies, tools, and resources to promote consistency and continuous improvement.
  • Establish and maintain collaborative relationships with internal business units and external stakeholders to support ETD practitioner development.
  • Facilitate integrated planning and collaboration across business units to develop and implement ETD practitioner assessment tools and frameworks.
  • Participate in and support strategic partnerships with higher education institutions and other organisations to implement joint initiatives.
  • Represent the organisation in relevant internal and external forums to strengthen collaboration and promote best practices.
  • Identify and leverage partnership opportunities to enhance programme quality, innovation, and institutional impact.
  • Manage the resources (people, finance, systems, assets) allocated within the directorate.
  • Travel extensively and work extended hours when required.

Requirements

  • A recognised Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent (NQF level 7) in the field of Education, Training, Human Resource Development, or a related field.
  • Registration with a relevant professional body.
  • Experience: At least 5 years’ experience, of which (3) years must be at supervisory/managerial level in curriculum development, systems management, instructional design, education, or capacity development or related field.
  • Knowledge In-depth knowledge of and experience in instructional methodologies and facilitation for online, virtual, face-to-face modes of delivery and adult learning principles.
  • Indepth knowledge of the public sector landscape and capacity building needs.
  • Knowledge and understanding of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa and public sector legislation.
  • Knowledge of professional bodies and regulatory body requirements (South African Qualifications Authority, Quality Council for Trades and Occupations, Council for Higher Education).
  • Theoretical and practical knowledge of best practice and cutting-edge ETD methodologies.
  • Batho Pele principles.
  • Proposal and report writing skills.
  • Excellent organising and planning skills, computer literacy in Microsoft Office Suite, and digital skills.
