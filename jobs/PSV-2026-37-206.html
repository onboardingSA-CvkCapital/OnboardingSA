Responsibilities

  • Provide high-quality diagnostic imaging i.e. perform highly specialized paediatric and obstetric radiographic procedures, including neonatal chest X- Rays, mobile/bedside imaging in the NICU/PICU and theatre imaging.
  • Maintain strict radiation protection standards.
  • Participate in quality assurance and equipment maintenance.
  • Participate in image quality audits to eliminate repeat exposures.
  • Promote good health practice and ensure optimal care of patients.
  • To perform reception, clerical duties as required.
  • Ensure the safety of paediatric patients, pregnant mothers accompanying custodians and health care staff.
  • Identify and escalate suspected physical abuse in compliance with Child Protection legislation.
  • Able to use compassionate communication, distraction techniques and appropriate immobilisation strategies to manage uncooperative and anxious minors.
  • Educate parents on the risks and benefits of radiation.
  • Implement infection prevention and health & safety protocols.
  • To comply with and promote Batho Pele principals.
  • Ensure compliance with the Ideal Hospital Framework and OHSC (Office of Health Standards Compliance).
  • Participate in 24/7 shift rosters, standby duties, and after-hours emergency cover.
  • Participate in implementation of departmental and institutional quality improvement plan.

Requirements

  • Matric/Senior Certificate Plus.
  • National Diploma/b Tech/Degree in Diagnostic Radiography.
  • Registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as a Radiographer.
  • Current registration as Radiographer (2026/2027).
  • 01 year experience as an independent practitioner Radiographer.
  • Experience in Paediatrics, Neonatal and Obstetrics’ & Gynaecology imaging or dual qualification in Diagnostic and Ultrasound will be an added advantage.
  • Grade 1: Nil Experience after registration with HPCSA in respect of RSA qualified who have performed community services and the appropriate/recognizable experience in respect of foreign qualified not required to perform community service.
  • Grade 2: at least 10 years appropriate/recognizable experience after registration as a Radiographer with the HPCSA in respect of RSA-qualified who have performed community service, and the appropriate/recognizable experience after registration as a Radiographer with the HPCSA in respect of foreign-qualified not required to perform community service.
  • Grade 3: at least 20 years with appropriate/recognizable experience after registration as a Radiographer with the HPCSA in respect of RSA-qualified who have performed community service, and 21 year appropriate/recognized experience after registration as a Radiographer with the HPCSA in respect of foreign-qualified not required to perform community service.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Training and Competencies Required:
  • In-depth understanding of paediatric anatomy, pathology and radiation risks
  • Ability to operate modern digital radiography systems, computer radiography and mobile units
  • Sound knowledge of radiation control and safety measures
  • Computer literacy
  • Knowledge of relevant health and safety Acts
  • Ability to communicate effectively and problem-solving skills
  • Good interpersonal skills to be able to perform in a multi-disciplinary team setting
  • Ability to perform basic quality assurance tests
  • Ability to work under pressure in a high-volume public health environment
