About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AIpowered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities,and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined,future-fit role. We are looking for passionate, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace datadriven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job The Thuthuka and other Schools projects run by SAICA’s Thuthuka Division focus on three strategic objectives: • To grow the number of CAs(SA), AGAs(SA) and ATs(SA); • To transform the demographics of the profession to match the country’s demographic profile; and • To introduce projects which will benefit society at large. The Thuthuka programmes focus on providing support to African and Coloured learners in overcoming barriers to their success through their studies towards becoming CAs (SA). This support begins at high-school level and continues until qualification. The Schools projects focus on building the CA pipeline. The objectives of this position are: • To implement projects in order to promote the CA, AGA, and AT designations and the accountancy profession amongst learners (career exposure platforms). • To implement projects that promote the importance of mathematics for learners seeking to follow the CA and AGA profession. • To implement career awareness projects that promote the importance of the involvement of parents and other stakeholders in the education of the learners. Viz:- Educators, principals and district Officials • To implement projects in order to promote the CA, AGA, and AT designations, the accountancy profession and key competencies such as ethics and financial literacy amongst learners and students. • To Establish and maintain relationships with related Stakeholders such as the Profession at Large and the Department of Education. • To implement projects in order to promote the CA, AGA, and AT designations, the accountancy profession and key competencies such as ethics amongst trainees and young professionals. • To collaborate with other professions who run Maths initiatives. • SAICA is responsive to all matters relating to transformation inside and outside the profession and timeously addresses these matters as they arise. Influences transformation agendas in other professions. • Implemented in order to give learners better prospects of passing Maths and to make learning Maths exciting • Grow the pipeline of future accountants, provide decent and meaningful opportunities and services for disenfranchised South Africans. Grow and transform the accounting profession, in terms of race and gender, and in line with the overarching national agenda The Assistant PM must also ensure that while they implement these projects: • The appropriate governance structures and measurement and reporting mechanisms are upheld and followed when delivering on projects.
