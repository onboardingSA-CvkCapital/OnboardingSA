PRODUCTION MANAGER: RETAIL PACKAGING Bergendal Rooibos, situated in Citrusdal, is an established player in the rooibos industry, specialising in the production, processing, packaging and export of premium-quality rooibos tea. Bergendal Rooibos offers an excellent opportunity for a dynamic Production Manager to join their team in the rooibos tea packaging division. The ideal candidate has excellent communication and leadership skills, is passionate about technical aspects, and excels in the practical execution of tasks, people management and shop floor management. Responsibilities will include (but not be limited to): *Focusing on technical aspects, the implementation of the packing plan, quality control and floor management *Managing the technical aspects of the production process, including the maintenance, operation, troubleshooting and repair of machinery to ensure uninterrupted production *Implementing and monitoring the packing plan to ensure the efficient production and packaging of rooibos tea products *Conducting regular quality control checks to ensure that products meet the required standards *Leading floor management, including the effective deployment of personnel to achieve optimal production *Reporting regularly to the General Manager on production progress, challenges and areas for improvement *Ensuring compliance with Food Safety, Health and Safety rules and standards (BRC). Essential qualifications, skills, and experience: • A relevant qualification in Engineering, Technical Management, or a related field will be advantageous; • Previous experience in a production or technical management role, particularly within a packaging environment, will be advantageous; • A sound understanding of technical principles and the ability to solve problems; • Strong practical insight and the ability to execute plans effectively.
