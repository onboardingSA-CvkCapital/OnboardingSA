About the role

Role Overview The Senior Cloud Engineer – Google Cloud Platform (GCP) is responsible for designing, implementing, and managing scalable, secure, and high-performing cloud infrastructure solutions within Google Cloud environments. The role works closely with development, operations, and security teams to ensure the reliability, scalability, optimisation, and operational excellence of cloud-based platforms and services. The successful candidate will drive cloud engineering best practices, automation, infrastructure-as-code adoption, and continuous improvement across modern cloud-native environments. Key Responsibilities • Design and implement scalable, reliable, and secure cloud architectures on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Manage and optimise cloud infrastructure to ensure high availability, scalability, and performance. • Develop automation scripts and tooling to streamline cloud operations and improve operational efficiency. • Implement and maintain infrastructure-as-code (IaC) solutions using Terraform. • Apply cloud security best practices and ensure compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements. • Configure and maintain monitoring, alerting, and optimisation practices across cloud environments. • Collaborate with development, operations, and security teams to integrate cloud solutions into broader system architectures. • Provide advanced troubleshooting and support for cloud infrastructure and operational incidents. • Implement and support CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment practices, preferably using GitHub Actions. • Drive continuous improvement initiatives across cloud engineering, automation, and operational practices. • Mentor and support junior engineers and contribute to technical knowledge-sharing initiatives. Essential Skills & Experience • Minimum 3 years’ experience in cloud engineering or cloud infrastructure roles. • At least 2 years’ hands-on experience working with Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Strong experience with GCP services including Compute Engine, Kubernetes Engine (GKE), Cloud Storage, BigQuery, Cloud Functions, and Pub/Sub. • Hands-on experience with Terraform and infrastructure-as-code methodologies. • Strong scripting and automation skills using Python, Bash, or similar languages. • Experience implementing and supporting CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation. • Strong understanding of cloud networking, architecture, and security principles. • Experience applying cloud security best practices and compliance frameworks. • Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving capabilities. • Strong communication and collaboration skills within cross-functional teams. • Experience mentoring or guiding junior engineers. Desirable Skills & Experience • Experience managing and integrating multi-cloud environments. • Exposure to big data technologies and tooling within GCP ecosystems. • Experience tuning and optimising cloud platform performance. • Exposure to Kubernetes administration and container orchestration best practices. • Experience supporting enterprise-scale cloud environments. Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field. • Advanced postgraduate qualification would be advantageous. • GCP Professional Cloud Architect certification or similar cloud certifications are highly desirable. Behavioural Competencies • Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset. • Excellent collaboration and stakeholder engagement skills. • Ability to adapt quickly to emerging technologies and evolving cloud practices. • Strong leadership and mentoring abilities. • High attention to detail and commitment to operational excellence. • Proactive and continuous improvement mindset. What We Offer • Competitive salary and benefits package. • Opportunity to work on enterprise-scale cloud transformation initiatives. • Exposure to modern cloud-native technologies and engineering practices. • Collaborative and innovative engineering environment. • Professional growth and certification opportunities. • Opportunity to contribute to impactful cloud and technology solutions.
