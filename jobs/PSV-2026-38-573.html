About the role

The Department of Health and Wellness (Western Cape) is hiring a Head Clinical Unit (Medical) Grade 1 (General Surgery) (Burns), based in Tygerberg Hospital, Parow Valley. This is a permanent post, paying R2,168,145 a year. How to apply: Applications are submitted online via www.westerncape.gov.za/health-jobs (click “online applications”). Closing date: 09 November 2026, 17:00PM Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: No payment of any kind is required when applying for this post.

Responsibilities

  • Provide and oversee safe, effective and efficient specialist burns care, including operative, peri-operative, critical care and after-hours service delivery.
  • Provide overall clinical governance and clinical leadership of the Burns Unit, including development, implementation and monitoring of protocols, morbidity and mortality review, clinical audit, patient safety and quality-improvement activities.
  • Provide strategic and operational management of the Burns Unit, including service planning, access and referral management, patient flow, resource stewardship, cost-centre oversight and responsible use of blood products, laboratory services, medicines, consumables and theatre resources.
  • Lead, supervise and develop medical staff and trainees in the Burns Unit, including performance management, roster oversight, professional conduct, mentorship and multidisciplinary teamwork.
  • Liaise with hospital management, General Surgery, Anaesthesia, Critical Care, Nursing, Rehabilitation services and referring clinicians to strengthen coordinated burns care and referral pathways across the Tygerberg drainage area.
  • Provide undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, bedside training, tutorials and specialist supervision, and contribute to relevant examinations and academic programme activities.
  • Lead and supervise research, audit, data collection and service-evaluation activities in Burns Surgery, with active participation in relevant institutional and provincial governance structures where required.

Requirements

  • Minimum educational qualification: Appropriate qualification that allows registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as a Medical Specialist in General Surgery or Plastic Surgery.
  • Registration with a professional council: Registration with the HPCSA as a Medical Specialist in General Surgery or Plastic Surgery.
  • Experience: At least 3 years’ appropriate experience after registration with the HPCSA as a Medical Specialist in General Surgery or Plastic Surgery.
  • Inherent requirement of the job:
  • It will be expected of the Head: Clinical Unit to work commuted overtime, limited to 12 hours, if operational requirements so demand
  • Participation in the after-hours consultant call system is required
  • The incumbent must be able to function independently in a high-volume specialist burns service and provide leadership across normal and after-hours service delivery.
  • Competencies (knowledge/skills): Advanced clinical and operative competence in General Surgery or Plastics Surgery, including the management of complex and critically ill burns patients.
  • Proven clinical leadership and managerial capability in a specialist surgical service, with the ability to lead a multidisciplinary Burns Unit and supervise senior and junior medical staff.
  • Knowledge and experience in clinical governance, morbidity and mortality review, audit, quality improvement, protocol development and monitoring of standards of care.
  • Ability to manage service access, patient flow, referral pathways, human resources and scarce clinical resources in a high-volume tertiary service.
  • Experience in undergraduate and postgraduate teaching and supervision, with the ability to contribute to specialist surgical training and academic programme development.
  • Appropriate research experience, including supervision of research, data analysis, presentation and publication.
  • Computer literacy and database competence are required.
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication, relationship-building and teamwork skills.
  • Insight into local healthcare delivery, diversity, transformation and equity.
