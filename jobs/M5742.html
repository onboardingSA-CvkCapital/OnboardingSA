About the role

Pay@, a leading payment aggregator and provider of secure payment solutions, is looking for a Senior Data Engineer to join their growing team in Stellenbosch. This role focuses on designing, building, and maintaining scalable data pipelines and architectures that enable advanced analytics and support data-driven decision-making across the organisation. The ideal candidate is a proactive, self-directed individual with strong technical expertise, excellent problem-solving ability, and the ability to work both independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment. Responsibilities include: • Design, build, and maintain scalable ETL/ELT data pipelines using Python, SQL and cloud-native tools. • Integrate and harmonise data from multiple sources, including transactional systems, accounting platforms, and CRM tools. • Develop and manage data warehouse structures across environments such as SQL Server and PostgreSQL. • Ensure data quality, reliability, and performance through monitoring, validation, and optimisation processes. • Collaborate with data architects, analysts, and business stakeholders to deliver fit-for-purpose data products. • Implement and maintain data governance, documentation, and best practices. • Research and implement new data technologies and improvements to existing systems. • Support deployment processes, version control, and system integration. • Contribute to data modelling, architecture decisions, and technical strategy. • Build prototypes and proof of concepts where required. • Assist with disaster recovery planning and system resilience. • All other tasks related to the role. Minimum requirements: • Degree in a relevant field (Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics or similar role). • 8+ years’ experience in a data engineering or similar role. • Strong proficiency in SQL and Python. • Proven experience building and maintaining data pipelines and data architectures. • Strong communication skills in English with the ability to work effectively with both technical teams and non-technical business stakeholders. • Own reliable transport. Advantageous: • Experience with data orchestration tools (e.g. Airflow). • Understanding of data warehousing and dimensional modelling. • Exposure to systems such as SQL Server, PostgreSQL, Sage One, Xero, Business Central, HubSpot, or Microsoft Dataverse. • Experience with Agile / Scrum delivery practices. • Experience in fintech, payments, or financial services. • Experience documenting data architectures, data models, or data dictionaries.
