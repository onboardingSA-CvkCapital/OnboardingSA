About the role

In a nutshell (your purpose) … Our purpose is simple: to change people’s lives for the better through wellness. We deliver social wellness experiences that enable people to achieve their personal wellness goals through holistic physical, mental, nutritional, and social wellbeing. As an Acquisition Marketing Support, you will support regional teams in generating, quantifying, and qualifying leads, while accelerating the lead-to-sale conversion ratio across your allocated region. You will assist with the implementation and execution of localised marketing campaigns, events, activations, and lead-generation initiatives, while building and nurturing strong relationships with customers, suppliers, and strategic partners. A day in the life (your duties & responsibilities) … Local Market Research • Analyse industry trends, competitor activity, and customer preferences. • Identify new market and growth opportunities. • Provide insights that support the refinement of regional marketing and sales strategies. Event & Campaign Management • Plan, coordinate, execute, and manage promotional and lead-generation activities. • Support activations both inside and outside the club environment. • Ensure campaigns and events are aligned with regional sales priorities and business objectives. Partner & Supplier Relations • Build and maintain strong relationships with third-party vendors, activation venues, and regional suppliers. • Coordinate suppliers and external partners to ensure effective and efficient campaign execution. • Identify opportunities to improve supplier efficiencies and value. Sales & Pipeline Acceleration • Collaborate with regional Sales and Corporate teams to translate sales priorities into actionable regional plans. • Support initiatives designed to increase lead generation, lead quality, and lead-to-sale conversion. • Identify opportunities to accelerate the sales pipeline. Performance Tracking & Reporting • Track and report on lead volumes, conversions, and campaign performance. • Monitor regional activity and campaign performance metrics, including: • CPL — Cost Per Lead • CPS — Cost Per Sale • ROI — Return on Investment • Provide accurate and meaningful reporting to support business decision-making.We Can’t Live Without… Qualifications & Experience • Matric. • Relevant tertiary qualification in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field. • Experience executing marketing campaigns, activations, and lead-generation initiatives at a regional level. • Experience tracking and reporting on: • Campaign performance • Lead volumes • Conversion rates • Cost Per Lead (CPL) • Cost Per Sale (CPS) • Return on Investment (ROI) • Strong stakeholder and supplier coordination experience. • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. • Experience working with CRM, lead-management, or marketing platforms. • Valid driver’s licence and own vehicle. • Willingness to travel within the allocated region.
