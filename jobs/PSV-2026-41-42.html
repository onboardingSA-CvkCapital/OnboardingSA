About the role

The Department of Home Affairs is hiring a Director: Finance and Support, based in Provincial Manager’s Office North West. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 to R1,551,807 a year. How to apply: Applications compliant with the “Directions to Applicants” above, must be submitted online at https://erecruitment.dha.gov.za or financerecruitment@dha.gov.za Reference number to quote: HRMC 72/26/2 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will be responsible for, amongst others, the following specific tasks: Manage all Finances and Supply Chain Management (SCM) in an efficient and effective manner.
  • Manage the provincial budget and monitor expenditure in line with financial requirements and the unit's objectives.
  • Develop finance strategies and plans for the province.
  • Procure goods and services in accordance with delegations.
  • Ensure invoices received are properly checked for correctness and payment effected within 30 days of receipt.
  • Ensure effective revenue collection and management.
  • Ensure cash is banked and revenue is accounted for.
  • Manage and oversee petty cash management.
  • Ensure financial records are filed in the prescribed manner.
  • Develop and implement an effective audit action plan to address both AGSA and Internal Audit findings and report on progress made in addressing any existing or potential audit findings.
  • Attend and participate in Back-to-Basic and Audit Steering Committee meetings.
  • Resolve and provide advice on all financial matters in the province.
  • Ensure compliance to policies, standards, and guidelines in line with recognized financial provisions (i.e. PFMA, Treasury Regulations, PPP (Preferential Procurement Policy Framework, and the Modified Cash Standard (MCS).
  • Manage the distribution of inventory and store operations, with an emphasis on vendor supply chain programs to provide lower cost of product.
  • Manage and provide guidance to suppliers in distribution and supply chain initiatives.
  • Manage and co-ordinate the tender processes within the province.
  • Confirm availability of funds prior to the issuing of orders for goods and services.
  • Ensure the successful management of assets and properties in the province.
  • Oversee the implementation of Human Resources processes within the province.
  • Oversee the implementation of communication processes within the province.
  • Manage all it support functions within the province.
  • Manage and implement strategic objectives and innovation.
  • Ensure the implementation of effective risk and compliance management practices.
  • Ensure effective and efficient management of human, physical and financial resources within the Unit.
  • Coach and guide staff on best practices and compliance with regulatory requirements.

Requirements

  • B Degree in Financial Management/Accounting at NQF level 7 as recognised by SAQA is required.
  • 5 years’ experience in Financial Management and Support is required.
  • 5 years in a Middle /Senior managerial position is required.
  • Knowledge of Public Service Act and Regulations.
  • Knowledge of the departmental Legislations and Prescripts.
  • Knowledge and application of Public Finance Management Act (PFMA) and Treasury Regulations.
  • Knowledge of Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act and Regulations.
  • Knowledge of Public Audit Act.
  • Mandates from the government including cluster and Government Programme of Action.
  • Knowledge of SCM and Asset/Fleet and Property management.
  • Knowledge of Human Resource Legislations and Prescripts.
  • Willingness to travel and work extended hours.
  • Weekend working hours will be required occasionally.
  • Completion of the Senior Management Services Pre-entry Certificate upon appointment.
