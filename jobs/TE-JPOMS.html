About the role

ROLE PURPOSE The Junior Property Operations Manager is responsible for supporting the effective operational management of buildings and ensuring that facilities, maintenance, service contractors, tenant installations and building-related activities are managed efficiently and in accordance with operational, financial, quality and statutory requirements. The role oversees day-to-day building operations, coordinates maintenance and service providers, supports tenant installations and smaller projects, manages operational administration and contributes to the effective management of building-related risks, compliance and costs. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Building Operations & Facilities Management • Manage and coordinate emergency, reactive and planned maintenance activities. • Oversee building housekeeping, cleaning, security and allocation of building sites. • Conduct regular housekeeping, reinstatement, vacancy and building inspections. • Conduct CAD versus vacant-space inspections and maintain accurate building inventories. • Monitor adherence to building house rules and report breaches of user clauses. • Manage key control and the issuing and auditing of parking access permits. • Attend to and follow up on logged maintenance and service calls. • Liaise with internal and external stakeholders on building operations and housekeeping requirements. • Maintain accurate operational records and documentation. • Monitor risk management requirements, including access control. • Support the implementation of emergency plans and procedures. Maintenance & Service Contractor Management • Coordinate and monitor service and maintenance contractors. • Ensure service providers meet agreed service levels and contractual requirements. • Manage and maintain service level agreements (SLAs) with vendors and service providers. • Issue and manage work orders. • Monitor the quality and timely completion of maintenance and repair work. • Liaise with contractors and internal stakeholders to resolve operational issues. Budgeting & Expense Control • Provide input into annual property expense budgets. • Monitor and control monthly operational expenditure. • Create and manage purchase orders on the relevant system. • Maintain accurate stock registers. • Monitor costs and identify opportunities for improved expense control. • Support accurate financial and operational reporting. Building Administration & Compliance • Maintain accurate building records, registers and operational documentation. • Monitor compliance with statutory requirements, including the Occupational Health and Safety Act. • Coordinate and support the implementation and monitoring of statutory requirements. • Coordinate and process Public Liability and Property Damage claims. • Coordinate and chair OHSA meetings. • Ensure operational activities are conducted in accordance with relevant policies, procedures and quality standards. • Support risk identification and mitigation across the building. Energy & Municipal Management • Monitor energy consumption and perform regular meter readings. • Assist with the management of municipal accounts and electrical recoveries. • Identify and report discrepancies or potential areas for improved utility management. Tenant Installations, Revamps & Upgrades • Coordinate tenant installation activities and smaller projects. • Act as an on-site project manager for smaller tenant installations and projects. • Develop and coordinate specifications for smaller tenant installation projects. • Assist with the management of revamps and building upgrades in conjunction with the Centre Manager or Property Manager. • Monitor project progress, quality, timelines and contractor performance. Operational Reporting & Inspections • Maintain inspection programmes and ensure inspections are conducted as required. • Record and report on building conditions, operational issues and maintenance requirements. • Maintain accurate records of building activities and operational interventions. • Complete daily foot-count records in shopping centres where applicable. • Provide relevant operational information and reports to management. People Management • Assist with performance management of operational staff. • Identify training and development requirements. • Support the development of team members and promote effective performance. • Coordinate team activities to ensure operational requirements are met.
