About the role

Ready to kickstart your career in Sales Administration? Frogfoot is looking for a Sales Administrator Intern to join our Sales team and gain hands-on experience supporting the sales process from customer requests and feasibility checks through to quotations, orders, and installations. If you're organised, detail-oriented, comfortable working with computers, and enjoy helping people, this is your opportunity to gain real-world experience, develop your administrative skills, and learn how a fast-paced sales environment operates. If you're ready to hop into the pond, learn new skills and make an impact, we'd love to meet you! About Frogfoot Frogfoot is a leading open-access fibre infrastructure provider in South Africa. We build and operate fibre networks that connect homes, businesses and communities, working with multiple Internet Service Providers to deliver reliable connectivity across the country. We're passionate about connectivity, innovation and developing people who are ready to grow their careers with us. Purpose of the Role The Sales Administrator Intern will provide administrative and operational support to the Sales team, assisting with customer requests, feasibilities, quotations, service orders, change orders and general sales administration. The role provides practical exposure to the sales process while developing the intern's administrative, communication, customer service, computer and organisational skills. You'll work closely with the Sales team and internal departments to ensure that customer information is captured accurately and that sales requests are processed efficiently and timeously.
