About the role

Capitec is hiring a Data Engineer. Python | SQL | AWS | Redshift | Data Lakes | Data Warehouses | ETL/ELT | Batch Processing | Streaming / Real-time Pipelines | Git | CI/CD | Terraform / Infrastructure as Code | Data Modelling | Data Quality | Data Governance | Workflow Orchestration | Power BI / Microsoft Data Stack exposure | AI tooling and automation We're looking to connect with Data Engineers across a range of experience levels, from professionals building their foundational skills through to highly experienced engineers leading complex data initiatives. You may be a strong fit if you have experience with SQL, Python, data warehousing, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure or GCP), ETL/ELT pipelines, data modelling, and Agile delivery practices. Depending on your level of experience, you may be contributing to data pipeline development, system analysis and solution design, independently building and maintaining cloud-native data solutions, or taking technical ownership of large-scale platforms, architecture, performance optimisation, governance, CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code, and engineering standards. We welcome individuals who are passionate about data, enjoy solving complex problems, collaborate effectively with stakeholders, and are looking to grow their expertise in areas such as cloud engineering, data architecture, AI-enabled engineering, platform design, and technical leadership.
