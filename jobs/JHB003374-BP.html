About the role

The Assistant Accountant supports the Finance Manager and Financial Accountant with the day-to-day financial operations of a group of hotels and lodges. The role ensures accurate financial records, reconciliations, reporting, and compliance while assisting with month-end processes across multiple properties. The position plays a key role in maintaining financial controls and supporting operational decision-making.
