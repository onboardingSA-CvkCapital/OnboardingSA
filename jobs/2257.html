Duties and responsibilities: •    Provide formal teaching for undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in the Department of Childhood Education. •    Act as a supervisor for postgraduate students in the relevant field. •    Engage in the Work Integrated Learning Programme of the faculty (Teaching Practice). •    Ensure active involvement in research in a relevant field of Childhood Education and publish research articles in accredited national and international journals. •    Active involvement in curriculum development relating to the relevant field of Childhood Education. •    Apply for external funding grants for research and/or teaching and learning projects. •    Mentor and support junior colleagues in the department and faculty. •    Contribute to leadership and administrative tasks in the department and faculty. Inherent requirements: Senior Lecturer •    A Doctoral degree in Education on NQF Level 10 with specialisation in Childhood Education and/or Foundation Phase. •    A minimum of three (3) years relevant experience in a Higher Education Environment. •    Proven experience in teaching and learning management and/or module leadership. •    Experience as external examiner for postgraduate students. •    Proven experience in the presentation of research papers/posters at national and international scholarly conferences, where there is evidence of peer review of papers. •    Proven national recognition for specialist expertise and research in a specific area of scholarship. •    An NRF rating or demonstrated potential for obtaining an NRF rating. •    Proven experience in supervision of graduated honour’s and master’s degree students. •    A proven research record of relevant publication/s in peer-reviewed national and internationally accredited scientific journals. •    Proven experience of service as an active member of a national or international scholarly society or committee or agency concerned with research at higher-education levels. •    Evidence of involvement as a PI, Co-PI or Researcher in a grant-funded research project in the past 5 years. •    Proven experience in one or more engaged scholarship projects in the past 5 years. Recommendations: •    Experience in the use of different research methodologies and/or data analysis tools •    Experience in grant writing and/or preparing research funding applications. Required competencies: •    Results Orientated: -    The ability to cope with a frequently changing environment and to adapt to evolving situations. -    The ability to be reliable, responsible, dependable and to fulfil obligations. •    Strategic Thinking: -    The ability to focus on details, work towards perfection, and approach work in a neat and organised manner. -    The ability to plan work and to follow plans. •    Business Acumen: -    The ability to adhere to rules and strictly follow work regulations. -    Proficient in using MS Office. •    Leading: -    The ability to lead, take charge of situations, and offer opinions and directions to others. -    The ability to be cooperative with others, display a good-natured attitude and encourage people to work together. •    Building Coalitions: -    The ability to negotiate, sell, influence and to persuade others. -    The ability to make decisions through consultation, collaboration and working with close supervision.
