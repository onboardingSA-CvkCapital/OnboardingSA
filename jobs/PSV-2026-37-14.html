About the role

The Department of Cooperative Governance is hiring a Deputy Director: Awareness and Advocacy, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Applications must be submitted electronically via email to: dcog19@tttrecruitment.co.za For application enquiries contact: To the T Recruitment Tel No: (010)102 3607 Reference number to quote: DD/AA-02 Closing date: 30 October 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will perform the following duties
  • Manage the development and implementation of the Integrated Public Awareness Strategy (IPAS)
  • Manage the coordination and implementation of national public awareness and advocacy campaigns, as well as commemorative events
  • Manage the development and dissemination of strategic disaster risk communication and public awareness content to support the implementation of the Integrated Public Awareness Strategy (IPAS).
  • Manage the coordination of the National Capacity Building Coordinating Committee for disaster management awareness, advocacy and capacity development.

Requirements

  • A 3-Year National Diploma or Degree in Disaster Management/Development Studies/Social Sciences or equivalent qualification at NQF level 6/7 as recognised by SAQA.
  • 3-5 years’ experience in Disaster Management.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office packages (MS Word, MS PowerPoint, MS Teams, MS Excel and Outlook e.t.c).
  • A valid driver’s licence.
  • Extensive travelling.
  • Generic Competencies
  • Planning and Organising
  • Coordination
  • Problem Solving & Decision Making
  • Project Management
  • People Management & Empowerment
  • Client Orientation & Customer Focus
  • Team Leadership
  • Diversity Management
  • Communication (Verbal & Written)
  • Technical Competencies
  • In-depth knowledge of: Disaster Management Legislation, Frameworks and Policies
  • Public service Acts and Regulations
  • Functioning of Provincial and Local Government
  • Skills development and Training
  • Knowledge generation and dissemination
