About the role

Purpose To increase profitability for allocated products by conducting accurate and reliable forecasts, aligning inventory levels with identified demand, and improving income or revenue forecasts. Key responsibilities for this position include, but are not limited to: Forecasting Designing and generating weekly and monthly statistical forecast reports Continuously improving forecasting techniques, methods and approach to ensure they are aligned to company objectives Relating and measuring the impact of forecast accuracy Support and drive strategic project plans to successfully launch innovative products. Weekly & Monthly demand for Demand Management System. Monthly consensus meeting with sales management. Weekly SPM meeting with sales management. Communicate regularly with HQ & Manufacturing to identify supply issues and SFA Long Term forecasting, confirmation of kits with HQ for Local manufacturing. Demand Planning procedures and processes Develop and maintain documentation and standard operating procedures for demand planning processes and systems. Conduct current and future forecasting analysis and ensure forecasting processes and methods are followed. Assemble and analyze all data relevant to creating the sales forecast (historical sales, market trends, seasonality, promotions and inventory levels) Establish and utilize best methods to create forecasts and respective inventory targets. Pricing Assist with the update and maintenance of monthly price lists (per currency) Communicate internally about pricing and on systems Maintain fixed pricing from PM Assist with pricing quotes, give information on foreign exchange conversions Inventory Management Make recommended adjustments to forecast and inventory targets based on changes in demand and market trends. Communicate with HQ about the new product and facilitate the registration of SABS to get the certificate Review recommended sales forecasts and inventory goals focusing on new product introduction and making sure that stock is available for promotions. Monitor and support channel inventory (sell-in/sellout) basis Assist with monitoring of back orders Tracking PO’s & Direct Orders to ensure Business Plan, Moving Plan target is achieved, ultimate goal to maximize sales and reduce inventory costs Assist with information of Graded inventory Product Management Review product data to ensure that sales staff is kept up to date on new developments regarding the companies Responsible for the issuing and control of all sample products Give input to HQ about new product and will give us new pricing for the product Gap analysis to understand non achievements for Sales Forecasting accuracy and address with Sales Management Team. Present at S&OP meeting to top management (Pending Issues/ supply and production issues/ageing Inventory & action plans). Assist with Moving Plan sales allocations & backorders. Management of End of Life & New Product Information (Demand, Shipment and delays analysis). Manage DIO-Tracking and actions Plans-Local and Export Assist Product Manager with Ad Hoc duties as and when necessary Reporting Prepare, report and communicate forecast and inventory measurements to all stakeholders. Highlight key variances and change forecasts accordingly Progress report on forecasts for set targets Basic admin (requesting approvals for product transfers, request price approvals, listing documents, price queries internal and external, provide images and specifications, sample stock management, request approval for scrapping)
