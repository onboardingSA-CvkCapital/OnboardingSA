About the role

The world's largest fund administration and middle office solutions providers is looking for a Programme Manager - Cybersecurity and Information Security to join their team in Cape Town, Western Cape or Johannesburg, Gauteng. Programme Leadership and Delivery: • Lead complex, business-critical programmes that deliver strategic change across the organisation and for our clients. • Provide overall programme leadership, coordinating multiple interdependent projects and workstreams. • Ensure delivery aligns with business objectives, regulatory requirements, and risk management expectations. • Translate executive-level objectives into executable programme plans. • Maintain delivery momentum across multiple teams and workstreams. • Work on delivery as well as coordinating programmes — this is a hands-on leadership role. Cyber and Information Security: • Drive the delivery of Information Security and Cyber Security initiatives as a key focus of the role. • Embed security considerations throughout the programme lifecycle. • Apply a strong and current understanding of security frameworks, standards, industry best practices, and governance. • Engage effectively with security, technology, risk, compliance, and business teams. • Translate technical concepts into clear business outcomes for non-technical stakeholders. • Challenge where appropriate to ensure security requirements are met. • Apply SOC standards and governance requirements across programmes. Governance Risk and Reporting: • Manage programme-level governance, risks, dependencies, budgets, and stakeholder communications. • Provide clear and transparent reporting to senior stakeholders. • Ensure strong governance ability across all programme activities. • Apply Waterfall, Agile, and hybrid delivery approaches as appropriate. • Maintain oversight of regulatory, operational resilience, and risk management expectations. Stakeholder Management: • Engage effectively with senior leaders and executive-level stakeholders. • Influence and challenge senior leaders where necessary. • Build confidence in programme delivery across the organisation. • Communicate complex technical information clearly to non-technical audiences. • Manage competing priorities and navigate high levels of ambiguity. • Operate effectively across global time zones and various cultures. Methodologies: • Apply the most appropriate delivery methodology — Agile, Waterfall, or hybrid. • Utilise portfolio and delivery management tools such as Azure DevOps, Jira, or Microsoft Project. • Demonstrate exceptional organisational, planning, and communication skills. • Ensure fluency in English, both written and spoken. Requirements: • Minimum 5 years' experience operating as a Programme Manager, leading large-scale programmes comprising multiple projects and workstreams. • Strong prior background in Project Management, with a proven track record of successfully delivering complex change initiatives. • Significant experience delivering Information Security and/or Cyber Security programmes, with demonstrable knowledge of current security standards, frameworks, and industry best practices. • Ability to engage effectively with security, technology, risk, compliance, and business teams, translating technical concepts into clear business outcomes. • Proven experience managing programme-level governance, risks, dependencies, budgets, and stakeholder communications. • Excellent stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence and challenge senior leaders and build confidence in programme delivery. • Experience working in fast-paced, complex environments with competing priorities and high levels of ambiguity. • Strong understanding of Agile, Waterfall, and hybrid delivery approaches, with the judgement to apply the most appropriate methodology. Nice to have: • Experience delivering change within Financial Services, Banking, Asset Management, Fund Administration, FinTech, or other regulated environments. • Familiarity with regulatory, operational resilience, information security, or cyber security requirements applicable to financial institutions. • Experience leading large-scale transformation, integration, migration, or onboarding programmes. • Familiarity with Azure DevOps, Jira, Microsoft Project, or similar portfolio and delivery management tools.
