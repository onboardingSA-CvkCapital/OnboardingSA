About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring an Artisan Production A: Plumber, based in ST MARY’S DISTRICT HOSPITAL MARIANNHILL. This is a permanent post, paying R253,341 a year. How to apply: All applications should be forwarded to: Hand Delivery Address: Human Resources Department St Mary’s Hospital 1 Hospital Road Mariannhill, 3610 Reference number to quote: SMMH 01/SYST/2026 Closing date: 10 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  •  Installation, repairs and maintenance of high pressure and low-pressure geysers.  Installation and repairs to toilets, urinals and other ablution facilities such as shower and baths, etc.  Installation and repairs to water and sewerage reticulation system.  Clear blocked drains, gutters, down pipes, wastewater lines and branch lines.  Take and record water supply readings daily.  Maintenance on fire hose main supply, fire hose and fire hydrants.  Daily reporting of faults, job progress and daily completion of job progress and daily completion of job card / timesheet as per instruction.  Be prepared to visit primary health care clinics to perform maintenance duties when required.  Installation of gutters and down pipes and galvanized pipes.  Compile and submit reports, provide input on the compilation of technical reports.  Deputize Artisan Production C when required to do so.  Form part of multi-disciplinary team doing rounds in the hospital and primary health care clinics and take the necessary steps to fix all identified shortfalls.  To take responsibility for in-house training and advancement of subordinates.
  •  To perform standby duties and after hours call outs.  To supervise Handymen and assist other tradesman aids when requested to do so by Chief Artisan and / or Systems Manager.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 (senior certificate)
  • Appropriate Trade Test Certificate in terms of section 13(9)(2)(h) of the Manpower Act of 1981 as amended
  • A minimum of 0-3 years experience as a Plumber in an appropriate field
  • Proof of current and previous experience endorsed by Human Resources
  • Valid Drivers Licence
  • All successful candidates will have to spend a minimum of one (01) year in service
  • Knowledge of Occupational Health and Safety Act and safety standards
  • Knowledge of basic maintenance and repair of broken pipes
  • Knowledge of safety systems of work
  • Ability to work independently
  • Ability to work under pressure
  • Good communication skills (both verbal and written)
  • Knowledge of Batho Pele Principles
