About the role

The Department of Higher Education and Training is hiring an Assistant Director: Student Support Services, based in Northern Cape Rural TVET College, Central Office Upington. This is a permanent post, paying R605,742 a year. How to apply: Apply online at https://ncrz83.ngnscan.co.za/ Reference number to quote: NCRTVET/685/10/2026/SUP/RS/PL/CTO/03 Closing date: 20 November 2026. Applications received after the closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Applicants are required to submit copies of qualifications and other relevant documents on application and must submit a fully signed Z83 form and detailed Curriculum Vitae. Certified copies of qualifications and other relevant documents will be submitted by shortlisted candidates. Applications received after the closing date and those that do not comply with the requirements will not be considered. It is the applicant’s responsibility to have foreign qualifications and national certificates (where applicable) evaluated by the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA). The Department reserves the right not to fill the posts. The successful candidate will be subjected to personnel suitability checks and other vetting procedures. Applicants are respectfully informed that correspondence will be limited to short-listed candidates only. If notification of an interview is not received within three (3) months after the closing date, candidates may regard their application as unsuccessful. The Department will not be liable where applicants use incorrect/no reference number(s) on their applications. All shortlisted candidates, including the SMS, shall undertake two pre-entry assessments. One will be a practical exercise to determine a candidate’s suitability based on the post’s technical and generic requirements and the other will be integrity (ethical conduct) assessment. Selection panels shall score both technical exercises as an additional criterion in the interview process.

Responsibilities

  • Oversee administration of the Student Support Services Section.
  • Manage student counselling across the college.
  • Provide student with career guidance, counselling and academic support.
  • Oversee and maintain the sport, recreation, arts and culture activities for students.
  • Facilitate student governance and student leadership development.
  • Manage mainstreaming of gender and disability within students.
  • Management of all Human, Financial and other resources of the Section.

Requirements

  • Appropriate recognized Bachelor's Degree/National Diploma (NQF Level 6) or equivalent qualification in Psychology or Social Work.
  • At least 5 years (5) working experience in the TVET sector education institution of which at least 3 years should be at managerial level.
  • Registration with a professional body (e.g., SACE or HPCSA) etc. Digital literate and competent in Word/Excel/PowerPoint/Outlook/Teams.
  • A valid driver's license and willingness to travel.
  • Competencies
  • In-depth understanding of the CET Act (Continuing Education and Training Act) and the National Strategy for Learner Support
  • Knowledge of the NSFAS eligibility criteria and administration processes
  • Familiarity with the TVET College sector and Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET) policies
  • Understanding of Student Governance (SRC) constitutions and electoral processes
  • Knowledge of monitoring and evaluation frameworks for student success
  • Strategic Leadership: Ability to manage diverse teams and implement departmental mandates.
  • Financial Management: Skills in budgeting and expenditure control.
  • Conflict Resolution: Proficiency in mediating student grievances and campus unrest.
  • Communication: Strong verbal and written reporting skills for internal and external stakeholders as well as the College Council.
  • Data Analysis
  • Ability to interpret student performance and wellness data to inform relevant interventions
  • Proven track record in managing Student Financial Aid portfolios
  • Experience in facilitating Student Representative Council (SRC) elections and leadership training
  • Management of student health and wellness programs (HIV/AIDS, counselling, and disability support)
  • Experience in coordinating extra-curricular activities (Sports, Arts, and Culture)
  • Experience in managing disciplinary procedures and student conduct
  • A Postgraduate qualification in Educational Management or Higher Education
  • Certification in Project Management
  • Specialized training in Student Counselling or Career Guidance
  • Experience working with the COLTECH system
