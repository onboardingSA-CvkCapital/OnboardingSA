About the role

The Senior Videographer / Art Director's primary function is the end-to-end production of video and motion content that supports Fidelity Services Group's brand, marketing and internal communication objectives, owning the visual quality and consistency of all filmed and motion-based output from concept through to final delivery. Beyond video production, the role has an evolving mandate to assist the Marketing Manager in establishing and formalising an internal marketing division. Drawing on a combined background in design, branding and IT infrastructure, the incumbent will also help identify, implement and manage the technology and tooling that increase the division's agility, spanning content production, workflow automation and creative technology, not only video output. Key Responsibilities Videography & Motion Content • Plan, shoot and produce high-quality video content across corporate, marketing, recruitment, training and internal communications use cases. • Lead motion graphics and post-production work, including editing, colour grading, sound design and visual effects, to a broadcast-ready standard. • Direct on-location and studio shoots, managing crew, equipment, lighting and shot composition. • Develop storyboards, shot lists and creative treatments that translate briefs into compelling visual narratives. • Maintain a consistent visual identity and brand tone across all video and motion deliverables. • Manage the technical pipeline for video production, including equipment selection, file management and archiving of raw and finished assets. Art Direction & Brand • Provide art direction across video and supporting visual assets, ensuring alignment with brand guidelines and campaign objectives. • Collaborate with the Marketing Manager on concept development for campaigns, launches and internal initiatives. • Review and quality-control creative output from junior team members and external suppliers where applicable. Team Formalisation & Marketing Support Evolving Responsibility • Assist the Marketing Manager in formalising an internal marketing division, including defining workflows, production standards and content processes. • Contribute to building out equipment, software and resourcing requirements for an in-house creative and production capability. • Support the onboarding and mentoring of future videography, design or junior creative hires as the division grows. • Help establish templates, briefing documents and approval processes to streamline creative production as the team scales. Creative Technology & Tooling Evolving Responsibility • Apply IT infrastructure knowledge to evaluate, recommend and help implement software and systems that improve the marketing division's agility and output beyond video alone, including asset management, automation and collaboration tools. • Identify opportunities to apply AI and emerging creative technologies to streamline content production, design and campaign workflows. • Act as a technical resource and point of liaison between the marketing division and IT, ensuring tools and systems are fit for purpose and well maintained. • Support the division's technology roadmap as it scales beyond video into broader design and marketing production. Requirements Experience & Qualifications • 12+ years' experience in videography, motion design or a related visual communications role, ideally including senior or lead-level responsibility. • A diploma or degree in Visual Communications, Film, Motion Design or a related field, or equivalent demonstrable experience. • A strong portfolio or showreel demonstrating range across corporate, brand and campaign video work. • Experience working within, or helping establish, an in-house or agency creative production function is advantageous. • Experience in the security, corporate services or similar B2B sector is an advantage but not essential. Technical Skills • Advanced proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects; working knowledge of the broader Adobe Creative Suite. • Experience with camera systems, lighting and audio equipment for professional shoots. • Motion graphics and VFX capability; familiarity with 3D tools such as Blender or 3D Studio MAX is a strong advantage. • Sound understanding of brand guideline application and design consistency across formats. • Working knowledge of IT infrastructure and systems, with the ability to evaluate and help implement tools that support a growing marketing division, including asset management, automation and AI-assisted workflows. Core Competencies • Strong creative direction and storytelling ability, with an eye for detail and visual quality. • Project management skills, with the ability to run multiple shoots and deadlines concurrently. • Collaborative, able to work closely with a Marketing Manager and cross-functional stakeholders, including marketing, HR and operations. • Comfortable operating with a degree of ambiguity and proactive in helping shape team structure and process from the ground up. • Clear communicator, able to present creative concepts and rationale to non-creative stakeholders. Key Performance Indicators • Volume and quality of video and motion content delivered on brief, on brand and on schedule. • Consistency of visual identity across internal and external video output. • Progress against milestones for formalising the internal marketing team, including structure, workflows and resourcing. • Stakeholder satisfaction across departments requesting creative and video support. • Turnaround time from brief to final delivery and effective use of production resources.
