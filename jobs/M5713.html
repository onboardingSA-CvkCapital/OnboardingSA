About the role

ACCOUNTING & SYSTEMS CLERK Laastedrif Agri (Pty) Ltd., is an established commercial agricultural enterprise, specialising in the cultivation of fresh fruit and vegetables. Based in Ceres, Laastedrif Agri supplies a range of local supermarkets as well as international markets. Laastedrif Agri has an excellent opportunity available for a Accounting & Systems Clerk to join their Finance team at the Head Office in Ceres. The ideal candidate has excellent numerical and problem-solving abilities, is highly accurate and detail-oriented, performs well under pressure, and takes a proactive approach to improving processes. This role will report to the Financial Manager and will play an important role in maintaining the company's financial systems (Sage X3), supporting accounting processes, and providing assistance to the Finance Department. Responsibilities will include (but not be limited to): *Maintaining, supporting and improving the Sage X3 system, including system testing, troubleshooting and user support *Preparing financial and system-related reports for management *Processing creditor invoices, reconciling supplier statements, and providing debtor and creditor administration *Maintaining accurate financial records and supporting audit requirements *Identifying and implementing system and process improvements, including user training where required *Working closely with internal stakeholders to ensure efficient financial administration. Essential qualifications, skills, and experience: • Matric required, a relevant tertiary qualification will be advantageous; • Approximately 3 years’ experience in a relevant accounting role; • Strong computer literacy, including accounting software and Microsoft Office; • Good communication skills in both Afrikaans and English.
