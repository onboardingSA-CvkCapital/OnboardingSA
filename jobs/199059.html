About the role

Stefanutti Stocks is looking for a skilled Linesman to join our team and play a key role in the installation, maintenance and repair of electrical transmission and distribution infrastructure. The role involves working safely with electrical equipment, installing power lines and related equipment, erecting utility poles and towers, and supporting site activities to ensure reliable and efficient power networks. Key Responsibilities: • Install and maintain overhead transmission and distribution lines, poles, crossarms, insulators, switches and related equipment. • Erect and secure utility poles using appropriate construction equipment and tools. • Install, test and maintain electrical equipment, including conductors, transformers, voltage regulators and switchgear. • Perform inspections and testing to identify electrical faults and equipment defects. • Diagnose and repair faults on transmission and distribution networks. • Carry out maintenance and repairs safely and efficiently to minimise downtime. • Climb utility poles and transmission towers and work at heights using approved access equipment. • Operate truck-mounted buckets and other specialised equipment where required. • Dig trenches and pole holes and assist with associated civil works. • Read and interpret electrical drawings, specifications and construction plans. • Drive company vehicles and transport tools, equipment and materials to and from work sites. • Follow all applicable electrical, safety, quality and environmental requirements. • Work effectively as part of a site team and coordinate activities with supervisors, electricians and other construction personnel. • Maintain tools, equipment and work areas in a safe and serviceable condition. • Report defects, incidents, progress and work completed to the relevant supervisor. Qualifications: • Grade 12/Matric • Relevant NQF Electrical qualification or equivalent • Electrical trade qualification/Trade Test relevant to overhead lines or electrical work • Working at Heights certification Skills: • Knowledge of overhead transmission and distribution line construction. • Knowledge of electrical equipment, conductors, insulators, transformers and switchgear. • Ability to interpret electrical drawings and technical specifications. • Strong electrical fault-finding and troubleshooting skills. • Competent in using electrical tools, construction equipment and specialised access equipment. • Ability to work safely at heights and around electrical infrastructure. • Strong understanding of electrical, construction and OHS requirements. • Good physical fitness and stamina for demanding outdoor work. • Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills. • Good communication and teamwork skills. • Ability to work in varying weather and site conditions. • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality. Experience: • 2-5 years’ experience as a Linesman or in overhead power line construction. • Experience installing and maintaining utility poles, power lines, crossarms, insulators, switches and switchgear. Experience with transmission and distribution lines, preferably MV/HV. • Experience testing conductors, switches, transformers and voltage regulators. • Experience with pole erection, line installation and associated construction activities. • Experience working on poles and transmission towers at height. • Experience using bucket trucks or other specialised access equipment will be advantageous. • Experience diagnosing faults and carrying out repairs and maintenance. • Experience working on construction sites and within multidisciplinary teams. • Knowledge of relevant electrical standards, safety procedures and construction practices. • Experience operating work vehicles and construction equipment will be advantageous.
