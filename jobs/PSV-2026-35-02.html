About the role

The Department of Agriculture is hiring a Senior Agricultural Scientist (Senior Lecturer) in Grootfontein Agricultural Development Institute, based in Eastern Cape (Middelburg). This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to 20 Steve Biko Street, Agriculture Place, Arcadia, Pretoria, 0002 or by email SASrecruit88@nda.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/3/1/88/2026 Closing date: 16 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Target: African Males, Coloured Females and persons with disability
