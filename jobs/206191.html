About the role

Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the-farm foods for consumers. Apart from its various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture, with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now operate across the entire food supply chain, including processing, logistics, storage, handling and national and international market channels. This "house of brands", established with the support of our farmer shareholders, forms the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. More About VKB Mechanization VKB Mechanization is a leading provider of agricultural equipment, mechanization solutions and technical support to farming businesses across South Africa. Working alongside globally recognised equipment manufacturers, we help producers improve productivity, operational efficiency and profitability through world-class machinery, precision farming technologies and expert support. As agriculture becomes increasingly technology-driven, mechanization has never been more critical. Our success depends on attracting exceptional sales leaders who understand both the agricultural producer and the machinery that powers their success. Why This Role Matters Lead the Future of Agricultural Equipment Sales VKB Mechanization is seeking an exceptional leader to drive our national Whole Goods sales strategy and lead one of South Africa's most respected agricultural equipment sales teams. This is not a traditional sales management role. We are looking for a proven Whole Goods sales leader who has successfully led mechanization sales teams, understands the complexity of agricultural equipment markets, and knows how to translate opportunities into sustainable growth and profitability. As National Sales Manager: Whole Goods, you will be responsible for driving national sales performance across our equipment portfolio, developing high-performing regional sales teams, strengthening supplier and customer partnerships, and ensuring VKB Mechanization remains the preferred mechanization partner to South African farmers. If you have a proven track record in agricultural machinery sales leadership and a passion for developing winning teams, we would like to hear from you. Why Join VKB?
