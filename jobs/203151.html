About the role

Frogfoot is in search of a Service Delivery Co-ordinator to jon the Service Delivery - Special Projects. Purpose of the Role This role is perfect for someone who thrives on managing workflow administration and communication activities to ensure consistent, high-quality feedback and the efficient processing of service order tickets, in line with the Service Delivery Communication Plan and SLA targets. The role involves close collaboration with cross-functional teams across all stages of the Service Order fulfilment process, engaging with suppliers to ensure the timely delivery of products and services required for order completion, and proactively escalating issues or delays to the Service Delivery Manager when needed. You will oversee and coordinate the day-to-day tasks relating to any orders received from clients or internal teams, ensuring installations are completed smoothly with minimal disruption or downtime. Working closely with clients, contractors, and internal teams, you will play a key role in meeting service delivery objectives and client expectations. Strong organisational skills, exceptional attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively are essential for success in this role.
