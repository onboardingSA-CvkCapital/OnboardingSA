About the role

🚀 We're Hiring: Co-ordinator: Operational Facilities & Health and Safety 📍 Location: Western Cape 📊 Reporting Line: Manager: Operational Facilities Management 💼 Job Purpose Lesaka Merchant is looking for a Co-ordinator: Operational Facilities & Health and Safety to support the delivery of environmentally sustainable, fit-for-purpose, and scalable operational facilities across the Merchant Operations Division. This role is responsible for ensuring optimal space utilisation, facilities maintenance, occupational health and safety compliance, and effective operational support across a fast-growing fintech environment. The successful candidate will work closely with internal stakeholders and service providers to ensure facilities remain safe, functional, compliant, and aligned to business needs. 🔍 Key Responsibility Areas Operational Facilities Strategy Administered • Ensure space and place utilisation supports the diverse requirements of the Merchant Operations Division. • Assess existing facilities and recommend efficient and effective facility models aligned with business growth. • Ensure the Merchant Operations Division brand identity and culture are reflected across operational facilities. • Support the implementation of business processes and operating rules that optimise facilities and improve employee and customer experience. • Provide input into the redesign of critical processes that enable efficient, scalable, and merchant-centric facilities management. Operational Facilities Maintenance, Processes, Systems & Business Rules • Implement and maintain Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). • Coordinate and track maintenance activities, repairs, and preventative maintenance schedules. • Monitor space utilisation, asset movements, and furniture management. • Maintain complete building and facilities documentation and compliance records. • Conduct routine facilities inspections and recommend corrective actions. • Manage facilities-related requests and ensure prompt communication until resolution. • Monitor and support services and utilities management within budget. • Compile facilities maintenance registers and provide trend analysis on recurring issues.Occupational Health & Safety Implemented • Maintain OHSA registers, risk assessments, safety logs, and compliance documentation. • Conduct regular Health & Safety inspections across all facilities. • Track and manage injuries on duty, incidents, and near misses. • Compile monthly, quarterly, and annual safety reports. • Maintain records of Safety Officers, Fire Marshals, and First Aiders. • Monitor compliance of PPE, safety equipment, workwear, and tools of trade. • Organise and coordinate fire drills, safety meetings, and employee inductions. • Conduct risk assessments for field technicians, remote workers, and operational environments. • Provide Occupational Health & Safety onboarding for new employees.
