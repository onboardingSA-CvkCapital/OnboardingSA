About the role

Rand Water is hiring a Principal Civil Engineering Assistant in Bulk Water Services - Operations, based at Rietvlei, Glenvista. Rand Water is currently looking for the Principal Civil Engineering Assistant to provide design drafting support to the Design Office in the Project Planning Department of the SAM Division in fulfilling the draughting requirements in the design of various civil engineering projects which contribute to optimal asset performance, optimal integrated engineering designs and exceptional customer services. The application of professional engineering drafting techniques to single and multi discipline projects of varying complexity or situations is required.

Responsibilities

  • Assist senior engineers on the design and or draughting aspects of larger, more complex, multidisciplinary projects
  • Implement Rand Water standards on projects and designs
  • Working within the generally provided Rand Water engineering guidelines, based on an understanding of the company and its overall goals
  • Ability to use AutoCAD 2014 or later
  • Able to produce advanced concrete reinforcement detail drawings and bending schedules given design (area of steel) - All detailing to be done in accordance with SANS 10144 or able to produce structural steel detail drawings.
  • Able to use either Autopadds or Rebarmate.
  • Must be able to produce working drawings with minimal supervision.
