About the role

Mechanical Design Engineer - Product Development and Consulting What You'll DoLead product development projects end-to-end, from concept through detailed design, prototyping, and release.Own client consulting engagements — assessing customer design workflows, CAD standards, and engineering processes, and recommending improvements.Develop and review complex CAD models, drawings, BOMs, and technical documentation, ensuring compliance with design release and revision control processes.Apply advanced Design for Manufacturing (DFM) principles to optimize designs for cost, quality, and manufacturability.Present recommendations, and run technical demonstrations, workshops, and training sessions for client teams.Build and manage relationships with international clients, acting as a trusted technical advisor.Mentor junior engineers and contribute to team capability development.Work independently on complex problems while collaborating effectively across multidisciplinary and international teams. What We're Looking For • BEng (Mechanical or Mechatronic Engineering) qualification. (Master’s would be a big plus!) • (3-5) or (8–10) years' experience in mechanical design, product development, AND engineering consulting. • Strong design software experience, with a track record of leading design projects. • Deep understanding of drawings, BOMs, revision control, manufacturing, and design release processes. • Proven experience consulting with clients — able to engage confidently with engineering managers, technical teams, and international stakeholders. • Strong problem-solving, communication, and project delivery skills, with the ability to work independently and lead within a team. • Experience working with international clients, ideally across multiple regions and time zones. • Experience with PDM, PLM, ERP, or engineering data management systems. • Exposure to FEA, CFD, or other simulation tools. • Experience with design automation, templates, workflows, or engineering process improvement.
