About the role

ROLE PURPOSE To provide specialist technical documentation, solution costing and proposal support for Huawei solutions by developing and maintaining accurate, consistent and commercially aligned solution documentation.The role is responsible for translating approved technical requirements and solution inputs into accurate SmartPlan designs, Bills of Quantities (BoQs), Bills of Materials (BoMs), costing, High-Level Designs (HLDs), Low-Level Designs (LLDs), Statements of Work (SoWs), technical proposals and bid documentation. The role ensures that technical and commercial information is accurate, traceable, appropriately governed and aligned throughout the solution planning, costing and proposal lifecycle. ROLE REQUIREMENT • Develop Huawei SmartPlan designs in accordance with customer requirements and approved solution architecture. • Perform solution sizing and capacity planning to determine appropriate Huawei products, equipment, software, licences and quantities. • Develop and maintain HLDs and LLDs, including supporting technical diagrams and solution documentation. • Translate technical requirements and solution inputs into clear, structured and professional technical documentation. • Prepare Statements of Work (SoWs) and supporting implementation documentation. • Ensure technical documentation accurately reflects the approved solution and customer requirements. • Produce accurate BoQs and BoMs based on SmartPlan designs and approved solution requirements. • Select and validate appropriate Huawei products, equipment and licences, ensuring compatibility with the proposed solution. • Prepare accurate commercial costing and licensing estimates based on approved BoQs, BoMs, product selections and solution requirements. • Validate the alignment between solution design, product selection, quantities, licensing and costing. • Identify and resolve discrepancies between technical requirements, BoQs, BoMs and commercial information. • Prepare professional technical proposals and tender responses that accurately represent the proposed Huawei solution. • Support technical responses for RFIs, RFPs and RFQs, ensuring customer requirements are addressed accurately and within agreed submission timelines. • Compile and coordinate technical inputs required for bids and proposals. • Ensure bid documentation is complete, accurate, consistent and compliant with submission requirements. • Collaborate with Presales, Solution Architecture, Engineering, Sales, Commercial, Finance and Project Management teams to obtain and validate required technical and commercial inputs. • Engage with Huawei stakeholders to validate product, licensing and solution-related information where required. • Clarify technical and commercial requirements with relevant stakeholders to ensure accurate and complete documentation. • Support the coordination of inputs and deliverables to meet proposal, bid and customer submission timelines. • Build effective working relationships across technical and commercial teams to facilitate accurate and timely solution development. • Apply appropriate technical writing, document governance and version-control practices across solution documentation. • Review SmartPlan designs, BoQs, BoMs, costing, HLDs, LLDs, SoWs and proposals to ensure technical and commercial information remains aligned. • Conduct quality assurance checks to identify inaccuracies, inconsistencies, omissions and version-control issues. • Maintain traceability between customer requirements, solution design, product selection, costing and final proposal documentation. • Ensure documentation is maintained in accordance with agreed standards, processes and governance requirements. • Validate documentation completeness, consistency and alignment before submission for review or approval. • Recommend corrections to BoQs, BoMs, costing inputs, technical proposals and supporting documentation. • Escalate unresolved technical, commercial, product, licensing or governance discrepancies to the relevant accountable stakeholder • Presales, Solution Architecture, Engineering, Sales, Commercial, Finance and Project Management. • Huawei vendor contacts and relevant internal approval stakeholders, where required. • Accuracy and completeness of BoQs, BoMs, costing inputs, HLDs, LLDs, SoWs and technical proposals. • Turnaround time for bid, proposal and solution documentation deliverables. • Reduction in documentation errors, rework, version-control issues and approval delays. • Adherence to document governance, confidentiality, quality assurance and internal approval standards. • Huawei SmartPlan or equivalent solution design/configuration tools. • Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint, including strong Excel capability for costing, BoQ and BoM preparation. • Approved proposal templates, document repositories and version-control platforms used by the business.
