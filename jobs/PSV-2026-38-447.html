About the role

The Provincial Treasury (Gauteng) is hiring a Messenger/Driver in Office of the Chief Financial Officer, based in Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R201,093 a year. How to apply: Applications should be submitted online at: https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za Reference number to quote: REFS/056981 Closing date: 09 November 2026 @00:00 midnight Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • To provide driving functions for Gauteng Provincial Treasury.
  • Delivery and collection of items.
  • Provision of shuttle services.
  • Delivery and collection of vehicles.
  • Delivery and collection of staff.
  • General Administration function.
  • Conduct daily vehicle inspections.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 or equivalent appropriate qualification.
  • 1- 2 years’ experience.
  • Valid driver licence as well as a Professional Driving Permit (PDP).
  • Basic Computer literacy, Knowledge of the procedures to operate the motor vehicle.
  • Knowledge of prescripts for the correct utilization of motor vehicles.
  • Knowledge of the procedures to ensure that the motor vehicle is maintained properly, Knowledge of the procedures to perform messenger functions, Sound interpersonal skills.
