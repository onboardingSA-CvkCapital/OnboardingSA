About the role

A well-established and growing packaging and security printing company seeks an experienced Shift Supervisor basedin Mobeni to oversee the packaging department. The purpose of this role is to oversee the smooth running of the shift. To take responsibility for ensuring all resources are available when and where needed. They are responsible for the overall discipline and management of employees during shift. They are also responsible for ensuring the required output & targets are achieved. They must ensure that all equipment / machinery is maintained. They must assist to develop and action projects to reduce bottlenecks and continuously improve in the department.
