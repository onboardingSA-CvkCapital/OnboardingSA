About the role

Centurion Property Bookkeeper Are you an analytical, methodical, and detail-oriented finance professional with experience in property administration? A well-established property company is looking for a dynamic Bookkeeper / Accountant to join their head office team on a full-time, permanent basis to manage end-to-end property finance administration alongside maintenance co-ordination. If you possess strong numerical acumen, excel at tenant accounts and cashbook reconciliations, and want to build a long-term career with an established organization, this is your opportunity. What’s on Offer? - Salary: Negotiable depending on experience and proven capabilities - Position Type: Full-Time, Permanent (after 3-month probation period) - Benefits: Standard company-aligned benefits and statutory contributions (Provident Fund compulsory contribution of 7.5% gross salary with an 8% employer contribution, annual December bonus, and 18 days annual leave) - Hours: Monday to Friday, standard commercial office hours (flexibility occasionally required for month-end reconciliations, billing cut-offs, or urgent maintenance projects) - Growth: Excellent mentorship, career progression, and stability within an established property network Key Responsibilities - Tenant Debtors & Billing: Maintain the tenant age analysis, execute rental/debt collections, generate monthly billing statements, prepare adjustment journals, handle reconciliations, and calculate tenant deposit refunds. - Utility Recoveries & Municipal Accounts: Administer utility recoveries and import tenant billing; reconcile municipal accounts against the general ledger; open and close municipal accounts; and control prepaid electricity payments via council portals. - Creditors & Cashbook Management: Execute full creditors functions on the property management system; manage daily ABSA bank downloads and upload to system ledgers; perform daily cashbook reconciliations; process bond accounts and Director credit card slips; and manage petty cash. - Audit Preparation: Assist the Financial Accountant with annual external audit preparations and resolve audit-related queries. - Property Maintenance Co-ordination: Assist with the planning and implementation of preventative maintenance schedules; process job cards; obtain comparative supplier quotes; track materials and stock; and monitor maintenance deadlines and budgets. - Stakeholder Liaison: Handle tenant queries and concerns professionally; deal with city councils; and liaise closely with contractors, suppliers, and internal maintenance teams. Are You the Perfect Fit? Minimum Requirements: - Education: Grade 12 / Matric PLUS a completed Degree, National Diploma, or Certificate in Accounting or a related financial field. - Experience: Minimum of 3 years’ proven experience in property administration, property bookkeeping, or a similar environment. - Software Proficiency: Advanced working knowledge of MS Excel; ability and willingness to learn specialized property software. - Licensing: Valid South African Driver’s License. - Core Attributes: Exceptional attention to detail, strong numerical and analytical skills, ability to use initiative, excellent interpersonal and communication skills, and a methodical, collaborative approach. Beneficial Skills & Experience: - Hands-on knowledge of MDA Property / MRI Property Central systems. - Experience with preventative maintenance planning schedules, contractor management, and project costing. Strict Legal Requirements & Documentation (Non-Negotiable): Background screening and verification checks will be conducted. Candidates must provide: - Valid South African ID (Proof of South African citizenship). - Valid Driver’s License. - Clear criminal record. - Clear ITC credit check (no judgments, defaults, or blacklistings). How to Apply Ready to take the next step in your property finance career? Ensure you have the following documentation ready: - Detailed CV (clearly outlining your property bookkeeping experience and software packages used). - ID Copy & Driver's License. - Matric Certificate & Tertiary Accounting Qualifications. - 3 Months Recent Payslips.
