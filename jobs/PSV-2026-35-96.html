About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Land Reform Specialist in Strategic Institutional Partnerships, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivered during office hours to: 600 Lilian Ngoyi Street (formerly known as Van der Walt Street), Berea Park, Pretoria, 0002 or by email to Post245@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/245 Closing date: 16 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian, White Male, and Coloured, Indian and White Female, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct quality assurance of submissions for legal compliance.
  • Review and assess submissions enroute to National Land Acquisition and Allocation Control Committee (NLAACC) and joint venture agreements, for legal and policy compliance.
  • Conduct verification that supporting documentation is complete and legally sufficient.
  • Verify alignment of agreements with applicable legislation, departmental policies, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
  • Identify legal, financial, governance, and operational risks contained in agreements and recommend corrective actions.
  • Prepare quality assurance reports with recommendations to correct deficiencies or achieve compliance for management approval.
  • Monitor implementation of quality assurance recommendations.
  • Provide technical advice on compliance and contract management matters.
  • Manage, analyse, investigate, and report on enquiries relating to complaints.
  • Receive, register, and acknowledge complaints and enquiries relating to Strategic Institutional Partnerships and land reform projects.
  • Conduct preliminary assessments and determine the nature and severity of complaints.
  • Gather and analyse relevant information, reports, agreements, and supporting documentation to identify root causes of disputes, complaints, and implementation challenges.
  • Conduct investigations through stakeholder consultations, interviews, and site visits where necessary.
  • Determining the legal, regulatory, and policy framework applicable to each matter.
  • Draft investigation reports with findings, conclusions, and recommendations.
  • Maintain accurate reports of complaints and case outcomes.
  • Provide advisory support to provinces regarding complaint management processes.
  • Manage informal dispute resolution processes.
  • Receive and assess disputes.
  • Facilitate engagement sessions between beneficiaries, strategic partners, communities, landowners, and government stakeholders.
  • Identify and analyse causes of conflicts arising from partnership agreements and project implementation.
  • Mediate and negotiate amicable resolutions to disputes before escalation to formal legal processes.
  • Develop dispute resolution strategies and action plans.
  • Ensure all parties are informed of their rights, roles, and obligations.
  • Draft settlement agreements and record outcomes of mediation processes.
  • Monitor compliance with agreed resolutions and remedial actions.
  • Escalate unresolved matters through appropriate governance and legal channels.
  • Prepare and maintain records and reports on disputes managed and resolved.
  • Promote stakeholder relations and collaborative problem-solving approaches.
  • Development of policy and operational documents.
  • Research and analyse policy gaps, implementation challenges, and emerging trends affecting Strategic Institutional Partnerships.
  • Draft, review, and update policies, frameworks, guidelines, SOPs, terms of reference, templates, and operational manuals.
  • Conduct benchmarking exercises against best practices and legislative requirements.
  • Facilitate stakeholder consultations during policy development processes.
  • Incorporate legal, governance, financial, and operational considerations into policy documents.
  • Prepare policy submissions, concept documents, discussion papers, and implementation plans.
  • Develop monitoring and evaluation mechanisms for policy implementation.
  • Coordinate policy approval processes and stakeholder engagements.
  • Review existing policies and recommend amendments where necessary.
  • Provide technical support and guidance on policy interpretation and implementation.
  • Training and capacitating provinces through workshops.
  • Identify training and capacity-building needs within provincial and district offices.
  • Develop training materials, presentations, manuals, toolkits, and guidelines.
  • Facilitate workshops on land acquisition SOP, land allocation SOP, policy implementation and joint venture agreements.
  • Conduct awareness sessions on legislative and policy developments.
  • Provide technical support and mentoring to provincial officials.
  • Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of training interventions.
  • Prepare workshop reports and recommendations for continuous improvement.
  • Coordinate stakeholder participation and logistics for workshops.
  • Build institutional capacity through knowledge sharing and best practice dissemination.
  • Prepare and maintain reports on the outcomes.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements:
  • A Grade 12 Certificate and a LLB or Bachelor of Commerce in Law
  • At least 3 years’ experience in the relevant filed
  • A valid driver’s licence
  • Job related knowledge:
  • Knowledge of contract management
  • Knowledge of public service management framework
  • Knowledge of policy development
  • Job related skills:
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Facilitation and presentation
  • Financial management
  • Team management
  • Conflict management
  • Problem solving
  • People management
  • Computer literacy (Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Project)
