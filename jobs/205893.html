About the role

About the Role Fidelity Services Group is looking for a hands-on Software Engineer to join our technology team. The successful candidate will design, develop and maintain secure, scalable and reliable software solutions that support business-critical operations. This role is suited to an experienced professional who enjoys solving complex technical problems, strengthening engineering practices and collaborating with stakeholders to deliver meaningful outcomes. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, test, debug, deploy and maintain high-quality software applications. • Translate business and stakeholder requirements into technical specifications and practical solutions. • Contribute to software architecture, focusing on scalability, performance, maintainability and extensibility. • Troubleshoot complex software defects and implement sustainable solutions. • Conduct code reviews and promote coding standards, testing practices and engineering discipline. • Develop and maintain technical specifications, system designs and supporting documentation. • Collaborate with product, design, quality assurance and other cross-functional teams. • Mentor junior engineers and contribute to continuous improvement and technology adoption. • Apply secure software development principles and support automated build and deployment practices. • Contribute to production reliability, monitoring, incident resolution and continuous service improvement. Minimum Requirements • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field. • Proven experience designing and developing complex software applications. • Strong proficiency in one or more programming languages, including Java, C#, Python, JavaScript or TypeScript. • Sound knowledge of software development methodologies, version control, integrated development environments and testing frameworks. • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, communication and collaboration skills. • Demonstrated ability to guide and mentor junior engineers. Advantageous Experience • Cloud environments. • Continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines. • Automated testing and observability. • Secure software development practices. • Production support and system reliability. Technology Exposure • Programming languages: Python, JavaScript, Java, C#, TypeScript. • Front-end: React, Angular, Vue.js. • Back-end: Node.js, Django, Ruby on Rails. • Mobile: Swift, Kotlin, Flutter. • Data and integration: SQL, NoSQL, API development, Microsoft Power Platform. Core Competencies Technical expertise, analytical thinking, attention to detail, collaborative delivery, adaptability, leadership and continuous learning. What We Offer The opportunity to work on meaningful, business-critical solutions in a collaborative environment that values engineering quality, knowledge sharing, innovation and continuous professional development. How to Apply Interested candidates should submit an updated CV and a brief summary of their relevant software engineering experience through the approved recruitment channel before the advertised closing date. If you are passionate about building dependable software, solving complex technical challenges and improving the way systems are delivered, we welcome your application. We reserve the right not to make an appointment to any advertised position. Whilst preference is given to existing employees at all times and the choice made is purely on merit, Historically Disadvantaged Candidates and Black Female Candidates are encouraged and supported in the FSG team. Fidelity Services Group (Pty) Ltd supports global fair practice and business ethics on continuous development of our human capital as the key resource to our success in the markets in which we participate. Should you not be contacted regarding this position within 10 working days from the closing date, please regard your application as unsuccessful.
