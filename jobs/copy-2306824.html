About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AIpowered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined, future-fit role. We are looking for passionate, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace datadriven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. If you are ready to be part of this next chapter, make a meaningful impact, and you are a suitably qualified and experienced Difference Maker wanting to go further on this exciting journey, you are encouraged to apply. Purpose of the job To manage the sustainability reporting, integrated reporting and thinking area in so far as influencing the standards and legislation, keeping members abreast of the latest developments, providing technical support to members and managing relationships with key stakeholders. Key/main outputs Output 1: Stakeholder engagement and management • Identification of key stakeholders. • Building and maintaining relationships with key stakeholders in terms of the sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking stakeholder management plan. • Engaging with key stakeholders in order to identify collaborative opportunities on issues of mutual interest. • Manage internal process to ensure deployed members to external structures properly represent the views and interests of SAICA and report back on the outcomes of stakeholders. • Represent SAICA on external structures and forums relevant to this role. This would include representation at both local and global structures. • Provide reports to the Head of Sustainability and Integrated Reporting and/or the technical committee on outcomes of stakeholder engagements. Output 2: External media and internal magazine • Formulate an external media plan, in consultation with Head of Sustainability and Integrated Reporting and Brand team. • Build reputation and influence through focused technical/thought leadership content, built on current affairs and market movements, also opening opportunities to profile members. • Collaborate with members and stakeholders on key research areas to ensure sustainability and integrated thinking concepts are central to decision making. • Ensure compilation of effective technical content for publication in SAICA’s accountancy magazine and other internal or external publication platforms. Engage with the media and speaking on public platforms adhering to SAICA’s Media Policy. Output 3: Keeping members abreast of the latest developments through the relevant communication channels, including media engagement • Continuously provide relevant and timeous updates to members and articles in a cost effective and innovative manner. • Proactively seek to engage with the media to raise awareness and ensure SAICA views remain relevant. • Manage the integrated thinking, sustainability and integrated reporting page of the SAICA website to ensure that it is kept up to date. • Reviewing and performing quality control, where relevant, on technical content relating sustainability matters for consumption by members/business community. • Identifying relevant sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking topics for the member publications such as newsletters. • Develop and implement communication plan, in consultation with Head of Sustainability and Integrated Reporting, to ensure that members are appropriately and timeously communicated with as it relates to sustainability matters. Output 4: Compiling submissions on proposed changes to standards, legislation and other relevant proposed pronouncements • Overseeing the sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking standard-setting activities within SAICA and identify relevant mechanisms to influence the local and global financial reporting standardsetting process. • Ensuring that submissions (reactive/proactive) on proposed sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/pronouncements (e.g. exposure drafts, discussion papers) are made to the relevant standardsetting bodies or regulators timeously. • Managing the technical structures and committee processes involved in influencing the standard-setting process. • Conduct research, where relevant, that informs submissions. • Perform quality control over submissions made to standard-setting bodies/regulators. Output 5: To develop technical guidance such as guides or FAQs • Review relevant new and proposed sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking pronouncements through consultation with the relevant financial reporting structures. • Identify issues affecting the profession and members and perform a needs analysis. The outcome of this analysis may necessitate the development of a technical resource. • Manage and/or conduct research that informs the development of the technical resource. • Work closely with the technical committee in achieving the issuance of the guidance/technical resource. • Ensure that quality assurance processes are in place that provide a level of comfort on the quality of the technical resources issued prior to publication. Output 6: To manage the technical query system • Manage and/or review the sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking queries. • Ensure that queries are responded to timeously. • Manage the technical query system in so far as it relates to sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking by performing a quality control review on the relevant queries. Output 7: Planning and delivery of sustainability events • Identify and validate members and stakeholders needs. • Identify the relevant sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking experts for SAICA seminars/workshops. • Develop the sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking CPD plan, in consultation with the Head of Sustainability and Integrated Reporting. • Implement the sustainability reporting and integrated reporting/thinking CPD plan. • Develop and implement the marketing plan for the identified CPD interventions, in consultation with the Brand team. • Present and/or facilitate the events. Output 8: Committee Management • Oversee the sustainability committee and ensure that is effectively managed. • Ensure that structures are managed in line with the internal governance processes. • Manage the implementation of the structure’s work plan and outputs. • Ensure that structures are regularly kept up to date on key developments.
