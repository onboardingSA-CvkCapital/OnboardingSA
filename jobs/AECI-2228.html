About the role

AECI is hiring a SS&C Specialist: Materials. Responsible for driving the execution of sourcing strategies and leading strategic procurement initiatives across assigned Materials, Packaging and Moulded components categories within AECI Mining. Plays a critical part in delivering sustainable cost optimisation, managing supplier performance, and supporting the development of category strategies, while partnering and working cross functionally with internal stakeholders to deliver measurable business value. Work closely with Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Operations, Technical, Commercial, and Quality stakeholders to ensure security of supply, cost competitiveness, supplier performance and continuous improvement across the portfolio. Accountable for supporting category strategy execution, leading sourcing projects, managing supplier relationships, negotiating commercial agreements, and identifying opportunities for cost reduction, innovation, localisation and operational improvement.

Responsibilities

  • Development of sourcing strategies
  • Develop strategic sourcing approaches for managed materials
  • Execute approved category strategies for Materials, Packaging, and Moulded components
  • Lead sourcing activities for materials and packaging commodities to ensure operational requirements are met
  • Drive end-to-end sourcing activities, including RFx processes, supplier evaluations, commercial negotiations, and supplier selection
  • Support the development and continuous refinement of category strategies through market intelligence and demand insights
  • Conduct ongoing spend analysis, market and supplier research to identify opportunities, risks, and alternative supply sources
  • Continuously monitor supplier markets, risks and availability to support operational continuity
  • Identify alternative suppliers and sourcing opportunities to improve security of supply and responsiveness
  • Provide input into Procurement dashboards and tracking tools
  • Support procurement planning through spend analysis, demand forecasting and supplier insights
  • Proactively identify and mitigate supply and commercial risks within assigned categories
  • Ensure procurement activities align with operational, maintenance and shutdown requirements
  • Cost Optimisation and Value Delivery
  • Conduct detailed spend analysis for managed raw materials to identify cost-saving opportunities, demand drivers, and initiatives to reduce operational inefficiencies and procurement lead times
  • Identify, develop, and implement cost-saving and value-creation initiatives aligned to procurement targets
  • Support the implementation of category-led initiatives, to ensure value delivery and in line with procurement targets
  • Apply total cost of ownership (TCO) principles to enable optimal sourcing decisions
  • Perform cost modelling, scenario analysis, and should-cost modelling using market and supplier data
  • Support standardisation and optimisation initiatives across engineering and maintenance procurement
  • Track and report on savings delivery and benefits realisation.
  • Support optimisation of raw materials holdings to balance operational reliability and working capital requirements
  • Identify opportunities to reduce emergency procurement through improved planning, supplier alignment and sourcing strategies
  • Supplier relationships management
  • Build and maintain a qualified supplier base for managed raw materials spend
  • Manage supplier performance across quality, cost, service delivery and responsiveness
  • Support supplier segmentation and contribute to supplier category and/ or diversification strategies
  • Monitor supplier performance through defined KPIs (cost, quality, delivery, service, sustainability) and drive continuous improvement initiatives
  • Lead supplier performance reviews and implement corrective actions where required
  • Drive supplier innovation and continuous improvement
  • Stakeholder engagement and collaboration
  • Partner with internal stakeholders (Operations, Commercial, Finance, S&OP, R&D) to support plant reliability and operational continuity
  • Support shutdown and turnaround planning through sourcing readiness, supplier coordination and critical materials availability
  • Contribute to S&OP processes and support demand planning inputs for key suppliers
  • Provide data-driven insights and recommendations to support business needs
  • Continuously ensure alignment between procurement activities and operational requirements
  • Support budgeting processes through accurate spend and cost analysis

  • Contracts management
