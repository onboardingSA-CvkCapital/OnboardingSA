About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Director: Water and Sanitation Services Support in Water and Sanitation Services Management: NW Cd: Provincial Operations: Northwest, based in Mmabatho. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 271126/02 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic leadership and business planning for the directorate.
  • Advice top management and the legislature as well as relevant sector bodies on policies and strategies relevant to the water and sanitation services outputs.
  • Facilitate the rendering of support to water services institutions.
  • Ensure the promotion of department sector support strategy.
  • Promotion of intergovernmental relations.
  • Coordinated the attendance and representation to the provincial meetings (Provincial Coordinating Forums, District Forums, Municipal Forums).
  • And develop reports and presentations for such meetings.
  • Coordination, development and implementation of disaster management plans Effective planning and support of the water sector.
  • Provide planning and support for the water sector through provisions of technical and engineering support with IDP, WSDP, MIG and where possible on RBIG and WSIG.
  • Develop water and sanitation master plan in the province.
  • Provide support and guidance to WSAs regarding technical engineering of Operations and Maintenance on water and sanitation infrastructure services.
  • Perform and coordinate comprehensive investigations on water and sanitation challenges within WSAs.
  • To support Implementation of Sanitation services.
  • ENQURIES: Mr L Mditshane Tel No: (018) 387 9504.

Requirements

  • A relevant NQF level 7 qualification in Civil Engineering or Natural Science.
  • 5 years’ experience at middle/senior management level.
  • Five (5) to 10 years’ experience in water services management environment.
  • Successful completion of the Pre-entry Certificate for the Senior Management Service (SMS) as endorsed by the National School of Government.
  • Knowledge of water engineering and technical business planning.
  • Understanding of project management and principles.
  • Knowledge of strategic planning resource allocation and human resources.
  • Knowledge of Public Service Act, Public Finance Management Act and regulations.
  • Knowledge Technical report writing and presentation skills.
