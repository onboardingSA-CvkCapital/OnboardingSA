About the role

The Department of Health (Limpopo) is hiring a Deputy Director-General: Tertiary, Specialised and Health Care Support Services, based in Provincial Office [Polokwane]. This is a permanent post, paying R1,885,710 a year. How to apply: The circular of advertised vacant posts will be posted on the following websites: https://erecruitment.limpopo.gov.za/, www.ldoh.gov.za, www.dpsa.gov.za and Provincial Department social media. Applicants must apply on the following website: https://erecruitment.limpopo.gov.za/ Closing date: 06 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide leadership and strategic direction and policy in the branch.
  • Manage the coordination of tertiary, specialised and health care support services.
  • Manage the provision of forensic medical services.
  • Manage the provision of specialised and private health services.
  • Manage and utilise human resources in accordance with relevant directives and legislation.

Requirements

  • Qualifications and Experience
  • A National Senior Certificate/Grade 12/Matric plus an appropriate recognized postgraduate health related degree or equivalent qualification at NQF level 8 as recognised by the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA)
  • Eight [8] years’ experience in Senior Management
  • Proven experience of the health sector
  • Good background in service delivery, turn-around and change management strategy
  • Computer literacy including MS Outlook, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, Literacy in virtual meeting platforms
  • A valid driver's license, except for people with disabilities
  • Knowledge And Skills
  • Proven service delivery track record
  • Interacting with policy makers and key players in Health Care
  • Sound knowledge of the research methodology
  • Knowledge of all Health and related Acts and Regulations
  • Strategic Capability and Leadership
  • People Management and Empowerment
  • Programme and Project Management
  • Financial Management
  • Change Management
  • Knowledge Management
  • Service Delivery Innovation [SDI]
  • Problem Solving and Analysis
  • Client Orientation and Customer Focus
  • Communication Personal attributes
  • Responsiveness
