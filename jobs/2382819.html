About the role

Roles and Responsibilities • Create customer invoices from warehouse shipments confirmed in Acumatica. • Ensure invoices agree with authorised customer orders, confirmed quantities, agreed prices and applicable VAT. • Process customer receipts and allocate payments according to remittances. • Process approved credit notes, debit notes, reversals, refunds and write-offs. • Ensure no credit note, debit note, refund or write-off is processed without complete supporting documentation and prior written management approval. • Maintain accurate cashbooks and complete regular bank reconciliations. • Investigate and resolve reconciling items and financial discrepancies promptly. • Process approved payroll journals received from HR. • Process month-end journals, accruals, depreciation and other approved accounting entries. • Reconcile the total debtors and creditors ageings to the relevant general ledger control accounts. • Reconcile inventory valuation to the general ledger. • Maintain the fixed-assets register. • Review supplier reconciliations, payment schedules and supporting payment packs. • Review unusual, high-value or unsupported transactions and escalate discrepancies to management. • Complete month-end closing and review the trial balance. • Prepare and submit accurate monthly management accounts and financial reports to management. • Prepare monthly cash-flow reports and forecasts. • Report on debtors, creditors, inventory, cost of sales, wages, recurring commitments and unusual financial movements. • Ensure financial transactions are supported by the required documentation in Acumatica. • Maintain accurate and complete accounting records in accordance with company SOPs. • Assist the auditors by providing requested accounting records, reconciliations and supporting documentation. • Identify weaknesses in financial processes and recommend improvements to internal controls. • Perform other reasonable finance-related duties assigned by management.
