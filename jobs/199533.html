About the role

Ready to kickstart your career in Inventory, Procurement & Supply Chain? Frogfoot is looking for an Inventory & Procurement Intern to join our Finance team and gain hands-on experience across inventory management, procurement administration, stock control, supplier coordination and reporting. If you're organised, detail-oriented, analytical and eager to learn, this is your opportunity to gain real-world experience, develop your skills and build a strong foundation for your career in Supply Chain, Procurement or Inventory Management. If you're ready to hop into the pond, learn new skills and make an impact, we'd love to meet you! About Frogfoot Frogfoot is a leading open-access fibre infrastructure provider in South Africa. We build and operate fibre networks that connect homes, businesses and communities, working with multiple Internet Service Providers to deliver reliable connectivity across the country. We're passionate about connecting people, developing talent and creating opportunities for individuals to grow their careers. Purpose of the Role The Inventory & Procurement Intern will provide administrative, operational and reporting support to the Procurement and Inventory function. You'll gain practical experience in stock management, procurement processes, purchase orders, supplier administration, stocktakes, reconciliations, reporting and ERP systems, while working alongside experienced professionals. This role is designed to develop your administrative, analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills while giving you practical exposure to the day-to-day world of procurement and inventory management.
