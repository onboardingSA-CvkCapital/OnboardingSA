About the role

Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company, providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the farm-foods for consumers. Apart from the various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now also operate in all areas of the food supply chain: processing, logistics, and national and global market channels. This “house of brands” we’ve established with the support of our farmer shareholders is the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. Receiving Clerk Location: Magogong Join a team that’s hungry, humble, smart, bold — and plays to win together. About the Role We’re looking for a detail-driven Receiving Clerk who takes pride in getting things right the first time. You’ll be responsible for ensuring stock is received, recorded, and controlled accurately, while delivering excellent service to our clients. If you’re organized, reliable, and enjoy working in a dynamic agricultural environment — this is your chance to make an impact where it counts. At VKB, we believe in people who are hungry to grow, humble in teamwork, smart in execution, and bold in thinking. If that sounds like you — we’d love to meet you.
