About the role

Faircape Health forms part of the Faircape Group, a diversified group of companies with interests across Healthcare, Retirement Living, Property Management, Construction, Renewable Energy, Water Utilities, Telecommunications, and related services. As part of our continued innovation in healthcare delivery, Faircape Health is developing a Longevity and Preventative Health Centre, focused on extending healthspan through advanced diagnostics, preventative medicine, personalised health optimisation, and evidence-based lifestyle interventions. The centre will provide structured preventative health services to individuals seeking to better understand, optimise, and maintain their long-term health through comprehensive medical assessments, advanced diagnostics, and personalised health strategies aimed at reducing disease risk and supporting healthy ageing. We are expanding our team and seeking an experienced and forward-thinking Medical Officer – Longevity & Preventative Medicine to join our growing clinical team. Working alongside our Clinical Lead – Longevity & Preventative Medicine, the successful candidate will provide direct clinical care within the longevity programme, conducting comprehensive health assessments, interpreting diagnostic and biomarker data, identifying health risks, and developing personalised, evidence-based strategies to support long-term health and disease prevention. This role is well suited to a doctor with a strong interest in preventative medicine, cardiometabolic health, healthy ageing, and evidence-based health optimisation, who would like to practise within an innovative and multidisciplinary healthcare environment.
