About the role

PURPOSE OF ROLE The Project QC Inspector is responsible for ensuring adherence to quality requirements and promoting a strong quality culture among fellow employees. Forming part of the Project Management team, the role supports quality initiatives that drive best practice and continuous improvement in line with business objectives. The successful candidate will oversee the implementation of Quality Assurance (QA) and Quality Control (QC) measures appropriate to the Industrial Division's project execution, supporting Project and Construction Management, suppliers, and subcontractors in delivering compliant, high-quality outcomes. KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS • Implement Quality Control functions and activities across a project, including inspection schedules and the collection of Quality Records. • Perform vendor inspections and audits, and report on findings. • Support Project and Construction Managers in dealing with clients on quality-related matters. • Assess implementation of Quality Plans and Quality Control Plans (QCPs/ITPs) on site, and with fabricators, suppliers, and sub-contractors. • Evaluate qualifications of welders, NDT technicians, and sub-contractor inspection personnel. • Coordinate tracking systems for correct identification of materials and consumables, and oversee equipment calibration. • Evaluate and approve Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS), Procedure Qualification Records (PQR), and Welding Personnel Qualification Records (WPQR). • Identify, analyse, and close out Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs) in collaboration with the Project Manager and Engineering. • Review, update, and issue QMS procedures, templates, and work instructions, and monitor changes to applicable standards. • Plan, conduct, and participate in internal, supplier, and sub-contractor audits; track and close out findings and corrective actions. • Perform root cause analysis (RCA) and verify effectiveness of corrective actions; identify opportunities for improvement. • Conduct quality awareness training and coach construction leads and project teams on quality requirements and best practice. • Compile quality performance metrics, maintain department documents and registers, and prepare inputs for SHEQ management review meetings. QUALIFICATION • Technical or Quality Management qualification, or equivalent (essential). • Minimum SAIW Level 2 Diploma in Quality Management, or equivalent (essential). • Qualification or extensive experience in site civil works, or mechanical fitting and assembly (preferred). • ISO 9001 Auditor qualification or similar (advantageous). • Qualification or experience in the EC&I field (beneficial). EXPERIENCE • Minimum 5 to 7 years' experience in an EPC / engineering and construction environment in a Quality Inspector or Quality Management role. • Experience in Fabrication, Construction, and Maintenance within Petrochemical, Power Generation, or Industrial Plant/Building fields preferred. • Demonstrated experience reviewing and implementing quality management systems and procedures. • Experience in audit management, corrective action processes, and Root Cause Analysis (RCA) methodology. • Experience compiling and reviewing Data Books, Quality Control Plans (QCPs/ITPs), and sub-contractor documentation. • Experience with Electrical and Instrumentation installations, and on remote cross-border projects, advantageous. • Valid driver's licence required. COMPETENCIES Knowledge • ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 requirements, and ISO 19011 auditing principles. • Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) project environments. • Regulatory and quality governance frameworks, and Electronic Document Management Systems (EDMS). • Compilation of QCPs, ITPs, Data Dossiers, project-specific Quality Plans, tracker registers, and QC meeting minutes. • Computer literate (Word, Excel, PowerPoint).Behaviour • High level of integrity, professionalism, and accountability. • Strong attention to detail and a methodical approach. • Self-motivated with the ability to work independently. • Confident subject-matter expert with sound judgement. • Collaborative team player who builds strong stakeholder relationships. • Proactive, forward-thinking, and solution-orientated. • Demonstrates and promotes a strong Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality culture.Skills • Quality system development and implementation. • Internal project and external sub-contractor auditing. • Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and problem-solving techniques. • Strong written and verbal communication, with stakeholder engagement and training delivery. • Proficiency in MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Adobe Acrobat, and project systems (e.g. IFS, Primavera, MS Project). • Experience in data analytics and data management advantageous.
