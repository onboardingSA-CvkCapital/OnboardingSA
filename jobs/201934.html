About the role

Pepkor Lifestyle is seeking a Senior Functional BlueSword Logistics Consultant who will be responsible for the functional design, implementation, integration, and support of BlueSword logistics solutions within a complex SAP warehouse and logistics environment. The role requires a strong background in SAP Warehouse Management and Logistics, combined with the ability to understand end-to-end warehouse processes and translate operational requirements into effective BlueSword solutions. The consultant will work closely with warehouse operations, SAP functional teams, technical teams, integration teams, vendors, and business stakeholders to ensure that BlueSword solutions integrate effectively with the broader SAP landscape. The consultant will lead requirement unpacking and solution design workshops, produce functional specifications, support configuration and integration activities, coordinate testing, and provide 3rd level support for incidents and problems. The role will also participate in projects, change requests, continuous improvement initiatives, and warehouse transformation programmes. The Senior Functional BlueSword Logistics Consultant will act as a subject matter expert for BlueSword-related warehouse and logistics processes while applying strong SAP warehouse management knowledge to ensure solutions are scalable, supportable, and aligned with operational requirements.
