About the role

LiquidCapital is a world leader in its field of specialisation offering a variety of financial products for motor vehicles. LiquidCapital aims to creatively solve problems and add value to people by ensuring Mobility for Good. LiquidCapital forms part of the Motus Group, a leading JSE listed Company in the Automotive Industry where performance is driven by three keystones, 1. Leaders, 2. Innovation & 3. Customer Champions. LiquidCapital is seeking to employ a qualified and experienced Executive Personal Assistant (PA). The successful candidate will join a dynamic and highly skilled Executive Team. The successful candidate will report directly to the Managing Director. PURPOSE:The Executive Personal Assistant provides high-level, confidential and proactive administrative and operational support to Executives, ensuring the effective management of the Executives' diaries, correspondence, meetings, travel, documentation, stakeholder engagements and day-to-day priorities. The role requires a highly organised, professional and resourceful individual who can anticipate the needs of the Executives, manage competing priorities, exercise sound judgement and maintain strict confidentiality when dealing with sensitive business, financial, employee and strategic information. The Executive Personal Assistant acts as a key point of coordination between the Executives, internal stakeholders, clients, service providers and other external parties, ensuring that commitments, deadlines and follow-ups are effectively managed. The ideal candidate will be a highly experienced, polished and proactive Executive Personal Assistant who is accustomed to operating in a demanding corporate environment and supporting a senior executives. They must be capable of working with a high degree of independence, confidentiality and discretion, while remaining approachable and service-oriented. The successful candidate should naturally anticipate requirements, stay ahead of deadlines and priorities, and ensure that the Executive's time is managed effectively.
