About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies in Midrand has an opportunity available for a Group Debtors Manager to join the Finance team. The main purpose of this role will be to lead the Group Debtors function by driving effective debt collection, accurate billing, revenue recovery, account administration, medical aid claims management, stakeholder engagement, financial reporting, and compliance. Accountable for maintaining healthy debtor books, improving cash flow, reducing aged debt, strengthening controls, managing key stakeholders and leading teams to achieve departmental objectives in line with company policies, and governance standards.
