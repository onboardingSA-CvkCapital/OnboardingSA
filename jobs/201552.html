About the role

About the Role: To Obtain Profit Contribution by Managing Staff, Establishing & Accomplishing Business Objectives & Ensuring Compliance with Service Level Agreements (SLAs) Awareness and Compliance with the Company’s Quality, Health & Safety and Environmental Management Systems.
