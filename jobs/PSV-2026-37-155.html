About the role

The Department of Human Settlements (Gauteng) is hiring a Director: Organisational Efficiency in Corporate Management Services, based in Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Please apply online at https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: REFS/056668 Closing date: 13 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: The Department is committed to achieving employment equity and diversity in accordance with its approved Employment Equity Plan. Preference will be given to suitably qualified Coloured (Male & Female), White female, African male and Persons with disability (PwD) candidates in order to advance the Department’s Employment Equity objectives and address identified underrepresentation at the relevant occupational level.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and monitor the implementation of Operations Strategy and Design.
  • Manage and monitor the development of Standard Operating Procedures and Business Process for the department.
  • Develop change management and communication strategies to ensure smooth transition of employees.
  • Initiate and manage Organisational Design.
  • Plan, implement, monitor and institutionalise Service Delivery Improvement programmes.
  • Manage the implementation of the Diversity Management programmes in the department.
  • Assess and analyse Service Delivery Model to identify service improvements by identifying resource and functional alignment inefficiencies.
  • Manage the development and implementation of human resource strategy and plan.
  • Manage the development and implementation of Employment Equity Plans.
  • Manage resources of the unit (human resources, finance, assets)

Requirements

  • Matric plus an undergraduate qualification (NQF7) in Public Administration/Business Management/Human Resource Management/Production/Operations Management/Management Services as recognised by SAQA.
  • At least 5 years relevant experience at middle/senior managerial level in Organisational Development/Service Delivery Improvement/Human Resource Planning & Systems environment or related field.
  • Possession of the SMS Pre-Entry Certificate (required prior to appointment).
  • Possession of a valid driver’s license.
  • Knowledge:
  • Public Service Act and Regulations
  • Labour Relations Act of 1995
  • Occupational Health and Safety Act
  • Employment Equity Act
  • Basic Conditions of Employment Act
  • Public Finance Management Act and Treasury Regulations
  • Job Evaluation and organisational design principles
  • Certification as a Job Evaluation Analyst or Panel will be an added advantage
  • Competencies:
  • Strategic capability and Leadership
  • People Management and empowerment
  • Programme and Project Management
  • Financial Management and Change Management
  • Problem solving and analysis, Client orientation and customer focus, Service Delivery Innovation
  • Skills:
  • Good written and verbal communication skills
  • Planning, organising and execution skills
  • Knowledge management
  • Negotiation skills
  • Change management
  • Language proficiency and ability to manage conflict
