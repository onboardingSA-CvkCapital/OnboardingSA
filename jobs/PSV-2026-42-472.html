Responsibilities

  • Reporting to the Chief Director, the incumbent will be responsible for leading and coordinating the provision of financial management support, oversight and capacity-building initiatives to municipalities and municipal entities within the assigned district (Bojanala District Municipality, Dr Ruth Segomotsi Mompati District Municipality, or Ngaka Modiri Molema District Municipality).
  • The incumbent will drive the implementation of the Municipal Finance Management Act (MFMA), municipal financial reforms and related governance initiatives aimed at strengthening municipal financial sustainability, accountability, compliance and service delivery outcomes.
  • Key responsibilities include: Manage and enhance efficient and effective financial management within municipalities and municipal entities in the district.
  • Lead and coordinate support to municipalities in the areas of budgeting, financial planning, revenue management, expenditure management, accounting, financial reporting, financial asset management and liability management.
  • Manage the implementation, monitoring and evaluation of municipal budgeting frameworks and processes, ensuring compliance with applicable legislative and regulatory requirements.
  • Provide strategic guidance and support to municipalities on budget preparation, implementation, adjustments, funding compliance and long-term financial sustainability.
  • Oversee the monitoring, analysis and reporting of municipal financial performance, financial health and service delivery outcomes.
  • Manage and promote compliance with Generally Recognised Accounting Practice (GRAP), MFMA reporting requirements, Treasury Regulations and other applicable financial management prescripts.
  • Provision of support in the implementation and monitoring of municipal revenue enhancement programmes, debt management initiatives, cash flow management strategies and other measures aimed at improving financial sustainability.
  • Manage the monitoring and evaluation of municipal financial asset and liability management practices and provide support to strengthen financial controls.
  • Provide strategic oversight and support on Supply Chain Management (SCM) governance, compliance, performance improvement initiatives and implementation of SCM reforms.
  • Monitor municipal compliance with financial management legislation, regulations, norms and standards and ensure appropriate corrective measures are implemented.
  • Oversee the monitoring and implementation of audit action plans, internal control improvements and remedial actions arising from Auditor-General findings, oversight recommendations and governance interventions.
  • Support municipalities in strengthening institutional capacity through targeted financial management support, mentoring, training and capacity-building programmes.
  • Provide strategic advice and recommendations to municipal leadership and Provincial Treasury on emerging financial risks, governance challenges and intervention requirements.
  • Lead, develop and manage personnel within the District to promote a high-performance culture, accountability, innovation and service excellence.
  • Manage the Directorate.
  • Provide administrative support and manage human resources and financial resources.

Requirements

  • As a minimum a Bachelor’s Degree or Advanced Diploma in Finance/Financial Management/Economics/Accounting or equivalent NQF 7 qualification.
  • 5 years’ experience at a middle/senior managerial level in public or private sector finance environments.
  • Experience in municipal finance environment will be an added advantage.
  • A pre-entry certificate for SMS is compulsory.
  • No appointment will be made without such a certificate.
  • A valid driver’s license as it will be expected to travel.
  • Ability to write reports, analyse financial statements, compile multi-year budgets, annual financial statements and cash flows.
  • Computer literacy.
  • Good communication skills (written and spoken).
  • Interpersonal skills.
  • Client orientation and customer focus.
  • Willingness to be placed at any district in the Province as directed by Provincial Treasury.
