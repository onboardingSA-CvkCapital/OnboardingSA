About the role

The Department of Co-Operative Governance and Traditional Affairs (Gauteng) is hiring a Deputy Director: Municipal Performance Monitoring, Evaluation and Reporting (Municipal Property Rates Compliance) in Municipal Finance Monitoring and Evaluation, based in Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Applications should be submitted strictly online at https://jobs.gauteng.gov.za/Public/Jobs.aspx For attention: Human Capital management Reference number to quote: REFS/057053 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Management of municipal compliance and implementation of the Municipal Property Rates Amendment Act (MPRAA): Monitor compliance and conduct assessment on municipal calendar events in terms of MPRAA.
  • Manage, process, and advise on all applications, submissions and requests submitted to the department for condonations.
  • Manage, process, and advise on all applications, submissions and requests submitted to the department for the extension of the validity of the municipal general valuation rolls.
  • Advise municipalities on corrective measures where non-compliance with MPRAA is identified.
  • Assessment of municipal property rates policies:
  • Conduct assessment of draft and approved municipal property rates policies
  • Provide feedback on findings and recommendations to municipalities on the outcomes of the assessment of municipal property rates policies
  • Conduct workshops with municipalities and VABs on compliance and implementation of the Municipal Property Rates Amendment Act: Compile quarterly reports on the progress made on the implementation on the MPRAA and issues emanating from the VABs.
  • Monitor and report on the resolutions taken from the MPRAA workshops.
  • Management of stakeholders in terms of MPRAA related issues: Respond to various MPRAA related questions posed by the different stakeholders and oversight bodies including the Gauteng Provincial Legislature.
  • Work with relevant stakeholders on MPRAA related issues i.e Property Rates related organisations.
  • Liaise with the legal representatives/Senior State Counsel on Court issues about MPRAA and manage the outcome of such queries and results.
  • Management of sub-unit:
  • Manage human resources in terms of training and development
  • Management of performance management within the sub-unit
  • Management of sub-unit’s annual performance and operational plan
  • Risk management within the sub-unit and Conflict management

Requirements

  • Matric plus NQF level 7/Degree in Property Studies, Real Estate and Property Estate Management or relevant qualification recognised by SAQA.
  • Coupled with at least 3 – 5 years’ experience in property management environment.
  • At least 3 years at supervisory (Assistant Director) level and 5 years’ experience in property management environment.
  • Competencies:
  • Real estate
  • Auditing
  • Project management
  • Data management
  • Planning and research
  • Data collection
  • Management and analysis
  • Report writing
  • Analytical skills
  • Problem solving
  • Communication
  • Presentation
  • Coordination
  • Negotiation
  • Managing conflict
  • Financial management
  • Strong computer literacy
  • Monitoring
  • Sound knowledge of the Municipal Property Rates Amendment Act (MPRAA) and its supporting regulations as well as other related local government legislation
  • Understanding of public sector environment provincial and national legislation (DPSA Act & Regulations, PFMA), sound and interpretation of directives including managerial skills.
  • A valid drivers’ license.
