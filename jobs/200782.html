About the role

Job Purpose To translate Renault South Africa's business and Marketing & Communications strategy into integrated brand, product marketing and communications plans that strengthen brand equity, increase customer consideration and support commercial growth. Reporting directly to the Vice President: Marketing & Communications, the General Manager is responsible for leading the planning and execution of brand management, product marketing, advertising, PR and communications, sponsorships and partnerships, activations, dealer marketing and relevant agency relationships. The role ensures that strategic priorities are converted into high-quality, coordinated and measurable marketing execution across the business. The role requires a senior marketing leader who combines strong strategic capability with executional excellence, commercial awareness, consumer insight and the ability to lead teams and agencies to consistently deliver against agreed business and marketing objectives. Minimum Experience • 8–10 years' experience in marketing, brand management, communications or integrated marketing, with demonstrated experience at senior management level. • Proven experience developing and implementing brand and integrated marketing strategies. • Experience managing agencies, significant marketing budgets and multi-channel campaigns. • Experience in automotive or another competitive consumer environment advantageous. • Experience within a multinational or global brand environment advantageous.Minimum Qualification • Bachelor’s Degree / Diploma or equivalent Marketing
