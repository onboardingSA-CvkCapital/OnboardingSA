About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Land Reform Advisor in Tenure Reform Implementation, based in Mpumalanga (Mbombela). This is a contract post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 7th Floor Block E, Bateleur Office Park Building, 17 Van Rensburg Street, Mbombela, 1200 or by email to Post389@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/389 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian and White Males and African, Coloured, Indian and White Females and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Referral of Labour Tenants cases to Court.
  • Evaluate if the project is ready for referral.
  • Engage Project Officers, Landowner and Labour Tenant applicants.
  • Compile referral package for approval by the Director: Tenure Reform Implementation.
  • Liaise with National Programme Manager
  • Labour Tenants/Deputy Director: Labour Tenants for further screening of referrals
  • Liaise with State Attorney and the appointed Attorney when referring cases to the Court
  • Assist in the provision of advice/review of sale agreements, land acquisition transactions and legal advice
  • Evaluate/advice on all the sale agreements
  • Evaluate all the land acquisition, donation, certification and disposal submissions before presentation at Provincial Technical Committee and Quality Assurance.
  • Receiving legal instructions from internal clients and stakeholders.
  • Conduct legal research to provide advice on the legal process and agreements.
  • Analyse and formulate a legal opinion.
  • Provide legal reports and submissions.
  • Assist with the establishment of Legal Entities.
  • Facilitate the establishment of legal entities.
  • Conduct legal entity workshop.
  • Draft Communal Property Association (CPA) Constitutions or Trust Deeds for land reform beneficiaries.
  • Facilitation of adoption of the Constitutions/Trust Deeds and election of Committees/Trustees, Compile submissions for registration of the CPAs and or Trusts.
  • Liaise with the Registrar's offices (High Court/CPI).
  • Assist in the provision of litigation support for the Labour Tenants.
  • Draft referrals.
  • Issue notice of instruction to appoint State Attorney.
  • Attend court and attend pre-trials.
  • Serve referrals on interested parties.
  • File the notice of referrals.
  • Facilitate the implementation of Court orders.
  • Assist with Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA) responses.
  • Co-ordinate the PAIA request and response.
  • Review PAIA responses from the districts before submission to the Chief Information Officer.
  • Ensure adherence to the PAIA prescripts.
  • Disseminate the requested information after approval by the Chief Information Officer.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements
  • Applicants must be in a possession of a Grade 12 Certificate and a Bachelor's Degree in LLB/B-URIS or related equivalent qualification (NQF Level 7)
  • At least 2 years working experience in the Land Reform environment
  • A valid driver's licence
  • Job related knowledge
  • Knowledge of land reform policy, land law and other relevant acts and legislative prescript
  • Contract management
  • Knowledge of the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA)
  • Understanding of all land reform programmes
  • Civil and commercial law and its application
  • Job related skills
  • Interpersonal relations
  • Planning and organising
  • Conflict management
  • Problem solving
  • Analytical thinking
  • Computer literacy
  • Report writing ability
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Willingness to travel
