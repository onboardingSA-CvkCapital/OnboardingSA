About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Snr Specialist Ops Inclusive Procurement, based in Rustenburg, North West. Manage and ensure the operational implementation of a strategic programme on Inclusive Procurement ambitions and drive positive socio-economic impact within our host communities. Safety: Create a safety culture through productive and authorized operational systems/processes that allow all employees (including contracting partners) to provide their outputs in a risk-controlled environment. Oversee consistent application of Safety & Health principles in all discipline interactions and take personal responsibility for safety of self and others to ensure zero harm and elimination of fatalities. Performance and Delivery People & Teams Compliance

Responsibilities

  • Provide Inclusive Procurement contributions to the overall Supply Chain business strategy, particularly pertaining to creative partnering and robust supplier development.
  • Embrace an agile Supply Chain by being adaptive and flexible to accommodate and focus on emerging and changing business priorities.
  • Practical support and change management at business level, with a specific focus on supporting Host Community suppliers and other SMME's/new entrants.
  • Ensure correct and appropriate application and interpretation of IP requirements.
  • Contribute to the overall community and IP strategy.
  • Support the implementation and progress monitoring of the strategic programmes.
  • Support the implementation of change, engagement & communication plans that will support the effective deployment of IP strategy.
  • Provide area-specific input to the overall Inclusive Procurement scorecard and targets and support the tracking of performance against these at business level.
  • Support the development of a future demand pipeline that will guide supplier development, initiatives, performance etc.
  • Support asset-based implementation of IP initiatives in agreements by providing guidance to asset-based teams.
  • Provide input to category portfolio and value delivery planning, support category IP strategy and sourcing plan development, commercial & initiative plans and agreements to ensure formal commitment are embedded.
  • Support the implementation of new ways of working related to IP at asset level.
  • Contribute to selection of host community suppliers for development programmes and tenders (Take part in RFI process).
  • Provide input to gap analysis of host community supplier capacity.
  • Asset based oversight of established partnerships (incl. JV's) between large suppliers and HC suppliers.
  • Support the implementation of IP initiatives at the asset, raise any issues to commercial teams and provide progress feedback.
  • Effect continuous growth in number of suitable IP suppliers (HC or otherwise) within relevant areas.
  • Facilitate early escalation of on-site issues relating to host community suppliers.
  • Identify barriers and/development opportunities related to host community suppliers and propose solutions.
  • Educate and coach asset-based teams on regulatory requirements as well as the Company position on these.
  • Manage the implementation of social & sustainability strategies and initiatives that relate to Inclusive Procurement and how these contribute to the sustainable mine plans.
  • Supporting the implementation of the Company policy and standards on Communities and Inclusive Procurement, reflecting our sustainability, inclusion & diversity ambitions as well as any other national imperatives. Support engagement of host community suppliers & business forums, relevant local governmental bodies.
  • Manage the target setting, approval, monitoring and reporting of SLP IP targets for the operation. Provide support during the SLP IP audits from the regulatory authority.
  • Implement a purpose-driven, high-performing culture that is aligned with the company values, is inclusive and promotes diversity, supports engagement and fosters continuous improvement to work processes.
  • Ensure compliance with internal and external regulatory, legislative and permitting requirements.
