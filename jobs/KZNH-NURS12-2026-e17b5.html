About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring a Professional Nurse Grade 1/2/3 General, based in HARRY GWALA REGIONAL HOSPITAL. This is a permanent post, paying R337,359 to R397,392. How to apply: ALL APPLICATIONS SHOULD BE FORWARDED TO Reference number to quote: NURS12/2026 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Implement standards, practices, criteria and indicators for Quality Nursing Care.
  • Practice Nursing and Health care in accordance with laws and regulations relevant to nursing and healthcare.
  • Implement policies and procedures, clinical guidelines and protocols and all other legislative requirements for the nursing profession.
  • Perform clinical nursing practice in accordance with scope of practice and nursing standards.
  • Participate in implementation of Quality Improvement programmes, Infection Prevention and Control and Occupational health and safety.
  • Provides direction and supervision for the implementation of Nursing Care Plans.
  • Uphold Batho Pele Principles and patient rights charter.
  • Provide a safe, therapeutic environment as laid down by the Nursing Act.
  • Maintain accurate and complete patient records according to legal requirements.
  • Ensure the provision of accurate statistical information for data management. 2
  • Maintain a constructive working relationship with nursing and other stakeholders.
  • Utilize human, material and financial resources efficiently, effectively and economically.
  • Participate in health promotion and illness prevention initiatives within the institution and outreach programmes.
  • Promote a culture of ethics and professionalism within the nursing profession.
  • Participate in Continuing Professional Development.
  • Must be able to work shifts as per allocation
  • including night shift, weekends and Public Holidays.

Requirements

  • Senior Certificate/National Senior Certificate/Matric.
  • Degree/Diploma in General Nursing Science and Midwifery (obtainable from University/ College)
  • Registration certificate with the South African Nursing Council (SANC) as General Nurse and Midwifery.
  • Proof of current registration with SANC (2026).
  • GRADE 1: No experience required GRADE 2: A minimum of TEN years appropriate/recognisable experience in Nursing after registration as Professional Nurse with the SANC in General Nursing GRADE 3: A minimum of TWENTY years appropriate/recognizable in Nursing after registration as Professional Nurse with the SANC in General Nursing.
  • Knowledge and insight into nursing processes and procedures.
  • Nursing statutes and other relevant Public Service Acts.
  • Decision making and problem-solving skills.
  • Interpersonal skills and dealing with conflict management.
  • Knowledge and implementation of National Core Standards, OHSC, MMLLH and IHRM.
  • Knowledge of hospital Quality Assurance and Infection control practices.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Computer literacy to adequately manage information according to facility requirements.
