About the role

At Spitz, we are a leading force in premium footwear and apparel, offering world-class brands such as Carvela, Gianni Chiarini, Lacoste, Nina Roche, and Tosoni. With the addition of Carvela Donna & Bello body sprays and Verve by Kurt Geiger, we continue to elevate South African style, offering both luxury fashion and iconic fragrances. Our culture of craftsmanship, innovation, and teamwork fosters an environment where individual contributions are valued, and excellence is celebrated. If you’re passionate about fashion, thrive in a dynamic environment, and want to make a lasting impact, Spitz offers the ideal opportunity to grow your career. Your Next Career Move Starts Here! Join Spitz in Bryanston, Johannesburg as a Senior Graphic Designer and play a key role in producing visual communications for The Spitz Group for internal programs and external communication using templates and then translating these for the relevant advertising mediums. As our Brand Graphic Designer you will be required to create content for a variety of marketing channels, including websites, magazines, product packaging, banners, signage and social media platforms.
