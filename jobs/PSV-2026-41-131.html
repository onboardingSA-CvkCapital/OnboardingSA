About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring an Administrative Clerk: Communication Support (Photography) in Communication and Marketing, based in Head Office (Pretoria). This is a contract post, paying R237,453 a year. (12 Months Contract) Chief Directorate: Communication and Marketing How to apply: Email Applications for the position of Administrative Clerk: Communication Support (Photography) must be submitted to: Recruitment26-98@dpw.gov.za Reference number to quote: 2026/400D Closing date: 27 November 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • End-to-End Video Production: Script, storyboard, film, and edit compelling video content for social media (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube), internal corporate campaigns, and digital ads.
  • Digital Content Curation
  • Social Media Contribution: Execute short-form, authentic video strategies to optimize organic brand discovery and maintain a consistent publishing cadence
  • Fieldproduce interviews, capture beneficiary/client testimonials, and shoot highquality videos at organizational events
  • Create engaging social media advertisements, digital banners, basic animations, and promotional graphics
  • Draft sharp, platform-appropriate digital copy for web, newsletters, and video captions that seamlessly aligns with our brand voice
  • Monitor, analyse, and report on video metrics and engagement to iteratively improve performance
  • Provide administrative support in relation to procurement, travel and accommodation, personnel administration support and financial administration.

Requirements

  • At least Grade 12 (Senior Certificate) or equivalent.
  • A valid driver’s licence.
  • No previous experience is required, however demonstrable experience in videography will be an advantage.
  • Advanced proficiency in video editing tools (Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve).
  • Familiarity with the Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, InDesign) and digital design tools like Canva.
  • Hands-on experience operating mirrorless/DSLR cameras, mobile video rigs, audio lavaliers, and lighting setups.
  • Computer literacy (MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook).
  • Good verbal and written communication skills, good organisational and planning skills, and good telephone etiquette.
  • Knowledge of the mandate of the Department of Public Works and Infrastructure, Public Service policies and regulations, corporate identity, and digital and social media.
  • Basic numeracy.
  • Ability to work independently and in a team, to solve problems, and to maintain confidentiality.
  • Willingness to adapt the work schedule to the needs of the office, including working after hours and travelling when required.
  • Added Advantage: Relevant qualification and training in videography e and/or NQF 06 qualification in Video Technology or Multimedia production, Film technology.
