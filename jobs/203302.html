About the role

About the Role ZARbet is looking for a hands-on Digital Marketing Executive to take ownership of the day-to-day running of our paid digital channels. You'll be the person keeping our Google Ads, Meta, and iOS App Store presence running smoothly — monitoring campaigns, monitoring spend, monitoring creative, and keeping our reporting sharp. This is an execution-focused role: our Commercial Intelligence and Marketing Executives set the strategy and targets, and you make sure it runs like clockwork every single day. What You'll Do Google Ads: • Manage the daily running of live Google Ads campaigns — budget pacing, ad approvals, and troubleshooting disapprovals. • Roll out new ad creative and copy variations as provided by the marketing team. • Reallocate budget and pause/resume campaigns per instructions. Meta (Facebook/Instagram): • Launch and monitor Meta ad sets, keeping delivery and spend on track. • Keep on top of ad account health and resolve rejections quickly. App Store: • Maintain our iOS App Store listing and coordinate app update submissions. • Monitor and respond to app reviews and ratings. Reporting: • Produce accurate, on-time weekly and monthly reports on channel performance. • Keep campaign trackers and documentation up to date. What We're Looking For • 1–3 years' hands-on experience running live Google Ads accounts. • Practical experience with Meta Ads Manager. • Exposure to App Store Connect or similar app listing tools (advantageous, not essential). • Strong Excel/Google Sheets skills for reporting. • Excellent attention to detail and reliability — you thrive on consistent, accurate execution. • Comfortable working within regulated/gambling advertising policies (training provided). • Diploma or degree in Marketing, Digital Media, or a related field, or equivalent experience. • Google Ads certification is a plus — or willingness to obtain one in your first 90 days.
