About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Human Resource Practitioner: (Pmds and Hrd) in Mzimvubu Tsitsikamma Catchment Management Agency Division: Human Resource Management, based in Kugompo. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: Interested applicants must submit their applications via the online link https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za Reference number to quote: MTCMA Closing date: 16 October 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Interpret and Implement PMDS policies and procedures.
  • Implement midterm and final assessment for non-SMS members.
  • Implement PMDS in the Department.
  • Assist in analysing the alignment of individual performance agreement with the business plan in the department.
  • Keep records of individual performance agreement.
  • Provide support in the implementation of PMDS decision.
  • Supply statistics on PMDS issues in the department.
  • Compile monthly reports.
  • Facilitate Departmental training needs assessment.
  • Conduct Work Skills Plan workshop.
  • Provide assistance in the development of training programs.
  • Implement WSP according to delegations.
  • Develop new training programmes.
  • Analyse skills development gaps.
  • Facilitate the allocation of Bursary.
  • Implementation of induction programmes.
  • Provide support on human resource administration enquiries.
  • Render policy advisory service to the department based on the legislative mandates.
  • Supervise staff and resources in the sub-directorate.
  • Assist in the implementation of approved resolutions.

Requirements

  • A National Diploma (NQF level 6) in Human Resource Management or relevant qualification as recognized by SAQA.
  • 1 years' functional experience in HRD and Performance Management environment.
  • Knowledge:
  • Performance Management Methodology, Principles and Strategies
  • Digital Acumen/Relevant HRM Systems/Learning Systems/Performance Systems etc. Customer Service (Batho Pele Principles)
  • Technical knowledge

  • Basic Conditions of Employment Act
  • Quality management and processes
  • Public policy and framework
  • PFMA and National Treasury Regulations
  • LRA, EE Act, SDA & BCEA
  • COIDA

  • Legislative Requirement:
