About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Senior Administrative Officer in National Rural Youth Service Corps, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 524 Stanza Bopape and Steve Biko Street, Suncardia Building, 6th Floor, Arcadia, 0002 or by email to Post326@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/326 Closing date: 16 November 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian and White Males, and Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate administrative support for NARYSEC recruitment and participant administration.
  • Coordinate administrative and logistical support for recruitment, screening, shortlisting. interviews, participant verification and induction processes in accordance with approved NARYSEC Standard Operating Procedures.
  • Prepare recruitment documentation, interview packs. attendance registers and related administrative records.
  • Verify participant application documents and maintain recruitment databases and participant records.
  • Prepare participant appointment, registration and contractual documentation.
  • Administer participant movements, including appointments, transfers. resignations, terminations, stipend deductions and related Human Resource documentation.
  • Respond to participant administrative enquiries and provide programme support services.
  • Accompany participants during approved programme activities where required.
  • Provide administrative support for Provincial Programme Implementation.
  • Prepare official correspondence, submissions, memorandum. reports and other programme documentation.
  • Coordinate meetings, prepare agendas. compile minutes and maintain records of decisions.
  • Maintain participant files. attendance records and programme documentation throughout the programme lifecycle.
  • Coordinate and quality assure. participant payroll and PERSAL certification documentation prior to submission.
  • Maintain records relating to community service, skills development and participant progression.
  • Coordinate stakeholder communication and maintain administrative records relating to programme implementation.
  • Perform general administrative support functions to facilitate effective programme delivery.
  • Provide administrative support for financial planning, budgeting and reporting, including the compilation and monitoring of the Directorate Management Plan (DMP), Procurement Plan and financial reports.
  • Coordinate the procurement of goods and services in accordance with the approved DMP, Procurement Plan and departmental procurement procedures.
  • Maintain the invoice register, track payments with Finance and follow up on outstanding invoices.
  • Coordinate Information Management and Programme Reporting.
  • Compile participant statistics and monthly, quarterly and annual programme performance reports.
  • Compile information required for the Annual Performance Plan (APP), Integrated Operational Plan (IOP) and other management reporting requirements.
  • Maintain programme information systems and databases, including extracting and analysing NIMS reports.
  • Maintain Portfolio of Evidence and supporting documentation for programme implementation.
  • Maintain participant filing systems and records in accordance with departmental records management policies.
  • Monitor the completeness, accuracy and integrity of programme information and supporting documents.
  • Prepare management information and statistical reports for Provincial and National Office.
  • Coordinate Travel, Logistics and Programme Support Services.
  • Coordinate travel, transport and accommodation arrangements for programme activities and maintain related administrative records.
  • Prepare travel, accommodation and conference submissions with supporting documentation.
  • Liaise with Supply Chain Management and service providers regarding logistical arrangements for programme activities.
  • Coordinate logistical arrangements for recruitment, interviews, medical screening, training, community service, graduation ceremonies and stakeholder meetings.
  • Maintain travel, accommodation and logistics registers.
  • Provide logistical support during official NARYSEC events and activities.

  • A Grade 12 Certificate and a National Diploma (NQF Level 6) in one of the following: Public Administration/Public Management/Business Administration/Business Management/Office Management and Technology/Management Assistant or relevant equivalent qualification
