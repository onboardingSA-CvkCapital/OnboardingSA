About the role

Frogfoot is in search of a Regional Community Relations Officer for the Western Cape area. Purpose of the Role The Regional Community Relations Officer (RCRO) serves as Frogfoot's primary representative within Rise communities within a designated region and acts as the critical link between Frogfoot, community stakeholders, local government structures and residents. The role is responsible for building trust, supporting fibre deployment activities, identifying and mitigating community-related risks, promoting digital inclusion and driving fibre adoption through effective stakeholder engagement. The RCRO will work closely with contractors, operations, sales, marketing and other partners to ensure that community interests are understood and incorporated into rollout plans while supporting Frogfoot's commercial and social impact objectives. The ideal RCRO is someone who is equally comfortable speaking with a ward councillor, school principal, community activist, informal trader or senior Frogfoot executive. They combine strong relationship-building skills with operational discipline and have a genuine passion for expanding digital access and economic opportunity within South African communities.
