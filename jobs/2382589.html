About the role

The Site Supervisor is responsible for the management of our technical staff on customer sites who requires our services on their sites on a permanent basis. S/he are responsible for ensuring all matters relating to mechanical maintenance and repair, services planned and un-planned and for on-site inventory control. This position ensures the customer’s fleet asset, and the designated workshop area is maintained in accordance with legislative requirements. The Site Supervisor facilitate the efficient delivery of mechanical services related to our equipment and solutions through communication, liaison, negotiation, and coordination with internal and external clients to manage client relations. S/he supervise, plan and coordinate fleet servicing and maintenance activities, including managing and allocating company resources to ensure agreed time, cost, quality, safety, and environmental targets are met. The Site Supervisor control the workshop management system and processes by scheduling and recording the allocation of jobs, monitoring timely, cost-effective repairs and workshop income and expenditure and facilitate accurate fleet maintenance records. S/he must ensure all current policies and procedures are correctly adhered to.
