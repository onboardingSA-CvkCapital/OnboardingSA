About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Deputy Director: Skills Development in National Rural Youth Service Corps, based in Northern Cape (Kimberley). This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: Magistrate Court Building, 6th floor, Knight Street, Corner of Stead Street, Kimberley, 8300 or by email to Post390@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/390 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: Coloured, Indian and White Males, and African, Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Conceptualise and develop a Provincial Skills Development Plan.
  • Determine the skills requirements of the confirmed exit opportunities of the NARYSEC youth.
  • Identify and develop learning programmes, aligned to the confirmed exit opportunities.
  • Compile Provincial Skills Development Plan.
  • Facilitate and coordinate the implementation of the Provincial Skills Development Plan.
  • Develop Terms of References (TORs) for all learning programmes to be Implemented.
  • Identify possible training providers to offer the required training.
  • Prepare training approval submissions and ensure approval of planned training.
  • Contract training providers.
  • Prepare all logistical arrangements for the implementation of the learning programmes.
  • Ensure Implementation of all approved learning programmes.
  • Quality assure and monitor the implementation of the Provincial Skills development Plan.
  • Ensure that only accredited training providers are utilised for training.
  • Quality assure training approval submission documentation.
  • Monitor all training being implemented.
  • Compile Monitor and progress reports for training being implemented.
  • Ensure certification of NARYSEC youth.
  • Engage training providers on completion dates for training.
  • Engage training providers on issuing of certificates on completion of training.
  • Obtain certificates from training providers.
  • Prepare and submit portfolios of evidence for skills development Annual Performance Plan (APP) reporting.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements
  • Applicants must be in a possession of a Grade 12 Certificate and a National Diploma in Education or Skills Development/Training/Human Resource Development or relevant equivalent or higher qualification (NQF Level 6)
  • At least 3 years’ experience at junior management level in a skills development environment
  • A valid driver’s licence
  • Job related knowledge
  • Skills Development legalisation
  • Skills Development strategies
  • Skills Development planning
  • Skills Development implementation approaches
  • Skills Development quality assurance and monitoring
  • Skills Development certification processes
  • Curriculum conceptualisation and development
  • Research and reporting
  • Knowledge to operate computer software packages (Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint)
  • Job related skills
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving
  • Planning and organising
  • Decision making
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Influencing and leading
  • Delegation ability
  • Teamwork and negotiation
  • Conflict management
  • Research and report writing
  • Presentation and stakeholder management Skills
