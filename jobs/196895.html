About the role

With the humble beginnings of a single store in Johannesburg CBD opened by Anthony and David Spitz in 1968, SPITZ has grown from strength-to-strength to become synonymous with the excellence of the exclusive international brands it offers in store. An exciting opportunity exists for a Import Logistics Controller at SPITZ in Bryanston, Johannesburg.  The purpose of the role is to co-ordinate and ensure the efficient and accurate execution of the forwarding and clearing process within the company. Monitor product import processes to ensure compliance with regulatory or legal requirements and on-time deliveries. Communication of bookings to relevant stakeholders and resolution of queries raised.
