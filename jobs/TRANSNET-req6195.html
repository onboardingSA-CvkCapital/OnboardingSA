About the role

Transnet (Transnet Pipelines) is hiring a Specialist: SAP PM & MM, based in Durban Central. The role reports to the Manager: Application Systems. Grade: G. Preference will be given to persons with disabilities. • To be involved with the development, maintenance, enhancements, improvements, end user support and training of the functional areas of a SAP ERP system, PM & MM modules in order to meet business needs and processes. • Responsible for business analysis and business process improvement by identifying the impacts and requirements for the business processes to support the organization’s To-Be vision within the Operations System, and for verifying that the business objectives are being met by the Operations project team. • Works closely with the technical team in the design and development of information, data models, reports, forms, interfaces, and conversions.
