About the role

ROLE PURPOSE To ensure the effective day-to-day management, maintenance, security oversight, administration, and general upkeep of the scheme, while serving as the primary on-site representative of the Trustees. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: 1. Staff Supervision • Supervise cleaning staff and any security personnel or access control personnel. • Monitor staff performance and attendance. • Report staff-related concerns to the Trustees or Managing Agent. 2. Building Maintenance • Conduct regular inspections of common property and facilities. • Attend to minor repairs and maintenance where practical. • Identify maintenance requirements and obtain quotes where required. • Coordinate and supervise contractors performing maintenance and repair work. • Ensure common property areas are maintained in a clean, safe, and presentable condition. 3. Compliance and Rule Enforcement • Monitor compliance with Conduct Rules and Management Rules. • Report breaches, complaints, and recurring issues to the Trustees. • Assist in maintaining good order within the scheme. 4. Parking and Access Control • Monitor and administer parking allocations and ensure compliance with parking rules. • Assist with access control procedures where required. 5. Administration and Reporting • Maintain records, files, and correspondence relating to the building. • Liaise with the Managing Agent on operational matters. • Submit a monthly written report to the Trustees covering: o Maintenance matters o Security concerns o Rule violations o Contractor performance o General building issues 6. Resident and Contractor Liaison • Serve as a point of contact for owners, tenants, contractors, and service providers. • Facilitate communication between residents, the Managing Agent, and Trustees. 7. General Duties • Attend Trustee meetings when requested. • Respond to emergencies affecting the scheme. • Carry out any reasonable duties relating to the operation, maintenance, and management of the property. WORKING HOURS: • Monday to Friday: 07:00 to 16:00 • Saturday: 07:00 to 11:00 • Lunch Break: 12:00 to 13:00 (Monday to Friday) • Sundays and Public Holidays: Off duty except for emergencies. BENEFITS: • Accommodation provided by the Scheme • Electricity and water included as part of the accommodation benefit • Any annual increases or bonuses to be considered by the Trustees at their discretion and subject to performance and budget availability.
