About the role

About the Role- TO START ASAP Our client, a leading organisation in the construction and engineering sector, is seeking an experienced Executive Operations Assistant (Personal Assistant) to support the CEO as their operational right hand. This role goes beyond traditional administrative support. The successful candidate will be responsible for protecting executive time, improving operational follow-through, coordinating communication, supporting client and project administration, and ensuring that key actions are tracked and completed. The ideal candidate is highly organised, proactive, detail-oriented, and able to operate independently while maintaining confidentiality and professionalism. Key Responsibilities Executive Diary and Time Management • Manage the CEO’s diary, meeting schedules, preparation time, and follow-up sessions. • Coordinate and confirm meetings, prepare virtual meeting links, and send reminders. • Ensure the CEO is fully prepared for meetings and engagements. • Protect executive focus time and minimize unnecessary interruptions. Communication and Inbox Management • Assist with email, WhatsApp, and client communications where appropriate. • Draft professional correspondence and responses. • Identify and escalate urgent matters. • Track pending responses and ensure timely follow-up. • Ensure important communications are not overlooked or delayed. Meeting Preparation and Action Tracking • Prepare agendas, client briefs, supporting documents, and meeting packs. • Attend meetings when required and record key action items. • Follow up with team members, clients, and suppliers to ensure actions are completed. • Monitor progress and provide updates on outstanding tasks. Client and Sales Administration • Assist with proposal preparation, quotation follow-ups, client onboarding, and renewal reminders. • Maintain accurate CRM records, including contacts, opportunities, and follow-up activities. • Support the smooth handover of clients from sales to implementation teams. Internal Operations Coordination • Coordinate activities between sales, marketing, implementation, finance, and external suppliers. • Monitor project deadlines, overdue actions, and recurring management activities. • Provide visibility to the CEO on priorities requiring attention. • Support operational efficiency across departments. Systems and Document Control • Maintain accurate and organized records on monday.com, GoHighLevel, Google Drive, Microsoft Office, and other company systems. • Ensure all proposals, reports, policies, meeting notes, and client documents are properly stored, named, and easily accessible. • Promote and maintain internal process discipline and best practices. Finance, Supplier, and Administrative Support • Assist with invoice follow-ups and supplier communications. • Track subscriptions, recurring expenses, and administrative obligations. • Prepare documentation required for accounting and financial reporting. • Escalate potential risks before they become urgent issues. • Support management reporting and meeting preparation. Recruitment and People Administration • Assist with recruitment activities, including job advertisements, interview scheduling, candidate communication, and onboarding processes. • Maintain employee records and support HR administration. • Coordinate training reminders and staff development activities. • Ensure a professional candidate and employee experience. • Conduct regular staff check-ins, monitor workloads, track attendance, and assess task completion. Confidential Executive Support • Handle sensitive company, client, employee, and financial information with the highest level of discretion. • Exercise sound judgment when managing confidential matters. • Maintain professionalism and confidentiality at all times.
