About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Human Resource Practitioner: Human Resource Development in Water and Sanitation Service Management: NC CD: Provincial Operations: Northern Cape, based in Kimberley. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 131126/27 Closing date: 13 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Interpret and implement PMDS policies, procedures, guidelines, and directives.
  • Coordinate and facilitate the implementation of PMDS within the region.
  • Administer mid-year and annual performance assessments for non- SMS employees.
  • Conduct quality assurance on Performance Agreements, Mid-Term Reviews and Annual Assessments.
  • Capture and maintain PMDS records on the PERSAL system.
  • Maintain accurate records and ensure the safekeeping of confidential employee information.
  • Distribute PMDS templates, guidelines and circulars to managers and employees within prescribed timeframes.
  • Compile and provide PMDS statistics and reports as required.
  • Provide advice and support on PMDS-related enquiries.
  • Facilitating departmental training needs assessments and identify skills development gaps.
  • Coordinate and implement the Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) in accordance with delegated authority.
  • Conduct Workplace Skills Plan workshops and provide guidance to managers and employees.
  • Assist in the development, coordination and implementation of training and development programmes.
  • Facilitate the allocation and administration of bursaries within the region.
  • Coordinate and implement induction programmes for newly appointed employees.
  • Compile monthly training and development reports.
  • Assist with the implementation and administration of the Internship Programme.
  • Support the implementation and monitoring of the Mentor-Mentee Programme within the region.
  • Provide administrative and developmental support to employee capacity-building initiatives.
  • Maintain effective filing systems for correspondence, training records, certificates, and related documentation.
  • Retrieve records and information for reporting, auditing, and compliance purposes.
  • Assist with the maintenance of service records on PERSAL upon request from the supervisor.
  • Ensure compliance with records management and confidentiality requirements.
  • Compile and submit monthly and quarterly PMDS and Human Resource Development activities.
  • Provide administrative support on human resource development and performance management matters.
  • Assist with the implementation and monitoring of approved resolutions and action plans.
  • Supervise, guide, and monitor the performance of staff within the sub-directorate.
  • Ensure the effective utilisation and management of allocated resources.
  • Promote compliance with departmental policies, procedures, and service delivery standards.

Requirements

  • A National Diploma (NQF level 6) in Human Resource Management or relevant qualification as recognized by SAQA.
  • 1 years' functional experience in HRD and Performance Management environment.
  • The disclosure of a valid unexpired driver’s license will serve as an added advantage.
  • Knowledge of the Skills Development Act and related prescripts.
  • Knowledge of Public Service Regulations and relevant HR prescripts.
  • Knowledge of Record management and administrative skills.
  • Computer literacy (MS Word, Excel, Outlook).
  • Knowledge and experience of PERSAL systems.
  • Knowledge of registry duties and practices.
  • Planning and organising skills.
  • People and Diversity Management skills.
  • Be client Orientation and Customer Focus.
  • Good verbal and written communication skills.
  • Accountability and Ethical Conduct.
  • Flexibility and teamwork.
  • Basic knowledge of problem solving and analysis.
  • Attention to details and willingness to travel.
