About the role

Standard Bank is hiring an Employee Experience Solutions Specialist, People & Culture, DIO, based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. To provide expert advice on functional People and Culture (P&C) solutions, analysing requirements in order to design, build, implement, maintain and optimise specific Employee Experience solutions(s); that are integrated across the global People and Culture landscape and aligned to overall business strategy and requirements. To monitor the automation and compliance of all systems by managing the systems licensing and managing vendor systems across all geographies to adhere to regulatory and legislative requirements.
