About the role

PURPOSE OF ROLE The Head of Safety (Head of SHE) is the custodian of safety across the organisation. Reporting directly to the CEO, the successful candidate will own the strategic mandate for Safety, Health and Environment across the business and be accountable for ensuring full compliance in every industry, jurisdiction, and regulatory environment in which Lesedi operates. This is a senior, high-visibility position: the incumbent sets the direction, standards, and tone for SHE across the organisation, and ensures it is consistently understood and applied at every division, project, and site. The role carries significant external accountability as Lesedi's principal representative on safety matters with clients, regulators, and industry bodies, and is the escalation point for serious safety incidents with client, regulatory, or reputational consequence. The Head of Safety drives a proactive Zero Harm culture, a strong nuclear safety culture, and continuous improvement across the organisation. This position is a legal appointment in terms of Section 16(2) of the Occupational Health and Safety Act, read together with Construction Regulations. KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS Strategic Leadership, Corporate Governance & Executive Reporting • Own the strategic mandate for SHE, translating it into divisional and site-level plans across all locations. • Report SHE performance, risk, and incidents to the CEO, EXCO, Board, and Social & Ethics Committee. • Set enterprise-wide SHE standards and non-negotiables, and hold divisional and site leadership accountable against the SHE strategy and budget. • Champion Zero Harm as a lived organisational value across all levels of leadership.External Stakeholder, Client & Regulatory Engagement • Act as Lesedi's primary point of contact for safety matters with clients, regulators, and industry bodies. • Represent Lesedi in client SHE audits, pre-qualification assessments, and other high-level compliance meetings. • Lead engagement with the Department of Employment and Labour and other regulatory bodies. • Manage incident notification, client liaison, and reputational risk during significant SHE events.SHE Governance, Compliance and Regulatory Management • Ensure full compliance with SHE legislation, construction regulations, and nuclear safety requirements across all jurisdictions. • Review and update legal registers, SHE procedures, and compliance documentation. • Conduct SHE inspections, audits, and compliance assessments across all operational sites. • Ensure statutory reports, notifications, permits, and SHE records are accurately maintained and submitted.Nuclear Safety Culture and Risk Management • Lead and promote nuclear safety culture initiatives, setting the tone for how safety is discussed and escalated. • Conduct safety culture assessments, behavioural observations, and risk evaluations. • Review baseline risk assessments and ensure alignment with operational and legal requirements. • Investigate incidents, near misses, and non-conformances, and drive corrective action to closure.Integrated Management Systems (IMS) and Continuous Improvement • Maintain and improve the SHE components of the Integrated Management System (IMS). • Ensure compliance with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 19443, and ASME NQA-1 across every division. • Coordinate internal and external SHE audits and track corrective actions to closure. • Analyse SHE performance data and KPIs to identify improvement opportunities and report at executive level.Operational SHE Support and Emergency Preparedness • Support projects and operational teams with SHE guidance, ensuring corporate standards are applied at site level. • Ensure project safety files and contractor SHE documentation comply with client specifications. • Coordinate emergency preparedness plans, drills, and incident response activities. • Deliver SHE awareness training and monitor PPE, occupational hygiene, and environmental management.Team Leadership and Stakeholder Collaboration • Lead, mentor, and guide SHE personnel and specialist teams across divisions and sites. • Chair the SHE Committee, Nuclear Safety Culture Committee, and related governance meetings. • Develop and manage SHE budgets and monitor expenditure against approved budgets. • Provide executive
