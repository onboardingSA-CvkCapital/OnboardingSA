About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies Head Office has an opportunity available for a Social Media Coordinator for the Biogen team. The Social Media Coordinator will be responsible for coordination and assisting the Social Media team with developing and implementing social media plans to increase the companies brand awareness and engagement on all social media platforms. Minimum Requirements Essential: • Grade 12 – Matric or a Senior Certificate. • Diploma in Marketing/ Digital Marketing/ Communications or related field • Minimum of 2- 3 years’ experience working in a similar capacity in either an agency or corporate setting.Advantageous • Retail Marketing experienceJob Description Social Media: • Co-ordinate and maintain the social media business presence across various targeted social media channels. • Provide on-going oversight and coordination of all aspects regarding social interfaces such as content, broadsheets, analysis, new ideas (contests, promos, online/social exclusives etc.), lead generation and content syndication methods • Stay up-to-date with social media trends and changes to algorithms and adjust implementations accordingly. • Assist in planning, organizing and executing social media activities within the business. • Ensure maintenance and updates of content for social media campaigns. • Ensure that social media campaigns are executed parallel to the marketing calendar.Content: • Support event awareness on the relevant social media channels. • Provide suggestions on new ways to attract prospective customers through presence, responding appropriately to both positive and negative reviews and comments. • Monitor internet for brand related topics of conversation.Customer Relations: • Collaborate with other
