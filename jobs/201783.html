About the role

Join Our Team | Credit Risk & Compliance Are you analytical, detail-oriented and confident in making sound credit decisions? Do you thrive in a fast-paced environment where accuracy, customer service and risk management go hand in hand? We are looking for a Credit Vetting Agent to join our team and play a key role in ensuring that customer applications are assessed accurately, efficiently and in line with our Credit Risk and Compliance requirements. Key Roles & Responsibilities • Implement and apply existing Credit Vetting policies and procedures across Consumer and Enterprise environments, ensuring credit risk is effectively and efficiently managed. • Manage and ensure RICA compliance throughout the customer onboarding process. • Assess and analyse bank statements and financial information to determine customer affordability and financial capacity. • Review customer information, identify potential risks and make sound, informed credit decisions. • Analyse information from various sources and use sound judgement to approve, decline or escalate applications where required. • Meet and consistently achieve performance and productivity targets while maintaining a high level of accuracy and quality. • Identify and resolve potential issues or discrepancies during the credit vetting process. • Provide professional and efficient service to internal and external customers.Skills, Competencies & Attributes We are looking for someone who demonstrates: • Exceptional attention to detail and accuracy. • Strong analytical and decision-making skills • Strong problem-solving abilities with a proactive approach to resolving issues. • Excellent verbal and written communication skills. • The ability to work effectively under pressure and meet deadlines. • Strong reporting and administrative skills. • A customer-focused approach with a commitment to delivering excellent service. • A target-driven and results-oriented mindset.Qualifications & Experience Minimum Requirements: • Matric / Grade 12.Advantageous: • 3 years' experience in Credit Risk Assessment or Credit Vetting. • Knowledge and understanding of Telco products and services. • Knowledge of the RICA Act and its application within customer onboarding. • Knowledge of the National Credit Act (NCA). • Experience assessing affordability and analysing financial documentation.Why Join Us? This is an opportunity to be part of a team where your analysis, judgement and attention to detail directly contribute to responsible customer onboarding and effective risk management. If you are driven, analytical and ready to make an impact in a dynamic Credit Risk environment, we would like to hear from you!
