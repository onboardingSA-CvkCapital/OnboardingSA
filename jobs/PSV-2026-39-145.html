About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Director: Water Services Infrastructure Development and Refurbishment Programmes in Water and Sanitation Services Management: GP CD: Provincial Operations: Gauteng, based in Gauteng. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 131126/01 Closing date: 13 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide strategic direction in the implementation of policies on water services infrastructure programmes, in conjunction with provincial COGTA and municipalities, to ensure that grant-funded projects are optimally prioritised on an ongoing basis.
  • Ensure that policies on water services infrastructure grant programme are aligned to the departmental strategic plan. ensure that plans are available for water services infrastructure grant programmes.
  • Facilitates and support implementation of water services infrastructure grant programmes for provincial operations.
  • Ensure that Regional Bulk Infrastructure programme is implemented in the province.
  • Various water sector consulted on water issues.
  • Needs of business sectors catered for on water bulk infrastructure grant programmes.
  • Implement water policies for regional bulk infrastructure supply of water.
  • Promote partnership between government public entities, private sector, and civil society regarding water services infrastructure grant programmes.
  • Ensure that water supply is available for all business sectors.
  • Avail raw water supply for all municipalities.
  • Ensure that all other water key stakeholders are communicated regarding water issues. budgeted for in medium term expenditure framework.
  • Compile budget for building water services infrastructure grant programme.
  • Monthly reporting.
  • Early warning system management.
  • Ensure that budgeted funds are used properly.
  • Ensure that monitoring and evaluation for water services infrastructure grant programmes are properly conducted in provinces.
  • Compile reports on monitoring and evaluation.
  • Ensure projects are carried out as planned.
  • Implement water policies for regional bulk supply of water.
  • Compile monthly and quarterly reports.

Requirements

  • A Degree in Civil Engineering or Construction Project Management at NQF level 7 qualification.
  • 10 years programme and project management in civil engineering within the water sector-built environment.
  • 5 years’ experience at middle/senior management level.
  • Experience should preferably be in the water sector.
  • Successful completion of the Pre-entry Certificate for the Senior Management Service (SMS) as endorsed by the National School of Government.
  • Working knowledge of Public Finance Management Act.
  • Possess a general understanding of contract administration, business principles, and business law.
  • A valid and unexpired driver’s license.
  • A broad understanding of each engineering discipline.
  • Working knowledge of drafting of agreements Effective computer skills (Microsoft Office Software, lotus notes, and other company and discipline-specific software applications).
  • Effective and excellent communication skills both verbally and in writing with management, colleagues, and individuals inside and outside the Department.
  • Effective analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Knowledge and understanding of Human Resources Management Legislations, policies, practices, and procedures.
  • Knowledge of Public Financial Management Act (PFMA).
  • Framework for managing performance information.
  • Knowledge of implementing policies of PMDS.
  • Client orientation and customer focus.
  • Service Delivery Innovation (SDI).
  • Problem management and empowerment.
  • Accountability and ethical conduct.
