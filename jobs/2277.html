To ensure your application is complete, you must submit ALL documents listed under the inherent requirements, as well as the following standard documentation: •    A detailed Curriculum Vitae. •    Copies of your qualifications, or official proof of qualifications obtained (If you hold foreign qualifications, please include SAQA accreditation). •    A copy of your identity document (South African ID or passport for foreign nationals). •    Confirmation of employment in the form of a letter, appointment contract, service certificate, or recent payslip from your current or most recent employer (applicable to external applicants only). Duties and responsibilities: •    Open a docket or inquiry in respect of reported incidents or crimes. •    Conduct investigations in respect of on- and off-campus reported crimes or incidents. •    Open inquiries in respect of reported incidents off campus and arrange for the necessary assistance to students. •    Interview suspects and victims according to Protection Services Guidelines for Investigations. •    Submit the docket and/ or enquiry document to the Line Manager after an investigation is concluded in consultation with the Line Manager. •    Report serious incidents and cases, such as murder, death threats and others, to the Line Manager. •    Compile an investigation report after the investigation is concluded. •    Compile an investigation report in serious cases for submission to the Executive Management Committee via the office of the Assistant Director: Investigation Services. •    Liaise with SAPS Investigators/ Detectives for updates regarding reported cases involving UFS staff and /or students. •    Establish a close working relationship with the student bodies in relation to investigations, incidents reported and not reported, and involve them in the search for solutions. •    Provide assistance and support to Operational Security in relation to security and crime awareness campaigns for staff and students. •    Identify potential issues that can be addressed in awareness campaigns and escalate to the relevant site managers/ line managers, e.g., the boundaries campaign, the GBV campaign, the substance abuse campaign, etc. •    Recruit and manage own informers. •    Collect and share information on planned crime incidents and stability issues that can impact on the institution, staff and students. •    Identify and detect actual and perceived threats facing the university through the collection of stability-related and crime-related intelligence. •    Sensitise Line Management to all collected information to enable proper implementation of security countermeasures and to ensure all available information is shared via the Protection Services Site Managers. •    Determine crime patterns using various sources such as Online Intelligence Risk Manager and regular liaison with SAPS, etc. •    Collect crime stats weekly and monthly from SAPS and analyse the statistics. •    Represent Protection Services during awareness sessions for new students. •    Ensure availability and accessibility of all crime-related information through the submission of intelligence/ information reports on the CiiMS Platform (Information Reports). •    Ensure availability of serious incident/ crime reports and submit to Line Management for approval before dissemination to clients. •    Ensure that reported incidents are captured on CiiMS and escalated to investigations as required. •    Ensure that all information is updated on CiiMS and that profiles are created for all offenders/ alleged offenders identified and linked to relevant cases. •    Ensure that dockets are closed out and the system updated. Inherent requirements: •    A three (3) year National Diploma on NQF Level 6 in Forensic Investigation, Policing, Security Management or closely related field. •    A valid South African driver’s license (attach a copy). •    A minimum of three (3) years' experience in an investigation environment. •    A valid Grade B PSIRA certificate (attach a copy) or willingness to acquire at own cost within six (6) months of employment. Recommendations: •    Experience in a higher education environment. •    Formal training in criminal investigation (attach proof). •    Formal training in a crime intelligence environment (attach proof). Required competencies: •    Results Orientated: -    The ability to stick with tasks, not give up, and overcome obstacles in completing one's task. -    The ability to be reliable, responsible, dependable and to fulfil obligations •    Strategic Thinking: -    The ability to focus on details, work towards perfection, and approach work in a neat and organised manner. -    The ability to carefully analyse information and use logic to address issues and problems at work. •    Business Acumen: -    The ability to adhere to rules and strictly follow work regulations. -    Proficient in using MS Office. •    Leading: -    The ability to maintain high levels of personal motivation, energy and enthusiasm. -    The ability to be cooperative with others, display a good-natured attitude and encourage people to work together. •    Building Coalitions: -    The ability to be self-assured and at ease with people in all types of social situations. -    The ability to interact with others and establish personal connections with people.
