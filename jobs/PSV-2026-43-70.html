About the role

The Government Pensions Administration Agency is hiring a Senior Vetting Investigator: Security Management, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R605,742 a year. Facilities Management Permanent The purpose of this role is to monitor the execution of vetting fieldwork investigations within the GPAA. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.gpaa.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: SNR/VET/INVEST/SECU/MANG/2026/11-1P Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) No late applications will be considered Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: One permanent position of Senior Vetting Investigator is currently available at Security Management of GPAA.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will be responsible for the management of customer relationships in the: Region, which inter alia include but is not limited to: Conduct vetting field-work investigations: Gather relevant information.
  • Conduct proper analysis and quality check on the information.
  • Compile and submit reports to management and SSA on all vetting files and report completed on a regular basis.
  • Conduct vetting investigations in respect of secret and top secret levels.
  • Administer files, projects and task teams dealing with matters related to vetting projects/processes: Participation in project and task teams dealing with a variety of subject area.
  • Conduct vetting and security related research and development.
  • Ensure that the company screening, Personnel Suitability Checks and Individual screening are conducted.
  • Implement and manage process within the Department, including the management of files and reports by ensuring quality control and effective and efficient workflow of work done by Vetting officers and report on all work allocated.
  • Identify skills development needs and provide training and development opportunities for Vetting Officers.
  • Provide advice and guidance on the interpretation and application of legislation, policies and procedures.
  • Monitor the proper utilization of equipment, stores and expenditure.
  • Administer the departmental performance management and development system within the sub-directorate.
  • Provide inputs for the development and implementation of policies, guidelines, norms and standards in vetting and investigations: Analyse, research and evaluate all vetting related information.
  • Assist in the development, implementation and maintenance of investigation operating procedures.
  • Provide advice and guidelines on the interpretation and application of legislation, policies and procedures.
  • Manage and development of staff
  • Manage the performance of the unit which involves coaching, mentoring, and take corrective action where required, develop performance standards and evaluates team and individuals
  • Monitor staff regarding human resource such as leave, recruitment and grievances
  • Compile the work plans for the section including the consolidation of operational plans into the directorate’s overall work plan
  • Provide effective communication channels and systems between the department and the State Security Agency (SSA) and other related agencies: Liaise regularly with SSA, SAPS, SASS, Defence, Home Affairs and other critical stakeholders for advice, assistance and to obtain additional information.
  • Establish and promote relationships with external stakeholders, including credit information providers, to access information.
