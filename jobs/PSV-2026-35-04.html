About the role

The Department of Agriculture is hiring a Senior Laboratory Assistant in Inspection Services, based in Western Cape Stellenbosch. This is a permanent post, paying R237,453 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to 20 Steve Biko Street, Agriculture Place, Arcadia, Pretoria, 0002 or by email SLArecruit86@nda.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/3/1/86/2026 Closing date: 16 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Target: African Males and Females and persons with disability.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare samples and perform routine analysis for the determination of i.e. chemical resides/dairy/processed food/liquor products composition components (quality control) which would, inter alia, entail the following, Labelling of samples.
  • Collecting/taking samples for preparation (i.e. fruit, vegetables, grain and or other agricultural products/processed foods bread, dried fruit, fruit juice, honey vinegar).
  • Preparation of sample material by e.g. weighing, drying, cutting etc. Preparation of media (eliza kits).
  • Preparation and staining of smears.
  • Preparation of stains, buffers and reagents.
  • Visual inspection and collection of infected material if applicable.
  • Perform extractions and basic analyses of samples.
  • Record analyses results.
  • Storage of samples.
  • Packing and/or transport samples.
  • Assist in decontamination and disposal of used samples.
  • Removal of waste.
  • Perform basic maintenance on selected equipment and instrumentation.
  • Report all faulty equipment and instrumentation.
  • Perform verification and calibration on selected equipment and instrumentation.
  • Maintain tidiness of laboratories, clean/sterilise glassware and equipment.
  • Clean laboratory facilities.
  • Clean glassware and laboratory equipment.
  • Perform administrative and related functions which would inter alia, entail the following: Inventory control of equipment and consumable for equipment.
  • Recordkeeping and updating of the identified register (i.e. Register book, sample referral, sample received, record of results, inventory of standards, reagents and chemicals, waste removal).
  • Assist with invoicing of analyses.
  • Comply with health and safety regulations.

  • Applicants must be in a possession of a Grade 12 Certificate.
  • At least 3 years relevant experience in Laboratory and Horticulture.
  • Job Related Knowledge:
  • Knowledge of the Public Service and Departmental prescripts /policies

  • Problem solving techniques

  • Data processing
  • Applicable relevant analytical techniques
  • Efficient managing of stores and ordering of miscellaneous stores
  • Use of relevant laboratory and office equipment
  • Manual dexterity
  • Communication capabilities
  • Insight capabilities in the application of quality assurance documentation
  • Accuracy, thoroughness and timeous Ness
  • Computer skills, application/usage of computer software
  • Alert and troubleshooting skills
  • Administrative and organisational skills Language capabilities with regard to the understanding of quality system documentation
  • Planning and organising abilities
  • Self developmentally inclined
  • Team building
  • Trainings skills/give guidance
  • Extended working hours
  • Handling of hazardous chemicals
  • Valid driver’s license and the ability to drive
