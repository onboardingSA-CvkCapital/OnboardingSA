About the role

Description The Mid-Level Full-Stack Developer is responsible for designing, developing, testing, and supporting high-quality software solutions across the full technology stack, including user interfaces, backend services, APIs, databases, and cloud-based infrastructure. Working closely with senior team members, product stakeholders, and cross-functional stakeholders, the role focuses on delivering well-defined product features that are secure, scalable, maintainable, and aligned with business requirements. This role requires a developer who is comfortable taking ownership of scoped work from implementation through testing, review, and deployment while collaborating effectively with the broader engineering team. The successful candidate will contribute to technical excellence through adherence to software engineering best practices, automated testing, secure coding principles, and continuous improvement. Why This Role Matters The Mid-Level Full-Stack Developer plays a critical role in transforming business requirements into reliable, scalable software solutions that deliver value to customers. By contributing across the frontend, backend, API, and database layers, this role ensures that product features are developed efficiently while maintaining high standards of quality, security, and maintainability. Through collaboration with senior team members and product stakeholders, the role helps accelerate product delivery, improve platform stability, and support the long-term evolution of the organisation's technology ecosystem. This position also strengthens engineering capability by contributing to technical documentation, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement initiatives that enhance development practices across the team. Full-Stack Software Development • Design, develop and maintain application features across frontend, backend, APIs, workers and databases. • Deliver production-ready functionality using modern software engineering practices. • Take ownership of assigned features from development through deployment. • Ensure software meets functional and technical specifications. • Produce maintainable, scalable and well-structured code. 2.
