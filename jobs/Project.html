About the role

Job Title: Project Cost Controller Location: Rustenburg Job Type: 3 month contract, expected to work Full-Time hours (Onsite) Key responsibilities • Control the approved Capital Budget by ensuring accurate comparisons for management visibility, between order detail and the detail within the approved Capital Budget before placement of orders/contracts. • Control over the approved Capital Budget by timeous identification of potential cost overruns and negative trends against Contracted / Order amounts by being actively involved in the project and by scrutinizing the details of the monthly budget versus actual quantities and costs together with schedule performance and trends. • Compiling cost variances (including all change notices) and obtaining potential cost impacts of variances from relevant team members timeously. • Accurate and timeous recording of actual costs incurred to ensure accurate reporting of cost status at each monthly interval to the Project Manager, Controls Manager, and external stakeholders. • Generating Reports and reports on: o Plan vs Actuals. o Cash flow forecasts. o BAC and EAC. • Manage project contingency in accordance with management/client’s protocols. • Drawing trends, forecasting, and generating time-phased cost flows reflective of project estimate at completion per control account. • Preparation of budget impacts and sourcing of funds resulting from a request for change. • Ensures timeous processing of approved transfer of funds as and when required, resulting from approved change requests. • Generating Monthly Cost Reports to indicate true cost status and key project cost metrics for decision-making and corrective actions. • Ensures timeous settlement of final accounts and order closeout upon completion of work. • Ensures governance and/or Limits of Authority followed in terms of Budget Limits for approvals. • Coordinate and compile project cost forecasts. • Day-to-day cost control including verification of time sheets and/or other supporting documentation. • Communicate due dates for payments to stakeholders. • Expediting payments of invoices including verification of supporting documentation. • System training may be offered by the client to enable you to assist with: o Running reports on SAP o Raise orders into SAP. o Load invoices into SAP. o Emergency order and payment in SAP. o Load new contracts into SAP. o Balancing SAP CAS accounts monthly and completing SAP month end. o Verification of all quotes and invoices for correctness before processing and submission for signatures. • Assist client/management with Project closure, closeout, Lessons learnt, etc. and reporting • Assist on Procurement activities like PO Amendments, Scope finalising, expediting approvals on PR and PO, • Track outstanding Procurements and facilitate sessions with End users and Procurement departments to finalise its processing, • Update Procurement dashboard for management presentations, • Consistent follow up with suppliers and contractors for cash flow forecast and cost timing updates, • Draw reports from SAP (Budget, Actuals, Commitments), • High level knowledge of EVM, CPI and SPI, • Assist in generating / completing Change control reports in terms of Cost and Cashflow forecast * Draft and update the PoP (procurement operating plan), * Daily & weekly updates and record keeping of the in progress, outstanding and overdue requisition and purchase order file, * Some expediting – adherence to schedule promised delivery dates, * Track import freight, Requirements National Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in: Cost Management, Quantity Surveying, Finance or Accounting CCP or CCT qualification is an advantage 15 years of experience as a project cost controller in mining Must have experience using the following software: · SAP · Excel pivot tables · Power BI
