About the role

Heineken Beverages is hiring a Temp Admin Clerk, based in Germiston. Heineken Beverages has an exciting opportunity available to a suitable candidate for potential temporary placement in our Cellar department. This position will be based at Wadeville Production.

Responsibilities

  • Achieve production requirements against targets
  • Ensure strict adherence to stock take procedures
  • Compile weekly bulk requirement report
  • Manage the decant area
  • Administration of process flow
  • Monitor cellar bulk movements
  • Compile weekly and monthly reports
  • Create requisitions for the purchasing of raw materials and consumables
  • Monitor production data and records on SAP
  • Manage administrative functions of the Cellar
  • Solutions and innovations to contribute towards continuous improvement
  • Established networks with suppliers / service departments and internal customers
  • Liaise with Production Planner regarding bulk requirements
  • Implemented initiatives of business plans
  • Attend daily cross-functional production meetings
  • To reflect good leadership behaviour - towards colleagues, superiors and company values.
  • Assist Cellar Team Leaders with blending and ABM

Requirements

  • Grade 12
  • 1 to 3 years’ administrative experience will be beneficial
  • Structured and Organized
  • Good leadership qualities
  • Proven verbal and written communication skills (Bilingualism)
  • Executing administrative functions on the SAP system would be beneficial.
  • Computer literate in MS Office, with Excel on an intermediate level of competence
  • Strong numerical aptitude and analytical abilities are essential
  • Attention to detail and accuracy is essential.
  • Display good product knowledge
  • Demonstrate a high ability to plan and organize cellar activities
  • Strong inter- and intra-personal skills
  • Good administrative skills
  • Demonstrate the ability to perform under pressure
  • Ability to action tasks spontaneously without being urged on to do so
  • Active team player
  • Ability to deal with conflict with a problem-solving approach
