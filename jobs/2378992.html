About the role

Provide specialised care where every moment matters. This opportunity in Rustenburg, North West is suited to a qualified Neonatal Intensive Care Nurse who is committed to delivering exceptional care to critically ill and premature newborns while working within a highly skilled multidisciplinary team. Key Responsibilities: •                        Deliver specialised nursing care to neonates requiring intensive care. •                        Monitor and assess neonatal patients, responding promptly to changes in their condition. •                        Administer medication and specialised treatments in accordance with clinical protocols. •                        Operate and monitor neonatal equipment, including incubators, ventilators, and monitoring devices. •                        Support and educate parents throughout their baby's hospital journey. •                        Maintain accurate clinical documentation and ensure compliance with quality and infection prevention standards. •                        Work collaboratively with neonatologists and the multidisciplinary healthcare team. Minimum Requirements: •                        Relevant Degree or Diploma in Nursing. •                        Current registration with the South African Nursing Council (SANC). •                        Post Basic qualification in Neonatal Intensive Care Nursing. •                        Minimum of 2 years' experience within a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit. •                        Demonstrated clinical competence and technical skills within a NICU environment. •                        Understanding of the private healthcare industry, its challenges, and role players will be advantageous. •                        Computer literacy. •                        Excellent communication, interpersonal, and patient care skills.
