About the role

The Provincial Treasury (Limpopo) is offering 3 internships in Chartered Accountancy Trainee in Financial Governance, based in Head Office - Polokwane. The stipend is R487,197 a year. (3 years with effect from 1st February 2027 to 31st January 2030 (36 months) Branch: Financial Governance How to apply: If you believe you meet the requirements and have the aspiration to succeed, please complete in full and send the new Z83 Form and the TE008 (CAA Selection Form) available on www.limtreasury.gov.za to caa@treasury.limpopo.gov.za. Only shortlisted candidates will be required to submit certified copies of South African ID and full official academic certificate/s and transcript/s. Alternatively, applications may be sent by post to: The Head of Department, Limpopo Provincial Treasury, Private Bag X9486, Polokwane, 0700 OR hand delivered at Ismini Towers, Office No. G002 (Ground Floor) Registry, 46 Hans van Rensburg Street, Polokwane, 0699. Closing date: 20 November 2026 @ 16H00. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Candidates already having a training contract with another training office will not be considered.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidates will have access to diverse practical financial management, decision making and control experience and an opportunity to apply their knowledge in wide ranging financial reporting frameworks, Companies Act, Public Finance Management Act and Regulations with targeted exposure to multiple industry sectors in which our entities operate.
  • Limpopo Treasury is the provincial allocator of financial resources to departments and entities.
  • The wide variety of sectoral focus by departments and entities that Limpopo Treasury supports, and monitors makes for a challenging and fulfilling training environment for prospective trainee Chartered Accountants in the Public Sector.

Requirements

  • CTA or Postgraduate Diploma qualification in Accounting.
  • The qualification graduates with keen interest in establishing a career in the Public Sector.
  • The qualification should not older than 2 years.
  • Applicants currently studying towards completing the said qualifications that will enable them to write SAICA’s Assessment of Initial Competence in January 2027 are encouraged to apply.
