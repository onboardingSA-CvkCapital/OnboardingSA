About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Reliability Engineer, based in Mokopane, Limpopo. This role is at a Band 6 level reporting to the Engineering Asset Manager.

Responsibilities

  • Identify, assess and escalate equipment-related safety and reliability risks, including risks affecting safety-critical assets and controls.
  • Provide reliability input into incident, accident and equipment-failure investigations and track assigned corrective actions.
  • Lead or participate in asset criticality assessments, FMECA, RCM and maintenance-tactics reviews.
  • Develop and review preventive and predictive maintenance task lists for assigned equipment, based on failure modes, risk, condition and maintenance history.
  • Identify the special tools, facilities and supporting requirements needed to execute identified maintenance tasks safely and effectively.
  • Provide reliability and maintainability input into specifications, commissioning, modifications and acceptance of new or changed equipment.
  • Develop and annually review equipment management and improvement plans, including LOAP actions, in consultation with operational stakeholders.
  • Define practical corrective and preventive actions, assign measures of effectiveness and verify whether improvements are sustained.
  • Monitor MTBF, availability, maintenance effectiveness and asset-health trends for the assigned area and translate results into actions.
  • Integrate condition-monitoring findings, inspections, defects, remaining-life information and OEM recommendations into risk-based maintenance actions.
  • Coordinate the follow-up of oil analysis, vibration, thermography, structural inspections and other asset-health findings applicable to the assigned area.
  • Establish and drive maintenance-quality and precision-maintenance initiatives.
  • Identify repeat workmanship or repair-quality concerns and coordinate corrective actions.
  • Provide reliability input into planning, Prioritisation, scheduling and work-package quality.
  • Review completed work, technical feedback and failure history to assess task effectiveness and identify required changes.
  • Maintain reliability records, task lists, technical documents, defect registers, action logs and performance reports in the applicable systems.
  • Support accurate master data, bills of material, equipment structures and reporting requirements affecting reliability decisions.
  • Develop bills of material, rotable requirements, preservation requirements and remaining-life information.
  • Develop life-cycle cost models, repair-versus-replace studies and reliability-improvement business cases.
  • Research relevant technology and digital opportunities and develop or coordinate controlled trials and implementation programmes in accordance with applicable governance and approval requirements.
  • Build personal competence through structured exposure, mentoring, formal learning and evidence of applied reliability engineering work.
  • Contribute to a purpose-driven, high-performing and accountable culture that is aligned with company values, promotes inclusion and diversity, supports employee engagement and fosters continuous improvement.

  • At least 5 years' relevant Engineering experience.
  • Relevant experience in maintenance, engineering or asset-intensive operations, with demonstrated exposure to reliability analysis, maintenance strategies, condition monitoring, RCA or defect elimination.
  • Maintenance and reliability engineering principles

  • RCA
