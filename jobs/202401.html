About the role

Main Purpose of the Job To manage, control, and maintain engineering documents and records in accordance with company standards and project requirements. The ideal candidate will have extensive experience in document control processes within engineering environment and a strong understanding of technical documentation and regulatory compliance. Education, experience and competencies • National Senior Certificate/Grade 12 and tertiary qualification in document control/records management • 3-5 years experience in documentation creation, editing, management and control • 1-3 years in a senior or lead role • Familiarity with ISO 9001 or similar quality management systems (advantage) & Electronic/Electrical knowledge (advantage) • Computer literate: Microsoft OfficeResponsibilities • Create and update development technical and Section 21 Master files • Establish and maintain a document control system for all engineering documents, including drawings, specifications, datasheets, calculations, and reports. • Ensure the accurate and timely registration, distribution, and retrieval of documents to internal teams and external stakeholders. • Coordinate with engineers, project managers, and contractors to ensure version control and document integrity. • Maintain electronic document management systems (EDMS) and ensure metadata and document attributes are correctly captured. • Review and audit documentation for compliance with company and industry standards (e.g., ISO). • Archive inactive or obsolete documents and support retrieval of historical data for audits or project reviews. • Train and support junior document controllers on document management best practices. • Liaise with QA/QC and compliance teams to ensure all documentation meets audit and quality assurance standards. • Prepare regular reports on document control status, outstanding approvals, and document turnaround times. • Ensure all documentation packs are according to the company's and ISO9001-2015 standards. • Maintain all product related technical documents and registers. • Control Engineering documentation. • Verify technical validity of existing product and service-related documents. • Update and keep record of new drawings list. • Develop and update all QMS related process flows. • Review, update and receive approvals for all related documents. • Generate document number according to ISO9001-2015 standards, update product index approval spreadsheet • Analyse and implement improvements on current product and service-related processes. • Update control documents monthly. • Compile and archive final data packs. • Assist with stock take, update stock take master sheet when required.
