About the role

Pepkor Lifestyle is looking for an energetic individual to join our dynamic team. We are looking for a SAP Basis Consultant who will be responsible for ensuring SAP systems operate reliably and efficiently and to maintain the integrity of the SAP environment. Job Purpose As a SAP Basis Functional Consultant at Pepkor Lifestyle, you will play a critical role in managing the technical infrastructure of our SAP systems, ensuring stability, security, and optimal performance. The ideal candidate should have a deep understanding of SAP Basis Administration, strong problem-solving skills, and a passion for supporting SAP landscapes. The SAP Basis Consultant is responsible to ensure SAP systems operate reliably and efficiently and to maintain the integrity of the SAP environment. The SAP Basis Consultant is responsible to introduce and manage technical changes into the SAP environment to minimise risk and achieve high reliability, availability and performance of each SAP instance.
