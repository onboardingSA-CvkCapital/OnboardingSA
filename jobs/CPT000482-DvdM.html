About the role

Prepare and send quotations and invoices. Provide product specifications and relevant product information. Process and manage customer orders. Arrange deliveries and coordinate logistics. Follow up with clients to ensure orders and services meet their expectations. Maintain professional and positive client relationships. General Administration Provide general administrative support to the business. Maintain organised customer and order records. Requirements & Qualifications The ideal candidate will have: Strong computer literacy and confidence using digital systems. Proven ability and confidence in using AI tools.
