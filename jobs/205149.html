About the role

Marketing Manager: Brand and Digital Department: Marketing Location: Cape Town – Work from Home, in line with organisational requirements- Potentially Hybrid Reporting to: Marketing Director Salary: Market-related FTC- 12 month FTC Role Purpose The Marketing Manager: Brand and Digital is responsible for owning how the company presents itself in the market through strong brand execution, integrated paid and organic campaigns, digital channels and brand experiences. The role leads brand-focused marketing activity that builds awareness, relevance, perception and market demand, including campaigns supporting the overall brand, customised offerings and key accounts. The role provides senior ownership of the website and organic social media presence, setting priorities and direction while supporting team members manage day-to-day execution. The role also leads brand events and activations and manages the external marketing partners required to deliver high-quality brand experiences. The position works closely with the Marketing Manager: Programmes, who leads programme and enrolment-focused marketing, lead generation and revenue-focused campaigns, as well as the Communications and Stakeholder Relations Specialist, who leads communications, thought leadership, PR, reputation and strategic stakeholder engagement.
