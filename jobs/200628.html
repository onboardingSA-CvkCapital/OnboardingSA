About the role

Introduction Grain Field Chickens, a subsidiary of VKB Agriculture (PTY) Ltd, is a dynamic role player in the integrated broiler industry with an abattoir in Reitz in the North-Eastern Free State. We offer you the opportunity to establish yourself within a professional, corporate environment in the countryside. Job Description The successful incumbent will be responsible for assisting a qualified artisan with the day-to-day duties in a particular trade and will have sufficient relevant experience to perform the assistant work required. Job Requirements • Grade 12 or NQF Level 4 Equivalent. • N2 Certificate - Electrical or Mechanical. • Related mechanical courses done will be advantageous. • 2 Years’ experience in a similar position in a chicken abattoir/ manufacturing plant. • Thorough understanding of health & safety requirements. • Basic awareness/ knowledge of Poultry Plant Machinery. • Willingness to work overtime incl. shifts, weekends/ holidays when required.Duties and Responsibilities Will be the assistant to the artisan with the following: • Will be responsible for maintaining, cleaning, and sorting of the workshop. • Limit downtime in the plant to prevent unnecessary loss of production. • Daily inspection of all machinery and tools inside the production plant and writing a report about inspection findings. • Ensure monthly planned maintenance on all machinery and tools are done as specified. • Inspect conveyers continuously to ensure conveyers are operational and functional in order for production to continue. • Repair all relevant buildings and infrastructure on request of management. • Apply and enforce ISO / HACCP / Occupational health and safety / 20 keys in the workplace (must be aware of all the safety requirements). • Assist with breakdowns in the plant to repair damage as soon as possible and ensure minimum breakdown time. • To liaise with internal departments on an ongoing basis to ensure the quality of service and products. • Perform any other duties as may be required by management.Skills Required • Good observation and analytical skills. • Good communication skills. • Attention to detail. • Excellent client service orientation. • Conflict handling. • Ability to work under pressure. • Ability to follow directions, instructions and work as a team
