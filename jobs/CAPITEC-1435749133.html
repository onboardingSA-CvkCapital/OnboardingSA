About the role

Capitec is hiring a Data Solutions Analyst, based in Stellenbosch. Over 23 million people bank with us. The data that powers every decision behind that - from product design to credit risk to client experience - needs to be clean, trusted and built to scale. That's where you come in. The Credit Access Data team is growing. We work at the intersection of granting, pricing and decision science, building the data foundations behind new credit products from the ground up. This isn't a maintenance role. You'll have a direct say in how our data warehouse grows, shape architectural decisions and work with senior stakeholders to turn complex business questions into reliable data that teams across our entire organisation can act on. You'll also lead a Community of Practice, bringing data practitioners together to share knowledge and raise standards across the business. We work openly, we move fast and we trust people who know their craft, if you see data as an outcome, not just an output, this is the role for you.

Responsibilities

  • Define how data is structured, sourced and stored - so that one trusted set of data powers many different teams and tools across our business
  • Work directly with business stakeholders to understand what they need, then turn those needs into clear data requirements and well-documented solutions
  • Write user stories with clear acceptance criteria and coordinate delivery across development and QA teams
  • Lead test plans, validate transformation logic and drive defect resolution - so that what gets built is exactly what was agreed
  • Define and maintain data quality rules, engage governance processes and make sure issues get resolved, not just reported
  • Build relationships across multiple business areas and adapt how you communicate depending on who's in the room

  • Ways of working:
  • Comfortable writing user stories and coordinating development and QA teams through full delivery cycles
