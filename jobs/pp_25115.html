About the role

Location: Johannesburg Scania is proud to launch its 2026 Graduate Programme, offering aspiring young professionals the opportunity to gain hands-on, practical exposure across four key business areas: Finance, Procurement, Assurance, and Marketing. This structured programme is designed to give graduates real workplace experience, supporting the day-to-day operations of their department while working closely with and assisting managers on a variety of tasks. Whether your interest lies in numbers, sourcing and supply, governance and risk, or brand and communications, this programme offers a genuine opportunity to build foundational workplace skills, gain exposure to how a leading commercial vehicle business operates, and take the first confident step in your career. We are looking for driven, reliable, and eager-to-learn individuals who are ready to bring energy and commitment to any of these four departments • Completed a relevant 3-year Diploma or Degree (see department requirements below) • South African citizen or permanent resident • Valid South African ID document • National Senior Certificate (Matric) • Strong work ethic, punctuality, and a commitment to continuous learning • 1. Finance Graduate Relevant Diploma or Degree in Accounting or Finance. The Finance graduate will support day-to-day finance functions, gaining exposure to core accounting operations and administrative processes within the department. Key Responsibilities • Daily banking • Vendor parking of invoices • Debtors and creditors age analysis • Cashbook, accounts receivable and accounts payable support • Reconciliations • Filing and completion of bank forms, and general administration 2. Procurement Graduate Relevant Diploma or Degree in Procurement, Supply Chain Management, Logistics, or a related field. The Procurement graduate will be exposed to the day-to-day operations of the procurement function, assisting the department manager with smaller, lower-responsibility tasks while learning how sourcing, ordering, and supplier administration work in practice. Key Responsibilities • Assisting with day-to-day procurement and sourcing operations • Supporting managers with smaller tasks and ad hoc requests • General procurement administration and documentation • Filing and record-keeping 3. Assurance Graduate Relevant Diploma or Degree in Internal Auditing, Risk Management, Accounting, or a related field. The Assurance graduate will support the day-to-day operations of the department, assisting the manager with smaller, lower-responsibility tasks and general administrative work related to governance, risk, and assurance processes. Key Responsibilities • Assisting with day-to-day assurance and administrative operations • Supporting managers with smaller tasks and ad hoc requests • Document preparation, review support, and record-keeping • Filing and general administration 4. Marketing Graduate Relevant Diploma or Degree in Marketing, Communications, Brand Management, or a related field. The Marketing graduate will support the day-to-day operations of the department, assisting the manager with smaller, lower-responsibility tasks across campaigns, content, and general marketing administration. Key Responsibilities • Assisting with marketing campaigns and content creation • Supporting social media and digital marketing activities • Assisting with event coordination and brand activations • Supporting basic market and competitor research • General marketing administration and documentation WHAT WE OFFER: • A structured graduate development programme across a leading commercial vehicle business • Hands-on exposure to real departmental operations and processes • Mentorship and support from experienced managers and staff • Practical workplace skills to strengthen your professional foundation • Exposure to how a leading commercial vehicle business operates • A valuable stepping stone for your future career
