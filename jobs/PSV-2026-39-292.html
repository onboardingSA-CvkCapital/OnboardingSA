About the role

The Department of Economic Development (Gauteng) is hiring a Registry Clerk: Executive Support in Directorate: Executive Support, based in Umnotho House Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R237,453 a year. How to apply: Apply online to the http:jobs.gauteng.gpg.gov.za website only. Closing date: 20 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Provide registry counter services.
  • Attend to registry clients and respond to telephonic and other registry-related enquiries.
  • Receive and register handdelivered mail, files and documents in the appropriate registry registers or electronic systems.
  • Date-stamp, sort and categorise incoming documents in accordance with established registry procedures.
  • Verify and acknowledge receipt of documents submitted at the registry counter, ensuring completeness and providing confirmation of receipt where required.
  • Distribute and route documents and files to the relevant officials or offices for further processing.
  • Process Incoming and Outgoing Correspondence.
  • Prepare outgoing correspondence for dispatch in accordance with departmental procedures.
  • Verify recipients’ details, addresses and supporting documents before dispatch.
  • Dispatch official correspondence through approved channels, including courier, postal services, email or hand delivery.
  • Monitor the delivery of outgoing correspondence and follow up on delayed or undelivered items.
  • Maintain proof of dispatch and delivery for accountability and audit purposes.
  • Track outstanding correspondence and report delays or discrepancies to the relevant supervisor.
  • Maintain an Effective Filing and Records Management Service.
  • Operate office equipment, including photocopiers, scanners, printers and binding machines, to support registry operations.
  • Reproduce and scan official documents as required for departmental recordkeeping and administrative purposes.
  • Monitor the availability of paper, toner and other consumables required for the effective operation of office equipment.
  • Perform basic routine maintenance and troubleshooting of office equipment in accordance with operating instructions.
  • Operate and Maintain Office Equipment for Registry Functions.
  • Report equipment faults and arrange for repairs or servicing through the appropriate departmental channels.
  • Coordinate the Processing of Documents for Archiving and Disposal.
  • Identify records due for archiving or disposal in accordance with approved records retention schedules.
  • Assess and prepare records for archiving or disposal in line with departmental records management procedures.
  • Compile and update inventories and lists of records earmarked for archiving or disposal.
  • Arrange the transfer of records approved for archiving to the designated archival storage facility.
  • Facilitate the disposal of records upon approval by the authorised officials and in accordance with applicable legislation and departmental policies.

Requirements

  • Grade 12.
  • 1-2 years’ experience in the Office of an Executive.
