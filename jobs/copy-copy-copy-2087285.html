About the role

Job Description: Key Responsibilities Technical Inspection and Repairs • Inspect returned products and perform technical fault diagnosis • Repair products where possible and determine root cause of failure • Evaluate product failures and provide detailed technical reports • Assist with technical evaluation of new products and NRCS compliance requirements • Support NRCS certification testing processes Field Support, Site Inspection and Technical Reporting • Attend installation sites where Spazio products are installed, when required • Inspect installed lighting fittings, drivers, wiring, and associated components • Identify and diagnose installation faults, product faults, electrical supply issues, or external causes • Assess installation quality and compliance with product specifications and electrical standards • Identify incorrect installation practices that may affect product performance or safety • Take photographic evidence and maintain accurate records during site inspections • Prepare clear, accurate, and professional technical reports outlining findings, root cause, and recommendations • Provide technical reports to management and clients to support technical decisions and warranty evaluations • Complete all call out reports accurately and maintain detailed records • Represent Spazio Lighting professionally when interacting with clients, contractors, consultants, and electricians onsite • Support the technical team with technical reports and customer assistance Return and Inventory Management • Manage and control returned stock inventory accurately • Ensure proper logging, tracking, and processing of returns • Maintain accurate records of repairs, failures, and returned products • Maintain and improve returns and repair processes to ensure efficiency and accuracy Operational and Administrative Duties • Ensure efficient daily operations within the Repairs and Returns function • Liaise effectively with Procurement, Warehouse, Technical, and Sales departments • Respond to customer queries and attend technical meetings when required • Prepare monthly and ad hoc reports as required by management • Adhere to company procedures, service level agreements, and compliance requirements • Perform additional duties, projects, and responsibilities as required by management
