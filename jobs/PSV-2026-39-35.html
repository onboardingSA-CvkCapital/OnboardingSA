About the role

The Government Printing Works is hiring a Director: Strategic Sourcing and Asset Management, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Link: GPW 26/012 Reference number to quote: GPW 26/012 Closing date: 17 November 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage sourcing and procurement services in the Department
  • Manage the accuracy, speed and efficiency in the procurement
  • Ensure that value for money, open and effective completion, ethics and fair dealing is adhered to
  • Ensure openness in the procurement process
  • Ensure continuous improvement in the efficiency of internal processes and systems
  • Ensure that adequate and timely information is provided to suppliers to enable them to participate in the procurement process
  • Ensure compliance with legislation in the submission
  • Ensure monitoring and full implementation of the approved procurement plan
  • Manage assets acquisition and disposal
  • Ensure the development of asset management and inventory control plans and the implementation thereof
  • Ensure the development, implementation and enforcement of proper asset managing control measures
  • Manage and monitor the effective and efficient physical verification of tangible and intangible assets
  • Manage and monitor the disposal of assets in line with all relative legislation regulations, prescripts and GPW policies and procedures
  • Coordinate the disposal committee, manage, investigate and report on all losses of assets
  • Ensure the preparation of complete and accurate financial disclosures in line with GRAP requirements
  • Manage inventory control services
  • Manage the development and enforcement of counting methodology
  • Daily and periodic counts are conducted and monitored
  • Effective reconciliation of monthly reports
  • Oversee and manage the developing, implementing of inventory control and asset management plans, enforce proper inventory and asset control measures.
  • Oversee the performing of inventory control functions.
  • Ensure that the monthly reconciliation to the general ledger is effectively managed: Manage the monthly and annual fixed asset reconciliation process.
  • Resolve reconciliation discrepancies and make corrections.
  • Certify the correction journal and review update of fixed asset register.
  • Ensure the running of monthly depreciation on AX.
  • Ensure the development of standard operating procedures and methodologies.
  • Ensure that correct processes are implemented and followed to avoid audit findings.
  • Manage financial compliance and prevent financial misconduct: Implement and monitor internal controls to prevent unauthorised, irregular, fruitless and wasteful expenditure.
  • Ensure compliance with the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA), Treasury Regulations, Supply Chain Management prescripts, and organisational policies.
  • Monitor financial transactions and expenditure to identify potential instances of financial misconduct and non-compliance.
  • Manage the Directorate
  • Develop the operational plan for the Directorate and ensure its implementation
  • Allocate and manage resources at functional levels to ensure the delivery on set targets for the Directorate
  • Formulate, identify Policy shortfalls and necessary amendments
  • Conduct financial planning and account for allocated budget
  • Ensure adherence to policy and statutory directives relevant to the functional terrain of the Directorate
  • Monitor, evaluate and report on the performance of the directorate

Requirements

  • A Grade 12 plus Degree (equivalent to NQF level 7 as recognised by SAQA) in Supply Chain/Purchasing/Logistics Management/Finance/Public Management/Public Administration.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at middle/senior level (Deputy Director) in a Supply Chain Management environment.
  • CIPS certificate will be an added advantage.
  • Valid Driver’s license.
  • Working knowledge of the tender procedures.
  • Added advantage: Estimating of printing work, proven knowledge of printing processes, equipment and paper, planning (estimating) and/or scheduling.
  • Other
  • Willingness to travel
  • Knowledge of the Public Service Regulatory Framework
  • Knowledge of the Public Service Act
  • Knowledge of the PFMA, Financial Management, National Treasury Regulations, prescripts, GRAP, and business practices
  • Knowledge of financial management, government budgeting processes, management accounting, revenue management, risk management, auditing, and forensic accounting.
  • Knowledge of public service/supply chain/financial legislative frameworks.
  • Knowledge of Corporate Governance (King Report).
