About the role

SENIOR PROCUREMENT OFFICER Drive Strategic Sourcing. Build Global Partnerships. Deliver Exceptional Value. Are you a strategic, commercially minded procurement professional who thrives on negotiation, supplier relationships and finding innovative ways to secure the right products at the right price? An exciting opportunity is available for an experienced Senior Procurement Officer to take ownership of a dynamic procurement function, balancing fast-turnaround local sourcing with high-volume, cost-effective international importing from China. This is a hands-on, high-impact role for someone who enjoys working across procurement, supplier management, international trade, logistics, quality control and cost optimisation. 🎯 PURPOSE OF THE POSITION The Senior Procurement Officer will be responsible for ensuring that the procurement department operates efficiently, strategically and seamlessly. You will develop and implement procurement strategies, build strong local and international supplier partnerships, negotiate competitive commercial terms and ensure that goods and services are sourced at the best overall value while maintaining quality, compliance and delivery standards. 🔑 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 1. Strategic Sourcing & Supplier Management • Identify, evaluate and qualify local branding houses, stockists and international/Chinese manufacturers. • Develop and maintain strong, long-term supplier relationships. • Build strategic partnerships with Chinese agents and factories to secure competitive pricing and priority production capacity. • Negotiate supplier agreements, pricing, lead times and commercial terms. • Develop a diversified supplier base to minimise supply-chain risk and over-reliance on individual suppliers. 2. Local & International Procurement Manage a dual-track procurement strategy: 🇨🇳 China Procurement • Manage the end-to-end procurement and importing process for high-volume orders. • Plan around extended international lead times and Chinese public holidays. • Coordinate production schedules and container space. • Monitor orders closely from initial quotation through to final delivery. 🇿🇦 Local Procurement • Source products from reliable local stockists and suppliers for urgent and fast-turnaround requirements. • Identify opportunities where local sourcing provides advantages in terms of speed, availability and overall cost. 3. Financial Negotiation & Cost Control • Negotiate highly competitive supplier pricing and payment terms. • Calculate the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for imported products. • Evaluate FOB and CIF pricing while factoring in: • Customs duties and tariffs • Agent fees • Freight costs • Clearing costs • Inland logistics • Other associated import expenses • Ensure procurement decisions deliver genuine commercial value, not simply the lowest unit price. • Negotiate appropriate payment structures with both international and local suppliers. 4. Quality Assurance & Branding Compliance • Establish and manage a robust sample approval and sign-off process. • Ensure pre-production samples, including blank and branded products, are approved before orders proceed. • Monitor supplier quality and product specifications. • Ensure imported products meet required company standards, regulatory requirements and applicable safety/certification standards. • Address quality concerns proactively with suppliers and implement corrective action where required. 5. Logistics, Customs & Supply Chain Coordination • Coordinate closely with freight forwarders, shipping lines and clearing agents. • Manage sea freight and air freight requirements according to order urgency and cost considerations. • Monitor shipments from production through to final delivery. • Ensure accurate tariff classifications and supporting documentation. • Work proactively to prevent customs delays, additional costs and unnecessary supply-chain disruptions. • Maintain clear communication between suppliers, logistics partners and internal teams.
