About the role

The Faircape Group constitutes a group of companies with interests in a number of activities including Healthcare, Retirement Holdings, Restaurant and Catering and related activities. Operating in six prime locations across the Western Cape, we are known for our commitment to excellence and innovation. We are seeking a skilled and experienced Senior Chef de Partie to join our Catering team at Noordhoek Manor in Noordhoek. Conveniently located just off Ou Kaapse Weg, the facility is approximately 10 minutes from Fish Hoek and easily accessible from the Southern Suburbs and Blue Route Mall area. The successful candidate will play a key role in supporting kitchen operations, maintaining exceptional food quality standards, guiding and mentoring junior kitchen staff, and ensuring the smooth delivery of daily meal services within our retirement and healthcare environment. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment, take pride in delivering beautifully prepared meals, and want to grow your career with a respected, forward-thinking company, this could be the perfect opportunity for you.
