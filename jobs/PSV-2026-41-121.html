About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring an Artisan Foreman (Electrical), based in Kimberley Regional Office. This is a permanent post, paying R397,329 a year. How to apply: Email Applications for the position of Artisan Foreman (Electrical) must be submitted to: RecruitKIM26-27@dpw.gov.za Reference number to quote: 2026/393 Closing date: 27 November 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure effective and efficient electrical maintenance services-: Inspect electrical equipment, installations and facilities to identify faults and maintenance requirements.
  • Perform and supervise preventative and corrective electrical maintenance work.
  • Service electrical equipment and installations according to prescribed maintenance schedules.
  • Perform electrical installation and upgrade work as required.
  • Monitor work in progress and check completed electrical maintenance work against specifications.
  • Provide technical guidance to Artisans on electrical maintenance activities.
  • Perform and supervise troubleshooting and repair of electrical equipment and installations-: Identify and troubleshoot electrical faults and system malfunctions in equipment, installations and wiring.
  • Use appropriate electrical testing equipment and tools to identify faults.
  • Perform and supervise repairs and replacement of faulty electrical components.
  • Test repaired electrical equipment and installations to confirm functionality.
  • Check that electrical repair work is performed according to applicable technical and safety requirements.
  • Identify electrical components, materials and equipment required for repairs.
  • Refer faults requiring higher level technical intervention to the Chief Artisan.
  • Perform administrative and related functions-
  • Maintain records and registers of electrical maintenance, repairs and faults attended to
  • Record materials, equipment and tools used for electrical work
  • Identify material and equipment requirements
  • Assist with obtaining quotations for required electrical materials and equipment
  • Compile and submit work progress and maintenance reports
  • Provide information required for maintenance and operational planning
  • Maintain records of scheduled and completed maintenance work
  • Apply relevant occupational health and safety requirements during electrical work
  • Supervise employees to ensure an effective service delivery-
  • Perform general supervision of employees
  • Allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by subordinates
  • Advise and lead subordinates with regard to all aspects of the work
  • Manage performance and conduct discipline subordinates
  • Ensure that all subordinates are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively
  • Ensure good record keeping
