About the role

IVANPLATS (Pty) Ltd, a subsidiary of Ivanhoe Mines, is advancing the development of its world-class palladium-rhodium-nickel-copper-gold project in the Limpopo Province near Mokopane. This unique mineral deposit will be exploited using large mechanised state-of-the-art underground mining methods, deploying the latest technology to develop this “mine of the future”. Suitably qualified and experienced candidates are invited to apply for the following vacancies required for the project development. Role purpose: The Occupational Hygienist will support the identification, assessment, monitoring, and control of occupational health hazards across the operation, ensuring compliance with applicable standards and contributing to a safer working environment Suitable candidates must possess the following minimum qualifications and skills: • National Senior Certificate, Grade 12 Matric or equivalent. • NQF Level 7 Qualification in Occupational Hygiene or related field, or Environmental Health. • Chamber of Mines (CoM) Intermediate Certificate in Mine Environmental Control (IMEC). • Chamber of Mines (CoM) Certificate in Mine Environmental Control (COMEC). • Registration with the South African Institute of Occupational Hygiene (SAIOH) as an Occupational Hygienist. • Valid drivers license. • Knowledge of legal framework governing South African Mines. • Computer literate in MS Office. • Management of Occupational Hygiene Systems. • Data Science and analysis. • Linkage of Occupational Hygiene measurements and medical records.Suitable candidates must affiliate with a Professional Body: • Registration with the South African Institute of Occupational Hygiene (SAIOH) as an Occupational Hygienist l • Member/Associate with the Mine Ventilation Society Of South Africa (MVSSA). Suitable candidates must have the following work experience: • Minimum of 8 years’ experience in the field of and Occupational Hygiene and Mine Environmental Engineering. • Minimum of 4 years’ experience in Metallurgical Production and Processing Plants.
