About the role

Draughtsman (3D Scanning & Revit Modelling) VKB Properties | Head Office, Reitz 3-Year Fixed-Term Contract Introduction to the VKB Group The VKB Group is an agricultural company, providing support to farmers and producing straight-from-the farm-foods for consumers. Apart from the various consumer brands, the VKB Group is also represented by its regional brands: VKB, GWK and NTK. Our roots are firmly planted in agriculture with farming at our core, continuously growing and evolving throughout the years. Starting with providing everything needed on the farm to produce food, we now also operate in all areas of the food supply chain: processing, logistics, and national and global market channels. This “house of brands” we’ve established with the support of our farmer shareholders is the foundation for building the agribusiness of the future. Build the Digital Future of South Africa's Agricultural Infrastructure What if your Revit models became the foundation for managing some of South Africa's largest grain handling and industrial facilities? At VKB Group, we are investing in the digital transformation of our national property portfolio. We are looking for a technically skilled and forward-thinking Draughtsman (3D Scanning & BIM Modelling) to join our Properties Team and help create the digital record of critical infrastructure across our operations. This is not a traditional drafting role. You will work with advanced 3D laser scanning, point cloud technology, BIM workflows and Revit modelling to transform real-world facilities into accurate digital assets that support future planning, maintenance and development. Reporting directly to the Group Architect, you will have the opportunity to travel across the VKB footprint, capturing, documenting and modelling grain handling facilities, warehouses, industrial plants and commercial properties that form the backbone of one of South Africa's leading agribusinesses. Why Join VKB Properties? This opportunity offers far more than traditional drafting work: ✔ Work on large-scale grain silo complexes, industrial facilities and strategic infrastructure projects ✔ Gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge 3D laser scanning and reality capture technologies ✔ Build detailed BIM models and digital twins of existing facilities ✔ Travel to operational sites across multiple provinces ✔ Contribute to the long-term management of one of South Africa's largest agricultural property portfolios ✔ Work alongside architects, engineers and property specialists on impactful projects ✔ Be part of a team that embodies the VKB values of Humble, Hungry, Smart, Bold and Team Player What You Will Be Doing As part of the VKB Properties Team, you will: • Perform 3D laser scanning of grain silo
