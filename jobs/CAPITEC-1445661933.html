About the role

Capitec is hiring a Data Analyst, based in Stellenbosch. At Capitec, we're building a bank that puts simplicity, innovation, and data-driven decision-making at the heart of everything we do. We are looking for a Data Analyst II to join our Credit Risk team and play a key role in shaping credit granting decisions across multiple lending products. This opportunity is ideal for candidates from Financial Advisory, Risk Advisory, Quantitative Analytics, Actuarial Consulting, Data Science, Decision Science, and Credit Risk Analytics backgrounds, particularly those who have developed their careers within leading consulting firms and are looking to apply their expertise within one of South Africa's most innovative banks Why this role? You will work closely with Credit Risk Owners and Decision Science teams to support and enhance application credit granting strategies. The role offers exposure to a broad range of credit products, analytical modelling, scorecard development, and model validation while influencing critical lending decisions that impact both business performance and client outcomes. What you'll be doing Partner with the Application Credit Risk team to support credit granting strategies across multiple lending products. Translate complex data into actionable insights that improve credit decisioning. Contribute to model development, analytical modelling support, and model validation activities.
