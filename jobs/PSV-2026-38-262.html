About the role

The Department of Community Safety (Gauteng) is hiring an Assistant Director: Transport and Logistics in Supply Chain Management, based in Head Office Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Applicants must apply online at: www.gautengonline.gov.za / http://professionaljobcentre.gpg.gov.za. Reference number to quote: REFS/057141 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage transport service according to departmental policy.
  • Ensure that the is a vehicle asset register.
  • Monitor vehicle movement.
  • Manage and keep records on accidents.
  • Licensing Department owned vehicles.
  • Verifying and processing the monthly invoice.
  • Compiling Payment for g-flee/stannic.
  • Issuing Trip authority.
  • Booking vehicle for repairs and maintenance.
  • Facilitating the renewal of petrol cards.
  • Processing fuel claims and requesting of new fleet.
  • Receive a requisition and process requisition.
  • Receive and check goods.
  • Capture goods on relevant registers.
  • Return damaged incorrect and substandard goods.
  • Issue goods as required.
  • Stock taking according to stock taking plan.
  • Comparison of stock counted with official records.
  • Capturing Stock count on SAP.
  • Receive a cellphone request and process the request.
  • Cellphone Administration (Blacklisting, Insurance claims and repairing).
  • Processing payment and managing cellphone contracts.
  • Maintaining Cellphone register.
  • Calculate rate per kilo and process the request.
  • Assisting with quotes and receiving applications.
  • Process application, calculate rate per kilo and process the request.
  • Administrate the scheme process (maintenance, repairs and accident).
  • Workshops for users from Head Office and Regional Offices.
  • Provide information and support documentation required by AG and GAS.
  • Advise users procedures and reforms.
  • General supervision of employees.
  • Ensure that all are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively.
  • Develop, implement and monitor work systems and processes to ensure efficient and effective functioning.
  • Manage performance, conduct and discipline.

Requirements

  • Matric Certificate plus a 3-year National Diploma (NQF level 6)/Degree (NQF level 7) ln Supply Chain Management/Logistics/Transportation Management.
  • 3 years’ experience in supervisory/Logistics Manager/Transport Officer/Transport Admin Clerk.
  • Certificates In Computer Training Course, Call centre, Transport and Fleet Management, Advance Management Development Programme, Monitoring and Evaluation and Fleet Management will be an added advantage.
  • All valid relevant driving licenses (A and EC).
  • No criminal record or case pending against you.
  • Knowledge and Skills:
  • MS Word
  • MS Excel
  • MS PowerPoint and MS Outlook: Internet and E-mail
  • Customer relationship management, ELS and SAP
  • Interpersonal relations, conflict management, and communications
  • PERSAL, FLEETMATIC, C VISION and Transport Policy
  • Interviewing, negotiation and facilitation skills
  • Inventory policy, cellphone policy and presentation skills
  • Report writing, computer literacy and advanced driving
