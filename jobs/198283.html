About the role

About the Role: We are currently seeking an experienced and competent Night Receptionist at our beautiful Safari Hotel & Convention Centre in Rustenburg, North West province.  The successful candidate will serve as the first point of contact for guests arriving during night-time hours.  This role is primarily focused on delivering exceptional customer service while managing front desk operations, ensuring a welcoming and efficient experience for all guests and visitors. Key Responsibilities: • Greet and check in guests as they arrive, ensuring a smooth and friendly welcome. • Manage reservations, handle inquiries, and provide accurate information about hotel services and local attractions. • Handle guest requests efficiently, ensuring their needs are met promptly and courteously. • Process payments, manage cash transactions, and maintain accurate financial records throughout your shift • Oversee the security of the hotel during night hours, ensuring the safety of guests and staff. • Coordinate with housekeeping and maintenance teams regarding guest needs and room status. • Address any guest complaints or issues with professionalism, following up to ensure resolution. • Prepare night audit reports and maintain organized records of guest information and transactions.Requirements: • Proven experience in a customer-facing role, preferably in hospitality or hotel management. • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a strong focus on customer service. • Familiarity with hotel management software and basic computer proficiency. • Ability to work independently and remain calm under pressure during busy periods. • Strong attention to detail and organizational skills to manage multiple tasks effectively.Qualifications & Experience: • Grade 12 or equivalent qualification • Must have 2+ years of reception and reservations experience within the hospitality industry • Must be able to work on Protel or Opera Booking system (PMS) • Certification in hospitality management or related fields is an advantage. • Knowledge of local attractions and amenities to better assist guests. • Strong customer liaison skills and a friendly and professional manner essential.
