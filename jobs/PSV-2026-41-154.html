About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Deputy Director: Asset Management in Finance WTE Dir: Asset Management, based in Pretoria Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 271126/12 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Take overall accountability for the proper accounting of all assets in the Asset Register in compliance with National Treasury Guidelines.
  • Review the reconciliation of the Asset Register against the Trial Balance and supporting records, and ensure reconciling items are cleared.
  • Review documents, submissions and journals, and ensure journals are correctly posted in the accounting system.
  • Review the updating of new additions, transfers and disposals, and review the monthly Asset Register reconciliation and asset movement reports.
  • Review all entries made on the Asset Register against minimum requirements.
  • Ensure assets earmarked for disposal are updated in the Asset Register.
  • Ensure all losses and disposed assets are retired from the Asset Register in line with policy and approval.
  • Ensure monthly depreciation is run and correctly posted against the different accounts.
  • Review asset management policies and procedures and ensure they are implemented by users across the component.
  • Promote ethical conduct, accountability, confidentiality and integrity in the management of assets.
  • Ensure internal controls over assets are designed, implemented and monitored.
  • Manage movable assets across the component.
  • Manage finance leases, including lease registers, expiry monitoring and verification.
  • Manage assets under construction and construction equipment, including capitalisation, transfers and reporting.
  • Review the remaining useful lives of assets and test assets for impairment.
  • Coordinate and manage the asset verification process, including oversight of staff.
  • Compile and approve Asset Management monthly reports for submission to Senior Management.
  • Manage the budget for the Sub- Directorate: Movable Assets, including providing inputs, monitoring expenditure and ensuring value for money.
  • Submit inputs for the preparation of quarterly and annual financial statements, including disclosure notes on assets, depreciation, impairments, leases and disposals.
  • Manage the internal and external audit process, including AGSA, by preparing audit files, responding to audit queries, implementing audit findings and monitoring action plans to closure.
  • Ensure audit readiness throughout the year through accurate record-keeping and timely reconciliations.
  • Manage the performance of staff within the Sub-Directorate against performance work plans.
  • Ensure staff members are properly trained, developed and supported to perform their duties.
  • Build a culture of accountability, ethical conduct and continuous improvement within the component.

Requirements

  • A relevant NQF level 7 qualification with accounting three (3) as Major Subject.
  • 3 years related experience at supervisory/management level (ASD) in Asset Management within accrual system.
  • Knowledge and understanding on Human Resource Management Legislation, policies, practices and procedures.
  • Knowledge and experience of SAP.
  • Knowledge of Public Finance Management Act (PFMA), GRAP standards and Treasury Regulations.
  • Public service anti-corruption strategy and anti-corruption and fraud prevention measures.
  • Knowledge of equal opportunities and affirmative action guidelines and laws.
  • Knowledge of administrative and clerical procedures and systems.
  • Departmental policies and procedures.
  • Government financial systems.
  • Principles and practice of financial accounting.
  • Framework for managing performance information.
  • Business strategy transaction and alignment.
  • Problem solving and analysis.
  • People and diversity management.
  • Client orientation and customer focus.
  • Good communication skills (verbal and written).
  • Accountability and ethical conduct.
