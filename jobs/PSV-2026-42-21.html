About the role

The Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment is hiring an Artisan Production Grade A: Aquaculture Innovation and Technology Development, based in Cape Town (Sea Point, Marine Aquaculture Research Facility). This is a permanent post, paying R253,341 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.environment.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: FIM31/2026 Closing date: 07 December 2026, 16:00. No late applications will be accepted Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor the Marine Aquaculture Research Facility (MARF) production systems.
  • Assemble minor components for aquaculture systems operations.
  • Ensure all produced or adapted components meet operational and safety requirements.
  • Perform routine backwashing of filtration systems and ensure correct functioning of seawater circulation systems.
  • Perform maintenance and repairs of life support systems of the MARF.
  • Undertake minor plumbing repairs including leaks, pipe replacement, fittings and valve adjustments.
  • Perform minor electrical tasks.
  • Test repaired or serviced systems to ensure functionality and compliance with operational standards.
  • Perform administrative and related activities.
  • Maintain accurate maintenance logs, repair record and systems inspection registers.
  • Provide inputs into maintenance planning and operational scheduling.
  • Provide inputs into aquarium systems and adaptations.
  • Provide support in minor layout adaptations and modifications of plumbing, pipework and pump configurations within aquaculture systems.

Requirements

  • Appropriate Trade Test Certificate in Electrical/Plumbing.
  • Knowledge of legal compliance regarding electrical and plumbing.
  • Technical knowledge of electronical systems and plumbing repairs.
  • Knowledge and skills of aquaculture/aquarium systems.
  • Planning and organising and communication.
  • A valid driver’s licence.
