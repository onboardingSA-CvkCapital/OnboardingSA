About the role

The Receptionist is responsible for delivering exceptional guest service by ensuring a smooth and efficient front office operation. The successful candidate will be the first point of contact for guests, managing check-ins and check-outs, handling reservations, processing payments, and responding to guest enquiries. The Receptionist must have strong hands-on experience with Opera Property Management System (PMS) and demonstrate excellent communication, organisational, and customer service skills.Key ResponsibilitiesWelcome guests in a warm, friendly, and professional manner.Perform efficient guest check-ins and check-outs using Opera PMS.Verify guest details, process registrations, and allocate rooms.Handle guest enquiries, requests, and complaints promptly and professionally.Manage reservations, amendments, cancellations, and room allocations.Answer and direct telephone calls and respond to email enquiries.Process guest payments, invoices, deposits, and refunds accurately.Balance cash, card transactions, and daily shift reports.Maintain accurate guest profiles and reservation records in Opera PMS.Coordinate with Housekeeping and Maintenance to ensure rooms are guest-ready.Communicate effectively with all hotel departments to ensure seamless guest service.Promote hotel facilities, services, and special offers to maximise revenue.Maintain a clean, organised, and welcoming reception area.Assist with concierge services, including local information and guest arrangements.Ensure compliance with company policies, procedures, and health and safety standards.Complete daily reception administration and reporting.
