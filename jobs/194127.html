About the role

NBC Holdings is a leading force in the South African employee benefits arena, providing a comprehensive range of employee benefits, products and services to our valued clients. We are looking for a Dynamic Senior Consultant that will be responsible for efficiently and effectively managing a portfolio of funds, ensuring compliance with quality, time and standards. Driving business development initiatives and contributing to the overall profitability of the organisation. This role requires a strong focus on client satisfaction, business retention, and strategic engagement with key stakeholders, including trade unions and employer representatives.
