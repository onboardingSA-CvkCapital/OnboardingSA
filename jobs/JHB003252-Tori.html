About the role

The Senior Lodge Anchor / Assistant Lodge Manager is responsible for supporting the Lodge Manager in the day-to-day operation of the lodge while ensuring exceptional guest experiences and maintaining the highest standards of service. This role oversees front-of-house operations, assists with staff management, coordinates interdepartmental communication, and ensures the smooth running of all lodge activities. The successful candidate leads by example and contributes to achieving operational excellence and guest satisfaction.Key ResponsibilitiesAssist the Lodge Manager with the daily management and operation of the lodge.Lead and supervise Front Office, Housekeeping, Food & Beverage, and Guest Services teams.Ensure exceptional guest service from arrival to departure.Welcome VIP guests and manage special requests.Handle guest feedback and resolve complaints professionally and promptly.Conduct daily operational briefings and departmental meetings.Coordinate guest itineraries, transfers, activities, and special occasions.Monitor service standards across all departments to ensure consistency.Assist with staff scheduling, performance management, coaching, and training.Support recruitment, onboarding, and employee development initiatives.Ensure compliance with lodge policies, health and safety regulations, and company standards.Monitor stock levels and assist with procurement where required.Assist with budgeting, cost control, and operational reporting.Oversee front office administration, reservations, and guest billing when necessary.Ensure the lodge and guest areas are maintained to luxury hospitality standards.Work closely with Maintenance, Safari, Spa, and Kitchen teams to ensure seamless operations.Act as the Lodge Manager in their absence.
