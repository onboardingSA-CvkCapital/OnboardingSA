About the role

Our client is seeking an experienced Engineering Manager to provide technical leadership across multidisciplinary mining projects. This is an excellent opportunity for a seasoned engineering professional with extensive EPCM experience, strong mechanical engineering expertise, and proven knowledge of bulk material handling and conveyor systems. The successful candidate will play a key role in delivering high-quality engineering solutions from concept through to commissioning and project close-out. Key Responsibilities • Lead multidisciplinary engineering teams across mining infrastructure projects. • Manage engineering delivery through all project phases, including studies, detailed design, procurement support, construction, commissioning and handover. • Provide technical governance for conveyor belt systems and bulk material handling infrastructure. • Review engineering designs, calculations and technical deliverables to ensure compliance with project and industry standards. • Coordinate engineering interfaces between design, procurement, construction and commissioning teams. • Manage client relationships and serve as the primary engineering point of contact. • Support project planning, scheduling, budgeting and change management activities. • Participate in design reviews, constructability assessments, risk workshops and safety reviews. • Provide technical support during procurement, supplier evaluations and construction activities. • Contribute to proposals, tenders and business development initiatives. Minimum Requirements • BSc, BEng or BTech in Mechanical Engineering. • Professional registration with ECSA as Pr Eng or Pr Tech Eng is preferred. • Project Management certification will be advantageous. • 10–15 years' relevant mining engineering experience. • At least 5 years' experience leading multidisciplinary engineering teams. • Extensive experience with conveyor systems, bulk material handling and mining infrastructure. • Strong understanding of EPCM project delivery. • Knowledge of South African and international engineering standards. • Excellent leadership, communication and stakeholder management skills.
