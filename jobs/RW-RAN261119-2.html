About the role

Rand Water is hiring a Senior Mechanical Technician (M Hay band) in Bulk Water Services - Operations, based at Zuikerbosch, Three Rivers East, Vereeniging. To provide technical assistance to the Principal Mechanical Diploma Technician so as to ensure that; optimal asset integrity and productivity, increased availability and reliability, reduced cost and risk and improved profitability. To ensure our asset care activities achieve 98% availability of our production equipment through the latest maintenance techniques.

Responsibilities

  • Provide technical expertise and assistance.
  • Carry out vibration measurement on a monthly basis for each critical plant
  • Draft inspection report for 5 yearly pump assessment
  • Draft pump performance reports after commissioning
  • Coordinate and communicate with other discipline within maintenance cluster
  • Assist in inspection of equipment for its healthiness and mechanical by conducting routine plant inspection
  • Perform technical investigation & root cause analysis
  • Perform continuous improvement i.e reliability and safety

Requirements

  • Grade 12 ( National Senior Certificate)
  • National Diploma in Mechanical Engineering
  • 3 years experience in mechanical maintenance improvement environment of rotary equipment in water industry which includes preventative corrective and overhaul.
  • Valid driver's licence with the ability to pass RW K53 test
  • Experience in centrifusgul pump (back pull out / vertically split, end supervised /horizontally split, multistage, vertical, submersible maintenance
  • Experience in centrifugal blowers, reciprocating compressors, axial and radial compressors, gearboxes, shaft alignment, mechanical seals, white metal bearing
  • Experience in condition monitoring, vibration analysis, thermography ultrasonic and oil analysis.
  • Knowledge
  • Application of RCM principles.
  • Sound knowledge of bearings, linkages, lubrication, loads & tolerances.
  • Mechanical benchmarking techniques.
  • Advanced mechanical fault finding techniques
  • Knowledge of pumps and valves
  • Knowledge on water purification processes.

  • Planning and organising
  • Implementation of asset management Principles

  • Good interpersonal skills (verbal and written)
  • Ability to work with others in the teams to ensure site objectives are met.
  • Attitude
