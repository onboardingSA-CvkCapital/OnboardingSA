About the role

LiquidCapital is a world leader in its field of specialisation offering a variety of financial products for motor vehicles. LiquidCapital aims to creatively solve problems and add value to people by ensuring Mobility for Good. LiquidCapital forms part of the Motus Group, a leading JSE listed Company in the Automotive Industry where performance is driven by three keystones, 1. Leaders, 2. Innovation & 3. Customer Champions. LiquidCapital is seeking to employ a qualified and experienced Policy Admin Team Leader.  The successful candidate will join a dynamic and highly skilled Policy Admin Team. The successful candidate will report directly to the Policy Admin Manager. JOB DESCRIPTION: To plan, coordinate and control the quality of a team's work activities that supports the processing and administration of policies, contributing to client satisfaction and effective service delivery. KEY DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES: PROCESS • Assist a team with the identification and resolution of problems and provide solutions that lead to: • improved operational service delivery and quality. • Execute own and teamwork in line with governance and compliance processes. • Identify and apply known solutions to operational problems within the team environment and escalate unresolved issues. • Monitor and evaluate operational processes for quality and effectiveness and notify stakeholders accordingly. • Monitor and report on own and team's transactional activities to provide timely information for decision making in area of accountability. • Plan, coordinate and deliver own and team activities to ensure that agreed standards and operational objectives are met. • Understand and execute administrative functions as required by the position. • Provide operational support related to policy and administrative activities and initiatives to contribute to the overall growth and profitability of the business area.FINANCE • Coordinate and monitor daily work activities within the team environment to prevent financial losses. CLIENT • Deliver services and/or products that create a culture which aims to meet or exceed customers' expectations in the business. • Coordinate team activities to meet and exceed customer expectations.PEOPLE • Act as change agent and provide support to the team throughout the implementation of identified change initiatives. • Coordinate and participate in learning interventions to establish a learning and growth culture within the team.QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE: • 2 - 3 years' experience in a similar environment • Grade 12 • Advanced Excel • Proven track record of managing a teamSKILLS AND PERSONAL ATTRIBUTES: • Excellent telephone skills with professional telephone manner, and the capability to communicate with enthusiasm and drive • Excellent communication (verbal and written) - Good command of the English language • Advantageous – Bilingual/Multilingual • People skills • Ability to manage and lead a team • Assertive • Ability to multi-task - excellent time management • Accuracy and attention to detail • Ability to work under pressure • Own and live up to company values.
