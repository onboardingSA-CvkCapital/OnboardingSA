Responsibilities

  • Provide Advanced Life Support and Advanced Medical Services for the District and Sub-District.
  • Respond to emergency calls in the district.
  • Assist with community awareness programmes within the district.
  • Conduct the treatments of Hepatitis B programme of staff and monitor the progress thereof.
  • Lecture patient management and demonstrate use of equipment to the internal operational staff, other health sector staff and public: participate in practical exercise.
  • Assist Emergency Care Officer’s with Emergency calls (red codes) and critical patients.
  • Actively participate in quality assurance projects.
  • Control, issue and check drugs, record issues in register and submit registers.
  • Wash, clean and disinfect the interior and exterior of the vehicles, equipment and maintain unit in a good working order.
  • Conduct Proper Shift handover.
  • Report discrepancies on equipment, motor vehicles, services and make recommendations to the Shift Leader.
  • Maintain accurate and reliable records.
  • Abide by all standard operating procedures.
