About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Restitution Advisor in Operational Management, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R605,742 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 524 Stanza Bopape and Steve Biko Street, Suncardia Building, 6th Floor, Arcadia, 0002 or by email to Post377@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/377 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian and White Males, and Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Check legal compliance.
  • Check research report e.g Rule 3 and Rule 5.
  • Check section 42D.
  • Check Gazette Report.
  • Attend to negotiations with the farmer/landowner.
  • Prepare response to the representation for the farmer/landowner.
  • Check Section 42 E expropriation.
  • Check claimant verification.
  • Liaise with the landowner.
  • Check financial compensation funds.
  • Prepare settlement agreement.
  • Check CPA constitution.
  • Provide litigation support in the Restitution Branch.
  • Draft referrals.
  • Issue notice of instruction to appoint state attorney.
  • Facilitate the process of legal representation.
  • Exchange of court document (Pleadings).
  • Attend courts.
  • Attend Pre-trials.
  • Serve referrals on interested parties.
  • File the notice of referrals.
  • Facilitate the implementation of court orders.
  • Facilitate signing of Affidavits by RLCC/CLCC.
  • Attend consultations with State Attorney and Advocates.
  • Draft legal documents.
  • Draft legal documents deed of sale.
  • Draft settlement agreement.
  • Draft sale agreement.
  • Add draft distribution agreement.
  • Draft Memorandum of Understanding.
  • Draft lease agreement.
  • Draft caretaker ship agreement.
  • Draft legal opinion and documents.
  • Transfer private or state land.
  • Facilitate the registration of transfers.
  • Facilitate the signing of agreements for both parties.
  • Facilitate transfer of payments.
  • Facilitate signing of POA by the DG/City Manager.
  • Issue instruction to conveyancers to invest the first payment of the purchase price.
  • Coordinate and intervene in obtaining appointment letters.
  • Monitor the transfer regularly on the weekly basis and inspection.
  • Issue instruction to Conveyancer for transfer and pay the balance.
  • Issue instruction to Conveyancer inclusive of Section 42D.
  • Facilitate the requested clearance rates from municipality.
  • Prepare memorandums for payment of rates.
  • Acquire proof of payment of rates.
  • Obtain certificates from municipality.
  • Monitor the transfer process until the end and inform claimants.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements
  • A Grade 12 Certificate and an appropriate LLB or B Proc degree (NQF Level 7) or any related relevant qualification
  • At least 4 years extensive post-qualification legal professional and advisory experience
  • Experience in conveyancing and vetting of documents
  • Post qualification experience in court litigation
  • A valid driver’s licence
  • Job related knowledge
  • Knowledge of Restitution of Land Rights Act
  • 22 of 1994
  • Law and other relevant acts and Legislative prescripts
  • Specialised knowledge of Constitutional Law
  • Law of contracts
  • Knowledge of SA law, in particular land reform laws
  • Knowledge of conveyancing and vetting of documents
  • Job related skills
  • Proven supervisory and management ability
  • Ability to draft legal opinions and contracts
  • Negotiation, research and very good drafting ability
  • Ability to think independently, analytically and innovatively
  • Good problem-solving ability
  • Mediation and conflict resolution
  • Computer literacy
  • Skills in court litigation
  • Preparedness to travel and work irregular hours under tremendous pressure
