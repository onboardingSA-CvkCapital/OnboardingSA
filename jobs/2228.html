About the role

To ensure your application is complete, you must submit ALL documents listed under the inherent requirements, as well as the following standard documentation: •    A detailed Curriculum Vitae. •    Copies of your qualifications, or official proof of qualifications obtained (If you hold foreign qualifications, please include SAQA accreditation). •    A copy of your identity document (South African ID or passport for foreign nationals). •    Confirmation of employment in the form of a letter, appointment contract, service certificate, or recent payslip from your current or most recent employer (applicable to external applicants only). Duties and responsibilities: Project Administration •    Compile, keep and update detailed records of project participants. •    Prepare all registration forms and accompanying documents for enrolment meetings. •    Prepare attendance registers, feedback and assessment forms for training sessions and mentoring visits. •    Travel from time to time to fulfil project requirements. Material Centre Administration •    Compile training material lists to provide service providers with the necessary detail in terms of each project's activity material requirements. •    Perform technical editing, quality control and final computer layout and compile module electronically. •    Supervise and coordinate the manufacturing of project activity material and worksheets for teacher professional development training sessions. •    Delegate and oversee the packaging of project activity material and worksheets in the Key Concepts in Science material centre. •    Dispatch project material to participating teachers by means of courier or other mechanisms according to pre-determined timelines. Quality Control •    Perform quality control of all project training material, including modules before sending to project participants. •    Perform quality control of delivered items from suppliers. •    Perform quality control and reconcile the number and content of boxes with details of the project participants and material lists before dispatching it to participating teachers. •    Follow up on receipt of dispatched activity material by participating teachers. Financial Administration •    Request quotations from suppliers and follow UFS procurement policy and procedures. •    Reconcile  expenditure relating to  project activity material and worksheets. •    Maintain detailed records of income versus expenditure for the worksheets and activity material for the different projects in order to compile an updated budget performance document. •    Conduct cost analysis/calculation regarding different projects in collaboration and on request of the Project manager/ Programme Director. Inherent requirements: •    Bachelor’s degree or a Advanced Diploma/B.Tech degree/Postgraduate Certificate on NQF Level 7. •    A minimum of two (2)  years’ relevant  experience in project and/or financial administration as well as stakeholder management. •    A valid driver's license (attach a copy). Recommendations: •    Working knowledge of UFS procurement policies and procedures. •    Demonstrate proficiency in the use of ICT and  digital platforms. •    Experience within the higher education environment. Required competencies: •    Results Orientated: -    The ability to set high standards, establish tough goals, and to work to achieve success. -    The ability to deal calmly and effectively with high stress situations. •    Strategic Thinking: -    The ability to deal with several activities at a time. -    The ability to plan work and to follow plans. •    Business Acumen: -    The ability to adhere to rules and strictly follow work regulations. -    Proficient in using MS Office. •    Leading: -    The ability to lead, take charge of situations, and offer opinions and directions to others. -    The ability to be cooperative with others, display a good-natured attitude and encourage people to work together. •    Building Coalitions: -    The ability to interact with others and establish personal connections with people. -    The ability to make decisions through consultation, collaboration and working with close supervision. Assumption of duties: 01 September 2026 or as soon as possible. Closing date: 30 July 2026.
