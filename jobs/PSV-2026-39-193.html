About the role

The Department of Education (Eastern Cape) is hiring a Deputy Director: Events Management, based in Zwelitsha. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 to R1,098,195 a year. How to apply: Applications must be submitted via the provincial e-recruitment system accessible at: https://ecprov.gov.za (under Careers) and/or at https://erecruitment.ecotp.gov.za. The Provincial e-Recruitment System is available 24/7 and closes at 23: 59 on the Closing Date. To report any e- Recruitment System challenges send an email with your (i) ID Number, (ii) profile email address, (iii) details of your issue to: erecruitment@ecdoe.gov.za. Do not send any applications to this email address; should you do so, your applications/ CV will be regarded as lost and will not be considered. Technical support is limited to working hours: (08:00 16:30 Mon-Thursday and 08:00- 16:00 on Friday). Direct all enquiries to the specified enquiries person or email Address. Reference number to quote: DOE02/10/2026 Closing date: 13 November 2026. Applications received after closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the development and maintenance of the event management framework and instruments.
  • Manage the provision of logistical arrangements for departmental events.
  • Manage formal interaction and liaison with other event role players, inclusive of security and protocol personnel.
  • Manage the provisioning of advice on the design of the marketing tools.
  • Coordinate research of venues, suppliers and contractors for events.
  • Manage the coordination of suppliers and all event logistics.
  • Manage all pre-event planning and drills.
  • Oversee the dismantling and removal of event and clearance of the venue.
  • Maintain high standards by ensuring that the team/section produces excellent work in terms of quality/quantity and timeliness.
  • Resolve problems of motivation and control with minimum guidance from manager.
  • Delegate functions to staff based on individual potential provide the necessary guidance and support and afford staff adequate training and development opportunities.
  • Ensure timeous development and implementation of Work Plans and Personal Development Plans (PDP’s) for all subordinates.
  • Manage daily employee performance and ensure timely Performance Assessments of all subordinates.
  • Ensure management, maintenance and safekeeping of assets.

Requirements

  • An appropriate recognized (3-year) undergraduate NQF level 6/7 in Events Management, Business Administration/Management, Communications, Project Management, Public Relations or any related field as recognised by SAQA.
  • 3 - 5 years at Assistant Director level in Event Coordination environment.
  • Experience in Protocol, Communications and Stakeholder Coordination will be an added advantage.
  • A valid driver’s license.

  • Familiarity with corporate Identity (CI) and branding standards.
  • Knowledge of Education Sector will be added advantage.
