About the role

Standard Bank is hiring a Strategy Enablement Head, based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. Facilitate Business Line/ Group Function (GF)/ Region strategy enablement by translating strategic ambition into integrated, scalable initiatives. Drive alignment, execution, and transformation within the Business Line/GF and geographies. Influence decision-making, shape strategic governance, and embed strategy into the organization's operating rhythm, culture, and performance architecture. Essential Functions Shape strategy by advising the executive team on market positioning, competitive advantage, and strategic trade-offs applicable to area of responsibility Business Line/GF/Region. Lead strategic scenario planning and foresight for long term horizon. Embed execution discipline through tools, standards, and capability building and resolve systemic execution barriers and escalate risks to executive forums. Track and ensure delivery against strategic KPIs and performance frameworks to ensures that strategic outcomes are realised.
