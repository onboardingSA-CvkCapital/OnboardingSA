About the role

The Department of Community Safety (Gauteng) is hiring 3 Senior Provincial Inspector posts in Traffic Management, based in Head Office Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: Applicants must apply online at: www.gautengonline.gov.za / http://professionaljobcentre.gpg.gov.za. Reference number to quote: REFS/057011 Closing date: 09 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.
