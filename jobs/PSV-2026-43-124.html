About the role

The Department of International Relations and Cooperation is hiring a Director: Marketing in Public Diplomacy, based in Head Office (Pretoria). This is a contract post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Please e-mail your application to Dirmarketing26@dirco.gov.za Reference number to quote: DIRCO1127/05 Closing date: 21 December 2026 - Applications received after the closing date will not be considered. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and organise national and international events to promote Brand South Africa: Develop marketing strategy and implementation plan.
  • Design and monitor guidelines on methods to organise functions and events.
  • Develop event-specific concept papers and project plans.
  • Maintain the image of the department and country by implementing first-class events both domestically and internationally.
  • Manage service providers including venues, event management thereof (guests list, benefits etc) including sponsors.
  • Manage Departmental branding and corporate image
  • Develop and implement departmental branding policy in line with GCIS prescripts
  • Develop and implement departmental branding and corporate imaging for all business units
  • Develop physical branding and guidelines for Missions abroad
  • Oversee the development of branding for national and international events
  • Disseminate branding to key departmental stakeholders
  • Manage the sourcing and provision of South African promotional and exhibition materials
  • Guide South African Missions and business units on the coherent imaging and branding of South Africa
  • Establish and manage stakeholder Relations
  • Identify new stakeholders both domestically and internationally that will assist DIRCO to implement its mandate while projecting a positive image of the Department and the Country
  • Maintain positive relationships with key departmental stakeholders
  • Manage the interaction between South African Missions and stakeholders locally regarding branding South Africa
  • Develop and implement Memoranda of Understanding between DIRCO and its partners
  • Develop and maintain stakeholder database
  • Draft and respond to sponsorship proposals
  • Manage the coordination of internal departmental events in line with relevant policies, plans and strategies: Develop policies and strategies for internal events management.
  • Manage the development of project plans to ensure implementation of projects from conceptualisation to delivery stages.
  • Deliver events on time within budget that meet client’s expectations.
  • Communicate and maintain timelines and priorities on every project.
  • Manage onsite inspections and projects.
  • Ensure excellent customer service and quality delivery.
  • Manage the overall performance of the Directorate and participate in Corporate Governance: Formulate and manage the implementation of the directorate’s Operational Plan.
  • Contribute to cooperate governance of the department.
  • Represent the Directorate in departmental governance structures and process to contribute to improved governance.
  • Participate in the development and monitoring of the Audit Action Plan.
  • Participate in the development of the Departmental Strategic Plan.
  • Manage budget in line with the PFMA and other relevant prescripts of government.
  • Empower and capacitation of officials (personal development plans).
  • Ensure that performance agreements are in line with the Directorate's Business plan.

Requirements

  • A bachelor’s degree (NQF 7) in Marketing, Communication Studies or related field.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at Middle Management/Senior Managerial level.
  • A pre-entry certificate (Nyukela certificate) obtained from the National School of Government (NSG) submitted prior to appointment.
  • Knowledge
  • Knowledge and understanding of relevant Public Service Wide legislation
  • Knowledge and understanding of South African Foreign Policy
  • Knowledge and understanding of International Relations, Political and Social Science communications
  • Knowledge of National Policies and Priorities
  • Knowledge of media production, communication and dissemination techniques and methods
  • Competencies
  • Financial Management
  • People Management and Empowerment
  • Client Orientation and Customer focus
  • Knowledge Management
  • Service Delivery and Innovation
  • Strategic Capability and Leadership
  • Planning and organising
  • Time management
  • Strategic Planning
  • Policy Analysis and Development
  • Facilitation skills
  • Report writing
  • Diplomacy
  • Ability to work as team and independently
  • Ability to work under pressure
  • Political and Cultural Sensitivity
  • Networking
  • Communication (Written, Verbal and Liaison)
  • Problem-solving
  • Computer Language proficiency
  • Negotiation, Adaptability, Conceptualisation, Analytical thinking
  • Sense of urgency, Honesty and Integrity
