About the role

Dermalogica South Africa is seeking a commercially driven Junior Trade Marketer: Retail and Concept Stores to support the execution of trade marketing initiatives that bring brand strategy to life in retail, create consumer demand and drive sell-out performance across retail partners and Dermalogica Concept Stores. Reporting to the Trade Marketing Manager / Marketing Manager, the role will support the planning and execution of retail campaigns, product launches, promotions, visual merchandising, consumer activations and in-store experiences. The role is responsible for ensuring marketing activity is translated effectively into the retail environment, with a clear focus on execution excellence, consumer engagement, conversion and commercial results. The role works closely with Sales, Retail Operations, Education, Ecommerce, Marketing, stockists and agency partners to ensure activations are delivered on time, to brand standards and with clear commercial objectives. The role will also monitor retail performance, gather field insights and recommend opportunities to improve execution and drive demand.
