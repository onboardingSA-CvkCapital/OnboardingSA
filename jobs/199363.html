About the role

The Water Research Commission (WRC) is a dynamic hub for water and sanitation knowledge, innovation and intellectual capital, providing leadership and undertakes research, development and innovation of water and sanitation solutions. It engages stakeholders and partners involving water and sanitation challenges and opportunities, which are crucial to South-Africa’s sustainable development and economic growth and is committed to promoting a better quality of life for all. The WRC is a schedule 3A public entity and adherence to all relevant legislation is imperative to the organization’s strategy and values. Applications are invited from suitably qualified, experienced and passionate individuals for the Pretoria-based position of: CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER (REF: CFO) 5 -YEAR FIXED-TERM CONTRACT The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) role at the Water Research Commission (WRC) is a fixed-term executive position requiring a highly qualified, results-driven financial professional. The ideal candidate should have strong financial management, strategic planning, problem-solving, leadership, and decision-making skills. The CFO will be responsible for overseeing the organisation’s financial operations, ensuring sound financial governance, effective resource management, compliance, and supporting the WRC in achieving its strategic objectives. The successful candidate will report to the Chief Executive Officer. The Chief Financial Officer will be responsible for the development and implementation of the finance strategy; accountable for internal and external audit results; ensure compliance; provide strategic financial support to Board and EXCO; ensure effective and efficient functioning of the Finance Branch; drive performance excellence and demonstrate exemplary leadership behaviours. Suitable applicants must be in possession of an Honours Degree or Post-graduate qualification (NQF level 8) in Accounting or Finance related field. Chartered Accountant (CA) or equivalent professional qualification would be an added advantage. MBA/MBL/MCOM (NQF level 9) or other equivalent Internationally recognised Finance qualification will also be an added advantage. 10 years’ Financial Management experience is required of which at least 5 years should be at Senior Management level. Experience within the Public Sector would be an added advantage. The appointment will be made in line with the WRC’s employment equity plan and individuals with a disability and designated groups are encouraged to apply. Applications must be submitted to Human Resources at  response3@multilead.co.za and CVs should contain at least three (3) references. No hand delivered applications will be accepted. Closing date will be 04 September 2026. If you are not contacted by 31 January 2027, please consider your application as unsuccessful.
