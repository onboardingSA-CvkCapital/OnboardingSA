About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Senior Administrative Officer: Facilities Management Services in Facilities Management Services, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R413,001 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 600 Lilian Ngoyi Street (formerly known as Van Der Walt Street) Berea Park, Pretoria, 0001 or by email to Post391@dlrrd.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/391 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: Coloured, Indian and White Males, and Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Administer the acquisition of additional, alternative, new and consolidated leased, state-owned and renewal of leased accommodation.
  • Compile and verify needs assessment in terms of approved funded post establishment, apply space norms and standards and compile user specified accommodation schedule.
  • Compile submissions to the delegated authority concerned for certification of needs assessment and user specified accommodation schedule.
  • Submit certified needs assessment and user specified accommodation schedule to Department of Public Works and Infrastructure (DPWI) Pretoria Head Office UDM for the issuing of a procurement instruction.
  • Conduct follows up with the DPWI Head Office UDM to ensure the preliminary/actual cost analysis schedule is received timeously.
  • Submit preliminary/actual cost analysis schedule received from DPWI Head Office UDM to the Director: Facilities Management Services for certification and then submit back to DPWI Head Office UDM.
  • Obtain copy of issued Procurement Instruction from DPWI Head Office UDM, place hard copy on case file and update the ‘Procurement Instruction Register’ (hard copy on file and electronic copy on Property Register vault).
  • Obtain copy of the lease agreement issued from the concerned DPWI Regional Office, place hard copy on case file and update the database of copies of all lease agreements received from DPWI.
  • Submit renewal list/memorandums on a quarterly basis to Provincial Shared Service Centre’s (PSSCs) to confirm intention to renew, terminate or need for alternative/additional accommodation and conduct follow ups to ensure the confirmation is received timeously.
  • Coordinate day-to-day maintenance for leased buildings.
  • Update the following Registers on the Property Register Vault: Existing Leased Accommodation.
  • DLRRD Procurement Instruction Register (PIs issued and in process to be issued).
  • Provide inputs on the following templates for the User Immovable Asset Management Plan (UIAMP): Template 2A: Property (Leasing) Requirements.
  • Template 2B: Property (Stateowned): Cost of user charges.
  • Template 4: Budget Requirements (Medium Term Expenditure Estimate).
  • Conduct site meetings for the Department.
  • Inspect premises identified by DPWI and invite all concerned DLRRD roleplayers (client Directorates, Information and communications technology (ICT), Security Services and Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA) officer).
  • Co-ordinate and submit all Tenant Installations/refurbishment requirements to DPWI and conduct follow ups.
  • Attend official site handover meeting regarding the acquisition of additional, alternative, new and consolidated leased and state-owned accommodation and submit Back to Office Report (BToR) to management on the outcome/findings and make recommendations.
  • Conduct site inspections and condition surveys to existing DLRRD leased facilities to determine the overall condition of the building, the space utilisation rate of the building, the need for maintenance/refurbishment/reconfiguration, accommodation to be relinquished, etc. Verify accommodation occupied by or allocated to DLRRD and compile/update office space inventory register per building/facilities.
  • Verify and update the Departmental Immovable Property Asset Register/Database for leased and state-owned accommodation used by DLRRD for office accommodation, functional accommodation as well as residential accommodation.
  • Administer payments of all DLRRD’s municipal services accounts to service providers.
  • Obtain/receive the billing invoice from the relevant municipality/service providers.
  • Verify invoices (occupancy, account number, physical address and billing items).
  • Capture the certified account/invoice on the manual property payments system.
  • Complete a Sundry Payment Advice for each municipal account/invoice, attach all the source documents and submit them to the delegated authorities for verification, recommendation and approval.
  • Forward approved Sundry Payment Advice to the directorates Internal Control and Financial Accounting for verification and payment on Basic Accounting System (BAS).
  • Obtain payment stub/payment advice, compile the expenditure payment allocation and submit it to the relevant municipality/service provider.
  • Administer payments for leased and state-owned accommodation to National Department of Public Works and Infrastructure (NDPWI).
  • Verify invoices received from NDPWI and submit to the directorates Internal Control and Financial Accounting for processing.
  • Verify and reconcile disputed properties, adjustment invoices and credit notes against the Property Payment Registers to ensure the non-payment of duplicating invoices.
  • Maintain and update the Property Payment Monitoring System for use in expenditure processing and for audit purposes.
  • Maintain and update an “actual lease” expenditure register wherein the actual lease tariffs of all existing leases are captured per financial year.
  • Liaise and resolve disputed invoices with NDPWI via e-mail and signed letters for audit purposes.
  • Attendance of meetings with NDPWI on property payment matters.
  • Submit monthly reports on the “Effecting of Payments within 30 Days” as well as the Branch: Corporate Support Services (CSS) list of invoices received and paid.
  • Prepare interim and annual financial statement input.
  • Compile and submit inputs on accruals (operating leases, state-owned accommodation user charges and municipal services).
  • Compile and submit inputs on the operating lease commitments for disclosure notes in the Annual Financial Statements (AFS) and the Interim Financial Statements (IFS).
  • Compile and submit inputs on related parties (office accommodation and municipal services for the Deeds and the Office of the Valuer General) in the Annual Financial Statements (AFS) and the Interim Financial Statements (IFS).
  • Update the Lease Renewal Register monthly and place on the Property Registers Vault.
  • Render administrative and secretariat support service to the Departmental Accommodation, Parking and Residential Committee (DAPRC).
  • Provide secretariat support to DAPRC as required including preparation of agenda and meeting documentation in consultation with the Chairperson.
  • Produce and disseminate accurate minutes to meet participants to agreed standards and timeframes.
  • Manage all administrative tasks including arranging venues, catering, equipment, etc.

Requirements

  • Minimum requirements
  • A Grade 12 Certificate and a National Diploma in Public Administration/Public Management/Business Administration/Business Management/Supply Chain Management/Bachelor’s Degree in Property Management/Built Environment or related equivalent qualification
  • Minimum 2 years’ experience in facilities and office management working environment
  • Job related knowledge
  • Knowledge of the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA)
  • Public Service Regulations
  • Financial procedures
  • Treasury Regulations
  • Basic Accounting System (BAS)
  • National Building Regulations
  • Occupational Health and Safety Act
  • Government Immovable Asset Management Act (GIAMA)
  • Job related skills
  • Computer literacy
  • Interpersonal relations
  • Organising and Planning
  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Analytical thinking
  • Problem solving
  • Financial management
