About the role

Logistics Coordinator Ceres Koelkamers, situated in the Schoonvlei industrial area in Ceres, has an opportunity available for an energetic and dynamic Logistics Coordinator to join their team. The successful candidate will have a practical and proactive approach, strong attention to detail, and be comfortable working in a fast-paced environment. The role requires someone who can work independently, take responsibility, and perform effectively under pressure. Responsibilities will include, but not be limited to: • Managing and controlling pallet stock • Coordinating the complete and accurate dispatch of pallets to export and local markets according to loading instructions • Managing and optimising cold room space • Monitoring and managing cold room temperatures • Managing and coordinating personnel • Liaising with packhouses and marketers All other duties relevant to the position. Essential qualifications, skills, and experience: • Matric is required, a relevant tertiary qualification will be advantageous; • Minimum 3 years’ relevant experience in the fruit industry or a packhouse/logistics environment; • Strong computer skills; • Basic technical skills and interest; • Excellent communication skills (Afrikaans & English); • Own transport and a valid Code 8 driver’s licence; • Willingness to work overtime and weekends when required.
