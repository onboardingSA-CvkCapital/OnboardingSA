About the role

Data Practice Lead Role Overview The Data Practice Lead is responsible for growing and leading Dariel's Data Architecture, BI, and Analytics capability. This role combines technical credibility with commercial leadership, consultative client engagement, practice development, and delivery oversight. The Data Practice Lead has a matrix reporting line into the CTO and holds accountability for the Data Architecture capability across Dariel. This includes architectural standards, consulting quality, BI strategy, delivery governance, and people leadership. The successful candidate will engage with clients beyond initial sales introductions, uncover business challenges, articulate value-driven solutions, contribute to proposals and RFQs, and maintain trusted stakeholder relationships throughout the client lifecycle. This is not a purely architectural role. The candidate must be equally comfortable discussing business outcomes in executive boardrooms, facilitating discovery workshops, supporting sales opportunities, and guiding technical teams through delivery. The role exists to provide leadership across strategy, architecture, consulting, delivery governance, capability development, and business growth while strengthening Dariel's position as a trusted technology partner. Success in this role is measured through client value creation, capability growth, consulting excellence, and successful delivery outcomes.
