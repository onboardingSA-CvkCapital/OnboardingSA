About the role

SAICA has embarked on an exciting transformation to become an insights-driven, technology-enabled, and AI-powered organisation. As part of our journey, we are streamlining processes, enhancing our digital capabilities, and strengthening our focus on sustainability in the accounting profession. This transformation presents an opportunity for you to grow and contribute to SAICA’s vision in a redefined, future-fit role. We are looking for enthusiastic, skilled professionals who are eager to innovate, embrace data-driven decision-making, and help shape the future of the profession. Purpose of the job This role handles of all day-to-day operational requirements of performing facilities and/or maintenance work on the SAICA building. Ensure all SAICA facilities are fit for purpose, well maintained, and creates a clean and appealing environment for the SAICA employees, visitors and all other stakeholders. Key/main outputs Output 1: Building maintenance • Liaise with contractors and service providers working on site to ensure all work is correctly completed and on time. Submit signed Job Card to Facilities Manager. • Check faulty lights in and around the buildings every morning and replace them where necessary. • Monitoring the standards of cleanliness of the premises and furnishings and reporting any problems or faults to the Facilities Manager and Ensuring that adequate supplies of cleaning materials and other supplies are available. • Repair office furniture and fittings, doors, windows etc. as and when required. • Attending minor plumbing repairs as when required. • Hang artwork when required. • Install whiteboard as and when required. • Attend to all helpdesk queries related to building maintenance as assigned. Ensure prompt response and attendance to calls logged and assigned. • Update Facilities Management Call management system daily • Monthly cleaning of plant and storerooms • Check and report, weekly, the states of all generators in the SAICA buildings via checklist and submit to Facilities Manager. • Assist internal clients with storing parcels in their storage rooms. • Perform duties outside of working hours as and when required. • Conduct weekly building inspection and write report to the Facilities Manager to attend to any repairs or maintenance work required at the premises. • Inspect the operation of HVAC system, switch on HVAC system in the mornings and off in the afternoons. • Inspect ablution facilities including water leaks daily. • Perform all Handymen duties as and when required. • Update assets register. • Asset Verification. • Ensure assets are well maintained and report damaged and lost assets. Output 2: Garden and driveway maintenance • Ensure that the garden is in good condition at times, ensure that weeds are cleared from the garden and driveway by the external garden maintenance team. • Ensure that the irrigation system is working at all times. • Check and ensure garden maintenance is performed as per contract, submit signed checklist for approval to Facilities Manager after each service interval. • Report any areas of concern to the Facilities Manager. Output 3: OHSACT • Participate in the SAICA Occupational, Health and Safety implementation. • Ensure all Fire prevention and detection equipment is maintained as per statutory requirements and compliance. • Ensure all maintenance activities are performed according to safety regulations. • Assisting the Safety Officer in ensuring that the legal provisions relating to Occupational, Health and Safety at work are properly adhered to, including the safe storage of any flammable/toxic substances on site. Output 4: General • Ensure all buildings are neat and tidy. • Clearing and maintain all plant and equipment areas. • Assist with any general work. • Assist with ad hoc maintenance including moving of boxes and furniture, to be moved where necessary. • Setup workstations for meetings. • Supply milk and other requirements to coffee stations. Output 5: embrace and drive organisational transformation • Actively contribute to cost containment efforts by identifying opportunities for operational efficiencies, optimising resources, and ensuring value-driven decision-making in daily activities. • Support SAICA’s transformation into an insights-driven organisation by leveraging data and analytics to enhance decision-making, improve processes, and drive continuous improvement in your area of work. • Effectively utilise digital tools and technology to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and improve service delivery. Stay informed about new and emerging technologies relevant to your role. • Adopt and embrace AI-driven tools and automation to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and effectiveness in job-related tasks, while continuously seeking opportunities to improve processes through innovative technology solutions. • Identify and support SAICA’s management of risks related to sustainability, including environmental risks, regulatory changes and social risks and integrating them into business continuity and risk management plans.
