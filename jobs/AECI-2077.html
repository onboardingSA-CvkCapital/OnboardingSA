About the role

AECI is hiring a Plant Operator, based in Umbogintwini. To operate Plant and Equipment in Designated sections in accordance with standards operating procedures.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure efficient shift handovers
  • Handover shift by communicating the details of the past shift from incoming operator, ensuring that all information on problem areas are conveyed to operator and Shift leader.
  • Sign and acknowledge all information in handover logbook.
  • Acknowledge all open permit to work clearances.
  • Establish process start up conditions
  • Correct commissioning of plant Utilities.
  • Ensure that plant section is safe for start-up.
  • Ensure the effective commissioning of section or equipment to a specific state:
  • Ensure appropriate documentation is available for batch start up e.g.: works order, process log and manufacturing manual.
  • Test equipment on request.
  • Ensure Effective communication of process condition
  • Inform shift leader of process conditions.
  • Communicate in an accurate, concise, clear and prompt manner.
  • Alarms and Deviations are promptly and correctly responded to.
  • Promptly supply concise and complete information to the Shift leader.
  • Communicate accurate and concise information to the laboratory personnel.
  • Communicate accurate and concise information to the engineering department.
  • Ensure Effective response to abnormal process conditions
  • Identify changing process conditions and investigate.
  • Seek help from appropriate personnel.
  • Promptly report to Shift team leader and take correct action to solve and contain the problem, with minimizing loss and waste, but done safely.
  • Ensure effective shut down of process operations
  • Ensure effective shut down by preparing plant and liaising with relevant personnel.
  • Plant and equipment are correctly and safely isolated according to relevant plant procedures.
  • Ensure effective administration
  • Ensure that the hand over logbook is completed accurately.
  • Sign off log sheets and records accurately.
  • Ensure that all open work permits are closed off at end of shift.
  • Efficient control over Utility and raw material usage, to prevent wastage.
  • Create Purchase Orders.
  • Create works order using Syspro.
  • Help Dip tanks for stock take.
  • Perform various calculations.
  • Act for Shift leader in his absence.
  • Sheq
  • Ensure that all safety standards are maintained.
  • Test and checks on all safety equipment carried out by ensuring PMS are completed in the specified time frame.
  • Rectify and report safety hazards promptly.
  • Ensure that all team members are using the correct PPE.
  • Attend safety meeting and complete plant safety inspections monthly.
  • Attend permit to work meeting.
  • Assist in incident investigations.
  • Maintain good housekeeping standards on the plant.
  • Unsafe conditions reported and rectified e.g. spillages.
  • Correct storage of equipment and stock.
  • Staff Management
  • Carry out PJOs on Junior Operators, identify areas that require attention.
  • Assist the production manager in updating manufacturing manuals and process logs.
  • Ensure Effective communication within the production team.
  • Assist in the Training of Junior operators and students.

Requirements

  • Education, Knowledge and Skill Requirements
  • Grade 12 with Mathematics and Science
  • Minimum 2/3 years’ experience in a chemical plant.
  • Minimum qualification, NQF3 or Chemical Eng. Dip.
  • Behavioural Competencies
  • Leadership
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Excellent Work Ethic, Teamwork.
  • Flexible and integrity.
  • Diligent and Able to work under stress conditions.
  • Attention to detail.
  • Motivated and committed.
  • Problem solving.
