About the role

To provide a professional, friendly, and efficient front office service to all guests, ensuring a seamless guest experience from reservation through to departure. The Receptionist serves as the first point of contact for guests and is responsible for creating a welcoming environment while maintaining high service standards. Key Responsibilities & Duties: • Welcome guests and visitors in a professional and friendly manner. • Manage guest check-ins and check-outs efficiently and accurately. • Handle all incoming telephone calls, emails, and guest enquiries. • Process reservations, amendments, cancellations, and special requests. • Maintain accurate guest profiles and reservation records. • Provide guests with information regarding facilities, services, activities, and local attractions. • Coordinate guest requests with housekeeping, maintenance, food and beverage, and other departments. • Assist with arranging transfers, excursions, and special guest experiences. • Process guest payments, invoices, and daily cash-ups. • Ensure all guest accounts are accurate and up to date. • Handle guest complaints and concerns promptly and professionally. • Maintain a clean, organized, and welcoming reception area. • Prepare daily arrival, departure, and occupancy reports. • Monitor and manage reception stationery and office supplies. • Ensure compliance with company policies, procedures, and service standards. • Assist with administrative duties, filing, and record keeping. • Promote lodge facilities and services to enhance the guest experience. • Maintain confidentiality of guest and company information. • Support the Front Office Manager with operational and administrative tasks. • Work closely with all departments to ensure seamless guest service delivery. • Ensure exceptional customer service standards are maintained at all times. • Assist with shift handovers and communicate important operational information accurately. • Perform any other reasonable duties required to support the efficient operation of the property. Minimum Requirements: • Grade 12 (Matric). • Previous reception, front office, or hospitality experience advantageous. • Computer literate with proficiency in Microsoft Office. • Experience with reservation or property management systems advantageous. • Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English. • Valid driver's license advantageous. Skills & Competencies: • Exceptional customer service skills. • Professional telephone etiquette. • Strong administrative and organizational abilities. • Excellent attention to detail. • Ability to multitask and work under pressure. • Problem-solving and conflict-resolution skills. • Strong interpersonal and communication skills. • Ability to work shifts, weekends, public holidays, and overtime when required.
