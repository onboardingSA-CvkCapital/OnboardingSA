About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Planned Maintenance Administrator, based in Mokopane, Limpopo. This role is at a band Level 8 and reporting to the Planned Maintenance Officer.

Responsibilities

  • Asset Care Strategy.
  • Assist in addressing all key elements of asset care strategy using KPIs.
  • Participates in cross functional engineering and production teams to minimize reactive maintenance.
  • Support programs to minimize unplanned downtime on key equipment.
  • Support cross functional teams to address cleaning, lubrication and first line maintenance functions.
  • Participate in the development of RCM based task lists for all maintenance significant equipment.
  • Participate in failure analysis techniques for cross functional teams.
  • Identifies significant recurring failure patterns by analyzing maintenance history.
  • Support preventive actions to ensure cost effective maintenance programs.
  • Makes formalized recommendations to Planned Maintenance Officers.
  • Daily, executes capacity planning and ensures that both preventative and corrective maintenance is covered.
  • Assists in ensuring that shutdowns follow preset targets in accordance with critical path analysis.
  • Assist in ensuring that all work is controlled by works orders and information loops are closed.
  • Liaises with Foremen to confirm that all technical manuals and documents are up to date.
  • Administer KPI reports.
  • Assist in ensuring that the SAP PM, MM and CO systems are properly supported.
  • Participate in RCM exercises for all new equipment before commissioning.
  • Participate in Group wide RCM template development and review for primary production equipment.
  • Administer the skills matrix and training plans for all maintenance personnel.
  • Participate in root cause analysis exercises.
  • Administer the critical spare parts lists.
  • Support the material management strategy.
  • Actively support the codification and bills of material initiatives.
  • Actively participate in spares and material acquisition.
  • Supports a housekeeping program.
  • Facilitate or manage a tool store function.
  • Implement a purpose-driven, high-performing culture that is aligned with the company values, is inclusive and promotes diversity, supports engagement and fosters continuous improvement to work processes.
  • Ensure compliance with internal and external regulatory, legislative and permitting requirements.

Requirements

  • Technical knowledge required for role
  • Basic artisan training module
  • Business Cantered Maintenance training module
  • Working knowledge of client disciplines - Metallurgy, Mining, Engineering
  • Ability to plan and organize
  • Ability to schedule and manage maintenance activities
  • Grade 12 or equivalent
  • Valid EB/Code 08 driver’s licence
  • Experience, Knowledge and skills
  • 1 year experience in the Planned Maintenance office
  • Experienced in using a computerized planned maintenance system
