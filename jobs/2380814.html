About the role

OFFICE ADMINISTRATOR / BOOKKEEPER – FULL-TIME POSITION We are looking for a career-driven, reliable, highly organised and detail-oriented Office Administrator / Bookkeeper to join our team on a full-time basis. Experience in the property, rental or property management industry will be highly advantageous, as the role involves both general office administration and day-to-day bookkeeping responsibilities relating to tenants, rentals, suppliers, contractors and property expenses. Working Hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 – 17:00 Key Responsibilities – Office Administration • Answering and managing incoming phone calls professionally • Managing general office correspondence and enquiries • General office administration and day-to-day office management • Filing, printing, scanning and maintaining accurate physical and electronic records • Data capturing and ensuring information is kept accurate and up to date • Preparing documents, letters, schedules and reports when required • Maintaining organised tenant, supplier, contractor and property files • Coordinating plumbers, electricians and other contractors • Dispatching contractors to different properties/sites as required • Following up on maintenance jobs and ensuring work is completed • Keeping records of maintenance requests, quotations, invoices and completed work • Liaising with tenants regarding maintenance, accounts and general property matters • Following up on outstanding documents, quotations, invoices and information • Working confidently with Microsoft Excel • Maintaining spreadsheets, schedules and administrative reports • Ensuring tasks are followed through from start to completion • Assisting management with general administrative duties as required Key Responsibilities – Bookkeeping & Accounts The successful candidate will also be responsible for various bookkeeping and financial administration duties, including: • Preparing, capturing and processing invoices • Capturing supplier invoices and expenses accurately • Preparing customer/tenant invoices and statements • Rental collection and monitoring monthly rental payments • Following up with tenants regarding outstanding and overdue rental payments • Maintaining accurate tenant account records • Allocating rental payments received to the correct tenant accounts • Recording deposits, rental payments, utilities and other charges • Monitoring outstanding debtors and preparing debtor reports • Following up on overdue accounts and maintaining records of collection efforts • Processing and reconciling supplier accounts • Preparing supplier payment schedules • Checking supplier invoices against quotations, statements and work completed • Ensuring contractors and suppliers are paid correctly and that supporting documentation is available • Capturing payments and receipts into the accounting/property management system • Performing bank reconciliations • Reconciling bank statements against payments received and expenses paid • Identifying and investigating discrepancies on accounts • Maintaining accurate cashbooks • Processing petty cash transactions and reconciling petty cash • Maintaining records of income and expenses • Capturing municipal, electricity, water, levy and other property-related accounts where applicable • Allocating expenses to the correct property or account • Preparing monthly income and expense summaries • Assisting with monthly management accounts and financial reports • Maintaining creditor and debtor ledgers • Reconciling creditor statements to the accounting system • Preparing age analysis reports for debtors and creditors • Monitoring outstanding supplier invoices and payments • Ensuring all financial documents are properly filed and supported by invoices, receipts or proof of payment • Assisting with VAT documentation and preparation where required • Assisting accountants or auditors with supporting documents and information • Maintaining organised financial records for year-end and audit purposes • Checking figures carefully and ensuring all capturing is accurate • Assisting with budgeting, expense tracking and cash-flow information where required • Generating financial and rental reports for management • Maintaining confidentiality of company and financial information Property / Rental Administration Because this role is connected to property administration, the candidate should ideally understand or be willing to learn: • Tenant accounts and rental statements • Rental collections and arrears • Deposits and tenant balances • Property expenses • Maintenance costs • Contractor invoices • Municipal and utility accounts • Lease-related administration • Tenant queries • Property inspections and maintenance coordination • Keeping individual property records accurate and up to date • Allocating income and expenses to the correct properties • Following up with tenants, landlords, suppliers and contractors when necessary Requirements • Previous office administration experience • Previous bookkeeping, accounts or financial administration experience • Experience in the property, rental or property management industry is highly preferred • A good understanding of tenants, rentals, reconciliations and account follow-ups • Strong knowledge of Microsoft Excel • Ability to work confidently with spreadsheets and formulas • Experience with MDA, Pastel, Sage, Xero or similar supervision • Professional, responsible and dependable • Proactive and willing to take initiative • Honest and trustworthy, particularly when dealing with financial information • A driven, This position would suit someone who is looking for a long-term career opportunity, wants to grow within the company and is prepared to take responsibility for ensuring that the administrative and financial side of the office runs efficiently.
