About the role

The Department of Economic Development (Gauteng) is hiring a Director: Executive Support in Directorate: Executive Support, based in Umnotho House Johannesburg. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Apply online to the http:jobs.gauteng.gpg.gov.za website only. Reference number to quote: REFS/057144 Closing date: 20 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and quality-assure HOD briefings, submissions, memos and related correspondences.
  • Manage end-to-end meeting logistics, that is, scheduling, agenda setting, document packs, minute-taking, and resolutions tracking.
  • Operate a decision-tracking system, log directives, allocate actions, monitor progress and produce weekly exception reports.
  • Standardise templates and procedures for executive documents (Cabinet/MEC submissions, minutes, reports).
  • Provide research and synthesis on policy, legislation and programme issues to support HOD decisions.
  • Maintain secure, organised records and a central repository for executive correspondence and decisions.
  • Embed PFMA, Treasury Regulations, DPSA directives, POPIA, MISS/MPSS, and applicable provincial frameworks in office processes.
  • Run quarterly compliance reviews on submissions, delegations, records and decision logs, remediate gaps and report assurances.
  • Coordinate responses to audit findings (GAS/AGSA), track management action plans, and verify closure evidence.
  • Maintain the HOD Delegations of Authority register, ensure alignment to financial and administrative delegations.
  • Identify executive office risks, keep a risk register and implement control measures including confidentiality and information security.
  • Facilitate policy/procedure updates and compliance training for executive office staff and document custodians.
  • Maintain an integrated HOD calendar linking strategic priorities, statutory deadlines and stakeholder engagements.
  • Operate an executive workflow dashboard-incoming/outgoing items, due dates, dependencies and bottleneck alerts.
  • Coordinate quarterly performance reviews with Strategy/M&E, compile executive performance packs and insights.
  • Synchronise HOD directives with programme plans and operational cycles, escalate slippage and propose recovery actions.
  • Implement service standards for turnaround times on submissions, queries and approvals and monitor compliance.
  • Produce monthly performance and workload analytics for the Office of the HOD (volumes, turnaround, quality scores).
  • Manage protocols and channels between the HOD and MEC/EXCO, Office of the Premier, Treasury, DPSA, and sector departments.
  • Coordinate executive meeting packs (MEC briefings, clusters, FORUMs), ensuring completeness and strategic alignment.
  • Track undertakings from intergovernmental forums, drive inter-departmental cooperation to close actions on time.
  • Prepare stakeholder engagement plans, briefs, and follow-up matrices and ensure issues are resolved and feedback loops closed.
  • Maintain a stakeholder risk and issues log, advise the HOD on emerging sensitivities and mitigation options.
  • Produce concise, outcomefocused communiqués and post-engagement reports for executive audiences.
  • Implement and monitor a secure records management system for executive documents, aligned to POPIA and MISS/MPSS.
  • Establish classification, retention, and disposal schedules, audit compliance and correct deviations.
  • Oversee the drafting, formatting, and clearance of executive correspondence to ensure consistency with corporate identity.
  • Operate version control and approvals workflow for briefs, speeches, media responses and communiqués.
  • Maintain an executive knowledge base
  • Policies, cabinet decisions, provincial priorities, sector insights, and precedents
  • Introduce digital tools/workflows (e.g., document trackers) and train staff to improve accuracy and turnaround
  • Perform strategic and operational planning
  • Manage stakeholder relationships
  • Oversee the administrative support functions Perform people management functions
  • Perform financial management functions
  • Perform asset management functions

Requirements

  • NQF Level 7 in Public Administration/Public Management/Political Science/Business Administration qualifications.
  • Nyukela Certificate for entry into SMS (Senior Management Service) from the National School of Government submitted prior to appointment.
  • At least 5 years of experience at a middle/senior managerial level in executive support/governance/administration.
  • Demonstrable experience in PFMA/Treasury Regulations compliance, secretariat services, and intergovernmental coordination.
  • Code 8/10.
