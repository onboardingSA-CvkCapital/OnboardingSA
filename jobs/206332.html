Job Title              :Agency Manager Location              :Clinix Health Group – Hyde Park Reports to           :Chief Medical Officer JOB SUMMARY The Agency Manager is responsible for the strategic, operational, and financial management of the Clinix Health Group Agency function. The role plans, organises, executes, and monitors all clinical and non-clinical agency staffing activities across the Group, ensuring optimal utilisation of agency resources, cost efficiency, operational effectiveness, regulatory compliance, and service excellence. The incumbent drives workforce optimisation initiatives, recruitment strategies, stakeholder engagement, and continuous improvement programmes that support business performance and quality patient care.
