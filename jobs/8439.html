About the role

INTRODUCTION: Komatsu South Africa has an opportunity for an Operations Manager to join the Africa Operations team based at the Head Office in Germiston. The candidate should have a business-related diploma (NQF 6 or higher), 8 years of work experience,including 3 years in a leadership role overseeing technical staff, and a track record in customer liaison, financial management and problem resolution in the mining/construction field. SUMMARY OF JOB ROLE: Horizon Planning: • Participates in operations strategic plan development with divisional team and Operations Executive. • Interprets, manages and cascades branch plans, business priorities, objectives and outputs. • Assesses the impact of risk mitigating plans and preventative action strategies and recommends alternatives to Operations leadership. Human Capital Management: • Participates in planning human resources needs & requirements for the branch for approval by Executive leadership. • Lives the organisation's culture and values. • Conducts and plans individual development and career path discussions with the team. Directs and manages Branch Human Resources Financial Management: • Provides input into the financial requirements of the branch. • Participates in the budgeting process for Unit Sales, Parts Sales & R&M Contracts. • Participates in the planning of audits in collaboration with the Finance/audit function when required. Contracts/Supplier Management: • Assumes overall accountability for adherence to Procurement & Supplier Management policy & procedure. • Reports on contractual issues, metrics and other related information. Liaison & Changement: • Provides change leadership. • Liaises with and fosters sound ethical stakeholder relationships. • Communicates organisational goals and direction clearly, consistently and regularly to all branch staff. Unit Sales: • Networks, builds and maintains sound business relationships with customers. • Negotiating terms and sale with the customer. • Monitors market trends and analyse business opportunities with a view to maximising parts and equipment sales potential. Service: • Participates in and approves maintenance planning activities. • Manages the overall servicing process. Manages and assumes accountability for correct job costing information. Parts Sales: • Manages the parts sales process. • Checks compliance to stock count policy & procedure and reviews stock reports. • Executes periodic checks and implements discipline with regards to administration and paperwork relating to the collating and filing of documents for audit purposes. Warehouse Management: • Obtaining warehousing standards from Parts Division with regard to warehouse layout (planograph) and optimal arrangement of storage. • Checking that equipment in the warehouse is in good working order. • Manages the warehouse function for the branch. Product Support: • Manages the Product Support function for the branch. • Checking that resources for optimal product support are in place (manuals, information, tools, sheets, etc.). • Provides feedback to centralised product support on machine trends, data and or other issues which may impact improvements/customer satisfaction
