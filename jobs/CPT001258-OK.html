About the role

Refrigeration Technician - Qualified Red SealA well-established refrigeration company based in Worcester is seeking a qualified and experienced Refrigeration Technician to install, maintain, service and repair commercial and industrial refrigeration systems.The successful candidate must be a qualified Red Seal Refrigeration Technician with strong fault-finding abilities and at least five years’ post-trade experience. Key Responsibilities • Install, service, maintain and repair commercial and industrial refrigeration systems. • Diagnose refrigeration, electrical and mechanical faults. • Conduct preventative and scheduled maintenance. • Repair and replace compressors, condensers, evaporators, motors, pumps, valves and related components. • Perform pressure testing, leak detection, evacuation and refrigerant charging. • Monitor system temperatures, pressures and operating performance. • Read and interpret electrical diagrams, technical drawings and equipment manuals. • Complete service reports, job cards and maintenance records accurately. • Attend breakdowns and emergency call-outs when required. • Ensure tools, equipment and company vehicles are maintained correctly. • Liaise professionally with clients and provide feedback on repairs and maintenance requirements. • Ensure all work complies with health, safety, quality and environmental requirements. • Identify opportunities to improve equipment reliability and energy efficiency. • Provide technical support and guidance to junior technicians or assistants where required. Minimum Requirements • Grade 12 / Matric. • Red Seal Trade Test qualification as a Refrigeration Technician or Refrigeration Mechanic is essential. • Minimum of five years’ post-trade refrigeration experience. • Strong experience with commercial and industrial refrigeration systems. • Sound knowledge of refrigeration cycles, electrical controls and mechanical components. • Proven fault-finding and diagnostic ability. • Experience working with compressors, condensers, evaporators and control systems. • Ability to read electrical diagrams and technical drawings. • Knowledge of refrigerant handling, leak detection and system charging. • Valid driver’s licence. • Willing and able to attend after-hours breakdowns and standby call-outs. • Must be physically fit and comfortable working in plant rooms, cold rooms and confined spaces. • Must be able to work independently and manage jobs from diagnosis through to completion. • Reliable transport or the ability to travel to Worcester daily. • Strong communication, customer-service and administrative skills.
