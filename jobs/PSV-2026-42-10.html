About the role

The Department of Agriculture is hiring a Farm Aid (Various) in Inspection and Quarantine Services, based in Western Cape Stellenbosch. This is a permanent post, paying R144,024 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to 20 Steve Biko Street, Agriculture Place, Arcadia, Pretoria, 0002 or by email FAVrecruit109@nda.gov.za Reference number to quote: 3/3/1/109/2026 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Target: African Male, Indian Male, White Male and African Female, White Female and Persons with disability.

Responsibilities

  • Perform general activities in respect of grounds and crop production through inter alia the following: Cutting, seeding and planting of plants in gardens.
  • Soil cultivation and preparation e.g. cleaning, hoeing, making seed beds for planting.
  • Apply chemical crop protection e.g. operate a knapsack.
  • Fertilizing of plants.
  • Tending of crops/orchards/wineyards e.g. pruning.
  • Weeding etc. Perform general routine maintenance of plants in facilities in terms of the following: Irrigation and watering of plants in facilities.
  • Assist with spraying of pesticides in facilities.
  • Pruning and fertilizing of plants in quarantine and other facilities.
  • Dispose of organic waste such as leaves.
  • Perform general routine activities in respect of infrastructure, which would include inter alia the following: General maintenance of facilities.
  • Disposal of farm waste material.
  • Maintain road, parking areas, channels and concrete walkways.
  • Cleaning and unblocking of blocked drains on premises.
  • Clean and empty waste collection bins weekly.
  • Cleaning and maintenance of roads, pavements and green houses, tunnels and glass house.
  • Assist with inspection of office buildings and official houses for maintenance needs.
  • Perform general activities in respect of equipment and assets which would include.
  • Interalia the following
  • Care of equipment e.g. cleaning, oiling, sharpening, basic service, etc. Assist with movement of assets between offices or buildings
  • Loading and off-loading of plants, potting soil, moving of plants between quarantine facilities
  • Perform minor service and repairs on gardening equipment such as weed eaters and lawn mowers
  • Washing of departmental vehicles

Requirements

  • Applicants must be in a possession of an ABET level 2/3 Certificate or equivalent (NQF 1 or 2).
  • No experience required.
  • Job Related Skills Basic numeracy skills.
  • Basic literacy skills.
  • Teamwork.
  • Reliability.
  • Results driven.
  • Customer focus.
  • Quality orientation and personal motivation.
  • Resource utilisation.
  • Working extended hours.
