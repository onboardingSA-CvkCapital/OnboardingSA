About the role

Stefanutti Stocks is seeking a motivated and technically strong MEP Manager to join our Western Cape Region. You will work closely with project teams, subcontractors, consultants, and clients to ensure projects are delivered safely, efficiently, and within programme and budget.The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating and managing all Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) activities on construction projects. The role focuses on ensuring that MEP systems are properly planned, procured, installed, and commissioned in accordance with project specifications, quality standards, and contractual requirements. documentation, reporting, and records are maintained. • Support the implementation of project quality plans.Cost & Contract Management • Assist with managing project costs and contractual requirements related to MEP works. • Ensure timely procurement of labour, materials, plant, and subcontractors. • Review and assist with monthly cost reports. • Support the Quantity Surveyor with variations,
