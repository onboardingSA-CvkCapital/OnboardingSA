About the role

Pepkor Lifestyle is looking for an experienced and commercially driven Merchandise Manager to lead our Buying teams and oversee the end-to-end merchandise value chain. The successful candidate will be responsible for driving profitable sales, optimizing merchandise performance, managing supplier relationships, and ensuring exceptional customer outcomes while leading a high-performing team. Key Responsibilities • Stores Accountability: Oversee the merchandise strategy and assume full accountability for the following stores: Rochester, Home.Tech.Sleep,Boardmans and House & Home (non RSA). • Lead and manage the Buying teams to deliver business objectives. • Drive sales, gross profit, and merchandise performance across all categories. • Develop and maintain strong supplier partnerships and negotiate strategic trading agreements. • Oversee product ranging, promotional planning, and marketing initiatives. • Monitor merchandise performance, inventory health, and key business metrics. • Ensure customer-centric decision-making across all merchandise activities. • Lead, coach, and develop a high-performing team while driving succession planning and employee engagement. • Ensure compliance with company policies, processes, and governance standards. Minimum Requirements • Matric with a Degree or Diploma in Business Management, Procurement, or a related field. • 5+ years’ buying and procurement experience within the Lounge, Case Goods, and Patio Furniture. • 3+ years’ merchandise management experience, including direct people management. • Proven track record in supplier management, contract negotiation, and range planning. • Proficiency in SAP and Google Workspace/Microsoft Suite. Key Competencies • Commercial & Financial Acumen: Entrepreneurial thinking with strong analytical and financial skills to drive strategy and profitability. • Negotiation & Influence: Outstanding capability in vendor management, contract negotiation, and stakeholder influence. • Leadership & People Management: Proven ability to manage direct reports, communicate vision, and present effectively to business stakeholders. • Planning & Execution: Strong organisational skills with a strategic approach to merchandise and category management. Join a dynamic retail environment where innovation, collaboration, and customer focus drive our success. This is an exciting opportunity to make a meaningful impact while leading a talented merchandise team.
