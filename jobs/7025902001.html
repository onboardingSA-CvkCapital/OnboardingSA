About the role

Introduction: The Regional Sales Manager is responsible for translating B&T’s national sales strategy into consistent, measurable regional execution through the leadership, coaching and performance management of the sales team. Description: Regional Sales Leadership • Lead, manage and motivate External Sales Representatives within the region. • Translate national sales strategies into actionable regional execution plans. • Create a high-performance sales culture focused on accountability, customer service and results. • Drive achievement of regional sales targets, gross profit targets and growth objectives. • Ensure alignment between sales activities and business objectives. • Promote collaboration between sales teams, stores and support functions. Customer Acquisition and Market Development • Drive new customer acquisition across targeted market segments. • Increase B&T’s visibility and presence within the regional trade market. • Identify new business opportunities and emerging customer needs. • Develop and implement customer growth plans for key accounts. • Expand share of wallet within existing customer portfolios. • Monitor competitor activity and provide market intelligence to support business decisions. • Build and maintain strong relationships with key customers and stakeholders. Sales Performance Management • Develop regional sales plans aligned to national objectives. • Establish clear sales targets and activity expectations for team members. • Monitor and manage sales performance through agreed KPIs and scorecards. • Conduct regular performance reviews and coaching interventions. • Ensure effective pipeline management and forecasting disciplines. • Analyse sales trends and implement corrective actions where required. • Drive accountability for customer acquisition, customer retention and revenue generation. Customer Relationship Management • Ensure consistent and effective utilisation of CRM systems. • Monitor customer interactions, sales opportunities and pipeline progression. • Drive customer engagement and relationship-building initiatives. • Ensure accurate customer data and reporting. • Support the implementation of customer segmentation and targeting strategies. • Resolve escalated customer issues and ensure satisfactory outcomes. Sales Capability Development • Coach and develop sales representatives to improve sales effectiveness and customer engagement. • Identify capability gaps and implement development plans. • Conduct store visits and sales observations to improve performance. • Support onboarding and integration of new sales employees. • Promote best practice sharing and continuous learning across the sales team. • Assist in building future sales leadership capability within the region. Operational Alignment and Execution • Work closely with Regional Operations Managers and Store Managers to ensure alignment between sales and operational execution. • Ensure customer commitments can be delivered effectively through operational capabilities. • Facilitate communication between sales teams and operational leadership. • Support stock availability, service delivery and customer experience initiatives. • Ensure sales growth is achieved without negatively impacting operational efficiency. Governance, Compliance and Commercial Excellence • Ensure compliance with company policies, procedures and sales governance requirements. • Promote ethical selling practices and sound commercial decision-making. • Monitor discounting practices and margin performance. • Ensure adherence to approved customer onboarding and credit processes. • Drive consistency in sales execution across all territories within the region. • Support the implementation of national sales standards and methodologies. Reporting and Business Insights • Prepare weekly, monthly and quarterly sales performance reports. • Monitor regional sales trends, customer performance and market opportunities. • Provide recommendations to the National Sales Manager to improve sales effectiveness. • Track progress against strategic sales objectives and growth targets. • Ensure accuracy and integrity of sales reporting and forecasting. Requirements: Minimum Qualifications • Degree or Diploma in Business Management, Sales, Marketing, Commerce or related field. • Relevant sales leadership qualifications advantageous. Experience • Minimum 5-8 years sales experience within a commercial, wholesale, retail, building materials or related environment. • Minimum 3 years leadership experience managing sales teams. • Proven track record of consistently achieving sales and profit targets. • Experience managing customer relationships and key accounts. • Experience leading geographically dispersed teams advantageous. • Experience using CRM systems and sales performance management tools.
