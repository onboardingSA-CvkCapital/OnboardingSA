About the role

Transnet (Transnet Rail Infrastructure Manager (TRIM)) is hiring a Specialist: Enterprise Supplier Developm, based in Johannesburg. The role reports to the Snr Manager: SD ED and Transformation. Grade: F. 1. To support the implementation of Broad-based Black Economic Empowerment (BBBEE), Enterprise and Supplier Development ("ESD"), Preferential Procurement and Localisation strategy aligned to the broader national policy and regulatory framework. 2. To liaise with supply chain community across Transnet Rail Infrastructure Manager ("TRIM") to support the implementation, monitoring and reporting of BBBEE, ESD.3. To support the training initiatives for Supplier development to drive compliance within TRIM. 4. To track, monitor and report on BBBEE, ESD expenditure, as well as imported spend and local content. 5. Supports the execution of the integrated Supply Chain Management strategy (iSCM), specifically through participation in the Supplier Development Centre of Excellence (SDCOE).
