About the role

M-Sure is a world leader in its field of specialisation offering a variety of financial products for motor vehicles. M-Sure aims to creatively solve problems and add value to people by ensuring Mobility for Good. M-Sure forms part of the Motus Group, a leading JSE listed Company in the Automotive Industry where performance is driven by three keystones, 1. Leaders, 2. Innovation & 3. Customer Champions. M-Sure is seeking to employ a qualified and experienced Product and Pricing Specialist.  The successful candidate will join a dynamic and highly skilled Finance team. The successful candidate will report directly to the Head of Risk and Analytics. JOB DESCRIPTION: The Product and Pricing specialist is a senior technical specialist responsible for building and leading pricing analytics, predictive modelling, portfolio analytics, and decision-science capabilities. The role translates complex data (claims, financials, customer behaviour, and operational data) into actionable insights that support pricing, product profitability, and strategic decision-making. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: • Lead pricing analytics, including claims frequency/severity, cost drivers, sensitivity analysis, and profitability reviews • Develop and maintain statistical and predictive models (e.g., GLM/GAM) for risk segmentation, customer behaviour, retention, and financial performance • Apply optimisation, simulation, and scenario modelling to improve pricing, operations, and portfolio outcomes • Build portfolio monitoring dashboards and early-warning indicators to track performance, risks, and model drift • Support dynamic pricing and decision engines, including model inputs, rules, and monitoring frameworks • Ensure all models and analyses are well-documented, reproducible, explainable, and aligned with governance standards • Translate technical outputs into clear recommendations for business stakeholders and decision forums • Collaborate with Product, Finance, Claims, Technology, and external partners to embed analytics into business processes • Support knowledge transfer and internal capability buildingEDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE: • Postgraduate degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Actuarial Science, or related field • 6–10 years’ experience in data science, pricing analytics, actuarial modelling, or decision science (preferably in financial services/insurance) • Experience with predictive modelling, optimisation techniques, and real-world data challenges • Strong skills in Python, SQL, statistical modelling, and data visualisation (e.g., Power BI) • Solid understanding of model governance, validation, and business application of analytics SKILLS AND PERSONAL ATTRIBUTES REQUIRED: • Strong commercial acumen and problem-solving ability • Ability to translate complex analytics into business decisions • Effective stakeholder communication and collaboration • High standards of governance, documentation, and ethical data use • Clear credit and criminal record • Own and live up to company values
