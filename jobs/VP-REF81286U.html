About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Metallurgist, based in Rustenburg, North West. Safety: Create a safety culture through productive and authorized operational systems/processes that allow all employees (including contracting partners) to provide their outputs in a risk-controlled environment. Oversee consistent application of Safety & Health principles in all discipline interactions and take personal responsibility for safety of self and others to ensure zero harm and elimination of fatalities. Performance and Delivery: Operational Planning: People & Teams: Compliance This role is at GBF 6 level reporting to the Section Manager Production Processing

Responsibilities

  • Supports the Section Manager Production Processing in maximising production by supporting the technical control of the operation providing health and safety is not compromised.
  • Provide people support within the section by assisting production staff by supporting technical workload, assigned tasks and reviewing/ recognising outputs of team members.
  • For area within the section discern and identify trends and leading indicators of technical problems which may lead to a reduction in operating efficiency or put at risk the integrity of the overall operation.
  • Support the sections Business Improvement structure through dedicated Analyse and Improve initiatives, embedding a culture of learning and continuous improvement for benchmark performance.
  • Support the Technical team to ensure overall integrity, sustainability and reliability of the area in the section as required.
  • Implement a purpose-driven, high-performing culture that is aligned with the company values, is inclusive and promotes diversity, supports engagement and fosters continuous improvement to work processes.
  • Support required compliance governance in the context of discipline on a sectional level, through the implementation and management of assurance systems, feedback management routines and discipline specific trigger action response plans for areas as defined by technical superiors.
  • Ensure compliance with internal and external regulatory, legislative and permitting requirements.

Requirements

  • Technical Knowledge and Skills
  • Value chain management within discipline, and awareness of interdependencies across value chain steps.
  • Business improvement tools and techniques
  • Developing skills in solving complex technical investigations and meeting long term business needs.
  • Developing skill in process alignment and identifying opportunities to maximise performance levels across the mining value chain.
  • Developing project management skills - drives the sharing of project and resource management best practice to continuously improve project capabilities and resource management.
  • Developing skill in Process and Chemistry know-how - acts as a thought leader for technical analysis applied to the relevant section’s flow sheets required for supporting the operation.
  • Proficient communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Proficient computer literacy.
  • Bachelor's degree (Hons) in Metallurgy, Chemical engineering, Chemistry, or related science
  • (NQF level 8)
  • Completed AAP’s Advanced Metallurgical Developmental Program or equivalent
  • Experience
  • 3 years’ relevant experience
