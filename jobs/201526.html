About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies require the services of a reputable Pharmacist Assistant Post Basic Qualified - PBQ for their Roodepoort store. You will be responsible for dispensing medications, providing pharmaceutical expertise to patients and healthcare professionals, and ensuring the safe and effective use of prescription medications. Minimum Requirements: • Grade 12 / Matric • Post Basic Pharmacist Assistant Qualification • Registered with the South African Pharmacy Council (SAPC) • Willing and able to work retail hours Advantageous: • Knowledge of Retail/ FMCG operations • 3rd Additional Language • Knowledge of or experience in SAP and E-Scripting • Unisolve Experience Duties and Responsibilities: • Pharmacy substance according to a formula and Standard Operating Procedures approved by the responsible pharmacist • assist with the manufacturing of a non-sterile or sterile medicine or scheduled substance according to a formula and Standard Operating Procedures approved by the responsible pharmacist the re-packaging of medicine the distribution and control of stock of Schedule 1 to Schedule 5 medicines
