About the role

Executive Sous ChefA prestigious luxury hospitality and leisure destination in the Garden Route is seeking an experienced Executive Sous Chef to support the Executive Chef in the management and control of all culinary operations within the Food & Beverage department.The successful candidate will play a key role in overseeing food production, food service operations, catering and event planning, and back-of-house kitchen functions. This role requires a passionate culinary professional who can deliver exceptional guest experiences, maintain high operational standards, drive innovation, and ensure cost-effective food production across multiple outlets.This position reports directly to the Executive Chef and forms part of the senior culinary leadership team. Key Responsibilities • Assist the Executive Chef in the overall management and leadership of all culinary operations. • Ensure the consistent delivery of exceptional food quality and presentation standards across all dining outlets. • Stay informed of emerging culinary trends and industry developments. • Develop innovative menu concepts and product offerings aligned with guest preferences and market trends. • Analyse recipes and menu items to determine accurate food costs, selling prices, and profitability. • Create special menu offerings to maximise stock utilisation and minimise wastage. • Coordinate food production planning, budgeting, purchasing, and inventory control processes. • Monitor and control departmental expenditure to ensure adherence to budget targets. • Assist with forecasting, stock management, and procurement planning. • Ensure efficient kitchen operations through effective resource allocation and workflow management. • Maintain and improve food production standards, operational efficiencies, and service delivery. • Lead, coach, mentor, and develop kitchen teams to achieve departmental objectives. • Conduct staff training and skills development initiatives. • Monitor employee performance and implement corrective action where required. • Promote a culture of teamwork, accountability, and continuous improvement. • Ensure compliance with all food safety, hygiene, health, safety, environmental, and quality standards. • Monitor adherence to company policies, procedures, and legislative requirements. • Support the planning and execution of catering functions, special events, and banqueting operations. • Maintain high levels of guest satisfaction by responding effectively to feedback and operational challenges. • Work closely with other departments to ensure seamless guest experiences. Minimum Requirements • 3-Year Chef's Diploma from an internationally recognized culinary institution. • Minimum 6 years' experience within a professional culinary environment. • At least 3 years' experience in an Executive Sous Chef role within a luxury 5-star hotel, resort, or similar hospitality environment. • Classical culinary training essential. • Strong knowledge of food costing, menu engineering, and kitchen financial controls. • Computer literacy with proficiency in Microsoft Office. • Working knowledge of SAP will be advantageous. • Sound understanding of financial management principles. • Strong leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills. • Ability to perform effectively in a fast-paced, high-volume environment. Competencies • Leadership and team development • Strategic and operational planning • Financial and commercial awareness • Problem-solving and decision-making • Attention to detail • Creativity and innovation • Time management and organisational skills • Effective communication and interpersonal skills • Ability to perform under pressure • Commitment to service excellence
