About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Snr Specialist Project Procurement, based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. Support the management of end-to-end project procurement, at a tactical, operational and strategic level to ensure security of supply, cost control, sustainability and optimal supplier performance for declared projects. Safety: Create a safety culture through productive and authorized operational systems/processes that allow all employees (including contracting partners) to provide their outputs in a risk-controlled environment. Oversee consistent application of Safety & Health principles in all discipline interactions and take personal responsibility for safety of self and others to ensure zero harm and elimination of fatalities. Performance and Delivery Contribute to the overall SC strategy, risk identification, critical tasks & performance targets. Translate strategies into planning and scheduling for Project Procurement Management activities. Actively participate in value delivery target setting, tracking and initiative development. Accurate contract administration of project related procurement contracts. Provide specialist support for performance management of contractual KPI’s. Conduct Vendor and Material Master data updates. Ensure effective tracking and reporting of supplier performance.
