About the role

Valterra Platinum is hiring a Section Ventilation Officer, based in Mokopane, Limpopo. This role is at a band Level 7 and reporting to the Shaft Ventilation Engineer.

Responsibilities

  • Interpretation of legal requirements.
  • Ensures adherence to legal compliance through monitoring and reporting.
  • Responsible to ensure that policies, programmes, standards, codes of practice and procedures are maintained in accordance with legal and Group requirements.
  • Statutory Plans are in place and up to date (Ventilation plans and mine rescue plans).
  • Measure compliance to Section 11 and 12 of the Mine Health and Safety Act (Occupational Health Risk Assessment and Monitoring).
  • Measure compliance to Environmental Engineering and Occupational
  • Hygiene Regulations of the Mine Health and Safety Act pertaining to monitoring and reporting at Business Area level.
  • Ventilation and Occupational Hygiene programmes are properly administered.
  • Assist in the development of best practices, standards and procedures.
  • Advice on management on Business Area Ventilation and Occupational Hygiene planning, technical systems and administration.
  • Assist in the implementation and maintenance of Emergency preparedness programmes.
  • Report and advice on Ventilation and Occupational Hygiene matters.
  • Participate in mining Ventilation and Occupational Hygiene technology studies.
  • Provide solutions to specific Ventilation and Occupational Hygiene matters (known or identified via trend analysis).
  • Adhere to ventilation and occupational hygiene policies, procedures and standards.
  • Sets daily, weekly and monthly ventilation and occupational hygiene targets.
  • Maintain an effective risk assessment programme.
  • Participate in audits.
  • Ensure follow up of risk assessments and audits findings, and the implementation of recommendations made.
  • Conduct daily ventilation and occupational hygiene measurements.
  • Meet time deadlines pertaining to reporting (daily, weekly, monthly and ad-hoc).
  • Provide information and advise regarding ventilation and occupational hygiene matters related to a specific workplace or section of the business area.
  • Advises on changes to plans, schedules, procedures and standards to minimize the effect of unexpected or unsafe work practices.
  • Ensures adherence to legal compliance in terms of required reports.
  • Valid statistics for ventilation and occupational hygiene are available for a specific workplace or section of the business area.
  • Timorous submission of reports.
  • Occupational related exposure histories are recorded and reported
  • Maintenance of equipment.
  • Condition of ventilation and occupational hygiene equipment and instruments.
  • Monitoring quality of equipment and materials supplied to ensure adherence to safety and risk specifications.
  • Utilize labour efficiency.
  • Ensure sub-ordinate competency and promote training activities.
  • Identify and advise on suitable training interventions and programmes.
  • Provide ventilation and occupational hygiene coaching.
  • Assist in future planning for existing operations.
  • Seek improved and / or alternative engineering control measures for optimized ventilation and health hazard elimination and / or control.

Requirements

  • Qualifications
  • Valid EB/Code 08 driver’s license
  • Elementary certificate in Mine Environmental Control
  • Chamber of Mines Intermediate Certificate in Mine Environmental Control
  • Associated member - Mine Ventilation Society of South Africa
  • Advantageous certificates
  • Certificate in Environmental Health
  • Certificate in Occupational Hygiene
  • Certificate in Occupational Hygiene Risk Assessment
  • Certificate in Occupational Hygiene Legislation
  • Experience, Knowledge and skills
  • Practical experience of at least 5 years in the Ventilation and Occupational Hygiene Field.
  • Experience of at least 2 years in the Mining environment.
  • Experience in Massive mechanised mining
  • Computer literate in MS Office, SAP, and other specialised technical programs.
  • At Valterra Platinum, you will join a team committed to excellence and impact.
