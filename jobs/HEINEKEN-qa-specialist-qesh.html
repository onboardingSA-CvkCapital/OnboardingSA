About the role

Heineken Beverages is hiring a QA Specialist QESH, based in Johannesburg. To support the prevention of food safety, quality, environmental, safety and health issues by validating and verifying the effectiveness of Quality, Environment, Safety and Health (QESH) governance, assurance and control mechanisms across the site. To support production and its supporting functions in maintaining stable, capable and compliant processes through effective QESH standards, process verification, risk identification and improvement follow-up. To drive and support the implementation of QESH requirements into operational workflows, standards, checklists and daily management routines. To promote proactive ownership of QESH standards within operations, moving from reactive issue management to prevention and condition-based management. To support the PQ pillar, TPM, continuous improvement activities and QESH performance improvement through data analysis, trend identification, corrective action follow-up and verification of effectiveness. To ensure QESH governance activities support consumer protection, legal compliance, product conformance, process stability and alignment with HEINEKEN global quality and food safety expectations. To ensure that effective QESH management systems are in place and maintained so that the site retains its License to Trade and applicable FSSC and ISO certification status.

Responsibilities

  • Safety, Food Safety and Sustainability
  • Review and support the integration of QESH requirements into operational workflows, standards, checklists and daily management routines.
  • Support implementation and verification of food safety and QESH standards across operations, including production, warehousing, laboratories, utilities and relevant support functions.
  • Monitor compliance to applicable legal, regulatory, internal and HEINEKEN global requirements related to QESH.
  • Support risk identification, risk assessment and follow-up of corrective and preventive actions for QESH-related risks and incidents.
  • Support supplier, service provider and contractor compliance where activities may impact QESH requirements.
  • Contribute to Brewing a Better World and local sustainability objectives through QESH governance, monitoring and improvement support.
  • Quality of the Process and Product
  • Monitor process and product quality data to identify trends, risks, recurring non-conformances and improvement opportunities.
  • Verify that corrective and preventive actions are implemented and effective in eliminating root causes of quality, food safety and QESH non-conformances.
  • Support the implementation and maintenance of Q-points, quality factors and condition management requirements across relevant operational areas.
  • Support operations in linking defects, complaints and losses to process conditions, material quality, equipment condition and human factors.
  • Review and support the optimisation of product and process specifications, control plans and operating standards to ensure product conformance and consumer protection.
  • Support supplier performance monitoring, trials, documentation and issue resolution where raw materials, packaging materials or services impact quality or food safety.
  • TPM and Continuous Improvement
  • Participate in and support the PQ Pillar to develop autonomous teams focused on improving quality culture, process control and operational ownership of quality.
  • Use problem-solving and continuous improvement methods to support root cause analysis and sustainable elimination of quality, food safety and QESH losses.
  • Support the implementation and verification of preventive actions to reduce repeat non-conformances, complaints, incidents and process deviations.
  • Share best practices and lessons learned across departments and sites to improve QESH performance.
  • Support improvement projects linked to First Time Right, customer complaints, process stability, supplier performance, hygiene, quality losses and QESH compliance.
  • Promote proactive quality and QESH ownership by coaching and supporting operational teams in standards, controls and daily routines.
  • Quality Process Management and QESH Governance
  • Ensure that QESH management systems are established, implemented, maintained and continually improved to meet applicable legal, regulatory, FSSC and ISO requirements and maintain the site’s License to Trade and certification status.
  • Review and support the periodic update of QESH procedures, standards, checklists, control plans and specifications to ensure they remain effective and aligned to global and local requirements.
  • Verify that routine quality and QESH controls are embedded into operational processes and owned by the relevant process owners.
  • Support internal and external audits, inspections and governance reviews by preparing evidence, identifying gaps and following up on actions.
  • Monitor closure and effectiveness of corrective and preventive actions from audits, incidents, complaints, deviations and management reviews.
  • Support the governance of deviations, concessions, change controls and risk assessments where QESH compliance may be impacted.
  • Provide guidance to operations on QESH requirements, standards and escalation processes.
  • NPI Support
  • Participate in NPI projects to ensure quality, food safety, QESH and legal requirements are identified, assessed and implemented through the stage-gate process.
  • Support the validation and verification of raw materials, packaging materials, product specifications, process requirements and operational readiness before launch.
  • Review QESH risks linked to new products, processes, materials, suppliers or equipment and ensure appropriate controls are defined.
  • Support operational trials, documentation, approvals and communication with relevant stakeholders.
  • Ensure NPI projects are supported with clear QESH requirements, effective risk controls and timely completion of quality and food safety deliverables.
  • Management of information and reporting
  • Maintain accurate QESH and quality reporting systems to support effective decision-making and governance.
  • Analyse process, product, complaint, audit, incident and performance data to identify trends, risks and opportunities for improvement.
  • Prepare QESH and quality reports, dashboards and summaries for site leadership, Quality Assurance management and relevant governance forums.
  • Track and report progress on corrective actions, preventive actions, audit findings, deviations and improvement plans.
  • Ensure QESH documentation, records and evidence are maintained, controlled and available for audits, inspections and management reviews.
  • Use data insights to support proactive risk management, process stability, product conformance and continuous improvement.

  • Experience in continuous improvement methodologies such as TPM, Lean, Six Sigma or structured problem solving.
