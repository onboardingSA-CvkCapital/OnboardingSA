About the role

Are you an experienced Logistics & Warehouse Manager who knows how to keep stock moving, operations controlled and teams performing at their best?An established organisation is looking for a hands-on and commercially minded Logistics & Warehouse Manager to take ownership of warehouse operations, inventory control and logistics execution within a fast-paced operational environment.This is a key leadership role for someone who can look beyond the day-to-day and challenge plans, identify operational gaps, improve efficiency and hold teams and service providers accountable. What you'll be doing • Lead and oversee day-to-day warehouse and logistics operations, including receiving, storage, picking, staging, loading and dispatch. • Review and challenge logistics plans to ensure routes, loads, vehicles, capacity and priorities are aligned with operational requirements. • Monitor execution against plans and ensure delays, capacity constraints and service risks are addressed quickly. • Take ownership of inventory accuracy, including cycle counts, stock takes, reconciliations and variance investigations. • Ensure appropriate controls are in place for damaged, obsolete, quarantined and returned stock. • Monitor warehouse capacity, stock ageing, space utilisation and slow-moving inventory. • Oversee transport execution, including transporter performance, deliveries, turnaround times, proof of delivery, claims and transport costs. • Investigate late, short, damaged or failed deliveries and drive corrective action. • Lead warehouse supervisors and operational teams through clear standards, coaching, performance management and skills development. • Ensure compliance with HSE, safe material handling, security, housekeeping and operational procedures. • Drive accurate ERP/WMS transactions and strong system discipline. • Analyse operational performance, identify bottlenecks and lead root-cause analysis and continuous improvement initiatives. • Partner with Procurement, Production, Sales, Finance, Quality and Maintenance to ensure operational priorities remain aligned. • Drive improvements in safety, service, inventory accuracy, productivity, waste reduction and cost control. • Prepare operational reports covering performance trends, risks, root causes and corrective actions. What we're looking for • Grade 12 / National Senior Certificate is essential. • Relevant diploma or degree in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Warehouse Management, Operations Management, Industrial Engineering or a related field. • Minimum 5 years' progressive experience within warehousing, logistics, distribution or supply chain operations. • At least 2 years' experience in a supervisory or management role. • Proven experience managing inventory accuracy, stock takes, receiving, storage, picking, dispatch and transport execution. • Strong experience managing frontline teams, productivity, performance and training. • Experience within manufacturing, packaging, FMCG, industrial distribution or another high-volume environment will be advantageous. • Strong ERP/WMS and stock-management system experience. • Good knowledge of Microsoft Excel; reporting or BI exposure will be advantageous. • Proven experience in operational improvement, root-cause analysis, KPI reporting and cost control. • Working knowledge of occupational health and safety and warehouse compliance requirements. • Valid driver's licence where required. The person we're looking for You’re a hands-on operational leader who is comfortable being on the warehouse floor, making decisions and holding people accountable. You don't simply accept a logistics plan — you question assumptions, identify risks and make sure the plan can actually be executed. You’re analytical and process-driven, with a strong eye for detail and a genuine focus on inventory accuracy, service delivery, safety and cost. At the same time, you’re a people leader who can set clear expectations, coach teams and create accountability without losing sight of the bigger operational picture. If you're ready to take ownership of a high-volume warehouse and logistics operation and make a measurable impact on service, productivity, stock accuracy and cost, we'd like to hear from you.
