About the role

The Department of Correctional Services is hiring a State Accountant: Income in Financial Accounting, based in National Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: should be submitted online at https://careers.dcs.gov.za/. Emailed, post or hand delivered applications will not be considered. Reference number to quote: HO 2026/11/75 Closing date: 09 November 2026 @ 15H45. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Appointment under the Public Service Act.

Responsibilities

  • Responsible for handling accounts payable and accounts receivable.
  • Reconcile monthly revenue collected and pay-over revenue collected to National Treasury.
  • Clearing of suspense accounts.
  • Coordinate the review process of annual tariffs.
  • Issue financial circulars for annual tariffs to be used in each financial year.
  • The incumbent will liaise with the following bodies/sections in the execution of his/her tasks - National Treasury, regions and management areas.
  • Execute responsibilities of officials as stipulated in Section 45 of the PFMA.
  • Management of performance information, resources, finance and assets.

Requirements

  • An appropriate Degree/National Diploma in Finance related field with Financial Accounting with Cost and Management Accounting as a major subject.
  • 1-2 years relevant finance experience.
  • Knowledge of BAS.
  • Computer literate.
  • Valid driver’s licence.
  • Competencies And Attributes: Good knowledge of Public Finance Management Act, Treasury Regulations and Basic Accounting System.
  • Knowledge of Public Service Regulations, Financial Administration Procedures and Supply Chain Manuals, Transport, Procurement and Asset Management.
  • Ability to access and utilize information.
  • Plan, organize, leadership and control.
  • Conflict resolution, liaison (internal and external), analytical thinking, problem solving, productivity, responsible, accountable, integrity, committed and innovation skills.
