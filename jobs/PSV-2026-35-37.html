About the role

The Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment is hiring 2 Assistant Director: Programme Monitoring and Evaluation posts, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.environment.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: EP24/2026 Closing date: 19 October 2026, 16:00. No late applications will be accepted Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct performance monitoring and evaluation for the Environmental Programmes.
  • Contribute to the process of identifying focus areas for monitoring and evaluation Conduct desktop analysis of all active projects, Capture data from the fieldwork, analyse and develop reports with recommendations.
  • Communicate findings of the programme monitoring and evaluation reports to the relevant stakeholders.
  • Contribute to the development and implementation of a coordinated M&E system for the EP, Contribute in the process of developing and review of M&E Framework and M&E plans Monitor adherence to the provisions of the M&E Framework.
  • Contribute to the dissemination of the Framework and the M&E plans to the stakeholders.
  • Develop action plans for implementation of recommendations from the programme monitoring and evaluation reports.
  • Follow up on the Implementation of the recommendations from programme monitoring and evaluation reports, Coordinate and monitor the implementation of recommendations from the programme monitoring and evaluation reports.
  • Develop progress reports on implementation of recommendations from the programme performance monitoring and evaluation reports, Facilitation of external evaluation studies: Facilitate confirmation of the area for evaluation, facilitate appointment of External Evaluators: Confirm the evaluation scope and determine methodology to be employed, Coordinate the data collection process and make inputs to the evaluation report, Facilitate the communication of findings and approval of the evaluation reports.
  • Contribute to the Development of the monitoring and evaluation newsletters: Source information mainly from the finalised and approved Programme Monitoring and Evaluation reports Prepare articles for the newsletter.
  • Contribute to the process of finalising the newsletters, circulation/publication to the stakeholders within the branch.

  • Policy and development implementation Strategic Planning Management, Leadership Management, People Management, Change Management, Financial Management, Sound Research, Analytical, organising, Planning, Presentation, Good communication skills (verbal written), Good interpersonal, Coordination stakeholder liaison.
  • Ability to work under pressure and long hours.
