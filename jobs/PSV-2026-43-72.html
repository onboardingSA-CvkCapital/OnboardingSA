About the role

The Government Pensions Administration Agency is hiring an Assistant Director: Management Information and Analytics, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. Ref NO: ASD/MIA/2026/11-1P Strategic Support Services Permanent The purpose of this role is: To provide management information and analytics for informed decision making. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.gpaa.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: ASD/MIA/2026/11-1P Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) No late applications will be considered Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: One position of Assistant Director: Management Information and Analytics is available at the Government Pension Administration Agency - Strategic Support Services Section

Responsibilities

  • Manage information and generate business intelligence.
  • Develop, maintain and improve management information systems and reporting frameworks.
  • Design and produce dashboards, scorecards, analytical reports and performance reports Provide support to MIA internal and external clients through statistical services to GPAA.
  • Generate operational, tactical and strategic intelligence to support executive decision-making.
  • Monitor organisational performance indicators and identify trends and variances.
  • Perform predictive and trend analysis to support planning and risk mitigation.
  • Ensure data is transformed into actionable information for management.
  • Analyse performance gaps, irregularities, variances and emerging trends.
  • Analyse data to generate meaningful insights and reports.
  • Analyse large datasets from multiple systems and data sources.
  • Conduct statistical and quantitative analysis to identify patterns, risks, opportunities and performance gaps.
  • Develop automated reporting solutions and reporting standards.
  • Ensure the accuracy, completeness and reliability of analytical outputs.
  • Prepare reports for governance committees, management structures and external stakeholders.
  • Support statistical reports based on the Oracle and other IT platforms.
  • Manage data and information in line with governance requirements.
  • Support implementation of data governance frameworks, standards and procedures.
  • Promote data quality, integrity, security and compliance with organisational policies.
  • Monitor compliance with data management policies and public sector regulations.
  • Identify and resolve data quality issues with business units.
  • Conduct tests to ensure that intelligence is consistent with GPAA defined requirements.
  • Support the tracking of incoming and finalisation of requests for MIA verified data sets.
  • Provide evidence-based analysis for performance monitoring and evaluation.
  • Support stakeholder engagement and facilitate business requirements.
  • Engage internal and external stakeholders regarding information requirements.
  • Translate business requirements into analytical and reporting solutions.
  • Provide advisory services on performance information and analytics.
  • Conduct workshops and training on reporting tools and dashboards.
  • Facilitate user acceptance testing and implementation of reporting solutions.
  • Support project management with regards to MIA projects.
  • Provide support to solve business blockages pertaining to data processes.
  • Transfer knowledge to internal users on newly implemented business intelligence.
  • Lead and support employees to foster effective performance and teamwork.
  • Manage the performance of the unit which involves coaching and mentoring.
  • Develop performance standards and evaluate teams and individuals.
  • Ensure compliance with HR policies and performance management requirements.

  • A relevant three-year National Diploma/Degree in Information Management, Data Analytics/Science, Statistics, Economics or any other equivalent qualification.
  • At least 3 - 5 years’ relevant experience within management information, data analytics or a related analytical environment coupled with at least 2 years’ supervisory/junior management experience.
  • Knowledge of management information procedures and systems.
  • Knowledge of MIS business intelligence, oracle, SQL, PowerBI.
  • Knowledge of statistical analysis.
  • Knowledge of Public Service Act and Regulations, Public Service Legislation and Regulatory framework.
  • Knowledge of management information and analytical terminology concepts and practices.
  • Knowledge of GEPF CIVPEN and oracle.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office packages.
  • Organising and coordination skills.
  • Critical thinking capability.
  • Problem solving skills.
  • Time management capability.
  • Communication skills.

  • Project management.
  • Dynamic, flexible and reliable.
