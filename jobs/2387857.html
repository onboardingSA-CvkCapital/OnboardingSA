About the role

NFB Asset Management is a botique asset management company based in Sandton, Johannesburg and are seeking to employ a Compliance and Administrative Assistant to provide compliance and administrative support. KEY RESPONSIBILITY AREAS: Compliance Assistant Responsibilities FAIS: Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act (“FAIS”) • To maintain a FAIS Compliance Risk Management plan for the business as required. • To ensure that there is a strong and effective FAIS policy and procedures manual in place. • To ensure that FAIS policy, processes and procedures are compliant with legislative and regulatory requirements to ensure that the business operates in a compliant manner. • Ensure that all required reports are accurate and timeously submitted to the relevant regulator/s. • Monitoring and supervising compliance with FAIS in line with best compliance methodology, guidelines, and standards. • Identifying potential compliance breaches, investigating compliance issues and irregularities as may be appropriate and required and reporting on these appropriately and promptly. • To report to the MD on the status of FAIS compliance in the business. • To generate relevant compliance reports, follow up on outstanding actions and report all non-compliance issues to the MD. • To liaise with employees to answer queries, guide and identify and implement interventions in accordance with business policy requirements. • To proactively identify any required changes to existing processes to enable the business to be compliant, provide advice to the MD on actions to be taken and assist with implementation of compliance controls. • Training of staff on FAIS compliance issues with the aim of establishing a culture of compliance within the business. • Serve as a point of contact and establish good working relationship with regulators and external compliance officers. • Ensuring that registers of the FSCA and other regulators as may be appropriate are appropriately updated and maintained in respect of information relating to the business (which will include ensuring that all required profile changes are conducted). • Ensuring that agreements and documentation regularly used by the business comply with FAIS requirements. • Ensuring that FAIS representatives and KIs remain fit and proper and are appropriately authorised to represent the business. Attending industry forums, seminars, working groups etc. as may be appropriate and practical. FICA: Financial Intelligence Centre Act (“FICA”) • Ensuring that FICA and Anti Money-Laundering (“AML”) regulations are complied with by the business. • Ensuring that the business effectively implement and maintain the approved FICA RMCP. • Ensuring that FICA verification and screening during the client on-boarding process is effectively performed in line with the FICA RMCP. • Ensuring that clients are reverified and screened from time to time in line with the RMCP. • Monitoring and supervising compliance with FICA and AML regulations in line with best good compliance methodology, guidelines, and standards. • Identifying potential and actual compliance breaches and/or suspicious transactions and other reportable transactions and reporting on these appropriately and promptly. • Training of staff on FICA and AML compliance issues with the aim of establishing the culture of compliance within the business. • Serve as a point of contact and establish a good working relationship with regulators, external compliance officers, and service providers. Administrative Assistant Responsibilities • Setting up appointments, primarily in respect of Board, Investment Committee and Product Committee meetings. • Executing all travel arrangements (flights, accommodation, transfer, immigration documents, including the documentation thereof for accounting purposes) for all team members. • Maintaining employee absence schedules (business travel, leave, public holidays, office closures, etc.). • Attending general meetings and taking meeting notes. • Other administrative duties hat would reasonably be required of you and • Helping create and maintain a positive working environment at all times. General • Act with diligence, care and degree of competency required from a compliance administrator. • To communicate effectively, provide support, create awareness with employees and managers as may be appropriate, and recommend improvements to minimize risk and recommend best practices. • Training of staff on relevant compliance issues with the aim of establishing a culture of compliance within the business. • Assist with compliance in respect of other regulations and legislation as may be appropriate and required from time to time. • Assist with drawing up of board resolutions and presenting it for signature utilising electronic signing facility. • Assist with reviewing of contracts and agreements as and when required. • Ensure that the Employee remains fit and proper in terms of any regulations that may be imposed at any future time to legally perform the Compliance role required of you in terms of this contract of employment. • General administrative duties that may be required from time to time as may be appropriate given the role of the Employee. • Any reasonable travel as may be required from time to time.
