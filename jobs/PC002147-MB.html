About the role

Our client is seeking an experienced and strategic Head of Procurement in Isando, Johannesburg, to lead and drive procurement excellence across their organization. The ideal candidate will have 10–15 years of progressive procurement and supply chain experience, including at least 5 years at senior management or executive level, together with a relevant tertiary qualification. Proven experience managing regional procurement portfolios across Sub-Saharan Africa and delivering procurement transformation programmes within decentralized corporate structures is essential. Experience with bonded warehouses will be advantageous.
