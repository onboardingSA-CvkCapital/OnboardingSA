About the role

Main Purpose of the Role We are seeking a skilled and experienced Electrician to join our team. The successful candidate will be responsible for the installation, maintenance, inspection, troubleshooting, fault-finding and repair of the site’s electrical infrastructure, plant, and equipment to ensure safe, reliable, and efficient operations. The role will support the availability and reliability of critical infrastructure while minimising equipment downtime and maintaining high standards of safety, quality, and service delivery. The incumbent will work across a range of electrical and associated mechanical systems, utilising specialised tools, equipment and technical techniques to assemble, dismantle, align, calibrate, and maintain machinery and components in accordance with required operational standards. Previous experience within laboratory, facilities management, engineering, or other critical infrastructure environments will be advantageous. The Electrician will also contribute to the continuous improvement and effectiveness of the department’s maintenance management system, including SAP Plant Maintenance (SAP PM), by ensuring accurate work execution, effective maintenance planning, comprehensive documentation, and timely feedback on completed maintenance activities.
