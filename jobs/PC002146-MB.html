About the role

Our client is seeking a detail-oriented and organized Procurement Assistant to join their team in Isando, Johannesburg. Strong communication, interpersonal, organisational, and time-management skills are essential, together with knowledge of purchasing and sourcing, stock control, and B-BBEE procurement requirements. If you are proactive, accurate, and have a strong work ethic, we would love to hear from you.
