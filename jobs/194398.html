Financial Analyst (Newly Qualified CA(SA)) Pinelands, Cape Town Hybrid About the Role Confluent Services, a Financial Services group based in Cape Town, seeks a newly qualified CA(SA) to join the finance team as a Financial Analyst. The successful candidate reports to the Head of Finance Operations, with a dotted line to the Head of Credit Solutions and direct visibility to the Group CFO. Primary focus is analytical, supporting the credit solutions business with financial reporting, partner revenue analysis and margin insights.
