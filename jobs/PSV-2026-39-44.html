About the role

The Government Printing Works is hiring 2 Chief Production Clerk: Travel and Id Document Dispatch/Administrative Support Services posts, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. Directorates: High Security Printing and Security Printing How to apply: Link: GPW 26/021 Reference number to quote: GPW 26/021 Closing date: 17 November 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor the movement, work in progress, and distribution of documents: Plan and schedule the Section’s work to ensure the timely and correct distribution to the High Security Dispatch Division, the couriers and customers.
  • Check that all the batches of documents sent from the Personalisation Section and Quality Control were received Coordinate and monitor production workflow, work-inprogress and the movement and distribution of high-security printed materials.
  • Plan and schedule the Section’s activities to ensure the timely completion and correct distribution of documents to internal divisions, couriers and customers.
  • Collaborate with relevant departments and business units, including design, prepress, printing, personalisation, quality control, finishing and dispatch, to facilitate the smooth flow and timely completion of production activities.
  • Verify that all batches received from production sections and Quality Control are accurately recorded, securely transferred and signed for.
  • Ensure the proper preparation of finished goods for dispatch, including the accurate application and verification of customer information, delivery addresses, waybills and supporting documentation.
  • Expedite urgent documents, track outstanding or delayed jobs on production systems, and follow up to resolve production or distribution delays.
  • Coordinate with internal departments, external parties, shipping companies and courier service providers to arrange transportation and ensure the accurate and timely delivery of printed materials.
  • Monitor shipment progress, trace dispatched materials, follow up on proof of delivery, and address delivery discrepancies or delays.
  • Process waybills, proof-ofdelivery documentation, courier invoices and allocation notices.
  • Maintain accurate records of finished goods transferred and distributed.
  • Monitor and maintain inventory levels of printing materials, supplies and consumables, including paper, ink and other production-related items.
  • Track usage, initiate replenishment where required, and maintain an organised inventory system to prevent shortages or excessive stock.
  • Ensure that the Section and its employees are adequately equipped and resourced.
  • Keep and manage accurate records, registers, statistics and correspondence.
  • Use applicable digital systems and software to capture, organise, store, retrieve and report information efficiently.
  • Maintain the Section’s filing system, incoming and outgoing correspondence registers, movement registers and signed handover documentation.
  • Prepare basic letters, reports and other correspondence, respond to routine enquiries, and submit accurate reports within the prescribed timeframes.
  • Manage safety, health, environment, risk and quality within the Section.
  • Ensure compliance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, SHERQ requirements, security controls and all applicable GPW production policies and procedures.
  • Ensure that all procedures and systems relating to the handling, movement, storage and protection of high-security printed materials are strictly observed.
  • Provide administrative and logistical support to the Section.
  • Administer leave, attendance, timesheets, overtime approvals, overtime claims, time corrections and personnel records.
  • Maintain accurate leave and attendance registers and ensure that all supporting documentation is completed and submitted within the required timeframes.
  • Facilitate the effective management of procurement, equipment, materials and facilities within the Section.
  • Supervise and manage employees within the Section.
  • Allocate, coordinate and monitor the work of staff and provide functional and technical guidance.
  • Conduct performance reviews in accordance with GPW policies, promote teamwork, and apply disciplinary measures in accordance with applicable prescripts and procedures when necessary.
  • Assist with the administration and coordination of staff training, coaching and development.
  • Participate in the development, review and implementation of relevant policies, procedures and operational systems.
  • Research and remain informed of technological developments, digital systems and emerging practices within the printing and distribution environment to support continuous improvement, operational efficiency and the maintenance of high-quality standards.

Requirements

  • Grade 12 Certificate.
  • At least 3 years’ appropriate experience in the industry.
  • Must be able to obtain a positive security clearance.
  • Knowledge of processes and equipment.
  • The incumbent will be required to work rotating shifts, including weekends, public holidays, and night shifts, based on operational requirement.
  • Extended working hours may be required.
  • Ability to communicate with all levels of management.
  • A valid driver's license will be an added advantage.
