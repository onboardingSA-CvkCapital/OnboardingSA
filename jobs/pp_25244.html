About the role

Closing Date: 18 September 2026 Location: Johannesburg Role Objectives: • The Hollard IT Learnership Programme is designed to provide aspiring technology professionals with practical workplace experience, digital and technical skills development, and a strong foundation for a career in Information Technology. • The programme combines structured learning with hands on exposure to technology environments and business operations. Advert Summary: • Are you passionate about technology, innovation, and solving problems? • Ready to gain real world experience while building a nationally recognised qualification? What You”ll Do: • Gain exposure to IT environments, digital business operations, and technology enabled business processes. • Participate in structured learning activities and workplace projects. • Assist with basic IT administrative and operational tasks. • Develop problem solving, analytical, and critical thinking skills. • Collaborate with technology teams, mentors, and colleagues. • Learn about systems, applications, data, digital tools, and business technologies. • Complete learning requirements towards nationally recognised qualifications. • Build professional workplace skills including communication, teamwork, and professionalism. Educational Requirements • Completed Grade 12 / Matric. • Passed English – 60% or higher and an additional South African language Passed • Maths – 60% • Passed CAT or Physical Science – 50% •
