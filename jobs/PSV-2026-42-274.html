About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring an Assistant Director: Supply Chain Management in Infrastructure Management: Eastern Operations CD: Water Resource Infrastructure Operations and Maintenance Dir: Operations Eastern, based in Midmar Dam. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 041226/07 Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: NB: Shortlisted candidates will be subjected to practical exercise and integrity (Ethical conduct) assessment.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate, review, research, analyze and plan the procurement needs of the department (in line with Procurement Strategy).
  • Analyze requirements, undertake research, determine and develop proposals for implementation.
  • Asses the results of the research on the market, interprets and develop proposals for procurement methodology.
  • Ensure compliance with quality requirements.
  • Determine whether specifications should contain any special conditions.
  • Coordinate review, collect and collate information for the annual procurement plan.
  • Collect information from the relevant role players according to the prescribed template.
  • Check (engage) and analyze the procurement inputs submitted.
  • Confirm availability of budget.
  • Check alignment against strategic and other objectives.
  • Consolidate into Demand Management Plan and submit for approval by the accounting officer.
  • Supervise officials under SCM section.
  • Compile Bid/quotation specifications as required.
  • Compile and publish request for proposals as required.
  • Ensure maintenance of the supplier database.
  • Perform general quality assurance of information in the supplier database (check and verify supporting documents).
  • Allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by supervisees., Advice and lead supervisees regarding all aspects of the SCM work.
  • Manage performance, conduct and discipline of supervisees.
  • Ensure that subordinates are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively.
  • Administer variations of contracts.
  • Ensure evaluations of Bids, price quotations in line with SCM policies Evaluate applications for variations, amendments and cancelations and submit for approval by delegated authority.
  • Undertake dispute resolution and ensure that all documents are prepared and available to resolve disputes.
  • Execute risk assessments in accordance with the SCM risk plan/strategy.
  • Apply the determined risk assessment tools.
  • Obtain and process risk and performance management information.
  • Utilize fraud mechanisms to identify fraud/abuse.
  • Process and analyse risk management information.
  • Report fraud.
  • In the case of abuse report thereon and develop proposals for corrective measures.
  • Ensure that SLA/contracts are concluded in line with SCM policy of DWS.

Requirements

  • A relevant NQF Level 7 qualification in Supply Chain management/Logistics/Financial Accounting/Financial Management.
  • 3 years supply chain experience at a Supervisory level.
  • Working knowledge of Public Finance Management Act (PFMA), Treasury Regulations guidelines.
  • Knowledge and understanding of practices related to PPPFA regulations, Human Resource Management Legislation, policies, practices and procedures.
  • Public Service Anti-Corruption Strategy and anti-corruption and fraud prevention measures.
  • Knowledge of equal opportunities and Affirmative action guidelines and laws.
  • Knowledge of administrative and clerical procedures and systems.
  • Departmental policies and procedures.
  • Proficiency in Ms Word, Excel and Power-point packages.
  • Governmental Financial System (SAP).
  • Principles and practice of financial accounting.
  • Framework for managing performance information.
  • Business strategy transaction and alignment.
  • Problem solving and analysis.
  • Client Orientation and Customer Focus.
  • Verbal and written Communication Skills.
  • Accountability and Ethnical Conduct.
  • Minimum code 8 driver’s license.
