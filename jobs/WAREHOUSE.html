Experience in warehouse environment Able to communicate effectively Matric certificate Clean criminal record Purpose This SOP outlines the procedures for ensuring that Company stock is in good condition, the truck is properly equipped and in good condition for loading, and that the load is secured safely and correctly. Scope This SOP applies to all personnel involved in the inspection, handling, and loading of Company stock. Responsibilities • Warehouse Supervisor: Oversee the entire loading process, ensure compliance with the SOP. • Checkers: Follow procedures to inspect, handle, and load stock correctly. • Checkers: Ensure the truck is in good condition, and safety equipment is in place and functional. • Responsibility: Direct report to transport DPT vice manager and branch manager and checking report monthly share in the DPT meeting. • Authority: have the right to reject all the stock load into the truck. • KPI: need to clarify. Procedure 1. Pre-Loading Inspection of Stock • Inspect Boxes and Units: • Ensure all boxes are intact, with no signs of damage such as tears, dents, or watermarks. • Open a random sample of boxes to check the condition of the units inside. Ensure they are clean and free of any defects or damages. • Total percentage of stock to be checked is 1-2%, in the case that there are more disputes, it needs to be increased to 2-4%, if this still does not eliminate the issue then 100% checking will apply. • Label to be created, including serial number and model number • Confirm that the quantity of units matches the packing slip and is consistent with the inventory records. • Ensure Cleanliness: • Verify that all units are clean and in good condition before loading. • Wipe down units if necessary to remove any dust or residue. • Document Inspection: • Record the condition of the stock and any issues found during the inspection in the inspection log. • Notify the Warehouse Supervisor of any discrepancies or damages immediately. 2. Truck Inspection Before Loading • Truck Condition: • Inspect the truck for any leaks, or damages that could affect the safety and integrity of the load. • Ensure that the loading area of the truck is clean, dry, and free of any debris or substances that could damage the stock. • • Safety Equipment Check: • Verify that all required safety equipment is present and in good working order, including: • Straps, ropes, or other securing devices • Padding or other protective materials • Emergency equipment such as fire extinguishers. • Document Inspection: • Record the truck inspection results, noting any issues and actions taken to address them. • Only proceed with loading if the truck is deemed to be in satisfactory condition. 3. Loading Procedure • Correct Loading of Stock: • Ensure stock is loaded according to the established loading plan, securing all items to prevent movement during transit. • Use appropriate securing devices to prevent shifting or damage during transportation. • Preventing Damage During Loading: • Handle all stock with care, using proper lifting techniques and equipment to avoid dropping or bumping items. • Place padding or protective materials between items if necessary to prevent scratches or other damage. • Verification: • Confirm that the stock being loaded matches the loading documentation (e.g., delivery order, manifest). • Double-check that all units are accounted for and loaded as planned. • Photos of loaded stock to be taken, while loading, beginning of first carriage, mid-way during loading and end of loading of the stock (6m and 12m of trailers). Photos are to be uploaded on a drive and shared for all relevant parties to view. 4. Post-Loading Verification • Final Inspection: • Perform a final check to ensure all stock is securely loaded, and the truck is properly sealed. • Inspect the loading area to ensure no items have been left behind. • Documentation: • Complete the loading checklist, including confirmation that the stock matches the loading documentation and is in good condition. • Submit the completed checklist and inspection logs to the Warehouse Supervisor for review. Quality Assurance • The Warehouse Supervisor will review the completed checklists and inspection logs at the end of each loading session to ensure compliance with this SOP. • Any deviations from this SOP must be documented and reported to management immediately for corrective action.
