About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring a Clinical Nurse Practitioner (Bambanani Clinic) in Nursing-PHC, based in Appelsbosch Hospital. This is a permanent post, paying R495,423 to R581,931 a year. How to apply: should be forwarded to: The Chief Executive Officer, P/Bag X215, Ozwathini, 3242. Applicants are encouraging to hand delivered or email to zakhele.nxumalo@kznhealth.gov.za or courier their applications. This office will not be responsible for late or lost applications due to unreliable post office. For attention: Human Resource Manager Reference number to quote: BAM01/2026 Closing date: 11 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: All shortlisted candidates, including SMS, shall undertake two pre-entry assessments. One must be a practical exercise, and the other must be integrity (ethical conduct) assessment. Selection panel shall score both technical exercises as an additional criterion in the interview process. Note: Equity Target: African Male.

Responsibilities

  • Participate in Operational Plan Development.
  • Reduction of complains by 10% monthly.
  • Effective consultation with all relevant multi-disciplinary teams: daily - identification and report patient’s safety incidents.
  • Conduct clinical audits monthly.
  • Attend or facilitate staff meetings Provide quality comprehensive primary health care by promoting preventative, curative and rehabilitative service for the clients and the community.
  • Provide information and education to communities and households on health and related matters.
  • Increase the number of stable clients decanted to external pick-up points and adhere clubs by 5%.
  • To participate in health promoting awareness as per health calendar.
  • Strengthen relations between CHW school health teams.
  • Manage noncommunicable and communicable diseases by screening assessing, earlydiagnosing, treatment and retention to care.
  • Implement priority programmes (hast, epi, imci, mcht+n, ccmdd, mcd etc) according to standardized guidelines, SOPs and protocols.
  • Prepare and participate in facility information meetings or performance management meetings (huddle, Nerve Centre, information or quarterly performance meeting, etc. Participate in Ideal clinic norms and standards (OHSC) achievement and sustainability.
  • Client prioritization, management and prompt referral of all emergency cases.
  • Conduct staff inservice training on relevant topics.
  • Monitoring of junior stuff.
  • Involvement in research activities.
  • Participate in leave management.
  • Participate in drawing up and implementation of procument plans, maintenance plan and operational plan.
  • Attend facility feedback about budget and expenditure meeting.
  • Verification of stock-taking activities.
  • Prevent fruitless and wasteful expenditure e.g. maintenance of cold-chain, FEFO/FIFO method and unauthorized vehicle trips.
  • Asset management and verification.

Requirements

  • Degree/National Diploma in General Nursing and Midwifery.
  • Only shortlisted candidate will submit the current registration with SANC (2026) as a Professional Nurse and Midwifery.
  • A Post Basic nursing qualification with a duration of at least 1 year in Curative skills in Primary Health Care accredited with SANC.
  • Grade 1: at least 04 years appropriate/recognisable experience in nursing after registration as Professional nurse with SANC in General Nursing.
  • Grade 2: at least 14 years appropriate/recognizable experience in nursing after registration as Professional Nurse with SANC in General Nursing.
  • At least 10 years of the period referred to above must be appropriate/recognisable experience in specific specialty after obtaining the 1year post-basic qualification in the relevant specialty.
  • Certificate of Service endorsed by Human Resource as proof of experience.
  • (Only if shortlisted) Additional information NIMART Certificate Computer literacy.
  • Valid Driver’s license Code B (08) will serve as recommendation only if shortlisted.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Training, and Competencies Required
  • Knowledge of SANC rules and regulations
  • Knowledge of Nursing procedures, relevant Acts and policies
  • Knowledge of Batho Pele principles and Patient’s Right Charter
  • Knowledge of Code of conducts labour relations
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills
  • Decision making and problem solving skills
  • Basic Financial management
