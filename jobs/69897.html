About the role

Our client, a leading international animal nutrition and feed manufacturing company, is looking for a Health, Safety & Risk Officer to join their team based in Middelburg, Eastern Cape. This is an excellent opportunity for a proactive Health & Safety professional with experience in a manufacturing or FMCG environment who is passionate about creating a safe workplace, ensuring legal compliance, and driving a strong safety culture. Minimum Requirements • Matric (Grade 12) • Relevant Health & Safety qualification • Minimum 3 years' experience in a Health & Safety, SHE, SHEQ or HSE role • Manufacturing or FMCG experience is essential (feed milling experience advantageous) • Sound knowledge of the Occupational Health and Safety Act • Valid driver's licence • Proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel and Outlook) Key Responsibilities • Ensure compliance with all Health, Safety and Risk legislation, company policies and procedures. • Promote and maintain a strong safety culture throughout the site. • Conduct Health & Safety inductions for new employees and contractors. • Coordinate Health & Safety training and awareness initiatives. • Investigate incidents and ensure appropriate corrective and preventative actions are implemented. • Conduct risk assessments, workplace inspections and safety audits. • Facilitate monthly Health & Safety meetings and maintain accurate records. • Ensure compliance with PPE requirements across the site. • Monitor housekeeping standards and drive continuous improvement initiatives. • Maintain quality, safety and risk management systems. • Prepare and maintain Health & Safety reports and documentation. • Work closely with production and maintenance teams to improve plant safety and operational compliance. We're Looking For Someone Who • Is highly organised and detail orientated. • Has excellent communication and interpersonal skills. • Is proactive and able to work independently. • Is confident influencing others and promoting safe working practices. • Has strong problem-solving and decision-making skills. • Is committed to maintaining high standards of compliance and continuous improvement. An excellent opportunity exists to join a well-established international organisation offering long-term career growth within a dynamic manufacturing environment. Salary is market-related and will be discussed with shortlisted candidates.
