About the role

The National Prosecuting Authority is hiring a Communications Officer (Multimedia), based in Head Office Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. (Re-advert) Strategy, Operations and Compliance: Chief Directorate Communications How to apply: e mail: Recruit2026361@npa.gov.za Reference number to quote: RECRUIT 2026/361 Closing date: 16 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Producing engaging photo and video content for diverse audiences.
  • Produce and edit high-quality photo and video content for diverse audiences using Adobe Creative Suite and other relevant software.
  • Apply visual storytelling, including basic animation and graphic design, across multimedia platforms.
  • Plan, organise, and manage multimedia projects from concept to delivery.
  • Execute professional photography and videography.
  • Present content effectively and collaborate with stakeholders, demonstrating strong communication and listening skills.
  • Maintain high levels of accuracy and attention to detail in all outputs.

Requirements

  • An appropriate B Degree (NQF 7) or 3 year National Diploma (NQF Level 6) in Multimedia, Digital Media Design, Digital Marketing, Communication, Public Relations or a related field as recognised by SAQA.
  • At least 2 years’ relevant experience in multimedia production/digital content creation.
  • Valid Driver’s Licence.
  • Proven work experience as a Multimedia Specialist, Multimedia Officer, Digital Content Creator or similar role.
  • Demonstrable competency in acting Independently, Professionally, and with Accountability and Credibility.
  • Able to work under pressure and meet tight deadlines daily.
  • Reliable, with interpersonal skills and teamwork.
  • Time management and change management.
  • Good knowledge of government programmes, project management and coordination skills, client-oriented and customer-focused.
  • Communication skills and computer literacy.
  • Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Illustrator), Apple Mac OS, DaVinci Resolve, Canva and Microsoft Office Suite.
  • Strong technical knowledge of multimedia technologies, digital platforms, and production equipment.
  • Experience in videography, photography, video editing, graphic design and digital content production.
  • Ability to operate professional DSLR/SLR cameras, video cameras, audio equipment, lighting equipment and related multimedia production tools.
  • Experience in hybrid events management and live streaming.
  • Experience in capturing, selecting, editing, enhancing and archiving professional images and video content.
  • Knowledge and experience in digital platform management, including website, social media, intranet and Viva Engage.
  • Experience in branding, events management, and content development and production.
  • Willingness to travel and to work flexible hours when required.
  • Willingness to work with heavy equipment including tripods, cabling, lighting and podcasting equipment.
