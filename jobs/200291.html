About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies’ Distribution Centre in Cape Town, Stikland has an opportunity available for a Mezzanine Supervisor to join the team. The main purpose of this role will be to achieve operational excellence within the Dis-Chem Retail Distribution Centre, incorporating planning, procurement, replenishment, inventory,picking, checking, dispatch and warehousing. This includes the daily planning and releasing of waves, maintenance of the order system, emergency orders, replenishment, picking, and Invoicing in some cases, of stock purchased from the DC in order to meet both legal and Store /Customer requirements.
