About the role

Introduction This position will be based at Minopex Head Office in Woodmead, reporting to the Executive Vice President. Description The purpose of the position is to provide comprehensive executive support to the Executive Vice President by managing priorities, coordinating meetings and governance activities, facilitating stakeholder communication, and overseeing executive administration, travel, and confidential information. The role serves as a key liaison between the EVP, Minopex executives, employees, DRA Group stakeholders, clients, and external partners, ensuring efficient coordination of executive activities, proactive management of commitments, and effective follow-through on strategic actions and decisions.
