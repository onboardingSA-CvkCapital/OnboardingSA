About the role

AECI is hiring a SHEQ officer, based in Umbogintwini. To implement and monitor effiective SHEQ management systems, drive continuos improvement towards the SHEQ framework & ensure compliance through audits, inspections and reporting

Responsibilities

  • Implement and maintain SHEQ policies, procedures, standards and systems
  • Conduct regular workplace inspections, safety observations and compliance audits
  • Identify hazards, assess risks and ensure implementation of appropriate control measures
  • Monitor compliance with applicable SHEQ legislation, regulations, standards and company requirements
  • Investigate incidents, accidents, near misses and non-conformances, and track corrective actions to closure
  • Maintain accurate SHEQ records, registers, reports, permits and documentation
  • Coordinate and monitor risk assessments, toolbox talks, safety meetings and SHEQ awareness programmes
  • Monitor environmental compliance, including waste management, pollution prevention and housekeeping
  • Support the implementation and maintenance of ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems
  • Conduct and coordinate internal SHEQ audits and assist with external audits and inspections
  • Monitor contractor and supplier SHEQ compliance
  • Coordinate emergency preparedness, drills and response activities
  • Compile and submit SHEQ reports, statistics, dashboards and performance indicators
  • Track corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) and verify their effectiveness
  • Provide SHEQ training, induction and awareness to employees and contractors
  • Promote a strong safety, environmental, quality and continuous-improvement culture
  • Provide management with SHEQ advice, trends, risks and recommendations
  • Ensure SHEQ objectives and targets are monitored and achieved
  • Escalate significant risks, incidents, legal non-compliances and unsafe conditions promptly
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of SHEQ processes and operational performance
  • Update, draft and implementation of system procedures and work instructions
  • Updating all SHEQ trackers

Requirements

  • Qualifications, knowledge and skills requirements
  • Diploma or Degree in Occupational Health and Safety, Environmental Management, Quality Management or related
  • Minimum 3 years’ experience in a chemical industry plant environment, preferably production related
  • Relevant SHEQ/OHS certifications, such as SAMTRAC, NEBOSH, or equivalent will be advantageous
  • Excellent knowledge of SHE legislation and best practice
  • Excellent investigation capability and report writing and presentation skills
  • Valid First Aid and Incident Investigation training/certification
  • Training in ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems
  • Valid driver’s licence may be required depending on the role
  • Competencies (skills, knowledge and characteristics)
  • Excellent Communication skill
  • Problem Solving
  • Attention to detail
  • Team Player
  • Excellent Work Ethic,teamwork
  • Good Presentation Skills
  • Work Independently
