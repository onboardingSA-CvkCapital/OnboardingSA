About the role

Stefanutti Stocks Inland is seeking a General Foreman to supervise workers, materials and plant across designated areas of a construction project. The role is responsible for coordinating site activities, maintaining quality and safety standards, and ensuring work is completed efficiently according to programme and deadlines.Key Responsibilities: • Supervise and coordinate all site activities relating to bulk earthworks, roadworks, stormwater drainage, pipelines and other civil infrastructure. • Lead and manage foremen, operators, labourers and subcontractors, allocating work, resources, materials and equipment to achieve daily and weekly production targets. • Interpret and accurately implement construction drawings, specifications, setting-out requirements, gridlines, datum lines and benchmarks. • Monitor progress against construction programmes and schedules, ensuring work is completed efficiently and within agreed deadlines. • Coordinate with Site Engineers, Supervisors, Project Managers and subcontractors to align priorities, resolve technical issues and maintain workflow and sequencing. • Oversee plant and equipment utilisation, ensuring correct application, servicing and maintenance. • Conduct regular inspections of workmanship and materials, complete quality assurance checklists, address non-conformances and ensure compliance with project specifications. • Enforce SHEQ requirements and legal standards, promoting a strong safety culture and maintaining good housekeeping across the site. • Maintain accurate records of drawings, personnel, production, operational data, site activities and progress reports, ensuring superseded drawings are removed from circulation. • Monitor and control material usage and wastage to maximise productivity and cost efficiency. • Report daily progress, breakdowns, work stoppages, site constraints and resource requirements to the Site Agent or Project/Construction Manager. • Monitor staff performance, maintain discipline and sound labour relations, and address employee grievances and welfare matters in line with company policies. • Mentor and develop junior staff, promoting skills development, motivation, teamwork and staff retention. • Ensure all employees and subcontractors comply with company policies, procedures, project requirements and applicable legislation.-Qualifications: • Grade 12 • Trade certificate/NQF 5/BEng./Diploma in Civil Engineering or related field Skills: • Strong understanding of earthmoving equipment, construction methodologies, and productivity management • Ability to read and interpret drawings and specifications accurately. • Excellent leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills. • Solid knowledge of construction safety regulations and best practices. Experience: • Minimum 10 years' experience in bulk earthworks and civil construction, including large dams, roads and earthworks • Proven experience in supervisory or foreman roles on large-scale civil or mining infrastructure projects • Valid driver's license, own vehicle fit for the construction environment • Willingness to work away from home for extended periods. Competencies: • Leadership and team coordination • Planning and resource management • Quality and safety awareness • Decision-making under pressure • Strong interpersonal and communication skills • Accountability and results orientation We are committed to Employment Equity when recruiting internally and externally. It is company policy to promote from within wherever possible. Therefore, please be aware that internal candidates will be considered first before reviewing external applicants, provided that this supports the achievement of our Employment Equity goals.
