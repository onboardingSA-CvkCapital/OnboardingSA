About the role

The Department of Health (Limpopo) is hiring 17 Professional Nurse (Specialty) (Various) Grade 1 - 2 (Pn - B1]) (Shifts) posts. This is a permanent post, paying R495,423 a year. How to apply: The circular of advertised vacant posts will be posted on the following websites: https://erecruitment.limpopo.gov.za/, www.ldoh.gov.za, www.dpsa.gov.za and Provincial Department social media. Applicants must apply on the following website: https://erecruitment.limpopo.gov.za/ Closing date: 06 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Candidates applying for the above facilities should have the following specialities: Advanced Midwifery, Child Nursing Science, Operating Theatre, Advanced Psychiatry, Oncology, Trauma, Intensive Care.

Responsibilities

  • Provide direction and supervision for the implementation of the Nursing plan [Clinical practice/quality patient care].
  • Implement standards, practices, criteria and indicators for quality Nursing [quality of practice].
  • Practice nursing & health care in accordance with the laws and regulations relevant to nursing and health care.
  • Maintain a constructive working relationship with nursing and other stakeholders.
  • Utilize human, material and physical resources efficiently and effectively.

Requirements

  • Qualifications and Experience: A National Senior /Grade 12/Matric plus Basic R425 qualification [i.e. diploma/degree] in nursing or equivalent qualification that allows registration with the South African Nursing Council as Professional Nurse.
  • A post basic nursing qualification in one of the above specialty nursing areas with duration of at least 1 year, accredited with the SANC in one of the specialties referred to.
  • Current registration with the South African Nursing Council [SANC] as Professional Nurse.
  • Experience for Grade 1: at least four [4] years appropriate /recognisable nursing experience after registration as Professional Nurse with the South African Nursing Council [SANC] in General Nursing.
  • Experience for Grade 2: at least fourteen [14] years appropriate/recognizable nursing experience after registration as Professional Nurse with the SANC in General Nursing and at least ten [10] years of the period referred to above must be appropriate/recognizable experience after obtaining the one [1] year post-basic qualification in Speciality.
  • Must be able to work shifts [Including night duty and public holidays].
  • Willingness to work extra hours on short notice.
