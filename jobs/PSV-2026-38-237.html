About the role

The Presidency is hiring a Deputy Director: Financial Accounting, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: The Presidency, Private Bag X1000, Pretoria, 0001 or Hand deliver at Government Avenue, Union Buildings, Pretoria or by email: applications@presidency.gov.za For attention: Ms L Mphahlele Reference number to quote: FINACC/PRES/2026 Closing date: 06 November 2026 (16:30 walk ins and 00:00 on email) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: NB: Email applications must on the subject line state Only the Reference number: FINACC/PRES/2026 (with no spaces)

Requirements

  • A Senior Certificate plus an appropriate Bachelor’s degree/or National Diploma OR an equivalent qualification on NQF level 6.
  • At least 3 years' relevant experience in Financial Accounting at a supervisory/managerial level.
  • Good understanding of Tax administration and Debtors Management.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office, especially Advanced Excel.
  • Knowledge of BAS and PERSAL.
  • Candidate must have the following competencies:
  • Communication, both oral and written
  • Client orientation and customer focus
  • Honesty and integrity
  • Service delivery innovation
  • Knowledge Management
  • Financial Management
  • Problem solving and analysis
  • Strategic capability leadership
  • Program and project management
  • People management and empowerment
  • Change Management
  • Public Service Regulatory Framework
  • Policy formulation process within Government
  • Business and management principles involved in strategic planning, resource allocation, human resource modelling, and leadership techniques
  • Analytical thinking, Report writing, and Presentation
  • An understanding of Financial Statements will be an advantage
  • DUTIE:
  • Manage the Subsistence and Travel, Tax Administration, and Debtors Management sub-directorate
  • Develop and maintain policies and processes
  • Monitor the policy and legislative framework to ensure that cognizance is taken of new developments
  • Monitor revenue collected and expenditure incurred and submit reports and plans as required
  • Monitor compliance with financial prescripts
  • Provide advice and guidance to role players on revenue and expenditure procedures
  • Expenditure Management – Ensure that payment for compensation of employees, Subsistence and Travel claims and advances, goods and services, transfers, and subsidies complies with applicable regulations.
  • Accounting – Provide financial administration and accounting services and inputs for the compilation of the Interim and Annual Financial Statements.
  • Tax - Provide advice and guidance to all stakeholders on all tax matters and ensure that the Department complies with all tax prescripts and reporting requirements.
  • Debtors Management – Perform secretarial duties of the Loss Control Committee and ensure compliance with Debtors Management regulations and reporting requirements.
  • Maintain discipline.
  • Manage performance and development.
  • Undertake Human Resource and other related administrative functions.
  • Establish, implement and maintain efficient and effective communication arrangements.
  • Develop and manage the operational plan of the Sub-Directorate and report on progress as required.
  • Develop, implement and maintain processes to ensure proper control of workflow.
  • Compile and submit all required administrative reports.
  • Serve on transverse task teams as required.
  • Procurement and asset management for the Sub- Directorate.
  • Plan and allocate work.
  • Quality control of work delivered by employees.
