About the role

Our client a award-winning South African designer Jewellery  /  Clothing brand based in Franschhoek are seeking a friendly, reliable, and style-conscious Retail Sales Assistant with experience in clothing and/or shoes and/or jewellery to join their team.This role is ideal for someone passionate about fashion, customer service, and retail sales, who enjoys working in a dynamic, customer-focused environment. Responsibilities: Provide exceptional customer service by assisting customers with product selection and styling advice Drive sales by meeting and exceeding individual and store targets Maintain a strong knowledge of products, including materials, sizing, and current trends Process transactions accurately using the point-of-sale (POS) system Assist with visual merchandising and ensure the store is clean, organised, and visually appealing Restock shelves and manage inventory levels as needed Handle customer queries, returns, and exchanges professionally Represent the brand in a positive and professional manner at all times Requirements: Previous retail or sales experience (preferably in fashion or accessories) Strong interpersonal and communication skills A passion for fashion and styling Energetic, proactive, and customer-focused attitude Ability to work flexible hours, including weekends and public holidays Reliable and punctual Own transport Preferred Qualities: Experience working in boutique or premium retail environments Confidence in upselling and building customer relationships Basic understanding of retail operations and merchandising Working Hours: 9am - 5pm, with shifts scheduled based on store needs (week days but can include weekends and peak trading periods). What We Offer: A supportive and stylish working environment Opportunity to grow within the brand Please send cv, qualifications, references, salary expectation and photo to: desmaine@vinerecruitment.co.za
