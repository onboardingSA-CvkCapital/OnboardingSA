About the role

The Department of Water and Sanitation is hiring a Scientist Manager Grade A in Water Resource Management Dir: Water Information Integration Sd: Integrated Water Resource Studies, based in Pretoria Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R1,143,468 a year. How to apply: All applications to be submitted online on the following link: https://erecruitment.dws.gov.za/ Reference number to quote: 271126/05 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The successful candidate will be responsible for, but not limited to, the following: Lead a multidisciplinary team of scientists, plan, coordinate and conduct catchment and systems-based integrated water resource studies (quantity, quality, and ecology) focusing on the impact of land use, water use activities, and climatic pressures on water resources (surface and groundwater).
  • Coordinate and establish processes to provide an annual overview of the status of water resources in the country through the National State of Water Report, Monthly bulletins, and weekly synopses.
  • Lead and coordinate the development of catchment-based hydrological and water quality models.
  • Configure, calibrate, set up, and run simulations for HEC-HMS, ACRU, WRSM2000, WRYM, SWAT+, and WQSAM models.
  • Conduct parameter sensitivity and model uncertainty analysis, including stochastic methods for propagation of modelling uncertainties for decision-making, communication and intelligent knowledge integration.
  • Provide capacity building and mentoring.
  • Analyse water resource data and apply artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to augment catchment modelling efforts.
  • Coordinate the implementation of a programme for the digitalisation of the water resource and water services monitoring systems.
  • Contract management and performance management of professional service providers.
  • Financial planning and management for projects.
  • Development of business case and bid specifications for water resource studies projects.
  • The enhancement and maintenance of a SWAT+ web-based decision support system for the Integrated Vaal River System.
  • Coordinate responses to water-resource-related queries from the media, etc. Liaise with water sector stakeholders, including other Government Departments and the public, on water issues through workshops, joint projects and other facilitation techniques.
  • Identify opportunities for collaborative research, innovation, knowledge generation and technology development.

Requirements

  • MSc degree in Hydrology.
  • A PhD in Hydrology or a relevant natural scientific discipline will serve as an added advantage.
  • 6 years' post-qualification BSc (Hons) Natural Science experience in the field of Hydrology.
  • Compulsory registration with SACNASP as a professional Natural Scientist.
  • The disclosure of a valid, unexpired driver's licence.
  • Proven years of experience in the application of hydrological, systems and water quality models (WRSM2000, ACRU, WRYM, HEC-HMS, SWAT+, WQSAM).
  • Proven years of experience in conducting water resource studies (water quality, quantity and ecology).
  • Knowledge or experience in the water monitoring and assessment value chain (water resource data collection, data auditing and management protocols, analysis, interpretation, knowledge dissemination and information systems).
  • The ability to apply scientific principles to integrate and interpret water quality or quantity data to draw scientifically sound conclusions.
  • Sound knowledge of project management.
  • Knowledge of geographical information systems (GIS) tools (ArcPro and QGIS).
  • Understanding of research and development.
  • Technical report writing.
  • Possesses a high-performance culture.
  • Professional judgement.
  • Presentation skills.
  • Decision-making and analytical skills.
  • Creativity and customer focus.
  • Good communication skills, both verbal and written.
  • Problem-solving and analysis.
  • Applicants must be able to travel nationally and internationally as required to represent the Department and the sector.
