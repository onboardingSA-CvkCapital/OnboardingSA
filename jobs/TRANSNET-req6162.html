About the role

Transnet (TPT Saldanha IOT) is hiring an Engineering Manager, based in Saldanha. The role reports to the Snr Engineering Manager: Maintenance Exe. Grade: E. The Engineering Manager - Reliability, Planning and Engineering Services is accountable for providing technical direction, advise, leadership and management to the Reliability Management, Maintenance Planning and Engineering Services section, through developing, improving and implementing effective and efficient maintenance work management (Planning, Scheduling, Coordinating, Tracking Controlling and Reporting); maintenance performance measurement (KPls); maintenance strategy optimization (Maintenance Plans) and inspections to enhance or improve plant, equipment, machinery and facility Safety, Operability, Maintainability, Availability, and Reliability (SOMAR).

Responsibilities

  • Develop, implement and coordinate the execution strategy and plans for Reliability Management, Maintenance Planning and Engineering Services section to ensure the effective and efficient planning, scheduling, coordination, tracking, inspection and reporting on maintenance, shutdowns and refurbishments at Transnet Port Terminals.
  • Budget compilation, forecasting, cost optimization and control.
  • Develop, review, update, implement, enforce and maintain the maintenance planning systems and processes to support scheduled maintenance (Systems to support weekly, monthly and yearly maintenance plans).
  • Develop, review, update, implement and maintain Key Performance Indicators (metrics).
  • Develop and implement maintenance reporting, data capturing and history recording system or process and ensure compliance to the system.
  • Develop, review, update, monitor and coordinate team members' performance scorecards and development of learning and growth plans, provide ongoing coaching and handle personnel related queries to ensure good performance delivery, retention and talent development.
  • Liaise with other plant support disciplines and production area teams and provide advice and assistance to internal and external customers regarding maintenance planning, scheduling and inspection requests or queries.
  • Develop, review, update, implement and maintain the reliability management and maintenance work management (planning, scheduling, coordinating, tracking, reporting, and inspections) work procedures, standards, norms and specifications.
  • Conduct research, assessment or benchmark within reliability management and maintenance planning industry and implement initiatives to ensure that Transnet
  • Port Terminals keeps up to date with the latest trends.
  • Develop and implement root-cause failure analysis (RFA) methodology and I or system and ensure compliance to the system.
  • Develop maintenance auditing system and process and ensure that maintenance audits (internal and external) are performed.
  • Develop, maintain and implement maintenance plan improvement system and/or process (Reliability Centred Maintenance etc.)
  • Alignment of TPT maintenance and reliability quality specifications to the international best practice and codes to ensure latest technology and standards are incorporated
  • Develop, implement, communicate and maintain Quality System for maintenance, reliability, shutdowns and refurbishments.
  • Compilation, review and management of maintenance contracts.
  • Coordinate and manage compilation, review and approval of legal appointments for maintenance and reliability functions.
  • Develop, implement and maintain systems and processes to ensure statutory and technical assurance compliance (Code of Practice, Occupational Health and Safety act, internal and external audits).
