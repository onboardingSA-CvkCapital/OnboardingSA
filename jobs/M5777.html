About the role

Packhouse Manager 32 Degrees South Fruit (Pty) Ltd, an established packhouse in the Koue Bokkeveld, is seeking an experienced Packhouse Manager to take responsibility for the effective management of the entire packhouse and CA storage facility. Responsibilities will include, but not be limited to: *Managing the daily packing, sorting, handling and dispatch of fruit in accordance with production, quality and market requirements *Managing refrigeration, cold rooms and CA storage to ensure an optimal cold chain and product quality *Controlling fruit, packaging material and packhouse supplies inventory, including accurate stock records and product rotation *Leading and managing packhouse and warehouse staff, including workforce planning, productivity, training and discipline *Ensuring compliance with quality, food safety, hygiene and safety standards (BRCGS & HACCP) *Monitoring productivity, losses, costs and operational efficiency, and reporting to the Board of Directors. Essential qualifications, skills, and experience: • Minimum of 5 years’ relevant management experience in a fruit packhouse, CA storage facility or similar environment; • Strong people, inventory and operational management skills, with knowledge of all applicable legislation; • Knowledge of optical sorting; • Knowledge of fruit handling, packing and cold-chain management (including CA storage); • Ability to work under pressure and manage longer/irregular working hours during the season.
