About the role

Standard Bank is hiring a Trade Support Specialist, Vanilla, based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. Middle Office/Trade support for the Africa Trading Desk, with expanded responsibility for Money Markets and Interest Rate Derivative (IRD). Supporting Global Markets Clients and our Sales and Trading business, in managing and facilitating the operational trade lifecycle (including allocating, validating and enriching trades, identifying and resolving exceptions and queries and implementing continuous improvement measures) to support trades originating on Global Markets Desks ensuring that trades can be effectively settled, comply with regulatory standards, mitigate operational risk and create a positive client experience. The successful candidate will be responsible for providing operational support to the Africa Trading Desk, including coverage on South African public holidays when required.
