Responsibilities

  • Co-ordinate the project initiation stage.
  • Open the project file.
  • Provide input during the development of the procurement strategy.
  • Provide input in the development of the Terms of Reference (T.O.R).
  • Prepare Professional Service Provider’s (PSP) payment certificate for the feasibility report.
  • Co-ordinate the project planning and design stage.
  • Organise meetings with the appointed PSP and district/local municipalities to communicate and agree on the requirements.
  • Prepare the submission payment certificate for Supply Chain Management (SCM) returnable schedules.
  • Provide input during the development of Service Level Agreement (SLA).
  • Organise meetings with the professional services.
  • Provide input during the first draft of planning and design deliverables.
  • Prepare payment certificate to the PSP for Geotech, Environmental Impact Assessment, house plans and design reports.
  • Submit project enrolment file to the Project Management Unit.
  • Provide input during the development of the Procurement Strategy for contractors required.
  • Provide inputs during the development of (TOR) and present to the Bid Specification Committee for approval.
  • Organise meetings with the appointed contractors and district/local municipalities to communicate and agree on the requirements.
  • Provide inputs during the development of the SLA.
  • Liaise with PSP’s for the site handover.
  • Coordinate the project implementation stage.
  • Facilitate the construction of services.
  • Facilitate the construction of housing units.
  • Facilitate the rectification of housing units.
  • Draft invitation letters for the introduction of stakeholders to municipalities.
  • Liaise with the contractor in preparation for handing over of documents.
  • Issue the copies of building drawings, milestone breakdown, PIPs and specifications to the Control Works Inspector.
  • Liaise with Social Facilitation and Beneficiary Administration units for the establishment of Project Steering Committees and appointment of the Community Liaison Officer.
  • Conduct progress and technical meetings.
  • Conduct site visits to monitor progress on site.
  • Verify value created on site, validate and process project claims.
  • Make follow-ups with District support services on the payment claims.
  • Submit copies of the payment stubs and payment reconciliation to the PSP and file.
  • Prepare and submit quarterly contractor performance reports.
  • Analyse and validate expenditure against the value created.
  • Provide input during the preparation of variation orders.
  • Prepare motivation for the issuing of non-performance notices.
  • Prepare and present reports to various stakeholders.
  • Provide support to the various stakeholders during project visits and verifications.
  • Coordinate project Close-Out stage.
  • Facilitate the submission of the close out report.
  • Validate required documents in the project file.
  • Source any outstanding required documents in the project file.
  • Submit the project file to CCPM for project close out.
  • File the approved project close out resolutions.

Requirements

  • National Senior Certificate, National Higher Diploma (Built environment field) with at least 4 years’ and six months certified experience or a BTech (Built environment field) with at least 4 years’ certified managerial experience or Honours Degree in any Built Environment with at least 3 years’ experience.
  • Compulsory registration with the SACPCMP as a Professional Construction Project Manager on appointment.
  • Knowledge of Human Settlements policies and legislative frameworks.
  • Built environment standards, CIDP regulations, financial and contract management, stakeholder and community engagement.
  • Good communication skills, project management skills, presentation skills, report writing skills, negotiation, facilitation, analytical and critical thinking, conflict management.
  • Ability to work in a team.
  • Computer literate.
  • A valid driver’s licence.
