About the role

Role Overview The Google Cloud Senior Cloud Engineer is responsible for designing, implementing, and managing scalable, secure, and high-performing cloud infrastructure solutions on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). The role works closely with development, operations, and security teams to ensure the reliability, scalability, and optimisation of cloud-based applications and services. The successful candidate will provide technical leadership, automation expertise, and operational support while driving best practices across cloud engineering and infrastructure management. Key Responsibilities • Design and implement scalable, reliable, and secure cloud architectures on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Manage and optimise cloud infrastructure to ensure high availability, performance, and operational efficiency. • Develop automation scripts and tooling to streamline cloud operations and infrastructure management. • Implement and maintain infrastructure-as-code solutions using Terraform. • Apply cloud security best practices and ensure compliance with relevant industry standards and regulations. • Configure and maintain monitoring, alerting, and performance optimisation practices across cloud environments. • Collaborate with development, operations, and security teams to integrate cloud solutions into broader system architectures. • Provide advanced troubleshooting and support for cloud infrastructure and operational incidents. • Support CI/CD pipeline implementation and optimisation, including GitHub Actions workflows. • Mentor junior engineers and contribute to technical knowledge sharing within the team. • Drive continuous improvement initiatives across cloud operations, automation, and performance optimisation. Essential Skills & Experience • Minimum 3 years’ experience in cloud engineering or cloud infrastructure roles. • At least 2 years’ hands-on experience working with Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Strong experience with GCP services including Compute Engine, Kubernetes Engine (GKE), Cloud Storage, BigQuery, Cloud Functions, and Pub/Sub. • Proficiency with infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform. • Strong scripting and automation experience using Python, Bash, or similar languages. • Experience implementing and supporting CI/CD pipelines, preferably using GitHub Actions. • Strong understanding of cloud networking, architecture, and security principles. • Experience applying cloud security best practices and compliance requirements. • Excellent troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving abilities. • Strong collaboration and communication skills within cross-functional teams. • Experience mentoring or guiding junior engineers. Desirable Skills & Experience • Experience working within multi-cloud environments. • Exposure to big data technologies and tooling within GCP. • Experience with cloud performance tuning and optimisation. • Exposure to Kubernetes administration and container orchestration best practices. • Experience supporting enterprise-scale cloud environments. Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field. • Advanced degree would be advantageous. • GCP Professional Cloud Architect certification or similar cloud certifications are highly desirable. Behavioural Competencies • Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities. • Excellent collaboration and stakeholder engagement skills. • Ability to work effectively in fast-paced and evolving technical environments. • Strong leadership and mentoring abilities. • Adaptability and willingness to learn emerging technologies. • Proactive mindset focused on operational excellence and continuous improvement. What We Offer • Competitive salary and benefits package. • Opportunity to work on enterprise-scale cloud transformation projects. • Exposure to modern cloud technologies and engineering practices. • Collaborative and innovative engineering environment. • Professional growth, learning, and certification opportunities. • Opportunity to contribute to impactful and meaningful technology solutions.
