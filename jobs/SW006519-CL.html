About the role

ACCOUNTANTCape Town Northern Suburbs: Our client, an established and award-winning dealership within the motor industry, is seeking a thorough, organised and hands-on Accountant to join their team. The successful candidate will take responsibility for the day-to-day accounting and bookkeeping function through to trial balance, while providing accurate financial and administrative support to the business. This is a hands-on role suited to an experienced accounting professional who takes ownership, works accurately and is comfortable working independently in a fast-paced environment. Xero experience is essential. Key Responsibilities • Managing the full bookkeeping and accounting function through to trial balance • Processing and reconciling debtors, creditors and bank accounts • Managing petty cash and processing journals • Preparing and processing customer invoices • Assisting with payroll processing and related administration • Preparing PAYE, UIF and SDL summaries • Liaising with suppliers and assisting with account-related queries • Processing financial transactions accurately and timeously • Performing regular reconciliations and ensuring financial records are accurate and up to date • Maintaining well-organised financial documentation and records • Assisting with general financial and administrative duties as required • Supporting the business with accurate financial information and reportingMinimum Requirements • Accounting Degree / Diploma from a recognised institution • Minimum 3 years’ experience in a similar accounting position • Proven experience working on Xero Accounting Software is essential • Good knowledge and understanding of accounting principles and standards • Sound knowledge of bookkeeping and financial processes • Experience with payroll and statutory submissions advantageous • Excellent communication skills in both English and Afrikaans • Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel • Valid driver's licence • Thorough, highly organised and detail-oriented • Strong numerical and analytical ability • Hard-working, motivated and reliable • High level of initiative, enthusiasm and drive • Ability and willingness to work longer hours when requiredThe ideal candidate will be a hands-on and dependable accounting professional who takes pride in accuracy, enjoys taking ownership of their work and can manage a broad accounting function with minimal supervision. They will have the initiative and drive to identify and resolve issues, meet deadlines and provide reliable financial support to the business.
