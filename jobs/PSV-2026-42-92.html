About the role

The National School of Government is hiring a Deputy Director: Curriculum Development, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Apply Using This Link: APPLICATION FOR EMPLOYMENT DD: CURRICULUM DEVELOPMENT, REF NO: NSG: 22/2026. - Fill out form Reference number to quote: NSG 22/2026 Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • The incumbent will be responsible for the following Key Results Areas: Coordinate the conceptualisation, design, development, review, and updating of curriculum and learning programmes aligned to public sector capacity development needs.
  • Develop and maintain curriculum development plans, project schedules, concept notes, business cases, and related implementation documentation.
  • Conduct research, policy analysis, and benchmarking to inform the relevance, quality, and continuous improvement of curricula and learning programmes.
  • Integrate innovative, African-centered, transformational, and participatory approaches into curriculum design and learning content.
  • Coordinate the development and integration of face-to-face, blended, online, and digital learning solutions, including relevant instructional design methodologies and technologies.
  • Ensure curriculum, learning materials, and digital content are learner-centered, accessible, outcomes-based, and aligned with applicable quality and instructional design standards.
  • Coordinate curriculum quality assurance, programme evaluation, and moderation processes to ensure the quality and credibility of learning programmes.
  • Establish and maintain effective partnerships with internal business units, government departments, higher education institutions, and relevant external stakeholders.
  • Facilitate stakeholder consultations, reference group engagements, and project meetings to strengthen curriculum development and implementation.
  • Develop procurement specifications, terms of reference, and related documentation for the appointment of curriculum development service providers.
  • Coordinate the sourcing, appointment, and performance management of curriculum development service providers in accordance with quality and contractual requirements.
  • Facilitate governance and management processes by presenting curriculum proposals, quality findings, stakeholder inputs, and improvement recommendations for consideration and approval.
  • Coordinate the piloting, testing, and implementation of curriculum programmes to ensure their readiness, relevance, and fitness for purpose.
  • Coordinate the implementation of online, blended, and technology-enabled learning programmes in collaboration with relevant digital learning and operational teams.
  • Monitor curriculum development projects, implementation milestones, service delivery targets, budgets, and timelines to ensure effective delivery programme.
  • Compile and present periodic and ad hoc reports on curriculum development activities, programme performance, implementation progress, and operational matters.
  • Maintain accurate curriculum records, programme databases, and support documentation in accordance with governance, information management, and audit requirements.
  • Monitor learner engagement, programme outcomes, and implementation feedback to identify operational improvements and support the continuous enhancement of learning programmes.
  • Establish and maintain collaborative relationships with internal business units and external stakeholders to support Curriculum Development.
  • Facilitate integrated planning and collaboration across business units to develop and implement curriculum development tools and frameworks.
  • Participate in and support strategic partnerships with higher education institutions and other organisations to implement joint initiatives.
  • Represent the organisation in relevant internal and external forums to strengthen collaboration and promote best practices.
  • Identify and leverage partnership opportunities to enhance programme quality, innovation, and institutional impact.
  • Manage the resources (people, finance, systems, assets) allocated within the directorate.

Requirements

  • A recognised Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent (NQF level 7) in Education, Training and Development, Instructional Design, Educational Technology, Curriculum Design and Development, Digital Learning, or a related field.
  • Registration with a relevant professional body.
  • Experience
  • At least three (3) to 5 years’ of experience in curriculum development, instructional design, eLearning development, capacity development, education and training, digital learning, or a related field, of which 3 years must be at the supervisory or equivalent level
  • A certificate in any online, blended, and technology-enabled learning programmes will be an added advantage
  • Knowledge
  • Knowledge of and experience in instructional methodologies and facilitation for online, virtual, and face-to-face modes of delivery
  • Knowledge of the public sector landscape and capacity building needs
  • Knowledge of instructional design principles, adult learning theories, curriculum architecture, and digital pedagogy
  • Knowledge of eLearning authoring tools, multimedia learning development, Learning Management Systems (LMS), and virtual learning technologies.
  • Knowledge of online and virtual facilitation methodologies, learner engagement strategies, assessment design, and digital accessibility standards.
  • Knowledge and understanding of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa and public sector legislation.
  • Knowledge of professional bodies and regulatory body requirement.
  • Excellent organising and planning skills, computer literacy in Microsoft Office Suite, and digital skills. eLearning content development and digital learning design skills.
  • Learning technology integration and virtual facilitation skills.
  • Multimedia scripting, storyboard development, and online assessment design skills.
  • Data analysis and learning analytics interpretation skills.
  • Preference will be given to African Females, Youth, African Males and Coloured Males and people with disability in accordance with our employment equity requirements.
