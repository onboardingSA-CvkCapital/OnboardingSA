About the role

The Office of the Chief Justice is hiring an Assistant Director: Facilities Management, based in Limpopo Provincial Service Centre Polokwane. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 to R573,897 a year. How to apply: Applications can be sent via email to 2026/182/OCJ@judiciary.org.za Reference number to quote: 2026/182/OCJ Closing date: 04 December 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: The Organisation will give preference to candidates in line with the Employment Equity goals.

Responsibilities

  • Provide effective facilities and auxiliary services management processes and systems at service centres, implement policies and procedures related to facilities and auxiliary services management at service centres, facilitate and coordinate facilities management services for the Service Centres, assist in the planning and implementation of facilities maintenance and repair projects at Service Centres, ensure provision of general maintenance, cleaning and parking services, manage and maintain the switchboard and reception services, prepare and submit budgets in consultation with Court Manager and Line Function for facilities maintenance and repair projects, monitor implementation of facilities contracts and services, ensure enforcement on compliance with the use of Service Level Agreements (SLA’s) in place for facilities Services, monitor performance of all contracted facilities management service providers within the Service Centre and Courts, engage stakeholders within the facilities environment on contracted services delivered at Service Centres, ensure that monthly meetings with contracted facilities service providers, stakeholders are held, and minutes filed, compile technical reports on facilities maintenance and repairs that require attention or urgent work to be executed, ensure provision of an efficient maintenance of PSC and Court facilities, formulate and implement preventative maintenance plans to support the objectives of the PSC and Courts, conduct regular facilities inspections and identify areas for improvement, maintenance and servicing, implement and monitor repairs and maintenance projects, develop schedules for routine maintenance and repairs for building systems e.g. electrical, plumbing, etc, coordinate with key stakeholders, external contractors and vendors for specialised repairs and works, monitor compliance to services rendered under facilities contracts, routine inspections conducted with DPWI FM Manager of facilities and owners of leased properties, monitor the provision of infrastructure services, conduct ergonomic assessments of workstations and office equipment within OCJ, ensure that facilities planning aligns with OCJ accommodation needs, norms and goals, ensure quality assurance of infrastructure services against the set standard and legislation, ensure that OCJ comply with prescribed building regulations, ensure compliance with health and safety standards and regulations, respond to emergencies and ensure timely resolution of facilities related issues, supervise and develop staff, ensure general supervision and development of employees and contractors, allocate duties and perform quality control on the work delivered by officials, manage leave and general personnel administration of staff, advise and lead supervisees with regard to all aspects of the work, manage performance, conduct and discipline of employees, ensure that all employees are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively.
  • Develop, implement and monitor work systems and processes to ensure efficient and effective functioning, address enquiries and provide advice and guidance on asset allocation and control.
