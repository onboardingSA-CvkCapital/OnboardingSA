About the role

The Department of Local Government (Western Cape) is hiring an Assistant Director: District and Local Performance Monitoring, based in Department of Local Government, Western Cape Government. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 – R573,897 a year. How to apply: Only applications submitted online will be accepted. To apply submit your application online only: via http://www.westerncape.gov.za/jobs or https://westerncapegov.erecruit.co Reference number to quote: LG 32/2026 Closing date: 19 October 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the draft annual Municipal Performance report in terms of section 47 of the Municipal Systems Act, Collect, validate and evaluate municipal data & information relating to municipal compliance reports, Develop and submit recommendations for municipal support, capacity-building initiatives, and appropriate interventions, Analyse trends in municipal compliance through municipal monitoring and execute follow up research arising from municipal trend reports, Managerial functions.
