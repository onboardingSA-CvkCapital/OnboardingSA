Description: Starke Ayres is the foremost African specialist and global supplier of premium vegetable, flower and lawn seed varieties – both for commercial and home garden planting. The following position has become available for a Research Site Manager, based in Greytown. RESPONSIBILITIES: • Directs and coordinates subordinates’ activities, payroll, and recordkeeping. • Controls the supervision of farm labour (temporary and outsourced) and administration pertaining to contracts, payroll data, including attendance registers, overtime schedules and leave schedules. • Forwards payroll information through to the payroll department timeously • Recruit temporary staff in line with labour needs and ensure all company regulations with regards to staff recruitment and contracts are adhered to. • Supervise day-to-day activities, organise rosters and schedule tasks/activities that need to be undertaken by the farm team. • Execute breeding activities in tunnels, net houses and open field trials • Monitor plant health (wilting/heat stress, nutritional abnormalities, chemical burn, physiological abnormalities, physical damage) • Supervise accurate and timeous data collection (plant, flower, fruit, seed, yield data) • Provide regular plant health reports including photographs • Ensure that subordinates maintain a high level of productivity • Responsible for obtaining quotes, ordering and purchasing of chemicals, coal, fuel, fertilizer, safety equipment, propagation material, packing material, pollination material, stationery and kitchen equipment in line with requirements and budgets. • Order supplies / equipment once approved by the Research Operations Manager. • Forwards all invoices and purchase documents to the Office Administrator in Kaalfontein for capturing. • Ensure compliance with legislation regarding health and safety on the premises. • Maintain a high level of awareness and vigilance for workplace safety, amongst all staff through implementation & monitoring of Health & Safety policies and procedures. • Where needed assist the field supervisors with field preparation, fertilization, irrigation and weed and pest control. • Assist with identifying needs for new equipment / implements and forwards to the Research Operation Manager for inclusion in the annual budget. • Attends to general and emergency maintenance requirements as and when required which includes, but is not limited to, buildings, equipment, pumps, generators, boilers, irrigation, pipes, tunnel / multi-span structures and pump house. Requirements: REQUIREMENTS: Qualification: • Grade 12 (Essential) • Agricultural Diploma or similar (Preferential) Experience: • 3+ year experience in farm management Knowledge: • Computer literate • Tunnel Growing
