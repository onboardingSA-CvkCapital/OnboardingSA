About the role

The Department of Co-Operative Governance, Human Settlements & Traditional Affairs (Mpumalanga) is hiring a Persal System Controller (Salary) in Financial Management, based in Head Office (Mbombela). This is a permanent post, paying R605,742 a year. How to apply: Applications must only be submitted online through the Provincial e- Recruitment Portal at: https://erecruitment.mpg.gov.za. Applicants are required to register and create a profile on the system before submitting their applications. Reference number to quote: 2026/COGHSTA/35 Closing date: 27 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage PERSAL in respective components of the Department, Ensure compliance with PERSAL Policy and Procedure.
  • Conduct information sharing sessions with PERSAL Users, Co-ordinate steering committee meetings, Monitor the interactions between Users and Supervisors.
  • Ensure that the linking between the users and the supervisors is done in line with the policy.
  • Give support to users and supervisors.
  • Ensure training of PERSAL of users.
  • Keep database of all PERSAL account for the effective utilization of the PERSAL System.
  • Ensure compliance with the PERSAL Access Security.
  • Communicate all PERSAL Massages.
  • Draw User Report and Analise it.
  • Attend to National Treasury Steering Committee Meetings.
  • Monitor and ensure that the department manuals are properly complied and updated.
  • Compile Departmental Manuals and keep them updated.
  • Consult and communicate approved Manuals with Stakeholders.

Requirements

  • A National Senior Certificate (Grade 12) and a recognised National Diploma at NQF level 06 in Human Resource/Public Administration/Public Management.
  • At least 3 years relevant experience.
  • The following certificates will be regarded as additional to the qualification: Introduction to PERSAL, Personnel Administration, Salary Administration, and PERSAL Controllers’ Course.
  • Knowledge of PERSAL system, PERSAL and knowledge of the System Change Control (SCC).
  • Computer literacy and communication skills, Financial Management and Knowledge Management, Problem Solving.
  • Microsoft Excel.
  • A valid driver’s license.
  • Skills and Competencies
  • Ability to manage and develop staff
  • Excellent computer literacy skills in (Microsoft Office (especially), Ms Word, Excel, etc.)
  • Extensive knowledge of EPS, LOGIS and BAS with sound knowledge of applicable policies (PFM, Treasury Instructions and HRM)
  • Good written and communication skills
  • Proven ability to work independently in a high-pressure environment while complying to due dates
  • Risk assessment and Management, Problem solving skills
