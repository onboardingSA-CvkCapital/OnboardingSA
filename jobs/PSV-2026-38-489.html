Responsibilities

  • Provide safe, timely and high-quality diagnostic ultrasound services in accordance with clinical indications, approved protocols and patient-centred care standards.
  • Perform and accurately interpret ultrasound examinations within the scope of practice, and issue clear, complete and timely diagnostic reports.
  • Promote patient dignity, privacy, informed consent, health education and continuity of care throughout the examination process.
  • Perform ultrasound procedures competently, applying infection prevention and control, patient identification and clinical risk-management measures.
  • Undertake patient reception, appointment coordination, registration and related clerical duties as required.
  • Maintain accurate clinical records, examination registers, service statistics and routine operational reports.
  • Support the effective day-to-day delivery of ultrasound services, including workload prioritisation, referral pathways and efficient use of available resources.
  • Uphold Batho Pele principles, professional ethics and respectful communication with patients, families and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Inspect, operate and care for ultrasound equipment safety, reports faults promptly and support equipment maintenance and service continuity.
  • Provide guidance, supervision, mentorship and practical training to junior staff and students within the scope of professional practice.
  • Participate in quality assurance, clinical audits, patient-safety initiatives and continuous quality-improvement activities.
  • Comply with applicable professional standards, occupational health and safety requirements, the Ideal Hospital Framework and Office of Health Standards Compliance requirements.

Requirements

  • Senior Certificate/Grade 12 plus an appropriate qualification that allows for the required registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) in Ultrasound Radiography (2026).
  • Certificate of Registration with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) as an Ultrasound Radiographer (Independent Practitioner).
  • Persons in possession of foreign qualification must attached an evaluation certificate from the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA).
  • Current registration confirmation/Annual practice with HPCSA as an Ultrasound Radiographer.
  • Experience Grade 1: Nil experience after registration with HPCSA in respect of RSA qualified who have performed community services, and 1 year appropriate/recognizable experience in respect of foreign qualified not required to perform community service.
  • Grade 2: at least 10 years’ appropriate experience after registration with the HPCSA as an Ultrasound Radiographer.
  • Grade 3: at least 20 years’ appropriate experience after registration with the HPCSA as an Ultrasound Radiographer.
  • Knowledge, Skills, Training And Competencies:
  • Sound knowledge of obstetrics & gynaecology, general, cardiac and vascular ultrasound musculoskeletal ultrasound scanning techniques, protocols and procedures
  • Knowledge of radiographic procedures, equipment and accessories associated with relevant regulations
  • Sound knowledge of Radiation control regulations, safety measure and policies
  • Knowledge of occupational Health and Safety and other relevant Acts, policies and regulations, namely, infection control measures, etc. Knowledge of basic Quality assurance procedures in Diagnostic Radiography.
  • Ability to communicate effectively and problem solving skills.
  • Sound report writing and administrative skills.
  • Computer literacy.
  • Good interpersonal relations and ability to perform well within a team.
  • Ability to work autonomously.
