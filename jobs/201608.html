About the role

Join Entyce, part of AVI Limited, and work behind iconic brands including Five Roses, Freshpak, Trinco, Frisco, Koffiehuis, Hug in a Mug, Ellis Brown and House of Coffees. Based at our Bryanston Head Office, you’ll work alongside commercial, corporate and specialist teams helping shape our brands, support our factories and keep the business moving. With opportunities to build expertise, work across functions and take ownership of meaningful work, Entyce offers more than a job it’s a place to build a lasting career. An exciting position has become available for a Revenue Growth Manager.  The successful candidate will be based in Bryanston and will report to the Finance and Revenue Executive. The Revenue Growth Manager will be responsible for driving revenue growth, pricing and margin management, and financial planning and reporting. The role combines commercial revenue management with core finance support, partnering closely with Sales, Marketing, Finance and Supply Chain to deliver profitable, sustainable top-line growth.
