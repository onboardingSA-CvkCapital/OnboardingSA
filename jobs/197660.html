About the role

homechoice is a leading South African homeware retailer. For over 40 years we've helped our customers create beautiful homes they love with an innovative range of quality products they can afford. The Delivery Driver is responsible for providing safe, reliable and customer-focused delivery services by operating a Code 10 / Code C1 vehicle to transport goods accurately, efficiently and on time. The role ensures that deliveries and collections are executed safely, legally and cost-effectively while maintaining excellent service standards and representing the homechoice brand professionally in the field.
