About the role

Our client is looking for an experienced Coupa & SAP Procurement Consultant to conduct a four-week assessment of their procurement and invoice processing environment. The consultant will review recurring incidents across Coupa, SAP, ESB and HR systems, identify root causes and determine whether issues can be resolved through existing platform functionality, configuration changes, process improvements or custom development. Key Responsibilities • Assess end-to-end procurement and invoice processing processes across Coupa and SAP. • Review Coupa, SAP, ESB and HR system integrations and interfaces. • Analyse historical production incidents and identify recurring issues and root causes. • Review areas including master data, duplicate invoices, VAT/tax validation, asset validation, procurement validation and supplier governance. • Assess existing Coupa configuration, including validation rules, invoice matching, supplier controls and user interface controls. • Review relevant SAP configuration, including tax determination, account determination, validation/substitution rules and master data controls. • Identify integration gaps, failures and process bottlenecks. • Conduct stakeholder interviews and workshops to understand business pain points and operational challenges. • Recommend configuration and process improvements before considering custom development. • Identify automation and user experience improvement opportunities. • Develop a remediation plan outlining recommended solutions and implementation complexity. • Produce clear documentation and actionable recommendations for stakeholders. Requirements • 5+ years' experience in Coupa, SAP Procurement or related procurement technology environments. • Strong hands-on experience with Coupa procurement and invoicing functionality. • Strong understanding of SAP procurement / P2P processes. • Experience analysing system incidents and performing root-cause analysis. • Experience reviewing system integrations and interfaces, preferably involving ESB. • Strong understanding of procurement, invoice processing, master data and supplier governance. • Experience assessing whether business issues can be resolved through configuration versus custom development. • Strong stakeholder engagement and workshop facilitation skills. • Excellent analytical, problem-solving and documentation skills. • Experience within a banking or large enterprise environment would be advantageous.
