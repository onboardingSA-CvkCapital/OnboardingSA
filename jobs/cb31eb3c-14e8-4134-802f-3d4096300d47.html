About the role

Centurion Junior Graphic Designer Are you a talented, motivated, and creative visual designer with an eye for detail? A leading supplier of automotive parts in South Africa and neighbouring countries with national branches is looking for a dynamic Graphic Designer to join their head office marketing and creative team on a full-time, permanent basis. If you have a genuine passion for visual storytelling, excel at turning creative concepts into high-performing digital and print assets, and want to build a long-term career with an industry leader, this is your opportunity. What’s on Offer? - Salary: R22,000.00 – R29,000.00 CTC PM (negotiable depending on experience and proven capabilities) - Status: Full-Time, Permanent (Skilled Grade) - Benefits: Provident fund and annual bonus - Hours: Monday to Friday, standard commercial office hours (flexibility required for major retail campaign rollouts and promotional deadlines) - Growth: Excellent mentorship, career progression, and stability within an established, national retail and distribution network Key Responsibilities - Brand Integrity: Create high-quality designs that align strictly with corporate brand guidelines and visual standards across all channels. - Print Production: Design and prepare print-ready materials (DTP), including trade brochures, promotional flyers, event posters, point-of-sale displays, and product packaging. - Digital & Web Assets: Develop and produce engaging digital content, including website banners, UI elements, e-commerce promotional graphics, email newsletters, and social media assets. - Product Photography & Retouching: Create, deep-etch, colour-correct, and edit product imagery for both online catalogues and offline print materials. - Asset Management: Systematically organize and maintain the central digital asset library, brand logos, templates, and raw project files. - Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with internal marketing, product, and e-commerce teams to ensure campaign briefs are completed on time and aligned with sales objectives. - Trend Awareness: Stay up to date with contemporary design trends, digital tools, and production techniques to continuously elevate the brand’s creative output. Are You the Perfect Fit? Minimum Requirements: - Education: Grade 12 / Matric PLUS a completed Degree or National Diploma in Graphic Design, Visual Communication, or Multimedia Design. - Software Proficiency: Practical proficiency in Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and MS Office. - Design Fundamentals: Strong technical foundation in typography, visual composition, colour theory, layout design, and pre-press production processes. - Experience & Portfolio: Proven commercial or junior design experience backed by an active portfolio of evidence (PDF or web link) showcasing print and digital execution. - Core Attributes: Exceptional attention to detail, ability to work well under deadline pressure, strong teamwork and communication skills, and an eager desire to learn and grow. Beneficial Skills & Experience: - Prior design experience within the automotive, wholesale, retail, or e-commerce sectors. - Familiarity with motion graphics, basic video editing (Premiere Pro / After Effects), or web CMS platforms. Strict Legal Requirements & Documentation (Non-Negotiable): Background screening will be conducted. Candidates must provide: - Valid South African ID (Proof of South African citizenship). - Valid Driver’s License. - Clear criminal record. - 3 months of recent consecutive payslips / salary advice. How to Apply Ready to drive your design career forward? Ensure you have the following documentation ready: - Detailed CV (clearly outlining your design experience and software proficiencies). - Portfolio of Evidence (active web link or PDF attachment showing recent work). - ID Copy & Driver's License. - Matric Certificate & Tertiary Design Qualifications. - 3 Months Recent Payslips.
