About the role

DEBTORS & CREDITORS CLERKElsies River, Cape TownR25 000 – R35 000 CTC per monthPermanent | Full-Time | Office-Based Monday to Friday | 08:00 – 17:00An established business based in Elsies River is looking for an experienced and highly accurate Debtors & Creditors Clerk to join its finance team.This is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented finance professional who is confident managing both debtors and creditors, has strong Sage and Excel skills, and is comfortable working in a busy, deadline-driven environment. Key Responsibilities • Manage the full debtors and creditors function. • Process customer invoices, supplier invoices, credit notes and payments. • Perform debtor and creditor account reconciliations. • Follow up on outstanding customer accounts and manage collections. • Prepare supplier reconciliations and ensure payments are processed accurately and on time. • Capture financial transactions accurately on Sage. • Allocate receipts and payments to the correct accounts. • Investigate and resolve customer and supplier account queries. • Maintain accurate financial records and filing systems. • Assist with month-end procedures and financial reporting. • Provide general administrative support to the finance team when required. Minimum Requirements • Matric / Grade 12 is essential. • Minimum 3 years' experience in a Debtors & Creditors Clerk, Accounts Clerk, Bookkeeper or similar finance position. • Proven hands-on experience using Sage is essential. • Strong understanding of debtors, creditors, reconciliations and general bookkeeping principles. • Strong Microsoft Excel skills. • Proficient in Microsoft Office. • Excellent numerical ability and attention to detail. • High level of accuracy when processing financial information. • Strong communication and organisational skills. • Ability to work independently and meet deadlines. • Relevant bookkeeping, accounting or finance qualification would be advantageous. • Reliable transport is essential due to the location and working hours. Personal Attributes The successful candidate will be: • Honest, trustworthy and dependable. • Highly organised and detail-oriented. • Professional in their approach. • Comfortable working under pressure. • A strong problem solver. • Able to prioritise effectively. • A positive and cooperative team player.
