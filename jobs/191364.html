About the role

🚀 We’re Hiring: CRM QA & Testing Manager Deliver the quality assurance that keeps Africa's most loved Merchant Operating Platform reliable, secure and ready for every release. 🏢 Business Unit: Technology 👤 Reports to: Head of CRM Why This Role Matters Every merchant onboarding journey, customer interaction and Salesforce enhancement depends on quality. As CRM QA & Testing Manager, you will ensure every CRM solution is thoroughly tested, every release is production-ready and every deployment strengthens confidence in our technology. You will lead CRM quality assurance across the Salesforce ecosystem, establishing testing standards, driving automation and ensuring every release meets the highest standards of reliability, performance and security. From test strategy and defect management to user acceptance testing and governance, you will build a culture where quality is embedded into every stage of delivery. About Lesaka Lesaka is a fintech company on a mission to digitise commerce in Africa. We are listed on NASDAQ (LSAK) and the JSE (LSK) and serve merchants, consumers and enterprises across the continent. From spaza shops and taxi ranks to restaurants and city traders, our products power the small exchanges and the big steps of African commerce. One platform. Many products. One ambition: to be the most loved Merchant Operating Platform in Africa. How We Operate Our strategy starts from a simple operating truth. Exceptional customer experiences come from exceptional staff experiences. We balance four pillars across everything we do: • Customer Experience • Staff Happiness • Cost Discipline • Risk Control Each one amplifies the others when properly aligned. We have organised around one merchant journey with clear execution and accountability. Our North Star Deliver exceptional experiences to our customers and our staff. We measure ourselves against CHAP (Customer Happiness), SHAP (Staff Happiness), Cost-to-Serve Efficiency and Regulatory Impact. Happy customers. Happy staff. Disciplined cost. Controlled risk. The Role You will lead the CRM Quality Assurance and Testing function, ensuring every Salesforce enhancement, integration and release is validated through structured testing, robust governance and continuous quality improvement. Working closely with CRM Development, Product, Architecture, Sales and Operations teams, you will define testing strategies, oversee release quality and build automated testing capabilities that improve delivery confidence and reduce production risk. The role contributes directly to Customer Experience through reliable CRM releases, Cost Discipline through testing automation and quality improvements, and Risk Control through disciplined testing, governance and defect prevention. You will lead a team of three quality assurance specialists responsible for protecting the quality and stability of the CRM platform.
