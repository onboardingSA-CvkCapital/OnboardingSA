About the role

About the Role Regal Distributors is seeking an accomplished, commercially minded and hands-on Senior Marketing Manager to lead the Group’s marketing and communications function. This is a senior management appointment with responsibility for the strategic direction, leadership and day-to-day performance of the marketing department. The successful candidate will act as custodian of the Regal brand and will be accountable for marketing activity across the organisation , including branches, key accounts, projects, corporate branding, product brands, digital channels, e-commerce and retail execution. The role operates in a fast-paced, high-volume B2B environment and requires a marketing leader who can combine strategic thinking with strong execution, sound commercial judgement and proven people-management capability. The successful candidate must be comfortable working closely with executive management, sales teams, Product Managers, Product Specialists, external agencies, suppliers and other key stakeholders. Key Responsibilities Marketing Strategy & Brand Management • Act as custodian of the Regal brand and maintain consistent brand standards across the organisation. • Develop, implement and monitor the annual marketing strategy and advertising plan for Regal and the brands within its portfolio. • Develop measurable marketing initiatives aligned with business objectives. • Establish marketing goals based on historical performance, market forecasts and emerging trends. • Develop strategies to attract new customers, retain existing customers and strengthen Regal’s market position. • Identify and develop appropriate co-branding and partner marketing opportunities. • Work closely with senior executives to ensure effective marketing across branches, key accounts, projects and corporate channels. Budget Management & Commercial Performance • Develop and manage the annual marketing budget. • Ensure marketing expenditure is appropriately controlled and delivers value to the business. • Track the effectiveness and return on investment of marketing campaigns and initiatives. • Prepare regular marketing reports, analysis and presentations for executive management. • Adjust marketing plans and campaigns in response to performance data, market conditions and business priorities. Market Intelligence & Campaign Management • Research and analyse market trends, customer demographics, competitor activity, pricing and relevant industry information. • Use market intelligence to develop effective marketing strategies and campaigns. • Develop targeted promotional campaigns to support sales and address ageing or strategic stock. • Work with OEMs and brand partners to secure promotional funding and support where appropriate. • Oversee campaign development from concept through to execution and measurement. Digital Marketing & Communications • Develop and lead digital marketing initiatives across relevant platforms. • Oversee email marketing, social media, website content, industry publications, third-party blogs and other digital channels. • Provide strategic direction for content creation, creative development and campaign execution. • Oversee public relations and sponsorship activities that support brand awareness and reputation. • Maintain effective customer database segmentation to support targeted and relevant communications. Website & E-Commerce • Work with Product Managers and Product Specialists to develop accurate and compelling product and solutions content. • Oversee product photography and presentation standards. • Work closely with the E-Commerce Manager and web developers to improve website functionality, layout and user experience. • Ensure website content remains relevant, accurate and commercially effective. • Drive ongoing SEO optimisation to improve organic search visibility and rankings. • Support the continued development and effectiveness of Regal’s digital and e-commerce platforms. Design Studio & Creative Management • Lead and oversee the marketing and design function. • Manage departmental briefs, workflow, priorities and deadlines. • Ensure creative projects are delivered accurately, professionally and within agreed timelines. • Provide creative direction across print, digital, retail and corporate marketing material. • Oversee the quality and production of promotional material, advertisements, campaigns and other marketing collateral. • Maintain strong brand consistency across all creative output. Branch & Retail Marketing • Oversee marketing support for Regal’s branch network. • Manage the development and execution of in-store marketing material, including point-of-sale advertising, signage, posters and localised campaigns. • Ensure consistent presentation of the Regal brand across branch and retail environments. • Support branch-specific and regional marketing requirements where appropriate. Product Launches & Events • Plan and execute new product launches in conjunction with Product Managers and Product Specialists. • Develop marketing initiatives for new products, services and solutions. • Organise and oversee customer functions, launches, promotional events and other marketing activities as required. Leadership & People Management This role carries direct responsibility for the leadership and performance of the marketing team. The successful candidate will be expected to: • Provide clear leadership, direction and accountability to the marketing department. • Manage, motivate and develop team members. • Set objectives, allocate responsibilities and manage performance. • Prioritise departmental workloads and ensure effective delivery against deadlines. • Mentor and develop employees while maintaining high professional and performance standards. • Build a collaborative, accountable and results-focused team environment. • Take ownership of departmental performance and ensure commitments are delivered. Proven direct people-management experience is essential. Experience limited to managing agencies, suppliers or projects without direct responsibility for employees will not, on its own, meet the leadership requirements of this position. Personal Attributes The successful candidate should be: • A confident and credible senior leader. • A hands-on, proactive self-starter. • Commercially aware and results driven. • Strategic while remaining execution focused. • Highly organised and able to prioritise effectively. • Accurate, deadline driven and attentive to detail. • Creative and innovative while maintaining strong commercial discipline. • Comfortable making decisions and taking accountability. • Able to lead, motivate, challenge and develop a team. • Professional and able to maintain appropriate confidentiality and integrity. • Comfortable operating under pressure in a fast-paced environment. Working Environment This is an on-site position based at Regal’s Head Office in Greenstone, Edenvale. The role requires close collaboration with executive management and multiple functions across the organisation. Limited travel may be required from time to time. The Opportunity This position offers an experienced marketing leader the opportunity to take ownership of a broad and commercially important marketing function within an established organisation. We are looking for someone who can set the strategy, lead the team and ensure the work gets delivered — a senior marketing professional who understands that successful marketing requires creativity, commercial awareness, strong leadership and disciplined execution. If you have a proven track record of leading people, managing a broad marketing function and delivering measurable results in a demanding business environment, we would like to hear from you. Apply to join Regal Distributors as our Senior Marketing Manager.
