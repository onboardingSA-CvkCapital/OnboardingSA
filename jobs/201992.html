About the role

BU ADMINISTRATOR PROGRAMMES -TEMP Fixed term assignment – to end 31 December 2026 (Reporting to HEAD OF PROGRAMMES) JOB PURPOSE To provide administrative support and assistance to the Head of Programmes and staff within the Programmes Unit, to enable the achievement of the deliverables for the Unit. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Travel Management • Co-ordinate travel bookings for the Programmes Unit in terms of Soul City Institute’s procurement policy. • Manage the relationship with the travel agent. • Ensure cost efficiency and value for money with respect to bookings. Diary and Telephone Management • Diary management – schedule and co-ordinate internal and external meetings and workshops. • Telephone management – receive and screen calls, take comprehensive messages and relay messages timeously to appropriate people. Document Management and Filing • Screen and deal with (action/reply/redirect where appropriate) incoming correspondence, enquiries and requests. • Control incoming and outgoing mail. • In consultation with the Head of Programmes, disseminate information and/or documents on behalf of the Unit. • Maintain accurate and up to date filing records for the Programmes Unit. • Handle confidential and sensitive documentation. • Assist with the co-ordination, receipt, checking and submission of invoices to the finance unit in order to facilitate timeous payment. • Organise photocopying, printing, binding and deliveries by messengers and couriers. General Administration • Liaise with internal and external stakeholders. • Organise logistical (venue, catering, audio visual equipment, stationery, travel) and other requirements (distribution of agenda, minutes, reading material) for workshops and meetings. • In consultation with the Head of Programmes, draft correspondence on behalf of the Unit. • Prepare and type correspondence, minutes, reports and other documents as required. • Prepare PowerPoint presentations. • Order and issue stationery for the Programmes Unit. • Prepare and submit leave records for the Programmes Unit to the HR unit. • Develop and maintain an accurate stakeholder contact database. • Co-ordinate unit meetings. • Relieve at reception, when necessary. • Provide administrative assistance and support to other Business Unit Administrators, when necessary.Human Resources Management • Take ownership and accountability for all deliverables and responsibilities assigned to the position of Business Unit Administrator within the Programmes • In consultation with the Head of Programmes, identify own training and coaching requirements to achieve optimal performance. • Contribute to the development of a committed Programmes team with individuals who work well together and provide each other with mutual support, assistance and cooperation. • Comply with all HR policies and procedures.QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE • A three-year Diploma, Degree or equivalent qualification in Administration/Secretarial competence. • Minimum three years’ experience in senior Administration or Secretarial competence.COMPETENCIES • Advanced proficiency in MS Office - including word, excel, PowerPoint and Outlook. • Excellent English verbal and written communication skills. • Excellent planning and organisation skills. • Meticulous and able to pay attention to detail. • Excellent interpersonal skills. • Able to multi-task and prioritise. • Good work ethic and positive solution focused attitude. • Self-motivated, a self-starter and able to work with limited supervision. • Able to work under pressure and meet deadlines. • Able to use own initiative and take ownership of his/her tasks. • Able to do research on internet. • Discrete and able to deal with sensitive information in a confidential and professional manner. • Good team player. • Honesty and integrity.
