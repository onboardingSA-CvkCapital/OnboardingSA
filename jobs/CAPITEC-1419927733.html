About the role

Capitec is hiring a Senior Copywriter, based in Stellenbosch. To create impactful, audience-centric copy and concepts that strengthen the brand, drive engagement, and support business objectives across all customer touchpoints. The role requires a versatile writer capable of translating complex briefs into compelling creative solutions across traditional and digital platforms. We are looking for a highly strategic and experienced Specialist: Copywriter to join our creative team. This role is suited to an intermediate to senior-level copywriter with a strong advertising background and proven experience developing compelling campaigns across multiple channels. The successful candidate will be equally comfortable crafting a campaign concept for television and radio as they are writing digital content, social media copy, customer communications, and integrated marketing campaigns. This role requires someone who can think beyond execution and contribute strategically to brand storytelling, campaign development, and creative problem-solving.

Responsibilities

  • Strategic Contribution
  • Participate in campaign ideation and creative concept development.
  • Interpret and respond to complex marketing briefs.
  • Contribute strategic thinking during campaign planning and messaging development.
  • Collaborate with designers, marketers, product teams, agencies, and stakeholders to deliver integrated solutions.
  • Quality & Brand Governance
  • Ensure all copy meets brand, legal, compliance, and quality standards.
  • Maintain consistency of voice and messaging across channels.
  • Adapt messaging for different audience segments and communication objectives.
  • Collaboration
  • Partner closely with designers and creative teams throughout campaign development.
  • Present and motivate creative recommendations to stakeholders.
  • Provide constructive feedback during creative reviews.
