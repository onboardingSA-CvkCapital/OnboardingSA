About the role

Based in East London, seeking a competent and experienced Group Human Resources Manager to manage our Group Shared Services HR function and provide professional HR support to management across the Group. Key Tasks and Responsibilities: Strategic HR Business Partnering: Partner with executive management and business leaders to identify people-related risks, workforce challenges and organisational development opportunities. Provide practical HR advice, recommendations and support aligned with the Group’s strategic objectives. Build the trust of management. HR Governance, Administration and Reporting: Maintaining high standards of HR reporting and administration (e.g. employee record-keeping and document management). Conducting periodic audits of HR files, policies, processes and records to ensure compliance, accuracy and consistency across the Group. Recruitment, Selection and Termination: Support managers in identifying staffing requirements, preparing job specifications, coordinating recruitment processes, screening applications, participating in interviews and facilitating appropriate appointment and termination processes. Training and Development: Support managers in identifying skills gaps, training requirements and employee development opportunities. Coordinate approved training initiatives, administer company-funded studies and help ensure learning and development activities align with business needs and succession planning objectives. Performance Management: Develop and maintain performance management frameworks, templates and processes. Support managers in setting performance expectations, conducting reviews, providing constructive feedback and managing performance concerns, including performance improvement processes where required. Employee Relations: Advise and support managers in the management of employee relations matters, including grievances, disciplinary processes, misconduct investigations, incapacity processes, disputes and workplace complaints. Compensation and Benefits: Assist management in maintaining appropriate remuneration structures and employee benefit arrangements. Policy, Governance and Risk: Identify people-related and HR-related risks and assist in developing appropriate mitigation measures. Draft, review and maintain HR policies, procedures and governance frameworks. Monitor changes in employment legislation and communicate relevant developments to management. Conflict Resolution: Facilitate constructive workplace resolution processes and assist managers in managing interpersonal conflict and workplace challenges appropriately and fairly. Succession Planning and Talent Management: Work with management and leadership teams to identify key-role dependencies, succession risks, future leadership requirements and talent development opportunities across the Group. Employee Engagement and Organisational Culture: Develop employee engagement initiatives, analyse workforce feedback and provide recommendations aimed at strengthening culture, retention and organisational effectiveness. Legal and Regulatory Compliance: Promote compliance with labour legislation and employment-related regulatory requirements. Assist with regulatory inspections, reviews and audits and help ensure that management remains aware of applicable obligations. Reporting and Administration: Provide meaningful reporting to executive management and subsidiary leadership teams. Coordinate HR information and documentation required for internal and external audits, budgeting and governance purposes.
