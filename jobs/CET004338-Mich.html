About the role

We are seeking a skilled and reliable Millwright to join a dynamic manufacturing environment. This role is ideal for a hands-on individual with strong electrical and mechanical experience who thrives in a fast-paced 24/7 production setting. REQUIREMENTS Desired Experience & Qualifications: • Red Seal Millwright Trade Qualification • Minimum of 3–4 years' post-qualification experience as a Millwright • Strong electrical and mechanical maintenance and fault-finding expertise • Proven experience working in a milling environment (essential) • Excellent verbal and written communication skills • Experience within a rendering plant or animal feed manufacturing environment will be highly advantageousNB:  Proven experience in operating, maintaining, and troubleshooting the following machinery in a PLC/SCADA-controlled environment: • Boilers • Cookers • Conveyors & Conveyor Systems • Pumps & Motors Maintenance: • Maintain and service all company equipment, including machinery and tools • Ensure all plant and factory equipment is kept in good working condition • Respond to and resolve breakdowns safely and efficiently • Report all faults, deviations, and breakdowns to the supervisor during shifts • Perform lockout procedures when working on plant equipment • Maintain the cleanliness of all machinery and work areas • Complete defect tracker logs during or after shifts • Conduct weekly plant equipment maintenance inspections • Attend weekly production and safety meetings • Perform both electrical and mechanical maintenance tasks • Repair and maintain pumps (mono, centrifugal, and submersible) • Remove foreign materials from pumps and screw systems • Respond to maintenance callouts as required • Use diagnostic tools such as mustimeters effectively • Replace three-phase motors and bearings on machinery • Conduct fault-finding on electrical and mechanical systems • Clean steam boiler tubes • Ensure generators are serviced and filled with diesel daily • Ensure all electrical panels are properly secured • Inspect pumps for leaks and address issues promptly • Ensure all moving equipment is fitted with appropriate safety guards • Ensure all screw lids are properly secured and bolted Compliance & Safety: • Adhere to all company policies and procedures • Always follow lockout and safety procedures • Ensure all safety guards are correctly installed and maintained • Promote and maintain a safe working environment for all employees • Report any unsafe practices or conditions to management immediately IMPORTANT NOTES: • Candidates must be willing to relocate to the plant area in Mpumalanga • Applicants residing in Johannesburg or other metropolitan areas who intend to commute will not be considered • The role requires shift work (07:00 – 19:00 on a rotational basis), making daily commuting impractical • A guaranteed 13th cheque is included • Performance-based bonuses are awarded based on individual performance and achievement of KPIs.
