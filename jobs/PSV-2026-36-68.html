About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring an Assistant Director: Acquisition Management (SCM), based in Cape Town Regional Office. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: for the position of Assistant Director: Acquisition Management (SCM) must be emailed to: RecruitCPT26-69@dpw.gov.za Reference number to quote: 2026/353 Closing date: 23 October 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the development of the Procurement Plan.
  • Facilitate the approval of Procurement Plan.
  • Communicate the Procurement Plan.
  • Monitor that goods and services is rendered according to the Procurement Plan.
  • Monitor the procurement of goods and services in line with the Procurement Plan.
  • Advice on the performance of procurement in line with the plan.
  • Manage the acquisition of goods and services.
  • Provide and implement supply chain management policy, strategies and processes.
  • Manage the acquisition of goods and service as per the transversal contract and participation contract.
  • Provide and consolidate requests for invitation of quotation.
  • Verify and advice on specifications for goods and services.
  • Analyse the received quotation.
  • Provide approval of received goods as per the specification.
  • Serve in Bid Committees as a member.
  • Monitor the implementation of guidelines for appointment of bid committees.
  • Facilitate the appointment of Bid committee members.
  • Manage logistical arrangements for Bid committee meetings and provide advisory support to Bid Committees.
  • Provide secretariat function to the PBAC.
  • Facilitate and monitor evaluation of bids and quotations.
  • Develop and define processes for bids evaluation.
  • Monitor adherence to advertised criteria in the evaluation process.
  • Facilitate and verify captured information for all bids and quotations.
  • Prepare an evaluation report- submission.
  • Facilitate the clearance of the successful bidders.
  • Customise and implement any changes on standard evaluation criteria.
  • Implement and Oversee Risk and performance management strategies in the Supply Chain Management Unit.
  • Identify and mitigate risks within the Unit.
  • Manage all administrative matters, correspondence and complex enquiries are attended to promptly.
  • Assist in the compiling of monthly SCM performance reports and verification of all monthly SCM reports.
  • Ensure implementation and compliance of the Instruction Notes as issued by National Treasury as well as Departmental SCM Policy.
  • Manage full compliance to all Demand Management, Acquisition Management and Contract Management prescripts, policies, practice notes and delegations of authority.
  • Develop and maintain internal control measures for procurement.
  • Ensure the effective management of Demand, Acquisition and Contract processes.
  • Manage an appropriate response to internal and external auditors, other organs of state and Audit Intervention Plans.
  • Monitor and liaise with CIDB about professionals’/contractor’s information.
  • Evaluate and monitor performance and appraisal of employees.
  • Ensure capacity and development of staff.
  • Enhance and maintain employee motivation and cultivate a culture of performance management.
  • Manage discipline.
  • Provide job description to subordinates.
  • Manage division leave matters.

Requirements

  • At least three-year tertiary qualification (NQF Level 06) in Supply Chain Management/Procurement/or in the relevant field.
  • Relevant years of experience in Supply Chain Management environment with appropriate working experience at supervisory Level.
  • A valid driver’s license.
  • Computer skills in Microsoft Office Applications.
  • Extensive knowledge and working experience in Public Sector procurement process, rules and regulations: PFMA, PPPFA, BBBEE, CIDB, Treasury Regulations Relevant and Supply Chain Management prescripts.
  • Skills:
  • Good verbal and written communication skills
  • Basic numeracy
  • Advanced interpersonal and diplomacy skills
  • Problem solving skills
  • Decision making skills
  • Statistical skills
  • Computer literacy
  • Presentation skills
  • Planning and organising skills
  • Ability to work independently and under pressure
  • Personal Attributes:
  • Analytical thinking
  • Innovative
  • Creative
  • Resourceful
  • Ability to work under stressful situations
  • Ability to communicate at all levels
  • People orientated
  • Able to establish and maintain networks
  • Trustworthy
  • Assertive
  • Hard-working
  • Selfmotivated
  • Ability to work independently
  • Must be prepared to adapt work schedule in accordance with office requirements
