About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies’ in Stikland, Cape Town has an opportunity available for a DC Inbound Junior Retail Assistant Manager to join the team. The main purpose of this role will be to achieve operational excellence within the Dis-Chem Distribution Centre, incorporating inbound management predominantly. You will also be required to demonstrate an understanding of receiving, warehousing, picking/ packing, replenishment, manifesting/ marshalling and dispatch, in order to meet legal and operational requirements. Essential: • Grade 12 / Matric • Relevant Degree/Diploma in Logistics or Supply Chain Management • Minimum 2 Years relevant experience in Distribution, Logistics or Supply Chain EnvironmentAdvantageous: • Relevant experience in the field of Logistics/Supply Chain Management • Experience in the Retail/Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Experience with SAP, EWM or other ERP systems
