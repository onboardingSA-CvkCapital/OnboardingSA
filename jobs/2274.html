Duties and responsibilities: Provide psychosocial assessment and support services to students by: •    Conducting psychosocial assessments of students experiencing personal, academic, emotional or social challenges. •    Providing short-term counselling and psychosocial interventions within the scope of professional social work practice. •    Developing intervention plans aligned to individual student needs. •    Attending to psychosocial emergencies after hours as needed and where possible. •    Providing trauma-informed support where appropriate. •    Provide input on policies and procedures that assist in management of students. •    To support student wellbeing, resilience and academic success. Provide crisis intervention services by: •    Responding to students experiencing mental health crises, trauma, bereavement, abuse, gender-based violence, financial or social distress. •    Assessing immediate risks. •    Supporting students who experience setbacks in academic performance. •    Coordinating emergency support and referrals where required. •    Ensuring timely intervention and student safety during crises. Manage student support cases by: •    Managing cases from initial assessment through resolution. •    Maintaining confidential case records. •    Monitoring student progress. •    Reviewing intervention outcomes and follow-up actions. •    Ensuring coordinated and effective psychosocial support services. •    Support other academic staff as it pertains to students in crisis the MBChB and LOP programmes. Coordinate referral services by; •    Identifying students requiring specialised intervention. •    Referring students to appropriate internal and external support services. •    Coordinating multidisciplinary support where required. •    Monitoring referral outcomes where appropriate. •    Ensuring students receive appropriate specialist support. Develop and facilitate student wellness programmes by: •    Planning and facilitating wellness initiatives addressing mental wellbeing, resilience, stress management, self-care and burnout prevention. •    Conducting awareness ·campaigns relevant to health sciences students. •    Promoting preventative wellbeing and student resilience. Collaborate with academic and support stakeholders by: •    Liaising with academic staff, programme directors, clinical training platforms and faculty management regarding students requiring support. •    Participating in multidisciplinary student support meetings. •    Providing professional recommendations on student support interventions. •    Building relationships with internal and external support service providers. •    Promoting integrated student support and coordinated intervention Ensure professional and legislative compliance by: •    Delivering services in accordance with SACSSP ethical standards, University policies and relevant legislation. •    Supporting compliance with HPCSA accreditation requirements relating to student support. •    Maintaining professional registration and Continuing Professional Development (CPD). •    Ensuring ethical, professional and compliant service delivery. Maintain student support records and management reporting by: •    Maintaining confidential student records. •    Compiling statistical and management reports on student interventions, referrals, wellness programmes and case outcomes. •    Safeguarding confidential information in accordance with POPIA requirements. •    Supporting institutional reporting, service improvement and governance. Inherent requirements: •    Bachelor of Social Work or equivalent qualification in the field of Social Work on NQF Level 8. •    A minimum of two (2) years' relevant experience as a registered Social Worker. •    A minimum of two (2) years' relevant experience in working with young adults or students. •    A minimum of two (2) years' relevant experience in counselling, crisis intervention and case management. •    Active registration with the South African Council for Social Service Professions (SACSSP) as a social worker (attach proof). Recommendations: •    Experience within a higher education institution or healthcare environment. •    Experience supporting students in professional or clinical training programmes Required competencies: •    Results Orientated: -    The ability to be reliable, responsible, dependable and to fulfil obligations. -    The ability to deal calmly and effectively with high stress situations. •    Strategic Thinking: -    The ability to deal with several activities at a time. -    The ability to be creative and open-minded when addressing work issues. •    Business Acumen: -    The ability to adhere to rules and strictly follow work regulations. -    Proficient in using MS Office. •    Leading: -    The ability to maintain high levels of personal motivation, energy and enthusiasm. -    The ability to be cooperative with others, display a good-natured attitude and encourage people to work together. •    Building Coalitions: -    The ability to be sensitive and understanding to the needs and feelings of others. -    The ability to interact with others and establish personal connections with people.
