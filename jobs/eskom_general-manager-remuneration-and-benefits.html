About the role

Position: General Manager Remuneration and Benefits Position/Task Grade: EEE-BAND Department: Group Human Capital Business Unit: Human Capital Area of specialization: Reporting to the Chief People Officer Human Capital the General Manager Remuneration and Benefits will provide enterprise-wide leadership for the formulation, implementation and governance of Eskom’s total reward strategy in support of the company’s overall mission, vision and strategic objectives. The role is accountable for ensuring that remuneration, employee benefits and recognition practices are market-related, internally equitable, financially sustainable and compliant with legislation and good governance, while ensuring that reward governance frameworks, policies and practices are effectively implemented and managed and fostering a positive and inclusive work culture. The incumbent will act as a trusted advisor to executive management, the Board and its sub-committees, engage organised labour and other key stakeholders, and use market benchmarking, workforce analytics and digital enablement to shape reward policies, practices and initiatives that drive employee engagement, productivity and long-term organisational performance.
