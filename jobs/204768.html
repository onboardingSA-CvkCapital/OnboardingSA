About the role

Job Title: HR Administrator Position reports to: HR Supervisor Based at: Atlantis Industrial- Western Seaboard or Blaauwberg regions, Western Cape Salary: Market- Related Industry: Import/ Export of Frozen Seafood Contract Type Permanent with a 6-month probation period. Working Hours Mondays – Fridays: 08h00 – 17h30 Hours may fluctuate due to operational requirements. Weekends/public holidays will be required. The successful candidate must be flexible. Job Purpose To support the HR department in maintaining a compliant, skilled and efficient workforce. The role provides day-to-day HR administration support and assists the Junior Payroll Officer with high-accuracy processing during intensive weekly pay periods. Qualifications/Skills • Grade 12 with Mathematics as a subject. • Tertiary qualification in HR Management/Industrial Psychology/Payroll and/or relevant field will be advantageous. • Solid proficiency in MS Office 365 (Intermediate-to-Advanced Excel is essential, alongside Word, Outlook, and collaborative tools, e.g. Teams, and OneDrive). • Proficiency in Sage 300 People and/or an HR Information System will be advantageous. • Proficiency in TracTech and/or a timekeeping system will be advantageous. Experience Needed • At least 2–3 years’ work experience in a similar role within an FMCG/manufacturing environment. • Prior exposure to the protein industry (e.g., meat, fish, dairy, etc.) will be advantageous. • Deep understanding of the Basic Conditions of Employment Act and Labour Relations Act. Requirements • Excellent written and verbal communication skills. • Strong planning and multitasking abilities to meet deadlines. • Exceptional attention to detail, numerical accuracy, and administrative quality control. • Strong data management, analytical, and reporting capability. • Ability to work cross-functionally and manage multiple priorities. • Ability to exercise sound judgment in decision making. • Open to learning new software systems and accepting short-notice changes with a positive, solutions-oriented attitude. • Proven ability to work independently under tight operational deadlines and weekly payroll pressures. • Good time management. • Comfortable working in a production/manufacturing environment and engaging effectively with employees across all levels, including the shop floor. • Applies immediate urgency to tasks—escalating faults quickly and driving workflows to rapid completion. • Possess a valid driver’s license and own reliable transport. Key Responsibilities • Collaborate with the Junior Payroll Officer to assist with preparing time and attendance data for weekly pay runs. • Assist in calculating and auditing weekly payable hours, variations, and deductions for approximately 600 employees spread across 4 distinct payrolls. • Maintain data accuracy within the TracTech timekeeping system, register new employees and capture biometric data. • Systematically manage and update staff payroll diary notes to track dynamic, weekly payroll changes. • Upload documentation and maintain digital records in Sage 300 People (HRIS). • Assist with auditing, processing and tracking leave applications in ESS to ensure leave balances are precisely maintained. • Assist with completing pension and provident fund applications, adjustments, and withdrawals. • Oversee the archiving, physical filing, and legal document retention lifecycle for all active and historical employee files. • Schedule, arrange, and log mandatory occupational medical examinations, including eye/vision tests and audiometry evaluations, for factory and office staff. • Attend to daily staff queries and execute standard administrative tasks to ensure smooth department operations. • Coordinate recruitment administration, including candidate screening, scheduling psychometric/skills assessments, and conducting employee inductions. • Assess attendance and performance data to determine disciplinary warnings, update employee disciplinary data on the various platforms and compile comprehensive, legally compliant files for disciplinary hearings. • Provide administrative assistance to the whole HR team.
