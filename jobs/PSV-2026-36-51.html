About the role

The Department of Land Reform and Rural Development is hiring a Deputy Director: Financial Management in Administration Support, based in Gauteng (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R932,292 a year. How to apply: Applications can be submitted by hand delivery during office hours to: 600 Lilian Ngoyi Street (formerly known as Van Der Walt Street) Berea Park, Pretoria, 0001 Or by email to Post277@Dlrrd.Gov.Za Reference number to quote: 3/1/1/1/2026/277 Closing date: 23 October 2026 at 16:00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE Targets: African, Coloured, Indian and White Males, and Coloured, Indian and White Females, and Persons with Disabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Provide effective financial management support.
  • Coordinate the preparation and implementation of the annual budget and operational plans.
  • Monitor budget performance and expenditure trends against approved allocations.
  • Ensure effective allocation and utilisation of financial resources.
  • Provide financial advice and support to management to facilitate informed decisionmaking.
  • Monitor compliance with financial policies, procedures, and applicable legislation.
  • Provide effective financial planning, monitoring and reporting.
  • Monitor budget implementation progress and identify all variances against approved budget.
  • Analyse financial performance and recommend corrective actions where necessary.
  • Prepare and manage monthly, quarterly, and annual financial management reports.
  • Ensure the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of financial reports.
  • Analyse financial performance and provide recommendations for improvement.
  • Coordinate the preparation of annual financial statements and audit processes.
  • Monitor the implementation of audit recommendations and action plans.
  • Coordinate year-end financial statement preparation and audit processes.
  • Provide effective financial management and administration.
  • Implement and maintain appropriate financial management systems and controls.
  • Provide support for the preparation of information on the status of financial resources as required.
  • Monitor project expenditure against approved budgets and funding allocations.
  • Support project managers in financial planning, forecasting, and expenditure monitoring.
  • Ensure compliance with project funding requirements and reporting obligations.
  • Render supply chain and other physical resources management services.
  • Provide financial oversight and support for procurement processes.
  • Manage the procurement of goods and services for the Directorate and Branch in accordance with applicable legislation and policies.
  • Ensure effective use of physical resources and equipment.
  • Monitor compliance with supply chain management policies and procedures.
  • Monitor asset management practices and ensure proper recording, safeguarding, and accounting of assets.
  • Ensure effective utilisation and control of physical resources and equipment.
  • Support inventory management and asset verification processes.
  • Render payments and expenditure management services.
  • Oversee the processing, verification, and authorisation of payments in accordance with delegated authority and financial policies.
  • Ensure suppliers are paid within prescribed timeframes.
  • Review payment reconciliations and resolve outstanding payment-related issues.
  • Monitor expenditure trends and implement measures to prevent unauthorised, irregular, fruitless, and wasteful expenditure.
  • Ensure all payments are supported by complete and accurate documentation.
  • Monitor and report on expenditure commitments and accruals.
  • Ensure compliance with payment processes and financial control measures.
