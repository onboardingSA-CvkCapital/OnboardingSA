About the role

Job Description Executive Assistant to the Enterprise CEO Reports to CoS (Office/Operations) — supporting Enterprise CEO Location Cape Town office Work pattern 5 days per week, in-office Hours Core hours apply (standard office hours with flexibility around start/end times, per company policy), with availability required around CEO's schedule, including occasional early/late support for events Purpose of the role The Executive Assistant provides comprehensive support to the Enterprise CEO, ensuring his diary, communications, travel, and stakeholder engagements run smoothly.
