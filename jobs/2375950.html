About the role

About IHC South Africa IHC South Africa is part of the Royal IHC Group, with the head office situated in The Netherlands. Royal IHC is a leading supplier of maritime technology and expert craftsmanship. Driven by innovation, our dedicated experts provide a competitive edge to our worldwide customers in the dredging, offshore, mining and defence industries. We deliver reliable, integrated solutions that improve operational efficiency and create a platform for more sustainable performance. As we navigate new waters, our aim remains unchanged: to discover the smartest and safest way forward for both our customers and our people. Together, we create the maritime future. About the Role We are looking for an experienced Tactical (Technical) Purchaser I to join our Procurement team. The successful candidate will be responsible for the tactical sourcing and procurement of fabricated components, cast components, mechanical / electrical / hydraulic equipment, project-related items / assemblies, spare parts, services and raw materials to support IHC South Africa's operational and project requirements, local or international. This role requires a commercially minded and technically aware procurement professional capable of managing sourcing activities, supplier relationships, procurement planning, commercial negotiations and supplier performance while ensuring materials and services are delivered on time, within budget and in accordance with technical and quality requirements. Key Responsibilities Tactical Sourcing & Procurement • Execute sourcing activities in line with approved procurement and category strategies. • Manage RFQs, supplier evaluations, bid comparisons and supplier selection processes. • Source and procure raw materials, fabricated components, mechanical equipment, consumables, services and spare parts. • Identify alternative suppliers and sourcing opportunities to improve competitiveness and reduce supply chain risk. • Support project procurement activities through proactive planning and supplier engagement. • Review technical specifications, Bills of Materials (BoMs), engineering drawings and project documentation to determine procurement requirements. Procurement Planning & Project Support • Develop procurement plans aligned with project schedules and operational requirements. • Identify long-lead items and critical supply chain risks and implement mitigation measures. • Monitor purchase order status, supplier confirmations and delivery schedules. • Coordinate procurement activities to support project milestones and production requirements. • Escalate supplier performance concerns, delivery delays and commercial risks proactively. Supplier Management & Performance • Build, develop and maintain strong relationships with local and international suppliers. • Monitor supplier performance against delivery, quality, cost and service KPIs. • Conduct supplier evaluations and support supplier development initiatives. • Support supplier onboarding, qualification and approved supplier management processes. • Resolve supplier-related quality, delivery and commercial issues. Commercial Negotiation & Cost Control • Negotiate pricing, delivery terms, payment terms and contractual conditions within delegated authority. • Conduct market research and supplier benchmarking to ensure competitive sourcing decisions. • Identify and implement cost-saving and value-engineering opportunities. • Support procurement budgeting and cost control activities. • Perform total cost of ownership (TCO) evaluations where applicable. • Assist with spend forecasting and procurement reporting. ERP, Reporting & Administration • Manage procurement transactions within the ERP system (IFS preferred). • Maintain accurate supplier, pricing, contract and procurement data. • Generate procurement reports and provide regular stakeholder updates. • Ensure procurement documentation is maintained in accordance with company procedures. • Maintain accurate records of RFQs, quotations, purchase orders, supplier correspondence and procurement activities. Cross-Functional Collaboration • Work closely with Engineering, Projects, Workshop, Logistics, Quality Control, Finance and Operations teams. • Participate in project planning meetings and provide procurement input and market intelligence. • Collaborate with Engineering to clarify technical requirements and supplier capabilities. • Coordinate with Logistics and Warehousing to ensure successful material deliveries. • Support procurement process improvement and continuous improvement initiatives.
