About the role

The Structural Revit Draughtsman is responsible for producing accurate and detailed structural drawings and Building Information Modelling (BIM) models for commercial, residential, industrial, and infrastructure projects. The role requires advanced proficiency in Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD, with a strong understanding of structural engineering principles, construction methods, and industry standards.
