About the role

Capitec is hiring a Senior Salesforce Developer, based in Stellenbosch. The technology making that possible needs to be just as bold. That's where you come in. We need a senior Salesforce developer who can take ownership of the platform that powers Funeral Cover, Life Cover and Credit Insurance for millions of South Africans - and help us build what comes next.

Responsibilities

  • You'll work on a platform that manages the full insurance lifecycle - from quote to claim - at scale, for real clients who depend on it.
  • Specifically, you'll
  • Lead Salesforce architectural decisions across our Insurance platform
  • Build and extend capabilities for policy lifecycle management, claims processing and client relationship management
  • Design and implement integrations across policy administration, claims intake, adjudication and payment processing
  • Build and maintain complex Apex logic, Lightning Web Components and Salesforce Flows that drive our insurance operations
  • Ensure our platform scales reliably as our insurance business grows - across Life Cover, Funeral Cover and Credit Insurance
  • Mentor junior and intermediate developers on the team
  • Drive development standards and engineering best practices within the Salesforce space
  • Evaluate and introduce Salesforce capabilities that move our Insurance platform forward
  • Your tech stack
  • Salesforce Financial Services Cloud - our insurance platform foundation
  • Apex, LWC, Salesforce Flow - how we build and extend the platform
  • REST/SOAP APIs, Platform Events, Change Data Capture - how we integrate
  • Java - your back-end foundation, directly transferable here
  • Salesforce DX & CLI - source-driven development and deployment pipelines

Requirements

  • Experience
  • 6+ years of software development experience, with meaningful Salesforce platform exposure
  • Back-end development experience in Java or similar object-oriented languages, considered a strong foundation for Salesforce development
  • A track record of designing and delivering complex Salesforce solutions end-to-end
  • Experience working in regulated or financial services environments is a strong advantage
  • The ability to explain technical decisions clearly - to engineers, architects and business stakeholders
  • A genuine interest in growing the engineers around you
  • Education
  • Minimum: Grade 12 National Certificate
  • Preferred: A relevant tertiary qualification in information technology
  • Certifications
  • Required: Salesforce Platform Developer I
  • Preferred: Salesforce Platform Developer II, Salesforce Administrator, or Salesforce Financial Services Cloud accreditation
  • Knowledge
  • You can apply expert-level knowledge and develop others' understanding across:
  • Apex programming - triggers, service classes, batch Apex, queueable Apex and scheduled Apex
  • Lightning Web Components - component structure, data connectivity, cross-component communication and Lightning Design System
  • Salesforce Flow - record-triggered flows, screen flows, scheduled flows and auto-launched flows
  • SOQL and SOSL - efficient querying, governor limit management and multi-object data retrieval
  • Insurance-specific data modelling - policies, claims, coverages, participants and financial transactions
  • API integration - REST and SOAP services, Named Credentials, OAuth 2.0 and external system authentication
  • Apex test class design - unit tests, integration tests and bulk operation tests with meaningful coverage
  • IT systems development processes (SDLC) and version control
