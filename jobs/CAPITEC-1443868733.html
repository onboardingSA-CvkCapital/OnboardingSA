About the role

Capitec is hiring a Software Engineer: Front-End, based in Stellenbosch. We're looking for an engineer who takes ownership. Someone who can pick up a requirement, understand the problem, work through an approach, build the solution and see it through to completion. You'll join the team at an exciting point in the app's evolution. We're moving towards a more modern, maintainable architecture and towards full-stack engineering, giving you the opportunity to grow your skills while working alongside experienced engineers.

Responsibilities

  • Own features and requirements from understanding the problem through to delivery
  • Build and maintain React components and features that are clean, reusable and easy for other engineers to work with
  • Integrate frontend applications with backend services and APIs, and troubleshoot issues across the stack
  • Participate actively in code reviews, both contributing to and learning from the engineering standards around you
  • Contribute to the frontend rewrite and help shape a more modern, maintainable architecture
  • Work closely with engineers and stakeholders to deliver features that work well and stand the test of time
  • Why Capitec?
  • Scale
  • The Capitec app is used by more than 26 million clients. Your work has the potential to reach people across South Africa every day.
  • A product people use every day
  • You'll be building technology that sits at the centre of how millions of people manage their money, from everyday payments and transactions to saving and managing their finances.
  • A high engineering standard
  • This is a large codebase with real technical challenges. You'll participate in code reviews, technical discussions and architectural decisions, with experienced engineers around you to learn from and challenge.
  • A team that's building for what's next
  • We're in the middle of a significant frontend rewrite and moving towards full-stack engineering. You'll have the opportunity to contribute to that change rather than simply maintaining what already exists.
  • Room to grow
  • You'll be encouraged to broaden your technical skills beyond the frontend, while working alongside senior engineers who can support your development.
  • What you can expect
  • You'll work in a large codebase while we transition from the existing frontend to a more modern architecture
  • Because this is banking, security, reliability and quality are critical to everything we build
  • You'll be involved in technical discussions from the start, working alongside experienced engineers and contributing your ideas
  • You'll have the opportunity to expand your skills beyond frontend as the team moves towards full-stack engineering

Requirements

  • What we're looking for
  • 3 to 5 years of professional software development experience
  • Strong, hands-on ReactJS experience
  • Good knowledge of JavaScript and modern frontend development
  • Solid understanding of frontend architecture and component design
  • Experience integrating frontend applications with APIs and backend services
  • A track record of writing maintainable, testable code within a large codebase
  • Comfortable working with Git and participating in code reviews
  • The ability to take ownership of your work and see it through independently
  • A bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related field
  • Nice to have
  • Java or Spring experience
