About the role

Job Purpose To develop new business and grow existing customer accounts by identifying mining technology needs, qualifying opportunities, positioning appropriate the company's capabilities and progressing opportunities to profitable orders. The role combines commercial selling with sufficient technical understanding to engage credibly with Mining, Engineering, IT/OT, Safety, Operations and Procurement stakeholders. Responsibilities • Identify target mines, customers, partners, projects and expansion opportunities across surface and underground operations. • Build relationships with decision-makers and influencers in Safety, Engineering, IT/OT, Operations, Mine Management and Procurement. • Maintain regular contact with strategic accounts and understand future mine plans, pain points, budgets and project timing. • Develop opportunities from first contact through qualification, proposal, negotiation and order/contract award. • Maintain accurate opportunity stages, next actions, value, probability and expected close dates. • Position the company as an integrated mining-technology solutions provider rather than a generic hardware supplier. • Understand and explain the commercial use of the company's capabilities including wireless connectivity, BLE, LoRa/LoRaWAN, mesh networking, RTLS, AoA, UWB, personnel/vehicle/asset location, rugged and intrinsically safe devices, cameras, INSIGHTS™, dashboards, data integration, RF surveys and custom-engineered solutions. • Lead with customer outcomes such as safety, visibility, communication, emergency response, productivity and decision support before discussing individual components. • Use current market drivers such as Missing Person Locator requirements appropriately without presenting the company as a single-regulation business. • Understand the operational problem before recommending technology. • Establish scope, users, areas, existing infrastructure, interfaces, constraints, timing, budget context and decision process. • Identify whether a site survey, proof of concept, technical workshop or Solutions Architect engagement is required. • Disqualify or pause opportunities where need, authority, commercial fit or technical viability has not been established. • Coordinate proposals, quotations, demonstrations and tender submissions with internal technical and commercial contributors. • Ensure pricing and commercial proposals reflect the approved technical scope, assumptions, exclusions and dependencies. • Conduct a formal handover to Project Management and Solutions Architecture after award so that delivery starts with a clear record of what was sold. • May qualify opportunities, arrange meetings, present approved capabilities and progress commercial discussions within delegated authority. • Must not commit to unverified RF coverage, positioning accuracy, battery life, integration compatibility, hazardous-area suitability, delivery dates or technical performance. • Must obtain technical approval for non-standard architectures, POCs, custom developments or integrations. • Must obtain management approval for non-standard discounts, contract terms, warranties, penalties or commercial exceptions. • Working understanding of TCP/IP networking, Wi-Fi, BLE, LoRa/LoRaWAN, mesh networking, RTLS/UWB and industrial IoT concepts. • Ability to understand surface and underground mining workflows and relate technology to operational outcomes. • Strong discovery, presentation, proposal and relationship-management skills. • Ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders and business value to technical stakeholders.
