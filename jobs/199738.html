About the role

TRAINEE: FINANCE & INSURANCE (12-MONTH FIXED-TERM CONTRACT | MOTUS RETAIL) Are you looking to launch your career in Finance & Insurance within the automotive retail industry? Motus Retail is looking for a motivated, customer-focused and commercially minded Trainee: Finance & Insurance to join our dealership network on a 12-month fixed-term contract. This is an exciting opportunity for a graduate or suitably qualified individual to gain practical, hands-on experience in the Finance & Insurance environment while learning from experienced F&I professionals. The successful candidate will be required to participate in relevant workplace learning and development activities during the 12-month contract. Trainees will be exposed to the full F&I process, including vehicle finance applications, customer engagement, value-added products, administration, compliance and deal processing. This 12-month opportunity offers the successful candidate the chance to gain valuable practical experience, develop industry-specific knowledge and build a strong foundation for a career in Finance & Insurance within Motus Retail. JOB PURPOSE The Trainee: Finance & Insurance will support the F&I team in managing finance and insurance processes relating to vehicle sales while gaining practical knowledge and experience of the F&I environment. The role will provide structured workplace learning and exposure to finance and insurance products, customer service, regulatory requirements, dealership processes and relationships with finance houses and insurance providers. The successful candidate will be expected to develop the knowledge, skills and competencies required to progress towards a career in Finance & Insurance.
