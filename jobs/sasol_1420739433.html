About the role

Sasol is a global integrated chemicals and energy company with a 75-year heritage. Through our talented people, we use our expertise and selected technologies to safely and sustainably source, manufacture and market chemical and energy products globally. When you join Team Sasol, you are joining a company that puts people at the center of everything we do. Sasol invests in its employees along every stage of the career path and offers development opportunities to help you cultivate your career in a culture that embraces diversity and inclusion. The Principal Officer (PO) is the accountable executive and visible representative of Sasolmed (Sasol’s in-house medical scheme), acting on behalf of the Board of Trustees to ensure that the Scheme is governed, managed and administered in compliance with the Medical Schemes Act, the Scheme Rules and applicable laws. The role requires a highly competent, credible and member-oriented professional who can support the Board of Trustees in ensuring effective governance, regulatory compliance, sound administration and responsive service to members. The Principal Officer must demonstrate excellent stakeholder relationship management, strong attention to detail, disciplined execution, a strong sense of urgency and deep knowledge of medical scheme governance. The Principal Officer acts on behalf of the Board of Trustees to: Lead the development of the Scheme’s long‑term strategy (3–5 years) and business plan; Ensuring alignment between Sasolmed’s strategy and Sasol’s broader employee value proposition; Identifying emerging risks, trends and innovations (e.g. digital health, value‑based care, demographic shifts); Scenario modelling for sustainability (e.g. ageing profile, chronic disease burden, cost drivers). The Principal Officer acts on behalf of the Board of Trustees to: Serve as the Scheme's primary liaison with the Council for Medical Schemes (CMS), ensuring timely submission of all statutory returns, responses to regulatory enquiries, management of inspections, implementation of directives and maintenance of a constructive regulatory relationship; Ensure that proper registers, books and records are maintained, and that accurate and complete minutes of all Board and Committee meetings and resolutions are recorded and retained; Ensure that robust governance, risk management and internal control systems are implemented, maintained, periodically reviewed and that material risks, control and compliance matters are appropriately reported to the Board and relevant Committees; Ensure all reasonable steps are taken for timeous payment of contributions to the Scheme in terms of the Medical Schemes Act and the Scheme's Rules; Ensure that the Board contracts and maintains professional indemnity insurance and fidelity guarantee insurance as determined by the CMS; Obtain expert advice on legal, accounting, governance and business matters as required, or when requested by the Board and Committees; Take all reasonable steps to protect confidentiality of medical records; Ensure that the Board is constituted in terms of the Medical Schemes Act and the Scheme's Rules; Monitor and manage the performance of the Administrator and third-party contracts, ensuring accountability, responsiveness, service quality, effective governance and the timely resolution of operational, compliance and control matters; Conduct and/or facilitate training, endeavor to ensure that Trustees attend mandatory training, and report on Trustee training; Follow up that the Scheme rules are approved by the CMS; and sign contracts on behalf of the Board in accordance with the Delegation of Authority (DoA). Ensure compliance with POPIA and all applicable information governance requirements, including oversight of member data privacy, cybersecurity controls, third-party data processors, data-sharing arrangements and incident response protocols; Coordinate the Scheme's assurance activities including internal audit, external audit, actuarial reviews, compliance monitoring and implementation of assurance recommendations. The Principal Officer is responsible to: Guide the Board in designing benefit and contribution structures; Ensure compliance with the Act in terms of benefits and contributions; Monitor the financial position of the Scheme; Ensure that the annual budget is prepared and approved by the Scheme; Monitor the solvency of the Scheme; Monitor the income and expenditure of the Scheme; Review actuarial valuation reports and ensure that Board decisions are informed by appropriate actuarial analysis; Monitor reserves, contribution adequacy, healthcare inflation trends and long-term financial sustainability of the Scheme. In terms of the Act, any notice given to the Principal Officer is deemed to be given to the Scheme. The onus lies with the Principal Officer to ensure such information is distributed to the relevant stakeholders including reports and information to assist the Sasol Wellness team to inform the Group Executive Committee (GEC) of wellness trends and identified health risks in the workplace, and actioned as appropriate; Ensure a communication strategy and plan is in place to effectively communicate with all relevant stakeholders at the appropriate time; Ensure that members receive adequate, accurate and timely information regarding their rights, benefits, contributions and obligations in terms of the Scheme Rules and the Medical Schemes Act; Act as the visible and credible face of Sasolmed to members, and ensure ongoing open communication channels between the Sasolmed office, service providers, the Administrator and members; Monitor member experience indicators including complaints, appeals, query resolution, call-centre performance, digital adoption and member satisfaction; Liaise with the Employer and be an integral stakeholder in the delivery of wellness initiatives in the workplace, as well as the provision of information to support integrated reporting; Ensure a formal complaints and escalation management framework exists, including monitoring trends, root-cause analysis and implementation of corrective actions; Leverages partnerships with Occupational Health Services to complement organisational health strategies, improve access to preventative and supportive care, and enhance the overall health and wellbeing of employee-members.
