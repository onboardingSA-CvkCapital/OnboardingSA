About the role

The Department of Health (KwaZulu-Natal) is hiring a Professional Nurse, based in Ethekwini District Office. This is a permanent post, paying R337,359. How to apply: ALL APPLICATIONS SHOULD BE FORWARDED TO: Hand delivery @ 15H00 EThekwini District Office HR Office 5A11 83 King Cetshwayo Highway Mayville Reference number to quote: ETH DO 09/2026 Closing date: 13 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the implementation of the School Health Programme in accordance with Integrated school health policies and guidelines.
  • Develop operational plans, schedules and micro plans to ensure equitable coverage of schools and timely delivery of health services.
  • Conduct in-service training and mentorship for nursing personnel to improve clinical competence and service quality.
  • Conduct comprehensive health assessments and screenings to identify learners with health problems, developmental delays and psychosocial needs.
  • ASIBE HEALTHY
  • Responsible for the Team and management of the vaccination station and be a vaccinator.
  • Supervise enrolled nurses and other healthcare personnel involved in the delivery of school health services.
  • Provide comprehensive nursing care, including assessment, management and referral of learners with identified health conditions.
  • Implement health promotion and disease prevention programmes to improve learners’ health and well-being such as health awareness events, awareness drives and activations
  • Manage school-based immunization activities and vaccination campaigns in collaboration with relevant healthcare teams and education stakeholders.
  • Identify learners at risk and ensure appropriate referral, follow-up and continuity of care.
  • Monitor the implementation of referral systems to ensure that learners receive appropriate treatment and follow-up care.
  • Support the implementation of the Integrated Multi Stakeholder Health Promotion and Wellbeing Strategy interventions addressing teenage pregnancy, HIV and AIDS, tuberculosis and malnutrition
  • Compile and submit accurate and timely statistical reports on school health activities and programme performance.
  • Maintain accurate, complete and confidential clinical records, referral registers school health files
  • Ensure compliance with infection prevention and control standards and occupational health and safety requirements.
  • Manage school health resources, equipment and medical supplies to ensure effective service delivery.
  • Promote community participation and parental involvement in learner and school health activities.
  • Manage emergency care and first aid for learners who become ill or sustain injuries at school
  • Report programme challenges, adverse incidents and service delivery gaps to the relevant supervisor and recommend appropriate interventions.
  • Engage, market and establish health promoting schools.
