About the role

We are looking for an experienced Interim Chief Financial Officer (CFO) to lead and support significant finance transformation initiatives over an initial 12-month contract. This is a hands-on leadership role suited to a commercially focused CFO who can operate at both strategic and operational level, drive transformation and deliver measurable improvements across the finance function. Key Responsibilities • Lead and drive major finance transformation initiatives. • Review and optimise the finance operating model. • Lead or support ERP implementation and optimisation. • Drive process automation, efficiency and cost optimisation. • Improve financial performance, controls and operational effectiveness. • Lead finance team restructuring and capability development where required. • Provide strategic financial leadership and support to the Board and Executive Committee. • Take ownership of execution and ensure transformation initiatives deliver tangible business outcomes. Key Requirements • Proven experience as a CFO, preferably within a medium to large organisation. • Strong track record in finance transformation, operational improvement or turnaround environments. • Experience with: • Finance operating model redesign • ERP implementation/optimisation • Process automation • Cost and performance optimisation • Finance team restructuring and capability enhancement • Strong Board and Executive-level stakeholder management experience. • Hands-on, delivery-focused approach with the ability to balance strategic leadership with execution. • Experience in automotive, manufacturing, industrial or similarly complex operating environments. Highly Desirable • Previous SYSPRO implementation, optimisation or operational experience. • Experience in interim, turnaround, transformation or project-based CFO assignments. Contract & Availability • Contract: Initial 12 months • Start Date: Candidates available within approximately 30 days preferred If you are a hands-on, transformation-focused CFO with the experience to make a meaningful impact within a complex operating environment, we would like to hear from you.
