About the role

The Department of Correctional Services is hiring a HR Practitioner: Service Benefits in HR Administration and Utilization, based in National Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 a year. How to apply: should be submitted online at https://careers.dcs.gov.za/. Emailed, post or hand delivered applications will not be considered. Reference number to quote: HO 2026/11/82 Closing date: 09 November 2026 @ 15H45. Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Appointment under the Public Service Act.

Responsibilities

  • Administer housing and accommodation benefits.
  • Administer employee financial assistance and debt management services.
  • Administer travel, transport and related employee benefits.
  • Administer and implement leave and employee benefits.
  • Management of resources.
  • Management of performance information.

Requirements

  • Recognized 3 year Degree or National Diploma in Human Resource Management or relevant qualification.
  • Knowledge of PERSAL.
  • 1- 2yrs relevant experience in human resource environment.
  • Computer literate.
  • Valid driver’s licence.
  • Competencies And Attributes:
  • Understanding of Public service policies and legislative frameworks
  • Knowledge of HR policy processes and implementation
  • In depth understanding of Public Service regulatory framework
  • Plan, organise, lead and control
  • Change management
  • Team leadership
  • Project management, presentation, conflict management, report writing, training and development, facilitation and training skills
  • Time management
  • Confidentiality
  • Coaching and mentoring
  • Service delivery and client orientation
  • Integrity and honest
  • Assertiveness
  • Ability to network
  • Influence and impact
  • Applied strategic thinking
  • Willingness to travel
