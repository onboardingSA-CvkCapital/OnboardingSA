About the role

ForAfrika is an African-founded and led organisation with over 40 years of impact across the continent. We partner with communities through our integrated development continuum: saving lives and building resilience to reach self-sufficiency through our Humanitarian & Development work and unlocking commercial viability through our Social Enterprise activities. We are Africans, delivering African-led solutions for Africa, and we stand by our promise: we’re committed to the long run, until the job is done and communities are enabled to thrive. Position Summary The role provides HR operational support for national and international staff contracted through ForAfrika, across the Global Support Office (GSO) and supported Country Offices. The role supports the effective delivery of HR services across payroll and benefits administration, recruitment, contracting and onboarding, performance management, employee relations, staff exits, HR systems and employee records. The HR Coordinator plays a key role in supporting a fair, compliant and accountable workplace by coordinating employee relations matters, including grievances, disciplinary processes, workplace concerns and HR investigations, as well as supporting Safeguarding and Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) investigations where required. The role ensures compliance with ForAfrika policies, procedures, accountability standards and applicable labour legislation. Acting as a central link between the GSO HR team, employees, managers and Country Offices, the HR Coordinator ensures timely HR support, accurate documentation and records, effective communication, and consistent application of organisational policies and procedures.
