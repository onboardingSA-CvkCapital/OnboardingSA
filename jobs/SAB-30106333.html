About the role

SAB is hiring a Head of Commercial Inspection, based in Bryanston. and trade investments. The role sets the risk-based inspection strategy, directs intelligence-led field activity, escalates material control failures, and ensures that revenue erosion, discount non-compliance and execution gaps are identified, quantified, remediated and prevented.

Responsibilities

  • Inspection Strategy and Operating Model
  • Define the commercial inspection mandate, annual risk-based plan, standards and coverage model.
  • Translate commercial risk priorities into targeted reviews across customers, channels, territories and investments.
  • Maintain appropriate independence, evidence standards and escalation protocols for the function
  • Commercial Investment Protection
  • Oversee assurance of discounts, rebates, promotions, trade spend, commercial assets and customer agreements.
  • Ensure unsupported investment is quantified and referred for recovery or remediation.
  • Direct thematic reviews where recurring weaknesses or emerging risks are identified.
  • Leadership of Intelligence and Field Execution
  • Lead the Commercial Intelligence & Compliance Hub and Field Inspection Execution teams.
  • Ensure data-led targeting, effective field deployment, consistent inspection methodology and quality assurance.
  • Build inspection capability through standards, coaching, calibration and field leadership.
  • Governance, Reporting and Escalation
  • Provide clear reporting on findings, financial exposure, root causes, corrective actions and recurring themes.
  • Escalate significant breaches promptly to relevant commercial, finance, compliance, audit and legal stakeholders.
  • Track recoveries and remediation to verified closure and hold accountable owners to commitments.
  • Stakeholder and Control Improvement
  • Partner with Sales, Trade Marketing, Revenue Management, Finance, Internal Audit, Compliance and Legal.
  • Recommend preventive control, process, system and policy improvements based on inspection evidence.
  • Promote a culture of disciplined commercial investment and consequence management.

Requirements

  • Key Competencies and Attributes
  • Commercial assurance and risk-based audit planning.
  • Strong understanding of FMCG sales, customer investment, pricing, discounting and route-to-market.
  • Financial exposure assessment, investigation oversight and executive reporting.
  • Data-led risk targeting and performance management.
  • High integrity, independence and sound judgement.
  • Strong commercial acumen and an owner’s mindset.
  • Leadership courage and the ability to challenge constructively.
  • Executive communication, stakeholder influence and decisive follow-through.
  • Ability to build a disciplined, field-oriented team.
  • Commitment to confidentiality, objective evidence and consistent application of company standards.
  • Ability to work across functions and maintain appropriate independence from audited activities.
  • Willingness and ability to travel into trade across assigned markets.
  • Ability to manage sensitive investigations and commercially significant findings.
  • Minimum Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Auditing, Business, Commercial Management or a related field.
  • Professional audit, forensic, risk or compliance qualification is advantageous.
  • Eight or more years of relevant FMCG commercial, finance, assurance, audit, risk or compliance experience, including leadership responsibility.
  • Demonstrated experience with customer investment, discounting, route-to-market or field execution controls.
