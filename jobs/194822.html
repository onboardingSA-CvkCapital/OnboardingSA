About the role

Optimi is currently looking for an individual with exceptional communication and administration skills to join the Assessment Development and Marking team as an assessment coordinator. The ideal candidate will be results orientated, committed to meeting timescales, and able to work well in a team. REQUIREMENTS • Grade 12. • 5 years’ administrative experience. • Excellent administrative skills. • Good client engagement skills. • Detail orientated. • Proficiency in Microsoft suite. • Proficient in English and Afrikaans. DUTIES • Coordinate assessment, moderation, and online marking processes. • Recruit, onboard, train, and support markers throughout the marking cycle. • Manage marker administration, including contracts, payment verification, and related documentation. • Meet assessment, marking, moderation, and subject delivery targets and deadlines. • Manage subject-related enquiries and escalated queries from learners, markers, and internal stakeholders. • Coordinate memorandum meetings, monitor marking progress, and interpret assessment statistics to drive follow-up actions. • Prepare reports, maintain accurate assessment records, and ensure compliance with assessment and moderation processes. This role is based in Centurion. Should you not receive a call from us within 14 days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
