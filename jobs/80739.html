About the role

Inspecting, diagnosing, repairing, and processing returned products, ensuring accurate technical evaluation and efficient management of returned inventory The role supports product quality assurance, customer satisfaction, and compliance requirements A key part of the role includes attending installation sites to inspect lighting fittings, identifying installation faults or product issues, and preparing professional technical reports for both the client and Company The Repairs & Returns Senior Technician reports to the Operations Manager Inspect returned products and perform technical fault diagnosis Repair products where possible and determine root cause of failure Evaluate product failures and provide detailed technical reports Assist with technical evaluation of new products and NRCS compliance requirements Support NRCS certification testing processes Field Support, Site Inspection and Technical Reporting Attend installation sites where Company products are installed, when required Inspect installed lighting fittings, drivers, wiring, and associated components Identify and diagnose installation faults, product faults, electrical supply issues, or external causes Assess installation quality and compliance with product specifications and electrical standards Identify incorrect installation practices that may affect product performance or safety Take photographic evidence and maintain accurate records during site inspections Prepare clear, accurate, and professional technical reports outlining findings, root cause, and recommendations Provide technical reports to management and clients to support technical decisions and warranty evaluations Complete all call out reports accurately and maintain detailed records Represent Company professionally when
