About the role

A leading specialized engineering firm delivering advanced industrial weighing systems, batch plant design, material handling, and process control integration across the food, mining, chemical, pulp & paper, tobacco, and pharmaceutical sectors, is seeking a Junior Mechanical Draughtsman with experience in industrial weighing systems, batching systems, and material handling to join their technical team. Responsibilities: • Mechanical Design: Create 2D/3D designs for weighing hoppers, feeders, silos, and loadcell integration. • Layouts & Systems: Develop batch plant layouts and support retrofit projects and on-site mechanical systems. • Documentation & BOMs: Generate accurate engineering drawings, Bill of Materials (BOMs), apply GD&T standards, and maintain drawing control. • Technical Collaboration: Partner with engineers, instrumentation specialists, and project teams to execute project briefs. • Site Support: Conduct site measurements, perform troubleshooting, and assist with project execution. Minimum Requirements: • National Diploma or Certificate in Mechanical Engineering/Draughting (NQF 5/6). • ECSA registration (or eligibility for registration) as a Draughtsperson/Technician is advantageous. • 2–3 years of practical draughting experience within a manufacturing or industrial environment. • High proficiency in AutoCAD and 3D modeling software. • Core technical knowledge of loadcells, silos, hoppers, and batch plants. • Strong understanding of engineering principles, structural design, and GD&T. • Proficient in MS Office (Excel, Word). Benefits: Competitive salary based on experience (salary can potentially be more based on experience/skills) IF you meet the above requirements and want to make a career-changing move, apply today by emailing your CV to itcareers@hireresolve.za.com
