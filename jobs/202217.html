About the role

JOB DESCRIPTION Job Title: ABSEN Technical Specialist Permanent/Temporary: Permanent Department/Division: AV & VC BU Reports to: Business Unit Executive Location: Nology Centurion Effective date: ASAP Job Purpose Summary: To provide specialist technical expertise across the Absen LED display portfolio, supporting the business from pre-sales solution design through installation, commissioning, troubleshooting, maintenance and after-sales support. The Technical Specialist will act as a key technical interface between Nology, Absen, resellers, integrators, rental and staging companies, installers and end customers. The role is responsible for ensuring that Absen solutions are correctly specified, configured, deployed and supported, while developing the technical capability of Nology and its partner network. The Technical Specialist will contribute directly to customer satisfaction, partner confidence, service quality and sustainable growth of the Absen business Key Duties & Responsibilities: Technical
