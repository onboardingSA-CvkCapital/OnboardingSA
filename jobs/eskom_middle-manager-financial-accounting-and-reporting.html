About the role

Area of specialization: To lead and manage the financial accounting and external reporting function by ensuring accurate, reliable, and compliant financial reporting in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), applicable legislation, and organisational policies while maintaining sound financial governance, discipline, and controls, overseeing the financial close and statutory reporting processes, and providing high-quality financial information to supports effective decision-making, stakeholder confidence and the achievement of the organisation’s finance strategy. Position/Task Grade: M17 Department: Finance Business Unit: Finance Location: Sunninghill- Megawatt Park-Gauteng
