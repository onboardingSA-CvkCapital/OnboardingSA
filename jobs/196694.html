About the role

We are seeking an experienced Logistics Planner to join a dynamic supply chain environment. The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating branch stock requirements, planning vehicle loads, and ensuring efficient logistics operations. The role requires strong logistics planning skills, attention to detail, and the ability to collaborate with internal teams to ensure effective stock management and customer service delivery.
