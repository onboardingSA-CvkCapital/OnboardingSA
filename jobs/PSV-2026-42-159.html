About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring an Assistant Director: Financial Accounting, based in Cape Town Regional Office. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Email Applications for the position of Assistant Director: Financial Accounting must be submitted to: RecruitCPT26-75@dpw.gov.za Reference number to quote: 2026/402 Closing date: 04 December 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Participate in the development, maintenance and implementation of departmental financial policies.
  • Advice line function on the implementation and interpretation of financial procedures and policies.
  • Facilitate the implementation of changes in public finance management policies.
  • Facilitate alignment of internal control policies in compliance with departmental policies.
  • Facilitate the implementation of Treasury Regulation in the department.
  • Issue guidelines to and train line managers on latest developments in public finance management regulations.
  • Assist in budget planning, adjustment and reporting.
  • Verify the capturing of payroll transactions on the accounting system.
  • Monitor income against budget and review reconciliations.
  • Oversee and undertake the development of corrective measures when required.
  • Coordinate and review disclosure notes on revenue collection.
  • Coordinate and collate financial supporting information for planning purposes.
  • Ensure completeness and accuracy of financial information.
  • Exercise control, monitor and supervises sub-ordinates (cashier duties, batch control and payments).
  • Check and authorise payment advise.
  • Control suspense accounts.
  • Monitor face value forms and subsistence and travel allowance (S&T) claims.
  • Authorize, approve or disapprove transactions on the financial system, BAS, SAGE, ARCHIBUS, WCS, PMIS LOGIS and PERSAL systems.
  • Oversee the verification of the validity and allocation of payments received via electronic transfers.
  • Review of audit files and annual financial statements to ensure compliance with GRAP.
  • Gather and select information to address audit queries.
  • Collate audit information required by auditors.
  • Address audit queries.
  • Monitor compliance to the PFMA, Treasury regulations, departmental policies and internal control procedures.
  • Update the risk register.
  • Provide general supervision of employees.
  • Allocate duties and do quality control of the work delivered by supervisees.
  • Advise and lead supervisees with regard to all aspects of the work.
  • Manage performance, conduct and discipline of supervisees.
  • Ensure that all supervisees are trained and developed to be able to deliver work of the required standard efficiently and effectively.
  • Develop, implement and monitor work systems and processes to ensure efficient and effective functioning.

Requirements

  • At least three-year tertiary qualification (NQF Level 6)/National Diploma in Financial Management or related qualification.
  • Relevant years of experience in the field at supervisory level.
  • Knowledge
  • Public Finance Management Act
  • National Department of Treasury regulations
  • Guidelines and directives
  • Departments Reconciliation processes
  • Understanding of Governments financial delegations
  • Management of governments departments chart of accounts
  • Government Budget systems and procedures
  • Government Financial Systems
  • Supply Chain Management
  • Financial prescripts (GAAP and GRAP)
  • Skills
  • Good verbal and written communication skills
  • Basic numeracy
  • Advanced interpersonal and diplomacy skills
  • Problem solving skills
  • Decision making skills
  • Numeracy
  • Statistical skills
  • Ability to work under stressful situations, ability to communicate at all levels, able to establish and maintain networks
