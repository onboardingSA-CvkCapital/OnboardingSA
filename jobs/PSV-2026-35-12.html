About the role

The Department of Employment and Labour is hiring an Assistant Director: Conditions of Service, based in Compensation Fund, Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R487,197 a year. How to apply: Direct Your Applications to: Jobs-CF28@labour.gov.za Reference number to quote: HR 5/1/2/3/34 Closing date: 16 October 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: Preference will be given to Persons with Disabilities, Whites and Indians

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the implementation of employee benefits, conditions of service and remuneration of employees in the Fund.
  • Coordinate the process of administering the incapacity leave process (Pillar).
  • Coordinate the maintenance of the Human Resource Information of the Fund.
  • Management of resources.

Requirements

  • Undergraduate qualification (NQF Level 6) as recognised by SAQA in Human Resource Management.
  • 4 years’ functional experience in human resource administration environment of which 2 years should be supervisory experience at Senior Practitioner level or equivalent level.
  • Knowledge:
  • Human Resource Management methodology, principles and strategy
  • Digital Acumen/Relevant HRM Systems/Performance Systems
  • Compensation Fund Objectives and Business Function
  • Compensation Fund services
  • Relevant Fund policies, procedures and processes
  • Stakeholders and customers
  • Customer Service (Batho Pele Principles)
  • Risk Awareness
  • Legislative requirements:
  • Public Service Regulations
  • Public Service Act
  • Basic Conditions of Employment Act
  • POPI Act
  • Skills:
  • Project Management and Planning
  • Quality Assurance and Improvement
  • Data Integrity and Management
  • Ethics and Governance
  • Monitoring and Evaluation
  • Data Gathering, Analysis
  • Data/Record Management
  • Research and Benchmarking
  • Stakeholder Development and Management
