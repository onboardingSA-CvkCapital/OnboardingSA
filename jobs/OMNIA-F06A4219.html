About the role

Omnia is hiring a Safety Officer in Group, SHEQ, based in Fourways, Monte Circle. The role reports to the Group Sustainability Manager. The role ensures compliance with applicable legislation, company standards, and industry best practices while promoting a proactive culture of safety, operational excellence, sustainability, and responsible facilities management.

Responsibilities

  • Occupational Health & Safety Management
  • Lead contractor and facilities management activities, ensuring safe, compliant, and efficient operations.
  • Drive health, safety, GREEN & ENERGY building initiatives across the organisation.
  • Oversee building maintenance, compliance, and facilities-related projects.
  • Manage operational changes and respond effectively to facilities emergencies and critical issues.
  • Safety Management
  • Champion a proactive safety culture by implementing and maintaining health and safety programmes.
  • Conduct audits, inspections, risk assessments, and incident investigations to ensure a safe working environment.
  • Ensure compliance with relevant legislation, regulations, and industry standards.
  • Monitor and report on safety performance, identifying opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Sustainability & Environmental Compliance
  • Support and implement sustainability and environmental compliance initiatives.
  • Drive programmes focused on energy efficiency, waste reduction, and responsible resource management.
  • Contribute to environmental reporting and the achievement of sustainability objectives.
  • Stakeholder Management
  • Build strong relationships with employees, management, contractors, and service providers.
  • Provide trusted advice and support on SHEQ, compliance, and facilities management matters.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to deliver safe, compliant, and effective workplace solutions.
  • Training & Awareness
  • Deliver engaging health, safety, environmental, and facilities-related training and awareness programmes.
  • Promote employee understanding of safe work practices, emergency procedures, and compliance requirements.
  • Support competency development and maintain accurate training records.

Requirements

  • Qualifications
  • National Diploma or Degree in Occupational Health & Safety, Environmental Management, Safety Management, Facilities Management, or related field.
  • Experience
  • Minimum 3-5 years' experience in Occupational Health & Safety within a manufacturing, chemical, industrial, engineering, mining, or similar environment.
  • Experience in facilities management and contractor management.
  • Experience conducting risk assessments, incident investigations, and compliance audits.
  • Knowledge of environmental and sustainability practices.
  • Key Competencies
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Excellent planning and organisation skills
  • Problem-solving and decision-making
  • Accountability and ownership
  • Excellent communication and influencing skills
  • Relationship management
  • Customer focus
  • Resilience and adaptability
  • Continuous improvement mindset
  • Ethical conduct and integrity
