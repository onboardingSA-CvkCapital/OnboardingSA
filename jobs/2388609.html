About the role

About the Role We are seeking a hands-on, energetic HR & Training Specialist to drive end-to-end human capital operations across our expanding group of entities, including financial services, insurance, micro-insurance, retail, and our dedicated training academy. Reporting directly to the CEO, this role combines end-to-end volume recruitment, payroll processing, SETA-aligned training administration, and generalist HR support. This is a dynamic operational role with clear progression potential toward a senior HR Manager position as the group grows. Key Responsibilities • Talent Acquisition (Primary Priority): Drive end-to-end recruitment for call centre, retail, admin, and technical roles. Source, screen, interview, execute MIE/background checks, and build shortlists for line managers. • Training & SETA Administration: Oversee operational administration for our insurance training academy, coordinate workplace skills development, and manage annual WSP/ATR submissions (specifically INSETA). • Payroll Processing: Audit and process monthly payroll inputs (leave, overtime, variables) using Sage-Pastel before handing off to Finance. • Employee Lifecycle & Engagement: Facilitate onboarding, manage probation reviews, conduct exit interviews, and handle offboarding/UIF documentation. • Performance & IR Support: Facilitate performance appraisal frameworks. Guide line managers on workplace discipline, draft warnings/charges, manage incapacity, and represent the company at the CCMA when required. • Compliance & EE: Ensure alignment with Labour Legislation (BCEA, LRA, EEA, SDA, POPIA), handle IOD claims, display statutory posters, and submit annual Employment Equity reports.
