About the role

The Department of Employment and Labour is hiring a Specialist: Employment Standards, based in Mmabatho Provincial Office. This is a permanent post, paying R1,101,468 a year. Re-advertisement, applicants who previously applied are encouraged to reapply. How to apply: Chief Director: Provincial Operations: Private Bag X2040, Mmabatho, 2735 or hand deliver at Provident House, University Drive, Mmabatho or email: Jobs- NW2@labour.gov.za Reference number to quote: HR4/4/10/11 Closing date: 11 December 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV. Note: EE target: Priority will be given to African Male, Coloured Male, Indian Male, Indian Female, White Male, White Female.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the implementation of Employment Standards Inspection Strategy, Policy and Procedures.
  • Develop and monitor the implementation of policy and work plan to ensure peaceful and harmonious employer employee relationship.
  • Manage and conduct the advocacy and educational programmes directed towards internal and external stakeholders.
  • Manage all the resources of the Sub-directorate such as Human Resources, Financial Resources, Assets, etc.

Requirements

  • A qualification at (NQF 6) as recognised by SAQA in B-Proc/B-Juris/Labour Relations/Industrial Relations/Labour Law/Employment Relations/B-Com Law/BA Law/LLB.
  • Valid driving license.
  • 5 years’ experience of which 2 years at Assistant Director level and 3 years functional experience in Inspection and/or enforcement of employment laws.
  • Knowledge
  • Public service and management issues
  • Ability to convert policy into action
  • Public service Regulation and Relevant prescripts
  • Departmental policies and procedures
