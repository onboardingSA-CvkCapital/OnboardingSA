About the role

Western Cape Education Department is hiring a District Director, based in Worcester. This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. District Office: Cape Winelands Education District Office How to apply: Applications must be submitted by using the following URL https://wcedonline.westerncape.gov.za/home/, via Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox. Reference number to quote: 338 Closing date: 20 November 2026 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Oversee the management of Public Ordinary and Public Special Schools within the district circuits to be effective and efficient in order to deliver quality education: Ensure that Public Ordinary and Public Special Schools are effectively and efficiently managed and governed.
  • Promote leadership and managing principals and support the management of staff (employees) in Public Ordinary Schools and Public Special Schools.
  • Ensure the implementation of the curriculum at Public Ordinary and Public Special Schools within the district.
  • Ensure Learner Support (Inclusive and Specialised Learner and Educator Support) services to Public Ordinary and Public Special Schools.
  • Oversee the district business planning, information management, school management and governance support and, people management within the Education District: Ensure the implementation and dissemination of information, facilitate ICT services and prepare, implement and monitor the district business planning processes and intergovernmental relations.
  • Provide developmental support to Public Ordinary-, Public Special Schools and Hostels to ensure effective and efficient school management and governance.
  • Develop, support and monitor the implementation of sound administration and financial management processes and the implementation of recommendations within Public Ordinary-, Public Special Schools and Hostels.
  • Provide infrastructure planning and monitor delivery of Public Ordinary-, Public Special Schools and Hostels within the district.
  • Administer and monitor learner transport.
  • Manage and monitor the implementation of the National School Nutrition Programme (NSNP).
  • Manage and monitor the implementation of School Safety and Occupational Health and Safety within Public Ordinary-, Public Special Schools and Hostels.
  • Manage and monitor the implementation of school enrichment programmes within Public Ordinary- and Public Special Schools.
  • Provide a people management service in the district.
  • Develop, manage, monitor and report on the workplace skills and the professional development plans for the district.
  • Monitor and administer the staff performance management systems within the district.
  • Manage the health and wellness programmes within the district.
  • Facilitate the compilation of the district people management plan and placement of interns within the district.
  • Oversee the delivery of the curriculum at Public Ordinary and Public Special Schools: Advise schools on national and provincial policies and assist schools to implement them appropriately.
  • Monitor, evaluate and support curriculum implementation.
  • Provide school-based assessments, internal and external examinations (National Senior Certificate - NSC), and national and provincial assessments at district level.
  • Facilitate and support the implementation of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) at Public Schools (including communication, e-Education and e-Administration).
  • Promote the establishment, development, administration and effective use of various school library models (including mobile libraries).
  • Oversee learner support to Public Ordinary- and Public Special Schools: Facilitate and coordinate specialised education programmes including psychological, social work, HIV/AIDS, career guidance, remedial and therapeutic services for all learners.
  • Advance learner health and well-being.
  • Oversee district level corporate service
  • Promote sound financial and supply chain management (SCM) services
  • Render a financial accounting service
  • Provide a management accounting service
  • Render an asset management service
  • Manage the SCM service
  • Monitor financial compliance of Public Ordinary-, Public Special Schools, hostels and provide pre-forensic investigations within the Education District.
  • Evaluation of approved budget of Public Schools and hostels.
  • Manage the evaluation of annual financial statements of Public Schools.
  • Manage the evaluation of the bi-annual financial statements of Public Schools.
  • Verify the monthly compliance of payments (e.g. municipal accounts, LTS, LTSM, etc.).
  • Perform a budget monitoring function in respect of Section 20 schools.
  • Manage pre-forensic investigations.
  • Provide a logistical support service.
  • Manage and monitor GG transport in the district.
  • Manage and maintain district records.
  • Provide a facility support service.
  • Manage and maintain the district- and Circuit Offices infrastructure and grounds.
  • Manage Occupational Health and Safety at the District and Circuit Offices.
  • Strategic Management
  • Define and review on a continual basis the purpose, objectives, priorities and activities of the Directorate
  • Participate in the Branch strategic planning process
  • Active involvement in the development and management of the strategic and business plans for the Directorate
  • Evaluate the performance of the Directorate on a continuing basis against pre-determined key measurable objectives and standards
  • Report to the Chief Director on a regular basis on the activities of the Directorate
  • Monitor and ensure compliance with relevant legislation and prescripts in respect of adequate and appropriate record keeping of the activities of the Directorate, and of the resources employed by it.
  • People Management
  • Participation in the recruitment of staff in the numbers and grades appropriate to ensure the achievement of the Directorate’s Business Plan
  • Motivate, train and guide staff within the Directorate, to achieve and maintain excellence in service delivery
  • Actively manage the performance, evaluation and rewarding of staff within the Directorate
  • Monitor information capacity building within the Directorate
  • Active involvement in the compilation of people management plan, a service delivery improvement programme, and an information resources plan for the Directorate.
  • Promote sound employee relations within the Directorate.
  • Actively manage and promote the maintenance of discipline within the Directorate.
  • Financial Management
  • Active participation in the budgeting process at Branch level
  • Preparing of the Annual and Adjustment Budgets for the Directorate
  • Assume direct responsibility for the efficient, economic and effective control and management of the Directorate’s budget and expenditure
  • Assume direct responsibility for ensuring that the correct tender and procurement procedures are adhered to in respect of purchases for the Directorate.
  • Report to the Chief Director on all aspects of the Directorate’s finances.
  • Assume overall responsibility for the management, maintenance and safekeeping of Directorate’s assets.
  • Ensure that full and proper records of the financial affairs of the Directorate are kept in accordance with any prescribed norms and standards.
