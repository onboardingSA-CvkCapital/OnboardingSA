About the role

Our banking client is seeking an experienced Group Specialist – Strategic Organisational Change to lead and drive enterprise-wide organisational change initiatives across HR products and services. Role Purpose The Group Specialist – Strategic Organisational Change is responsible for designing and implementing strategic HR product and service-related change initiatives across the business. The role applies HR and change management expertise to support organisational transformation across product design, process re-imagination, technology enablement, digitalisation, service delivery and people change. Key Responsibilities Strategic Change Management • Develop and implement integrated change management and communication strategies aligned to the HR Book of Work. • Partner with business leaders, HR teams, Product Owners and stakeholders to drive successful organisational change. • Design and deliver change solutions using communication, training, engagement, technology and process improvement initiatives. • Measure change effectiveness and business impact, ensuring sustainable behavioural and mindset shifts. • Coach stakeholders and build organisational change capability. • Apply data, insights and best practices to continuously improve change strategies.Delivery Management • Leading the planning, prioritisation and implementation of people change initiatives. • Conduct change readiness assessments and identify organisational impacts. • Monitor change adoption, measure outcomes and manage change risks. • Continuously improve change solutions based on performance insights. Finance, Risk & Governance • Demonstrate business value and ROI of change initiatives. • Manage change budgets and provide input into business cases. • Ensure compliance with organisational governance, policies and regulatory standards. • Identify and mitigate change risks while delivering initiatives on time and within budget.People • Coach and mentor leaders, Product Owners and peers on change management. • Support frontline change teams as a subject matter expert. • Conduct peer reviews and contribute to continuous improvement. Minimum Qualifications • Relevant Bachelor's Degree (or studying towards completion) in Humanities, Commerce or a related field. Experience Required • 5–8 years' experience delivering organisational and people change initiatives. • Proven experience supporting Enterprise HR products and services across people, process and systems. • Experience in digital business models and Agile transformations is advantageous. • Strong stakeholder engagement, facilitation and business partnering experience. • Demonstrated expertise in organisational change management and HR. • Proven experience designing and delivering strategic change communications. • Strong analytical, diagnostic and problem-solving skills. • Track record of delivering measurable business impact and sustainable behavioural change. Preferred • Agile mindset. • Agile/Scrum certifications (Certified Scrum Master, Certified Product Owner or SAFe SM/PO) are advantageous. • Experience facilitating executive workshops, design thinking sessions and organisational transformation initiatives. • Ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced environment while promoting collaboration, diversity and inclusion.
