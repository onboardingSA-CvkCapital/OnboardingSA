About the role

1. POSITION PURPOSE The Bookkeeper is responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date financial records of the company and ensuring that all accounting transactions are correctly captured, processed, reconciled and supported by appropriate documentation. The position requires a high level of accuracy, attention to detail, confidentiality and the ability to meet strict financial deadlines. 2. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Daily Accounting • Capture and process daily financial transactions accurately. • Process supplier invoices, credit notes and other accounting documentation. • Allocate payments and receipts to the correct accounts. • Ensure all transactions are correctly coded to the appropriate general ledger accounts. • Maintain accurate and complete accounting records. • Ensure supporting documentation is available for all transactions. Accounts Payable / Creditors • Capture supplier invoices and credit notes. • Reconcile supplier accounts to statements on a monthly basis. • Investigate and resolve discrepancies between supplier statements and the accounting system. • Ensure supplier statements are received and reconciled by the required deadline. • Follow up on outstanding invoices and account queries. • Ensure supplier accounts are accurately maintained. • Prepare creditor reconciliations for review and payment. Accounts Receivable / Debtors • Allocate customer payments accurately and timeously. • Assist with customer account reconciliations. • Investigate and resolve outstanding account discrepancies. • Follow up on unidentified receipts where required. • Assist with debtor reporting and collection activities. • Ensure customer accounts are maintained accurately. Banking • Capture and reconcile daily bank transactions. • Perform monthly bank reconciliations. • Investigate and resolve reconciling items. • Ensure all banking transactions are correctly allocated. • Monitor and follow up on unidentified deposits and payments. Cash and Petty Cash • Maintain accurate petty cash records. • Reconcile petty cash and supporting documentation. • Ensure all cash transactions are supported by appropriate documentation. • Assist with cash and banking reconciliations where required. General Ledger • Maintain accurate general ledger accounts. • Process journals and adjustments as authorised. • Perform account reconciliations. • Investigate unusual or incorrect transactions. • Ensure balance sheet accounts are properly supported and reconciled. Month-End • Complete all month-end processing within the required deadlines. • Prepare monthly reconciliations for review. • Ensure supplier statements and supporting documentation are included with reconciliations. • Assist with month-end journals and adjustments. • Identify and report discrepancies before month-end close. • Assist the Financial Manager/CFO with monthly financial reporting. Audit and Compliance • Maintain organised and complete accounting records. • Provide supporting documentation for internal and external audits. • Assist with audit queries and information requests. • Ensure accounting procedures and company policies are followed. Maintain confidentiality of all financial and company information
