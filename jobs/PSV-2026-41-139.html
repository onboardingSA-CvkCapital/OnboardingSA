About the role

The National Treasury is hiring a Chief Director: Strategic Procurement in Office of The Chief Procurement Officer, based in Pretoria. This is a permanent post, paying R1,554,696 a year. How to apply: To apply visit: https://erecruitment.treasury.gov.za/eRecruitment Reference number to quote: S122/2026 Closing date: 30 November 2026 at 12:00 pm (Midday) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Strategy and Policy Management: Design and develop national strategy and implementation plan for SCM Governance, Monitoring and Compliance framework.
  • Review policies as follows
  • Engage stakeholders on submissions of SCM reports, criteria, and schedules, assist with information in support of enforcement agencies' strategies, manage the SCM Restricted Suppliers List, and providing SCM case information updates to databases
  • Provide input into the development of SCM policy, norms, standards, frameworks and guidelines
  • Stakeholder Engagement
  • Engage stakeholders in the establishment of governance, monitoring and compliance strategies for improved SCM collaboration
  • Manage adherence of stakeholders on compliance to SCM policies, procedures, and regulations
  • Manage awareness sessions on SCM compliance with legislative documents with stakeholders
  • Monitoring and Evaluation
  • Assist with the report on Government Agencies and proposed enforcement processes
  • Assist with the management of the SCM Restricted Suppliers List in line with said guidelines
  • Manage the development of a monitoring and evaluation system on the performance of the SCM framework
  • Knowledge and Information Management
  • Assist with research initiatives on current trends, and good practices in the management of SCM
  • Manage the recommendation of solutions for SCM governance processes
  • Assist with the updating of the SCM governance, monitoring, and compliance of the Knowledge and Information Management platforms
  • Implementation of a generic state procurement system for all spheres of Government: Develop and implement a procurement regulatory environment that is responsive to the policy goals and objectives of government.
  • Influence government spending to contribute to sustainable economic, social, and environmental development.
  • Modernise state procurement by leveraging information technology.
  • Leverage the benefits of strategic sourcing and achieve savings on government spending.
  • Improve the performance of state procurement in respect of achieving ‘value for money’ and service delivery.
  • Improve the performance and efficacy of the state procurement system.
  • Develop the State Procurement system for Government: Improve the performance and efficacy of the state procurement system as it pertains to strategic procurement, including capital projects, infrastructure and resources.
  • Support the development of the state procurement system to address the development of small and medium enterprises (SMEs), green procurement and sustainable procurement practices.
  • Modernise state procurement by leveraging information technology as it pertains to strategic procurement.
  • Implement a national procurement strategy
  • Develop and implement a national procurement strategy to drive efficiencies and continuous improvement across government
  • Develop and implement a national strategic sourcing strategy and/or framework for critical and high-value procurement projects
  • Develop and implement a national strategic sourcing strategy for common goods and services
  • Perform commodity management
  • Client centricity, projects and risks management
  • Manage the client interface with National Treasury for all complex, critical, highvalue and/or high-risk procurement projects through their full project lifecycle
  • Manage a system for assessing, monitoring and evaluating complex, critical, high-value and/or high-risk procurement projects
  • Coordinate the governance and oversight of complex, critical, high-value and/or high-risk procurement projects
  • Manage national supply chain risks through risk monitoring, prediction and mitigation

Requirements

  • A Grade 12 is required, coupled with a minimum Bachelor’s Degree (equivalent to NQF level 7) in Supply Chain Management or Business Administration or Strategic Sourcing.
  • Registration with a relevant supply chain professional body will be an added advantage.
  • At least 5 years’ experience at a senior managerial level obtained in the development, implementation and management of large-scale public and/or private sector strategic procurement projects.
  • Knowledge and experience in strategic sourcing, contract administration, and business transformation.
  • Knowledge and experience in leveraged gains from strategic procurement within a public entity, multi-national corporation, or government department.
  • Knowledge and experience in the development, implementation and management of systems and processes pertaining to procurement.
