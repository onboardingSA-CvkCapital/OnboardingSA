About the role

Strand Warehouse Manager Are you an experienced, results-driven logistics leader with exceptional organizational skills and strong team management abilities? A well-established automotive logistics operation is looking for a proactive Warehouse Manager to lead and optimize their facility operations in a full-time position. If you thrive under pressure, take pride in driving operational efficiency and inventory accuracy, and excel at leading high-performing teams in a fast-paced environment, this is your opportunity to step into a key leadership role. What’s on Offer? - Salary: R15,000.00 – R20,000.00 Gross PM (Negotiable based on experience) - Status: Permanent following a successful 3-month probation period - Benefits (Post-Probation): MICFA Provident Fund (7.5% employee / 8% employer contribution) + Prorated December Annual Bonus - Leave: 18 days paid annual leave per annum - Hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 | Sat 08:00–13:00 (Overtime paid per MIBCO regulations) Key Responsibilities - Warehouse Operations Management: Plan, coordinate, and supervise daily warehouse workflows, ensuring smooth receiving, accurate put-away, fast picking/packing, and on-time dispatch. - Team Leadership & Performance: Direct, mentor, and manage warehouse staff (including Supervisors, Picker Packers, and Assistants); oversee shift scheduling, task delegation, and workplace discipline. - Inventory Control & Audit Compliance: Drive stock accuracy by overseeing regular cycle counts, full stocktakes, variance investigations, and WMS/ERP system updates. - Logistics & Supplier Coordination: Collaborate with transport providers and internal teams to optimize distribution, minimize delays, and ensure exceptional client satisfaction. - Housekeeping, Safety & Facilities: Enforce strict workplace health, safety (OHS), and housekeeping standards while ensuring facility equipment and forklifts are properly maintained. Are You the Perfect Fit? Minimum Requirements: - Essential Experience: Minimum 3–5 years of proven experience in a Warehouse Management or Senior Supervisory role (automotive, spare parts, or technical logistics experience is strictly preferred). - Skills & Education: Matric certificate required (Tertiary qualification in Logistics/Supply Chain is highly advantageous), advanced computer literacy, and proficiency in WMS/ERP software and MS Excel. - Logistics & Regulatory Knowledge: In-depth understanding of warehouse workflows, inventory principles, dispatch processes, and MIBCO regulations. - Track Record: Strong leadership capabilities, strategic problem-solving skills, high reliability, and a stable work history with contactable references. Strict Legal Requirements & Assessments (Non-Negotiable): Background screening will be conducted. Candidates must have: - South African-born Citizenship (with valid SA ID). - Clear criminal record (under no circumstances will candidates with a record be considered). - Ability to undergo a psychometric/personality assessment during the interview stage. How to Apply Ready to drive your career forward? Ensure you have the following documentation ready: - Detailed CV (with contactable references clearly showing your warehouse management and leadership experience). - ID Copy (Proof of SA-born citizenship). - Matric Certificate / Qualifications.
