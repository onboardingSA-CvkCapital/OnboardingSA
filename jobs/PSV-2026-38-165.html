About the role

The Department of Higher Education and Training is hiring a Provisioning Officer, based in Head Office. This is a permanent post, paying R338,106 – R398,277 a year. How to apply: Email Address: fpo28@emcol.co.za Reference number to quote: EMC1/28/26 Closing date: 06 November 2026 at 14H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as a secretariat to the Bid Evaluation Committee and Bid Adjudication Committee (includes obtaining approval).
  • Assist in the compilation of bid documents and submit specifications to the Bid Committee and finance department for budget purposes.
  • Prepare tender averts and receive submissions of expressions of interest.
  • Receive and open bid documents.
  • Arrange and coordinate tender briefing sessions and prepare attendance registers.
  • Compile a database of approved suppliers.
  • Source quotations from database according to the threshold values determined by the National Treasury.
  • Source quotations from accredited suppliers through College database.
  • Provide review of quotations, specifications and all documents supplied by the supplier to comply with the prescribed requirements.
  • Arrange and coordinate briefing sessions where necessary.
  • Implement procurement policies in line with relevant prescripts and legislation.
  • Generate purchase orders, submissions for approval and submission of purchase orders to the relevant parties.
  • Aid in the receipt of goods and services through the MIS and submission to Finance department.
  • Assist in the provision of an effective Contract Management service to the College.
  • Maintain proper relationship with suppliers within the code of ethics to ensure delivery of goods/services.
  • Provide logistical and disposal service.
  • Provide asset management service to the College.

Requirements

  • Matric or equivalent plus a recognized National Diploma/Degree in Supply Chain Management/Logistics/Public Management (NQF level 6).
  • 2 - 3 years’ experience in supply chain management or relevant environment.
  • Valid Driver’s License will be an added advantage.
  • Competencies:
  • Basic knowledge of supply chain policies, procedures and practices
  • Basic Knowledge and understanding of PFMA, Treasury regulations and applicable SCM prescripts
  • Knowledge of demand and acquisition will be added advantage
  • Knowledge and understating of legislative framework governing the Public Services
  • Knowledge of procedures in terms of the working environment
  • Knowledge of Coltech
  • Knowledge of Department of Higher Education mandate
  • Skills:
  • Planning and organizing
  • Report writing
  • Communication and interpersonal
  • Flexibility
  • Computer literacy
  • Customer care services
  • Teamwork
