About the role

About the Role We are seeking a Detail-Oriented Controller for our Vehicle Stock department at Ford Kempton Park. In this key role, you will focus on managing inventory processes, ensuring accurate record-keeping, and optimizing stock levels to support our operations and sales goals. Key Responsibilities - Oversee vehicle stock management processes, including receiving, tracking, and inventory control. - Ensure accurate documentation and reporting of vehicle stock levels and movements. - Collaborate with sales and procurement teams to forecast inventory needs effectively. - Conduct regular audits of vehicle stock to maintain accuracy and compliance with company standards. - Develop and implement efficient inventory management practices to optimize vehicle availability. - Provide insights and analysis on stock-related issues to senior management. - Lead and train the vehicle stock team to uphold high standards of accuracy and efficiency. - Coordinate with logistics partners to ensure timely delivery and receipt of vehicular inventory. Requirements - Demonstrable experience in inventory management or stock control, preferably within the automotive industry. - Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret data and produce reports. - Excellent organizational skills to manage multiple tasks and priorities effectively. - Proficient in using inventory management software and Microsoft Office Suite. - A detail-oriented mindset and a commitment to maintaining high levels of accuracy. Preferred Qualifications - Relevant qualifications in supply chain management, logistics, or a related field. - Experience with automotive stock management systems will be an advantage. - Familiarity with regulatory requirements related to vehicle inventory management. - Certification in project management or inventory control is a plus.
