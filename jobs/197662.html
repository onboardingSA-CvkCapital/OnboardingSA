About the role

Main Purpose The Project Management Intern – Operations provides hands-on support to the successful delivery of operational projects across the retail business. The role focuses on project coordination, operational reporting, administration, executive support, and process improvement to ensure initiatives are delivered efficiently and support business objectives. This internship is designed as a learning and development opportunity that builds foundational project management, operational coordination, and executive administration skills while providing exposure to cross-functional collaboration within a fast-paced retail environment.
