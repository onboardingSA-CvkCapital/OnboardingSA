About the role

At Cell C, we are not just a telecommunications company; we are a people-centric and consumer-focused organization committed to delivering exceptional experiences to our customers. In line with our dedication to customer-centricity, we are seeking a seasoned professional as a Principal Specialist: Cell C Foundation & Social Impact to join a dynamic team of #Unstoppables. Job Purpose To position Cell C as a Responsible, Inclusive Digital Ally by leading the Cell C Foundation as the primary delivery and governance vehicle for the Group's Social Impact agenda, advancing the Social ("S") component of Cell C's ESG strategy through credible, measurable, national-scale programmes. The role drives long-term, sustainable social impact focused on youth empowerment, digital inclusion, entrepreneurship, education, and gender-based violence (GBV) response. It ensures alignment with Cell C's brand promise, post-listing narrative, and the Group's strategic pillars, while operationalising social outcomes as inputs to enterprise value creation, licence to operate, and stakeholder trust. Note on scope: This role is not accountable for Cell C's overall ESG or Sustainability strategy (owned by the Chief Strategy Officer / EXCO Sustainability Sponsor). It is accountable for the credible design, delivery, measurement and reporting of the Social component of ESG, and for interlocking Foundation activity with the enterprise ESG scorecard, Social & Ethics Committee, and Integrated Reporting cycle.
