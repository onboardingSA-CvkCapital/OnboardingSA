About the role

Dis-Chem Pharmacies’ in Midrand has an opportunity available for a Project Coordinator to join their Head Office team. The Project Coordinator coordinates and delivers strategic and operational People & Culture projects, taking ownership of assigned workstreams and ensuring outcomes are achieved on time, within scope and budget, and to the required quality standards. Through disciplined planning, governance, reporting, stakeholder engagement, risk management, data analysis and change support, the role advances employee experience, organisational effectiveness, compliance and the organisation’s strategic People & Culture priorities.
