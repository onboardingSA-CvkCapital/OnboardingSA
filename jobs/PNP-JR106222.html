About the role

Pick n Pay is hiring a Retail Media Manager, based in Kenilworth, Cape Town. Experience coordinating initiatives through legal, compliance, data privacy, procurement or governance processes Experience working in a complex corporate environment with internal teams and external delivery partners

Responsibilities

  • Retail Media Operations and Workstream Delivery
  • Manage multiple retail media workstreams across in-store, onsite and offsite channels, ensuring that scope, owners, dependencies, decisions and delivery dates are clear.
  • Drive workstreams from brief or business requirement through planning, approval, implementation and close-out.
  • Identify blockers early, coordinate the relevant stakeholders and maintain momentum until issues are resolved.
  • Prioritise activity according to customer, commercial, operational and delivery impact, escalating material risks with clear recommendations.
  • Maintain practical workstream plans and status reporting.
  • Brand and Agency Stakeholder Management
  • Act as a key operational contact for brand and agency stakeholders across assigned campaigns and retail media initiatives.
  • Clarify requirements, responsibilities, timelines, inputs and approval expectations at the outset of each workstream.
  • Manage stakeholder expectations proactively and communicate risks, changes and decisions clearly.
  • Build credible working relationships while holding internal and external stakeholders accountable for agreed deliverables.
  • In-store and Commercial Coordination
  • Coordinate with Commercial, Marketing, Stores and operational teams on retail media activity that depends on supplier context, promotional plans, product availability, store implementation or category priorities.
  • Support the translation of commercial requirements into practical and deliverable retail media activity.
  • Assess in-store feasibility, lead times and operational dependencies with the relevant business owners.
  • Apply an understanding of Pick n Pay's Commercial environment to navigate stakeholders and resolve delivery issues effectively.
  • Digital Retail Media and Network Operations
  • Support the day-to-day administration and operational coordination of onsite and offsite retail media activity.
  • Work across eCommerce, Product, Marketing, Data, Technology, media platforms and delivery partners to coordinate access, inventory, data inputs, campaign setup, measurement and reporting dependencies.
  • Use a working awareness of supply-side digital media and retail media network operations to identify dependencies, clarify ownership and coordinate the relevant specialist stakeholders.
  • Support the implementation and operational readiness of new digital retail media products, capabilities and processes.
  • Legal, Compliance and Governance
  • Coordinate retail media workstreams through the appropriate legal, data privacy, brand, customer, procurement and compliance review processes, working with the relevant subject-matter owners.
  • Ensure that required documentation, business context, decisions and approvals are obtained and recorded before implementation.
  • Connect stakeholders with the appropriate subject-matter owners and follow actions through to resolution.
  • Help establish workable governance, approval paths and repeatable processes that reduce delays without bypassing required controls.
  • Continuous Improvement
  • Identify recurring delivery friction and implement practical improvements to roles, handovers, templates, workflows and escalation routes.
  • Document agreed ways of working and encourage consistent adoption across internal teams, brands, agencies and delivery partners.
  • Contribute to the development of a scalable and well-governed Pick n Pay Retail Media operating model.

Requirements

  • Competencies
  • Core strengths required
  • Strong workstream management, prioritisation and follow-through across a high volume of parallel activity
  • Strong brand and agency stakeholder management, including expectation setting, delivery coordination and constructive escalation
  • Structured problem solving and the ability to identify and resolve blockers quickly
  • Confident cross-functional influence without direct authority
  • High personal ownership, accountability and the judgement to act independently
  • Ability to work at pace across competing priorities and unfamiliar domains without requiring close supervision
  • Clear, concise and dependable communication
  • Working knowledge required
  • Understanding of in-store retail media formats, execution requirements and operational dependencies
  • Understanding of onsite retail media, including search, display and sponsored product environments
  • Understanding of offsite retail media activation, audiences, platforms and measurement principles
  • Ability to understand campaign and workstream requirements sufficiently to coordinate the appropriate specialist, commercial, operational and technical stakeholders
  • Knowledge and exposure that would be advantageous
  • Retail Commercial processes, stakeholder environment and supplier context, or comparable retail commercial experience
  • Supply-side digital media, ad operations and retail media network administration
  • Legal, compliance, data privacy, procurement and governance processes relevant to retail media
  • Experience translating between commercial, operational, legal and technical stakeholders
