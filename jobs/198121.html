About the role

At Frogfoot, we are in search of a Commercial Analyst to join our Product team. Purpose of the Role The Commercial Analyst is responsible for providing commercial insight that enables Frogfoot Fibre Networks to make informed strategic and operational decisions across its fibre portfolio. The role combines financial analysis, product performance analysis, pricing strategy, market intelligence, and commercial modelling to maximise profitability and sustainable network growth. The incumbent will partner with Product, Sales, Finance, Network Planning, Marketing, and Executive Management to evaluate existing products, identify new market opportunities, and develop business cases supporting investments in FTTH, FTTB, and FTTT initiatives. This role serves as the analytical engine behind commercial decision-making and product optimisation.
