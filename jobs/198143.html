About the role

Manager:  Alumni and Stakeholder Relations (Grade 16) Advancement and Communication Division The Advancement and Communication Division invites suitably qualified candidates to join their team. Main Objectives The key role of the Manager, Alumni and Stakeholder Relations, is to manage and strengthen mutually beneficial relationships between the University and its alumni, both locally and internationally, as well as with other strategic stakeholders, in support of the University’s Communication and Advancement strategy. The incumbent is responsible for driving sustainable alum engagement programmes and developing integrated stakeholder engagement strategies informed by ongoing research and insights into alum needs, interests, and institutional priorities. The role focuses on building, nurturing, and sustaining meaningful relationships across diverse alumni and stakeholder groupings, while creating opportunities and platforms for alumni to connect and reconnect with one another and with their alma mater. Working closely with the Fundraising function, and Communications and Media Relations, the role also contributes to cultivating a culture of giving among alumni and strengthening alum participation in advancing the University’s strategic objectives. In addition, the position seeks to foster connections between alums, prospective students, and current students, creating visible role models and strengthening lifelong engagement with the University. The Requirements A relevant B Degree (NQF level 7) plus approximately 7 years’ relevant experience, where such experience includes: - ·       Previous experience in alums and stakeholder relationship management, and/or be able to demonstrate a track record of success in relationship-building skills, familiarity with working to mobilise constituent groups. ·       Demonstrable success in running projects which are aimed at achieving support for philanthropic programmes. ·       Previous experience in a position where work plans may extend over several years and where a high degree of persuasiveness is seen as key to success. ·       At least 3 years’ management experience. ·       Excellent communication (written), presentation, networking and negotiation skills working with diverse stakeholders. ·       Demonstrable creative and conceptual ability with a focus on continuous innovation Application Process It is essential that prospective candidates read the Job profile for further information and ensure that all relevant documentation is submitted.
