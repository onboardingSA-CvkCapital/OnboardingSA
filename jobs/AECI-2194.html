About the role

AECI is hiring a Plant Operator, based in Boksburg. AECI Plant Health is seeking a dedicated and safety‑focused Plant Operator to join the Synthesis Plant team based in Lilianton. This role is ideal for an individual who thrives in a manufacturing environment, has strong attention to detail, and is committed to upholding high standards of quality, efficiency, and workplace safety. The successful incumbent will be responsible for the safe and efficient manufacture of in-specification products, ensuring compliance with production, quality, safety, health, environmental and operational standards. This role forms part of a 24-hour shift operation that runs throughout the year and requires a highly motivated individual with strong technical aptitude and leadership potential.

Responsibilities

  • Raw Materials Handling
  • The Plant Operator prepares job cards and verifies that the correct raw materials are received.
  • The role requires coordination with Raw Materials, Planning, and other departments to ensure smooth production processes.
  • Plant Operations
  • The Plant Operator ensures vessels and pipes are properly cleaned to prevent cross‑contamination.
  • They follow all Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and work instructions.
  • Equipment such as mixers, pumps, stirrers, and mills is operated in accordance with guidelines.
  • The individual completes log sheets, collects product samples, and ensures quality standards are met.
  • Will be required to drive a forklift and should be medically fit to operate a forklift and maintain annual licence requirements.
  • The operator maintains a clean, safe, and compliant working environment and assists with stock takes, problem‑solving, and additional tasks as needed.
  • Maintenance
  • The Plant Operator ensures that machinery and equipment under their responsibility are safe and well‑maintained.
  • The role involves identifying and maintaining critical spare parts, reporting breakdowns, and supporting maintenance teams.
  • Preventative maintenance activities and supervision of contractors may also be required.
  • Sheq:
  • Adhere to all Safety, Health, Environmental and Quality standards and procedures.
  • Identify and report incidents, hazards, near misses and process safety incidents.
  • Maintain high housekeeping standards and ensure safe working conditions.
  • Handle hazardous, flammable and toxic materials in accordance with site procedures.
  • R&D Support
  • Assist with the production of trial batches and new product development activities when required.

Requirements

  • Qualifications & Experience:
  • Minimum Grade 12.
  • Requires a diploma in Chemical engineering and chemical plant operations.
  • A minimum of 2 years’ relevant experience in a Chemical plant or manufacturing environment.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Will be required to drive a forklift and should be medically fit to operate a forklift and maintain annual licence requirements.
  • Extensive computer literacy and proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
  • Ability to work independently as well as within a team environment.
  • Understanding of SHEQ standards and requirements.
  • Ability to work a continuous 24-hour shift rotation.
  • Key Competencies:
  • Safety Orientation
  • Attention to Detail
  • Excellent Communication Skills
  • Planning and Organising
  • Following Instructions and Procedures
  • Coping with Pressure and Setbacks
  • Adapting and Responding to Change
  • Applying Expertise and Technical Knowledge
  • Adhering to Principles and Values
  • Strong Learning Agility
