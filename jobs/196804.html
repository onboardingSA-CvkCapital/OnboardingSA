About the role

Inpower, a specialist energy division within the Faircape Group, delivers integrated power and renewable energy solutions to commercial, industrial, and residential clients across the Western Cape. Our services include solar PV systems, battery storage, generators, power monitoring, and utilities management. We are seeking an Electrical Engineer or Engineering Technologist with a strong interest in the renewable energy sector to support our solar sales and business development function. This opportunity is well suited to an early-career professional looking to build their technical and commercial expertise while working alongside experienced engineers in a growing, future-focused environment. The successful candidate will support the Senior Sales Engineer by assisting with client research, market analysis, preliminary technical information gathering, designing and early-stage project feasibility inputs Key Responsibilities • Research and identify potential clients for solar PV installations across the Western Cape • Conduct market research to identify opportunities in commercial, industrial, and residential sectors • Gather and analyse relevant technical, site, and client information to support solar proposals • Assist the Senior Sales Engineer with lead qualification and preparation of preliminary project data • Maintain accurate records of potential clients, leads, and market insights • Engage with prospective clients to understand energy usage, needs, and requirements • Stay informed on renewable energy technologies, solar industry trends, and regulatory developments • Support site assessments and basic feasibility evaluations where required • Collaborate with internal engineering and operations teams as part of the project support process Minimum Requirements Qualifications • BEng / BSc / BTech in Electrical Engineering or Mechatronics •
