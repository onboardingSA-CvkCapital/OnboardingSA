About the role

The Department of Public Works and Infrastructure is hiring a Director: Risk Management, based in Head Office (Pretoria). This is a permanent post, paying R1,317,384 a year. How to apply: Email Applications for the position of Director: Risk Management must be submitted to: Recruitment26-88@dpw.gov.za Reference number to quote: 2026/376 Closing date: 20 November 2026 at 16H00 Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the development of risk management strategies, systems (methodologies, models and tools etc.), policies and annual risk management plan-: Undertake research on the latest development of policies, professional guideline and standards.
  • Ensure that all policies, guidelines and standards developed are aligned to the Departments strategic objective.
  • Provide information on risk management policies, methods and practices.
  • Manage and develop the Department’s business risk profile.
  • Ensure that risk management philosophy and culture is embedded in the organisation.
  • Monitor and ensure the implementation and continuous adherence to professional guideline and standards.
  • Oversee the management and implementation of the risk management-: Undertake studies and analysis for identifying risks to establish the internal and external organisation context.
  • Ensure development of risk management controls and contingency plans in the Department.
  • Manage the risk management assessment process.
  • Lead the implementation of risk strategies in conjunction.
  • Ensure provision of risk management advisory services and participate in relevant internal and external structures.
  • Manage the administration of risk analysis projects.
  • Manage, monitor and review the identified risk response activities.
  • Manage the development of risk profile (ensure that the risk register is maintained).
  • Prepare reports on implementation and progress of risk strategies.
  • Manage and undertake capacity development (inform, guide and advise employees on risk management matters).
  • Provide effective management of business framework-
  • Manage the development of analysis work
  • Design business cases for business process management
  • Ensure prioritisation and evaluation of business analysis projects, Administer business analysis programmes
  • Provide inputs to change management projects
  • Compile reports on the management of business architecture for senior management
  • Ensure collection and assessment of information regarding implementation of risk strategies and provide information to Strategic Management-: Conduct risk analyses on risk information.
  • Evaluate and prioritise risk analysis projects.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of integration of risk management within the organisation.
  • Ensure that risks attached to business processes are addressed in conjunction with business analysis.
  • Provide inputs with risk information.
  • Explore and suggest solutions to identified risks.
  • Provide inputs to change management components.
  • Manage the Directorate: Establish and maintain appropriate internal controls and reporting systems in order to meet performance expectations, Develop and manage the operational plan of the Directorate and report on progress as required, Manage performance and development of employees, Establish, implement and maintain efficient and effective communication arrangements, Compile and submit all required administrative reports, Quality control of work delivered by employees, Monitor the budget and expenditures for the Directorate.

Requirements

  • A minimum qualification at NQF level 7 as recognised by the South African Qualifications Authority (SAQA) in Risk Management/Public Administration/Financial Management or related field.
  • A minimum 5 years of experience at a middle/senior managerial level in the relevant field.
  • Knowledge of Risk management framework, business planning processes, applicable legislation, norms and standards related to the built environment industry, including the Public Finance Management Act, Treasury Instructions, Public Service Act, Public Service Regulations and the Minimum Information Security Standards (MISS) Act, functioning of national, provincial and local government, fundamental economics, structure and functioning of the Department, Parliamentary protocol processes, linkages with government clusters, Departmental standards and regulations.
