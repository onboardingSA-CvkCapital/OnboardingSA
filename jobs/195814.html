About the role

Role Overview The SDLC / Software Architect is responsible for defining, governing, and evolving software architecture and delivery practices across client engagements. Operating at the intersection of business needs and engineering execution, the role ensures scalable, maintainable, and high-quality software solutions. The position influences delivery teams, pre-sales engagements, and internal capability development, focusing on architectural leadership, SDLC optimisation, and enabling predictable delivery outcomes. Key Responsibilities Responsibility 1: SDLC & Architecture Ownership • Define and own end-to-end SDLC approaches aligned to client and business needs • Design and govern software architectures for scalability, security, and performance • Establish architectural principles, standards, and delivery guardrails • Lead system assessments and modernisation initiatives • Maintain clear architectural documentation (e.g., ADRs, system diagrams) Responsibility 2: Client Delivery Enablement • Act as a trusted advisor to clients and delivery teams • Guide complex technical decisions and trade-offs • Support predictable delivery across the SDLC • Assist with incident analysis and continuous improvement • Align technical execution with business strategy Responsibility 3: Pre-Sales & Solution Design • Support sales teams with architecture and solution input • Contribute to proposals, RFPs, and Statements of Work • Participate in client engagements to provide technical credibility • Assist with estimation and risk assessment • Articulate long-term value, cost, and risk implications of design decisions Responsibility 4: Technology & Delivery Leadership • Champion best practices in software design, testing, and delivery • Promote adoption of tools, frameworks, and modern delivery patterns • Identify and address architectural anti-patterns • Collaborate with leadership to improve delivery standards • Translate industry trends into practical, business-relevant solutions Responsibility 5: Human Capital Development • Mentor engineers, technical leads, and aspiring architects • Encourage structured thinking and architectural maturity • Support recruitment, assessments, and interviews • Contribute to competency frameworks and career development models • Provide technical input into performance management Requirements Essential Skills & Experience • Strong experience in software architecture and system design • Proven experience across the full Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) • Expertise in distributed systems and integration patterns • Solid understanding of DevOps, CI/CD, and modern delivery practices • Ability to influence technical direction across teams and stakeholders Desirable Skills & Experience • Experience in consulting or client-facing environments • Exposure to multi-system, complex architecture landscapes • Familiarity with architecture documentation frameworks (e.g., C4, ADRs) • Experience contributing to pre-sales or solution design initiatives Qualifications • Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or related field • Equivalent practical experience considered Behavioural Competencies • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills • Systems thinking and structured problem-solving ability • Leadership through influence rather than authority • Calm, pragmatic, and decisive under pressure • High emotional intelligence and adaptability • Strong ownership and accountability What We Offer • A senior, high-impact role shaping architecture and delivery quality • Exposure to diverse clients, industries, and technical challenges • Strong autonomy with executive support • Continuous professional development and growth opportunities • Flexibility to shape your role in line with business strategy • A collaborative culture focused on engineering excellence and long-term value
