Purpose of the role The Reconciliation Clerk will report to the Group Reporting Manager. You’ll keep a close eye on Unifi’s bank accounts, mobile wallets and cash over multiple jurisdictions, making sure everything adds up and nothing slips through the cracks. You’ll reconcile accounts, dig into anything that doesn’t look right, and work with our teams to get issues sorted quickly.You care about getting the details right, ask questions, stay curious and aren’t afraid to get stuck in.
