About the role

Growth Executive - Card Acquiring Sales Join Lesaka's High-Performance Growth Team Are you a driven sales professional who thrives on winning new business, building relationships, and closing deals? At Lesaka, we're transforming the way South African businesses accept payments. As a Growth Executive (Hunter), you'll play a pivotal role in expanding our merchant footprint by introducing innovative payment solutions that help businesses grow. This is more than a sales role it's an opportunity to build your own territory, develop long-term client partnerships, and directly influence Lesaka's growth while accelerating your own career. If you're energised by hunting for new business, enjoy being in the field, and want to be rewarded for delivering results, we'd like to meet you. About the role As a Growth Executive, you will be responsible for identifying, acquiring, activating, and growing new merchants across Lesaka's Card Acquiring portfolio. You will own the full sales cycle from prospecting and presenting solutions to closing deals, ensuring successful installation, and driving ongoing merchant engagement. Beyond acquiring merchants, you'll identify opportunities to cross-sell and upsell additional Lesaka solutions, including cash management and lending products, creating long-term value for both your customers and the business. Success in this role requires resilience, commercial acumen, strong relationship-building skills, and the ability to consistently deliver against ambitious sales targets. Because this is a field-based position, you must be comfortable travelling extensively within your allocated region and managing your territory independently. What You'll Be doing Win New Business • Identify, prospect and secure new merchant opportunities. • Build a strong sales pipeline through networking, referrals, cold calling and qualified leads. • Present Lesaka's market-leading Card Acquiring solutions and tailor recommendations to customer needs. • Convert opportunities into signed agreements, successful installations and active trading merchants. • Deliver and exceed monthly and quarterly sales targets. Grow and Develop Your Merchant Portfolio • Ensure merchants are successfully onboarded and actively transacting. • Drive merchant engagement to increase transaction volumes and long-term value. • Identify opportunities to expand existing merchants through additional payment terminals and value-added solutions. • Cross-sell complementary Lesaka products including cash management and lending solutions. Own Your Territory • Manage your region like your own business. • Prioritise high-value market segments including retail, supermarkets, hardware, fuel, hospitality and general trade. • Build lasting customer relationships that drive loyalty and retention. • Stay ahead of competitor activity and market opportunities. Use Data to Drive Performance • Maintain accurate CRM records and sales pipeline management. • Monitor conversion rates, merchant activation, transaction growth and portfolio performance. • Provide regular market intelligence and customer feedback to support business strategy. Continue to Grow • Stay informed on payment technology, fintech and merchant industry trends. • Participate in ongoing sales and product training. • Share best practices and contribute to a collaborative, high-performing sales culture. What Success looks like You'll be recognised for: • Consistently exceeding new business targets. • Building a high-quality merchant portfolio. • Driving merchant activation and transaction growth. • Increasing Average Revenue Per User (ARPU). • Growing terminal penetration within existing customers. • Maintaining a healthy sales pipeline and excellent CRM discipline. • Delivering an exceptional merchant experience. What You'll Bring Experience • Minimum 3 years' experience in external sales, business development or merchant acquisition. • Proven track record of consistently achieving or exceeding sales targets. • Experience in card acquiring, fintech, financial services or payments will be highly advantageous. • Strong prospecting, negotiation and closing skills. • Experience managing a sales territory independently. Qualifications • Matric (Grade 12) is essential. • A qualification in Business, Commerce, Sales or Marketing would be advantageous. • Valid driver's licence. • Reliable personal vehicle. • Willingness to travel extensively within your allocated region.What Makes you Successful You are someone who: • Loves hunting for new business and thrives on winning. • Is self-motivated and highly accountable. • Builds trust quickly and develops long-term customer relationships. • Thinks commercially and identifies growth opportunities. • Uses data to improve performance. • Is resilient, adaptable and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment. • Takes ownership of results and follows through on commitments. Key Performance Indicators Leading Indicators • Secured appointments and merchant engagements. • Lead-to-sale conversion rate. • Speed of merchant activation following onboarding. • Additional terminal placements within existing merchants. Lagging Indicators • Monthly transaction revenue growth (ARPU). • Achievement of quarterly new business targets. • Merchant activation and retention. • CRM accuracy and reporting discipline. Living the Lesaka Values Everything we do is guided by our values: • Entrepreneurial Spirit – We create opportunities and make things happen. • Integrity – We earn trust through honesty, transparency and accountability. • Collective Wisdom – We succeed through collaboration and shared knowledge. • Ownership – We take responsibility for our actions, our customers and our results. Why Join Lesaka At Lesaka, you'll join one of South Africa's leading fintech organisations, where innovation, ambition and customer success drive everything we do. You'll have the opportunity to: • Build a rewarding career within a growing fintech business. • Work with industry-leading payment solutions. • Be empowered to manage and grow your own territory. • Make a real impact by helping businesses succeed. • Be recognised and rewarded for exceptional performance.If you're passionate about sales, enjoy being on the road, and are motivated by exceeding targets while making a meaningful impact, this is your opportunity to grow with Lesaka.
