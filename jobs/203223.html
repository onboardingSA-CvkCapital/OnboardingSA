About the role

Build your career behind biscuits South Africans know and love. We’re looking for a passionate Human Resources Officer to join our NBL Isando Biscuits in Kempton Park, Gauteng. In this role, you will partner with and advise the business unit’s Line Managers to enable and improve line capacity and effectiveness about the following HR services: HR Projects, Learning and Development, Employee Wellness, Performance Management, Recruiting and Staffing, Talent Management and OD, and HR Reporting, Statistics and Compliance.
