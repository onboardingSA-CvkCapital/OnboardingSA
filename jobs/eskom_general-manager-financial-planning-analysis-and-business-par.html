About the role

To provide strategic leadership and financial stewardship by delivering insightful planning, forecasting, performance management, and business partnering that enables informed executive decision-making, enhances organisational performance, and creates sustainable long-term value. Drive a value-focused planning and performance culture by aligning financial strategy, business priorities and operational execution across all NTCSA business units.
