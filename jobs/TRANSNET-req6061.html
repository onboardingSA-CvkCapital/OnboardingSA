About the role

Transnet (Transnet Rail Infrastructure Manager (TRIM)) is hiring a Master Electrician, based in Worcester. The role reports to the Technical Supervisor. Grade: H3. To ensure reliable operational service of Electrical Sub-stations for train operations and transmission distribution and Traction systems. Under general supervision inspect, test, fault finding, repairs and maintain substations traction power supplies, overhead track equipment and transmission lines (all types) to ensure availability and efficient supply of electric power and support for the continuous operations of the company’s electric substation and signalling within company for the running of trains and other operational requirements in accordance with sound knowledge of substation circuitry, safety and business standards.

Responsibilities

  • 1.
  • Responsible for finding and investigating electrical faults within the substation and signal supply points and fixing them after thorough investigation has been conducted based on the approved maintenance program received from the Supervisor or instruction received from the centralised electrical control office Service and maintain high voltage circuit breakers, minimum and bulk oil, SF6 gas and vacuum breakers to detect malfunctioning and any leakages/damages and determine resistance and load for appropriate action plan
  • 2.
  • Service and maintain transformers (main and auxiliary) to detect malfunctioning and any leakages/damages and determine if they are in good working condition and decide whether immediate or pre-planned action is required
  • 3.
  • Check earthing of current and voltage transformers and the block jointing of secondary side cables to ensure that they are in good working condition by performing the following activities
  • 4.
  • Service and maintain 3kV DC silicon diode rectifier and wave filter equipment to detect malfunctioning and any leakages/damages and determine resistance and load for appropriate action plan
  • 5.
  • Examine the 3Kv DC Busbar and the under-voltage relay to determine any earth leakage circuits and ensure that under-voltage relay drop out and pick up operations are correct
  • 6.
  • Service and maintain high speed circuit breakers to ensure that they are in good condition and repair any detected failures and malfunctions in accordance with sound knowledge of substation circuitry, safety, and business standards
  • 7.
  • Service and maintain batteries and battery chargers to ensure that they are in good working condition by checking connection for tightness and adjust them accordingly where applicable and boost charge current in accordance with safety standards and battery ampere hour capacity
  • 8.
  • Ensure that the equipment is regenerated by obtaining reading from the PLC microprocessor, analyses and rectifies faults
  • 9.
  • Responsible for the safe storage and control of equipment and material kept on his/her vehicle
  • 10.
  • Carries out good housekeeping to ensure the working area, machinery and tools are kept clean and tidy after all repairs and maintenance work has been completed
  • 11.
  • Perform general administration related to the job by compiling and submitting reports to the Technical Supervisor for work performed and other related matters
  • 12.
  • Carries out pre-trip inspection on equipment, road vehicles, road-rail vehicles, employee protective equipment and lifting equipment to ensure that they are in good condition and usable
  • 13.
  • Attend to emergency callouts on breakdowns and any equipment failures
  • 14.
  • 25kv maintenance of outdoor and Vacuum Circuit Breakers ,11KV OCB and fault finding on faulty breakers
  • 15.
  • Seal leaking and sweating Transformers
  • 16.
  • Be able to adjust tap changers on the transformer as per required voltages
  • 17.
  • Trouble shooting on telecontrol equipment
  • 18.
  • Stringing of substation flying busbars
  • 19.
  • Installation of 11kv and 25kv Auxiliary Transformers
  • 20.
  • High Voltage and medium voltage joining and termination
