About the role

The Department of Employment and Labour is hiring an Assistant Director: UI Operations, based in Provincial Office Limpopo. This is a contract post, paying R605,742 a year. (Three (3) year contract) How to apply: Chief Director: Provincial Operations: Private Bag X9368, Polokwane, 0700 Or hand deliver at 42a Schoeman Street, Polokwane or UIFJobs- LP@labour.gov.za Reference number to quote: HR4/4/6/48 Closing date: 16 October 2026 at 16:00 (walk-in) and 23:59 (online) Use the new Z83 form and attach a detailed CV.

Responsibilities

  • Facilitate the employer services function in the province.
  • Coordinate the provision of assessment, validation and adjudication of claims.
  • Facilitate registry services in the province.
  • Coordinate local appeals and complaints in the province.
  • Coordinate general support.
  • Manage resources in the unit.

Requirements

  • Undergraduate qualification in Operations Management/Public Management or Administration, Business Administration or Management/Administrative Management/Management/Financial Accounting/Finance at NQF Level 6 as recognised by SAQA.
  • 4 years’ experience of which 2 years must be functional experience in Operations environment, and 2 years must be at a supervisory experience.
  • Valid driver’s License.
  • Knowledge:
  • Unemployment Insurance Act (UIA)
  • Unemployment Insurance Contributions Act (UICA)
  • Treasury Regulations
  • Public Service Act (PSA)
  • Public Service Regulation (PSR)
  • Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA)
  • Public Finance Management Act (PFMA)
  • Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)
  • Skills:
  • Computer
  • Report writing
  • People management
  • Time Management
  • Communication (both verbal and written)
  • Customer Relations
  • Supervisory skills
  • Coaching skills
