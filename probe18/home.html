Valterra Platinum is one of the world’s leading integrated producers of platinum group metals (PGMs). We are always guided by our values and our purpose of unearthing value to better our world.

Through our world-class portfolio of mining and processing operations in South Africa and Zimbabwe, we mine the metals that make modern life possible.

Our PGMs and their associated co-products power critical industries and contribute to the green technologies of the future. We undertake our work safely and responsibly, caring for our people, our communities, our broader stakeholders and the environment at every step. In this way, sustainability is integrated into everything that we do.
