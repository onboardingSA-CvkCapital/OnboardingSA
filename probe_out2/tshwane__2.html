About the City of Tshwane Metropolitan Municipality:



The City of Tshwane is a leading metropolitan municipality in South Africa, committed to driving economic growth, delivering efficient services, and fostering a sustainable, inclusive capital city. Its investment in innovative people solutions is central to its vision of a prosperous future for all residents.
