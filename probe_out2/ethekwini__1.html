E-Recruitment
Attracting highly-skilled people who will add value to the organization.

DIGITALLY YOU E-RECRUITMENT APPLICATION PORTAL

APPLICATION FORM

In line with the concept of Smart Cities, eThekwini Municipality has established 15 Centre’s of Excellence. The computer labs offer a host of services which include: free Wi-Fi enabling technology which can be used to apply for jobs via the City’s E-Recruitment portal. 

These facilities are fully functional and equipped with computers and free Wi-Fi which is made available to both Council staff and the public.

Jobseekers can view online vacancies from different organizations, create email addresses/user profiles/curriculum vitae. And, apply for bursaries/online National Student Financial Aid Schemes (NSFAS)/Central Applications Office/Tertiary Institutions. Scholars can use the facility for school projects if the Centre’s are not occupied.
