Generic appointment requirements applicable to all posts:
   •   Applicants must display competency in the post specific functions of the post;
   •   Be in possession of a Senior Certificate (Grade 12);
   •   Be proficient in at least two official languages, of which one must be English;
   •   Must be a citizen of the Republic of South Africa of which documentary proof must be
       furnished during the interview;
   •   Be in possession of at least a valid light motor vehicle driver’s licence;
   •   Must have no previous convictions or pending criminal/departmental or civil cases;
   •   applicants will be subjected to a vetting process which will include security screening and
       fingerprint verification. Must not have tattoo/s marks which will be visible when wearing any
       uniform of the Service.
   •   Applicants must be willing to work under pressure as well as work extended hours.
   •   Applicants shall be subjected to an interview and evaluation process.

Additional Post Requirements:
  • Applicants must display competency in the post-specific functions of the post. Applicants
       must be in possession of an applicable three (3) year Diploma / Degree (NQF level 6 or
       higher as recognized by SAQA) in Public Administration/ Public Management /
       Public Policy and Management, Archives and Records Management, Library and
       Information Science recorded on the National Learner Record Database (NLRD) as
       recognized by SAQA).

Core Functions:
   • Receipt, registration and coordination of requests for the development of new and the
      review of existing official directives.
   • Maintain the administration of the Master Record Index and filling of consolidation notices,
      official directives and approval notes
   • Locate, file and update approved hard copies of official directives, approval notes and
      consolidation notices.
   • Attend to enquiries relating to official directives.
   • Compile consolidation notices and ensure version control of approved official directives
      (National Instructions, Policies, Standard Operating Procedures, Standing Orders and
      Delegation of Powers). (NLRD).
