experience, stake holder


APPOINTMENT REQUIREMENTS
Applicants:
-Must have at least a three (3) year Degree or Diploma (accredited by SAQA on NQF
level 6 or higher) in Strategic Management, Public Administration, Public Management,
Governance, Public Policy, Political Science, Development Studies, Monitoring and
Evaluation, Policing, or another qualification relevant to the core functions of the post;
A relevant postgraduate qualification in Strategic Management, Governance, Public
Administration, Public Policy, Monitoring and Evaluation, or a related field will be an
added advantage;
-Successful completion of a Middle Management Learning Programme, Leadership and
Management Development Programme, Programme and Project Management training, or
other relevant management and leadership programmes will be an added advantage;
Possess proven management experience gained at national and/or provincial government
level in a government department, constitutional institution, public entity or other public-
sector environment;
-Demonstrate proven experience in a government or public-sector environment where
functions are performed in accordance with prescribed legislative, regulatory and policy
frameworks;
-Have proven experience in coordinating strategic planning, performance management,
governance, reporting, oversight, parliamentary, executive support or related functions
within a government environment; and;
-Must have a minimum period of three years’ appropriate managerial experience.

CORE FUNCTIONS:
-To manage and coordinate the development, review, approval and tabling of the SAPS
Strategic Plan, Annual Performance Plan (APP), Technical Indicator Descriptions (TIDs)
and related planning instruments.
-Coordinating the development, analysis and quality assurance of Annual Operational
Plans (AOPs) and other strategic planning deliverables across SAPS business units.
-Managing strategic planning processes, including environmental analyses, stakeholder
consultations and the integration of organisational priorities into departmental planning
frameworks.
-Coordinating departmental inputs and reports in support of national planning, budgeting,
monitoring and reporting processes, including the Budget Vote, State of the Nation
Address (SoNA), Estimates of National Expenditure (ENE), Adjusted Estimates of
National Expenditure (AENE) and other government-directed strategic initiatives.
-Coordinating the monitoring and reporting of strategic and governance initiatives,
including government-wide frameworks, plans and implementation programmes.
-Managing and coordinating all parliamentary liaison functions of the SAPS.
-Coordinating and monitoring parliamentary questions, enquiries, reports, resolutions and
commitments to ensure compliance with prescribed timeframes.
-Coordinating SAPS engagements with Parliamentary Committees and other oversight
structures.
-Maintaining systems and processes for monitoring parliamentary matters and reporting
on organisational compliance with parliamentary obligations.
-Providing strategic advice and support to executive management on planning,
governance, oversight and parliamentary matters.
-Manage and utilise human, financial, physical and information resources in the
immediate post environment in accordance with the strategies, priorities and regulatory
framework of the SAPS.
